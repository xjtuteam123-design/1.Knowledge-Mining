# Paper Metadata

- **Title**: (CoCrFeMnNi)97.02Mo2.98高熵合金σ相析出演变及力学性能 (Precipitation σ Phase Evolution and Mechanical Properties of (CoCrFeMnNi)97.02Mo2.98 High Entropy Alloy)
- **Authors**: Yao Xiaofei, Wei Jingpeng, Lü Yukun, Li Tianye
- **Journal**: Acta Metallurgica Sinica (Jinshu Xuebao / 金属学报)
- **Year**: 2020
- **Volume/Issue**: Vol. 56, No. 5, pp. 769–775
- **DOI**: 10.11900/0412.1961.2019.00330
- **Alloy system studied**: CoCrFeMnNi (Cantor) high-entropy alloy with Mo addition, specifically (CoCrFeMnNi)97.02Mo2.98
- **Research objective**: To investigate the effect of σ phase (CrMo phase) precipitation on the mechanical properties of (CoCrFeMnNi)97.02Mo2.98 high-entropy alloy through annealing heat treatment, and to reveal the evolution of σ phase quantity, size, morphology, and distribution with annealing temperature.
- **Material category**: Mo-modified CoCrFeMnNi high-entropy alloy (FCC-based HEA with σ phase precipitation). Note: This is not a refractory high-entropy alloy; the base alloy is the classical 3d-transition-metal Cantor alloy.

# Microstructure and Phase Constitution

- **Matrix phase**: FCC (face-centered cubic) solid solution — retained after all annealing treatments.
- **Second phase**: σ phase (CrMo phase), enriched in Cr and Mo.
  - Confirmed by XRD to be present at 1000 °C annealing.
  - At annealing temperatures below 900 °C, XRD did not detect σ phase peaks (volume fraction too low), but SEM/EDS confirmed its presence at grain boundaries.
- **σ phase precipitation evolution with annealing temperature**:
  - **700 °C**: Small amount of short strip-like second phase precipitated at grain boundaries; discontinuous distribution.
  - **800 °C**: More precipitation at grain boundaries, forming long strip-like discontinuous distribution; small amounts of granular precipitates near grain boundaries.
  - **900 °C**: Grain boundary σ phase coarsened, forming continuous distribution; near-grain-boundary precipitates increased, morphology changed to needle-like or strip/sheet-like.
  - **1000 °C**: Grain boundary σ phase refined, became granular and discontinuous; large amounts of intragranular σ phase precipitated (long needle/strip near grain boundaries, short needle farther from grain boundaries).
- **Sequence of precipitation**: σ phase first precipitates at grain boundaries, later within grains (intracrystalline).
- **XRD observation**: FCC peaks retained; (311) peak shifted to higher angle (larger 2θ) after 1000 °C annealing, attributed to reduced lattice constant as Mo leaves solid solution to form σ phase (Bragg's law: λ = 2d sinθ; smaller d → larger θ).
- **Grain size**: Not explicitly reported in numerical form in the original paper.
- **Elemental segregation**: σ phase enriched in Cr and Mo; matrix correspondingly depleted in these elements.
- **Strengthening mechanisms discussed**: Second-phase strengthening (σ phase precipitation strengthening), solid-solution strengthening of Mo in the FCC matrix.

# Element-Function Knowledge Cards

## Mo

**Applicable Alloy System**: CoCrFeMnNi (Cantor) high-entropy alloy

**Addition Amount or Composition Range**: 5 wt.% (2.98 at.%) Mo; nominal composition (CoCrFeMnNi)97.02Mo2.98

**Main Function**: Solid-solution strengthening when in solid solution; σ phase (CrMo phase) formation and second-phase strengthening after annealing/precipitation.

**Microstructural Mechanism**: Mo has a larger atomic radius than Co, Cr, Fe, Mn, and Ni. When Mo exists as substitutional atoms in the FCC lattice, it increases lattice constant and lattice distortion, producing solid-solution strengthening. Above the solubility limit (~5 wt.% in CoCrFeMnNi, per reference [22] cited in this paper), Mo promotes σ phase precipitation. The σ phase is a CrMo-rich intermetallic phase that acts as a hard second phase.

**Property Effect**: 
- Solid-solution strengthening: Slight strength increase without significant loss of ductility when Mo content < 5 wt.% and no σ phase forms.
- Second-phase strengthening: After annealing at 900–1000 °C, σ phase precipitation significantly increased strength. Yield strength increased from 253 MPa (700 °C annealed) to 326 MPa (1000 °C annealed); tensile strength increased from 510 to 582 MPa. Hardness also increased with σ phase fraction.
- Elongation decreased from 52.7% (700 °C) to 43.8% (900 °C), then recovered to 48.3% at 1000 °C due to intragranular σ-phase refinement.

**Potential Side Effects**: 
- When σ phase precipitates coarsely at grain boundaries (900 °C annealing), ductility decreases significantly.
- Excess Mo (> 5 wt.%) in as-cast or lightly annealed condition leads to brittle σ phase at grain boundaries, markedly reducing ductility (cited from reference [22] and [25] in the paper).
- Removal of Mo from solid solution (at 1000 °C) reduces the solid-solution lattice distortion.

**Original Evidence**: 
- "Mo与Co、Cr、Fe、Mn、Ni等元素相比原子半径较大，Mo以置换原子的状态存在于合金的晶体结构中，形成置换固溶体，使合金的晶格常数变大、晶格畸变增大" (Section 2.2)
- "当Mo含量大于5%时，合金基体中析出σ相，合金强度略有提高，但塑性明显降低，这说明CoCrFeMnNi高熵合金中Mo元素的固溶度约为5%" (Introduction)
- "退火温度为900和1000℃时，合金中析出σ相较多，第二相强化作用显著，因此，强度明显提高" (Section 2.4)
- EDS Table 1: σ phase contains 10.00–11.64 at.% Mo vs. 3.06–3.40 at.% Mo in matrix.

## Cr + Mo (σ phase / CrMo phase)

**Applicable Alloy System**: CoCrFeMnNi high-entropy alloy with Mo addition

**Addition Amount or Composition Range**: σ phase composition measured by EDS: Cr 28.33–30.33 at.%, Mo 10.00–11.64 at.%. Matrix contains Cr 20.42–21.42 at.% and Mo 3.06–3.40 at.%.

**Main Function**: Second-phase (precipitation) strengthening of the FCC matrix.

**Microstructural Mechanism**: The σ phase is a tetragonal or topologically close-packed (TCP) intermetallic phase enriched in Cr and Mo. It precipitates preferentially at grain boundaries at lower annealing temperatures (700–800 °C), coarsens into continuous strips at grain boundaries at 900 °C, and at 1000 °C becomes refined, granular, and discontinuous at grain boundaries while also precipitating extensively within grains. The precipitation sequence follows: grain boundary first → near-grain-boundary regions → grain interior.

**Property Effect**: 
- Precipitation of σ phase increases both hardness and strength through second-phase strengthening.
- At 900 °C annealing: continuous, coarse grain-boundary σ phase → strength increase but reduced ductility (elongation decreased to 43.8%).
- At 1000 °C annealing: refined, granular grain-boundary σ phase + abundant intragranular σ phase → simultaneous improvement of strength and plasticity. Yield strength 326 MPa, tensile strength 582 MPa, elongation 48.3%.
- Intragranular σ phase precipitation and refinement promote synchronous enhancement of strength and plasticity.

**Potential Side Effects**: 
- Coarse, continuous σ phase at grain boundaries (900 °C condition) causes embrittlement and reduced elongation.
- Excessive σ phase at grain boundaries can lead to brittle intergranular fracture tendency (implied by reduced ductility).
- Depletes the matrix of Cr and Mo solid-solution elements, potentially reducing corrosion resistance (not discussed in this paper).

**Original Evidence**: 
- "退火温度为1000℃时，析出σ相相对细小，且晶内大量析出，而900℃退火温度下，析出σ相相对粗大，且主要分布在晶界处，这导致了1000℃比900℃退火处理后合金的延伸率增大、屈服强度显著提高" (Section 2.4)
- "σ相在晶内析出及其细化，能够促进合金强度与塑性同步提高" (Conclusion 3)
- XRD: "在1000℃退火处理后出现CrMo相(σ相)衍射峰" (Section 2.2)

## Co, Cr, Fe, Mn, Ni (base Cantor alloy elements)

**Applicable Alloy System**: CoCrFeMnNi high-entropy alloy

**Addition Amount or Composition Range**: Equiatomic, 19.404 at.% each in the (CoCrFeMnNi)97.02Mo2.98 alloy

**Main Function**: Form the FCC solid-solution matrix with excellent room-temperature and cryogenic ductility.

**Microstructural Mechanism**: These five elements form a stable single-phase FCC solid solution. The matrix provides inherent ductility (elongation > 57% at room temperature for the base alloy, per reference [4–6] cited in this paper).

**Property Effect**: The FCC matrix maintains ductile fracture behavior (dimple morphology, necking) even after σ phase precipitation strengthening. The matrix ensures that the material retains good plastic deformation capability.

**Potential Side Effects**: The base alloy alone has relatively low strength (~380 MPa yield strength at room temperature for the unmodified alloy, per cited reference). Cr is consumed from the matrix into the σ phase during annealing, reducing solid-solution Cr content.

**Original Evidence**:
- "CoCrFeMnNi高熵合金室温下延伸率可达57%以上，其液氮温度下的延伸率更高，可达75%以上，但其强度相对较低，约为380MPa" (Introduction)
- EDS Table 1 shows matrix Cr and Mo depletion as σ phase forms.

# Strengthening Mechanism Knowledge Cards

## Solid-Solution Strengthening (Mo in FCC matrix)

- **Related elements**: Mo
- **Typical alloys**: (CoCrFeMnNi)97.02Mo2.98, CoCrFeNiMox, CoCrFeMnNi-Mo alloys
- **Key compositions**: Mo ≤ 5 wt.% (below solubility limit)
- **Microstructural changes**: Mo atoms substitute into FCC lattice sites; lattice constant increases; lattice distortion increases due to larger Mo atomic radius.
- **Property improvements**: Slight strength increase without significant ductility loss. When Mo content < 5 wt.%, strength increases slightly while plasticity remains essentially unchanged.
- **Risks and limitations**: At higher Mo contents (> 5 wt.%), σ phase formation occurs, transitioning from solid-solution to second-phase strengthening mechanism. Solid-solution strengthening contribution decreases when Mo leaves the matrix to form σ phase (e.g., at 1000 °C annealing).
- **Original evidence**: "Mo以置换原子的形态固溶于基体的晶体结构中，合金的强度略有提高，塑性未发生明显变化" (Introduction, citing reference [22]).

## Second-Phase / Precipitation Strengthening (σ phase)

- **Related elements**: Mo + Cr (σ phase / CrMo intermetallic phase)
- **Typical alloys**: (CoCrFeMnNi)97.02Mo2.98, Cr15Fe20Co35Ni20Mo10, CoCrFeNiMox
- **Key compositions**: Mo > 5 wt.% in CoCrFeMnNi; σ phase contains ~28–30 at.% Cr and ~10–11.6 at.% Mo
- **Microstructural changes**: σ phase precipitates at grain boundaries (700–900 °C) and within grains (1000 °C). Morphology evolves with annealing temperature: small discontinuous strips → thick continuous strips → granular discontinuous (grain boundary); granular → needle/strip (intragranular).
- **Property improvements**: 
  - Significant increase in yield strength (253 → 326 MPa from 700 to 1000 °C annealing)
  - Significant increase in tensile strength (510 → 582 MPa)
  - Hardness increases with σ phase fraction
  - When σ phase is refined and intragranular (1000 °C), strength and plasticity improve simultaneously (elongation recovered to 48.3%)
- **Risks and limitations**: 
  - Coarse, continuous grain-boundary σ phase (900 °C) reduces elongation to 43.8%
  - Severe grain-boundary σ phase accumulation can cause embrittlement
  - Below 900 °C, σ phase volume fraction is too low to provide significant strengthening (strength at 700–800 °C annealing: 510–513 MPa, essentially unchanged)
- **Original evidence**: 
  - "退火处理析出σ相具有明显的第二相强化作用，随着退火温度的升高，σ相析出量增多，硬度及强度均增大，当温度高于900℃尤为显著" (Conclusion 2)

## Grain-Boundary σ Phase Precipitation (Ductility-Limiting Factor)

- **Related elements**: Mo, Cr
- **Typical alloys**: (CoCrFeMnNi)97.02Mo2.98, CoCrFeNiMox
- **Key compositions**: σ phase at grain boundaries containing Cr 28.33–30.33 at.% and Mo 10.00–10.63 at.%
- **Microstructural changes**: At 700–900 °C, σ phase precipitates preferentially and coarsens at grain boundaries, forming continuous brittle networks at 900 °C.
- **Property improvements**: Strength enhancement through grain-boundary pinning and second-phase hardening.
- **Risks and limitations**: Continuous grain-boundary σ phase reduces ductility by promoting intergranular weakness; the σ phase–matrix interface is incoherent and brittle (per cited references [16, 19–21] for similar phases).
- **Original evidence**: "σ相在晶界处析出，且随Mo含量的增大，晶界处析出的σ相的量增多、同时形态变得粗大，从而导致脆性明显增大，延伸率显著降低" (Section 2.4, citing Qin et al. [25]).

## Intragranular σ Phase Precipitation and Refinement (Strengthening + Ductility Retention)

- **Related elements**: Mo, Cr
- **Typical alloys**: (CoCrFeMnNi)97.02Mo2.98 (1000 °C annealed condition)
- **Key compositions**: 2.98 at.% Mo; σ phase composition as above
- **Microstructural changes**: At 1000 °C, σ phase becomes refined and granular at grain boundaries while precipitating extensively within grains as long needles near grain boundaries and short needles farther away.
- **Property improvements**: Yield strength increases to 326 MPa (vs. 262 MPa at 900 °C) and elongation increases to 48.3% (vs. 43.8% at 900 °C), demonstrating simultaneous strength and plasticity improvement.
- **Risks and limitations**: Requires precise control of annealing temperature; prolonged exposure at 1000 °C may lead to over-aging (not explored in this paper).
- **Original evidence**: "σ相在晶内析出及其细化，能够促进合金强度与塑性同步提高" (Conclusion 3); "1000℃比900℃退火处理后合金的延伸率增大、屈服强度显著提高" (Section 2.4).

# Key Conclusions

1. **σ phase precipitation evolution**: With increasing annealing temperature (700→1000 °C), the amount of σ phase precipitated in (CoCrFeMnNi)97.02Mo2.98 increases. σ phase first precipitates at grain boundaries, then within grains. Grain-boundary σ phase morphology evolves from fine discontinuous strips → coarse continuous strips → granular discontinuous distribution.

2. **Second-phase strengthening effect**: Annealing-induced σ phase precipitation provides significant second-phase strengthening. Hardness and strength increase with increasing annealing temperature and σ phase volume fraction, with particularly significant strengthening above 900 °C.

3. **Effect of σ phase location and morphology on mechanical properties**:
   - Grain-boundary σ phase provides strengthening but reduces ductility.
   - Refined, intragranular σ phase (1000 °C annealing) enables simultaneous improvement of strength and plasticity.
   - Optimal condition: 1000 °C annealing — yield strength 326 MPa, tensile strength 582 MPa, elongation 48.3%.

4. **Fracture behavior**: All annealed specimens exhibit ductile fracture with necking and dimple morphology, indicating the FCC matrix retains its inherent ductility even after σ phase precipitation strengthening.

5. **Solid solubility of Mo**: Mo has limited solid solubility (~5 wt.%) in the CoCrFeMnNi FCC matrix; beyond this, σ phase formation becomes significant.

# Limitations and Applicable Scope

## Limitations of This Study

1. **Alloy system scope**: This is a 3d-transition-metal-based high-entropy alloy (Cantor-type), not a refractory high-entropy alloy or refractory metal alloy. The σ phase discussed is CrMo type, not applicable to Nb-W, Mo-W, or other refractory alloy systems.

2. **Temperature range**: Annealing temperatures tested were limited to 700–1000 °C; no data for intermediate temperatures between 900 and 1000 °C or above 1000 °C.

3. **No high-temperature mechanical testing**: All tensile tests were conducted at room temperature only. High-temperature strength and creep performance were not evaluated.

4. **No oxidation resistance data**: The paper does not address oxidation behavior or environmental stability.

5. **Limited microstructural quantification**: No quantitative data on σ phase volume fraction, size distribution, or grain size were provided. The paper relies on qualitative SEM observations.

6. **Grain size not reported**: Despite the importance of grain-boundary precipitation, grain size data are not explicitly provided.

7. **Single composition studied**: Only one Mo content (2.98 at.%) was investigated in this work, relying on prior work for the solubility limit determination.

8. **Heat treatment only**: No systematic study of cooling rate effects on σ phase formation; only furnace cooling was used.

## Applicable Scope

- The results are directly applicable to Mo-modified CoCrFeMnNi high-entropy alloys.
- The σ phase precipitation evolution and mechanical property trends may be relevant to other FCC-based high-entropy alloys containing Cr and Mo, such as CoCrFeNiMox systems.
- The concept of controlling σ phase morphology (grain-boundary vs. intragranular) to balance strength and ductility may inform the design of precipitation-strengthened FCC high-entropy alloys.
- The paper demonstrates a method to strengthen the classical Cantor alloy while retaining its characteristic ductile fracture behavior, with potential applications at moderate temperatures where σ phase stability is maintained.
