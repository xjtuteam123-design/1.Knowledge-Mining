# Alloy System and Research Object

- **Alloy system:** Nb1Zr alloy (niobium–zirconium alloy)
- **Application context:** Key material for manufacturing arc discharge tubes (arc tube sealing components) in high-pressure sodium lamps, a third-generation electric light source. The alloy is selected for its good sealing compatibility with sodium-lamp arc-tube ceramics, thermal shock resistance, ductility, and resistance to sodium-ion corrosion.
- **Product form studied:** Rod/bar (棒材), specifically wires/rods with a final diameter of approximately 2.87 mm (finished size range 2.867–2.874 mm).
- **Prior product form:** Tubular blanks were traditionally used; the study focuses on rod blanks that replace tubular blanks for deep-drawing of blind-hole Nb1Zr tubes.
- **Research object:** Processing–microstructure–property relationships for Nb1Zr alloy rods, specifically the effects of deformation process, annealing temperature, and holding time on recrystallized grain size.

# Processing and Heat Treatment

## Original (old) processing route

ϕ160 mm ingot → hammer forging (自由锻) to ϕ97 mm → vacuum heat treatment → radial forging to ϕ38 mm × L mm → saw cutting → surface turning (车面) → vacuum heat treatment → horizontal-vertical roll shape rolling (平立辊型轧) → degreasing and acid pickling → vacuum heat treatment → oxidizing drawing (氧化拉拔) → acid pickling (finished product) → final heat treatment.

## New processing route

ϕ160 mm ingot → **extrusion** to ϕ97 mm (using a 165 extrusion system; heated to 450 °C and held for 150 min) → vacuum heat treatment → radial forging to ϕ38 mm × L mm → saw cutting → surface turning → vacuum heat treatment → horizontal-vertical roll shape rolling → degreasing and acid pickling → vacuum heat treatment → **coating drawing** (涂层拉拔) → ultrasonic cleaning → final heat treatment.

## Key changes

1. **Extrusion (挤压)** replaces hammer forging (自由锻) as the primary breakdown step.
2. **Coating drawing (涂层拉拔)** replaces oxidizing drawing + acid pickling (氧化拉拔 + 酸洗) as the final sizing step.

## Heat treatment parameters

- **Intermediate vacuum heat treatments:** 1,200 °C / 90 min (all intermediate anneals)
- **Final heat treatments compared:**
  - Option A: 1,300 °C / 60 min (note: mechanical property table lists “1,300 °C / 90 min”; there is an internal inconsistency in the paper—the experimental section states 60 min, but the results tables state 90 min)
  - Option B: 1,350 °C / 60 min (selected as optimal)
- **Vacuum atmosphere:** All heat treatments conducted under vacuum.
- **Deformation amount (reduction):** Not explicitly quantified in the original paper beyond the diameter changes (ϕ160 mm → ϕ97 mm → ϕ38 mm → final ~ϕ2.87 mm).

# Microstructure and Phase Constitution

- **Matrix phase:** Not explicitly stated, but Nb1Zr is a BCC (body-centered cubic) niobium solid-solution alloy; the paper does not explicitly identify the phase.
- **Crystal structure discussion:** Not explicitly discussed in the original paper.
- **As-cast structure:** The paper states that “extrusion + radial forging” fully breaks up the as-cast structure (充分破碎铸态组织).
- **Recrystallized grain structure:**
  - Final anneal at 1,300 °C / 90 min: longitudinal grain size grade 6.0–6.5; grains described as relatively large and non-uniform (晶粒偏大且不均匀).
  - Final anneal at 1,350 °C / 60 min: longitudinal grain size grade 7.0–7.5; grain size grade 7.0 in longitudinal, 7.5 in transverse, and 7.0 in planar directions; grains described as fine, uniform, and equiaxed.
  - Target requirement: grain size grade ≥ 7.
- **Grain size (quantitative):** Only reported in ASTM grain size grade (级别), not in micrometers. The target criterion was grade ≥ 7.
- **Grain boundary features:** Equiaxed grain morphology after 1,350 °C / 60 min treatment; non-uniform grains after 1,300 °C / 90 min. No detailed grain-boundary characterization.
- **Second phases or precipitates:** Not discussed in the original paper. No carbides, oxides, or other precipitates are mentioned.
- **Elemental segregation:** Zr distribution in ingot (top/middle/bottom: 0.86/0.95/0.88 wt.%) is provided, indicating some axial segregation in the cast ingot; further segregation analysis is not provided.
- **Strengthening mechanisms discussed:** The paper is primarily a processing study; it does not explicitly discuss solid-solution strengthening, precipitation strengthening, dispersion strengthening, or grain-boundary strengthening in mechanistic terms. The focus is on recrystallization behavior and grain refinement through processing.

- **Key observation:** The 1,350 °C / 60 min treatment gives slightly lower strength (UTS ~260–265 MPa vs. ~280–298 MPa; YS ~140–146 MPa vs. ~167–184 MPa) but higher and more uniform elongation (25.0–26.5% vs. 22.1–23.5%). # Key Conclusions

1. **Extrusion + radial forging as primary breakdown:** Replacing hammer forging with extrusion (heated to 450 °C, held 150 min) followed by radial forging more thoroughly breaks up the as-cast structure, ensuring better material uniformity and fine uniform equiaxed grains.

2. **Optimal final heat treatment:** Vacuum annealing at 1,350 °C for 60 min produces a uniform, fine, equiaxed grain structure meeting the target grain size grade ≥ 7 (actual: 7.0–7.5 longitudinal, 7.5 transverse, 7.0 planar).

3. **Coating drawing replaces oxidation drawing + acid pickling:** The new route achieves a dimensional tolerance of −0.02 mm (measured range 2.867–2.874 mm, CPK = 2.01), far better than the old route's ±0.03 mm control level.

4. **Mechanical property comparison:** The 1,350 °C / 60 min treatment yields slightly lower strength but higher and more uniform elongation (25.0–26.5%) than the 1,300 °C / 90 min treatment (22.1–23.5%), with less scatter. For deep-drawing blind-hole Nb1Zr tubes, higher elongation is beneficial.

5. **Environmental benefit:** The new process eliminates the acid-pickling step for finished products, avoiding environmental pollution while maintaining dimensional accuracy.

6. **Hardness after 1,350 °C / 60 min:** 102.0–108.0 HV (slightly higher than 97.3–99.1 HV after 1,300 °C / 90 min).

# Limitations and Applicable Scope

- **Processing-focused paper:** This is primarily a processing engineering study; it does not provide detailed microstructural characterization (no TEM, SEM, EBSD, XRD, or phase identification).
- **No high-temperature mechanical properties:** No elevated-temperature tensile, creep, or rupture data are provided. The application is for sodium-lamp service, but high-temperature property data are absent.
- **No oxidation resistance data:** No service oxidation tests or data are reported.
- **Inconsistent heat-treatment labels:** The experimental section states final anneals were “1,300 °C / 60 min” and “1,350 °C / 60 min”, but the results tables list “1,300 °C / 90 min”. This inconsistency is not resolved in the paper.
- **Grain size reported only as ASTM grade:** No quantitative grain size (μm) is provided.
- **Strengthening mechanisms not discussed:** The paper neither identifies phases nor discusses solid-solution strengthening, precipitation strengthening, dispersion strengthening, or grain-boundary strengthening mechanisms.
- **Applicable scope:** The findings apply specifically to Nb1Zr alloy rods (~2.87 mm final diameter) for high-pressure sodium lamp applications, processed via the specific EB melting → extrusion → radial forging → shape rolling → coating drawing → vacuum annealing route described. Extrapolation to other Nb-based alloys, other product forms (sheet, foil, complex geometries), or other service environments should be made with caution.
- **Composition window:** Only a single nominal composition (Nb-1Zr, with 0.86–0.95 wt.% Zr) is studied; the effect of varying Zr content is not investigated.
- **No fracture toughness, fatigue, or creep data:** These properties are not evaluated in this paper.
- **Atmosphere effects during mechanical testing:** Not explicitly stated in the original paper.
- **Details of extrusion and drawing parameters:** Extrusion temperature (450 °C) and holding time (150 min) are given; other deformation parameters (extrusion ratio, die design, strain rate, drawing speed, lubricant type) are not provided.
