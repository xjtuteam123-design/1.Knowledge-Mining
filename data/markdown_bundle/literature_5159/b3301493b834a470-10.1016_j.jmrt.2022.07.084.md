abstract
The effect of Mn element on the microstructure and mechanical properties of Nb eSi based
alloys is studied, and the investigated alloys compositions are Nb e16Sie22TiexMn (x ¼ 0, 2,
4, 8 at.%). Solubility of Mn in Nbss phase is higher than that in silicide. This dissolution of
Mn in Nbss phase and silicide causes solid solution strengthening. Tiss phase and Mn
2Ti
phase eutectic structure precipitates between dendrites of 8Mn alloy, which affect room-
temperature fracture toughness and compressive strength adversely. After heat treat-
ment, Tiss phase and Mn
2Ti eutectic structure almost disappears in Mn-contained
alloys (Nb, Ti) 3Si phase decomposes and transforms to Nbss phase and a-(Nb, Ti) 5Si3
phase. The fracture toughness of heat treated 8Mn alloy has been improved greatly due to
Mn solution strengthening in Nbss phase.

1. Introduction
With the development of aerospace technology, the re-
quirements for high temperature materials are becoming
more and more stringent [ 1,2]. For commonly used nicked-
based alloys nowadays, the service temperature (1150
/C14 C)
has reached the 85% of its melting point [ 3]. Therefore, new
generation high-temperature materials which could service
above 1200
/C14 C is urgently needed. Nb eSi alloys have attracted
many attentions due to their high melting temperature
(~1800
/C14 C), relatively small density (6.6 e7.2 g/cm 3) and excel-
lent mechanical performance in high-temperature environ-
ments [ 4e7]. However, the room-temperature fracture
toughness and oxidation resistance of Nb eSi alloys at high
temperature is poor, these shortcomings limit its application
greatly [8,9].
NbeSi alloys is composed of Niobium solid solution (Nbss)
phase which has good ductility and silicide phases ((Nb, X)
3Si
and (Nb, X) 5Si3) which presents high hardness but brittle
[8,10]. The presence of Nbss phase could serves room-
temperature ductility to improve the toughness, and silicide
as strengthening phases can enhance the strength and creep
resistance of the alloy [ 11e13]. Nb eSi binary phase diagram
shows the eutectic point of binary Nb eSi alloy is 17.5% e18.2%
of Si. Compared to eutectic and hypoeutectic alloy, volume
fraction of Nbss in hypereutectic alloy is lower and the size of
Nb
5Si3 is bigger. This combination is harmful to fracture

2. Experimental procedures
The nominal compositions of Nb e16Sie22TiexMn are
Nbe16Sie22Ti, Nb e16Sie22Tie2Mn, Nb e16Sie22Tie4Mn and
Nbe16Sie22Tie8Mn. The purity of the elemental metal used
in this experiment is all above 99.95%. All the alloys
mentioned above were prepared by a vacuum non-
comsumable electric arc furnace under an argon atmo-
sphere of 0.05 MPa below standard atmospheric pressure,
equipped with a water-cooled copper crucible. Each ingot was
melted 5 times repeatedly to ensure that the composition of
alloys was uniform. Phase composition was analyzed by X-ray
diffraction which used the Paralytical, XRD, Empyrean under
40 kV and 8
/C14 /min’s scanning rate. Observation of micro-
structure morphology adopt Quanta 200FEG scanning electron
microscope with backscatter mode. Chemical composition of
constituent phases was test by the energy disperse spectros-
copy which based on the SEM. Electron diffraction image and
the analysis of distribution of chemical elements used trans-
mission electron microscope (Talos F200 /C2 ). Heat-treatment at
1450
/C14 C lasts 50 h was carried out by resistance furnace. As
shown in the Fig. 1, three 22 /C2 4 /C2 2 mm three-point bending
specimens with pre-crack were cut by electrode discharge
machine from each ingots to test the room temperature
fracture toughness. The pre-crack was also obtained by the
electrode discharge machine, its size is 2 mm in depth and
0.18 mm in width. The room-temperature fracture toughness
test adopted the electronic universal mechanical testing ma-
chine (Instron5569) and the loading rate was 0.2 mm/min, and
every alloy was tested three times to obtain the average value.

3.3. Room temperature mechanical properties
3.3.1. Room-temperature fracture toughness of as-cast alloys
Figure 10 shows the KQ value which represents room-
temperature fracture toughness of Nb eSi based alloys. As
the content of Mn in alloys increases (atomic ratio from 2 to 8),
the room-temperature fracture toughness shows a trend of
increasing at ﬁrst and then decreasing. When the atomic ratio
of Mn reaches 4 at.%, the fracture toughness of the as-cast
alloys get a peak (8.07 MPa m
1/2). When the atomic ratio ex-
ceeds 4 at.%, the fracture toughness is severely degraded.
Compared with Nbe16Sie22Ti alloy, the fracture toughness of
the 4 at.% Mn alloy is increased by 17%.
Figure 11 shows the BSE images of crack propagation path
on the notched samples which had undergone bending test. It
can be seen that the cracks of all the alloys mainly propagate
in two ways: one of which is propagate along the boundaries
between Nbss and (Nb, Ti)
3Si phases, the other is pass through
the inside of silicide phase leading transgranular fracture.
Cracks is also accompanied by deﬂection and branching in the
process of propagation in (Nb, Ti)
3Si, as shown in Fig. 11 .
Pointed out in previous research that the deﬂection and
branching of cracks increases fracture surface area, which
absorbs energy required for crack propagation [ 27e29]. This is
beneﬁcial to improve room-temperature fracture toughness.
Meanwhile, solid solution strengthening caused by Mn also
increase the hardness of silicide phase to hinder propagation
of cracks. However, due to the brittleness of silicide, its
improvement on fracture toughness is very limited. The
improvement of fracture toughness mainly depends on the
existence of Nbss. As ductile phase, crack disappears when it
encounters Nbss phase. Residual stress continues to propa-
gate at silicide or phase interface near Nbss phase, resulting
bridging, as shown in Fig. 11 [13]. As mention in Section 3.1,
Mn element tends to dissolve in Nbss and replace the posi-
tions of Ti atoms. This substitute process changes the lattice
constants. Based on XRD data, average lattice constants of
Nbss phase in as-cast alloys is calculated, as shown in Table 5.
Average lattice constant decreases with the increases of Mn
content in alloy. This indicates that lattice distortion in Nbss
gradually increases, solid solution strengthening effect of Mn
is also stronger. Although the volume fraction of Nbss phase
decreases with the increase of Mn concentration, the hin-
dering effect of Nbss on crack propagation increases, which
ameliorates room-temperature fracture toughness greatly.
The above is why the fracture toughness increases with con-
centration of Mn.
When the Mn element content reaches 8 at.%, Mn
2Ti
phase precipitated between particles. Under external force,
Mn
2Ti phase will become the source of cracks. Existence of
Mn2Ti is harmful to fracture toughness, so, the KQ of
Nbe16Sie22Tie8Mn decreases slightly compared to that
of 4Mn.

4. Conclusions
(1) With the increase of Mn, the volume fraction of Nbss
decreases. Solubility of Mn in Nbss phase is higher than
that in silicide. When the content of Mn reaches 8 at.%,
Tiss and Mn
2Ti eutectic structure precipitates at the
grain boundary.
(2) After heat treatment, Tiss phase precipitates in Nbss
phase, and (Nb, Ti) 5Si3 appears in Nb e16Sie22Ti alloy
forming a lamellar eutectic structure with Nbss
phase (Nb, Ti)
3Si in Nb e16Sie22Tie8Mn alloy trans-
forms into (Nb, Ti) 5Si3 and Nbss. The Nbss volume
fractions of alloys decreases after heat treatment except
for Nbe16Sie22Tie8Mn.
(3) Solid solution strengthening effect of Mn in Nbss
contributes to the improvement of room-temperature
fracture toughness greatly. However, the Mn
2Ti at the
interface in the as-cast 8Mn alloy is harmful to frac-
ture toughness. Due to the increase of Mn content in
Nbss, K Q value of as-cast Nb e16Sie22Tie8Mn alloy
has increased by 17% compared to as-cast
Nbe16Sie22Ti alloy, heat-treatment 8Mn alloy has
increased by 107% compared to heat-treatment
Nbe16Sie22Ti alloy.
(4) Silicide phase increases with the increase of Mn element
and the solid solution strengthening of Mn in Nbss in-
creases the strength which is beneﬁcial to the compres-
sive properties, but the precipitation of Tiss which is
ductile causes the reduce of yield strength, decreasing
compressive properties of Nb e16Sie22Tie8Mn. After
heat treatment, 4Mn alloy which has the most volume
fraction of silicide shows the best compressive strength.
