Introduction 
Nowadays, worldwide human beings are pursuing energy conservation and environmental 
protection, which can be facilitated effectively by producing lightweight engines and machines. 
Consequently, to continually improve the strength while maintaining sufficient plasticity of 
metallic materials becomes a lasting pursuit for material scientists. Whereas to improve strength 
by traditional methods, such as by introducing solid solution atoms, precipitates and dispersed 
particles, grain boundaries and utilizing phase transformation, invariably results in the reduction 
of plasticity, which is the so-called strength-plasticity trade-off.
1-3 
Significant progresses have been achieved with respect to overcoming the strength-plasticity 
trade-off issue in recent years. The effective methods available to simultaneously improve the 
strength and plasticity of metallic materials mainly cover four categories, namely the preparation 
of bimodal microstructure,
4,5 severe plastic deformation, 6,7 construction of hierarchical or 
gradient nanostructures 8-10 and introduction of high density nanotwins, 11-14 respectively. 
Considering the former three methods generally involve a large amount of plastic deformation,

accommodate large numbers of dislocations. In spite of these advantages, the introduction of 
high density nanotwins is mainly used in the strengthening and toughening of ductile materials 
such as copper, stainless steel, magnesium alloys and palladium films now.

technologies for γ-TiAl based alloys. Now there are mainly two effecti

Conclusions 
In summary, this research realizes high compressive strength and plasticity simultaneously at 
room temperature in a Cr-rich γ-TiAl based alloy by introducing high density deform ation 
nanotwins. A promising strategy composed of non-equilibrium continuous casting followed by 
annealing in ( α+γ) two-phase region is proposed to reduce the product ion cost of high 
performance γ-TiAl based alloys and further facilitate their larg e-scale industrial application. 
This strategy can generate numerous Shockley partial dislocations and stacking faults in γ-TiAl 
p
hase, which can act as effective heterogeneous nucleation sites for high density nanotwins in as-
annealed alloys during deformation, especially in the γ lamellae. This strategy could apply to 
o
ther brittle alloys with favorable twinning deformation mechanism and provide an 
implementable pathway for development of high strength and high ductility materials.
