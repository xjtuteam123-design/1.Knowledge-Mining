2. Experimental procedure
An extruded rod of Mo (99.97% purity; 5mm dia-
meter ·300mm long) was obtained from Alfa Aesar in
Ward Hill, MA. Ingot metallurgy methods were used
to produce a Mo solid solution alloy of composition
Mo–0.9wt.% Si and 0.15wt.% B (Mo–3.0at.% Si–
1.3at.% B). This composition was expected to yield a
microstructure that was marginally two-phase, composed
of a saturated Mo(Si,B) solid solution with 3–5vol.% of
the T2 phase (hereafter referred to as the Mo solid solu-
tion alloy). High purity Mo, Si and B were obtained
and a1.5in. diameter ingot was produced by arc melting
andre-meltingthematerialtoproduceahomogeneousin-
got.TheingotwascannedinpureMoandextrudedusing
a 4:1 reduction ratio. Asecond/C2475mm diameter ingot
that was/C24200mm tall was produced by vacuum arc re-
melting, and after scalping the surface of the ingot, it
was hot isostatic pressed (HIPed), cut into two halves,
and one half was isothermally forged into a pancake in
three steps from an initial height of/C2470mm to a ﬁnal
height of /C2425mm. Chemical analysis of the extrusion
yielded a composition of Mo–0.75wt.% Si–0.14wt.% B
(Mo–2.5at.% Si–1.2at.% B). Chemical analysis was not
performed on the isothermally forged material.
