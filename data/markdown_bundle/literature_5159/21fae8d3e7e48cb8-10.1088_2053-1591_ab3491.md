Abstract
Ti-6Al-4V alloy is extensively used in the low-pressure fan compressor blades in aero engine due to
corrosion resistance was high and speciﬁc strength at room temperature and reasonably at elevated
temperature. In case of autogeneous welding of thin to moderate sections of these alloys, high
frequency Gas Tungsten Constricted Arc Welding(GTCAW) offers many metallurgical advantages
than the conventional Gas Tungsten Arc Welding(GTAW) process such as fusion zone(FZ) grain
reﬁnement, minimized width of the heat affected zone(HAZ), fewer distortion, and residual stresses.
Titanium alloys welded by GTAW process produce Widmanstätten structures and result in poor
mechanical properties of the weld joints. Hence GTCAW was used to weld Ti-6Al-4V alloy to control
the resultant fusion zone microstructure by constricting the arc using high frequency current. In this
paper, the microstructural characterizations, hot tensile properties(RT, 150 °C, 250 °C, 300 °C) and
fracture surface morphology of the GTCA welded Ti-6Al-4V alloy joints are presented. High
frequency current pulsing of GTCAW process eliminated the formation of Widmanstätten structure
in fusion zone and enhanced the tensile strength compared to the constant current GTAW process.
1. Introduction
Titanium (Ti) alloys were applied in multifaceted engineering relevance owing to low density, outstanding
performance in elevated temperature, high resistance to corrosion and speciﬁc strength [1–3]. Ti-6Al-4V alloys
are α+β titanium alloy, Aluminum(Al) acts as α stabilizer; Vanadium (V) acts as aβ stabilizer. In Ti-6Al-4V
alloy, the allotropic transformation takes place when the temperature reaches 885°C, then α phase changes toβ
phase [4]. While theβ phase changing toα phase decides the properties and structure of alloys[5]. Weldability of
titanium and its alloyed classiﬁcations is good in general, though typical attention might be required while
welding. However titanium alloys are vulnerable to failure at elevated temperatures due to high afﬁnity to
oxygen and nitrogen. Therefore, there is high susceptibility for contamination when exposed to welding
environment [6–8].
Both conventional fusion welding and advanced solid state welding processes were effectively employed to
join the complex Ti-6Al-4V alloy structural components[9]. GTAW technique was simple and less expensive
compared to other fusion welding process, so it was oftenpreferred for fabrication[10]. Compared to base metal
(BM) the reduction of ductility(almost 50%) is the major problem encountered in GTAW process[11]. GTAW
process is always high heat input process so grain growth can be occurred in FZ and also in the HAZ[12].I nF Z
the formation of Widmanstätten structure(α+β structure) reduces the ductility and strength of the joints due
to low cooling rates(1.5 °Cs −1) [13].

Ti-6Al-4V alloys were also joined by high density energy process like electron beam welding(EBW) and laser
beam welding (LBW), which need full vaccum chamber and high expensive equipments[14, 15]. Heat input
associated in EBW and LBW was lower compared to GTAW process. So theβ-phase transformed toα-phase
were faster (525 °Cs −1 for EBW and 410°Cs −1 for LBW) [16]. Because of rapid cooling theβ-phase
transformed to α′ martensite structure in FZ. The strength and hardness in FZ was increased by presence ofα′
martensite, similarly ductility was reduced because in FZ the hard martensite was presented[17, 18].
In FZ by altering the grain size and microstructural morphology the joint performance can be improved. The
ductility was very poor in Ti-6Al-4V alloy joints due to hard martensite(α′) presented in FZ of high density
energy processes (LBW and EBW), large priorβ-grain structure in the GTAW process[19]. Many researchers
reported that current pulsing techniques give better grain reﬁnements in FZ and improve the ductility by
reduction of prior-β grain size. In PCGTAW process, the cooling rate is much higher(5 °C/s) than constant
current GTAW process. This avoids the Widmanstätten structure and alsoα′ martensite structure in the FZ.
Current pulsing causes agitation in the molten weld pool and also current level variation provides enhanced
effect on reﬁnement of grains[20, 21].
So All the above literature pointed out that FZα′ martensite and reduction of priorβ enhanced the strength,
hardness and ductility of PCGTAW joints of Ti alloys. However, acceptable level of ductility was not attained by
GTAW and PCGTAW process[22, 23]. Gas Tungsten Constricted Arc Welding(GTCAW) is a new variant of
GTAW process which uses current lower than 5A and used for welding of thin sheets less than 0.1mm thickness
[24]. In GTCAW process, the intermediary cooling rates were obtained between GTAW, LBW/EBW process.
The GTCAW operates at 20 kHz which results in highly constricted arc with columnar proﬁle, much similar to a
plasma arc. Additionally, arc is constricted with the magneticﬁeld induced by currentﬁeld produced by delta
current [
25, 26]. The GTCAW machine generates ultra-high frequency pulsing, and the magneticﬁelds around
the arc are programmable that helps in the control of arc constriction and it is shown inﬁgure 1. The arc
constriction causes narrow but deeper weld beads along with narrow HAZ[27].
However, in open literature the available information relating to high temperature tensile properties and
microstructure characteristics of GTCA welded Ti-6Al-4V alloy joints is very scant. It is also understood that
there is no information available in open literature related to the effect of delta current and delta frequency(high
frequency current pulsing parameters) on high temperature tensile properties of Ti-6Al-4V alloy joints. Hence,
this research work is planned to evaluate the hot tensile properties of autogenously welded high frequency gas
tungsten constricted arc welded Ti-6Al-4V alloy joints.

4. Discussion

input involved in this process Delta current plays a major role in restricting FZ area because the weld arc is

equiaxed grains. Fusion boundary of the BM itself was heated rapidly to above liquidus temperature during
welding.

5. Conclusion
1. GTCAW joint possesses higher TS and ductility than the GTAW joint at elevated temperature tensile tests.
Failure occurred well away from FZ and HAZ, i.e. at the BM region, in all the joints tested irrespective of test
temperatures. This suggested that the FZ of both GTAW and GTCAW joints are stronger than the BM.
2. High frequency pulsing current helps in increasing the tensile properties of GTAW joints of Ti- 6Al-4V
alloy at all test temperatures. The grain reﬁnement by increasing the delta frequency and dramatically
reduced prior β grain boundary that resulted in enhanced ductility.
3. Of the two types of welding modes, GTCAW resulted 40% smaller fusion zone area and 36% narrow heat
affected zone compared to GTAW. This is mainly due to arc constriction occurred due to high frequency
current pulsing.
