Effects of Forging Process on Microstructure and Properties of Niobium Alloy
with Low Density and High Strength
CAI Xiaomei, ZHENG Xin, BAI Run, WANG Feng, LIU Hui, XIA Mingxing, XUE Jianrong
(Northwest Institute for Nonferrous Metals, Xi'an 710016, China)
Abstract
：
The low density high strength niobium alloy bar with good plasticity was forged by using multiple upsetting-
stretching with air hammer and hydraulic hammer, respectively. And the influence of forging process on the microstructure
and mechanical properties of the alloy was investigated by OM and SEM observation and mechanical performance test. The
results show that when forging by air hammer, the impact force to the alloy is large, the stress concentration is easy to crack,
the deformation is not easy to go deep, the microstructure is not uniform, and the plasticity is poor. The fracture surface mainly
shows the mixed fracture surface of quasi-cleavage fracture and dimple fracture. When forging by hydraulic hammer, the alloy
is subjected to static pressure, the deformation is easy to go deep, the microstructure is more uniform, the plasticity is better,
and the fracture surface is dimple fracture. Hydraulic hammer forging can effectively improve the microstructure of the alloy
and thus improve the plasticity of the alloy.
Key words
：
Nb based alloy; hydraulic hammer forging; mechanical properties
