ABSTRACT Zirconium based alloy is a new type structural material with high speciﬁc strength
and good comprehensive properties. In this articl e, quasi–static compression at room temperature
and dynamic compression under various temperatures were performed to a new zirconium–titanium
alloy. It was found that this alloy showed good strength and plasticity, and the strength increased
and plasticity decreased with increasing strain rat e or decreasing temperature. A constitutive model
under dynamic compression was established based on Johnson–Cook model, and accorded well with
experimental results.
