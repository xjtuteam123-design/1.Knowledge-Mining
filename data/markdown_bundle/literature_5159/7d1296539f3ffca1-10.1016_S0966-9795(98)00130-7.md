2. Experimental procedure
2.1. Materials preparation
Button ingots with compositions Nb– xAl–10Ti
(x = 5, 13, 17, 20) (compositions are given in mol% in
this paper unless otherwise stated) were prepared by
arc-melting under an Ar atmosphere, using 99.86
mass% niobium, 99.99 mass% aluminum and 99.999
mass% titanium as starting materials. The buttons were
remelted more than twice to assure homogenization of
constituent elements. Microstructure control was car-
ried out by heat treatments or thermo-mechanical pro-
cessing. The Nb-rich portion of Nb–Al binary phase
diagram is shown in Fig. 1 [10]. Since Nb–Al alloys
containing less than 18 mol% Al are solutionized in the
Nb
