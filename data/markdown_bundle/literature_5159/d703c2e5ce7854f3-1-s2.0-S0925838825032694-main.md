1. Introduction
High-entropy alloys (HEAs) are one class of newly developed mate -
rials in recent years [1 – 3] , which consist of at least five principal 
metallic elements. Numbers of HEAs exhibit higher hardness and 
strength, better thermal stability, and oxidation resistance at both room 
and high temperatures [2,4 – 15] compared with conventional alloys, 
thus receiving extensive attention nowadays [16 – 21] . Among HEAs, 
refractory high entropy alloys (RHEAs), composed of at least five re -
fractory metals, i.e., Ti, V, Cr, Zr, Nb, Mo, Ta, Hf, and W [22 – 25] , possess 
superior mechanic properties, especially at high temperatures, to con -
ventional high-temperature alloys, such as nickel-based alloys [6, 
26 – 28] .
Among RHEAs, AlMo

nano-scale mixtures of disordered BCC_A2 and ordered BCC_B2 phases

outstanding high-temperature compressive strength, the ductility of the
