ABSTRACT 
The NbTaTiV-based refractory high-entropy alloys (RHEAs) show promising high-temperature properties and 
excellent processability. However, achieving ultra-strong strengths while maintaining room-temperature pro -
cessability is still a challenge. In this work, a new ultra-strong NbTaTiV RHEA reinforced with 0.35 wt% Al
2
O
3 
was successfully designed and synthesized. The characteristics of oxides and their effects on mechanical prop -
erties were systematically studied. It was found that the Al
2
O
3 
will fully disperse and reacted with NbTaTiV 
RHEA after milling and sintering at 1600 
◦
C, leading to the in-situ formation of dispersive Ti-(O, N) particles and 
the dissolving of Al atoms. The dual-phase RHEA exhibited a superior combination of yield strength (2075 MPa) 
and compressive plasticity (15%) while keeping high strength at a wide temperature range (25 – 1000 
◦
C). The 
ultra-strong strength of the present dual-phase RHEA is mainly attributed to the particle strengthening caused by 
the high dispersion of Ti-(O, N) submicron particles and the solid solution strengthening from the dissolved Al 
and interstitial atoms. It is revealed that RHEAs can be remarkably enhanced through oxide dispersion 
strengthening and solution strengthening, which provides a new sight for designing high-performance RHEAs. 
1. Introduction 
NbTaTiV Refractory high-entropy alloys (RHEAs) have been 
attracting tremendous attention in recent years due to their promising 
high-temperature properties and excellent room-temperature process -
ability [ 1 – 5 ]. The recent development trend of NbTaTiV-based RHEAs 
focused on the following two points: various alloying and the addition of 
ceramic particles. Alloying with elements like W, Mo, Hf, Zr, and Al and 
the addition of ceramic particles including Ti-O-C, TiC, and Al
2
O
3 
are 
effective methods to improve the strength of NbTaTiV-based RHEAs at 
room and elevated temperatures [ 6 – 8 ]. However, the insufficient in -
crease in strength and a sharp decrease in the plasticity caused by 
relatively large amounts of addition hinder further development. It is 
essential to develop a new way to obtain the high strength of the 
NbTaTiV-based RHEAs at room and elevated temperatures while 
maintaining moderate room-temperature processability. 
In terms of improving its strength, the addition of alloying elements 
or ceramic particles has been reported to effectively improve the 
strength of RHEAs. For example, alloying with Al element can decrease 
the lattice constant and lead to huge solution strengthening, thus 
enhancing the strength [ 9 ]. Besides, the addition of ceramic particles, 
including Ti-O-C, TiC, and Al
2
O
3
, is an effective method to improve the 
strength of RHEAs at room and elevated temperatures by inducing 
particle strengthening, interstitial strengthening and refining the grain 
[ 10 , 11 ]. Because of the dispersed Al
2
O
3 
particles and the supersaturated 
interstitial atoms, the grain size of HfNbTaTiZrV RHEA reduced from 
203 to 50 μ m, while the yield strength increased from 1300 to 2696 MPa 
[ 12 ]. However, the effect of the combination of the two strengthening 
methods mentioned above on RHEAs has not been fully understood so 
far. 
Since Al
2
O
3 
performs good physicochemical consistency with the 
element Ti, it can be chosen as an ideal reinforced phase for ductile Ti- 
contained alloys [ 13 ]. It is found that a large vacancy concentration was 
reached in pure Ti at high temperatures, promoting the Al ions diffused 
into the Ti region by the vacancy mechanism [ 14 ]. Further investigation 
reveals that the element Ti reacts with Al

Summarily, NbTaTiV RHEAs with dispersed nano-sized Al
2
O
3 
par -
ticles, residual air, and highly concentrated vacancies were obtained 
after the milling process. In the subsequent sintering process, with the 
promotion of high temperature and high concentration of vacancies, the 
introduced small amount of Al
2
O
3 
particles could react with Ti to form 
the TiO and the dissolving of Al. The dissolving Al atoms and interstitial 
atoms lead to a solution strengthening, and the supersaturated inter -
stitial atoms react with Ti to form dispersed Ti-(O, N) particles, as well as 
further enhancing mechanical properties. Therefore, the remarkable 
mechanical properties of the RHEAs are mainly related to the dispersion 
strengthening of dispersed submicron Ti-(O, N) particles, solid solution 
strengthing of dissolved Al, and the interstitial atoms. As for dispersion 
strengthening, usually, the dislocation by-pass mechanism occurs when 
the particles are above hundred nanometers or incoherent with the 
matrix [ 32 , 33 ]. In this study, the submicron Ti-(O, N) particles are large 
enough (the average diameter is 358.55 nm), thereby mainly strength -
ening the RHEAs through the dislocation by-pass mechanism. The lattice 
parameter of the RHEA sintered at 1600 
◦
C was determined to be 3.2380 
Å, which is smaller compared to the 3.2430 Å of the as-sintered NbTaTiV 
matrix [ 23 ]. As for solid solution strengthening, the lattice constant 
decreased with the solid solution of Al, O, and N, so the interplanar 
spacing became smaller and the dislocation became more difficult to 
slip, leading to an increased strength [ 34 , 35 ]. The grain boundary 
strengthening caused by refined grain structure also partly contributes 
to enhanced strength [ 36 , 37 ]. Besides, the moderate plasticity of the 
oxide-reinforced NbTaTiV RHEAs is mainly attributed to the high 
dispersion of submicron Ti-(O, N) particles, the solid solution of Al and 
interstitial atoms, and the refined grain structure [ 38 ]. 
Moreover, with the increasing sintering temperature, both the yield 
strength and fracture strength of the oxide-reinforced NbTaTiV RHEAs 
increase gradually. This can be attributed to several effects. First, the 
increasing sintering temperature leads to a higher amount of in-situ 
formed Ti-(O, N) particles, thereby causing stronger particle strength -
ening. Second, the higher sintering temperature also promotes the uni -
form diffusion of the Al, O, and N into the matrix, leading to a stronger 
solid solution strengthening. Third, the microstructure NbTaTiV matrix 
is gradually homogeneous with the increasing sintering temperature, 
which also contributes to the increase in strength. Therefore, a superior 
mechanical property is achieved in the oxide-reinforced NbTaTiV 
RHEAs. 
4. Conclusion 
(1) A novel oxide-reinforced NbTaTiV RHEAs with dispersive sub -
micron Ti-(O, N) particles, solid solution Al and interstitial atoms 
was successfully developed through MA and SPS methods. With 
the promotion of high temperature and high concentration of 
vacancies, the Al
2
O
3 
particles can react with Ti to form the TiO 
and the solution of Al, while the supersaturated interstitial atoms 
subsequently assemble in TiO, forming dispersed Ti-(O, N) 
particles. 
(2) The oxide-reinforced NbTaTiV RHEAs have ultra-high yield 
strength at both room temperature (2075 MPa) and high tem -
perature (690 MPa at 1000 
◦
C), while moderate compressive 
ductility ( > 20%) is also retained, showing a better combination 
of high strength and room-temperature processability than most 
of other reported RHEA and superalloys. 
(3) The ultra-high strength of the present dual-phase RHEA is mainly 
attributed to the particle strengthening caused by the high 
dispersion of Ti-(O, N) submicron particles and the solid solution 
strengthening from the dissolved Al and interstitial atoms.
