ABSTRACT
In order to ascertain the requirements of high-quality gas turbine components, an investigation was undertaken 
into the composition design of Ni-based single crystal superalloys utilising high-throughput, data-driven machine 
learning methodologies. A characteristic parameter space influencing high-temperature strength in superalloys 
was established through theoretical calculation and the extraction of data from the superalloy manual. By means 
of correlation screening, it was determined that V γ ′ 、 V
TCP 
and T γ ′ -solvus represent the crucial parameters, which 
were used as the basis for optimisation tasks. In accordance with the principles of domain knowledge, a series of 
constraints were established for the purpose of screening the prospective alloy components, with the objective of 
achieving a high level of temperature strength. Subsequently, the preferred composition alloys and the com -
mercial alloys were prepared through experimentation. Characterisation of the new composition alloys revealed 
that they exhibited a microstructure with 60 ~ 75 % cubic γ ′ phase, which demonstrated a relatively strong 
correlation with the optimisation tasks. Furthermore, the results show that the new alloys exhibit not only su -
perior high-temperature strength but also notable improvements in elongation, density and cost, conferring them 
with enhanced economic value and potential for wider application compared to the current commercial alloys. 
The correlation between microstructure and properties suggests that the enhancement in performance is 
attributable to the optimisation of key microstructure objectives, offering a promising avenue for alloy design.
1. Introduction
Single crystal superalloys have been widely used in various appli -
cations, ranging from turbine blades to combustion chambers, due to 
their excellent high-temperature strength, creep resistance, and oxidize 
resistance[ 1 – 3 ]. Ordinary single crystal superalloys contain more than 
ten elements and the interaction mechanism are complex so it is difficult 
to accurately design the composition of the alloy only based on the 
previous experimental and irregular calculated data. To optimize 
different aspects of material performance, significant efforts have been 
made to balance various alloy elements, including Al, Ti, Ta, Co, Cr, W, 
Mo, Re, Ru, and Hf[ 4 , 5 ]. However, traditional alloy design methods, 
which often rely on trial and error to discover new advanced superal -
loys, are inefficient and costly. To expedite this process, innovative 
design strategies are essential. By combining calculations, simulations, 
and experiments, researchers aim to develop ideal materials more 
efficiently than with previous empirical methods. In the process of 
research and development of single crystal superalloys, the rapid rise of 
computational materials science has promoted the transformation of 
materials research from the trial and error model to the calculation- 
driven research and development model[ 6 ]. The proposal of material 
genetic engineering has promoted the development of material big data 
and the comprehensive application of artificial intelligence technology 
in the material field[ 7 , 8 ]. The combination of big data and artificial 
intelligence is called the fourth paradigm of science or the fourth in -
dustrial revolution. The machine learning method is one of the core 
technologies of materials genetic engineering and is widely used in 
many fields, such as physics[ 9 ], genetics[ 10 ], bioinformatics[ 11 ], etc. In 
recent years, some researchers have combined machine learning with 
the design of new materials, and have achieved a series of achievements 
in magnetic materials[ 12 ], polymer materials[ 13 ], perovskites[ 14 ], 
photovoltaic materials[ 15 ], and superalloys[ 16 ]. Machine learning has

seven experiments. Based on 322 as-cast high-entropy alloy data sam -

enthalpy, and mixing entropy by sequential selection method, and 
established a support vector machine classification model. The model 
could classify the HEC body-centered cubic phase, face-centered cubic 
phase and solid solution without single phase with an accuracy of more 
than 90 %.
Previous studies have not adequately addressed alloy design in terms 
of both properties and elements. Furthermore, most studies focused on a 
single property instead of considering multiple properties. To minimize 
the risks associated with new material development and to expedite 
material research, this paper performed phase diagram calculations 
combined with machine learning to identify key parameters that affect 
the tensile strength of the alloy, based on a substantial dataset. Addi -
tionally, the relationship between target properties and alloy composi -
tions was established by integrating simulations, calculations, and 
experiments, resulting in alloy compositions with excellent target 
properties.

4. Conclusions
The main research goal of this paper is to accelerate the design of Ni- 
based single crystal superalloys based on data-driven machine learning 
methods. Parameters that have a strong correlation with tensile strength 
were analyzed using CALPHAD thermodynamic calculations and phase 
diagrams, along with machine learning techniques. Subsequently, new 
alloy compositions that may exhibit excellent microstructures were 
identified through constraint screening. After conducting experimental 
verification, it was found that the new alloys achieved ideal micro -
structures and demonstrated superior performance compared to clas -
sical alloys. Finally, radar maps of the designed composition alloys were 
created to address various target requirements. The main results of this 
paper are summarized as follows: 
(1) The key parameters affecting the tensile strength of the alloy 
were selected and used as the optimization targets. The predic -
tion models were established by machine learning methods to 
design.
(2) The performance of the samples were compared with classcial 
alloys and the results showed that the tensile strength of the 
designed alloys were comparable to that of the traditional alloys. 
Even the properties of some new composition alloys exceed the 
existing brands, showing excellent comprehensive performance 
advantages.
(3) It ’ s important to note that the new alloy compositions with po -
tential excellent properties were successfully designed, which 
provides an effective way for the rapid design and development of 
new materials. What ’ s more, the new component alloys exhibit 
higher hardness, lower density, and are lightweight compared to 
traditional alloys. This suggests that the optimized components 
designed in this paper may offer better overall performance and 
significant application potential.
