1. Introduction
At present, as the main materials of turbine blade, the used tem-
perature of Ni-based alloys has reached 1150
◦
C, which is closed to the
melting point of Ni [ 1 – 3 ]. It ’ s necessary to research and develop a new
superalloy to meet the requirements of aero-engines. More refractory
materials such as ceramic, C/C composite materials and some interme-
tallic materials have been researched. Ceramic materials have the
excellent high temperature performance, nevertheless, the intrinsic
brittleness made a great resistance to the practical application [ 4 ]. C/C
composite materials have the high strength and temperature perfor-
mance, but they have the poor interface bonding force and the difficult-
to-work property [ 5 , 6 ]. Nb
–
Si based alloys have a great collocation
with the strength and toughness, which attract a lot attention of
researcher.
Nbss and silicide are the main constituent phases of Nb
–
Si alloys,
ductile Nbss phases provide the toughness and silicides provide the
strength property. However, the poor fracture toughness and oxidation
resistance still have a great gap with the practically operating require-
ment [ 7 ]. Alloying is one of the most effective ways to balance the
combination property of Nb
–
Si superalloys. Numerous studies have
shown that element Ti can significantly enhance the fracture toughness
by decreasing the Prierls-Nabarro energy barrier [ 8 – 10 ]. It ’ s proved that
10 – 25 at.% content of Ti is favorable for the Nb
–
Si alloys, whereas Ti
will be harmful for the fracture toughness when the content exceeds 25
at.% [ 10 ]. Moreover, it ’ s easy to generate impurity phases with a high
content Ti in smelting process by its high active.
The same group element Zr is also reported. Sankar et al. [ 11 ] have
researched the system of Nb-16Si- x Zr (0, 2, 4, 6 at.%) alloys, the results
exhibited that trace amounts of Zr promoted the α Nb
5

3

proportion of γ Nb
5

3

2. Experimental procedures
The as-cast ingots with a nominal compositions of Nb-16Si-24Ti- x Zr
( x = 0, 4, 8, 16) alloys and Nb-16Si-(24- x )Ti- x Zr ( x = 4, 8, 16, 24) alloys
were prepared by vacuum non-consumable melting under high purity
argon atmosphere. All alloys were melted six times to achieve complete
homogenization and consistency of all kinds of alloy elements. The
selected materials have the high purity which exceed 99.95%.
The phase information is carried out by X-ray diffraction with the
range of 20 – 90
◦
. The microstructure and chemical composition of each
region is analyzed by Quanta 200F with the SEM and EDS. The software
of Image-Pro Plus 6.0 is prepared to analysis the amount of constituent
phases, and five images at least in different position were prepared.
The room-temperature compression is tested on AGX-plus with a
loading rate of 0.36 mm/min, and the size of samples is Ф 4*6 mm. The
Ф 6*9 mm samples are prepared for the thermal compression tests in
Gleeble 1500D thermal simulator at 1200
◦
C. To ensure the thermal
uniformity. The samples are heated with a 10
◦
C/s temperature rise rate
and keep 2 min in the experimental temperature. The fracture toughness
measurements were carried out on AGX-plus with the size of 20*4*2
mm. The K
Q
values are manifested by empirical formula [ 12 ] and three
samples are prepared to ensure the accuracy of experimental results.
