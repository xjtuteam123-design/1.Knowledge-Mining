High-performance near-room-temperature n-type Bi2Te3-based
thermoelectric alloys with superior mechanical properties
Jiaying Liu1, Feng Liu1,3, Yuzheng Li1, Boyang Ying1, Yongqing Wu3, Zefeng Tang3, Yiqi Cao4,
Tiejun Zhu1,2* and Chenguang Fu1*
ABSTRACT Hot deformation is an important technique for
fabricating high-strength n-type Bi2Te3-based thermoelectric
(TE) alloys. However, the poor controllability of the oxygen-
induced donor-like effect during the fabrication process often
results in an overhigh carrier concentration, significantly
worsening the near-room-temperature TE performance. Here,
by maximumly avoiding the oxygen-induced donor-like effect,
the carrier concentration of n-type TeI
4-doped Bi2Te2.7Se0.3
can maintain nearly the optimal one during the repetitive hot
deformation processes while the enhanced texture contributes
to higher carrier mobility. Moreover, the hot deformation-
induced dislocations and local distortions are conducive to
lowering the phonon transport. Consequently, a highzT of 1.0
and a large bending strength of 122 MPa are simultaneously
achieved at 300 K. Additionally, a 23-pair TE cooling module
of 8.20 × 6.10 × 1.65 mm
3 was fabricated, which shows a
maximum temperature difference of 82 K at the hot-end
temperature of 350 K. These results highlight the crucial role
of controlling the oxygen-induced donor-like effect in
achieving high-performance near-room-temperature n-type
Bi
2Te3-based TE alloys.
Keywords: bismuth tellurides, donor-like effect, near-room
temperature, thermoelectric materials, hot deformation
INTRODUCTION
Thermoelectric (TE) materials, which can convert heat into
electricity and vice versa, have been widely studied due to their
promising applications in power generation and solid-state
refrigeration. With the rapid development of precise tempera-
ture control such as 5G communications, and polymerase chain
reaction (PCR) thermal cyclers over the past decade, it has been
of considerable importance to develop near-room temperature
high-performance TE materials and micro-TE modules[1–4].
The performance of TE materials is governed by the dimen-
sionlessfigureofmerit zT(zT= S
2σT/κtot),where S, σ, T,and κtot
are the Seebeck coefficient, the electrical conductivity, the
absolute temperature, and the total thermal conductivity
(includingtheelectroniccomponent κ
e,thelatticecomponent κL
and the bipolar componentκb), respectively. To realize desirable
zT values, one can increase the high power factor (PF =S2σ)
through the optimization of carrier concentration (n) and band
engineering[5–8],and suppressthe κ
L byintroducing multiscale
phonon scattering[9,10].
Up till now, Bi2Te3-based alloys are the only commercialized
TE materials that have been employed for assembling the TE
cooling (TEC) modules [11,12]. Bi
2Te3 has the periodically
arranged quintuple layer in the order of Te1-Bi-Te2-Bi-Te1,
which is stacked by the weak van der Waals bonding[13]. The
zone melting (ZM) method is most commonly used in industry,
and the prepared highly-oriented Bi
2Te3-based alloys exhibit
decent TE properties[14]. Nevertheless, the weak van der Waals
force between the adjacent quintuple layers leads to easy clea-
vage and fracture of the TE legs when their dimensions are
lowered to 1 or 2 hundred micrometers, resulting in low yield
and high cost when producing TE legs for the micro-TE mod-
ules [15]. The powder metallurgy method is a means utilized to
enhance the mechanical properties of polycrystalline Bi2Te3-
based alloys by introducing considerable grain boundaries,
which could also effectively suppress theκL through enhancing
the scattering of phonon[16,17]. P-type polycrystalline Bi2Te3-
based alloys with both high strength andzT values have been
obtained. For instance, Xuet al. [18] applied the hot deforma-
tion (HD) approach on p-type Bi0.4Sb1.6Te3 ingots to obtain
enhanced bending strength and high zT of ~1.36 at 400K.
However, zT values of n-type polycrystalline Bi2Te3-based alloys
prepared by powder metallurgy methods are usually below 1.0
[19–21], and cannot match well with the highzT values of p-
type counterparts. Hence, improving the TE performance of n-
type polycrystalline Bi
2Te3-based alloys is essential for highly
efficient micro-TE modules.
Oneofthereasonsleadingtotheworse zTvaluesinthen-type
polycrystallineBi2(Te,Se)3 materialsisthattheelectricalmobility
(μ) degrades severely after grain refinement, owing to the wea-
kened texture. Due to the layered structure, the electrical
transport of Bi
2Te3 exhibits an obvious anisotropy[22]. That is,
the ab-plane (in-plane) electrical conductivity (σI) is usually

much higher than the c-axis (out-of-plane) electrical con-
ductivity (σO). Moreover, the electrical conductivity anisotropic
ratio of n-type single crystal (σI/σO ≈ 4–6) tends to be higher
than that of the p-type counterparts (σI/σO ≈3)[23]. As a result,
the μ of n-type polycrystalline Bi2(Te,Se)3 materials, which is
more sensitive to the (00l) texture, shows a more serious
reduction after grain refinement, unbeneficial for the TE per-
formance. Besides, the deviation from the optimal carrier con-
centration n owing to the oxygen-induced donor-like effect is
another reason leading to the uncontrollable near room-tem-
perature TE performance in n-type polycrystalline Bi2Te3-based
alloys [24,25]. Therefore, maintaining the optimaln by avoiding
oxygen intervention while improving μ by enhancing texture
during the fabrication process is important for obtaining high-
performance n-type polycrystalline Bi2Te3-based alloys for near-
room-temperature applications.
The HD technique has been demonstrated to be effective in
enhancing the (00l) textures and thus to increaseμ of n-type
Bi2Te3-based materials [17,26–28]. For instance, the μ of
approximately 157cm2 V−1 s−1 was obtained at 300K for the
multiple hot-deformed n-type Bi2Te2.79Se0.21, which was close to
the ZMed sample [17]. Meanwhile, plastic deformation could
introduce complex microstructures, including distortions, dis-
locations, and point defects into the matrix, which could induce
strong phonon scattering and thus suppress κ
L [17,18,29,30].
However, the occurrence of the donor-like effect during the
fabrication process often causes the over-high carrier con-
centration and thus deviation from optimal TE performance
unexpectedly (Fig. 1), which hinders the achievement of highzT
near room temperature [17,26,27,31]. Simultaneously, the
unstable n of HD samples with the increased number of defor-
mations adds uncontrollability to the TE performance of the
fabricated polycrystalline Bi
2Te3-based alloys. Very recently, we
found that the occurrence of the donor-like effect in the fabri-
cation of polycrystalline Bi
2Te3-based materials is highly related
to the oxygen-adsorption-induced evolution of native point
defects, i.e., the adsorption of oxygen and the subsequent for-
mationofBi 2TeO5 promotestheevolutionofnativedefectsfrom
acceptor-like Bi’
Te to donor-like Te·
Bi, which leads to the sub-
stantial increase of electron concentration[25]. Therefore, by
avoiding exposure to oxygen during the fabrication of poly-
crystalline Bi2Te3-based materials, the oxygen-induced donor-
like effect could be effectively eliminated, which could guarantee
a stable n while enhancing the texture and μ, benefiting the
realization of mechanically robust n-type polycrystalline Bi
2Te3-
based materials with good near-room-temperature TE perfor-
mance.
In this work, we focus on n-type TeI4-doped Bi2Te2.7Se0.3, of
which the electron concentration lies in the optimal range, to
carry out systematical studies of the evolution of TE properties
during the repetitive HD. The exposure to air is carefully avoi-
ded to eliminate the oxygen-induced donor-like effect. It is
found that an enhanced degree of texture is realized during the
repetitive HD accompanied by the controllable carrier con-
centration. As a result, a maximum PF of 4.2 × 10
−3 Wm −1 K−2
is obtained at 300K, which is one of the highest values among
the reported n-type polycrystals, comparable to that of the n-
type ZMed ingots. A high zT of ~1.1 at 375K and a large
bending strength of 122MPa have been simultaneously
achieved. Additionally, a TEC module is assembled, which
exhibits a maximum temperature difference (ΔT
max) of 82K at a
hot-end temperature of 350K.

CONCLUSIONS
In this work, n-type Bi2Te2.7Se0.3 + 0.1 wt.% TeI4 polycrystalline
alloys were prepared by maximumly avoiding the oxygen-
induced donor-like effect, enabling a good controllability of the
carrier concentration near its optimal one. Moreover, repetitive
hot deformation was performed to enhance the texture degree,
resulting in a high PF of 4.2 × 10
−3 Wm −1 K−2 at room tem-
perature. On the other hand, the HD-induced dislocations and
localdistortionshelpsuppressthe κL.Consequently,apeak zTof
1.1at 375K, ahigh zTof 1.0at 300K, and anenhancedbending
strength of 122MPa are obtained for n-type Bi 2Te2.7Se0.3 +
0.1 wt.% TeI4 materials. Additionally, the 23-pair micro-TEC
device, which exhibits the ΔTmax of 82K at Th = 350K, is suc-
cessfully fabricated. These results highlight an effective way to
fabricate high-performance near-room-temperature n-type
Bi2Te3 alloys with superior mechanical properties, which will
advance the micro-TEC applications.

高力学强度高近室温热电性能的N型Bi 2Te3基合金
刘佳莹 1, 刘锋 1,3, 李昱征 1, 应博阳 1, 吴永庆 3, 唐泽丰 3, 曹一琦 4,
朱铁军 1,2*, 付晨光 1*
摘要 热变形是制备具有高强度N型Bi 2Te3基热电材料的重要手段之
一. 然而, 氧诱导类施主效应的发生会大大增加制备过程中的不可控性,
可能导致电子浓度过高, 从而严重恶化材料近室温热电性能. 本文通过
最大限度地避免氧诱导的类施主效应, 使得N型TeI 4掺杂Bi 2Te2.7Se0.3 样
品的电子浓度在多次热变形的过程中依旧保持在最优载流子浓度区
间, 同时明显增强的织构有助于其迁移率的提升. 此外, 热变形过程中
引入的位错、局域应变等不仅会有效阻碍声子的输运, 还会提高材料
的力学性能. 最终, 在300K时zT值达1.0, 并且抗弯强度高达122MPa.
基于以上材料制作了一个23对的微型热电制冷模块. 当热端温度为
350K时, 最大制冷温差达82K. 以上结果表明控制氧诱导的类施主效
应在实现近室温高热电性能N型Bi 2Te3基合金中具有重要意义.
