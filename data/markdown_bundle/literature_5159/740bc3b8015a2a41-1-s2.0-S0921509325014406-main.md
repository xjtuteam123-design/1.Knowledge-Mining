1. Introduction
High/medium-entropy alloys (H/MEAs) have emerged as one of 
scientific frontiers due to their enormous potential for adjustable mi -
crostructures and comprehensive properties across a wide compositional 
range [ 1 – 5 ]. Nano-precipitates are identified as one of the most effective 
methods to enhance mechanical performance [ 6 ], the sluggish diffusion 
effect in H/MEAs reduces the growth rate of precipitates at elevated 
temperatures, forming the small-sized and dispersed particles. 
Currently, the nano-precipitates in H/MEAs usually include the

coherent precipitates in H/MEAs are influenced by the alloying ele -

relationship between the morphology of nano-precipitates and the me -

Based on the above analyses, the underlying mechanisms of 
abnormal deformation behaviour of A600 MEA are revealed. Firstly, the 
unique nano-lamellar structure, combined with the strongly ordered/ 
disordered lamellar boundaries, effectively increases the yield strength 
to over 1500 MPa. The coherent boundaries of FCC/L1
2 
structures in the 
A600 MEA maintain a continuous lattice, this structure facilitates the 
continuous transportation of dislocations across the phase boundaries 
without changing in the slip direction. When the applied stress exceeds 
the yield strength, dislocations can easily pass through the coherent 
nano-lamellar boundaries rather than be trapped at the boundary, 
thereby preventing the stress concentration deriving from dislocation 
pile-ups. In contrast, the A900 MEA contains large size L1
2 
precipitates, 
which is dislocation-impenetrable, thus induces the pronounced stress 
concentrations for dislocation pile-ups, consequently reducing its uni -
form elongation compared to the A600 MEA.
Secondly, the dislocation movement across the phase boundary 
promotes the sliding of long-range dislocations through the alternated 
FCC/L1
2 
lamellae, promoting microcosmically homogeneous plastic 
deformation, which enhances the stability of plastic deformation and 
avoids the early stage of crack initiation, resulting in such excellent 
ductility in A600 MEA even at ultra-high strength level.
Thirdly, the nano-lamellar precipitates induced by continuous pre -
cipitation are more homogeneous rather than inducing coarse precipi -
tation at grain boundaries. The fully recrystallization and homogeneous 
microstructure contribute to the uniform deformation, resulting in sta -
ble mechanical properties.
Fourthly, the Co-free compositional design provide the additional 
advantages on our MEA than other high strength Co-containing MEAs. 
For example, the addition of Cr and Al in our MEAs provides the good 
oxidation resistance, making it potentially suitable for applications in 
harsh environments, such as aerospace and aviation.
