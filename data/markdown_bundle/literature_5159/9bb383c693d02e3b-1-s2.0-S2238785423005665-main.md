abstract
Novel lightweight Al 0.5NbTi3VxZr2 (x ¼ 0.5, 1.0, 1.5) refractory high entropy alloys were
synthesized by vacuum arc melting. The microstructure evolution and mechanical prop-
erties of Al 0.5NbTi3VxZr2 alloys were analyzed. All Al 0.5NbTi3VxZr2 alloys have a single BCC
structure, and the addition of V does not change the microstructure of the alloys. At
elevated temperatures, V1.0 alloy shows good phase stability, and the compressive yield
strength of V1.0 alloy is 898 MPa at room temperature, 706 MPa at 873 K, and 110 MPa at
1073 K, respectively. Al
0.5NbTi3VxZr2 alloys reveal excellent compression deformability, all
samples can be compressed to more than 50% strain at room and elevated temperatures
without fracture. At room temperature, V1.0 alloy still possesses the best tensile properties,
with the yield strength of 694 MPa. The enhancement strength is due to the addition of V
element with small atomic radius, which is caused by a variety of strengthening mecha-
nisms, including solid solution strengthening and grain reﬁnement strengthening. Most of
the reported Al eNbeTieVeZr system refractory high entropy alloys have poor deform-
ability at room temperature, and most of the work is focused on their compression prop-
erties. The Al
0.5NbTi3VxZr2 refractory high entropy alloys prepared in the present work not
only have excellent compression deformability, but also excellent tensile properties. Our
present study not only promotes the development of toughening Al eNbeTieVeZr system
refractory high entropy alloys to challenge engineering applications, but also provides
guidance for the design of lightweight high temperature materials with a variety of
strengthening mechanisms.

1. Introduction
High entropy alloys (HEAs), also known as multi-principal
element alloys (MPEAs), baseless alloys (BAs) and composite
concentrated alloys (CCAs), were deﬁned as consisting of 5 or
more principal elements, representing a new alloy design
concept [ 1e3]. The appearance of high entropy alloys breaks
the traditional design concept, meanwhile, possesses various
excellent properties due to its unique characteristics, which
has become a hot topic in some research ﬁelds of alloy design
and preparation at present, causing extensive attention and
exploration by researchers [ 4,5]. However, the research on
high entropy alloys has only been going on for about 20 years.
Compared with traditional alloys, the development of HEAs
ﬁeld is still in the initial stage. The broad research space and
the excellent properties and great application potential of this
kind of alloys urgently require the development of
researchers.
The development and application of structural materials
with excellent high temperature properties are still one of the
distinctly important factors to promote the development of
science and technology. Up to now, high entropy alloys con-
taining refractory elements have been recognized as alloys
with great potential for scientiﬁc and technological develop-
ment due to their high melting point and excellent high
temperature properties. Since VNbMoTaW and NbMoTaW
refractory high entropy alloys (RHEAs) were ﬁrst reported
publicly in 2010, refractory high entropy alloy prepared based
on high melting point elements, as a representative branch of
HEAs, became an alloy that is likely to replace high tempera-
ture materials, and also provides a new alloy design idea for
the development of new high temperature structural mate-
rials [ 6,7]. Although after nearly ten years of exploration and
development, the research of refractory high entropy alloys is
still in a golden period, with a very broad space for explora-
tion. Although the element composition is complex, the phase
composition is usually a single body-centered cubic structure,
such as NbMoTaW and HfNbTaTiZr refractory high entropy
alloys. At 1600
/C14 C, VNbMoTaW RHEAs still has a service
strength of more than 400 MPa, and this unprecedented high
temperature property far exceeds the widely used Ni-based
superalloys (such as Mar-M247 and Inconel 718) [ 8,9]. Even
so, due to the negative enthalpy of binary mixing between
different atoms and other reasons, when high mixing entropy
cannot completely overcome the contribution of enthalpy of
mixing to free energy, the second phase will inevitably be
generated in RHEAs [ 10], such as Si-containing refractory high
entropy alloys [ 11,12]. However, extremely high densities
(over 10 g/cm
3), severe room temperature brittleness and high
cost limit the processing and application of these high tem-
perature materials [ 13e15]. Considering the lightweight re-
quirements for material design in the aerospace and energy
industries, researchers have developed a series of RHEAs
materials with low density and excellent properties [ 14e21].
For example, AlCrNbTiV RHEA of 5.82 g/cm
3 possesses a high
yield strength of 1550 MPa at room temperature and still
possesses a yield of 860 MPa at 800
/C14 C[ 17]. Another example is
the Al 0.8Nb0.5Ti2V2Zr0.5 RHEAs (5.35 g/cm 3), which yield
strength is 1415 MPa at room temperature and 760 MPa at
800 /C14 C[ 21]. Although these RHEAs have solved the problem of
high density, they still suffer from poor plasticity at room
temperature, which seriously limits their application. Ac-
cording to related reports [ 21e24], lightweight Al eNbeTi
eVeZr system RHEAs in AleNbeTieVeZr system tend to form
a brittle intermetallic compound phase due to the negative
enthalpy of Al eZr binary mixing and the large size difference
between Zr (160 nm) and other atoms. Although the strength
and hardness are improved, its plasticity at room temperature
is still poor. How to improve the room temperature plasticity
of these refractory high entropy alloys is one of the great
challenges facing us at present.
Changing the chemical composition or adjusting the
microstructure to improve the mechanical properties of alloys
is one of the methods commonly used. Therefore, in order to
explore refractory high entropy alloys with low density and
good combination of strength and plasticity, in the present
work, a series of new lightweight refractory high entropy al-
loys with excellent room temperature plasticity, Al
0.5NbTi3-
VxZr2 (x ¼ 0.5, 1.0, 1.5), were designed by using the solid
solution phase formation criterion and prepared by vacuum
arc melting. On the one hand, the presence of Al and Ti can
effectively reduce the density of the alloy and improve the
strength. The use of Ti, V, Nb and Zr can ensure that the alloy
has a high enough melting temperature to ensure their high
temperature properties. On the other hand, by regulating the
content of V, the effect of V on the microstructure and me-
chanical properties of Al
0.5NbTi3VxZr2 RHEAs was investi-
gated, which provided guidance and suggestions for the
design of new super-strong and tough lightweight refractory
high entropy alloys, and then designed lightweight RHEAs
materials with excellent properties according to the theoret-
ical research. Promote the application of refractory high en-
tropy alloys in aerospace, naval equipment, wear resistance,
high temperature and other ﬁelds.
2. Experimental procedures
The refractory high entropy alloys with nominal composition
of Al 0.5NbTi3VxZr2 (x ¼ 0.5,1.0,1.5) alloys were prepared by
vacuum arc melting under an argon atmosphere on a water-
cooled copper hearth. The purity of the alloying elements
was no less than 99.99 wt.%. To compensate for the Al loss by
evaporation, which was about 10 wt.% Al was added to each
sample prior to arc melting. Al

3.1. Phase prediction
Due to the high mixing entropy (DSmix), high entropy alloys are
generally easy to form a single solid solution phase [ 10,25e27].
However, due to the enthalpy of mixing ( DHmix), high entropy
alloys may also have intermetallic compound phases [ 26,27].
When the enthalpy of mixing is much greater than the en-
tropy of mixing, the second phase is often formed in the alloy.
On the contrary, when the effect of mixing entropy is much
greater than mixing enthalpy, high entropy alloys tend to
form a single solid solution phase [ 26,27]. Since the enthalpy
of binary mixing between Al and Zr is so negative ( DH

with the increase of V content, from 1183 MPa to 1421 MPa and
then to 1238 MPa. When the compressive strain reaches 50%,
the strength of the alloy increases from 1762 MPa to 2079 MPa
and then decreases to 1966 MPa. Combined with Fig. 2, when
the content of V is 0.5, the grain size that can adapt to defor-
mation in the alloy is large and the number is small, and the
stress concentration is easy to occur, resulting in the low
strength of the alloy. Due to the solid solution of V atoms with
smaller atomic radius into the BCC matrix, the grain size of
the alloy is reduced and ﬁner, so more grains can resist
deformation to disperse the stress, thereby reducing the stress
concentration and improving strength and deformability
[36e38]. In addition, the solution of V atom into the BCC ma-
trix also causes serious lattice distortion, hinders the slip of
dislocations, and leads to the enhancement of the strength
[39,40]. Therefore, when the V content increases to 1.0, the
strength of the alloy increases due to the joint action of solid
solution strengthening and grain reﬁnement strengthening.
However, when the content of V increases to 1.5, the alloy no
longer meets the condition of forming stable solid solution
and is in an unstable state, which leads to the decrease of the
strength.
When the test temperature increases to 873 K, the strength
of Al
0.5NbTi3VxZr2 alloys decreases slightly, but still possess
relatively excellent yield strength, which are 544 MPa, 706 MPa
and 660 MPa respectively, which are about 68.8%, 78.6% and
75.6% of the yield strength at room temperature. The highest
yield strength is still V1.0 alloy. Similarly, all samples showed
excellent deformability without fracture even when the
compressive strain exceeded 50%. The compressive stress-
strain curves of Al
0.5NbTi3VxZr2 alloys at 873 K can be
divided into three stages. The ﬁrst stage is the increasing
period of stress. At the initial stage of deformation, the stress
increases with the increase of deformation and quickly rea-
ches the maximum, that is, the yield point. At this stage, the
dislocation has no time to expand due to the rapid increase of
stress, which leads to the increase of dislocation density,
mutual delivery and entanglement of dislocation, resulting in
the increase of stress, and the increase of deformation makes
certain deformation energy stored in the alloy [41]. The second
stage is the stress reduction. At this stage, dynamic softening
plays a dominant role. The reduced strength of the alloys in-
creases the internal storage energy, and the higher ambient
temperature provides energy for dislocation and vacancy
movement, so the stress of the alloys are reduced [ 24]. The

4. Conclusion
In the present work, Al 0.5NbTi3VxZr2 (x ¼ 0.5, 1.0, 1.5) re-
fractory high entropy alloys were prepared by vacuum arc
melting. The effects of V on microstructure, density and me-
chanical properties of the alloys were studied. The following
conclusions are drawn.
(1) Al
0.5NbTi3VxZr2 (x ¼ 0.5, 1.0, 1.5) refractory high entropy
alloys all show a single BCC structure. After electro-
chemical etching, all alloys present a typical dendrite
structure, in which the ID region is slightly enrich in Al
and Zr, and the DR region is slightly enrich in Nb, Ti and V.
(2) With the increase of V content, the density of Al
0.5-
NbTi3VxZr2 alloys increases slightly, ranging from 5.78
to 5.81 g/cm3. Hardness increased from 310.3 HV to 378.8
HV.
(3) The compressive strength of Al 0.5NbTi3VxZr2 alloy in-
creases ﬁrst and then decreases with the increase of V
content at different test temperatures, and reaches the
maximum when V content is 1.0. All alloys exhibit
excellent deformability at both room temperature and
high temperature, and the samples can be compressed
more than 50% strain without fracture. Work hardening
occurs after yield of the alloy, and the strength of V1.0
alloy at room temperature is 2079 MPa when the strain is
50%.
(4) The tensile test at room temperature shows that the
strength of Al
0.5NbTi3VxZr2 alloys increases ﬁrst and
then decreases with the increase of V content, while the
strain keeps decreasing. V1.0 alloy shows good strength
and ductility, the yield strength, tensile strength and
fracture strain are 694 MPa, 910 MPa and 17.2%,
respectively.
(5) The strengthening mechanism of Al
0.5NbTi3VxZr2 alloys
is mainly attributed to solution strengthening and grain
reﬁnement strengthening. The V element with a small
atomic radius is dissolved into the alloy, resulting in
serious lattice distortion of the matrix, hindering
dislocation movement and leading to strength increase.
The addition of V also reduces the grain size of the
matrix and increases the resistance to plastic defor-
mation, resulting in an increase in the strength and
deformability of the alloy.
