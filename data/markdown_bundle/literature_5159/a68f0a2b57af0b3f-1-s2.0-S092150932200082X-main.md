ABSTRACT 
A novel high-entropy alloys (HEAs)-joining strategy was proposed by brazing Al
0 ⋅ 3
CoCrFeNi substrates at 1573 K 
in vacuum using a pure niobium interlayer. The brazed Al
0 ⋅ 3
CoCrFeNi joints have a eutectic structure of face- 
centered-cubic (FCC) and C14 Laves phases, exhibiting excellent strength-ductility synergy at both room and 
liquid-nitrogen temperatures. 
1. Introduction 
High-entropy alloys (HEAs) with multiple principal elements at or 
near equiatomic ratios have received increasing attention due to their 
unprecedented functional and low-temperature mechanical properties 
[ 1 – 3 ]. The single-phase FCC-structured Al
0 ⋅ 3
CoCrFeNi HEA has excel -
lent radiation resistance [ 4 ], high-temperature stability [ 5 ], and 
outstanding mechanical properties [ 6 ], especially at extremely low 
temperatures [ 7 ]. The tensile strength and ductility of Al
0 ⋅ 3
CoCrFeNi 
alloy fibers at 77 K are up to 1.6 GPa and 7.5%, respectively [ 7 ]. Thus, 
Al
0 ⋅ 3
CoCrFeNi HEA is a promising material for many cryogenic engi -
neering applications such as heat exchangers, helium-cooled divertors, 
mars rovers, etc. [ 8 ]. 
The brazing technique exhibits unique merits in the fabrication of 
complex parts due to its simplicity, cost-effectiveness, and superior 
adaptability to sample configuration [ 9 – 11 ]. To date, conventional 
Ni-based filler alloys such as BNi-2 [ 12 – 14 ], MBF601 [ 14 , 15 ] are 
commonly utilized in brazing HEAs. However, many unfavorable bo -
rides and phosphides generate in the brazing seam and diffusion affected 
zone, which deteriorate the plasticity of joints and accelerate failure. 
The AgCu filler alloys are adopted to braze CrMnFeCoNi to 304 steel [ 16 , 
17 ]. The brazing seam exhibits an Ag – Cu eutectic structure, which 
inevitably degrades the mechanical properties of joints at cryogenic 
temperatures due to the strength-ductility trade-off of conventional al -
loys [ 18 , 19 ]. 
To obtain a reliable and low-temperature resistant Al
0 ⋅ 3
CoCrFeNi 
alloy joint, we propose the vacuum brazing of Al
0 ⋅ 3
CoCrFeNi HEA with a 
pure Nb interlayer by exploiting interfacial reactions between 
Al
0 ⋅ 3
CoCrFeNi and Nb. The utilization of Nb interlayer is because that 
adding some Nb to CoCrFeNi alloy can form Laves/FCC eutectic HEAs 
which exhibit superior mechanical properties in a wide temperature 
window [ 20 , 21 ]. The microstructure, shear behaviors at both room and 
cryogenic temperatures, as well as the fracture mechanism of joint are 
investigated.

slow furnace cooling process allows the spinodal decomposition of the

4. Conclusions 
In summary, the eutectic-reaction bonding of Al
0 ⋅ 3
CoCrFeNi HEAs is 
achieved with a pure Nb interlayer. The joint with a chrysanthemum- 
like Laves/FCC[Nb]ss eutectic structure presents outstanding shear 
properties at both 298 K and 77 K. The excellent strength and ductility 
synergy of the joint at 77 K is mainly ascribed to the formation of nano- 
spaced SFs in the L1
2 
nanoparticles-precipitated FCC phase. This is a 
simple, clean, interfacial controllable brazing technique and can be used 
in various HEAs-joinings by exploiting similar interfacial eutectic re -
actions [ 40 ]. The method is expected to fabricate complex HEAs parts as 
illustrated in Fig. S1 [ 29 ], and promote their cryogenic applications.
