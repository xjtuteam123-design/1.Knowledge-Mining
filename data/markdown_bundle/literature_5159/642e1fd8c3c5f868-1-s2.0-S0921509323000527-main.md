1. Introduction 
Conventional alloys consist of one or two main elements, which were 
fine-tuned by adding a few alloying elements, and this series of alloys 
have been the focus of many researchers in the past. The appearance of 
high entropy alloy (HEA), as a new class of metallic materials, breaks the 
traditional design concept of single principal element of the alloy and 
creates a new alloy system [ 1 , 2 ]. In recent years, the high entropy alloys 
developed in response to the development of science and technology, 
which can be roughly divided into two systems: the HEAs system 
dominated by the fourth cycle elements Fe, Co, Ni, Cr, etc. [ 3 – 6 ], and the 
HEAs system with refractory elements Ta, Nb, W, Hf, etc. [ 7 – 10 ]. At the 
same time, high entropy alloys containing refractory elements exhibit 
remarkable high-temperature properties due to their high melting point. 
As one of alloys with great potential for both scientific and technological 
development, RHEAs have attracted considerable attention to many 
researchers [ 11 – 14 ]. Currently, most of the publicly reported RHEAs 
have a single BCC solid solution phase or a structure dominated by BCC 
solid solution phase, such as NbMoTaW and VNbMoTaW alloys [ 10 ]. 
There are also many RHEAs with BCC phase as the matrix and the Laves
