ABSTRACT

influence of Ru on comprehensive properties of Nb – 16Si – 24Ti alloys is researched in our paper. The results show

1. Introduction 
At present, the advancement of aerospace technology has put for -
ward higher standards on aero-engine materials. As the superalloy 
currently in service, Nickel-based superalloy ’ s operating temperature 
has reached 85% of the melting point [ 1 – 3 ]. Hence, it ’ s important to 
explore a new ultra-high temperature structure material. Nb – Si alloys 
have the great potential for higher generation of superalloys because of 
its higher melting point and excellent mechanical properties at high 
temperature [ 4 – 6 ]. 
Nb – Si binary alloys are composed of ductile Nbss phases and hard- 
brittle silicide (Nb
3
Si, Nb
5
Si
3
) phases, which can improve the compre -
hensive properties by synergic action [ 7 , 8 ]. However, the low fracture 
toughness is the nonnegligible bottleneck of its practical application 
[ 9 – 12 ]. According to Nb – Si binary phase diagram [ 7 ], the eutectic point 
of Si is 18.2 at.%. It ’ s obvious that large proportion of silicide phases is 
harmful for the toughness of Nb – Si alloys [ 13 ]. Therefore, element Si is 
usually controlled in hypoeutectic region. Moreover, alloying is still the 
most common way to balance comprehensive performance of Nb – Si 
based alloys. Ti is an important element to improve the fracture 
toughness, which can reduce the Prierls-Nabarro energy barrier [ 5 , 14 , 
15 ]. Nevertheless, excess Ti ( > 25 at.%) can decrease the high temper -
ature stability due to its low melting point [ 16 ]. Other alloying elements 
such as Zr [ 17 ], Cr [ 11 , 18 ], Hf [ 19 ], V [ 20 ], Fe [ 14 , 21 ], and B [ 22 ] have 
also been investigated for microstructure and mechanical properties of 
Nb – Si superalloys. 
However, the most elements have unfavorable effect for high- 
temperature performance due to their lower melting point than 
element Nb in Nb – Si alloys. It needs to be perfected for the influence of 
high-melting-point elements on phase transition and comprehensive 
properties of Nb – Si based alloys. Recently, some research suggests that 
element Fe can observably improve the comprehensive properties of 
Nb – Si alloys [ 23 – 25 ]. However, element Fe (1811K) will reduce the 
melting point of Nb – Si alloys. It ’ s obvious that the same group element 
Ru (2606 K) has a higher melting point than Fe. Therefore, it makes 
great interest for us to explore the effect of element Ru for Nb – Si based 
alloys. Moreover, element Ru has made the remarkable effect on other 
alloys such as TiAl and Nickel-based alloys [ 26 – 30 ]. Nevertheless, 
although there are some studies of Nb – Ru and Ru – Si thermodynamic 
phase diagrams [ 31 , 32 ], it ’ s still blank for the influence of Ru on 
microstructure and comprehensive properties of Nb – Si superalloys. 
Therefore, based on the above views, the details of element Ru on the 
phase selection, microstructure and compressive performance at RT and 
HT have been investigated in Nb – 16Si – 24Ti alloys for the first time in 
our paper.
