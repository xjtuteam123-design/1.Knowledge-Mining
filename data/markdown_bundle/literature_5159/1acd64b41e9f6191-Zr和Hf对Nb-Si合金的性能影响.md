abstract
To improve the fracture toughness, and reduce density and cost of Nb eSi alloy, the in-
ﬂuence of the substitution of Zr for Hf in Nb e16Sie16TiexZreyHf ( x þ y ¼ 4) alloys is
investigated in this study, and the microstructural evolution and mechanical properties,
including fracture toughness and compressive properties are analyzed. The results show
that the substitution of Zr for Hf in Nb e16Sie16TiexZreyHf (x þ y ¼ 4) alloys can reﬁne the
microstructure, and promote the formation of (Nb, X)
5Si3/Nbss eutectic. The fracture
toughness KQ value increases with the increase of Zr content, since the high Zr content
alloys exhibits the low c/a ratio and ﬁne microstructure. Moreover, the addition of 1~4 at.%
Hf in Nb e16Sie16Tie4Zr alloy has less inﬂuence on the mechanical properties. The sub-
stitution of Zr for Hf in Nb e16Sie16TiexZreyHf (x þ y ¼ 4) alloys can improve the fracture
toughness of alloys, and the Zr element exhibits the low density and cost compared with Hf
element, which indicates that Zr is a promising alloying element.

1. Introduction
NbeSi based ultrahigh temperature alloys are considered as
the promising ultra-high temperature structural materials for
use in aerospace engineering and automotive applications,
due to their higher melting point, lower density and high
temperature capabilities compared to Ni based superalloys
[1e4]. The microstructures of Nb eSi alloys are consisted of
ductile Nb solid solution (Nb
SS) phase and hard Nb 5Si3/Nb3Si
silicide phase. Nb 5Si3 phase is usually embedded in the Nbss
matrix, or vice versa, depending on their preparation methods
and compositions [ 5e7]. However, the industrial applications
of Nb eSi based alloys are still restricted by their low fracture
toughness and poor ductility [ 8,9].
Alloying is generally considered to be an effective method
for improving the fracture toughness of Nb eSi based alloys
[10e12]. Recent results have shown that the fracture tough-
ness of Nb eSi based alloys can be signiﬁcantly improved by
addition of ⅣB element, including Ti [ 1e10], Zr [ 13,14] and Hf
[2,4,15,16]. Ti element has been demonstrated to improve the
oxidation resistant and fracture toughness of Nb eSi based
alloys, 10~30 at.% Ti are usually added to Nb eSi based alloys

[1e9]. Hf element has been proved to be an effective way to
improve the ductility and fracture toughness of Nb SS phase,
and 4 at.% Hf can effectively improve the fracture toughness
of NbeSi based alloys [ 2,4,15,16]. The addition of 4 at.% Zr can
improve the fracture toughness of Nb eSi based alloys [ 13,14].
However, Hf element with the high density and high cost (900
USD/kg) is detrimental to the industrial application of Nb eSi
based alloys. As a congener of Hf, Zr element is expected to
replace Hf element for improving the fracture toughness of
NbeSi alloy, because the Zr element exhibits low density and
cost (35 USD/kg) compared with Hf element. To the best
knowledge of the authors, there is no study on the common
effect of adding Zr and Hf elements on the mechanical prop-
erties and microstructure for Nb eSi based alloys, including
the compression performance and fracture toughness, which
arouses our interest.
The main purpose of this study is to systematically inves-
tigate the common effect of adding Zr and Hf elements on the
mechanical properties and microstructural evolution of
NbeSi based alloys with the novel designed compositions.
Moreover, the inﬂuence of the substitution of Zr for Hf in
Nbe16Sie16TiexZreyHf (x þ y ¼ 4) alloy is investigated.
2. Experimental procedures
Button ingots of Nb e16Sie16TiexZreyHf ( x þ y ¼ 4) and
Nbe16Sie16Tie4ZrexHf ( x ¼ 1, 2, 3, 4), each with a weight of
120 g, were prepared using arc melting inside a water-cooled
copper crucible, by on-consumable tungsten electrode,
under the argon atmosphere. All alloys were melted ﬁve times
to homogenize the compositions of ingots. X-ray diffraction
(XRD) was performed to determine the phases constitution of
the investigated alloys, using the Cu K a radiation, under 40 kV
and scanning speed with 8
/C14 /min. The scanning electron mi-
croscope (SEM) in backscattered electron mode (BSE) was
performed to observed the microstructure, and the chemical
composition of investigated alloys was measured using the
energy disperse spectroscopy based on SEM.
Electronic Universal experimental machine was performed
to test the room-temperature compression properties with
the size of F 4m m /C2 6 mm. An electronic universal me-
chanical testing machine (Instron5569) was performed to test
the fracture toughness with the loading rate of 3 mm/s, and the
2.0 mm deep slit notches in specimen for fracture toughness
were cut by the electrode discharge machine. The computa-
tional method details have been previously described in Refs.
[16e18].
3. Results
3.1. Effect of Zr and Hf additions on phase constitutions
The addition of 4 at.% Hf or 4 at.% Zr can effectively improve
the fracture toughness of Nb eSi based alloys, therefore,
Nbe16Sie16TiexZreyHf (x þ y ¼ 4) alloys were investigated in
this paper. Fig. 1 shows the XRD proﬁles obtained from
Nbe16Sie16TiexZreyHf ( x þ y ¼ 4) alloys, and the indepen-
dent characteristic peaks of three phases of Nbss, (Nb, X) 3Si
and (Nb, X) 5Si3 phases can be detected in the investigated al-
loys. In Nb e16Sie16Tie4Hf alloy, all reﬂections can be
matched with Nbss and (Nb, X) 3Si phases. The independent
characteristic peaks and theta degree remain unchanged in
Nbe16Sie16Tie1Zre3Hf alloy compared with
Nbe16Sie16Tie4Hf alloy. The b-(Nb, X)
3Si5 phase could be
detected in Nb e16Sie16Tie2Zre2Hf alloy, and the indepen-
dent characteristic peaks of b-(Nb, X)5Si3 and (Nb, X)3Si phases
remained. The theta degree and independent characteristic
peaks remain unchanged, but there is a noticeable change in
the diffraction peak intensity of Nb e16Sie16Tie3Zre1Hf and
Nbe16Sie16Tie4Zr alloys, compared with
Nbe16Sie16Tie2Zre2Hf alloy.

3.3. Effect of Zr and Hf additions on mechanical
properties
Fig. 3 shows the average room-temperature fracture tough-
ness KQ value of Nb e16Sie16Ti-xZr-yHf (x þ y ¼ 4) alloys. The
average room-temperature fracture toughness KQ value of
Nbe16Sie16Tie4Hf alloy is 7.07 MPa m 1/2, and the average
room-temperature fracture toughness KQ value of
Nbe16Sie16Tie1Zre3Hf alloy is 7.10 MPa m 1/2, which is
similar to Nb e16Sie16Tie4Hf alloy. The average room-
temperature fracture toughness KQ value of
Nbe16Sie16Tie2Zre2Hf and Nb e16Sie16Tie3Zre1Hf alloys
increase to 8.00 MPa m 1/2 and 8.17 MPa m 1/2, respectively.
Nbe16Sie16Tie4Zr alloy exhibits the maximum average
room-temperature fracture toughness KQ value of
9.01 MPa m 1/2.
There are similar properties for Nb e16Sie16Tie4Hf and
Nbe16Sie16Tie1Zre3Hf alloys, since Nb e16Sie16Tie4Hf and
Nbe16Sie16Tie1Zre3Hf alloys have the similar constituent
phases and microstructure. The average room-temperature
fracture toughness K
Q value of Nb e16Sie16Tie2Zre2Hf and
Nbe16Sie16Tie3Zre1Hf alloys increase to 8.00 MPa m 1/2 and
8.17 MPa m 1/2, respectively, since the (Nb, X) 5Si3/Nbss eutec-
tics appear in the two alloys. Previous studies have reported
that the reﬁned microstructure can improve the fracture
toughness of Nb eSi alloys, and the reﬁned eutectics could
hinder the crack propagation compared to the blocky (Nb,
X)
3Si and Nbss phases [ 19,20]. The edge lengths of the unit cell
are a, b, c. In the hexagonal crystal system, the c axis repre-
sents the long axis, and a represents the short axis. Table 3
shows the c/a ratio of Nbss, which is reckoned based on the
XRD patterns, and the c/a ratio decreases with the increase of
Zr content. The decrease of c/a ratio could improve plastic
deformation capacity and fracture toughness of Nbss phase,
which contributes to the fracture toughness of Nb eSi based
alloy. The Nb e16Sie16Tie4Zr alloy exhibits the maximum
average room-temperature fracture toughness K
Q value of
9.01 MPa m 1/2, and the fracture toughness is improved by
27.4%, since the Nbe16Sie16Tie4Zr alloy exhibits low c/a ratio
of Nbss and high content of (Nb, X) 5Si3/Nbss eutectic.
Fig. 4 shows the average compressive properties of
Nbe16Sie16Ti-xZr-yHf ( x þ y ¼ 4) alloys. The
Nbe16Sie16Tie4Hf alloy exhibits the minimum compressive
strength of 1978 MPa, and the Nb e16Sie16Tie1Zre3Hf alloy
exhibits the maximum compressive strength of 2163 MPa. The
difference between the maximum compressive strength and
the minimum compressive strength is 185 MPa (9.35%), which
indicates the compression strength of different alloys varies
less. The change of Zr and Hf elements in Nb e16Sie16Ti-xZr-
yHf ( x þ y ¼ 4) have little inﬂuence on the compressive
strength.

loys is related to the decrease of P eN barrier energy like the

4. Conclusion
1. 2 at.% or more Zr in Nb e16Sie16Ti-xZr-yHf (x þ y ¼ 4) alloy
promote the formation of (Nb, X)5Si3/Nbss eutectic, and the
substitution of Zr for Hf in Nb e16Sie16TiexZreyHf
(x þ y ¼ 4) alloys can reﬁne the microstructure.
2. The fracture toughness KQ value increases with the in-
crease of Zr content in Nb e16Sie16TiexZreyHf ( x þ y ¼ 4)
alloy, since high Zr content alloys exhibits low c/a ratio and
ﬁne microstructure.
3. The addition of 1~4 at.% Hf in Nb e16Sie16Tie4Zr alloy has
less inﬂuence on the fracture toughness and compressive
strength.
4. The substitution of Zr for Hf in Nb e16Sie16TiexZreyHf
(x þ y ¼ 4) alloys could improve the fracture toughness of
alloys, and Zr element exhibits the low density and cost
compared with Hf element, which indicates that Zr is a
promising alloying element in Nb eSi based alloys.
