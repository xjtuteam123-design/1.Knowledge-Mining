3.1. Microstructures and mechanical properties of alloys 
Both as-cast Cr10 and Al10 RCCAs exhibit dendritic microstructures

3.4.1. Surface morphology 
Figure 6 shows the surface morphology of the oxidized samples. It 
can be seen that the oxide scale of Cr10 ( Fig. 6 (a)-(b)) is relatively dense

and 4 .
