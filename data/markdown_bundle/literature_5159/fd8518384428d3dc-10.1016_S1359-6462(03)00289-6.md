Abstract
Multiphase Mo–Si–Balloys are potential candidates for ultra-high temperature application. The T2/Moss and
(Mo,Nb)ss/T1/T2 eutectic alloys have been grown using an optical ﬂoating zone method. In this paper, their micro-
structure, fracture toughness at room temperature and strength at high temperatures are investigated.
