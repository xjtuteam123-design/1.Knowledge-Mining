ABSTRACT 
To determine the effect of mixing entropy on multicomponent alloys and to find the mixing entropy threshold for 
entropy-tailored materials, including high-entropy alloys and medium-entropy alloys, the microstructure and 
mechanical properties of 23 Ti – Zr – Nb – V alloys were investigated. The Laves phase content of Ti – Zr – Nb – V alloys 
was obtained using image recognition technology. According to the component threshold for Laves phases and 
HCP phase disappearance, V(Nb) – Zr(Ti) pseudo-binary phase diagrams, which can predict the phases of ternary 
Ti – Zr – V and Zr – Nb – V alloys, were established. By comparing the Laves phase content in Ti – Zr – Nb – V alloys 
obtained from experimental statistics, pseudo-binary phase diagrams and thermodynamic calculations, the Δ S
mix 
threshold for entropy-tailored materials was 1.10 – 1.21R. The dependence of the yield strength and fracture 
strain of the Ti – Zr – Nb – V alloys further reveals that the mechanical properties of entropy-tailored alloys are 
essentially controlled by Δ S
mix
. 
1. Introduction 
In the past decade, high-entropy alloys (HEAs) have attracted the 
attention of many researchers due to their unique structure, perfor -
mance and material design concept [ 1 – 4 ]. According to the definition 
proposed by Yeh, HEAs are comprised of five or more constituent ele -
ments whose concentration is 5 – 35 at% and the ideal mixing entropy 
Δ S
mix 
is generally higher than 1.6 R ( R is the ideal gas constant) [ 5 ]. The 
high configuration entropy makes HEAs more inclined to form a single 
solid solution (SSS) [ 5 ]. 
In recent years, the concept of high entropy has been constantly 
enriched. On the one hand, new high-entropy materials, such as high- 
entropy ceramics [ 6 , 7 ] and high-entropy composites [ 8 , 9 ], have been 
proposed. On the other hand, the number of components in HEAs is 
decreasing. Medium entropy alloys (MEAs), such as CrCoNi with a single 
FCC structure [ 10 – 12 ] and Ti – Zr – Ta with a single BCC structure [ 13 , 
14 ], have become a new focus. That is, the effect of entropy in multi -
component materials is receiving increasing attention. 
In 2019, Lu concluded that the complexity of alloys could be 
controlled by adjusting Δ S
mix
, named entropy engineering [ 4 ]. The 
materials obtained by entropy engineering can be called 
entropy-tailored materials. Both HEAs and MEAs can be considered as 
entropy-tailored materials. However, some issues are controversial. For 
example, what is the threshold of Δ S
mix 
for entropy-tailored materials? Is 
the classic multicomponent material, such as 304 stainless steels, also an 
entropy-tailored material? 
To solve these issues, Ti – Zr – Nb – V alloys, which are produced by 
adding Ti and Nb into VZr alloys, have been proposed. V-Zr is a typical 
system forming intermetallic phase. Addition of Ti and Nb with a good 
affinity with V and Zr, accords with the classical criterions single BCC 
solid solution of high entropy alloy. The effect of mixing entropy on the 
formation of solid solution structure, that is, the inhibition of interme -
tallic phase formation can be investigated by Ti – Zr – Nb – V alloys. Actu -
ally, to reduce the use of high-cost components, Mitrica et al. used the 
similar substitution methods and evaluated the application potential of 
HEAs in extreme environments [ 15 ]. The Δ S
mix 
of the Ti – Zr – Nb – V sys -
tem is controlled by adjusting the amounts of Ti and Nb. The effect of 
Δ S
mix 
on the structure and mechanical properties of Ti – Zr – Nb – V was 
investigated based on experiments, thermodynamic calculations and 
image recognition technology. Similar methods are used by 
Katz-Demyanetzin et al. in the characterization of Laves phase contents 
of Al
0.5
CrMoNbTa
0.5 
HEAs [ 16 ]. Then, the threshold for 
entropy-tailored materials was obtained. This work provides a reference 
for material design through entropy engineering.

4. Conclusion 
In this work, the contents of different phases of Ti – Zr – Nb – V alloys 
were obtained from XRD, SEM and TEM and image recognition. Using 
the threshold of the additional 3rd element content for the Laves phases 
and HCP phase disappearance, V(Nb) – Zr(Ti) pseudo-binary phase dia -
grams were established. The V(Nb) – Zr(Ti) pseudo-binary phase diagram 
can effectively predict the phases of the ternary alloys, which are mainly 
determined by Δ H . However, it cannot accurately display the phases of 
Ti – Zr – Nb – V alloys with high Δ S
mix
. By comparing the content of the 
Laves phase in Ti – Zr – Nb – V alloys obtained from experimental statistics, 
pseudo-binary phase diagrams and thermodynamic calculations based 
on the HEA database, the Δ S
mix 
threshold for entropy to affect the phases 
of multicomponent alloys was found to be 1.10 – 1.21R. When 
Δ S
mix
≥ 1.10 – 1.21R, the multicomponent alloy is an entropy-tailored
