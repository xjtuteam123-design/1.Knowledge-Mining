abstract
MgeGd system alloys have been widely studied because they are applied in automotive,
weaponry and aerospace applications and have a wide range of application scenarios due
to the excellent strength and heat resistance properties. This work aims to systematically
investigate the microstructure and high temperature mechanical properties of Mg-10Gd-
3Y-xNd-0.5Zr alloy, and found that the microstructure of Mg-10Gd-3Y-0.5Zr alloy was
mainly composed of a-Mg matrix, Mg
5(Gd, Y) phase and Mg 24(Y, Gd) 5 phase. In which Nd
element plays a role in the alloy to promote the precipitation phase precipitation and form
the new phase Mg
41Nd5, and the appropriate amount of Nd can reﬁne the grain size.
Moreover, Mg-10Gd-3Y- xNd-0.5Zr alloy has excellent mechanical properties in room and
high temperature, with ultimate tensile strengths above 280 MPa at 200 /C14 Ce300 /C14 C, 309 MPa
at 250 /C14 C, and especially at 350 /C14 C, the tensile strength is still up to 195 MPa. The main
strengthening phases are b" phase, b0 phase and 18-LPSO (Long-period stacking ordered)
phase. The Mg-10Gd-3Y-2Nd-0.5Zr alloy has excellent high temperature creep resistance at
(200
/C14 Ce300 /C14 C)/(50e90 MPa). When creeping at 200 /C14 C, b0 phase is completely transformed
into b" phase. After creeping at 250 /C14 C, b0 phase is transformed into b phase. After creeping
at 300 /C14 C, b phase grows signiﬁcantly. Meanwhile, when the creep temperature and stress
increase, the creep mechanism gradually changes from diffusion and grain boundary
sliding mechanism to dislocation slip and dislocation climbing mechanism.

1. Introduction
Mg-RE alloys have a broad application prospect in aerospace,
weaponry, automotive, electronics and other ﬁelds due to
their excellent strength and heat resistance properties.
Therefore, based on the demand of economic, social and
environmental beneﬁts, the development of Mg-RE heat-
resistant magnesium alloys with excellent performance have
become a top priority [ 1e4].
Gd in MgeGd binary alloy can effectively enhance the aging
hardening ability, and adding Y in Mg eGd alloy forms Mg-Gd-
Y alloy, which has excellent room and high temperature me-
chanical properties. For example, Rokhlin and Nikitina [ 5]
studied and developed the Mg-10Gd-3Y alloy, which has su-
perior mechanical properties and resistance to high-
temperature creep than the commercial WE54 alloy. The
addition of Zr element in the Mg-Gd-Y alloy can play a role in
reﬁning the microstructure and improving the high temper-
ature properties and creep resistance. According to the liter-
ature report [6e10], some Mg-Gd-Y-Zr alloys, such as Mg-9Gd-
4Y-0.5Zr alloy and Mg-10Gd-3Y-0.4Zr alloy, have mechanical
properties and creep resistance comparable to the commonly
used A356 aluminum alloy and the heat resistance tempera-
ture can reach 300
/C14 C, which is signiﬁcantly better than that of
WE54A alloy.
He et al. [ 7] developed a new Mg-Gd-Y series rare-earth
magnesium alloy with high-strength and heat-resistant
properties at a room-temperature tensile strength of
370 MPa, yield strength of 240 MPa, elongation of 4% in the T6
state, and tensile strength of up to 300 MPa at 250
/C14 C, which
has been applied in aerospace and other ﬁelds and promoted
the development of the magnesium and rare-earth industries.
In addition, the researchers added Nd element that can
strongly age hardening to promote the high temperature
performance and weaken the cost. As Liu [ 11] and Peng [ 12]
found that Mg-10Gd-3Y-4Nd-0.6Zr alloy and Mg-8Gd-3Nd-
0.6Zr alloy had the characteristics of high strength and heat
resistance. Zhang et al. [ 13] explored the effect of Nd on the
precipitated phase and mechanical properties of Mg-8Gd-
5Ye2Zn-0.5Zr alloy and found that Nd was able to inhibit the
formation of LPSO phase and hinder the transformation of
M
g5(Gd, Y, Zn) to LPSO phase. In addition, the best mechanical
properties were obtained when the addition amounts of Nd
was 0.5%. Luo et al. [14] replaced Gd in the Mg-Gd-Zr alloy with
Nd and found that Nd made the alloy form the Mg
12Nd phase
and greatly enhanced the alloy in terms of mechanical prop-
erties such as tensile strength and yield strength. High-
density b" phase and b
0 phase were the main strengthening
phases. Lei [ 15] added 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5 wt% Nd to Mg-4Gd-0.5Zr
alloy, and found that the extruded Mg-Gd-Nd-Zr alloy
showed a complete dynamic recrystallization phenomenon.
The addition of Nd weakened the texture strength, reﬁned the
grains, improved the yield strength, and formed a large
number of ﬁnely dispersed Mg
41Nd5 and Mg12(Gd, Nd) phases.
To further optimize the mechanical properties of Mg-Gd-Zn-
Zr alloy, Hong et al. [ 16] added 0.5, 1 wt% Nd to the alloy,
which played an important role in the microstructure reﬁne-
ment and homogenization, improving the tensile properties
and microhardness, but reducing the compressive strength,
and weakening the alloy tensile-compression yield asymme-
try. Gui et al. [ 17] studied the precipitation characteristics and
mechanical properties of Mg-2.9Gd-1.5Nd-0.3Zn-0.3Zr alloy by
equal neck angular extrusion, and realized that b
1 phase could
precipitate rapidly. The Mg-2.9Gd-1.5Nd-0.3Zn-0.3Zr alloys
have good comprehensive properties with ultimate tensile
strength, yield strength and elongation values of 210.9 MPa,
263.9 MPa and 27.9%, respectively, due to grain reﬁnement,
dislocation, precipitation of spherical b
1 phase, and texture
evolution. Zheng [ 18] explored the inﬂuence of Nd on the
microstructure and mechanical properties of hot-extruded
Mg-9Gd-0.5Zr alloy, and found that after adding Nd, the non-
dynamic recrystallized grains disappeared, and the grain
size was 1.68 mm uniformly distributed dynamic recrystallized
grains. A large number of nano-scale Mg
5(Gd, Nd) particles
were precipitated. The Mg5(Gd, Nd) phase provided nucleation
for dynamic recrystallization, promoted dynamic precipita-
tion, and strengthened the alloy through the Orowan mech-
anism. The yield strength and tensile strength of extruded Mg-
9Gd-2Nd-0.5Zr alloy can reach 321 MPa and 372 MPa.
The above studies show that the rare earth element Nd can
effectively improve the microstructure and enhance the me-
chanical properties. Therefore, this paper gives full play to the
good effect of rare earth elements on the microstructure and
properties, which is based on the design concept of multi-
alloying heat-resistant magnesium alloy. Combining the in-
ﬂuence of light rare earth, heavy rare earth and other alloying
elements, the composition design and preparation, micro-
structure analysis and tensile test of the Mg-Gd-Y-Nd-Zr alloy
were carried out, and the high temperature creep resistance
mechanism of the alloy was further studied.
2. Materials and experimental procedure
The raw materials selected for the preparation of Mg-10Gd-
3Y-xNd-0.5Zr (x ¼ 0, 1, 2, 3, 4 wt%) alloys are magnesium ingot
with 99.9% purity, Mg-25Nd, Mg-30(Gd, Y, Zr) master alloys.
The alloy smelting was conducted in an electromagnetic in-
duction furnace at the smelting temperature of 750
/C14 C with the
soaking time of 15 min, and the casting temperature of 730 /C14 C.
The casting was carried out in a metal-type mold, and a gas
mixture of 1 vol% SF 6þ99 vol% CO 2 was used for protection
during melting and casting. The solid solution treatment was
applied in a box-type resistance furnace, the solution tem-
perature was 535
/C14 C for 6 h, and the alloy was quenched with
hot water after being taken out of the furnace. Finally, a blast
drying oven was used for aging treatment with an aging
temperature of 225
/C14 C for 14 h, and the alloy was air-cooled
after discharge from the furnace.
The phase composition was analyzed by D8 Advance X-ray
diffractometer and analysis software Jade 5.0. Axio Vert A1
optical microscope and JSM-5610LV scanning electron micro-
scope with EDS were used to observe and analyze the micro-
structure and microscope composition. The metallographic
corrosion solution was 4 vol% nitric acid alcohol solution, and
the etching time was 5e60s. The morphology and composition
of the precipitates were observed by JEM-2100 high resolution
transmission electron microscope. The DJ2000 refrigeration
type electrolytic double jet thinner was used for electrolytic

Fig. 2 is the X-ray diffraction pattern of the Mg-10Gd-3Y- xNd-

3.4. Microstructure after creep
Fig. 10 shows the SEM morphology of Mg-10Gd-3Y-2Nd-0.5Zr
alloy after creep at 70 MPa/(200 /C14 C, 250 /C14 C, 300 /C14 C) for 100 h.
From Fig. 10 (a, c, e), we can see that with the creep temper-
ature increases, the grain size after creep gradually becomes
smaller and signiﬁcantly reﬁned. This is because the recovery
recrystallization is more likely to occur with increasing tem-
perature during creep, forming deformed subgrains. There are
grain boundary steps near the triple grain boundary ( Fig. 10(a,
c)). The formation of the grain boundary step is generally
considered to be the step formed by the dislocation cutting
through the small angle grain boundary, or the slip band
formed by the sliding dislocation group crossing the precipi-
tated phase and the slip step formed by the grain boundary.
The existence of the grain boundary step will hinder the
dislocation movement, but the existence of plentiful grain
boundary steps will make the alloy deform and soften.
Fig. 10 (b, d, f) shows the SEM morphology of the alloy after
creep at 70MPa/(200
/C14 C, 250 /C14 C, 300 /C14 C). The large angle of the
triple grain boundary is selected to reﬂect the morphology of
the precipitated phase on the grains with different orienta-
tions. It can be seen that there is no obvious change in a-Mg
matrix at 200
/C14 C. At 250 /C14 C, the precipitated phases formed in
the matrix have a regular distribution, 120 /C14 distribution be-
tween each other, and the size is about 2 e4 mm. These
mutually angled precipitated phases will form an interlocking
structure, causing dislocations to plug and entangle at their
intersections, thus preventing dislocation slip and improving
the creep resistance. After creep at 300
/C14 C, the regularity of the
precipitates distribution in the matrix is weakened, and the
hindrance to dislocations is correspondingly weak, and the
creep will be accelerated.

4. Discussion
4.1. Strengthening mechanism
Mg-10Gd-3Y-xNd-0.5Zr alloy has excellent mechanical prop-
erties at room and high temperature. The strengthening
mechanism can be explained from the following three
aspects.
1) Fine grain strengthening. The grain sizes of Mg-10Gd-3Y-
xNd-0.5Zr alloys are 84 ± 3 mm, 48 ± 2 mm, 43 ± 2 mm, 50 ± 3 mm
and 58 ± 3 mm, respectively. The smaller the grain size, the
more the number of grain boundaries. In the process of alloy
deformation, the existence of a large number of grain
boundaries is an effective resistance to dislocation move-
ment. The existence of quantitative grain boundaries makes it
difﬁcult to form cracks, and it is not easy to expand after the
formation of cracks. Even if the crack grows, it will change the
