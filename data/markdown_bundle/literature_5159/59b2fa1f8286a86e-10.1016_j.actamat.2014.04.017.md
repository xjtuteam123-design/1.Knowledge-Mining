Retraction notice to “Atomic scale interfacial and
compositional characteristics of the r and c phases of Ni-based
single crystal superalloys ” [Acta Materialia 60 (2012) 6631–6640]
F. Sun a, J.X. Zhang a,⇑, P. Liu b, Q. Feng c,d, S.C. Mao b, X.D. Han b

This article has been retracted: please see Elsevier Policy on Article Withdrawal ( http://www.elsevier.com/locate/
withdrawalpolicy).
This article has been retracted at the request of the authors and the Coordinating Editor.
The calculations at the core of the article were based on an assumption that the crystal structure of the r phase was
incorrectly approximated. Further research and intensive discussion with colleagues has helped the authors to realize
the assumptions made with their calculations cannot stand. This precludes from determining the site occupancy of each
element and the segregation in the r phase cannot be correctly approximated as a multicomponent phase by
W8Cr10NiCo11 in the calculations.
The authors have expressed deep regret and have asked for retraction of their article.
