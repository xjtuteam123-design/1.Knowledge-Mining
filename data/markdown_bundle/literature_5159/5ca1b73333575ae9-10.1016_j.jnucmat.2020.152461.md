Case Summary. /C15Stent loss during PCI occurs infrequently in the
current era (incidence 0.32%) and is more likely to occur in tortuous
and calci ﬁed coronary arteries.
/C15Lost stents can be successfully re trieved in the majority of cases
using a variety of retrieval techniques like low pressure balloon
inﬂation technique, small balloon technique, double wire technique
and loop snare technique.
/C15Stent loss is associated with an increased risk of complications,
such as signi ﬁcant bleeding and need for emergency coronary artery
bypass graft surgery.

Case Summary. This case represents the possibility that hinge motion
could make coronary artery dissection at the edge of stent. We found the
dissection thirty minutes later despite IVUS couldn’t show any injury in the
ﬁrst PCI. It may be a risk factor to place stent edge in tortuous vessels with
hinge motion. Additionally, a biolimus-eluting stent (NOBORI, TERUMO)
has thicker strut (125
mm) than other second-generation drug-eluting
stents, which may be also the risk of the phenomenon.
