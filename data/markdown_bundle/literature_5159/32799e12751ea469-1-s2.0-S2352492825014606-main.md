1. Introduction
Dual-phase titanium alloys, as exemplified by Ti-6Al-4V, are the 
most widely used Ti alloy grades for their good processibilities in 
combination with high strength and toughness [1,2] . However, their 
service temperature is insufficient, below 350 
◦

improve the high-temperature performance, complex alloying has been 
attempted, which led to Russian BT9 (Ti-6.5Al-1.5Zr-3.5Mo-0.3Si), US 
Ti-62222s (Ti-6Al-2Sn-2Zr-2Cr-2Mo-0.15Si), and Chinese TC21 
(Ti-6Al-3Mo-2Sn-2Zr-2Nb-1.5Cr-0.1Si) [4 – 6] , with the last one pos -

titanium alloys (ultimate tensile strength R
