CoCuFeMnNi bulk nanocrystalline alloy was prepared by mechanical alloying
(MA) and spark plasma sintering (SPS). The effects of sintering parameters,
such as temperature, heating rate, sintering pressure, pressing time, and
cooling mode on the compressive strength of bulk sintered alloys were ana-
lyzed, and the effects of sintering temperature and pressing time on the
magnetic properties of the alloys were investigated. The compressive strength
test results show that the compressive strength of the alloy ﬁrst increases and
then decreases with the increase of heating rate and pressing time. A model
was established to study the mechanisms of mechanical properties of the
CoCuFeMnNi alloy. The strengthening mechanisms such as grain reﬁnement
strengthening, solid solution strengthening, and cluster strengthening of
CoCuFeMnNi alloy was quantitatively analyzed. The magnetic test results
show that the prepared CoCuFeMnNi amorphous alloy powder and its bulk
alloy are typical soft magnetic materials. The saturation magnetic induction
strength of the bulk alloy increases with the increase of sintering temperature
and pressing time. However, the coercivity ﬁrst decreases and then increases.

necessary to develop new soft magnetic materials.
Some high-entropy alloys have excellent soft mag-
netic properties, which have been widely studied,

MA is a simple method of preparing high-entropy
alloys with good soft magnetic properties. 21–23 Zhao
et al. 24 successfully synthesized CoCr xCuFeMnNi
high-entropy alloy powders with supersaturated
solid solution structure by MA technology, and
studied its microstructure and magnetic properties.
It was found that CoCuFeMnNi powders had the

27,28 Combining MA technology with SPS sin-
tering technology to prepare bulk nanocrystalline
high-entropy alloy soft magnetic materials can not
only break through the limit of ‘‘critical cooling rate’’
on the grain size, but also realize the rapid proto-
typing of complex shaped parts. It is a promising
preparation method for bulk nanocrystalline alloy,
which has important research signiﬁcance and the
prospect of wide application. To our knowledge,
there has not been a report on the preparation of
CoCuFeMnNi by the combination of MA technology
and SPS technology. In this paper, CoCuFeMnNi
nanocrystalline soft magnetic material powder was
prepared by MA technology in a planetary high-
energy ball mill, and then CoCuFeMnNi bulk
sintered samples were prepared by SPS sintering
technology. The effect of the SPS sintering process
on its compressive strength and magnetic properties
was studied.
EXPERIMENTAL METHODS
Fe powder with a purity of 99.5% and Co, Cu, Mn
and Ni powders with purity of 99.9% (300 mesh)
were prepared with CoCuFeMnNi according to
atomic percentage (at%). MA experiments were
carried out using a planetary high-energy ball mill
under the protection of 99.99% high-purity argon.
The rotational speed of the ball mill was 300 r/min,
the ratio of ball to material was 30:1, and the ball
mill was shut down for 0.5 h for each ball mill. The
structure of the ball-milled powder was analyzed
using a D/max-2500 X-ray diffractometer (Cu K a)
from the Nippon Institute of Physics. The particle
morphology was observed by scanning electron
microscope (SEM); the thermodynamic characteris-
tics were tested by differential thermal analysis
(DTA) on a Netzsch STA449C scanning calorimeter
(Netzsch, Germany). When the scanning range was
20–90/C176 , the step size was 0.02 /C176 /s. The magnetic
properties of the powders were tested using a
vibrating sample magnetometer (VSM).
The ball-milled CoCuFeMnNi amorphous
nanocrystalline alloy soft magnetic material powder
was weighed and put into a cemented carbide mold
with a diameter of 20 mm for preloading; then the
compacts were put into the SPS furnace, and SPS
experiments were carried out under different
sintering temperatures, heating rates, sintering
pressures, and cooling modes. The compressive
properties of the bulk alloy were tested with a
Gleeble 3500 thermal simulator. The size of the
compressed specimen was U10 mm 9 15 mm and
the strain rate of the compression test was 1 s
/C0 1.
The fracture morphology was observed by SEM. The
magnetic properties of the bulk alloy were tested by
VSM. The sample used for EBSD (electron backscat-
ter diffraction) measurement was electropolished
with 80-20 perchloric acid and ethanol. The elec-
tropolishing current was 16 V for 20 s. EBSD
detection was carried out with a 0.3- lm step size
on a 300 9 300-lm area.

similar to that of many steels containing solute
carbon, and the mechanism may be the interaction

With the increase of sintering temperature, the
coercivity of the bulk CoCuFeMnNi sample is
obviously lower than that in the powder state;
when the sintering temperature is lower, the
coercivity of the bulk alloy sample decreases with
the increase of sintering temperature; but when
the sintering temperature rises to 905 /C176 C, the
coercivity begins to ﬂuctuate with the increase of
sintering temperature. The main reason is that
when the sintering temperature is low, the inter-
nal stress of the powder particles gradually
decreases, and the coercivity also decreases. How-
ever, when the sintering is carried out in the high-
temperature region near the initial crystallization
temperature, due to the precipitation of crystalline
phases in the sintering body, the grain boundaries
of these crystalline phases have an obstacle effect
on the movement of the domain wall, so that the
coercivity increases. Therefore, the crystallization
phase precipitated in the amorphous matrix can
just enhance the ferromagnetic exchange between
grains, thus increasing the saturation magnetic
induction. Therefore, the saturation magnetic
induction of bulk CoCuFeMnNi sintered samples
will increase with the increase of sintering
temperature.
When sintering at 925 /C176 C, with the extension of
pressing time, the saturation magnetic induction
strength of the CoCuFeMnNi bulk sintered sample
will gradually increase, while the coercivity will
decrease with the extension of pressing time at the
initial stage of pressing, but then increase with the
extension of pressing time. The main reason is that,
in the initial stage of pressing, with the extension of
pressing time, the internal stress of the bulk
sintered sample is released, which leads to the
decrease of coercivity; but with the extension of
pressing time, the space for further release of
internal stress is very small, but there will be
crystalline phase gradually precipitated in the
sintered sample, and the grain boundary of the
crystalline phase will hinder the movement of
magnetic domain, which will lead to the decrease
of coercivity and the force increases gradually. The
magnetism of CoCuFeMnNi bulk sintered sample is
slightly lower than that of widely used high-silicon
steel, and has the potential to be used as a soft
magnetic material.
CONCLUSION
(1) CoCuFeMnNi amorphous alloy powder was
prepared by MA at a ratio of ball to material of
30:1, a rotation speed of 300 r/min, and MA
time of 80 h. When the heating rate was 30/C176 C/
min, the initial crystallization temperature
was about 925 /C176 C and the crystallization peak
temperature was about 998 /C176 C;
(2) The results show that an increase of sintering
temperature, sintering pressure, and cooling
rate are beneﬁcial for improving the compres-
sive strength of the sintered block; an increase
of heating rate and pressing time make the
compressive strength of the sintered block
increase ﬁrst and then decrease. When the
sintering temperature is 925 /C176 C, the sintering
pressure is 500 MPa, the heating rate is 30 /C176 C/
min, and the pressing time is 5 min, the
results show that the compressive strength of
the sintered bulk alloy can reach 1496 MPa in
an argon atmosphere. A model was estab-
lished to study quantitatively the mechanism
of mechanical properties of CoCuFeMnNi
alloy. The main strengthening mechanisms
are grain reﬁnement strengthening, solid
solution strengthening, and cluster strength-
ening.
(3) When the sintering temperature is 925 /C176 C, the
sintering pressure is 500 MPa, the heating
rate is 30 /C176 C/min, and the pressing time is 3
min, the comprehensive soft magnetic proper-
ties of the sintered bulk alloy are better, and
the saturation magnetic induction is about
1.138 T. The coercivity is about 41 Oe.
