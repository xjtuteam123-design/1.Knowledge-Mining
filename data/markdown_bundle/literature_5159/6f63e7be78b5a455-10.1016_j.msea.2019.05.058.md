Abstract: The effect of aging on the microstructure and prope rty of a newly high 
entropy alloy (HEA) with balanced strength-ductilit y and good high temperature 
property is studied. The results show that the micr ostructure of AlCoCrFeMo 0.05 Ni 2 
high entropy alloy forging consists of Fe, Co rich FCC phase, Al, Ni rich BCC phase 
and Cr, Mo rich BCC phase. The area ratios of FCC a nd BCC phases are about 61% 
and 39%, respectively. After aged at 680 ℃ and 700 ℃, an Al, Ni rich needle-like 
phase with FCC structure is precipitated in the ori ginal FCC phase; the Cr, Mo rich 
BCC phase grows up, aggregates at the grain boundar y and forms finally block σ 
phase. The microstructure of the alloy aged at 700 ℃ is refined continuously without

1393 MPa, 19.5%, respectively. The elongation of th e alloy aged at 700 ℃ for 150h 
can reach up to 80% at 700 ℃. 
Keywords: high entropy alloy; aging; high temperature; mechanical property 
 
1. Introduction 
High entropy alloy (HEA), composed of five or more main elements, is a novel 
alloy design concept[1]. Many HEAs with good ductil ity, high hardness or 
compression strength have been reported in recent y ears[2-5]. However, most of them 
indicated that the initial strength or plastic leve l of a HEA with single FCC and BCC 
solid solution is quite low[6-13]. For example, ten sile strength of Al 0.3 CoCrFeNi is 
less than 350 MPa[14]. And a lot of HEAs with extre me hardness are difficult to be 
processed so that compression strength is often bei ng reported, while the tensile 
strength is lacked. Therefore, high entropy alloys with good strength-ductility balance, 
such as AlCoCrFeNi 2.1 [15], become a research hotspot. 
AlCoCr 2FeMo 0.5 Ni high entropy alloy has been reported as the allo y with high 
hot hardness and stability[16]. But there are too m any brittleness phases like σ phase 
in the alloy. Numerous cracks have been found in th e ingot at the same time. A new 
good strength-ductility balanced HEA with Al, Co, C r, Fe, Mo, Ni elements,

AlCoCrFeMo 0.05 Ni 2, has been designed by our group based on the phase formation

declared as a promising high temperature alloy[18], the room and high temperature 
strength of the designed alloy are slightly better. It maybe attributed to the addition of 
Mo element. 
Aging treatment is often used in a HEA to obtain a better mechanical property 
because of serious lattice distortion and supersatu ration of HEA[19-22]. For example, 
the initial yield strength of solution-treated Al 0.5 Cr 0.9 FeNi 2.5 V0.2 alloy is about 274 
MPa. The data can be increased remarkably up to 181 0 MPa after 72% cold rolling 
followed by aging for 1 h at 600 °C[23]. On the oth er hand, the study of as-cast HEA 
is abundant, while the processed HEA is lacked, esp ecially the forged HEA. And the 
forgings are better than cold-rolled alloys for sup eralloy. Thus, the effect of aging on 
the microstructure and property of AlCoCrFeMo 0.05 Ni high entropy alloy forging ( φ 
16 mm bar) is studied in this work.

3.2. Tensile property 
Tensile property at room temperature of the alloy aged at 680 ℃ has been tested, as 
shown in Figure 13. From Figure 13 (a, b), one can see that the strength of the alloy 
aged at 680 ℃ increases significantly. And with the increasing of aging time, the tensile 
strength, yield strength and yield ratio are basica lly stable at about 1370 MPa, 1050 
MPa, and 0.76, while the data of the as-forged allo y are 1208 MPa, 822 MPa, 0.68, 
respectively. Because of the microstructure of the alloy aged at 680 ℃, described above, 
is not refined obviously. Thus, the improvement of strength is mainly attributed to the 
strengthening effect of the needle-like phases. Fro m Figure 13 (c), it can be seen that 
the plasticity of the alloy with aging time less th an 72 h decreased slightly, the 
elongation was about 20%. After aged for 300 h, the elongation decreased to 8%. 
According to the analysis of microstructure above, the plasticity decreased significantly 
is attributed to a large number of brittle σ phase precipitated at the grain boundary. The 
tensile strength, yield strength, elongation of the alloy aged at 700 ℃ for 150 h are 1268 
MPa, 965 MPa, and 4.5%, respectively. It can be seen that the strength and elongation 
are lower than that aged at 680 ℃ for 300 h. The reason is that the needle-like phas e 
aged at 700 ℃coarsening, and σ phase becomes more and larger. Figure 14 shows the 
high temperature tensile stress-strain curves of the HEA aged at 700 ℃ for 150 h. When 
tested at 700 ℃, the tensile strength, yield strength and elongati on are 500 MPa, 350 
MPa, 80%, respectively, while the date of the alloy aged at 680 ℃ for 300h are 640 MPa,

4. Conclusions 
(1) The microstructure of AlCoCrFeMo 0.05 Ni 2 high entropy alloy forging consists of 
Fe, Co rich FCC phase, Al, Ni rich BCC phase and Cr , Mo rich BCC phase. The 
area ratios of FCC and BCC phases are about 61% and 39%, respectively. 
(2) The needle-like phase, which precipitated in the or iginal FCC phase during the 
aging process, is the mainly strengthening phase in AlCoCrFeMo 0.05 Ni 2 high 
entropy alloy. And the size of the needle-like phas e, which becomes larger with 
the increasing of aging temperature, is mainly dete rmined by the aging 
temperature instead of aging time. The σ phase at the grain boundary of the aged 
alloy, which can reduce the ductility significantly, is evolved from the Cr, Mo rich
