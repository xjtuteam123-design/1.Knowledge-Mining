abstract
Several high entropy alloys CoCrFeNiMnV x (x ¼ 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75) with different content of the inter-
metallic s phase are studied in uniaxial compression in temperature range 4.2 e300 K. Peculiarities of
stress-strain curves, stages of strain hardening, temperature dependences of the yield strength and the
strain rate sensitivity are registered and analyzed considering dislocation sliding as main deformation
mechanism in matrix fcc phase. In ﬂuence of s phase particles, deformation twins and stacking faults on
the deformation behavior of the CoCrFeNiMnV x alloys is discussed.

1. Introduction
In last decade mechanical properties of a new class of materials
e multicomponent high entropy alloys (HEA) are actively studied
[1e15]. Due to structure peculiarities, many mechanical properties
of HEAs (higher strength accompanied by high plasticity, high
fracture toughness, etc.) have advantages in comparison with
traditional multicomponent multiphase alloys. HEA consists of ﬁve
or more elements with near equiatomic concentration [1,2], which
distinguishes it from traditional multicomponent alloys having one
or two main components. High values of mixing entropy
(S ~ 13e19 J/mol) and consequently the decreased values of the free
energy are typical for HEAs, which should suppress the multiphase
structure and provide formation of the singe substitution solid
solution phase with mostly fcc or bcc crystal lattice. However, ac-
cording to the latest experimental studies [3,6,7,10,14,15]addition
of elements such as Al, Cu, V, Mo, Ti, etc. to typical equiatomic single
phase high entropy alloys CoCrFeNi and CoCrFeNiMn leads to the
formation of different phase structures, including a single face-
centered cubic (FCC) or body-centered cubic (BCC) phase, duplex
FCC phases, duplex BCC phases, as well as additional ordered or
intermetallic phases. Reasons of the multiphase structure in the
HEAs and its impact on their mechanical properties are not clearly
identiﬁed now and require further study. Effect of doping by
different concentration of V on the phase's content and distribution
was studied for CoCrFeNiMnV x alloys [6,7], and the relation of
microstructure and mechanical properties of the alloys were
studied at ambient temperature. It was found [7] that for x /C21 0.5
besides the matrix solid solution fcc phase, the additional s-phase
appears, which quantity depends on the V concentration. In Ref. [7]
for ambient temperature in uniaxial compression the in ﬂuence of
the V concentration on the microhardness value and characteristics
of the deformation curves was studied. However, clariﬁcation in the
wide temperature range (especially at cryogenic temperatures) of
the physical mechanisms, responsible for dependence of the alloy's
microstructure and mechanical properties on the concentration of
the doping elements, requires additional studies.
In this connection, the aim of this work is study of the me-
chanical properties in compression of the CoCrFeNiMnV x high en-
tropy alloys (x ¼ 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75) in temperature range 300 e4.2 K.

Serrations at the stress-strain curves of annealed CoCrFeNiMn
alloy start at rather large value of plastic strain (0.12). The reason of
such behavior lies in the nature of serrated plastic deformation
[18]: at low temperatures dislocations do not have enough energy
to individually move through the crystal, so they are accumulated
at the obstacles (barriers), and with increase of the ﬂow stress at
some point starts the avalanche-type collective motion of disloca-
tion, which leads to local strain and is accompanied by drop of the
ﬂow stress. Thereafter, accumulation of dislocation starts again and
process is repeated. So, smooth beginning of the stress-strain curve
in the annealed state of CoCrFeNiMn can be explained by insuf ﬁ-
cient quantity of strong enough barriers for dislocation motion in
this coarse-grained material with large grains (350 mm), and
serrated deformation starts after dislocation walls (cells) formation,
which can be such ef ﬁcient barriers for dislocation. As-solidi ﬁed
states of CoCrFeNiMn, CoCrFeNiMnV 0.25 and CoCrFeNiMnV 0.5
have larger quantity of barriers for dislocations due to smaller grain
size (50 e120
mm) in case of CoCrFeNiMnV 0.25, due to presence of
dendrite boundaries (CoCrFeNiMn) and s-phase (CoCrFeNiMnV0.5),
so serrations starts at smaller strains in comparison with the
annealed state of CoCrFeNiMn. Structural states with higher con-
centration of V (larger quantity of s-phase) provide large quantity
of barriers for dislocation accumulation, so serrated strain starts in
them right at the yield limit.

vated motion, which leads to more strong temperature dependence

5. Conclusions
The CoCrFeNiMnVx (x ¼ 0, 0.25, 0.5, 075) high entropy alloys are
complex one or two phase alloys with severely distorted crystal
lattice due to mixing of the elements with different atomic radius. It
is suggested from the analysis of CoCrFeNiMnV x deformation
behavior that majority of its mechanical properties (serrated strain
at 4.2 K, stages of strain hardening, temperature dependence of
strength, strain rate sensitivity) in wide range of low temperatures
can be described by processes in matrix fcc phase with the one
main deformation mechanism e sliding of full dislocations in sys-
tem {111}<110>, which was accompanied by two additional pro-
cesses: formation of stacking faults and twinning. The particles of
the second (s) phase can be considered as strong athermal barriers
for motion of dislocation, which increase the level of internal
stresses in the system. Observed high cryogenic strength of the
CoCrFeNiMnVx high entropy alloys, accompanied by high plasticity
in temperature range of 4.2 e300 K allows to consider this material
as perspective for cryogenic applications.
