1. Introduction
The single phase /C13-titanium aluminide alloys have
received muchattentioninrecentyearsduetotheirhigh
speciﬁc strength, high speciﬁc stiﬀness, high strength
retentionathightemperature,lowdensity( /C26/C243.8g/cm3),
good oxidation resistance and good creep resistance.
Neverthelesstheirpoorductilityandfracturetoughnessat
roomtemperatureisunacceptableforstructuralapplica-
tions. By the addition of third element (e.g.Cr, Mn, W,
Nb) and thermomechanical treatments, it is possible to
obtain balanced engineering properties of titanium alu-
minide alloys for future application as structural mate-
rials[1–2].
Recentlyourtensiletestsinthetemperaturerangefrom
25 to 1150
/C14C for the Ti–40Al–15Nb alloy indicate that
the UTS of this alloy exhibits a positive temperature
dependence,whichmaybeexplainedbythestressanom-
aly phenomena. The stress anomaly phenomena of TiAl
alloys have been studied by numerous researchers [4–
13,22,26,27,29]. Nevertheless the stress anomaly phe-
nomena of TiAl alloys with three phases and high Nb
contenthavenotbeeninvestigated.Atnormalstrainrate,
the ﬂow stress of the titanium aluminide alloy increases
with increasing temperature, depending strongly on alu-
minum content, third element addition and heat treat-
ment processes. In the literature [4–13,22,26,27,29], the
stress anomaly phenomena of the titanium aluminide
alloysgenerallyappearinthealuminumcontentsranging
from 45 to 55 at.%. So far, the highest temperature of
stressanomalyphenomenaoccurredinTi–55Al–10Valloy
at 800
/C14C [9]. The object of this study is to elucidate the
relationship between mechanical property and micro-
structureoftheTi–40Al–15Nballoyinthe temperature
range of 25 to 1150
/C14C. Particularly the stress anomaly
phenomena of the TiAl alloy with higher Nb content
wasinvestigatedandcomparedwithpreviousstudies.
