ABSTRACT 
The powder metallurgy high-strength Ti-1Al-8V-5Fe alloy (Ti-185 alloy) was investigated 
in this paper with different addition method of alloying elements, Al, V, Fe pure elements powder 
(EP) or 1Al-8V-5Fe ternary master alloy powder (MAP), based on TiH 2 powders at the sintering 
temperature from 1150 oC to 1350 oC. The results indicate that the Ti-185 alloy with the 1Al-8V-5Fe 
master alloy (Ti-185 MAP alloy) possesses the higher relative sintered density, less oxygen content 
and less α-phase volume fraction versus the Ti-185 alloy with Al, V and Fe pure elements (Ti -185 
EP alloy). No matter where the sintering temperature is 1150 oC, 1250 oC or 1350 oC, Ti-185 MAP 
alloy invariably has the higher yield strength and hardness which have a strong relationship to its 
higher density and less volume fraction of softer α-phase in comparison with Ti-185 EP alloy. 
KEYWORDS: alloys, blend, elements, metallurgy, powder, sintering, Ti-1Al-8V-5Fe, TiH2 
Introduction 
The beta-titanium alloys are considered as attractive materials because of the higher yield 
strength, toughness and corrosion resistance,[1,2] such as Ti-1Al-8V-5Fe (Ti-185) alloy which has 
high ultimate and shear strengths.[3,4] Despite the Ti-185 alloy developed in the early 1960s[3] has

Materials and Methods 
The powder metallurgy Ti-185 alloys were prepared using TiH2 and two different types of 
alloy element powders, such as Al, V, Fe pure element powders (EP) and 1Al -8V-5Fe ternary 
master alloy powders (MAP) as starting materials. 
The bulk sponge Ti was hydrogenated by homemade hydrogenation equipment which was 
then crushed by hi gh-energy ball mill (SPEX8000M) to prepare TiH 2 powders ( ≤ 100 µm). 
Elemental Fe powders (99.95% purity, ≤ 100 µm), Al powders (99.9% purity, ≤ 100 µm) and V 
powders (99.9% purity, ≤ 100 µm) were furnished by Institute of China Metal Metallurgy. 1Al-
8V-5Fe ternary master alloy was arc-melted under argon atmosphere which was then crushed into 
powders (99.9% purity, ≤ 100 µm). 
All of starting materials for Ti-185 EP or Ti-185 MAP alloys were blended in a planet ball 
mill mixer (PMQ0.4L, Shanghai Zhuo Di, China) for 2 h with the speed of 100 r·min -1, and then 
pressed by uniaxial cold press at 800 MPa in room temperature. The compacted samples were 
isothermally sintered in different temperatures (1150 oC, 1250 oC, 1350 oC) under a vacuum of

Results and Discussion 
Figure 1 shows the comparison of the relative sintered density and densification 
parameters for Ti-185 in different sintering temperatures. The sintering temperatures and type of 
adding alloying element have a noticeable effect on the density of Ti -185 based on titanium 
hydride mixtures. When the sintering temperature increases from 1150 oC to 1350 oC, the relative 
sintered density varies from 96.6% to 99.6% for the Ti-185 with 1Al-8V-5Fe ternary master alloys

densification parameter varies from 87.7% to 98.3% or 84% t o 93.7% for the Ti -185 alloys with 
1Al-8V-5Fe ternary master alloy or elemental powders. The sintered density and densification of 
Ti-185 MAP is always higher than that of Ti-185 EP in the same sintering temperature.

Some research have proved that sintering can be viewed as two processes: α-phase sintering below

densification. The shrinkage rate of Ti -185 EP and Ti -185 MAP are separately about 3.24% and

Conclusions 
The high-strength Ti-1Al-8V-5Fe alloys are produced by BEPM approach using element 
powders (EP) and 1Al -8V-5Fe ternary master alloy powders (MAP) based on TiH 2 powders, 
respectively. The PM fabrication of Ti-1Al-8V-5Fe alloys is effectively influenced by the different 
addition method of alloying elements . The sintering behavior, microstructure evolution and 
mechanical properties of Ti-185 alloys in different sintering temperatures are investigated and analyzed: 
1. The densification rate and relative sintered density increase with increasing the sintering 
temperature where the Ti-185 MAP alloy invariably has a higher density than the Ti-185 EP alloy 
at the same sintering temperature. Moreover, Ti-185 MAP alloy has the less oxygen content 
and α-phase volume fraction compared with Ti-185 EP alloy. 
2. No matter where the sintering temperature is 1150 oC, 1250 oC or 1350 oC, Ti-185 MAP 
alloy always has the higher yield strength and hardness which are attributed to that it has 
the higher density and less volume fraction of soft er α-phase in comparison with Ti -185 
EP alloy.
