Abstract. Multi-component Nb-(11,15)Si-5Hf-30Ti-4Al-4W-2B-(8 ,16)Cr alloys have been 
proposed, attempting to obtain the Nb-Si based allo ys with a comprehensive property. The results 
show that three phases of Nb 
SS , Nb 5Si 3 and Laves Cr 2Nb exist in the alloys with a Cr content of 16 
at%. With increasing Si and Cr contents the fractur e toughness KQ decreases, while the oxidation 
resistance at 1250°C and strength at 1250°C and 135 0°C exhibit an increasing tendency. The 15Si-
16Cr alloy shows the highest strength and oxidation resistance, and the lowest toughness; they are 
385MPa at 1350ºC, 215mg/cm 2 at 1250ºC for 100 h, and 5.45 MPa·m 1/2 at room temperature, 
respectively. The 11Si-8Cr alloy with an Nb SS /Nb 5Si 3 microstructure only has the highest toughness 
of 11.87 MPa·m1/2 , its strength and oxidation resistance are the lowest. 
 
Introduction 
Based on its relatively high melting point (2743K, higher than that of Ni) and room-temperature 
ductility, Nb-base alloys have the potential to substitute Ni-base superalloys for service at extremely 
high-temperatures. In order to enable Nb-base alloy s to achieve a balance of mechanical properties 
and oxidation resistance, the concept of multi-phas e structure has been developed. It involves the 
incorporation of ductile Nb with stiffening silicid es [1-3] and oxygen resistance phase Cr 
2Nb [4,5], 
to form a multi-phase Nb SS /Nb 5Si 3/Cr 2Nb microstructure, in which the Nb solid solution Nb SS offers 
room-temperature toughness, and the silicide and Cr 2Nb phases supply strength and oxidation 
resistance respectively at elevated temperatures. T hus, adjusting the proportion and morphology of 
the Nb SS , Nb 5Si 3 and Cr 2Nb phases in the Nb-Si based alloys may obtain the balanced properties 
that meet the requirements of ultra-high-temperature structural materials. 
Based on the eutectic composition Nb-18Si in the Nb -Si binary, two systems Nb-W(Mo)-Si 
[6,7] and Nb-Si-Cr-Ti-Hf-Al-Ge [4,5] have been developed. The Nb-W(Mo)-Si based system with a 
Nb SS /Nb 5Si3 microstructure is aimed to improve strength at ultra-high temperatures. It was reported 
that 0.2% yield strength at 1500°C is quite high at 465MPa in a composition of Nb-5Mo-15W-16Si-
5Hf, owing to W and Mo additions [6]. As for the mu lti-component Nb-Si-Cr-Ti-Hf-Al-Ge system 
with higher Cr additions, its target is to improve oxidation resistance, and a series of Nb-silicide 
alloys with nominal compositions given elsewhere we re investigated [4,5]. Short-term goal in the 
cyclic oxidation resistance, corresponding to a material thickness loss of 200 microns in 10-hour air 
exposure at 1370°C, has been established and succes sfully achieved for the Nb-silicide alloys. In 
this work, multi-component Nb-(11,15)Si-5Hf-30Ti-4Al-4W-2B-(8,16)Cr alloys were developed to 
obtain a three-phase Nb SS /Nb 5Si 3/Cr 2Nb microstructure. The comprehensive property of th e room-
temperature toughness, high-temperature strength an d oxidation resistance of the 
Nb SS /Nb 5Si 3/Cr 2Nb microstructure was primarily studied.

were named 11Si-8Cr, 11Si-16Cr, 15Si-8Cr and 15Si-1 6Cr alloys hereafter. Button ingots were 
prepared by arc-melting in 1atm Argon five to six times to uniform compositions throughout ingots.

Results and Discussion 
Typical microstructures of the four annealed alloys are shown in Fig. 1. For the 11Si-8Cr and 15Si-
8Cr alloys, the bright dendritic-like phase is the Nb 
SS , the grey phases on interstitial regions of the 
dendritic Nb SS is the Nb 5Si 3 silicide, no Laves Cr 2Nb intermetallic is observed, as seen in Figs. 1(a) 
and (c).When the nominal Cr content is at 16 at.%, the Laves Cr 2Nb phase is observed and the 
microstructures consist of the bright Nb SS phase, grey Nb 5Si 3 block and black Laves Cr 2Nb dots or 
strips distributed on boundaries of the Nb SS and Nb 5Si 3 phases, as seen in Figs. 1(b) and 1(d). The 
size of the Cr 2Nb phase is about 10 microns in width or diameter, and the volume fraction of the 
Cr 2Nb phase is about 7% for the 11Si-8Cr alloy, 11% for the 15Si-16Cr alloy.

remains at a relatively high level even at a plasti c strain larger than 15 %. Generally, the

Isothermal oxidation tests were performed at 1250°C in static air to investigate the oxidation 
kinetics of the four annealed alloys. The kinetic curves in Fig. 3 reveal that the oxidation kinetics is 
not totally parabolic. At the beginning of oxidation, the curves show a parabolic plot and the weight 
gain increases quickly with time. The weight gain r eaches the top value at time of about 30 hours 
and then almost remains a constant with the oxidati on time. Air exposure at 1250°C for 100 hours, 
the weight gains of the 11Si-8Cr, 15Si-8Cr and 11Si -16Cr alloys are equal to, of about 235~250 
mg/cm 
2, while that of the 15Si-16Cr alloy is about 215 mg /cm 2. The oxidation resistance cannot be 
improved significantly when the Cr content increases from 8 at.% to 16 at.%. 
From Fig. 3, Table 1 and measured KQ values, it is known that the 11Si-8Cr alloy has a 
disadvantage in the fracture toughness. It is belie ved that a higher K Q in the 11Si-8Cr alloy is 
attributed to a higher volume fraction of the ducti le Nb SS phase of more than 75% (see Fig. 1(a)). 
Contrarily, a microstructure with a higher fraction of the Nb SS phase or lower fraction of the 
stiffening Nb 5Si 3 and Cr 2Nb is unfavorable to the strength and oxidation res istance at high 
temperatures, according to the mixture rule [8]. A bad oxidation resistance of the Nb SS phase is due 
to the Nb SS phase had larger oxygen solubility and kinetic rate than the Nb 5Si 3 and Cr 2Nb phases [9]. 
Increasing of Si and Cr undoubtedly improves the strength, but decreases the fracture toughness. 
For the 15Si-16Cr alloy, the volume fraction of the stiffening Nb 5Si 3 and Cr 2Nb is as high as 65% 
(see Fig. 1(d)), while those stiffening phases only have the fracture toughness of less than 3 
MPa·m1/2 [10]. In addition, the larger block Nb 5Si 3 phase with size of as high as ~100mm is also 
harmful to the bulk toughness. It was reported that Cr embrittles Nb [10], on the other hand,

It seems that Cr additions cannot considerably impr ove the oxidation resistance at 1250°C. In 
general, the Laves Cr 2Nb plays a key role against oxygen and is beneficia l to the high-temperature 
oxidation resistance of the Nb-Si based alloys [4,5], but an outstanding oxidation resistance can be 
obtained only when the fraction of the Cr 2Nb phase is at 25~30% [4]. In this work, however, t he 
fraction of the Cr 2Nb phase is about 11% in the 15Si-16Cr alloy, which is not high enough to 
increase the oxidation resistance of the bulk Nb-Si based alloys. To obtain optimum phase 
constitutions of the Nb SS /Nb 5Si 3/Cr 2Nb microstructure and balanced properties in the ro om-
temperature toughness, high-temperature strength and oxidation resistance, modifying the nominal 
Cr and Si contents is of great importance for alloying the Nb-Si based alloys in the future. 
 
Summary 
In the multi-component Nb-(11,15)Si-5Hf-30Ti-4Al-4W -2B-(8,16)Cr alloys, the microstructure 
consists of 
 three phases of the Nb SS , Nb 5Si 3 and Laves Cr 2Nb at a Cr content of 16 at.%, the Laves 
Cr 2Nb phase does not exist at a Cr content of 8 at.%. Increasing of Si and Cr contents, i.e., the 
volume fraction of the stiffening Nb 5Si 3 and Laves Cr 2Nb phases, decreases the fracture toughness 
KQ, whereas increases the high-temperature strength c onsiderably. The oxidation resistance does 
not seem to be improved evidently at higher Cr and Si contents.
