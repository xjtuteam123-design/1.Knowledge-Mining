The behaviour of binary Nb3Al alloys as high-temperature structural materials 
is reviewed with regard to deformation of single phase Nb~A1 and Nb3A1/Nb~ 
(niobium solid solution) composites fabricated by various processes. Single- 
phase Nb3AI, which exists at 1873 K in the composition range between 21 and 
23 tool% AI, possesses an extremely high yield strength at high temperatures 
with the activation of a(100) dislocations on {001 } planes, although it is very 
brittle below 1500 K. Fine distribution of Nb~ in Nb~AI improves the fracture 
toughness and decreases the high temperature strength. The trends of Nb3A1 
alloy development will be discussed. 
Key words: Nb~AI, high temperature deformation, yield strength, dislocation, 
planar fault 
1 INTRODUCTION 
Current studies on replacements for superalloys as 
high-temperature structural materials have fo- 
cused on refractory intermetallics. Among them, 
Nb3A1 with the A15 structure is one of the most 
promising candidates because of its high melting 
point and low density. In addition, since the fabri- 
cation processes of Nb3A1 as a superconducting 
material have been extensively studied, ~9 some of 
these processes can be readily applied to the pro- 
cessing of high-temperature structural Nb3A1 al- 
loys. Moreover, the processing techniques cur- 
rently being utilized for Ni base superalloys, i.e., 
the various vacuum melting and casting tech- 
niques in ingot metallurgy and the gas atomiza- 
tion technique in powder metallurgy, are applica- 
ble to the processing of Nb3A1. ~°'~ It is known, 
however, that single phase Nb3A1 is very brittle at 
ambient temperatures because of its complex crys- 
tal structure and limited slip systems. Therefore, it 
is necessary for Nb3A1 to be toughened by intro- 
ducing a ductile phase for structural applications, 
even though the strength is thereby reduced. At 
present, however, not enough data are available to 
adequately estimate the best balance of high

strength at high-temperatures as shown in Fig. 9, 
they are so brittle that there is no ductility, even 
in compression tests at 1273 K and below. This 
brittle behaviour has limited their applications to 
high temperature structural components. One of 
the most promising methods for lessening this 
drawback is believed to improve fracture tough- 
ness at ambient temperatures, which can be 
achieved by the presence of Nb solid solution as a 
second phase. 46'47 Accordingly, various processes 
for producing Nb3A1/Nb~ composites have begun 
to be developed and some properties of the com- 
posites are evaluated. 
Yang and Vasudevan 48 have prepared

7 CONCLUSIONS 
Studies on binary Nb3A1 alloys have revealed that 
single phase Nb3A1 has an extremely high yield 
strength above 1500 K with the activation of 
a(100) dislocations on {001} planes, although it is 
very brittle below 1500 K. It is also found that the 
presence of Nbss in Nb3A1 alloys to improve frac- 
ture toughness at low temperatures leads to an ap- 
preciable decrease in strength at high tempera- 
tures. Therefore, further studies are needed to in- 
crease the high temperature strength in Nb~s as 
well as Nb3A1 by means of alloying it with various 
elements such as Ta, Mo, W, Zr, HI', etc. Also, it 
is necessary to control microstructure to achieve 
the best balance of high strength at high tempera- 
tures and high fracture toughness at ambient tem- 
peratures.
