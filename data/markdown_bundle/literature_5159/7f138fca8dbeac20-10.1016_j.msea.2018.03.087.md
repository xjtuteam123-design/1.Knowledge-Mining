Abstract 
The microstructure characteristics of GH4169 alloy aged with electric-pulse treatment (EPT) at 800℃ 
were investigated and tensile properties were examined at high temperature . The tensile test results 
indicate that t he ultimate stress, yield stress and fracture elongation increase simultaneously in the 
alloy aged with EPT compared with that of normal aged alloy. The vacancy concentration of the 
alloy aged with EPT increases proved by positron annihilation spectrometer compared with that of 
normal aged alloy. Vacancy concentration increase leads to increasing of yield stress at the initial 
stage of tensile deformation. Farther, vacancy induces ultrafine nm -sized γ′′ phase to precipitate 
during tensile deform ation at high temperature, which is the key factor on strength and ductility 
improvement. Meanwhile, EPT induces parts of γ′′ phase (precipitating during aged treatment) to 
precipitate around dislocation which impedes dislocation motion. 
 
Keywords Electric-pulse treatment, Strength, Ductility, Precipitate, GH4169 alloy

1. Introduction 
GH4169 alloy [1, 2] is a nickel-based superalloy extensively used in aeronautic gas turbine due 
to its excellent mechanical propert y, good oxidation and corrosion resistance. The alloy achieves 
remarkable properties through γ′ and γ′′ hardening precipitated phases, especially γ′′ phase [3, 4]. The 
γ′′ phase is coherency with γ matrix and the lattice misfit is a pproximately 2 %~3 % with coherency 
strain [5, 6], interacting with dislocation through coherency strain to strength the alloy. For satisfying 
high performance of aero-engine, both strength and ductility of the alloy at high temperature are 
required to be simultaneously improved for security service. 
The electric-pulse treatment (EPT) was first conducted on metal by Troitskii and Likhtman [7] 
in 1963, effects of EPT on mechanical property and microstructure have been widely researched in 
the recent year [8-13]. It has been proved [12-15] that ductility of metal remarkably enhances with 
EPT. Baranov [16] found that the strength of brass L62 increased with electric field . Meanwhile, it 
has been found [17] that the strength of boron steel increase s with EPT because of the fine 
Martensite. EPT has great potentials on influencing precipitate evolution behavior [18, 19]. In our 
previous research , t ransforming of γ′′ phase into δ phase which was restrained in GH4169 alloy 
resulting from large amount of vacancies induced with electric field [20]. Vacancy is an important 
factor on precipitate evolution behavior and property, i t has been speculated that EPT induces to 
form plenty of vacancies. However, no direct characterization analysis proves the conjecture. In 
present research , EPT is applied to GH4169 alloy for aged treatment. It is mainly aim ed at 
investigating the effect of EPT on tensile property of the alloy aged with EPT compare d with that of 
normal aged alloy. The mechanisms of EPT on microstructure characteristics and tensile properties 
are also discussed. 
2. Material and experimental 
The commercial hot rolling sheet GH4169 alloy is used for the present research and its chemical 
compositions (weight, %) is: C 0.030, Fe 18.73, Cr 18.9 9, Mo 3.0 1, Al 0.5 8, Ti 1.04, Nb+Ta 5.12, 
Mn 0.02, P 0.009, S 0.0 02, B 0.00 5, Ni bal. Alloy was subjected solution heat treatment at 1100℃ 
for 30 min with alloying elements dissolving in γ matrix. And then alloy was aged with EPT at 800℃ 
for 10 and 20 min, respectively. The temperature was measured by a Pt -6%Rh-Pt thermocouple

firmly welded on the specimen surface. For comparison, alloy was normal aged in furnace at 800 ℃ 
for 10 and 20 min, respectively. The EPT was performed by HPC-5 type electro -pulsing generator, 
which could generate pulse current with 10 Hz and 40 Hz frequency in present research. The current 
density je was 440 A/mm2 with duration tc of 30 μs. The mechanical property after heat treatment was 
investigated by tensile test . Tensile specimen was prepared with gauge size of 1.5×6×24 mm3 along 
rolling direction. Tensile test w as conducted by MTS 810 servo -hydraulic testing system at 800 ℃ 
with crosshead speed of 1 mm/min. Both microstructure characteristic and fracture surface 
morphology were analyzed by JSM -6510 scanning electron microscope (SEM). Precipitate 
morphology and dislocation configuration were examined by JEOL 2100 transmission electron 
microscope (TEM). The precipitate characterization at atomic level was analyzed with JEOL ARM 
200F scanning transmission electron microscopy (STEM) using high-angle annular -dark-field 
(HAADF) contrast . The vacancy concentration was examined with positron annihilation 
spectrometer (PAS). The PAS was performed using the fast -slow coincidence system with 22Na and 
the time resolution was about 19 5 ps. Both size and volume fraction of phase were calculated by 
quantitative metallography. 
3. Results 
3.1 Microstructure after aged 
Both γ′′ and γ′ phases precipitate from γ matrix with homogeneous distribution in the alloy 
normal aged at 800℃ for 20 min, as shown Fig. 1(a). However, it has been reported that the size of 
γ′ phase is about 10 nm and volum e fraction is about 1.9 % in GH4169 alloy normal aged at 800℃ 
for 30 h [ 21]. In present research, the alloy was only aged at 800 ℃ for 20 min . Therefore, the 
evolution of γ′ phase is not discussed and the effect of γ′ phase on tensile property is not taken into 
account. The average size of γ′′ phases is about 42 nm. The morphology of γ′′ phase in the alloy aged 
with EPT for 20 min as shown in Figs. 1(b) & (c). It is clear that EPT promotes γ′′ phase to grow up 
and parts of γ′′ phases precipitate around dislocation and distribute one by one in chain morphology 
as marked with arrow. The average size of γ′′ phase is about 100 nm in the alloy with 40 Hz-EPT. 
Table 1 shows size of γ′′ phase in alloy aged with different EPT frequency for 10 and 20 min . The 
average size of γ′′ phase increases with the increasing EPT frequency. The distance between γ′′ 
phases in the alloy with EPT is larger than that of normal aged alloy. Compared with normal aged 
alloy, the volume fraction of γ′′ phase is almost the same with that in the alloy aged with EPT. Fig.

1(d) shows SEM morphology of δ phase in the alloy normal aged, and no phase could be found at 
grain boundary. While, a little granular δ phases are at grain boundary in the alloy aged with EPT as

PAS is an effective technique that can sensitively detect the defects in metal [22-24]. Since 
defects are negatively charged that positrons are trapped there. Therefore, positron s convey 
information including both defect size and concentration. Table 2 shows the positron annihilation 
lifetime of GH4169 alloy after aged for 20 min . The lifetime spectrum is divided into two 
components including bulk (short lifetime τ 1) and defect (long lifetime τ 2). The short lifetime τ 1 is 
almost the same in the alloy normal aged and with EPT. Compared with normal aged alloy, the long 
lifetime τ 2 decreases from 170.0 ps to 158.5 ps, and the intensity of I2 increases about 3 4 % in the 
alloy aged with 10 Hz-EPT. The long lifetime τ 2 is the average of positron annihilation lifetime at 
defects mainly including vacancy and dislocation [ 25]. The intensity of I2 is the average of positron 
annihilated at vacancy and dislocation. It has been found that the lifetime of positron annihilation at 
single vacanc y is about 170.0 ps [ 25, 26] and at dislocation is about 113.0 ps [ 27, 28 ] in nickel. 
Therefore, it concludes that vacancy concentration is large increase and dislocation concentration is 
slight increase in the alloy aged with EPT comparing to those of normal aged alloy. In another word, 
EPT induces to form more vacancies. 
3.2 Tensile property 
The tensile properties o f the alloy after aged are listed in Table 3. Compared with those of 
normal aged alloy, the yield stress, ultimate stress and fracture elongation increase for the alloy aged 
with EPT. It is suggested that the microstructure evolution with EPT leads to synchronous improving 
of both strength and ductility. While, the yield stress and ultimate stress decrease, and fracture 
elongation increases in the alloy aged with 40 Hz-EPT compared with that of 10 Hz-EPT. 
4. Discussions 
4.1 Effect of EPT on microstructure 
When the alloy is aged with EPT, parts of γ′′ phases precipitate around dislocation and 
distribute one by one in chain morphology. The γ′′ phase precipitates between 595~870℃ [29], it 
satisfies γ′′ phase nucleation thermodynamics in present research. When the alloy normal aged in 
furnace, temperature field of specimen is homogenous and nucleation of γ′′ phase uniformly

## Page 6

distributes in the whole specimen. Compared with normal aged, the temperature rise mainly depends 
on the Joule heat effect of EPT [30]. Temperature rise can be described as ΔT= ρje
2(Cρ d)-1tc, where ρ 
is the resistivity, je is the electric current density, Cρ is the specific heat, d is the density of material, 
and tc is the duration of electric current. As well known, the electrical resistivity is sensitive to defect 
such as grain boundary, dislocation [31]. The resistivity in the area with defects is higher than that 
without defect. Joule heat effect enhances and the temperature rise is more rapidly around dislocation. 
Moreover, γ′′ phases prefer to nucleate around dislocation. 
The size of γ′′ phase in the alloy aged with EPT is larger than that of normal aged alloy. 
According to size of γ′′ phase in the alloy aged for 10 and 20 min shown in Table 2, it indicates that 
growth rate of γ′′ phase with EPT is higher than that of normal aged alloy. EPT induces the 
decreasing of growth energy obstacle of γ′′ phase . It is well known that diffus e-controlled phase 
coarsening process follows Ostwald theory [ 32, 33], depending on vacancy concentration and atom 
energy. According to reference [34], vacancy concentration increases induced by EPT. When the 
alloy aged with EPT, the temperature of alloy increases due to the electrons collision with atoms. The 
electric energy transforms to thermal energy so fast with the heat speed of 10 6s-1 [35]. The 
temperature rise in the alloy with EPT lags behind thermal expansion so that it forms instantaneously 
thermal pressure, leading to form a large number of vacancies. In present research, it has been proved 
that vacancy concentration increases induced with EPT examined by PAS. When the alloy aged with 
EPT, drift electrons collide with atoms and exchange energy with each other. The energy of atom 
increases with EPT. Both vacancy concentration and atom energy increase induced with EPT, and 
then γ′′ phase is promoted to grow up. 
It has been found [29, 36 ] that δ phase could precipitate at grain boundar y from γ matrix 
directly in cellular reaction (discontinuous dissolving precipitation controlling by migrate grain 
boundary) above 900℃ in normal. However, δ phase has already precipitated at grain boundary at 
800℃ aged with EPT as shown in Fig. 1(f). The increasing of both vacancy concentration and atom 
energy account for δ phase precipitating at grain boundary in the alloy aged with EPT at 800℃. 
4.2 Effect of EPT on tensile property 
Both strength and ductility synchronously improve in the alloy aged with EPT compared with 
that of normal aged alloy. The tensile propert y of GH4169 alloy is related to precipitate

## Page 7

characteristics including kind, size, morphology, distribute and volume fraction. Meanwhile, the 
grain boundary strength is an important factor on strength at high temperature. 
4.2.1 Ultrafine nm-sized γ″ phase precipitating induced with EPT during tensile deformation 
Tensile tests at high temperature are conducted after normal aged, there is no new phase (Fig. 
2(a)) in the alloy with 5 % strain . After aged with 10 Hz -EPT, also no new phase is in the alloy as 
shown in Fig. 2(b). However, after aged with 40 Hz -EPT, there are many new phases in the alloy . 
Large amounts of ultrafine nm -sized γ′′ phases precipitate during tensile deformation at h igh 
temperature as shown in Fig. 3(a). Figs. 3(b) & (c) show HR-TEM images of ultrafine nm-sized γ″/γ 
co-precipitates at atomic level. The fast Fourier transform pattern of the images taken from the <110> 
and <112> zone axis show that the matrix is FCC structure and precipitate is ordered D022 structure. 
The ultrafine nm-sized γ″ phase is coherent with γ matrix, and the misfit is about 2.03 %. The atomic 
structure of ultrafine nm-sized γ′′ phase is further characterized by high -angle annular dark -field 
(HAADF) STEM as shown in Fig. 3(d). Intensity of γ′′ phase (Ni3Nb) is higher than that of γ matrix 
(Ni). It is confirmed that EPT induces ultrafine nm -sized γ′′ phase to precipitat e in the alloy aged 
with 40 H z-EPT during tensile deformation. As discussions above, there are many vacancies in the 
alloy aged with EPT and vacancy concentration increases compared with normal aged alloy. The 
vacancy is the nucleation location for ultrafine nm-sized γ′′ phase precipitating. 
The TEM microstructure of cross-section near fracture surface is shown in Fig. 4. In the alloy 
normal aged, still no new phase (Fig. 4(a)) precipitates until failure. However, in the alloy aged with 
10 Hz-EPT, large amount of ultrafine nm-sized γ′′ phases precipitate with strain near fracture surface 
as shown in the circle in Fig. 4(b). In the alloy aged with 40 Hz-EPT, the size of ultrafine nm-sized γ′′ 
phase increases with the increasing strain as shown in the circle in Fig. 4(c) . The average size of 
ultrafine nm-sized γ′′ phase increases with the increasing EPT frequency (in the alloy with 
10 Hz-EPT is about 5 nm and 40 Hz-EPT is about 21 nm). 
4.2.2 Strength improvement 
Fig. 2 shows dislocation configuration in the alloy with 5 % strain after ag ed treatment. The 
dislocation density in the alloy aged with EPT is higher than that of normal aged alloy. The γ′′ phases 
around dislocation forming chain morphology impedes dislocation motion and dislocat ion tangle is 
serious around them as shown in Fig. 2(b), leading to increasing of strength.

Compared with normal aged , EPT induces to form large amounts of vacancies. Vacancy 
strengthening mechanism leads to increasing of yield stress during the initial stage of tensile 
deformation. At the same time, vacancy induces ultrafine nm-sized γ′′ phase to precipitate during 
tensile deformation. The strength improvement mainly originate s from ultrafine nm -sized γ′′ phase. 
The ultrafine nm-sized γ′′ phase is coherent with γ matrix, and elastic coherency strain, ordering and 
modulus hardening prevail as strengthening mechanisms. 
The granular δ phase precipitates at grain boundary, which pin s and restrains grain boundary 
slip at high temperature. The grain boundary strength improves. 
4.2.3 Ductility improvement 
The size of ultrafine nm-sized γ′′ phase is very small and lattice parameter is almost the same 
with γ matrix, leading to dislocation shear ultrafine nm -sized γ′′ phase. The elastic interaction is 
between precipitate and dislocation, thereby crack hardly initiates at phase-matrix interface owing to 
negligible strain accumulation. 
The γ′′ phase precipitates during aged treatment in the alloy with EPT or normal aged (Fig.1). 
The distance between γ′′ phases in the alloy with EPT is larger than that of normal aged alloy. The 
dislocation passes by γ′′ phase which adjusts dislocation motion , which leads to stress concentration 
decreases. The larger distance is the more easily dislocation pass es by, which is advantage for 
deformation. The plastic deformation ability enhances in the alloy aged with EPT. The elongation 
increases in the alloy with EPT compared with that of normal aged alloy. 
A few granular δ phases with high strength precipitating at grain boundary are hardly cut though 
by dislocation. Cracks have to propagate along granular δ phases so that both the propagation 
pathway and the energy consume increase, which leads to the elongation increase. 
4.2.4 Tensile property comparison between 10 Hz-EPT and 40 Hz-EPT 
Compared with 10 Hz -EPT, t he yield stress and ultimate stress of the alloy with 40 Hz -EPT 
decrease, and fracture elongation increases. The ultrafine nm-sized γ′′ phase grows up to about 20 
nm in the alloy with 40 Hz -EPT, which is beneficial for strength enhancement. The γ′′ phase 
precipitating during aged treatment grows up during tensile deformation, as the big arrow shown in 
Fig. 4. The volume fraction of γ′′ phase in the alloy is almost the same. The size of γ′′ phase in the 
alloy with 40 Hz -EPT is much larger than that with 10 Hz -EPT. However, the seriously coarse γ′′ 
phase that leads to strength decreasing. Therefore, the strength decreases in the alloy with 40

Hz-EPT comparing to that of 10 Hz -EPT. The coarse γ′′ phase forms precipitate free zone (PFZ) 
around them ( small arrow shown in Fig.4(c)). PFZ can adjust dislocation motion and release stress 
concentration which results in increasing of elongation. 
 
Conclusions 
(1) When the alloy aged with EPT, the size of γ′′ phase increases; parts of γ′′ phase precipitates 
along dislocation in chain morphology ; granular δ phases pin at grain boundary ; and vacancy 
concentration increases compared with normal aged alloy. Both vacancy concentration and atom 
energy increase assist in γ′′ phase growing up and δ phase precipitating. The Joule heat effect 
enhances around dislocation induced by EPT which leads to γ′′ phase precipitate around dislocation. 
Meanwhile, vacancy concentration increases induced with EPT which is the key factor on ultrafine 
nm-sized γ′′ precipitating during tensile deformation. 
(2) EPT induces to form multi-microstructure in GH4169 alloy, which results in the 
simultaneous improving of both strength and ductility. The strength enhances based on the following 
reasons: (a) Ultrafine nm-sized γ′′ phase has high coherency strain strength , which is the key factor; 
(b) γ′′ phases around dislocation in chai n morphology impede s dislocation motion and lead s to 
dislocation tangle; (c) The vacancy strengthening mechanism leads to increasing of yield strength; (d) 
The granular δ phases pin at grain boundary and impede grain boundary to slip at high temperature . 
The ductility improvement mainly depends on dislocation shearing ultrafine nm -sized γ′′ phase with 
negligible strain accumulation . Farther, the distance between γ′′ phases precipitating during ag ed 
treatment with EPT is larger than that of normal aged, which effectively adjusts dislocation motion.
