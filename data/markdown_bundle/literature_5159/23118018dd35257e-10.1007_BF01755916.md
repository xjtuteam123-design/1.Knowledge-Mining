being devoted to the protection of critical parts of high-temperature strength alloys from 
oxidation under long high-temperature action. For this purpose wide use is made of protec-

face condition and to a reduction in its protective functions, which has a significant in- 
fluence on the strength characteristics. For the characteristics of static (short- and 
long-term) strength these questions have been covered in a number of works [1, 2].

on the fatigue resistance of hlgh-temperature strength alloys has been studied to a lesser

Since the fatigue resistance depends significantly upon the surface condition and struc- 
ture of a material, it becomes necessary to evaluate the influence of coatings on its fatigue 
resistance under long high-temperature action comparable to the gas-turbine engine service 
llfe. However, high-temperature fatigue tests of such length are time consuming. In connec- 
tion with this, in this work the influence of heat-resistant coatings on the endurance of 
EI867 and ZhS6K alloys, which are used for the production of turbine blades, was investigated 
on the basis of comparative tests of specimens with coatings (different methods and thick- 
nesses) and without coatings after isothermal holds at 9500C for 1000 h. The temperature-- 
time conditions of the hold and the tests were selected taking into consideration the actual 
operating conditions of gas-turblne engine parts, which provides evaluation of the effective- 
ness of protection of the surface from oxidation by the investigated heat-resistant coatings. 
The heat-reslstant aluminum coatings were applied to smooth cylindrical specimens ac- 
cording to the normal method for turbine blades: a) aluminize in a 98~ FeAI + 2Z NH,CI ferro- 
aluminum powder; b) aluminosiliconize according to two variations, AS-I (bZ Si + 95Z AI) and 
AS-II (50Z Si + 50Z AI), by the method of painting with a suspension and subsequent diffusion 
annealing at 950eC for 4 h. The original thickness of the coatings was varied from 20-40 to 
50-70 ~m. 
The specimens were fatigue tested at the temperature of the preliminary hold of 950~ 
according to the standard method on a modernized MUI-6000 machine under conditions of pure 
bending with rotation with a symmetric cycle and a loading frequency of 50 Hz. The test 
base was taken as 2"10" cycles. The fatigue curves were calculated from the results of 
tests of 12-15 specimens on a BESM-4 computer.

The analysls of the fatigue test results presented in Fig. i and Table i showed that a 
preliminary long hold at the test temperature leads to a reduction in the characteristics of 
fatigue resistance both of the coatlng-protected and theunprotected specimens. In the case 
without a coating a stronger reduction in fatigue resistance of ~1867 alloy than of ZhS6K 
alloy is observed; in the studied range of lives for the first it was 30-40% and for the 
second 5-10%. 
It is known that both a change in structure and damage of the specimen surface as the 
result of oxidation have a significant influence on fatigue resistance under conditions of 
the long action of high temperatures. In connection with this it may be assumed that the 
effect of the reduction in fatigue resistance of ZhS6K and E1867 alloys revealed is caused 
by the appearance of both factors. If it is assumed that the degree of surface oxidation 
is about the same for either alloy (according to the data of [3] it is somewhat greater for 
ZhS6K alloy), then the greater reduction in fatigue resistance of E1867 alloy is caused by 
the change in its structural condition during the holds particularly by coagulation and 
solution of the hardening y'-phase, while ZhS6K alloy is more stable at this temperature 
and for it, apparently, the reduction in fatigue resistance to a greater degree is caused 
by surface oxidation. This is indicated by the change in the character of the fatigue 
curve of E1867 alloy. After prellmlnary aging of the specimens it is approximated by only 
a single sloped portion without a break (Fig. ib, curves 1 and i'). A slmilar influence of 
the long action of a temperature of 950~ on the fatigue resistance of E1867 alloy was noted 
earller [4] in connection with coagulation of the strengthening y'-phase. 
To evaluate the effectiveness of the protective coatings under conditions of cyclic
