ABSTRACT
Refractory high-entropy alloy (RHEA) is one of the most promising materials for high-temperature applications.
However, their application is inhibited by insufficient strength at elevated temperatures and brittleness at
ambient temperatures. Herein, we proposed a multiscale oxide dispersion strengthened (ODS) NbTaTiV RHEA
with an excellent combination of strength and plasticity at both room and elevated temperatures. The multilevel
microstructures are composed of a fine-grained BCC phase matrix (~1.2 μ m), submicron Ti-(O, N) particles
(~330 nm), and nanoscale Y
2
Ti
2
O
7
particles (~18 nm). Owing to the strengthening of combinatorial multilevel
microstructures, the yield strength of the RHEA was found as high as 1626 MPa, which exhibits a significant
improvement (~46%) in comparison with the NbTaTiV RHEA, without significant loss of ductility. The ODS
NbTaTiV RHEA also shows superior thermal-softening resistance, with yield strengths of 945 MPa at 700
◦
C and
482 MPa at 1000
◦
C. The enhanced strength is mainly attributed to the multilevel microstructures that mediate
the collaborative strengthening mechanism among dispersed strengthening, precipitation strengthening, and
grain boundary strengthening. This work presents a new insight to develop multiscale microstructures in RHEAs
for high-temperature structural materials.
1. Introduction
Refractory high-entropy alloys (RHEAs) beyond conventional re-
fractory metals with excellent high-temperature strength [ 1 , 2 ] and good
oxidation resistance [ 3 , 4 ] are desirable in advanced high-temperature
structural materials. For example, NbTaMoW and NbTaMoWV RHEAs
can maintain yield strength above 400 MPa at 1600
◦
C, which is
noticeably superior to the conventional refractory metals and Ni-based
superalloys [ 5 , 6 ]. However, such RHEAs suffer from high density and
brittleness at ambient temperature due to the inclusion of heavy ele-
ments such as W and Mo [ 7 – 9 ]. To solve these disadvantages, a series of
intrinsic plastic RHEAs based on lightweight refractory elements (Nb, Ti,
V, etc.) [ 10 – 13 ] have been developed, such as NbTaTiV RHEA that
exhibit considerable compressive plasticity ( > 50%) at ambient tem-
perature with acceptable density [ 14 ]. However, the strength of these
ductile RHEAs is insufficient compared with the RHEAs containing
heavy elements and needs to be further improved.
To date, many efforts such as adding principal elements and intro-
ducing hard secondary phases have been attempted to improve the
strength of the NbTaTiV RHEA [ 15 – 19 ]. The addition of W into the
NbTaTiV RHEA leads to severe microsegregation due to the huge dif-
ference in their lattice constant, which increases the strength from 965
MPa to 1420 MPa but decreases the fracture strain from over 50% to
13% [ 16 ]. The Al
2
O
3
-reinforced HfNbTaTiZrV RHEA exhibits an in-
crease in yield strength from 1986 to 2700 MPa accompanied by a
notable reduction in plastic strain [ 17 ]. In addition, by utilizing the
strengthing effects of carbide particles, researchers significantly
improved the strength of NbTaTiV RHEA at both ambient and elevated
temperatures, but also at the cost of sacrificing the ductility ( < 5%)
[ 18 , 19 ]. Hence, how to enhance the strength of these ductile RHEAs
while maintaining their ductility is an important issue.
The addition of fine-scale Y
2
O
3
particles and Ti elements to in-situ
produce nano oxide particles has been utilized as an effective method
to raise the mechanical properties of HEAs [ 20 – 23 ]. For example, the

2. Experiment procedures

The mechanical properties of materials depend on their microstruc-
ture. Specifically, for the ODS-NbTaTiV RHEA synthesized in this work,
its excellent combination of strength-ductility mainly comes from small-
sized subgrains and dispersed nano Y
2

increase in grain boundary introduced by subgrain structure not only
serves as an obstacle to dislocation movement but also disperses plastic
deformation in more grains, resulting in high strength and more uniform
plastic deformation [ 48 ]. The high-density nano Y
2

have a strong blocking effect on the dislocation motion, providing sig-
nificant strength enhancement. Meanwhile, considering its small size,
coherent interface with the matrix, and highly dispersed distribution,
the gliding dislocations would shear these nanoparticles rather than
loop them, thereby retaining the superior plasticity of the alloy [ 49 ].
Besides, the nano oxide particles are mainly distributed within the grain,
which is conducive to the generation and accumulation of dislocations
within the grain, rather than causing cracks along the grain boundary,
and also helps to maintain the plasticity [ 50 ].
The ODS-NbTaTiV RHEA exhibits a synergistic increase in strength
and ductility as the sintering temperature increases rather than the
strength-ductility trade-off. The phenomenon is mainly attributed to the
reduction of pores, homogenization of composition, and precipitation of
nanoparticles. The increase in sintering temperature from 1300

(1) Novel ODS-NbTaTiV RHEAs with multiscale oxides were syn-
