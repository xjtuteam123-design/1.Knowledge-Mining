Abstract 
When nickel-base superalloys deform by creep in a high temperature atmosphere, ouboidal shaped strengthening precipitates of 
7’ phase usually grow in the direction perpendicular to the loading axis. The mechanism of this ‘rafting’ phenomenon is studied 
in terms of the movement, reactions and accumulation of dislocations in y channels. Characteristics of the dislocations which 
accumulate on the y-y’ interfaces are discussed and the internal stresses generated by the accumulation of the glide dislocations 
are analyzed in terms of the geometrically necessary dislocations which accompany the gradient of the plastic shear strain. 0 1997

1. Introduction 
Nickel-base superalloys are widely used as a gas 
turbine blade material and their integrity during ser- 
vice is important. These superalloys consist of a 
cuboidal shaped 3” phase of an intermetallic com- 
pound and a 7 phase matrix of random alloy. The y’ 
phase is embedded in the y phase in a periodic man- 
ner, but after a short period of creep deformation at 
high temperature, the 7’ phase grows in the direction 
perpendicular to the loading axis [l-3]. This phe- 
nomenon is called ‘rafting’. So far, the mechanism of 
rafting has been discussed in terms of the minimiza- 
tion of the total elastic strain energy with respect to 
the shape of 7’ precipitates [4-61. The internal 
stresses generated by the accumulation of dislocations 
in y channels and their effect on the rafting phe- 
nomenon have also been discussed [7-121. 
Recently, we proposed a model for rafting [I 31 in 
which a difference in free energies at the channels of 
y phase parallel and perpendicular to the loading axis 
arose and it caused different local equilibriums be- 
tween y and . The different local equilibrium states

by glide dislocations are evaluated by assuming an 
equal distribution of plastic distortion in the y channels. 
But, in the actual slip process, the movement of disloca- 
tions is governed by the elastic stress field which is 
inherently non-uniform [ 131. Then, a different amounts 
of plastic slip and dislocation accumulations will take 
place in the ‘J channels perpendicular and parallel to the 
loading axis. If we assume, for example, 1.5 times wider 
spacing between dislocations in the vertical channels 
compared to that in the horizontal channels 1221, the

accumulation of strain energy in the y channels under a 
non-uniform distribution of elastic stresses. not only 
experimental but also analytical or numerical studies

5. Summary 
Movement and reactions of dislocations in y channels 
of nickel-base superalloys were studied and the internal 
stresses generated by the accumulation of the glide 
dislocations were discussed in terms of the geometri- 
cally necessary dislocations which accompany the gra- 
dient of the plastic shear strain. The following 
conclusions were obtained. 
(1) Characteristics of the dislocations differed be- 
tween those which accumulate on the y-y’ interfaces 
perpendicular to the loading axis and those which 
accumulate on the interfaces parallel to the loading 
axis. In the 3 channels perpendicular to the loading 
axis, reactions of partial dislocations took place and the 
misfit dislocations of pure edge type were formed. In 
the 7 channels parallel to the loading axis, dislocations 
dissolved into 30”-mixed and pure edge types and they 
accumulated on the interfaces. 
(2) The internal stresses due to the dislocation accu- 
mulation cancelled the elastic stresses of the negative 
lattice misfit in the y channels perpendicular to the 
loading axis, while in the 7 channels parallel to the 
loading axis, dislocation stresses were added to the 
misfit stresses. The elastic strain energy obtained from 
the total stresses of centrifugal force, lattice misfit and 
the dislocations was 1-2.1 MJ m- 3 which is sufficient 
to explain the rate of rafting.
