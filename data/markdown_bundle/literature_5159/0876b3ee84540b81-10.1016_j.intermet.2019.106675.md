5. Conclusions 
Compression experiments have revealed strong influences of 
composition and temperature on the mechanical behavior of B2-ordered 
Ti – Mo – Al alloys. The stoichiometric composition showed positive tem -
perature dependence of yield strength at 1073 – 1273 K while the off- 
stoichiometric compositions showed a continuous decline in strength 
with increasing temperature. TEM analyses of dislocations suggest the 
deformation of B2-ordered Ti – Mo – Al alloys is dominated by < 111 >
slip at elevated temperatures.
