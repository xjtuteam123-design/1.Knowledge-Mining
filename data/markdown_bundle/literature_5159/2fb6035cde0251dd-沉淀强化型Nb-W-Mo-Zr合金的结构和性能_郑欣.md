Microtructure and Properties of Deposit Strengthened Nb-W-Mo-Zr-C Alloy 
 
Zheng Xin, Zhang Tingjie, Li Zhongkui, Chen Shaokai, Ding Xu, Yang Xiongmao, Fu Jie 
(Northwest Institute for Nonferrous Metal Research, Xi’an 710016, China) 
 
Abstract: The microstructure characteristic s by solution and precipitation strengthenings for Nb-W alloy are introduced in this paper. It is 
found that the solution plusing aging treatments make carbide prec ipitates fine and uniform. Tensile test indicates that the st rengthening 
effects are notable at room temperature and high temperature. Al l kinds of possible strengthening mechanisms and their influenc e are also 
discussed. 
Key words: Nb-W alloy; solution strengthening; precipitation strengthening; microstructure and property
