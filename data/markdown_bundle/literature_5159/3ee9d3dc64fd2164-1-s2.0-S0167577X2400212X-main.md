1. Introduction 
Eutectic medium/high-entropy alloys (EM/HEAs), combining the 
advantages of both eutectic alloys and medium/high-entropy alloys, 
possess good formability and superior comprehensive mechanical 
properties which are regarded as a revolutionary new materials. 
Therefore, since the eutectic alloy concept is firstly proposed by Lu et al. 
[1] to develop multi-phase M/HEAs, there are many kinds of EM/HEAs 
have been designed and successfully prepared by casting technique, 
powder metallury and additive manufacture, such as FCC/B2, FCC/ 
Laves and BCC/B2-structured M/EHEAs [2] . Powder plasma arc addi -
tive manufacturing (PPA-AM) as one of AM technologies with the 
characteristics of high energy input and low cast, which can effectively 
fuse high melting point metal materials and it is widely adopted into 
produce EM/HEAs. Zhang et al. [3] fabricated CoCrFeNiX
0.4 
(X = Al, Nb,

stability in FCC/laves alloys. Shen et al. [4] investigated the effect of 
thermal cycling and heat treatment on the mechanical properties and 
microstructure of Al

HEA by using PPA-AM. But there is 
less research for study PPA-AMed FCC/B2 EMEA. In this work, we 
summarized the relationship between Al or Cr element and the ratio of 
Co + Ni/Al + Cr in EM/HEAs, preliminarily determined the Al and Cr 
contents, and then combined the JMatPro software to calculate the so -

EMEA alloy of Al

3. Results and discussion 
Based on previous research on predicting eutectics in the 
Al – Co – Cr – Fe – Ni system, it was found that Ni and Co can be considered 
as a whole, while Cr was closely related to the critical Al element for BCC 
eutectic phase formation [5] . In this study, the alloys were designed by 
retaining Al content consistent with Cr content in CoNi matrix. Fig. 1 (a) 
illustrates the relationship diagram between (Co + Ni)/(Al + Cr) ratio 
and Al (Cr) element in Ni-based eutectic system. The (Co + Ni)/(Al + Cr) 
ratio is concentrated between 1 and 2.5. Additionally, the eutectic 
composition falls within a narrow range, with Al content ranging from 
15 % to 21 % and Cr content ranging from 10 % to 26 %. The inter -
section points of the Al and Cr composition fitting line within the 
eutectic region, Al = 17 % and Cr = 17 % are selected as the initial 
composition, resulting in the new alloy composition Al
