ABSTRACT
At present, titanium alloy is widely used in the field of materials.
Because of its high specific strength and good corrosion resistance,
it is also widely used in sports equipment. To overcome the short-
comings of traditional sports equipment, such as low yield and weak
strength, the superplastic deformation effect of titanium alloy was
tested and analyzed in this paper. The superplastic strength of titan-
ium alloy was adjusted by adjusting the structure of the material. In
this paper, by adjusting the proportion of Ti, Mo, alloy, CD, and Fe,
the inductively coupled parameters of sports equipment were
improved by vacuum consumable melting. The macro high tempera-
ture tensile test of sports equipment was carried out, and the tem-
perature and strain rate under different states were recorded. Finally,
the mechanism of superplastic deformation of titanium alloy for
sports equipment is analyzed. The results show that the superplastic
titanium alloy has higher elongation after fracture at high tempera-
ture and lower strain rate. The elongation ratio is more than 90%
between 500
/C14C and 800 /C14C. At high temperature, the corrosion
resistance of titanium alloy is high a, The number of phases
decreases, and the alloy particles change from short rod to equiaxed.
When the strain rate is reduced, the mechanical properties of titan-
ium alloy are improved a, phase increases, the b the number of
phases decreased. From its mechanism, the elongation of titanium
alloy is caused by the dislocation movement caused by its structure.
Therefore, titanium alloy in sports equipment can generally increase
the short axis shape to improve the superplasticity of titanium alloy.

2. Titanium Alloy Materials and Superplasticity Evaluation Methods
2.1. Superplasticity of Titanium Alloy
TC4 titanium alloy is one of the most widely used titanium alloys with good compre-
hensive properties and weldability [ 11]. Titanium alloy T-joints are often used in

important load-bearing structures, which have high requirements for superplastic qual-
ity, joint performance, and deformation. TIG welding is one of the most widely used T-
joint welding processes of titanium alloys [ 12]. The welding heat source of this process
is stable and the superplastic forming is beautiful, but the welding efficiency is low and
the penetration ability is weak [ 13]. Large groove, multi-layer and multi-pass welding
method is needed in T-joint welding of titanium alloy plate, which leads to low welding
efficiency and large deformation after welding. Compared with TIG welding, laser weld-

high welding efficiency and small deformation after welding [ 15]. In this paper, the full
penetration test of 16 ? 20 mm TC4 titanium alloy T-type superplasticity without
groove was carried out by using high-power laser [ 16]. After welding, the superplastic
appearance and joint section morphology were detected and analyzed; at the same time,
with the help of high-power metallographic microscope, the microstructure and forma-
tion process of T-joints at different positions were analyzed [ 17].
According to the characteristics of electromagnetic forming process, it is suitable for
forming metal materials with good conductivity (such as gold, silver, copper, aluminum,
and their alloys), but for forming metal materials and nonmetal materials with poor
conductivity, the driving sheet or driving layer is needed [ 18– 19]. At present, the high
conductivity material is mainly used as the driving sheet, or a certain thickness of con-
ductive layer is coated on the side of the blank close to the coil [ 20]. In electromagnetic
forming, the energy stored in the capacitor will be dissipated in different forms during
the discharge process, only part of which is used for the deformation of the workpiece,
and different discharge parameters (capacitance C, voltage U and discharge frequency f)
will affect the forming efficiency. When the driving plate is used to drive the electro-
magnetic pulse forming, the different material and thickness of the driving plate will
also affect the electromagnetic forming efficiency [ 21]. Therefore, to improve the effi-
ciency of energy utilization, the appropriate driving plate (material and thickness, etc.)
should be selected when the process parameters of the forming equipment are deter-
mined [ 22].

3.2. Experimental Design
In this paper, the test sample is a right angle structure workpiece made of titanium alloy
sheet by right angle cross welding. The thickness of the test sample is 3 mm, and a rect-
angular groove is embedded in the vertical superplasticity of the cross structure to simu-
late the defect. The length, width, and depth of defect 1 are 3.0 mm, 0.3 mm, and
1.5 mm, respectively, which are used to simulate the crack defect. The length, width,
and depth of defect 2 are 3.0 mm, 0.3 mm, and 3.0 mm, respectively, the groove length
is along the superplastic extension direction. To ensure the detection accuracy, a 6-DOF
manipulator is used as the scanner. The computer controls the manipulator controller
to change the position and posture of the end fixture of the manipulator, and the trans-
ducer probe is clamped for scanning detection. At the same time, using water as the
wedge material, it is convenient to change the wedge parameters and detection scheme
flexibly, and the wedge design steps and delay time calculation process
remain unchanged.

4.2. Discussion
The tangential velocity of titanium alloy sports equipment has a great influence on the
thread forming quality. When the tangential velocity is small, the thread forming is
complete. With the increase of the tangential velocity, the forming quality becomes
worse. Through the simulation and analysis of thread rolling forming under different
tangential speeds, the optimal tangential speed of TC4 titanium alloy sports equipment
is 300 mm/s. The radial feed speed of titanium alloy sports equipment will not affect the
thread forming quality, but will affect the production efficiency. Through the simulation
and analysis of thread rolling forming under different radial feed speeds, it is deter-
mined that the larger the radial feed speed of TC4 titanium alloy sports equipment is,
the better without affecting the equipment. In the process of thread rolling, the friction
coefficient has a great influence on the quality of thread forming. If the friction coeffi-
cient is too small, it is easy to slip. If the friction coefficient is too large, the material

5. Conclusions
Taking the superplastic forming of TC4 titanium alloy sports equipment as the research
object, this paper establishes a three-dimensional finite element model of thread rolling
processing and analyzes the influence of radial feed speed, tangential speed and friction
coefficient of titanium alloy sports equipment on thread forming. Through comparative
analysis, the ideal processing parameters are obtained. At the same time, when using
the conventional alloy phase detection method to detect the right angle superplasticity
of thin plate, it needs to arrange a number of alloy phase probes with different angles
to cover the whole superplasticity area, which has the problems of low detection effi-
ciency and poor signal-to-noise ratio. Compared with conventional alloy phase detection
technology, phased array alloy phase detection technology has the characteristics of flex-
ible and controllable sound beam, large coverage, and high resolution because it uses
the corresponding excitation delay signal to control multiple array elements. In recent
years, phased array alloy phase detection technology has been gradually applied to
industrial nondestructive testing fields such as engine blades, pressure vessel, and com-
posite L-shaped components, and it provides a new method and idea for testing the
right angle superplasticity of thin plate. At present, in the aspect of superplasticity
detection, some foreign research institutions have formed a set of standard processes for
phased array alloy phase simulation, parameter optimization, defect identification, and
application. Therefore, how to control and improve the macro-and micro-forming qual-
ity of locally loaded components and realize the low-cost forming integrated manufac-
turing of forgings need further research.
