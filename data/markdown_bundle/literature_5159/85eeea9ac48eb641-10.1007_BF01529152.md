INFLUENCE OF STRESS CONCENTRATION ON THE FATIGUE RESISTANCE OF 
HIGH-TEMPERATURE STRENGTH NICKEL ALLOYS IN ASYMMETRIC MULTICYCLE 
LOADING. REPORT 2 
B. N. Sinaiskii and M. S. Belyaev UDC 620.178.38:539.43 
Determination of the limiting state of high-temperature strength nickel alloys with stress 
concentration with multicycle loading and the complex action of cyclic and static loads is 
an important question from the scientific and applied points of view. As the result of the 
length and laboriousness of fatigue tests, the obtaining of experimental information in the 
necessary quantity is difficult. 
In the literature sources there is little data on the limiting state of high-temperature 
strength nickel alloys at high temperatures under conditions of multicycle loading of un- 
notched specimens and specimens with stress concentration enveloping the range of failure 
stresses from only the cyclic component a a (peak factor A = aa/a m = ~) to failure from the 
static component a m (A = 0) [1-4]. 
The complexity of analysis of the stressed deformed state at the tip of a notch, espe- 
cially under high temperature conditions (T > 0.5 Tmelt) , when creep of the material occurs and 
stress relaxation is possible, has been responsible for attempts to evaluate multicycle fatigue 
in the presence of stress concentration on the bais of simplified empirical relationships. 
The evaluation of the suitability of one or another theortical relationship is based on an 
analysis and comparison with experimental results [3-7]. 
In this work the results presented in the first report [8] of investigations of the fa- 
tigue resistance of unnotched and notched speicmens (~a ~ 2.45) of EP-109 alloy at 800~ with 
different discrete values of the ratios of the cyclic and static components of the load were 
analyzed with the use of graphic representation of analytical relationships of the form a= =

CONCLUSIONS 
i. The use of Eq. (3) with the factor ~ equal to unity for description of the aa - a m 
limiting stress curves for specimens with stress concentration of ~P109, S-816, and N-155 
high-temperature strength wrought-nickel alloys at high temperature (T > 0.5 Tmelt) is ap- 
proximate. The factor ~ depends upon the peak factor A with a weak relationship to life. 
2. A modification of Eq. (3) is proposed for description of the limiting condition of 
high-temperature strength wrought-nickel alloys with stress concentration in multicycle load- 
ing with the use of the two factors ~= and ~m for the two areas of the limiting stress curves 
with primary fatigue failure (A > Acr) and failure as the result of cyclic creep (A < Acr). 
3. An experimental-calculation method of construction of limiting stress curves is pro- 
posed for specimens with stress concentration with the use of which, on the basis of experimen- 
tal data obtained for a limited base, the curves for longer (by one or two orders of magnitude) 
bases may be determined by calculation. 
The original data for calculation are the limiting stress curves for unnotched specimens 
and the boundary values of O_ic and Omc for the sought-for base, obtained with the assumption 
of use of the fatigue curves in a symmetric cycle without breaks, and also the life-independ- 
ent linear relationship ~a = a + b arctan A with the factors a and b experimentally determined 
for short bases.
