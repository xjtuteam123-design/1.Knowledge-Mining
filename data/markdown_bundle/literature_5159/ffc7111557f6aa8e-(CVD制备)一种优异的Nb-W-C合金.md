ABSTRACT 
A new kind of Nb-W-C alloy with special microstructure and excellent mechanical property was designed and 
prepared by CVD subsequent with homogenizing heat treatment. Its microstructure is composed of columnar 
crystals with specific orientation, needle-like Nb
2
C second phase and layered structure with Nb/W composition 
segregation. By analyzing the effect of homogenizing heat treatment on the property and microstructure of the 
alloy, it was found that the as-deposited layered structure was retained in the microstructure after heat treatment 
at 1400 
◦
C, but disappeared by treated at 1600 
◦
C. The layered structure can effectively separate Nb
2
C and 
inhibit the size coarsening of Nb
2
C during carbide formation. During the homogenization process, needle-like 
carbides can maintain the strengthening effect under the condition of slight grain growth, but excessively 
high homogenization heat treatment temperature coarsens the grains and carbides, and ultimately weakens the 
strength of the alloy. The comprehensive strengthening effect of grain refinement and carbide pinning makes the 
room temperature strength of the sample reach 670 MPa after homogenizing treatment at 1400 
◦
C for 6 h, which 
is about 35 – 45% higher than the as-deposited Nb-W-C alloy and the existing widely used Nb521 alloy produced 
by melting-forging-annealing technology. 
1. Introduction 
Niobium (Nb) has the characteristics of low density (8.57 g / cm
3
), 
high melting point (2741 K), high plasticity, low vapor pressure and 
good corrosion resistance against liquid metal corrosion. Elevated 
temperature niobium-based alloy has good high temperature specific 
strength and processing performance, which can be used to make parts 
with complex shapes. It is one of the important candidate materials for 
aerospace structural parts [1 – 5] . Traditional niobium-based alloys have 
been used in aerospace and nuclear industries since the 1960 s [6] , 
among which C-103 (Nb-10Hf-1Ti) alloy was the most widely used 
[7 – 9] . However, C-103 alloy is highly sensitive to oxygen, which easily 
leads to decrease in mechanical performance [10] . Since the 1990 s, 
with the development of space technology, higher requirements have 
been put forward for Nb-based high temperature structural materials. At 
present, there are dozens of Nb alloys used in aerospace industry, which 
are mainly strengthened by adding W, Mo, Zr and Hf. The addition of W 
and Mo elements in the alloy can form a replacement solid solution 
because of their high melting point and similar atomic radius, so as to 
improve the room and high temperature strength of the alloy matrix. 
Nb521 （ Nb-5 W-2Mo-1Zr ） alloy, as a representative of Nb alloys with 
moderate strength and plasticity, its room temperature strength is 
400 – 600 MPa, and its high temperature strength is higher at 
1000 – 1400 
◦
C [11 – 13] . 
And the addition of Zr and C can further improve the strength of the 
alloy, because C is easy to form a dispersed precipitation strengthening 
phase with Zr and Nb [14 – 16] . The strength of niobium alloy can be 
effectively improved by stabilizing fine carbides. The typical PWC-11 
(Nb-1Zr-0.1 C) alloy is realized by adding Zr and C elements, its good 
high temperature creep resistance is related to carbides. Therefore, the

However, due to the extremely high melting point of refractory 
alloying elements, the traditional melting process of the refractory alloy 
is usually complex and difficult. Chemical vapor deposition (CVD) [21] 
has the characteristics of high purity, uniform density, controllable 
thickness, controllable crystal structure and relatively low cost. Ac -
cording to the temperature of CVD process is much lower than the 
melting point of the material, and the products are pure and free of 
impurities, it has great advantages compared with powder metallurgy 
and melting methods. Auck et al. [22] has successfully prepared W-22% 
Re alloy by CVD at different annealing temperatures and time. Wei et al. 
[23] studied the CVD of Nb on Mo substrate under different deposition 
variables, the deposited Nb has high purity and compact structure, and 
the grain size can be controlled by adjusting the deposition parameters. 
Based on the above analysis of Nb-based structural alloys, refractory 
W and C were designed as alloying elements of Nb in this study, and CVD 
combined with homogenization heat treatment technology was adopted 
to produce a new kind of Nb-W-C alloy with excellent mechanical 
property. By characterizing the effect of microstructure evolution on 
mechanical properties of as-deposited and heat-treated alloys, the 
strengthening control factors of Nb-W-C alloy were clarified, and the 
microstructure optimization was guided to obtain performance 
improvement.

3.1. Microstructure evolution 
Fig. 4 illustrates typical metallography of the samples as-deposited 
and homogenizing-treated at different temperatures. In this figure, 
microstructure of the as-deposited sample and the samples treated at 
1400

phase. As shown in metallography images, the dark gray structure is 
second phase, the white bright structure is (Nb,W)
ss

structure runs through Nb-W grains and is perpendicular to the depo -
sition direction. When the homogenizing temperature increases from 
1400

morphology and distribution of the second phase also change signifi -

microstructure gradually grow from needle-like to long rod-like with the 
increase of homogenization temperature and holding time. At 1600
◦

the layered structure disappears. The higher temperature contributes to 
the redistribution and homogenization of Nb and W elements, making 
the composition gradient in the layered structure disappear. And the 
number and size of the second phase particles increases significantly. 
This is because after holding at a higher temperature, the elements are 
fully dissolved in the grains and promote precipitation during the sub -
sequent slow cooling process. 
Fig. 5 shows the electron channeling contrast imaging (ECCI) 
morphology of Nb-W-C alloy obtained by different heat treatments. The 
ECCI technique can not only obtain the atomic number contrast image 
(Z-contrast), but also provide the information of grain orientation dif -
ference, which can better characterize the microstructure characteris -
tics. In recent years, it has been well applied in metal material 
characterization [24,25] . 
Fig. 5 clearly shows that the grain size, grain orientation and layered 
composition gradient structure have changed significantly after 
different heat treatments. Under the same accelerating voltage, with the 
increase of solution temperature and holding time, the composition 
gradient structure in ECCI images disappears gradually. At the same 
time, the grain size gradually increases, accompanied by grain orienta -
tion transformation. The length and width of columnar grains in

grains gradually swallow up the small grains through the migration of 
grain boundaries, and the grains are coarsened.

(1) A new kind of Nb-W-C alloy with special structure was prepared
