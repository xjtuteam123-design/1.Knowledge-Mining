Abstract 
The investigation involves the fabrication of various nano oxide (Y2O3, Al2O3, La 2O3) dispersed 
W-Ni alloys by mechanical alloying for 10 h and sintering in Ar atmosphere at 1400 °C, 1500 °C 
for 2 h . The selected composition for the study is W 89Ni10(Y2O3)1 (alloy A), W89Ni10(Al2O3)1 
(alloy B) and W 89Ni10(La2O3)1 (alloy C) (in weight%). Alloy A exhibit least crystallite size and 
maximum lattice strain at 10 h of milling. The lattice parameter of W exhibit s expansion and 
contraction behavior during the milling. Microstructure of sintered alloys reveals the presence of 
both faceted and nearly spherical shaped grains. Bimodal grain size distribution is higher in alloy 
A at 1500 °C as compared to other alloys and 1400 °C sintering temperature. Texture study and 
Young’s Modulus value reveals that hardness of alloy A is higher against alloy B and alloy C . 
Maximum % relative sintered density, mean hardness, compressive strength of 89%, 5.53 GPa, 
2.25 GPa respectively has been achieved in alloy A at 1500 °C. 
Keywords: W-Ni alloy; Nano oxides; Mechanical alloying; TEM; Strength; Fractography.

Tungsten (W) is the major research attraction in recent times for high temperature applications 
owing to its superlative properties as compared to other refractory materials [1, 2]. Application 
of W in rocket nozzle, radiation shielding in nuclear reactor, kinetic energy penetrator in defense 
sector urges appreciable ductility and oxidation resistance. However, several research reports 
indicates poor ductility and oxidation resistance of pure W at elevated temperature [3, 4] , which 
leads to the development of W based alloys. Moreover, wear and oxidation is a major problem in 
W based rocket nozzles which alters t he dimension and significantly reduces the effectiveness [5, 
6]. W oxidizes at the rate of 1.4 × 10 -2 mg cm-2 s-1 at 1000°C and significantly increases with 
increase in temperature [7] unless any protective coating is provided. The intense oxidation leads 
to blistering and spallation of component during high temperature operation. Ni is a common 
addition in W to counteract the ductility issue whereas Cr, Pd, Y elements enhances the 
oxidation resistance of W by providing a protective layer and reduces the volatilization of WO3 
[8-11]. Sintering of W -Ni alloys at higher temperature than the melting point of Ni further 
enriches the densification through liquid phase sintering. Dispersing nano oxides is significant 
with respect to strengthening and minimize oxidation of W based alloys [12, 13 ]. However, 
selection of oxide to tailor the final property and fabrication cost is also essential. The nano-
oxides refine the particle size during mechani cal alloying and enrich the densification during 
sintering [12]. Recent study illustrates that dispersed oxides are located in both grain boundary 
and grain interior area [ 14]. Subsequently, bimodal grain size distribution to achieve a tradeoff 
between strength and ductility in oxide dispersion strengthened (ODS) W alloys i s also reported 
in several literatures [1 5, 16]. Strengthening by dispersed oxide at elevated temperature is 
influenced by the free energy of formation or stability [ 17]. Fig. 1(a -c) s hows the Gibbs free 
energy formation of Y2O3, Al2O3, and La 2O3 respectively with increase in temperature [18,19]. It 
is evident that Y2O3 shows high stability as compared to Al 2O3, and La 2O3 within 1200 °C. 
Therefore dispersion of Y 2O3 in W matrix is extensively investigated owing to its stability as 
compared to other oxides . Investigation illustrates that increased concentration of oxides causes 
increase in fabrication cost and depreciation of high temperature properties [20, 21]. W-Ni-Al2O3 
alloy has also previously fabricated by chemical and mechanical alloying route with significantly 
higher Al2O3 particle size with respect to current study [ 22, 2 3]. Genc et al. ha ve also fabricated 
W-1 wt.% Ni- 0.5- wt.% Y 2O3 alloy through mechanical alloying route by using coarse (8 µ m) 
Y2O3 particles [2 4]. Initial size of the oxide particles significantly influences the ball milling

Present study aims at fabrication of 1 wt. % nano oxide (Y2O3, Al2O3, and La 2O3) dispersed W-

The consolidation temperature has been selected to achieve a synergy between liquid phase 
sintering and mechanical properties. Further lowering the sin tering temperature for the alloys 
milled for 10 h may refine the grain size at a cost of reduction in liquid phase formation which 
will impact the densification. The consolidated alloys are subjected to microstructural and 
mechanical characterization. Exploration of texture evolution in the sintered alloys has also been 
carried out. 
 
2. Materials and Method 
Mechanical alloying of W (Sigma Aldrich, particle size: 0.6 -1 µ m), Ni ( Sigma Aldrich, particle 
size: < 50 µ m), Y 2O3 (Sigma Aldrich, particle size: < 50 nm), Al 2O3 (Sigma Aldrich, particle 
size: <50 nm), La 2O3 (Sigma Aldrich, particle size: < 100 nm) power has been carried out in a 
high energy planetary ball mill (Fritsch, P5) for 10 h to minimize contamination and overwelding 
of particles. The other milling parameters details are provided in previous report [ 25]. The 
elemental powder composition for mechanical alloying is presented in table 1. Powders have 
been ejected from the milling container at 1, 5 , 10 h of milling for investigation of phase 
evolution by X -ray diffraction (XRD) and microstructural changes by Scanning electron 
microscope (SEM) (JEOL, JSM -6084LV, accelerating voltage : 20 kV) with milling time. The 
10 h milled powers are also studied by high resolution transmission electron microscope 
HRTEM (JEOL, JEM 2100, accelerating voltage 300 kV). Measurement of crystallite size, 
lattice strain and lattice parameter is according to earlier literatures [26,27]. 
The 10 h milled powders are compacted in a hydraulic press by applying 500 MPa pressure and 
5 min of dwell time. The compacted cylindrical pellets of 10 mm diameter has been consolidated

points to the enhanced strength of the alloy as higher energy required for transgranular cracking 
mode. The coefficient of thermal expansion of W is comparatively less than dispersed Y 2O3 
[51, 52] leading to significant compressive stress generation in the matrix and improve the 
strength of the W matrix [53]. Porosities along the crack propagation route are evident in alloy C 
sintered at 1400 °C (Fig. 22 (f)). The stress concentration around the porosities further facilitates 
the movement of crack path. The porosities arises as inherent open pores after sintering or 
formation during compression study due to varied deformation behavior of adjacent matrix [54]. 
Closure of crack tip in alloy C at 1500 ° C owing to low interfacial energy results in augmented 
toughness of the alloy [53]. Discontinuous crack propagation or blunting of crack inside matrix 
(arrow marked) indicates plastic deformation at the crack -tip which further suggests ductile 
fracture of matrix. The brittle oxide particles may increases the fracturing tendency by inducing 
stress concentration at matrix/oxide interface. Deflection of crack is also observed in alloy C at 
1500 °C (Fig. 2 3(e)) owing to the presence of hard oxide particles. Increase de flection is a sign 
of absorption of higher energy and increase in fracture toughness of the material [ 55, 56]. 
Coarser grain size at 1500 °C may retain the crack inside or delay the propagation and thereby 
improves the ductility of the matrix [14]. The crack width of alloy C at the circle marked region 
in Fig. 23(e) changes from 0.22 µ m to 0.086 µ m suggests reduction in crack growth rate. Present

4. Conclusions 
Nano-Y2O3, Al 2O3, La 2O3 dispersed W-Ni alloys have been synthesized by mechanical alloying 
along with consolidation by pressureless sintering method in inert (Ar) atmosphere. Minimum 
crystallite size of W and maximum strain on W lattice is imposed by Y 2O3 dispersion. Formation 
of faceted and spherical sintered grain morphology reveal s the incidence of liquid and solid 
phases. The oxide particles are dispersed both at grain boundary and inside the W matrix, both 
contributing to the strengthening. Y2O3 dispersed W -Ni alloy exhibit superior densification, 
hardness and strength owing to enhanced fraction of finer grains (enriched grain boundary 
diffusion), solid solution strengthening and dispersion strengthening (grain boundary pinning) as 
compared to Al2O3, La2O3 dispersed W-Ni alloy at all sintering temperature. 
5. Novelty of the Work 
The investigation shows encouraging prospects of ODS W alloys with high strength, hardness, 
for structural applications. Bimodal grain size distribution and oxide dispersion inside W matrix 
has a positive influence on mechanical properties of the alloys. The results also show that 
dispersed Y 2O3 and Al2O3 is potential candidate for improving the mechanical properties of W 
alloys.
