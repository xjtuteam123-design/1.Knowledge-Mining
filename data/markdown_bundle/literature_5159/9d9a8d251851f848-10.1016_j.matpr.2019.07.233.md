abstract
The paper investigated the inﬂuence of the parameters of thermomechanical processing on the structure
formation in rods of 16 mm in diameter from ( a + b) and near- b-titanium alloys. It is shown that chang-
ing the temperature and speed modes of deformation and heat treatment of alloys makes it possible to
create structural and phase states which provide both enhanced ductility and increased strength at cer-
tain stages of production. A treatment mode has been developed for rods of VT16 and VT35 high-strength
titanium alloys, which ensures high manufacturability of semi-ﬁnished products at room temperature
and high operational durability for ﬁnished products.

1. Introduction
The connection of various structural elements of the airframe in
aircraft is carried out using a variety of fasteners (rivets, bolts).
These products must combine high manufacturability and speciﬁc
strength at room temperature, resistance to corrosion, cyclic and
other loads [1]. The complex of all requirements is most satisﬁed
with high-strength titanium alloys.
At present, the majority of fasteners are made of VT16 alloy by
cold reduction and upsetting [2]. VT16 alloy is well developed by
the metallurgical industry, although all possibilities of improving
the technology and increasing the complex of its operational prop-
erties are almost exhausted. And the development of aviation tech-
nology is on the way to reducing the weight of aircraft structures
and at the same time requires the use of fastening parts with
higher performance [3]. In this regard, the near- b-titanium alloys,
in particular, VT35 alloy, are becoming promising than ( a + b) tita-
nium alloys.
Despite VT35 alloy was developed quite a long time ago, it is not
fully adopted by the industry since it contains large quantities of
relatively scarce and heavy alloying elements. Also, during crystal-
lization ingots from this alloy are prone to dendritic segregation
[4]. At the same time, VT35 alloy has a high hardenability. In the
quenched state it is capable of cold plastic deformation, and aging
leads to signiﬁcant hardening while maintaining high fracture
toughness [5].
Therefore, in this work, we investigated the inﬂuence of
temperature-rate parameters of thermomechanical and heat treat-
ments on the regularities of phase composition and structure for-
mation in order to develop processing modes that provide the
required complex of technological and operational mechanical
properties in rods of VT16 and VT35 alloys.

3. Results and discussion
A batch of VT16 and VT35 alloys rods with a diameter of 18–
9 mm was made. The technological scheme for manufacturing rods
included forging in the b phase ﬁeld at temperatures of 50 /C176/C460 /C176C
above the polymorphic transformation temperature and subse-
quent rolling. Rolling of VT16 alloy was carried out in the upper
temperature range of ( a + b) phase ﬁeld, and VT35 alloy was rolled
in the lower temperature range of the b phase ﬁeld. Metallographic
studies of rods after thermomechanical processing showed that the
structure typical for this class of alloys was formed. Thus, the
deformed state of VT16 alloy rods is presented by primary globular
particles of the a-phase which remain in the structure during
deformation in ( a + b)-ﬁled, and secondary a-phase plates precip-
itated from the b-phase during cooling to room temperature
(Fig. 1a, b). The structure of the VT35 alloy rods after hot deforma-
tion is represented only by b-phase grains, since during air-cooling
b-phase does not undergo decomposition due to the high content
of b-stabilizing elements ( Fig. 1 c, d). It should be noted that
decrease of the rod diameter resulted in decrease of the structural
components sizes ( Fig. 1 ).
At the next stage of research the technological plasticity and
strength of the investigated rods were determined which are the
main criteria for assessing the possibility of manufacturing fasten-
ers from a speciﬁc material by the method of cold plastic deforma-
tion. Since after hot rolling the rods are in a deformed-hardened
state, which does not allow for high technological ductility
(Table 2 ), it is necessary to conduct additional heat treatment to
improve the plastic characteristics. Therefore, the rods from VT16
alloy were subjected to stepwise annealing, and the rods from
VT35 alloy were quenched from the single b phase ﬁeld.
Analysis of the mechanical tests showed that heat treatment
allows increasing the ultimate strain (US) at upsetting to a level
being sufﬁcient for cold upset and compression operations but does
not provide the required strength and shear stress (SS) ( Table 2).
Therefore, at the next stage of the work, the effect of strength-
ening heat treatment on the structure and properties of the semi-
ﬁnished products was investigated. Based on well-known facts [6],
aging of the VT16 and VT35 alloys was carried out at temperatures
of 450 /C176C and 475 /C176C, respectively. The total holding time for each
alloy was 35 h.
