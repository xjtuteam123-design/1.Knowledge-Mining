EFFECT OF HIGH-TEMPERATURE GAS MEDIUM ON THE STRUCTURE
AND MECHANICAL CHARACTERISTICS OF NIOBIUM ALLOY 10VMTs
V . V . Bukhanovskii1 and I. Mamuzi æ2
Translated from Metallovedenie i Termicheskaya Obrabotka Metallov, No. 3, pp. 31 – 37, March, 2008.
The structure, the characteristics of short-term and long-term static strength, the per second creep, and the duc-
tility of niobium alloy 10VTs are studied in the as-delivered state and after treatment by a high-temperature
aggressive gas medium in a temperature range of 20 – 2000°C.
INTRODUCTION
Niobium and its alloys occupy an important place among
refractory metallic materials used in aircraft and spacecraft
engineering [1 – 3]. The main obstacle preventing wide use
of niobium alloys for manufacturing parts and structure com-
ponents that serve under the action of high-temperature ag-
gressive and oxidizing gas media is their low high-tempera-
ture strength. For this reason niobium alloys are covered with
refractory coatings based on molybdenum and hafnium sili-
cides [4 – 6]. Deposition of protective coatings onto refrac -
tory materials is a complex and long process that requires
special equipment and is quite costly. In addition, niobium
alloys with silicide-ceramic coatings have a coarse crystal
structure and lower strength and ductility characteristics than
the materials in the initial state [7].
The use of refractory metals and alloys with protective
coatings is expedient when the design life of the part or the
structural component is equal to tens or hundreds of hours
and the operating mode involves repeated temperature and
force loading. However, in spacecraft engineering the service
life of some disposable structures operating in high-tempera-
ture gas media lasts for from several tens seconds to several
tens minutes. In this case niobium alloys can be used in the
initial state without refractory coating.
The aim of the present work consisted in studying the ef-
fect of a high-temperature aggressive gas medium (products
of combustion of jet engine fuel) on the structure and me -
chanical characteristics of niobium alloy 10VMTs under
short-term and long-term static loading for low time bases
and a wide temperature range.
METHODS OF STUDY
Alloy 10VMTs (TU 48-0533-108–84) of the N b–W–
Mo – Zr system is one of the most refractory intermediate-al-
loy niobium alloys with solid-solution hardening. Due to the
optimum content of alloying elements (10 – 12% W,
1.7 – 2.2% Mo, 0.5 – 1.0% Zr, 0.02% C) it possesses a
unique set of physicochemical, mechanical, and process
properties with favorable combination of refractoriness and
low-temperature ductility and adaptability to mechanical
treatment and welding [1 – 4]. We studied an alloy obtained
by the method of vacuum-arc melting with subsequent hot
deformation for sheets with a thickness of 1 mm. In the final
stage of fabrication the alloy was annealed in a vacuum fur -
nace at 1400°C for 2 h for leveling the structural and chemi -
cal inhomogeneity and removing the stresses. In the as-deli -
vered state sheets from alloy 10MVTs are isotropic and have
a recrystallized and fine-grained structure [1 – 3, 8].
The structure of alloy 10VMTs in the initial state after
operating in high-temperature aggressive gas medium and
after mechanical tests was studied by methods of light and
electron transmission microscopy on metallographic speci -
mens with the help of two-stage plastic-and carbon replicas
under “Neophot-2 and ÉM-100LM microscopes respec -
tively.3 The microhardness of the alloy was determined using
a PMT-3 microhardness meter and making at least 10 – 15
measurements both on the surface of sheet specimens and on

The microhardness of alloy 10VMTs in the initial state
was 162 kgf/c47mm2. For specimens subjected to the action of
high-temperature gas medium the microhardness measured
on the surface of the sheet was 600 kgf /c47mm2. However, the
measurement made on a transverse microsection of the same
specimen gave the same value of 164 kgf /c47mm2 over the
whole of the thickness of the sheet, i.e., the microhardness
was almost as in the initial state. This means that under the
used conditions simulating the operating mode of some com-
ponents of disposable spacecraft structures, saturation of the
surface of the sheet with gaseous fuel combustion products,
which substantially raises the microhardness of the material,
penetrates to an inconsiderable depth and produces no effect
over the whole of the thickness of the specimen. The same
factor seems to be responsible for the special features of
recrystallization of the alloy and for its structural inhomo -
geneity after service in high-temperature gas media.

After treating the specimens in a high-tem -
perature gas medium the characteristics of the
short-term static strength (the ultimate rupture
strength and the conventional yield point) of alloy
10VMTs increase by 15% on the average in the

of combustion of jet engine fuel drop by a factor
of 1.4 – 1.8 in the whole of the temperature range
studied. At room and elevated temperatures the
embrittlement of the alloy is higher than at high
temperatures. The extrema on the temperature
dependences of the alloy treated by high-tempera-
ture medium, which are connected with the ef -
fects of dynamic strain aging, are preserved.

CONCLUSIONS
1. Saturation of sheets from alloy 10VMTs with products
of combustion of jet engine fuel under the action of a high-
temperature gas flow increases substantially the microhard -
ness of its surface due to segregation of fine dispersed parti -
cles of a second phase. The saturation propagates to an in -
considerable depth, which is responsible for the special fea -
tures of recrystallization and for the structural inhomoge -
neity over the thickness of the sheets.
2. Preliminary treatment in a high-temperature gas me -
dium increases the characteristics of short-term static
strength of alloy 10VMTs by 15% on the average in the tem-
perature range of from 20 to 750°C and by 4 – 8% in the
temperature range of from 750 to 1500°C, and has virtually
no effect on these characteristics at higher temperatures. The
ductility characteristics of the alloy lower by a factor of
1.4 – 1.8 in the entire range of the studied temperatures.
3. Preliminary treatment by a high-temperature gas me -
dium increases the creep resistance and the long-term strength
of alloy 10VMTs at 1250 and 1500°C and affects little the re-
fractoriness at 1750°C. The creep curves for both structural
states studied remain qualitatively similar in the entire range
of temperatures and stresses applied.
