Thermogravimetric Investigations of Novel γ-γ′ Co-Al-W and Co-Al-Mo-Nb Cobalt-
Based Superalloys 
 
Grzegorz Moskal*, Damian Migas, Dawid Niemiec, Agnieszka Tomaszewska 
Silesian University of Technology, Faculty of Materials Engineering and Metallurgy 
Krasinskiego Street 8, 40-019 Katowice, Poland 
 *grzegorz.moskal@polsl.pl 
 
Abstract: Cobalt-based γ -γ′ superalloys are novel heat -resistant materials suitable for high 
temperature applications, such as components of turbine engine. These alloys exhib it 
favourable strength and corrosion resistance at high temperature owing to γ -γ′ microstructure, 
analogous to that of Ni -based superalloys. The aim of this paper is evaluation of oxidation 
behaviour of basic Co-9Al-9W (at.%) and new tungsten-free Co -10Al-5Mo-2Nb (at.%) 
alloys at elevated temperature. The investigation concerned thermogravimetric st udies in 
temperature range 40 -1200 °C. The oxidized surfaces after high temperature oxidation have 
been characterized using optical microscopy (OM), scanning el ectron mi croscopy (SEM) , 
energy dispersive X-ray spectroscopy (EDS) and X-ray diffraction analysis (XRD). 
Keywords: new γ-γ′ cobalt-base superalloys, thermogravimetry, Co-Al-W alloy, 
Co-Al-Mo-Nb alloy 
Introduction 
The improvement of heat-resistant superalloys is crucial for aircraft industry. The efficiency 
of turbine engines increases due to develop ment of materials providing high temperature 
thermal stability of the turbine components. The main factors responsible for decrease of the 
maximum service time of hot section components are hot corrosion, sulfide corrosion and 
high temperature oxidation [1]. Generally, the faster degradation concomitant to hot corrosion 
is related to utilization of fuel s containing various contaminations [2-3]. The occurrence of

Commercial Ni-based superalloys are commonly used materials for components designated 
for high temperature service, such as blades, nozzles, combustors and discs. However, 
relatively low hot corrosion resistance is substantial disadvantage, requiring usage of 
expensive and technologicall y challenging special bond coatings [ 7]. These coatings provide 
enhancement of hot corrosion resistance, however , are still not satisfactory in terms of long 
exposure at elevated temperature [ 8-9]. This limitations connected with growing demands for 
higher engine efficiency and operating temperature of components drives research regarding 
alternative materials for the high temperature applications. 
Ni-based superalloys exhibit remarkable creep and oxidation resistance owing to proper 
chemical composition a nd in particular due to the γ′ strengthening [ 10]. In 1971 C. S. Lee 
found an ordered fcc superlattice in the Co -Al-W system [11]. After 35 years , J. Sato et. al 
investigated discussed ternary system in detail and reported occurrence of the stable γ′ phas e 
[12]. These works opened possibilities for development of Co-based superalloys, an 
alternative for Ni-based analogous alloys. The γ′-Co3(Al, W) present in Co -Al-W system is a 
ternary compound with L1 2 structure, providing strengthening at high temperatur e [13-14]. 
The discussed phase exhibits strength superior to that of Ni 3Al at elevated temperatures [ 14]. 
The microstructure of new Co-based superalloys consists of a continuous face-centered matrix 
γ and the γ′ intermetallic compound, whose formation requ ires less than 1% of lattic e 
mismatch; therefore, obtainment of chemical and crystallographic stability is challenging [15-
16]. Furthermore, the cobalt matrix exhibits a better behavior in corrosive environments and 
elevated temperatures. The corrosion properties of pure cobalt [17 -20] and conventional

of new Co -based superalloys have made a lot of efforts in order to investigate effects of 
alloying elements on the γ′ stability [25-31]. Additions of Ti, Ta, Nb, Mo, Nb and W promote 
the formation of γ′ precipitates in Co-matrix and increase the soluvs temperature of discussed

Except of insufficient oxidation resista nce of ternary Co -Al-W alloys [32 -33] resulting from 
formation of non-protective oxide scale, the limitation of these alloys is occurrence of W. This 
element introduced to Co -based superalloy is partially responsible for high density 
(approximately 9.5 g cm-3 for Co-9Al-9W alloy)[34]. Furthermore, the homogenization of W -
containing superalloys is difficult due to slow diffusion of this element in Co [ 35]. The 
exposure of Co -Al-W al loys at 900 °C results in decomposition of γ′ phase and subsequent 
formation of Co 3W based D0 19 phases, w hose presence may be detrimental for mechanical 
properties due to their plate-like morphology [ 31]. Therefore, several authors have made 
efforts in order to find appropriate replacement for tungsten [36-38]. The possible replacement 
of W has been found in Mo which is γ′ -forming element [37]. In 2015 occurred first papers 
regarding novel W-free Co-Al-Mo-Nb superalloys [35, 39-40]. The Co-10Al-5Mo-2Nb (at.%) 
alloy exhibit lower density (8.36 g cm-3) compared to that of alloys based on Co -Al-W system 
(9.82 g cm -3 for Co -9Al-9.8W alloy)[40]. Moreover, the specific strength of Co -Al-Mo-Nb 
alloys is 17 MPa g −1cm−3 higher compared to that of W -rich alloys [40]. Due to favourable 
properties, the alloys based on Co -Al-Mo-Nb system are one of the most interesting future 
direction in the development of γ-γ′ Co-based superalloys.

Co-Al-Mo-Nb as-cast superalloys up to 1200 °C by thermogravimetry and subsequent

Experimental Research 
Research program 
In order to evaluate the oxidation behavior of investigated alloys, the thermogravimetric 
studies under non -isothermal conditions were carried out using NET ZSCH STA 449 F3 
Jupiter thermal analyzer. A Rectangular specimens of size 10 x 5 x 5 mm were prepared, the 
surfaces were ground, polished and ultrasonically cleaned in acetone. The Samples were 
placed in alumina crucibles. The experiment was carried in th e temperature range from 40 to 
1200 °C and heating rate of 10 °C min -1. The morphology of the oxide layers formed during 
test was investigated by Scanning Electron Microscopy (Hitachi S -4800). The plan -view 
observation were performed as well as investigati on of prepared cross -sections. In purpose of 
the chemical composition analysis across the surface and cross-sections, Energy Dispersive X-
ray Spectroscopy measurements were carried out. The phase composition of the formed scale 
was investigated by X -ray di ffraction analysis using an X′Pert 3 Powder diffractometer. The 
optical microscopic observations of cross sections were performed on a Nikon Eclipse MA200 
optical microscope. The OM micrographs were also used for layers thickness measurements.

cobalt-based superalloys ingots, prepared in a vacuum furnace by induction melting under Ar 
atmosphere (80 kPa) and temperature of casting 1550 °C. Technically pure metal s were

plot increased all the more. The total mass change a t final temperature achieved approx. 1%.

Conclusions 
 
The high temperature oxidation performance of Co -9Al-9W and Co -10Al-5M-2Nb cobalt -
based superalloys was performed. The oxidation behaviour of tungsten-free alloy was 
considerably worse, that was confirmed by the thermogravimetric analysis and measurements 
of the scale thickness. The alloy based on Co-Al-W system exhibits the higher oxidation 
resistance and possess a potential to be the material utilized at considerably higher 
temperature than Co -Al-Mo-Nb, whose the oxidation rate substantially increased at 
approximately 700 °C, whilst for W-containing alloy, the acceleration of oxidation occurred
