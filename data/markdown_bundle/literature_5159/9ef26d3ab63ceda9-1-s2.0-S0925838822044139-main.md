Nb content, the content of the α phase decrease d and the eu -

from continuous to discontinuous with increasing Nb content.

then decrease d with increasing Nb content. The Zr -20 Nb alloy

duration and intensity .

α brittle phase surrounds the β plastic phase pro vides the path of 
crac ks pr opagation and merg er to assist the formation of tin y 
fragments under impacting process.
