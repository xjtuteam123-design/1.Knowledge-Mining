11Abstract
12A promising NbMoTaWHf Refractory high-entropy alloy (RHEA) with an 
13outstanding yield strength of 223 MPa at 1600℃ and structure stability has been 
14identified after vacuum arc-melting. The alloy displayed a plastic deformation 
15behaviour and dual-phase structure stability after compressing at 1600℃. The superb 
16high-temperature strength was mainly ascribed to the high softening temperature and 
17precipitation strengthening. This work provides a promising high-temperature 
18structural material with good room-temperature plasticity and high strength at 1600℃ 
19and enriches the database of the NbMoTaW-based RHEAs.

18softening temperature and the precipitation strengthening. Firstly, a high softening 
19temperature is beneficial for high-temperature mechanical properties. Generally

14at 1600℃. According to the Orowan mechanism, when a moving dislocation 
15encounters a BCC2 particle, a dislocation ring forms, increasing the required external 
16force, which provides the deformation force, increasing strength.
174. Conclusion
18A promising NbMoTaWHf RHEA with outstanding strength and structural 
19stability at 1600℃ has been fabricated by vacuum arc-melting. The as-cast 
20NbMoTaWHf RHEA comprised dual BCC phase with Hf-riched BCC2 phase 
21dispersing in the BCC1 matrix. The alloy presented strong resistance to 
22high-temperature softening with a high yield strength of 223 MPa at 1600℃. After
