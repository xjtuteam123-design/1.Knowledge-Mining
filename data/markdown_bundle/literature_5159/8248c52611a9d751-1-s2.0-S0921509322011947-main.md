1. Introduction 
Ni-based oxide dispersion strengthened (ODS) superalloys exhibit 
outstanding high temperature properties and radiation tolerance and are 
considered as hopeful candidate materials for industrial gas turbine and 
nuclear reactor application [ 1 – 3 ]. Increasing high temperature strength 
is beneficial to improving performance and economics. Their high 
temperature strength is derived from solution strengthening, gamma 
prime ( γ
′
) phase strengthening (if the forming element of γ
′
phase (Al, Ti, 
Ta et al.) is introduced) and oxide dispersion strengthening [ 4 ]. 
Al is γ

beneficial for formation of γ

corrosion and oxidation resistance. Chiou showed that addition of 1 wt% 
Al promoted precipitation of γ

ratio, γ

superalloys [ 6 ]. In addition, Al is also oxide forming element and the 
addition of Al in Ni-based ODS superalloys resulted in formation of 
coarse Y – Al – O-type oxides, instead of Y

In high-Al ODS steels, oxide refinement and its influence on me -
chanical properties were widely investigated [ 10 – 14 ]. Gao indicated

5. Conclusions 
Three kinds of Ni-based ODS superalloys with different Zr/Hf addi -
tion, i.e. ODS-Z (0.6 wt% Zr), ODS-H (0.8 wt% Hf) and ODS-ZH (0.6 wt% 
Zr and 0.8 wt% Hf), were produced by MA and HIP. The effect of Zr and 
Hf on microstructure and tensile properties was investigated and char -
acterized. The following results are acquired: 
(1) The average grain size of ODS-Z, ODS-H and ODS-ZH is 785 nm, 
531 nm and 655 nm, respectively. The grain size can be effec -
tively refined by addition of Hf. The addition of Zr and Hf does 
not change average size and volume fraction of γ
