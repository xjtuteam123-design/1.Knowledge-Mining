Microstructural characterization and mechanical behavior
of Cr25Ni35NbM alloy dissimilar weld joint for application
in a hydrogen reformer furnace
Jingfeng Guo *, Wenwen Liu, Chunxiu Li, and Xiaoming Zhang
College of Physical Science and Technology, Yichun University, Yichun 336000, PR China
Received: 27 March 2020 / Accepted: 10 September 2020
Abstract. The microstructural characterization and mechanical behavior of Cr25Ni35NbM/15CrMo and
Cr25Ni35NbM/SUS321 dissimilar weld joints were studied in this paper. The microstructure, room temperature
and high temperature (1173 K) tensile strength of dissimilar weld joints were analyzed through optical
microscopy (OM), scanning electron microscopy (SEM) and electronic universal tensile testing machine. The
microstructure of HAZ in 15CrMo steel of Cr25Ni35NbM/15CrMo dissimilar weld joint transformed from
ferrite-pearlite into ferrite-martensite. A large volume fraction of a phase was found to have precipitated in the
HAZ of SUS321 austenitic stainless steel for Cr25Ni35NbM/SUS321 dissimilar weld joint. At room temperature,
the tensile strength and yield strength of these two type dissimilar weld joints is less than Cr25Ni35NbM alloy
similar weld joint. The high temperature tensile strength of these two type dissimilar weld joints is less than
Cr25Ni35NbM alloy similar weld joint. Both at room and high temperature, the fracture locations of two types
dissimilar weld joints are the HAZ of the base metal 15CrMo and SUS321 stainless steel, respectively. It indicates
that the weak part of the Cr25Ni35NbM alloy dissimilar weld joints is the low-performance base metals 15CrMo
and SUS321 stainless steel.
Keywords: dissimilar weld joint / Cr25Ni35NbM alloy / ferrite / martensite / a phase
1 Introduction
Dissimilar weldments are frequently applied in equipment,
such as chemical, petrochemical, nuclear and power
industries [1]. The dissimilar weld joints exhibit satisfactory
mechanical properties and appropriate high temperature
corrosion resistance at operating condition. The choice of
dissimilar weld joint in design not only can ef ﬁciently
utilize the characteristics of material, but also can reduce
equipment cost. In petrochemical industry, the furnace
tubes of a hydrogen reformer furnace working at elevated
temperature are made of heat-resistant steels (HK alloy
and Cr25Ni35NbM alloy) [ 2,3], and ﬂanges of the reformer
furnace are usually made of ferritic steels (CrMo Steel).
Currently, the material of pigtail pipe in the hydrogen
reformer furnace is austenitic stainless steel [ 4,5].
Dissimilar weld joints with cheaper steel in place of high
alloy steel make a considerable saving on cost. There are
several types steel used in the hydrogen reformer furnace.
The Cr25Ni35NbM heat-resistant alloy has excellent high
temperature creep strength, good oxidation and carburi-
zation resistance and satisfactory ductility. 15CrMo steel
is having well considered mechanical properties, good
thermal conductivity and ductility. SUS321 stainless steel
is having well considered mechanical properties, good
corrosion resistance and satisfactory resistance against
intergranular stress corrosion cracking. With high carbon
content, the microstructure of as-cast Cr25Ni35NbM alloy
consists of a g-solid solution matrix, eutectic carbides, and
niobium-based and chromium-based carbides [ 6– 9].
Hence, the weld joint of Cr25Ni35NbM alloy as a higher
weld cracking susceptibility, i.e. a higher high-tempera-
ture weld cracking susceptibility than general forged steels
[10]. The adoption of the combination between 15CrMo
steel and Cr25Ni35NbM heat-resistant alloy is based on
both technical and economic reasons, along with satisfac-
tory service performance as well as considerable savings
[11]. Owing to satisfactory service performance, SUS321
austenitic stainless steel is widely utilized in the pigtail
pipe of the hydrogen reformer furnace [ 12].
Dissimilar weld joints tend to frequent failures, as
reported in many scientiﬁc articles [1,13]. Therefore, a lot of
methods have been proposed to improve the service
performance of dissimilar weld joints. The selection of
proper welding process and ﬁller material for dissimilar

austenitic cast steel and forged (rolled) steel.
There is no systematic report on characterization
studies which is conducted on Cr25Ni35NbM alloy
dissimilar joint in a hydrogen reformer furnace by manual
GTAW processes. The work on Cr25Ni35NbM/15CrMo
and Cr25Ni35NbM/SUS321 dissimilar weld joints by using
25.35.4CNb wire as ﬁller material has been attempted in
this paper. For the weld joint combination, detailed studies
are conducted on microstructure characterization, room
temperature and high temperature (1173 K) tensile
strength and fracture morphology using optical microscopy
(OM), scanning electron microscopy (SEM) and tensile
testing machine.
2 Experimental and procedures
Cr25Ni35NbM/15CrMo and Cr25Ni35NbM/SUS321 dis-
similar weld joints with 25.35.4CNb as ﬁller metal is made
of manual GTAW. The welding materials are 25.35.4.CNb
wire which is made by Metrode Products Ltd UK. The
chemical compositions of the base metals and ﬁller metal
are listed in Table 1 , which were determined by Optical
Emission Spectrometer analyses. Cr25Ni35NbM alloy
tubes are made by centrifugal casting tube, while 15CrMo
steel tube and SUS321 austenitic stainless steel tube are
rolled tubes. The tubes diameter of test coupon is OD
108 mm with thickness 8 mm. And, the joint design is a
single V-groove with 3 weld beads ( Fig. 1 ).
For Cr25Ni35NbM/15CrMo dissimilar weld joints,
there are three weld layers, which the interpass tempera-
ture is about 100 K and the shielding gas is argon to
prevent oxidation ( Fig. 1). In order to prevent the welding
hot cracking in the weld joint, the weld joint is preheated
and post-weld heated. The preheating temperature is
above 723 K, and the post-weld heat treatment is
923 K ± 10 K. There are also three weld layers in
Cr25Ni35NbM/SUS321 dissimilar weld joint, and the
interpass temperature is less than 150 K with the argon as
shielding gas to prevent oxidation ( Fig. 1 ). The non-
destructive (NDE) welding inspection is carried out to
check the imperfection by visual testing (VT), penetration
testing (PT) and radiographic testing (RT). For mechani-
cal test, the preparation of tensile specimens is in
accordance with ISO standards.
In order to investigate metallography, the specimens
are grounded with silicon carbides papers, and polished on
woollen cloth. The Cr25Ni35NbM alloy and SUS321
stainless steel which are used for observation of micro-
structure are electrolytically etched in a solution of oxalic
acid (10%) at 5 V for 10 s. The microstructure of 15CrMo
base metal is etched in 4% nitric acid alcohol.

3 Results and discussion
3.1 Microstructure analysis of weld joint
The microstructure of both similar and dissimilar
Cr25Ni35NbM alloy weld joints was ﬁrst studied. The
as-cast Cr25Ni35NbM alloy consists a g-solid solution
matrix, eutectic carbides, niobium-based(NbC) and chro-
mium-based carbides(M 7C3), the skeleton shape eutectic
carbides are distributed on the dendrite boundaries
(Fig. 2a )[ 3,22,23]. The metallographic microstructure of
rolled 15CrMo steel consists of pearlite and ferrite
(Fig. 2b ). The microstructure of SUS321 stainless steel is
austenite with many twins. There are many second phases
precipitated at the grain boundary ( Fig. 2c ).
The OM images of the interface between weld metal
and base metal in Cr25Ni35NbM similar weld joint,
Cr25Ni35NbM/15CrMo and Cr25Ni35NbM/SUS321 dis-
similar weld joints are shown in Figure 3 . The resultant
heat from the welding process leads to fusion of the
adjacent region in the base metal. The melting has taken
place in a deeper part of Cr25Ni35NbM alloy ( Fig. 3a ).
Some unmelted grains appearing near the fusion boundary
can be attributed to the uneven distribution of the heat
from the welding process ( Fig. 3a). Figure 3 also shows the
mixed zone near the interface of weld metal and base metal,
duo to a similar melting range between ﬁller metal and base
metal [ 24]. The ﬁller metal and base metal are rich in
carbon, iron, chromium and nickel, so there is a high
melting point (more than 1500 °C) of matrix and many
carbides precipitated in the unmixed zone [ 25]. The high
melting point of the matrix and the reaction between the
matrix and the precipitates near the fusion boundary make
some grains surrounded by melted zone.
There is a white bright band at the fusion boundary of
weld metal and 15CrMo steel [ 26]( Fig. 3b). With a higher
corrosion resistance, a white bright band generated at the
interface of weld metal and 15CrMo steel. The dendrite of
the fusion zone near the interface are perpendicular to the
fusion boundary ( Fig. 3b ). It was observed that the
dendrite that grows in the same direction with the fastest
heat ﬂow direction in the fusion zone. Meanwhile, as a
result of the in ﬂuence of heat during the welding process,
the metallographic structure of HAZ in 15CrMo steel also
changed, and pearlite has been transformed into martensite
(Fig. 3b ).
A white bright band appeared at the interface of weld
metal and SUS321 stainless steel too [ 26]( Fig. 3c ). The
dendrite of the fusion zone near the interface are
perpendicular to the fusion boundary ( Fig. 3c ). It was
observed that the direction of the dendrites growth is the
fastest cooling direction in the fusion zone. The micro-
structure of the HAZ in SUS321 stainless steel have been
changed, due to the heating and cooling cycles during
welding process. There are many a phases precipitated in
the HAZ of SUS321 stainless steel ( Fig. 3c ).

The morphology of the HAZ of Cr25Ni35NbM similar
weld joint, Cr25Ni35NbM/15CrMo and Cr25Ni35NbM/
SUS321 dissimilar weld joints are shown in Figure 4. Due to
high concentration in chromium and nickel elements, the
Cr25Ni35NbM alloy has a high-metallurgical stability.
Hence, the microstructure of the HAZ of Cr25Ni35NbM
alloy is still the original as-cast structure ( Fig. 4a ). The
considerable variation is observed in the HAZ of 15CrMo
steel, the ferrite-pearlite transforms into the ferrite-
martensite ( Fig. 4b ). The morphology of a phase in the
HAZ of SUS321 stainless steel is shown in Figure 4c . The
heat from the welding process brings about the tempera-
ture increase of the adjacent region in the base metal
(15CrMo steel and SUS321 stainless steel). Then, the rapid
cooling leads to the microstructure transformation in the
HAZ of weld joint. The lamellar pearlite transformed into
lath martensite ( Fig. 4b). A lot of a phases precipitated in
the austenite matrix ( Fig. 4c). During the welding process,
the metallurgical stability of the base metal affects the
microstructure of the HAZ. The higher the metallurgical
stability of the base metal, the more dif ﬁcult microstruc-
ture transform of the HAZ.
The weld joint is equivalent to a small weld pool, so the
coarse dendrite structure is formed in the fusion zone after
solidiﬁcation (Fig. 5). The nickel and chromium content of
the ﬁller metal is very high, so a typical austenite dendrite
structure is formed in the fusion zone. There are many
carbides precipitated in the dendrite boundaries for the
Cr25Ni35NbM alloy similar weld joint ( Fig. 5a ). Due to
15CrMo steel and SUS321 stainless steel with low carbon,
carbon migrated from weld metal into base metal, the
carbides in the fusion of the dissimilar weld joint is
fewer than Cr25Ni35NbM alloy similar weld joint ( Figs. 5b
and 5c).
3.2 Mechanical properties at room temperature
There is a distinct difference of mechanical properties at
room temperature between Cr25Ni35NbM alloy similar
and dissimilar weld joints (Fig. 6). The tensile strength and
yield strength of Cr25Ni35NbM alloy similar weld joint is
higher than two type dissimilar weld joint. And the tensile
strength of Cr25Ni35NbM/SUS321 dissimilar weld joint is
higher than Cr25Ni35NbM/15CrMo dissimilar weld joint.
But the yield strength of Cr25Ni35NbM/15CrMo dissimi-
lar weld joint is higher than Cr25Ni35NbM/SUS321
dissimilar weld joint.
The values of room temperature mechanical properties
of Cr25Ni35NbM similar and dissimilar weld joint are
shown in Table 2 . The tensile strength and ductility of
Cr25Ni35NbM similar weld joint is similar to base metal,
but the yield strength of weld joint is higher than base
metal ( Tab. 2 ). The tensile strength of Cr25Ni35NbM/
15CrMo dissimilar weld joint is similar to the base metal of
15CrMo steel, and the yield strength is higher than 15CrMo
steel, while the ductility is less than 15CrMo steel. That ’s
owing to the microstructure of the HAZ changed from
ferrite-pearlite into ferrite-martensite. Cr25Ni35NbM/
SUS321 dissimilar weld joint tensile strength is similar
to the base metal of SUS321 stainless steel, and the yield
strength is higher than SUS321 stainless steel, while the
ductility is less (Tab. 2). Due to the high fraction of a phase

The Cr25Ni35NbM/15CrMo and Cr25Ni35NbM/

HAZ of 15CrMo steel is the area where the Cr25Ni35NbM/
15CrMo dissimilar weld joint is prone to break.The fracture
surfaces of similar and dissimilar weld joints are shown in
Figure 7 . The tensile fracture of Cr25Ni35NbM similar
weld joint is a mixture of cleavage fan pattern and tough
dimple, and some cracks in the fracture surface ( Fig. 7a ).
Dimples of varying size were observed in the fracture
surface of Cr25Ni35NbM/15CrMo dissimilar weld joint,
which indicated that the major fracturing mechanism to be
ductile ( Fig. 6b ). Blach et al. [ 27] have reported the
initiation of the formation of dimples at secondary phase
particles, which eventually resulted in different morphology
of each studied dimples fracture, according to its own
particular microstructural characteristics. Dimples, voids
and cracks are observed in the fracture surface of
Cr25Ni35NbM/SUS321 dissimilar weld joint, which
indicates that the major fracturing mechanism to be ductile
(Fig. 7c).
There is a corresponding relationship between the
fracture morphology and the fracture position of the weld
joint. The fracture morphology of dissimilar welds re ﬂects
the weak part of the weld joint. The differences of fracture
surfaces between similar and dissimilar weld joints also
reveal that the ductility of Cr25Ni35NbM similar weld
joint is lower than Cr25Ni35NbM/15CrMo and
Cr25Ni35NbM/SUS321 dissimilar weld joints.
3.3 Mechanical properties at high temperature
15CrMo steel have a good ductility and low cost, the
Cr25Ni35NbM/15CrMo dissimilar weld joint is widely
applied in the hydrogen reformer furnace. Owing to
SUS321 stainless steel with good ductility and well
resistance against intergranular stress corrosion,
Cr25Ni35NbM/SUS321 dissimilar weld joint is widely
used in the pigtail pipe of the hydrogen reformer furnace.
The stress-strain tensile curves of Cr25Ni35NbM
similar and dissimilar weld joint at 1173 K is shown in
Figure 8 . The high temperature tensile strength of
Cr25Ni35NbM similar weld joint is highest in this three
weld joint ( Fig. 8 ). The high temperature strength of
Cr25Ni35NbM/SUS321 dissimilar weld joint is the second,
and that of Cr25Ni35NbM/15CrMo dissimilar weld joint is
the lowest.
Table 3 shows the values of mechanical properties of
Cr25Ni35NbM similar and dissimilar weld joint at 1173 K.
The contents of nickel and chromium in ﬁller metal are very
high, so the Cr25Ni35NbM similar weld joint have a good
high temperature tensile strength. The high temperature
tensile strength of Cr25Ni35NbM similar weld joint is
similar to Cr25Ni35NbM alloy. The tensile strength of
Cr25Ni35NbM/15CrMo dissimilar weld joint is less than
Cr25Ni35NbM similar weld joint at high temperature.
With more nickel and chromium, the tensile strength of
Cr25Ni35NbM alloy is higher than 15CrMo steel and
SUS321 stainless steel at high temperature. Hence, the
tensile strength of Cr25Ni35NbM similar weld joint is
higher than Cr25Ni35NbM/15CrMo dissimilar weld joint.

4 Conclusion
In this investigation, the mechanical properties and
microstructural characteristics of Cr25Ni35NbM alloy
similar and dissimilar weld joints were studied. From the
investigation the following conclusions are drawn.
Both room and high temperature tensile strength of
Cr25Ni35NbM alloy similar weld joints is higher than two
types Cr25Ni35NbM alloy dissimilar weld joint.
Both at room and high temperature, the fracture location of
Cr25Ni35NbM/15CrMo and Cr25Ni35NbM/SUS321 dis-
similar weld joint are the HAZ of the base metal 15CrMo and
SUS321 stainless steel, respectively. It indicates that the
