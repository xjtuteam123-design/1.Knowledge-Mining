Microstructure and Properties of Directionally Solidified NbSi 2/ Nb 5Si 3 
Composites 
Wei Li 1,a , Haibo Yang 1,b , Aidang Shan 1,c , Lanting Zhang 1 and Wu Jiansheng 1 
1Key Laboratory of Ministry of Education for High Temperature Materials and Tests, School of

Keywords: Directionally solidification; NbSi 2/Nb 5Si 3 composites; High-temperature compression; 
Phase transformation; Orientation relationship 
Abstract: NbSi 2 is an attractive material for high temperature applications due to its high melting 
point, low density and good oxidation resistance. The high-temperature strength of NbSi 2 is expected 
to be further improved by incorporation with Nb 5Si 3, which performs a high creep resistance and 
strength at high temperature due to its complex crystal structure. In this paper, directionally solidified 
NbSi 2/ Nb 5Si 3 in-situ composites have been prepared using an optical floating zone method. Scanning 
Electron Microscopes (SEM) and X-ray diffraction (XRD) have been used to investigate the phase 
constitution and microstructure. The orientation relationship between Nb 5Si 3 and NbSi 2 is 
investigated by transmission electron microscopy (TEM). High-tempe rature properties of alloys are 
tested by compression at the strain rate of 1×10 -4/s at 1673K and 1773K. It was found that high 
temperature strength and phase constitution of directionally solidified alloys depended on the addition 
of Mo. 
Introduction 
Nb 5Si 3 performs high creep resistance and high melting point at high temperature over 1773K. As 
a reinforcement phase in high temperature, Nb 5Si3 was compounded with Nb to acquire a balance 
between high temperature strength and room temperature fracture toug hness [2-6]. Moreover, creep 
resistance of MoSi 2/Mo 5Si 3 eutectics has been reported to be far better than single phase MoSi 2 [7,8]. 
Hence, it is expected that the high temperature strength of NbSi 2 can be improved by incorporation 
with Nb 5Si 3. The addition of Mo can effectively increase the height of anomalous peak of NbSi 2 [9]. 
Thus, high temperature strength of NbSi 2/Nb 5Si 3 composites might be improved due to solid solution 
strengthening of Nb silicide with Mo addition. Work of Kim et al. also indicated that the stability of 
β-Nb 5Si 3 in Nb/Nb 5Si 3 composites is affected on by Mo addition. β-Nb 5Si 3 performs a lower yield 
stress than α-Nb 5Si 3 in Nb/Nb 5Si 3 composites [5,6]. Moreover, there may be a relationship between 
formation of microcrack and phase transformation in the NbSi 2/Nb 5Si 3 composites. Thereby, it’s very 
necessary to make clear the effect of Mo addition on the phase stability and high temperature strength 
of NbSi 2/Nb 5Si 3 composites. The purpose of this paper is to study the phase stability and 
high-temperature mechanical properties of NbSi 2/ Nb 5Si 3 composites with Mo. 
Experiment Procedure 
NbSi 2/ Nb 5Si 3 composites (Nb-57at.%Si and Nb-57at.%Si-2at.%Mo) were prepared in the form 
of ~80g bar by arc-melting the constituent elements in a water-cooled copper health. The purity of raw 
materials is 99.93wt.% for Nb and 99.999wt.% for Si. Hereafter the alloy s composition will be 
abbreviated as Nb-57Si instead of Nb-57at.%Si. Directionally solidified materials were produced 
using a Floating Zone (FZ) method at speeds of 10mm/h, and then annealed unde r Ar at 1473K for 
100 hours. The temperature of the samples decreased at cooling rate of 400K/h. The crystal structure 
of constituent phase was determined by X-ray diffraction (XRD) method. Specimens for compression 
test with dimensions of 3×3×5mm 3 were cut from directionally solidified samples by

According to phase diagram of Nb-Si system [10], Si content is 37.5% ( at.) in α- Nb 5Si 3 and 
39.6% (at.) in β-Nb 5Si 3 respectively. In β-Nb 5Si 3, the ratio between Nb and Si is lower than the 
stoichiometry 5:3. The former researcher reported[11] that there ar e two assumptions for the excess 
Si: 1)Si atoms substitute for Nb atoms: (Nb) 4(NbSi)(Si) 3; 2)vacancies in the Nb position: 
(Nb) 4(NbVa)(Si) 3. In Table 2, with Mo addition, the content of Nb does not distinctly change, while 
Si content in β-Nb 5Si 3 decreases close to that in α-Nb 5Si 3. And Mo content is equal to the decrement 
of Si content in β-Nb 5Si 3. So Mo atoms substitute for Si atoms in Nb-57Si-2Mo. Due to similar size 
between Nb atom and Mo atom, Mo atoms always substitute for Nb positi on in Nb 5Si 3 [5,6]. 
Therefore, it is more possible that Si substituting for Nb results in the increase of Si content in 
β-Nb 5Si 3. Moreover, a reasonable assumption can be obtained that Mo atoms substitute for the sites of 
Si Nb , where Si Nb denotes Si substituting Nb position. As a result, the -Si-Mo-Si- cha ins replace the 
–Si-Si (Nb) -Si- chains. Works of Chu et al. [12] had shown that the strength of bond rises from –Si-Si- 
and –Mo-Mo- chains to –Si-Mo-Si- chains. Hence, in Nb-57Si-2Mo alloys, substitution of Mo for the 
sites of Si Nb and formation of the strongly bonded –Si-Mo-Si- chains are responsible f or the 
stabilization of β-Nb 5Si 3. Similar to Mo 5Si 3, the bond of -Si-Nb-Si- chains is stronger than –Si-Si- 
and –Nb-Nb- chains. After Nb-57Si alloys are annealed at 1473K, the weakly bonded –Si-Si- chains 
are broken down and the strongly bonded –Si-Nb-Si- chains form. Consequently, β-Nb 5Si 3 transforms 
to α-Nb 5Si 3 in Nb-57Si alloys.

Summary 
1. NbSi 2/ Nb 5Si 3 in-situ composites is prepared by Floating Zone method. An orientation 
relationship of NbSi 2 [ 
0211 ] // Nb 5Si 3[001] and NbSi 2( 00 11 ) // Nb 5Si 3(200) exists between 
Nb 5Si 3 phase and NbSi 2 phase. 
2. The addition of Mo makes β-Nb 5Si 3 stable below 1473K in NbSi 2/ Nb 5Si 3 composites. 
3. Mo addition remarkably improves the strength of NbSi 2/ Nb 5Si 3 composites from 223Mpa to 593 
MPa at 1773K.
