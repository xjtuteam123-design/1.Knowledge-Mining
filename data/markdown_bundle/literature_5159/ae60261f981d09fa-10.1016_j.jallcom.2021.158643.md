Single crystal superallo y s are widely used for adv anced aer o-

infere nce can help to reach a more comprehensi ve and deeper un -

R evisiting Figs. 4–8 re minds us that R e reduces the stacking fault 
energy and conseq uently pr omot es earlier and more formation of 
stacki ng faults and partial dislocations. F rom the vie wpoint of 
thermodynamics, the R e can make more differen ce to single cryst al 
Ni-based superallo ys. As is well know n, the sys tem alw ay s sponta -
neously evol ves to the state that its to tal energy is minimized. The

caused by elastic and plastic activities), and ignor e o ther energy 
contribution. This is indeed what man y simulation methods do 
when studying single crystal Ni-based super alloy s, such as the pre -
sent authors’ w ork about phase-field simulations of single crystal Ni-

5. Conclusions 
The pr esent wor k studied the role of R e on lattice paramet er , 
stacking fault energy , elastic and plastic beha viors, as well as the 
distribution of R e in single crystal superallo ys by STEM-EDS, first 
principle and molecular dynamics methods. The main conclusions 
can be summarized as follow s. 
(1) R e prefers to locate in the γ phase in 2nd and 3rd gener ation 
single crystal superallo ys, due to the pot ential energy differen ce 
of R e replacing Ni or Al sites under different R e contents . The 
lattice paramet ers and γ / γ ’ misfit both increase with R e content 
nonlinearl y , and tend to conv erge as R e approaches its satur -
