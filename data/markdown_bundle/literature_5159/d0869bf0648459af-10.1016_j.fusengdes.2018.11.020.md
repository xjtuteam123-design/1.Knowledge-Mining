1. Introduction

dustrial fields due to their good workability, high temperature creep
strength, and high temperature oxidation resistance. For example,
austeniticsteelswithhighcontentofCrandNi,suchas310types,are
important structural materials for ultra supercritical power plants.
Austeniticstainlesssteelsarealsopromisingcandidatematerialsforthe

A310type(25Cr20Ni)ODSaustenticalloywithMoandwithout
carbon was fabricated by mechanical alloying and hot isostatic
pressing.ThemicrostructurewasanalyzedbyTEM.Theinfluencesof
thermalmechanicaltreatmentsandtheadditionofMoelementsonthe

conclusions:
•The mean size of the grains in the ODS310+Mo alloy is about
300nm;inthematrix,itexistsagreatamountofveryfineY 2Ti2O7
dispersedparticles,thesizeisbetweenseveralnmtotensnm.
•Thethermo-mechanicaltreatmentcaneffectivelycontrolthetensile
propertiesoftheODS310+Moalloy.Amongallthesamplestreated
by different thermo-mechanical treatments, the ones after forging
plushotrollingprossesspresentthemostfavorablematerialprop-
