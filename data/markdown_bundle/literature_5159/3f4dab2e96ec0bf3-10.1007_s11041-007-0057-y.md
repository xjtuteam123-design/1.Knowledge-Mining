EFFECT OF ION-PLASMA COATINGS ON CHARACTERISTICS
OF LONG-TERM STRENGTH OF ALLOY ZhS6U
S. A. Budinovskii, 1 S. A. Muboyadzhyan, 1 and E. B. Chabina 1
Translated from Metallovedenie i Termicheskaya Obrabotka Metallov, No. 6, pp. 37 – 41, June, 2007.
Results of a study of long-term strength of alloy ZhS6U after deposition of strengthening coatings from seven
nickel alloys of the Ni – Co – W – Mo – Cr – Al (Ta, Re, Nb, Ti) system, chromium or titanium carbides, and
of an additional external refractory SDP-2 coating of the Ni – Cr – Al – Y system common for all the composi-
tions are presented. The structure and the element composition of the coatings in the initial state and after test-
ing for high-temperature strength are studied.
INTRODUCTION
The use of high-temperature protective coatings on
castable high-temperature nickel alloys (ZhS) used for tur-
bine blades increases in most cases their long-term strength
in tests performed for 500 h and longer. This is connected
with the protection of their surface from oxidation for pre-
serving the bearing cross-section of the part, which does not
interfere with the physical (strength) properties of the ZhS it-
self. At the same time, it is known that in some cases the use
of methods of surface treatment of materials increases sub -
stantially their strength properties [1]. The existing methods
of surface engineering, such as ion implantation and laser
and beam treatment, do not ensure noticeable growth in the
strength of ZhS due to the small thickness of the modified
layer. The processes of diffusion, stress relaxation, and evo -
lution of the dislocation structure of the material of the blade
remove the advantages of the modified layer with respect to
the matrix metal under high temperatures.
The aim of the present work consisted in raising the
high-temperature strength of alloy ZhS6U by depositing
strengthening coatings by methods of ion-plasma treatment
of the surface in vacuum arc discharge or by forming a hard-
ened surface layer by vacuum heat treatment of the “alloy –
multicomponent coating” composition.
METHODS OF STUDY
In order to evaluate the possibility of ion-plasma
strengthening (IPS) of the surface of ZhS we used multicom-
ponent nickel coatings of the Ni – Co – W – Mo – Cr – Al
system additionally alloyed with tantalum, titanium, nio-
bium, and rhenium as well as layers based on Ni
3Al
(/c103/c162-phase) and NiAl ( /c98-phase) intermetallics obtained on the
basis of these alloys after deposition of aluminum on the sur-
face and vacuum heat treatment. In addition, we considered
composite coatings of the “hard-alloy” type deposited by the
plasma-chemical method from nickel alloys containing car-
bide-forming elements and commercially pure titanium and
chromium.
All the strengthening coatings considered in the work
were obtained by the ion-plasma method [2, 3]. Table 1 pre -
sents the compositions of the alloys used in the work, from
which we fabricated cathodes for depositing the coatings in
installations for ion-plasma sputtering.
The surface of the specimens was prepared and the
strengthening coatings were deposited by standard methods
in TKhPU installations [4] at arc current of 200 /c17710 A and
MAP-1(M) installations [5] at 700 /c17750 A. Before the depo -
sition of the coating we performed deep cleaning of the sur -
face of ZhS specimens by etching in the plasma of the mate -
rials of the coating at electrical potentials of the substrate no
lower than 600 V . This ensured subsequent deposition of the
strengthening coating on the atomically pure surface. Coat -
ings from multicomponent nickel alloys were obtained at a
substrate potential of 5 – 10 V and were 60 – 80 /c109m thick.
The specimens with coatings from alloys 4 and 5 were co-
vered with a second layer from commercially pure alumi -
num. In the process of vacuum heat treatment of these com -
positions a layer of alloyed nickel aluminide with a thickness
of 40 – 50 /c109m formed on the surface of the specimens [6].

The characteristics of long-term strength were deter -
mined in accordance with the requirements of GOST 10145
using ZST2 /c473-VIÉT testing machines and the methods ac -
cepted for evaluating the long-term strength of ZhS at high
temperatures. The specimens for the tests had a diameter of
the functional part equal to 5 mm; the test temperature was
1000°C and the test time was 10, 100, and 500 h. Metallo -
graphic and x-ray diffraction analyses of the structure and of
the ZhS-coating compositions were performed using a
“JCMA-733” microanalyzer.
RESULTS AND DISCUSSION
Table 2 presents the results of long-term strength tests of
specimens of alloy ZhS6U with strengthening coatings of
various types.
It can be seen that all specimens with strengthening
ion-plasma coatings from multicomponent alloys tend to in -
crease the endurance as compared with untreated specimens
and with specimens with a coating from pure SDP-2. The
most stable and favorable results are obtained after IPS with
the use of coatings from refractory alloys1, 2, and 3. Deposi-
tion of strengthening coatings from metal carbides or of lay-
ers of the “hard-alloy” type (alloys 6 and 7 ) did not raise the
endurance of the specimens in the tests for long-term
strength.

The coatings based on multicomponent nickel
alloys 1 – 3 after vacuum heat treatment have a
two-layer structure too. Figure 1 b presents the in -

such coatings (zone 1 ) contains 8 – 11% Al and
20 – 22% Cr. In the course of the vacuum heat
treatment the external zone is enriched with a low
amount (about 1%) of cobalt, tungsten, titanium,
and molybdenum. The internal zone of the coat -

that of alloy ZhS6U but contains a low amount of
tantalum and rhenium (1 and 1.8% for coatings 2
and 3, respectively). The microstructure of the in -
ternal layer of such coatings differs substantially
from the microstructure of the compositions with
metal carbides. It can be seen that the layer of the
coating has a double-phase structure. The mean
content of aluminum in this layer is about 9%. In
addition, the layer contains Cr, W, and Co in an
amount of 6 – 7% each and 1.5 – 2% Ti and Re
(each).
Coatings based on nickel aluminides and de-
posited from multicomponent nickel alloys4 and 5,

3 ) is formed of nickel aluminide obtained in the

For the further study we chose coatings with rhenium
[7, 8] and alloys 2 and 3, which have a composition close to
that of refractory nickel alloys VKNA25 and ZhS36 for cast-
ing turbine blades and possess a single-crystal structure (we
will denote them IPS-2 and IPS-3 in what follows). Table 4
presents the results of the tests of specimens of alloy ZhS6U
for long-term strength at 1000°C and different stresses.
We used the method of FGUP VIAM for computing the
mean and minimum values of long-term strength of alloy
ZhS6U with IPS for the probability of nondestruction equal
to 0.8. The results of the computation are presented in Ta -
ble 5, where we also give the rated characteristics of the
long-term strength of alloy ZhS6U without IPS.
It can be seen that IPS of the surface of the specimens of
alloy ZhS6U increases the long-term strength of the alloy un-
der the test conditions by up to 10 and 15% for the mean and
guaranteed strength values after 500-h and 1000-h testing,
respectively. The level of the least guaranteed values of
long-term strength of the specimens with IPS exceeds that of
the specimens of ZhS6U with directed structure. This seems
to be connected with the elimination of the effect of the sur -
face factor on the strength properties of the specimens after
the IPS. It should also be noted that the scale factor also in -
fluences the results of the tests, i.e., the strengthening coating
has a thickness of about 100 /c109m when the diameter of the
functional part of the specimen is 5 mm. It can be expected
that IPS of the surface of turbine blade feathers, which usu -
ally have a wall thickness on the order of 1000 /c109m, should
ensure a more substantial growth in the long-term strength of
the ZhS-coating composition.
The study of the microstructure of IPS-3 compositions
after testing for long-term strength (156 h) at a temperature
of 1000°C and a stress of 175 MPa has shown the absence of
noticeable changes on the alloy /c47strengthening coat interface
(see Fig. 1d ). The results of the study of the composition on
the surface of specimens of alloy ZhS6U with strengthening
coatings from alloys 2 and 3 after testing for long-term
strength at 125 MPa for 674.5 h and 800.5 h, respectively,
are presented in Table 6 and in Fig. 1d and e. After the tests,
the strengthening coating on the surface of the specimens
preserved the two-zone structure. All alloying elements were
distributed quite uniformly over the thickness of the coating.
The internal layer of the specimens (zone 2 ) was close in the
composition to that of the matrix alloy but contained less co-
balt and tungsten and more rhenium. The composition of the
Zh6U itself on the interface with the coating changed incon -
siderably with respect to the initial composition except for
the enrichment with rhenium to a depth of up to 20 /c109m from
the alloy/c47coating interface.
Our data show that the growth in the long-term strength
of alloy ZhS6U may be connected with formation of a hard -
ening layer with a thickness of about 100 /c109m on its surface
due to the thermovacuum treatment of the “alloy – multi -
component coating” composition in a mode including an -
nealing and homogenizing. Such a layer possesses a higher
strength and thermal stability due to the alloying of the hard-
ening /c103/c162-phase with tantalum and of matrix of the alloy with
rhenium.

It should be noted that in the subsequent operation of
parts with IPS the thickness of the hardened layer will in -
crease due to further diffusion of the elements increasing the
ZhS strength from the surface into the matrix just like in the
process of vacuum heat treatment. The process of IPS of the
ZhS surface can be combined with repair of turbine blades
after operation for restoring the geometric sizes of the profile
part of the feather.
CONCLUSIONS
Deposition of strengthening coatings with compositions
close to those of the alloys used for single-crystal casting of
turbine blades onto alloy ZhS6U ensures 10 – 15% growth in
the mean and guaranteed values of long-term strength in tests
performed for 500 and 1000 h at a temperature of 1000°C.
