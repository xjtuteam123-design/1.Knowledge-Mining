Abstract. The combustor chamber, diffuser and nozzle are the main components of the ramjet 
engine. In this study, the thermal strength of the combustion chamber of the ramjet engine was 
evaluated. The combustion chamber consists of an In conel alloy 718 liner and a 17]4Ph stainless 
steel housing. The liner is rapidly heated to a high temperature. The heated liner is cooled with a 
film cooling method that forms a cold boundary layer to separate the hot gas from the surface of the 
liner. The thermo]structural analysis is evaluated the thermal strength of super alloy structure with 
various thermal insulation performances by finite e lement method with code MSC/Nastran. The 
result of the analysis is compared with accelerated stress rupture test. The experiment is performed 
to get safety design and estimate actually life]time for combustor chamber under high temperature. 
In general, the work in this paper is helpful to further improve the understanding and evaluation of 
thermal strength of the super alloy structure with various thermal insulation performances. 
Introduction 
Ramjet and scramjet engines are kinds of air breathing engines, and have been recently highlighted 
since they are more efficient than other types of r ocket engines. The ramjet engine becomes 
attractive at supersonic flight speeds above Mach Number 2. The structure of the ramjet engine is 
more complex than a solid rocket motor and it is launched by a rocket booster at ramjet speeds. The 
ramjet has four times more capacity than a solid ro cket motor. This offers the possibility of 
reducing weight and volume so it can improve velocity and movement. [1, 2]. 
In this study, the thermal strength of a combustion chamber that consists of an Inconel alloy 718 
liner and 17]4Ph stainless steel housing is evaluated by the finite element method and accelerated 
stress rupture test. The results of the analysis and test are useful to select the structural material and 
to determine the shape of a combustion chamber which satisfies structural design safety.

Conclusions 
(1) Ruptures did not happen in accelerated stress r upture tests with a temperature of 649 
oC and a 
stress of 920.34 MPa on Inconel alloy 718 liner. Ru ptures also did not happen in accelerated 
stress rupture tests with a temperature of 500 oC and a stress of 610.2 MPa on 17]4Ph stainless 
steel housing. 
(2) For safety and best performance of the ramjet engine structure, a thermal isolation efficiency of 
about 75]80 percent is recommended for structural design. 
(3) The results of this study will be useful to evaluate stability as well as rupture predictions of the 
ramjet combustion chamber.
