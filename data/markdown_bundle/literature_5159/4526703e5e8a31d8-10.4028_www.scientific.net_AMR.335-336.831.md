sunhl@swjtu.edu.cn 
Keywords: TiAl-based alloy, peritectic reaction, aluminum content, microstructure, mechanical 
properties. 
Abstract. The effect of different aluminum content on perite ctic reaction and mechanical properties 
of pentatomic TiAl-based alloy was investigated. Th e results indicate that the grain size gradually 
increase with increasing content of aluminum and ad dition 45.7% aluminum in TiAl-based alloy 
results in that the peritectic reaction can increas e grain size greatly, respectively. The content of 
aluminum can increase the room temperature strength , high temperature strength but peritectic 
reaction can effectively reduce tensile strength. Aluminum content has a little effect on the ductilit y. 
The stress rupture life has positive correlation relationship with the content of aluminum. 
Introduction 
Titanium aluminum (TiAl) alloys have been considere d as viable structural materials for 
high-temperature application because of their attra ctive high elevated-temperature strength, higher 
creep resistance, good oxidation resistance and spe cific strength properties[1-2]. To research and 
develop multi-element TiAl-based alloys which conta in high niobium, tungsten (W+Nb>5%, at.%) 
and low aluminum (Al<45%, at.%) becomes an importan t direction in the history of TiAl 
development. Niobium and tungsten are refractory metal elements and diffuse slowly that solute in α
2 
and γ lamellar structure can strengthen the microst ructure so as to guarantee high-temperature 
strength and thermal stability. Thus, its alloys are promising structural materials for use in aerospace, 
automobile engines and gas turbine industries[3-5]. 
TiAl-based alloys are sensitive to its grain struct ure. It is greatly necessary to the quality of cast 
products by controlling grain size and microstructure. However, the different alloy elements are added 
to TiAl alloys could easily lead to peritectic reaction and decrease stability and properties. Therefore, 
the problem how to control the content of Al that can avoid peritectic reaction in order to produce fine 
grains, homogeneous and no harmful segregation phases of TiAl alloys containing Nb and W need to 
be further investigated. In this study, the effect of different aluminum content on solidification 
microstructure and mechanical properties of pentatomic TiAl-based alloy was analyzed.

20mm diameter were produced by using a plasma arc c old hearth (PACH) process, followed by hot 
isostatic pressing (HIP) at 1260 ℃ and 150MPa for 4h. Their nominal compositions(in a tomic

The cylindrical samples were used for tensile and lasting experiments and test-pieces had a gauge 
length of 23mm and a gauge diameter of 3.99mm which were conducted on WDW3100 testing 
machine in air at a strain rate of 0.7 ×10 -4/s. High temperature tensile tests were conducted a t 700 ℃. 
The tensile strength and elongation were gained from the curve of stress-strain. The stress rupture test 
was carried on RDW5050 testing machine at 700 ℃ in air and stress is 85% of its maximal tensile 
strength. All test data presented in this study represent an average of three tests. Microstructures were 
examined by back scattered electron (BSE) microscop y, using a FEI Quanta200 environmental 
scanning electron microscope(ESEM), operated at 20 kV.

Discussion 
Research on solid-liquid transition for Ti-Al binar y alloy has been relatively mature. The complete 
Ti-Al binary phase diagram was established by analy zing equilibrium solidification in Ti-Al binary 
alloy and revealed critical composition points[6-8] . According to the central part of Ti-Al binary 
phase diagram as shown in Fig. 4(a), it can be analyzed that the primary phases crystallize from liquid 
are different for alloys in the range 44.8 and 49.4 at.%Al. The two solidification paths are as fllows : 
L→β and L→α. Meanwhile, there exist two continuous peritectic reactions, as fllows: L+ β→α
p and 
L+ α→γp. The critical composition points have been precise ly measured. With temperature 
decreasing, the solidification of Ti-Al alloys cont aining different aluminum followed different 
crystallizing paths.

Based on binary Ti-Al phase diagram, in order to form grain-refining during solidification process, 
it is necessary to guarantee solidification path along L →β and prevent transformation into the section 
of peritectic composition because of rich in aluminum. the primary phase that crystallises from liquid 
is β, and on cooling, the β phase becomes unstable and transforms into the α phase. The orientation 
relationship between β and α is {0001} α//{110} β, <11 2 0>α// <111 >β. The α phase nucleates and 
grows on six possible {110} crystal plane of β phase when it is assumed that the difference of 180 ° is 
neglected, so as to form fine α grains[9-10]. With further solid state cooling, th e α phase becomes 
unstable and orders into fine α2 + γ lamellar grains by eutectoid transformation. Why the key problem 
lies in trying to avoid peritectic reaction(L+ β→αp). The change of microstructure during peritectic 
reaction shown in Fig. 4(b). Peritectic reaction grain generated directly from the liquid phase and it is 
often coarse-grained. The initial nucleation of β p hase exist within the grain. The Al content of the 
front of the interface in the liquid rise high, bec ause the solid-liquid interface of β phase and liq uid 
phase moving. Then, In the process of peritectic reaction(L+β→α), α phase content increased, β phase 
and liquid phase content decreases. And the segrega tion has become a serious. Subsequently, the 
eutectoid reaction(α→α 2+γ) occurs. And if the L+β→α transformation process has not finished, the β 
residual phase will be surrounded by α phase, the s olid phase transformation in the course of 
follow-up along the β→α →α 2 + γ transformation, but its orientation will be di fferent from the 
lamellar microstructure surrounding it, approximate 120 ° angle, as shown in Fig. 1 (c) the arrow 2. 
The α coarse grains of peritectic reaction(L+β→α) into α2+γ lamellar crystalline phase transition by

Tensile properties of the three alloys tested both at room temperature and at the temperature of 700 ℃

The W-Nb-B alloy elements are added to binary TiAl alloys, all of these critical composition 
points in TiAl binary phase diagram will change. Mu ltiple TiAl alloys containing Nb and W 
compared to binary TiAl alloys more difficult to ac hieve fine grains and no harmful segregation 
phases. Fine-grained TiAl alloy solidification due to the quality control of the refractory alloy by 
adding the number and different types vary greatly. tungsten Is the high melting point and heavy 
metals, but also a strong β-phase stabilizing element [11-13]. Add to other alloy elements (such as Ni, 
Cr, Mn, etc.) than tungsten more likely to cause co mponent segregation and dendritic segregation 
during solidification, more local scale changes in the inherent TiAl alloy solidification law( such as 
the critical components points of the initial cryst alline precipitate( L →β and L →α), the critical 
components points of the peritectic reaction(L+β →αp , L+α →γp, etc.) Therefore, for a pentatomic 
TiAl alloy system, the establishment of such a phase diagram is a very complex task. With a specific 
industrial value for certain multiple TiAl alloys, in addition to reference the corresponding ternary 
phase diagram, the more meaningful work is to deter mine the phase diagram with some of the key 
ingredients - temperature - phase transition point, to guide the corresponding level in science and 
technology development.

strength but peritectic reaction can effectively reduce tensile strength. Aluminum content has a littl e
