performances of Inconel690 heat transfer tube. The microstructure, mechanical performances and 
corrosion properties were studied under different h eat treated conditions. The optimized heat 
treatment process was obtained. The conclusions was as follows: with the increase of solution 
temperature from 1050 ℃ to 1150 ℃, the mean grain size varied from 12µm to 58µm, the room 
temperature tensile strength and yield strength red uced gradually, while the elongation elevated 
gradually. The alloy had a good resistance to pitti ng corrosion after solutionization during 
1090~1100 ℃.The massive carbides (M 
23 C6) were precipitated along grain boundaries after 
sensitization processing. And with the increase of sensitization temperature, its morphology 
transform from tiny semicontinuous to coarsening co ntinuously, multi-layered distribution emerged 
during higher sensitization temperature range. Along with the sensitization temperature rising, alloy 
690 has better resistance to intergranular corrosio n. As the sensitization temperature was higher 
than 750 ℃ with isothermal holding for 2 hours, the desensiti zation phenomenon occurred, and the 
ability of anti-intergranular corrosion for alloy 690 could be enhanced distinctly.

The heat treatment processes of alloy 690 included two important constituents, named solid 
solution and desensitization treatment. The main pu rpose of solid solution treatment was to obtain 
the even single-phase austenite structure through d issolving the carbides in matrix matal, and the 
pellet tiny,reasonable distribution carbides can be obtained after desensitization process, also the 
cold hot-working stress may be eliminated. Next, th e solid solution treatment can obtain suitable 
grain size and mechanical properties. Kai [1]  pointed out that the mean crystal grain size of 
Inconel 690 alloy increased from 7µm to 24µm after 1150 ℃×1h solid solution treatment. Qiu[2]  
 . discovered that intergrainular corrosion rate inc reased with the increase of solution temperature 
during 950~1150 ℃. Feng[3]  found that the good pitting resistance would be go tten when 690 
alloy was treated in 1050 ℃~1100 ℃. Therefore, the optimized solid solution treatment temperature 
was extremely essential to 690 alloy performance. 
Desensitization processing (long time sensitization processing) was one kind of special heat 
treatment process which would be carried on after t he solid solution treatment. Its purpose was to 
form the appropriate quantity and the distribution of carbide in the microstrcture, eliminate the grain 
boundary poor chromium area, enhance the property o f resistance to intergranular corrosion and 
intergranular stress corrosion for alloy 690. Li an d Zhou [4] had analyzed alloy 690 samples which 
was treated in 600-800 ℃ for 0.5-200hr by TEM, the results indicated that all the precipitated phases 
along the grain boundary was M 23 C6, the preferential nucleus place was dislocation ta ngle in grain 
boundary, and the carbides and matrix maintenanced cube-cube coherent orientation relation. It can

3.1 The influence of solution process 
Fig.2 showed the metallographic structures after so lution treated for 2 minutes at different 
temperatures and also indicated absence of second p hase in this alloy. The typical microstructure of 
austenitic with the full isometric grains indicated that the static recrystallization process has been 
finished. And the grain size increased with the inc resing of solution temperature, which increased 
from 12 µm to 58 µm with the solution temperature v aried from 1050 ℃ to 1150 ℃. when the solid 
solution temperature was above 1100 ℃, the grain size changed faster, the grain was mark ed 
coarsening, its mechanical and corrosion properties droped evidently. 
The effect of solution temperatures on the mechanic al properties was represented in Fig.3. It 
can be seen from the diagram that, as the solution temperature rising, tensile strength and yield 
strength declined, but elongation rose significantl y. That is, the room temperature tensile strength 
droped from 780MPa to 662.5MPa, yield strength reduced from 400MPa to 250MPa, the elongation 
rose 40%. According to reinforcement theory, for a single phase of austenite, there are second phase 
strengthening and solid-solution strengthening, but fine-grain strengthening was main mechanism 
for alloy 690, therefore, grain size was a major factor for its strength at room temperature.

3.2 The influence of sensitization process 
For heat transfer of evaporator tube, alloy 690 can not be used directly after solution treatment, 
it needed to be adjusted through desensitization process. Desensitization treatment process was used 
to prevent the intergranular corrosion and intergra nular stress corrosion cracking of the alloy in 
evaporater. The precipitation of carbides led to th e occurrence of intergranular chromium-depleted 
zone when this alloy used at 350 ℃ for a long time. But the carbides will precipitate fully and 
intergranular chromium-depleted zones can be elimin ated gradually through desensitization 
treatment by a long time. 
 3.2.1 Intergranular carbides in the sensitization process 
 Fig.5 showed the intergranular carbides morpholog y with isothermal holding for at different 
temperatures. At 650 ℃, small and few carbides scattered along the grain boundary, as shown in Fig. 
5(a). Due to alloy sensitized at lower temperature, the element diffused slowly, the nucleation and 
formation of carbides may be finished partly, but i ts size grew up slowly, the small and dispersed 
shape carbides was formed eventually. 
When alloy 690 was sensitized during 700~725 ℃, carbides were semi-continuous status in the 
grain boundary, and the quantity of carbides increa sed, some grew up rough, coarse precipitates 
located in the triple grain boundary junction, as shown in Fig.5(b). This was because when the alloy 
was sensitized during 700~725 ℃, diffusion speed of chromium increased, the number of grain 
boundary carbides increased compared with 650 ℃. But the precipitation of carbides was not 
enough to grow up in 2h, it could not overrided gra in boundary fully, semicontinuous form of 
M
23C6 presented. 
When alloy was sensitized during 750 ℃~850 ℃, the behavior of carbide precipitation was 
complicated, multiple arrangement in grain boundaries was appeared. The number of coarse carbide 
increased obviously, and network structure was form ed, as shown Fig.5(c,d). Carbides were 
generally considered to be nucleated in grain bound ary, but studies showed that nucleation coherent 
with the matrix was much easier than nucleation in chaotic arrangement grain boundary[9].

3.2.2 Effect of sensitization treatment on mechanical properties of alloy 690 
Sensitization treatment is a medium temperature agi ng treatment. In this sensitizing process, 
M23 C6 was precipitated along grain boundary, and the size of the precipitated phase was larger, they 
won’t produce dispersion strengthening effect. The strengthening of alloy 690 mainly relied on 
solid solution strengthening and grain boundaries s trengthening, in sensitizing treatment process, 
precipitates of carbide were mainly distributed in the grain boundaries, showing a slight influence 
on alloy grain size and mechanical performances. 
The influence of sensitization temperature on the a lloy grain size was shown in Fig.7. 
Comparing with the solid solution treatment, the in fluence of sensitization temperature on the alloy 
grain size was very small. The primary cause was th at the grain boundary migration driving force 
was insufficient in the sensitization temperature range, the grain size was remained basically stable.

3.2.3 Intercrystalline corrosion property after sensitization treatment 
Intercrystalline corrosion properties of alloy 690 which was sensitized for 2 hours at different 
temperatures were studied using EPR, as showen in F ig.9. It was quite clear that sensitization 
temperature had a great effect on the property of i ntercrystalline corrosion for alloy 690. When the

sensitization temperature was below 725 ℃, poor intergranular corrosion resistance was prese nted. 
Owing to the completion of desensitization above 75 0 ℃, the intergranular corrosion resistance of 
alloy became very well. The transition temperature zone was 725~750 ℃. Intergranular corrosion 
resistance of alloy 690 mainly depended on the chro mium-depleted zone near the grain boundary. 
There were a certain amount of M 23 C6 formed in grain boundary after 2h treatment at 725 ℃, and 
the obvious poverty area near chromium carbides app eared due to the slower diffusion speed of 
chromium, resulting in a decline in grain boundary corrosion resistance of alloys. With the 
sensitization temperature rising, the quantity of i ntergranular carbides increased for the acceleratio n 
of the diffusion speed of chrome, Comparing to the slight growth of carbide, the proliferation of 
diffusion speed was more dominated, therefore the d ifference of chrome concentration between 
grain boundary and near area would gradually reduce , its anti-corrosion capacity would improve 
gradually. When the sensitization temperature was h igher than 750 ℃, the quantity of precipitated 
carbide increased lightly, the diffusion of chrome had absolute advantage, the poverty district of 
chrome had basically disappeared, the alloy was in desensitivity state.
