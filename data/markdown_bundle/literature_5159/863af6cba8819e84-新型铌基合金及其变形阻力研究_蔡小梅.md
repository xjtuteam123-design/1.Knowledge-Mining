Abstract
:
The as-cast properties of a new niobium alloys and its plastic deformation resistance were studied． The results
showed that
:
In the cast
，
the alloy appeared as rod-like shape of the microstructure
，
with high strength and certain degree of
processing performance． After hot compression
，
flow curvers exhibited no flow softening at the embient temperature in low
strain rates

particularly at higher strain rates and lower test temperature． At 950 ℃ test temperature the redistribution of
solute caused the flow softening

but at 1050 and 1150 ℃ test temperature the dynamic recrystallization was the contributing
factor to the flow softening．
