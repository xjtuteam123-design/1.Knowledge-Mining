Abstract
Ternary element Nb addition on a Mo 3Si matrix resulted in improved mechanical properties. Solid solution additions on the alloys at
different Nb concentrations were evaluated. High temperature compression test at 1300 and 1400 ◦Ca t1 0−5 s−1 shown a strong temperature
and composition dependence. The calculated stress exponent from compressive analysis resulted to be n = 6. Diffusion analysis were
performed by using a couple of samples with and without Nb in two different conditions (t, at.%). The results from the strengthening obtained,
are interpreted by calculating the diffusion coefﬁcient. This increment in the creep strength is rationalized in terms of substitutional considering
the atomic sizes for Mo and Nb.

1. Introduction
Now days exist the constant demand for new materials
to support several service conditions such as high temper-
ature, oxidation and good mechanical properties. Among
this materials are the intermetallic compounds based on the
Mo–Si system which are interesting materials to operate
as structural materials and high temperature resistant as
heating elements and oxidation-resistant surface materials
specially MoSi
2 [1]. The main problem among this inter-
metallics compounds is their poor ductility and resistance at
room temperature which produce a considerable limitation
in structural applications. Speciﬁc attention has been given
to Mo
5Si3 with B addition, having found a considerable
improvement in their oxidation resistance [2,3]. Schneibel
et al. [4], studied creep properties on Mo 5Si3 with a con-
tinuous /H9251-Mo matrix, they reported a signiﬁcant increase
in their room temperature fracture toughness. The mechan-
ical properties of this type of alloys have been studied by
several researchers [5,6].

Recently other silicides with A15 structure have been
studied. For example the yield strength of Cr 3Si is reported
to be in the order of 450 MPa at 1300 ◦C [7] and Raj et al.
[8] reported the compressive strength of Cr 40Mo30Si30 of
approximately 200 MPa at 1323 ◦C.
At present the mechanical properties of this intermetallic
Mo3Si compound with Nb solid solution additions has not
been fully explored, so the aim of the present work is to
evaluate the effect of Nb addition under creep conditions.
These results are explained by considering an operating
substitutional process when the pseudo-binary (Mo,Nb)
3Si
is formed.

6. Summary
In this study, the creep behavior of alloys with Mo 3Si
matrix with Nb additions up to 20 at.% was investigated at
high temperatures. The alloys exhibited some plasticity at
1400
◦C, below this temperature the samples presented a
catastrophic behavior. Derived of this test we assume that the
DBTT is around of 1400
◦C for Mo 3Si with Nb additions.
A stress exponent of n ≈ 6 and n ≈ 5 for the alloys with
5 and 10 at.% Nb is obtained, which are in good agreement
with other studies [10]. It is concluded that Nb produced
a substantially strengthening in the Mo
3Si matrix, due to
a diffusive substitutional process. TEM work is in process
for analysis of defects generated during the compression
process.
