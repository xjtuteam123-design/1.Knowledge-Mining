ABSTRACT 
Although most BCC-based high-entropy alloys have high strength and excellent high temperature mechanical 
properties, their low plasticity greatly limit their application. It is a new challenge to develop new BCC-based 
high-entropy alloys with excellent combination of high strength and plasticity. In this study, we prepare a 
new BCC/B2 AlCrFeMnNi
2 
high-entropy alloy with a unique sunflower-like eutectic microstructure. This alloy 
has a high yield strength of 1206 MPa, a high fracture strength of 3437 MPa and large compressive plastic strain 
of 37.5%. The excellent compressive mechanical properties are superior to many other BCC/B2 high-entropy 
alloys. These properties can be attributed to its unique microstructure consisting of coherent interface be -
tween BCC and B2 phase and the bimodal distribution of the B2 precipitates. Our results can provide a mean -
ingful reference for improving the plasticity of BCC-based high entropy alloys. 
1. Introduction 
Abandoning the traditional alloy design concept, high-entropy alloys 
(HEAs) often form multi-principal solid solutions with simple crystal 
structure like face centered cubic (FCC), body centered cubic (BCC), or 
hexagonal close packed (HCP) [1] . The unique microstructures with 
long-range order and chemical disorder make HEAs exhibit many 
excellent properties, which makes HEAs become one of the most 
extensive studies. Recently, researchers have carried out many research 
work on the FCC-based HEAs [2,3] , owning to the high plasticity of FCC 
phase. For instance, the FCC + L1

increasing the plasticity of BCC-based HEAs.

3. Results and discussion 
The XRD pattern of the AlCrFeMnNi
2 
HEA suggests the presence of 
two phases which can be indexed as BCC and B2 phase in Fig. 1 (a). The 
lattice parameters of the BCC and B2 phase are 2.875 Å and 2.904 Å, 
respectively. We can see copious eutectic grains or colonies in the SEM 
image Fig. 1 (b). Depending on Li ’ s results [10] , an independent eutectic 
colony shall be the basic unit rather than a bulk sample, when discussing 
the free solidification behavior of the under-cooled eutectic alloys. For 
the current HEA, each eutectic colony is similar to the sunflower-like 
morphology, which is composed of flower disk region and petal re -
gion. The thick layers (light-grey area) separate all elliptical grains, 
which is related to the grain-boundary wetting [11] . This morphology 
can influence the phase transition and mechanical properties. The 
enlarged images (see Fig. 1 (c) and (d)) suggest that spherical or ellip -
soidal precipitates are in flower disk region and rod-shaped precipitates 
are alternately arranged in petal region. 
In order to further verify the crystal structure of the precipitates and 
matrix phase, TEM bright-field images are obtained in Fig. 1 (e) and (f). 
In petal region, the rod-shaped precipitates are identified as the primary 
ordered B2 phase (p-B2) by the unique B2 super-lattice reflections (100 
and 11 1) and the matrix is disordered BCC phase through the selected- 
area electron diffraction (SAED) patterns in Fig. 1 (e). Similarly, the 
ellipsoidal precipitates in disk region are identified as the secondary 
ordered B2 phase (s-B2) and the matrix is disordered BCC phase, as 
Fig. 1 (f) shows. And p-B2 phase and s-B2 phase maintain coherent 
relationship with BCC matrix phase, respectively. The p-B2 precipitates 
are about 0.5 – 2 μ m in width and 1 – 10 μ m in length, with the spacing 
between p-B2 precipitates ranging from ~0.1 to ~0.5 μ m in petal re -
gion. The s-B2 precipitates with the size ~200 nm are uniform distrib -
uted in disk region. EDS results ( Table S1 ) indicate that the ordered p-B2 
and s-B2 phases are enriched in Ni and Al and the disordered BCC phase 
is enriched in Fe and Cr, which is consist with our previous results in

BCC/B2 HEAs are the much-studied alloys, such as AlCoCrFeNi [5] , 
AlCoCrNi [12] , etc. The main reason for the low plasticity of this kind 
HEAs is that the spinodal decomposition microstructure composed of 
BCC and B2 phases cannot accommodate large plastic deformation. The 
coarse-grained HEAs, ultrafine-grained HEAs, and nano-grained HEAs 
with a single BCC or B2 phase are brittle due to the inherent brittleness 
of BCC crystal structure. In contrast, our newly designed AlCrFeMnNi
2 
HEA has the comprehensive compressive properties because of the 
unique sunflower-like eutectic microstructure. 
Fig. 3 shows the fracture morphology of the AlCrFeMnNi
2 
HEA. The 
elongated eutectic colonies can be obviously observed in Fig. 3 (a), 
which indicates a large plastic deformation occurred. In disk region, 
large deformation results in fragmentation of the disk region and for -
mation of fine fragment embedded with s-B2 precipitates (see Fig. 3 (b)). 
The elongation of the disk region and the appearance of holes suggest 
that s-B2 phase can hinder the dislocation movement increasing the 
strength, and at the same time hinder the crack growth improving the 
plasticity. In petal region, one can observe some cracks initiation along 
the phase boundaries between p-B2 and BCC phase, instead of pene -
trating brittle fracture morphology (see Fig. 3 (b)). The lath eutectic 
structure can change the crack direction and inhibit the crack propa -
gation along a single direction, thus improving the fracture strength. We 
further detect the TEM morphology after 20% compression strain, as 
shown in Fig. 4 . A large number of dislocations appeared in BCC phase. 
The accumulation of dislocations takes place around the coherent 
interface between p-B2 and BCC phase. The coherent interface can not 
only absorb dislocations, but also hinder the movement of dislocations, 
thereby synergistically improving the strength and plasticity [13] . Thus, 
the outstanding comprehensive properties can be put down to the 
coherent internal boundaries and the bimodal distribution of the B2 
precipitates. 
4. Conclusions 
In this paper, we successfully fabricate a new AlCrFeMnNi
2 
HEA with 
excellent comprehensive mechanical properties. This alloy has a unique 
sunflower-like eutectic microstructure, containing disordered BCC 
phase in the matrix, ordered ellipsoidal B2 precipitates in disk region, 
and rod-shaped B2 precipitates in petal region. This HEA achieves 
excellent compressive mechanical properties with a high yield strength 
of 1206 MPa, a high fracture strength of 3437 MPa and large 
compressive plastic strain of 37.5%. The good combination of strength 
and plasticity is due to the coherent interface between BCC and B2 phase 
and the bimodal distribution of the B2 precipitates.
