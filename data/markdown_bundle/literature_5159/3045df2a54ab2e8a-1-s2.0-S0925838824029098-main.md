ABSTRACT
The service temperature of commercial Ti-6Al-4V alloy usually does not exceed 400 ℃ . In this work, Ti-8Al-4V- 
1Nb- x B (wt%) alloy was designed based on Ti-6Al-4V by the Calphad method to improve the properties at high 
temperatures at a low cost. Compared to Ti-6Al-4V, the strength and elongation of Ti-8Al-4V-1Nb-0.1B increased 
by 20 % and 75 %, respectively. The Ti-8Al-4V-1Nb-0.28B achieved excellent strength 560 ± 21 MPa and elon -
gation 25.7 ± 1.4 % at 550 ℃ . The fracture of Ti-8Al-4V changed from intergranular to the combination of 
intergranular and transgranular with the addition of B. The solid solution strengthening of Al, the grain boundary 
α reinforced by TiB particles, and the increase in density of α / β interfaces and α / α high-angle grain boundaries 
accounted for the improvement of strength. The oxidation resistance was significantly improved because of the 
addition of Al and Nb elements. This work provided a new idea to enhance mechanical properties at high 
temperatures and increased the service temperature of Ti-Al-V by alloying and microstructure design at a low 
cost.
1. Introduction
The demand for high-temperature titanium alloys in modern in -
dustry become increasingly urgent, such as aircraft engines, exhaust 
systems of vehicles, and various components of spacecrafts, and the 
large casting components with complex structures play a key role in 
many fields [1] . BT9, BT18, Ti6242, Ti1100, Ti55, Ti60, and TG6 [2,3]
have commonly been used in high-temperature conditions (550 ~ 
600 ℃ ). Those alloys contain a high content of alloying elements with 
different thermal properties, which promotes casting defects during 
solidification because large amounts of alloying elements reduce the 
fluidity of titanium alloy [4] . Since the cost of raw materials and prep -
aration are high. Ti-6Al-4V (Ti64), a widely used commercial titanium 
alloy, has excellent formability and mechanical properties, but the ser -
vice temperature of Ti64 is 300 – 400 ℃ [2] , which limits the application 
of Ti64. Improving the high-temperature properties of Ti64 at a low cost 
is critical for its increased application. For titanium alloys, the elevated 
temperature strengthening mechanisms mainly include solid solution 
strengthening [5] and Hall-Petch strengthening [6] .
Many researchers have systemically investigated the effect of alloy -
ing elements on the microstructure and properties of titanium alloy, 
such as Al, V, Nb, Fe, and Cr [5,7 11] . The best strengthening efficiency 
was obtained by the Al addition at a low cost. Dislocation slip became 
more difficult with added Al content during plastic deformation because 
substantial Al atoms introduce obstacles to dislocation glide, and the 
obstacles are range ordered to form Ti
3
Al especially when the Al content 
is above 6.0 wt% [12 – 15] , which accounts for the increase in strength 
and decrease in ductility [16] . In addition, the formation of Ti
3
Al in -
creases with Al content, which is beneficial to the high-temperature 
strength but harmful to the ductility [17,18] . Therefore, the [Al] 
equivalent is an important basis for the composition design of titanium 
alloys, and Rosenberg [19] found the [Al] equivalent cannot exceed 8 wt 
% due to embrittlement caused by the formation Ti
3
Al. A minor addition 
of Nb to titanium alloy enhances creep resistance, fatigue strength, 
fracture toughness, and oxidation resistance at elevated temperatures 
[20] .

improved but the ductility decreased with Al content [2] . Grain

shells, large α Widmanst ¨atten structures, and basket-weave structures

structure, and refine α laths and α colony.
Introducing refiners to the molten titanium alloy is a widely used 
method to refine the grains and improve the mechanical properties of as- 
cast Ti-alloy without thermo-mechanical processing [9,25] . Previous 
studies have shown that some alloying elements (e.g. Fe, Cr [9] , Cu [26] ,

Sen and Tamirisakandala et al. [25,36 – 38] studied the effect of B con -
tent on the microstructure and mechanical properties of as-cast Ti64 
alloys. The size of prior β grains and α colonies decreased, and Wid -
manst ¨atten structures were gradually eliminated with increasing B 
content [25] . In addition, the eutectic TiB particles precipitated at grain 
boundaries pin the grain boundary α during plastic deformation at high 
temperatures, which enhances the strength.
This investigation was undertaken to design an as-cast α + β titanium 
alloy with excellent mechanical properties at high temperatures, which 
was based on the commercial Ti64 alloy by a simple and low-cost 
method because casting was high-efficiency and low-cost compared to 
the thermo-mechanical process, powder metallurgy, and additive 
manufacturing. The design of alloy composition was based on [Al] 
equivalent principles for titanium alloys and the Calphad method to 
control the microstructure and the elevated-temperature properties. The 
weak β -stabilizer Nb was used to improve fracture toughness [39] . The 
minor addition of elemental B was used to refine the grain and improve 
the ductility at high temperatures. The effect of the increase in Al, B, and 
Nb on the microstructure and high-temperature properties was 
investigated.

Compared to Ti64, the tensile strength of S0 was improved significantly,

increased Al content. The addition of B improved the strength and

with the addition of 0.1 wt% B, and the total elongation of S1 was twice

The fracture of Ti64 and S0 were mainly intergranular, and cracks 
exist on the grain boundaries ( Fig. 7 (a, b)). At high temperatures, the 
failure mainly occurred at grain boundaries because the grain boundary 
α shells were weak defects [41] , and cracks occurred on the grain 
boundaries and propagated in the direction perpendicular to the tensile 
direction. Compared to S0, the fractures of S1 and S2 were the combi -
nation of intergranular and transgranular ( Fig. 7 (c, d, e, f)), which 
accounted for the increased ductility than S0. Dimples were observed 
inside the grains, and many TiB particles were distributed along the 
grain boundaries in the fracture of S2, as displayed in Fig. 7 (e), (f). The 
high-density TiB particles on grain boundaries in S2 led to that cracks 
were easy to nucleate at TiB aggregations and extend along grain 
boundaries during deformation, which decreased the ductility.

temperature tensile properties of S1 and S2 can be attributed to Hall-

4. Conclusions
In this work, we fabricated a new as-cast α + β titanium alloy (Ti-8Al- 
4V-1Nb- x B) based on the commercial Ti-6Al-4V using the Calphad 
method and alloying at a low cost. Ti-8Al-4V-1Nb-xB exhibits 
outstanding mechanical properties and oxidation resistance at high 
temperatures.
1. Compared to the Ti64, the strength and elongation of Ti-8Al-4V-1Nb- 
xB are improved simultaneously, achieving 560 ± 21 MPa and 25.7 
± 1.4 % for Ti-8Al-4V-1Nb-0.28B at 550 ℃ . The mass gain of Ti-8Al- 
4V-1Nb-xB is half of Ti64 after heat treatment at 550 ℃ for 100 h.
2. The increase in Al content significantly enhances the elevated tem -
perature strength. The addition of Nb suppresses the generation of 
Ti
3
Al. In addition, the increased Al and Nb content improved the 
oxidation resistance of titanium alloys.
3. The aggregated TiB particles pin and break the continuous grain 
boundaries α shells, which changes the tensile fracture from inter -
granular to the combination of intergranular and transgranular, and 
accounts for the increased plasticity. The grain refinement and TiB 
particles provide the nucleation sites for α -Ti during β→ α trans -
formation, which leads to the increase in the density of α / β interfaces 
and α / α HAGBs. The grain refinement, increased density of α / β in -
terfaces and α / α HAGBs, and the grain boundary α pinned by TiB 
particles account for the enhanced strength with the addition of B 
element.
