LONG-TERM STRENGTH OF A COMPOSITION OF COMPLEX
REFRACTORY COATING AND SINGLE-CRYSTAL RHENIUM-ALLOYED
NICKEL ALLOY AFTER HIGH-TEMPERATURE HOLDS
V . P. Kuznetsov,1, 2 V . P. Lesnikov,1, 2 E. V . Moroz,1 M. S. Khadyev, 1 and I. P. Konakova 1
Translated from Metallovedenie i Termicheskaya Obrabotka Metallov, No. 8, pp. 7 – 12, August, 2013.
The long-term strength of a system of single-crystal nickel alloy ZhS36-VI with Cr – Al gas circulation coat-
ing (GCC) + Ni – Cr – Al – Ta – Re – Y ion-plasma coating (IPC) + Al – Ni – Cr – Y ion-plasma coating (IPC)
is analyzed under conditions close to the operating ones after a hold for 1 – 1000 h in a temperature range of
1050 – 1300°C.
Key words: single-crystal refractory nickel alloy, refractory coatings, phase composition, properties.
INTRODUCTION
Joint action of a high-temperature oxidizing environment
and stresses cause coagulation and partial dissolution of
/c103/c162-phase and formation of a great number of surface cracks in
refractory nickel alloys. Such processes give rise to softening
and final failure of the alloys. The time before failure is
shortened quite substantially.
Failure of refractory nickel alloys upon contact with an
oxidizing environment starts from the surface. This makes
application of nickel-base alloys impossible without protec -
tive coatings. Today the main tendency in advancement of
protective coatings for rotor vanes of aircraft gas turbine en -
gines (GTE) from refractory nickel alloys is to pass from sin-
gle-layer aluminide coatings to multilayer ones [1 – 3].
In our earlier works we have studied the structure and
phase composition of a complex diffusion-condensation

Cr – Y IPC protective coating and the reaction zone of its in-
teraction with single-crystal alloy ZhS30-VI after high-tem -
perature holds at 1050 – 1300°C for 1 – 1000 h [4].
The aim of the present work was to study the effect of a
complex Cr – Al GCC + Ni – Cr – Al – Ta – Re – Y IPC +
Al – Ni – Cr – Y IPC coating on long-term strength of sin -
gle-crystal alloy ZhS30-VI [001] after high-temperature
holds at 1050 – 1300°C for 1 – 1000 h.
METHODS OF STUDY
We studied specimens with single-crystal structure fabri-
cated from refractory nickel alloy ZhS36-VI with a Cr – Al
G C C+N i–C r–A l–T a–R e–YI P C+A l–N i–C r–Y
IPC protective coating after a standard heat treatment. The
average chemical composition of alloy ZhS36-VI, the mode
of its heat treatment, and the process of deposition of protec-
tive coatings on the castings have been described earlier in
[4]. The total thickness of the complex coating on all the
specimens in the initial state was about 80 – 90 /c109m.
Since the distribution of temperatures over the surface of
a turbine vane feather is nonuniform, the service life of the
vane is determined by the operating capacity of the “refrac -
tory nickel alloy – coating” composition in “hot” zones of
the vane with maximum surface temperature. We subjected
specimens of alloy ZhS-36-VI with a complex protective
coating to high-temperature holds for 1 – 1000 h in a range
of 1050 – 1300°C in the furnace air atmosphere.
The tests for long-term strength were performed at a
stress of 340 MPa at 975°C in the air atmosphere of the fur -
nace in a VPK-11 machine under uniaxial loading for cylin -
drical specimens with functional part 5 mm in diameter and
25 mm long. The specimens were cut from cast billets
14 mm in diameter subjected to high-temperature holds by
different regimes.
The structure, and the phase and chemical composition
of the alloy and of the protective coatings were studied with
the help of a computer-aided “Neophot-32” light micro -
scope, a “Phillips SEM 535”scanning electron microscope

Cr – Al – Ta – Re – Y IPC + Al – Ni – Cr – Y IPC coating on
the long-term strength of alloy ZhS36-VI we tested coated

results of these tests are presented in Fig. 1. It can be seen
that the endurance of alloy ZhS36-VI with the complex coat-
ing is 20 – 30% higher than that of the uncoated alloy
(Fig. 1). The positive action of the coating on the long-tern
strength of alloy ZhS36-VI is intensified upon growth in the
temperature and time of the holds.

In the specimens of alloy ZhS36-VI with protective coat-
ing microcracks also arise in long-term tests and propagate
over the tcp-segregations. The complex coating hinders the
high-temperature oxidation of the alloy and decelerates the
development of microcracks on the surface of the specimens
(Figs. 3 and 4), i.e., raises considerably the operating capac -
ity and the endurance of the single-crystal ZhS-36-VI.
The diffusion processes occurring in high-temperature
holds at 1050 – 1250°C yield a secondary reaction zone
(SRZ) on the “alloy ZhS36-VI [001] – diffusion zone” inter-
face [4, 7]; the reaction zone has a three-phase /c103– /c103/c162–
tcp-phase structure. The SRZ grows from the diffusion zone
of the coating into the depth of the alloy. The matrix of the
SRZ is a coarse/c103/c162-phase containing segregations of a tcp-phase.
Prolongation of high-temperature holds causes growth of the
SRZ and coarsening of the tcp-phases. Transition from an
acicular (lamellar) shape of the tcp-phase to a lumpy and
round shape develops in the direction from the matrix metal
to the coating (Fig. 4). At a temperature above 1250°C (the
start of intense growth of the /c103/c162-phase in the alloy) a SRZ
does not form under the coating, and the tcp-phases coagu -
late and expand in the diffusion zone of the coating.

The structural transformations on the “coating – matrix
metal” interface depend primarily on the composition of the
alloy and on the alloying elements in the /c103- and /c103/c162-phases.
The wavy pattern of the front of growth of the “SRZ – matrix
metal” interface is connected with the segregation inhomo-
geneity of the alloy in the dendrite arms and arm spacing.
The growth of the thickness of the SRZ obeys a parabolic
law, which is a sign of diffusion nature of its formation.
We established that the volume fraction of the tcp-phase
segregated in the arms of dendrites during a high-tempera -
ture hold affects the long-term strength of alloy ZhS36-VI.
We computed the arithmetic mean of the content of tcp-phase
for the data of measurement at 10 points with a size of
100 /c180100 /c109m lying in the arms of a dendrite cell (Fig. 6).
The fraction of the tcp-phase turned out to be independent of
the presence or absence of coating on the specimens. The
volume fraction of the tcp-phase in the structure of alloy
ZhS36-VI is determined primarily by the processes of diffu -
sion of W, Re, Mo and Cr in the /c103-solid solution and of Al,
T i ,N bi nt h e/c103/c162-phase, as well as by the rate of formation of
nuclei of the tcp-phase.
For long high-temperature holds the stability of the
microstructure of refractory alloys is an important factor de -
termining their mechanical properties in operation. We ana -
lyzed the fine structure of alloy ZhS36-VI with a complex
coating after high-temperature holds and tests for long-term
strength (Fig. 7).
The microstructure of single-crystal ZhS36-VI after a
standard heat treatment is represented by two phases, i.e., a
/c103-matrix and cubic segregations of a hardening /c103/c162-phase
(Ni
3Al). In the tests of specimens in the initial condition (af-
ter a standard heat treatment) for long-term strength we ob-
serve merging of particles the /c103/c162-phase and formation of a
raft structure; dislocation nets occur on the /c103/c47/c103/c162interface. In
this condition the alloy possesses the highest long-term
strength, because the raft structure is a barrier for displace-
ment of dislocations and development of creep [8 – 10].
In long-term holds at 1050 – 1200°C the /c103/c162-phase looses
its cuboid pattern, coagulates, and grows intensely in the di -
rection perpendicular to [001] (Fig. 7). The driving force of
such a change in the morphology of the /c103/c47/c103/c162structure of the
alloy is mismatch between the lattice parameters and the
moduli of elasticity of the /c103- and /c103/c162-phases. The layers of the
/c103-solid solution grow too. A special feature of the structure of
alloy ZhS36-VI [001] after long holds at 1050 – 1200°C is
the formation of tcp-phases with different morphologies
(Fig. 7b and e). Deciphering of electron diffraction patterns
has shown that these tcp phases are a /c109-phase of type
(Ni, Co)

have the following approximate chemical composition
(in wt.%): 23 Ni, 8 Co, 40 W, 20 Re, 4 Mo, and 5 Cr. Intense
formation of /c103-phase occurs in the arms of dendrite cells;
they are enriched with the /c103-forming elements of the alloy

/c103-phases and, as a consequence, depletion of the /c103-matrix
with respect to Re and W result in decrease in the long-term
strength of the alloy. The long-term strength of alloy
ZhS36-VI is correlated to the content of the formed /c109-phases
(Fig. 6).

CONCLUSIONS
1. The main feature of the structure of alloy ZhS36-VI
after long-term high-temperature holds is the formation of
tcp-phases with different morphologies, which have the fol -
lowing chemical composition (in wt.%): 23 Ni, 8 Co, 40 W,
20 Re, 4 Mo, and 5 Cr. Intense formation of these phases oc-
curs in the arms of dendrite cells; they are surrounded by a
shell from /c103/c162-phase.
2. Coarsening of the/c103/c47/c103/c162-structure, segregation of/c103-phases
and, as a consequence, depletion of the /c103-matrix with respect
to Re and W result in lowering of the long-term strength of
the alloy. The long-term strength of alloy ZhS36-VI is corre-
lated to the content of the formed /c109-phases.
3. The structural transformations on the interface of the
matrix metal (alloy ZhS36-VI with /c103/c47/c103/c162-structure) and the
diffusion zone of the complex coating cause the appearance
and growth of a secondary reaction zone during high-tempe -
rature holds; the zone has a three-phase /c103– /c103/c162– /c109structure.
4. The time before failure of alloy ZhS36-VI with a com-
plex Cr – Al GCC + Ni – Cr – Al – Ta – Re – Y IPC + Al –
Ni – Cr – Y IPC coating tested for long-term strength is
25 – 30% longer than that of the alloy without coating, de -
spite the formation of a secondary reaction zone under the
coating.
