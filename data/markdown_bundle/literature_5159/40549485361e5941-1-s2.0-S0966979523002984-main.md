1. Introduction 
Developing materials with a combination of high strength, high 
toughness and low mass density is particularly attractive for future key 
engineering applications [ 1 – 4 ]. The advent of high entropy alloys 
(HEAs) opens up a wide composition space in the inner region of 
hyper-dimensional multicomponent phase diagrams [ 5 – 8 ]. 
Al
x
CoCrFeNi HEA has been extensively studied for its controllable 
phase structure and mechanical properties. With the increase of Al 
content, the single FCC ( < 7 at.%) of Al
x
CoCrFeNi HEA is transformed 
into BCC structure ( > 18 at.%) composed of ordered B2 and disordered 
BCC phases, with the increase of strength and plasticity sacrifice [ 9 – 13 ]. 
When the Al content exceeds 20 at.% to 33 at.% (1 ≤ x ≤ 2), the strength 
and hardness is tending to be saturation (about 500 HV) and the 
ductility drops precipitously [ 14 – 16 ], which concludes that further 
increasing Al content has no benefit to the mechanical properties, 
resulting in the Al
x
CoCrFeNi HEAs with higher Al content has not 
attracted much attention and reports. 
In the current study, we prepared an as-cast high Al content 
Al
40
Cr
15
Fe
15
Co
15
Ni
15 
HEA composed of BCC, B2 and σ phases exhibiting 
ultrahigh room- and high-temperature strength-toughness and specific 
yield strength, which is distinct from previous reported and predicted. 
High-energy synchrotron XRD, electron backscatter diffraction (EBSD), 
scanning electron microscope (SEM) and high resolution transmission 
electron microscopy (HRTEM) were used to analyze the microstructure 
and strengthening mechanism in detail.

4. Discussion 
4.1. Strengthening mechanism 
The Al
40
Cr
15
Fe
15
Co
15
Ni
15 
HEA showed excellent strength – ductility 
combination, which is attributed to the following aspects. Firstly, the 
high strength of this HEA is mainly owing to solid solution strength -
ening. The difference in atomic size of elements leads to severe lattice 
stress and strain, the crystal structure and binding force between con -
stituent elements tend to variation as the surrounding elements. These 
imbalanced and asymmetric combinations may lead to extreme lattice 
distortion and uneven stress fields throughout the sample. These stress 
fields interact with dislocations, hindering their motion and increasing 
the yield stress of HEAs [ 17 ]. Also, the strength increase caused by solid 
solution strengthening is also related to the concentration of solute 
atoms. In this HEA, the Al content reaches 40 at.%, which is more than 
the reported Al
x
CrFeCoNi HEAs, and the atomic radius of Al (1.43 Å) is 
significantly larger than Cr, Fe, Co and Ni (average 1.243 Å), leading to 
higher lattice distortion and solution strengthening effect [ 17 , 49 ]. Sec -
ondly, the brittle Cr-rich σ phase in interdendrites can inhibit the 
dislocation motion and increase the alloy strength [ 50 ]. Thirdly, 
compared to other BCC-AlCrFeCoNi HEA with low Al content, the BCC 
spherical nanoprecipitates in Al
40
Cr
15
Fe
15
Co
15
Ni
15 
HEA have smaller 
size and higher density, which can not only enable the release of the 
stress caused by dislocations accumulation, but also absorb the external 
energy, delay cracks propagation, improve the ductility of the alloy. And 
the phase boundary and grain boundary can effectively hinder the 
dislocation motion, the decrease of grain size and phase region in the 
alloy is accompanied by the decrease of the dislocation motion mean 
free path, resulting in an increase of strength [ 13 , 51 ]. Furthermore, the 
phase interface of B2 phase and BCC precipitate is highly coherent. The 
lattice parameters of B2 and BCC phases are 2.875 and 2.878, respec -
tively, and the lattice mismatch is only 0.2 %, which effectively lowers 
the elastic misfit energy between the nuclei of the BCC nanoparticles and 
the B2 phase. The correlatived elastic interaction between nano -
precipitates and cutting dislocations is reduced, thus preventing crack 
initiation at the interfaces between precipitate and matrix owing to 
strain accumulation [ 1 , 30 , 52 ].
