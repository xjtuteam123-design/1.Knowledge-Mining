ABSTRACT 
The effect of moderate cryo-rolling (~65 % reduction) and annealing on microstructure and mechanical prop -
erties of a cobalt-free (FCC + B2) AlCrFe
2
Ni
2 
high entropy alloy (HEA) was investigated. The cryo-rolled HEA 
showed a nanocrystalline structure with profuse nano-twins and stacking faults. The lamellar structure gradually 
transformed into a complete microduplex structure with increasing annealing temperature. The remarkably 
heterogeneous microstructure at the intermediate annealing temperature consisted of a mix of lamellar and 
microduplex domains and showed strength-ductility synergy superior to other cobalt-free HEAs. The present 
results established the efficacy of moderate cryo-rolling in tailoring heterogeneous microstructure and tensile 
properties of HEAs. 
1. Introduction 
High entropy alloys (HEA) are developed as multicomponent sys -
tems with equiatomic/ near-equiatomic compositions [1,2] . The possi -
bility of stabilizing simple solid solution phases due to increased 
configurational entropy enabled HEAs to gain considerable attention 
[1] . The extension of this novel alloy design concept to non-equiatomic 
and multiphase systems has unveiled the gigantic composition space for 
realizing novel materials with advanced properties [3] . 
The properties of HEAs can be enhanced by compositional and 
microstructural tuning using thermo-mechanical processing. Notably, 
eutectic HEAs (EHEAs) such as (L1
2 
+ B2) AlCoCrFeNi
2.1 
have been 
developed with novel heterogeneous microstructures and remarkable 
properties [4] . However, the presence of costly constituents, e.g., cobalt, 
is prohibitive. To overcome this challenge, new cobalt-free and cost- 
effective HEAs like AlCrFe
2
Ni
2 
with promising properties are being 
developed by severe cold-rolling and annealing [5] . 
Meanwhile, cryo-rolling is a successful processing strategy for tuning 
heterogeneous microstructure and properties in HEAs [4] . However, the 
effect of cryo-rolling in AlCrFe
2
Ni
2 
is yet to be clarified. Herein, we 
demonstrate the efficacy of moderate cryo-rolling in enhancing the 
mechanical properties of AlCrFe
2
Ni
2 
HEA.

4. Discussion 
Cryo-rolling leads to a nanocrystalline lamellar structure due to the 
grain subdivision process [11] . The mechanism involves the accumu -
lation of dislocations, systematic increment in misorientation, and ulti -
mate transformation of the LABs into HABs. The low SFE of the FCC 
phase (restricting cross-slip) and faster generation and preservation of 
dislocations result in an accelerated structural evolution so that a 
nanocrystalline structure is obtained at a moderate deformation during 
cryo-rolling. 
The cryo-rolled nanostructure having profuse nucleation sites (HABs, 
shear bands, and other heterogeneities) ensures ultrafine microstruc -
tures after annealing. The gradual transformation of lamellar to a het -
erogeneous (lamellar + microduplex) and finally to a fully microduplex 
structure is attributed to the instability of lamellar morphology. The B2 
phase fraction increases at lower temperatures but shows a reversal in 
trend beyond 900 
◦
C. This is consistent with the increased stability of the 
FCC phase at higher annealing temperatures and with the general trend 
observed for dual-phase HEAs [12] .

softer FCC and harder B2 phases. The hardness of FCC phases is not 
much sensitive to decreasing temperature, while the hardness of BCC/B2

The heterogeneous microstructure of the 900 
◦
C annealed specimen 
appears most interesting. The microduplex region shows a higher frac -
tion of the hard B2 and thus higher KAM. Consequently, deformation of 
the heterogeneous microstructure having widely different hardness 
domains leads to mechanical incompatibility. The incompatibility favors 
strain gradients to develop, accommodated by the emission and pile-up 
of dislocations at the domain interfaces/boundaries [13] . These phe -
nomena lead to long-range back-stress resisting the yielding of the softer 
phase until the hard domains undergo yielding. Consequently, the 
overall yield strength and back-stress-induced strain-hardening are 
enhanced. The propensity of deformation twin formation further en -
hances strain-hardening. These contribute to a high strain-hardening 
rate with multistage recovery, ensuring superior strength-ductility 
synergy. 
5. Conclusion 
The cryo-rolled HEA showed the formation of a nanocrystalline 
lamellar structure with profuse nano-twins and stacking faults. The 
progressive transformation from lamellar to ultrafine microduplex 
structure happened with increasing temperature. The remarkably het -
erogeneous microstructure attained at the intermediate annealing tem -
perature (900 
◦
C/1h), consisting of a mix of lamellar and microduplex 
domains with different phase fractions, deformation states, and hard -
ness, offered remarkable strength-ductility synergy superior to other 
cobalt-free HEAs.
