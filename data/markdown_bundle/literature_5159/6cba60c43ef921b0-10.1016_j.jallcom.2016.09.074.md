1. Introduction 2 
As a near- α type titanium alloy, Ti60 alloy possesses excellen t combination of 3 
mechanical and physical properties, such as good st rength, high fatigue resistance and 4 
creep resistance characteristics at service temperature of 600°C, which has considered 5 
a candidate material for high temperature applicati on to fabricate engine parts such as 6 
aero-compressor disk and engine vanes in aviation a nd aerospace industries [1-4]. 7 
TiAl intermetallic compounds have attracted signifi cant interest for high-temperature 8 
applications due to their low density, excellent hi gh-temperature strength, high creep 9 
resistance and oxidation resistance [5-8]. Among th e TiAl alloys, high Nb-containing 10 
TiAl alloys (TAN) are regarded as the demonstration of developing high-performance 11 
and high-temperature TiAl alloys. The addition of element Nb into TiAl alloy not only 12 
promotes the room temperature ductility and the hig h temperature strength, but also 13 
improves the creep resistance and high temperature oxidation above 800 °C, however, 14 
the poor workability limits their practical applica tions [9-12]. In order to expand the 15 
applications of the above alloys in the engine manu facturing technical field, reliable 16 
methods and technologies for joining Ti60 alloy to high Nb-containing TiAl alloys are 17 
necessary. 18 
In the joining technologies of dissimilar materials , brazing has been considered 19 
as the most effective method due to its convenience and cost-effectiveness [13-15]. In 20 
recent years, many researches have focused on brazi ng titanium alloys or TiAl alloys 21

4. Conclusions 3 
Brazing of Ti60 alloy to TAN alloy was successfully achieved by using Ti-28Ni 4 
filler foil in this study. The interfacial microstr ucture and joining properties of the 5 
brazed joints were investigated in details. The int erfacial microstructure of Ti60/ TAN 6 
joints brazed at relative low temperature was Ti60 substrate/ Lamellar ( α+β) 7 
microstructure/ Ti 2Ni phase layer + α-Ti / α-Ti layer + Ti 2Ni/ Ti 3Al layer/ B2 phase 8 
layer/ TAN substrate. As the brazing temperature in creased, the diffusion of Ni atoms 9 
was accelerated, leading to the disappearance of Ti 2Ni and Ti 3Al phase and the 10 
significant growth of B2 phase. Fracture analysis i ndicated that cracks propagated in 11 
Ti 2Ni layer when brazing temperature was lower (1020 ° C and 1040 °C), and the 12 
mixture of Ti 3Al phase and Lamellar ( α+β) microstructure also do harm to the brazed 13 
joint. The absence of brittle phase such as Ti 2Ni and Ti 3Al benefited the joining 14 
properties of Ti60/ TAN joints, and the maximum she ar strength of ~140 MPa was 15 
obtained when brazing at 1080 °C for 10 min. Howeve r, higher brazing temperature, 16 
leading to the excessive growth of B2 phase in the brazing seam, deteriorated the 17 
joining properties. 18
