Introduction 
Advanced Ni-based superalloys contains relatively a large amount of refractory elements 
[2][3][4]. As an example, W increases the creep strength, but decreases the corrosion resistance at 
high temperatures. Re increases the high temperature properties, but makes homogenization 
difficult because of its very small diffusivity in Ni-based alloys. Ru and Co suppresses the 
precipitating of TCP phase in Ni-base alloys. In particular, Ru increases the creep strength [5]. It is 
important for designing superalloys to understand these phenomena such as the precipitation and 
the phase transition, which are related strongly to the atomic diffusivity. Purposes of this study are 
to estimate the interdiffusion coefficients of Ni-X-Y (X, Y= Co, Ru, W, Re) and to calculate the 
activation energies and frequency factors. Here, the interdiffusion coefficients are related to the 
atomic interaction [6].

Experimental Procedure 
Button ingots of pure Ni, Ni-5X (X = Co, W, Re), Ni-8Ru, Ni-5Co-5Y (Y = Ru, W, Re), and 
Ni-8Ru-5Z (Z = Re, W) were prepared by arc melting in high purity argon atmosphere. Row 
materials used were 4N Ni, Co (>99.5at%), 3N Ru, W (>99%), Re (>99%). The compositions of 
ternary alloys were so selected in the solid solution region at 1523K. The ingots were cut and cold 
rolled into plate specimens followed by annealing at 1523 in order to obtain large grains suitable for 
diffusion experiments. After polishing the plate specimens mechanically, two kinds of alloys were 
set into the diffusion couple with Mo holder. Here, Al 2O3 fiber was sandwiched between the two 
alloys. Then, the diffusion couple encapsulated in a quartz tube with argon was annealed for 
interdiffusion at 1523K for 72 hours. After this diffusion annealing the couple was cut parallel to the 
diffusion direction and the section was examined with a scanning electron microscope (SEM) 
equipped with an energy dispersive X-ray spectrometer (EDX) in order to measure the 
concentration profile. The origin of the concentration profile is set at the position of the Al 2O3 fiber.
