4. Conclusions 
This paper inv estigat es the synthesis and properties of 
AlCoFeMnNi-1 wt%CNT nanocomposite. In this study , elemental 
powders w ere first milled up to 40 h to attain a solid solution. CNT 
powder w as added at last 5 h of milling. Mechanicall y alloy ed

1 . Mechanical alloying up to 40 h is associated with the formation of

and EDS analy sis show ed that alloyi ng elements are perfectly 
homogeneousl y dispersed inside the matrix. 
2. SEM images of mechanically alloy ed powders show ed that 
powders initially had differ ent morphologies. First 20 h of milling

cimens in this alloying syst em is a dual α + γ structure. 
4. CNT -containing nanocomposite show ed a much impro ved ulti -

solid solution effects from CNT s.
