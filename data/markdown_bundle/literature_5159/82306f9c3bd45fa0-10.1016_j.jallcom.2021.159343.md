Het erog eneous microstructur es - which ha ve a multimodal grain

between high strength and reasonable ductility [2 1 ,22,28]. In the 
present alloy s, the “hard” phase corr esponding to the UFG-LPSO 
matrix decorat ed with numer ous nano-dispersoids deliv ers the ul -
trahigh stren gth, while the “soft ” phase corresponding to the dis -
cret e LPSO domains can undertake plastic deformation, pr eventing 
the alloy s from prematur e fractur e. U nder compression or bending, 
the LPSO phase can deform via a distinctiv e deformation pattern of 
kinking through basal dislocations slip [1 3,29,30]. How ever , the 
UFG-LPSO matrix can hardl y deform at RT owing to the ultrafine 
microstructur e and the strong pinning effect re sulting from the 
high-density nano-dispersoids. By contrast, the soft LPSO domains 
can re adily deform via kinking, contributing to the compressi ve 
plasticity at RT . A re lativ ely large angle of ~9.3° between the basal 
planes of the unbent and bent areas of the elongat ed grain in the sof t 
LPSO domains can be observed (Fig. 7(a)). In spite of the brittle 
q uasi-clea v age fractur e of the UFG-LPSO, as shown in Fig. 7(b), a 
ductile fract ure is observed in the area of soft LPSO domains, in -
dicating a relati vel y large plastic deformation at RT . 
A t elev ated temper atures, slight kinking of the UFG-LPSO can also 
be observed (Fig. 7(c)), whereas it w ould be larg ely suppressed by the 
stiff nano-dispersoids inside the kink band, as sho wn in the inset of 
Fig. 7(c). Mean while, despite gr ain boundary deformations such as 
grain boundary sliding may be activ e at elev ated temper atures due to 
the ultr afine gr ain size of the UFG-LPSO, it can also be effecti vel y 
pinned by the nano-dispersoids at grain boundaries (Fig. 7(d)). Thus, 
the hard UFG-LPSO matrix can be e xpected to maintain a high str ength 
at elev ated temper ature s, wher e superior strength sustaining up to 
250 °C is achieved for the hierar chical LPSO allo ys. In addition, the 
elev ated-t emperatur e str ength can be further impro ved via te xture 
strengthening [7] resulting from the elongated gr ains of the UFG-LPSO 
matrix and discret e LPSO domains with the basal planes paralleling to 
the e xtrusion directi on after e xtrusion, as shown in Fig. 2(e). Ho wev er ,

5. Conclusions 
(1) A nov el hierar chical long-period stacking order ed (LPSO) archi -

lo ys with high heat resistance in the Mg- Y -Zn based alloy . The 
hiera rchical LPSO is featured by a heter ogeneous microstructur e 
of UFG-LPSO matrix and discret e LPSO lath domains at the mi -

achiev ed for the first time to date. 
(2) The nov el hiera rchical LPSO alloy s ha ve ultrahigh strength - 
which is much higher than that of pr eviously report ed heat-re -

(3) The hard UFG-LPSO matrix decorat ed with profuse stiff nano-

the elev ated-t emperat ure strength can be further impro ved after

at room temper ature. 
(4) By tuning the processing conditions, hierar chical LPSO alloy s 
with adjustable UFG-LPSO fractions (r anging from ~52% to 
~85%) and corresponding mechanical properties can be synthe -
sized. The ultrah igh strength, thermal stability combined with 
low density and e x cellent specific properties demonstra te a 
pot ential microstructur al strat egy to design lightweight high- 
strength Mg alloy s for adv anced structural applications.
