ABSTRACT 
Alloying effects on the structural, elastic, as well as electronic properties of Ni-based model superalloys are 
systematically investigated by first-principles calculations. Twenty-seven transition-metal(TM) elements (3d:Sc- 
Zn, 4d:Y-Cd, 5d:Hf-Au) are taken into account in Ni-based ternary model superalloys in detail for the first time. 
The partitioning behaviors and site preferences of ternary alloying elements are primarily predicted based on our 
calculated substitutional formation energies and partitioning coefficients, with previous atom probe tomography 
(APT) experimental results for comparison. Temperature dependence of partitioning behaviors of TM elements in 
model superalloys is also presented in this work. It is found that the partitioning behaviors of Cr, Mn, Tc, Ru and 
Os can be reversed at a specific temperature, and these elements just lie near the boundary between the two 
blocks of Ni-site preferences and Al-site preferences of TM elements on the periodic table. The bulk moduli, 
Young ’ s moduli, shear moduli, ductile – brittle behavior, orientation-dependent elastic moduli and elastic 
anisotropy of Ni-based ternary model superalloys are systematically estimated. The calculated elastic parameters 
agree well with the available experimental results. The effects of TM elements on elastic properties are found to 
be closely associated with their d -electron number. The elements at the beginnings and ends of the TM series 
reduce these elastic properties, while the TM elements lying towards the center of each period can considerably 
increase these properties of superalloys, especially those elements with a half-filled d band, which exert greater 
influences on improving the elastic performance. Furthermore, different charge redistributions caused by each 
alloying addition can elucidate different effects thereof on the elastic properties of model superalloys to some 
extent. The stronger directional bonding and more covalent-like interaction between TM additions and host 
atoms lead to the superior elastic properties.

Ni-based superalloys, it is desirable and essential to investigate the effect

moduli of Ni-based ternary model superalloys are scarce experimentally, 
the present work is the first attempt to systematically predict the elastic 
properties of Ni-based ternary model superalloys with individual TM 
additions and to provide detailed theoretical reference for the further 
experimental research on elastic characteristics of Ni-based superalloys.

charge density differences) of these superalloys when the TM elements 
are doped in the appropriate phases. A summary of the conclusions 
drawn from the present work is given in the last Section of the paper.

4. Conclusions 
In the present work, first-principles calculations have been carried 
out to investigate the alloying dependence of the structural, elastic and 
electronic properties of Ni-based model superalloys. Twenty-seven 
transition-metal(TM) elements have been systematically considered. 
Our calculated elastic parameters are consistent with the available 
experimental data. The main results are briefly recapitulated as follows: 
1. The partitioning behaviors and site preferences of TM solutes 
between γ

APT experimental results for comparison, which can help to better un -

Ni-based superalloys. For Ni-based ternary model superalloys at 1143 K, 
the partitioning and site-preference behaviors of the TM elements are 
obviously divided into three parts on the periodic table: Sc, Ti, V, and Cr 
(the 3 d period), Y, Zr, Nb, Mo, and Tc (the 4 d period), and Hf, Ta, W, Re 
and Os (the 5 d period) prefer to substitute Al-sites of the γ

phase. Pd and Pt tend to substitute Ni-sites of the γ

behavior of the doping element is closely correlated with the tempera -

of Ni-site preferences and Al-site preferences of TM elements in Ni-based 
ternary model superalloys on the periodic table (such as Cr, Mn, Tc, Ru 
or Os) has a greater chance of being reversed. 
2. The effects of TM elements on bulk, Young ’ s and shear moduli of 
Ni-based model superalloys are expected to be related to their number of 
d -electrons. The elements at the beginnings and ends of the TM series 
will decrease these elastic properties, while the TM elements at the 
center of each period can considerably increase these properties of the 
superalloys, especially those elements with a half-filled d band, which 
have greater influences upon improvements in the elastic performance. 
Additionally, the orientation-dependent elastic moduli and Zener 
anisotropy factor of superalloys with different TM additions are also 
presented. The elements at the ends of each TM series, as well as Mn, Ru 
and Ir, will improve the anisotropy to a significant extent, while V and W 
reduce the anisotropy the most.
