Effects of different ratios of Ni to Al on the duct ility and toughness of Ni 33+x Al 28-xCr 30 Mo 4Nb 5 
(x=0, 6, 9, 12) alloys are investigated. High tempe rature compression test is also conducted. The 
results show that with ratio of Ni to Al up to 1.77 , γ phase precipitation result in ductility and 
fracture toughness enhanced at room temperature. Th e reinforced Cr 2Nb-type phase and γ phase 
benefit for the high temperature strength and room temperature ductility, respectively. 
Keywords: NiAl-Cr(Mo)/Nb alloy; γ phase; ambient ductility and toughness. 
1. Introduction 
NiAl is a potential high temperature structure mate rial due to its high melting point, low 
density, high Young’s modulus and excellent oxidati on resistance. But its low room 
temperature ductility, poor fracture toughness and inadequate high temperature strength 
limit its practical use. 
1,2 Fortunately, in recent years, in-situ eutectic NiA l-Cr(Mo) alloy 
with directional solidification provided room tempe rature fracture toughness of 24 
MPa•m 1/2 over that of polycrystalline NiAl (6 MPa•m 1/2 ), 3 but high temperature strength 
still needed to be enhanced, compared with the strength of applied Ni-based superalloys. 
Recently, many studies have found that Heusler phases Ni 2AlX(X=Hf, Ti) and Laves 
phase Cr 2Nb precipitating in NiAl-Cr(Mo) alloy have enhanced high temperature 
strength.4-7 Ductile γ (Ni solid solution) or γ’ (Ni 3Al) phase benefits ambient ductility and 
toughness of NiAl alloy. 8-15 It is considered that Cr 2Nb and γ phase are induced to 
NiAl-Cr(Mo) alloy may enhance high temperature stre ngth and increase ambient ductility 
synchronously. In our study, with 5at.% Niobium add ition to Ni 33 Al 33 Cr 30 Mo 4 
substituting for Al , Ni 33 Al 28 Cr 30 Mo 4Nb 5 formed dispersion strengthening Cr 2Nb phase to 
improve high temperature strength. 16 Ratios of Ni to Al are changed in 
Ni 33+x Al 28-xCr 30 Mo 4Nb 5 alloys to investigate effects of different ratios of Ni to Al on the 
ductility and toughness of the alloy.

2. Experimental Procedures 
The alloy used for this investigation has compositi ons of Ni 
33+x Al 28-xCr 30 Mo 4Nb 5 (x=0, 6, 
9, 12)(atom percent throughout the paper). The mate rial used was arc-melted under an 
argon atmosphere protection from starting materials of 99.98% Ni, 99.9%Al, 99.98%Cr, 
99.9%Mo and 99.7%Nb using a non-consumable tungsten electrode. Each alloy button 
was turned over and remelted four times to get a ho mogeneous specimen. Since weight 
losses were less than 0.5%, the compositions of the alloys were considered to be equal to 
our nominal compositions. The alloy buttons were ho mogeneous at 1173K for 24h in air 
and cooled to room temperature in the furnace. The compression specimens were cut 
from the homogenized buttons with the size of Φ 4mm × 6mm column by 
electro-discharge machining, and all major surfaces were mechanically ground with 
800-grit SiC abrasive before compression test. Micr ostructures of the samples were 
observed by scanning electron microscope (SEM) ， primarily using back-scattered 
electron(BSE) imaging and the volume fractions of e ach phase were calculated by an 
image analysis method. The compositions were detect ed by Energy Dispersive X-ray 
Analysis (EDX) attached to the SEM. X-ray diffracti on analysis was used to identify the 
structures of the phases in a Rigaku D/MAX2200PC us ing Cu Κα radiation .The 
transmission electron microscope(TEM) was used to c haracterize the microstructure of 
trace phases and determine the new phase. The compr ession test was conducted in air 
with a MTS880 machine from RT to 1373K and the reco rded stress-strain curves were 
converted to the true stress-strain curves via the assumption of the constant volume.

4. Conclusion 
Effects of different ratios of Ni to Al on the room temperature ductility and toughness of 
Ni 
33+x Al 28-xCr 30 Mo 4Nb 5 alloys are investigated. The results show: 
(1) Ni 33+x Al 28-xCr 30 Mo 4Nb 5 is composed of four phases: NiAl phase, Cr(Mo) pha se , 
(Cr,Ni) 2Nb phase and γ phase precipitation at grain boundaries with ratio s of Ni to Al up 
to 1.77. With increase of ratios of Ni to Al from 1 .77 to 2.81 the volume fractions of γ 
phase increase. 
(2) With increase of the volume fractions of γ phase, room temperature compression 
ductility and fracture toughness of the alloys impr ove about 397% and 238%, 
respectively. Furthermore, room temperature yield s trength is enhanced but high 
temperature yield strength at 1373K decreases.
