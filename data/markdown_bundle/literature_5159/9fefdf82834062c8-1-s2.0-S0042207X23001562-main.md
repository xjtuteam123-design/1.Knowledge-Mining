ABSTRACT 
In order to further improve the strength and toughness of W – Ta solid solutions and ensure the high specific 
gravity characteristics of the material, the effect of low content of Nb on the microstructure evolution and 
performance of W – 30Ta solid solutions was performed. The results showed that the phase composition of 
W – Ta – Nb alloys was single phase W – Ta – Nb solid solutions. The compressive strength of W – Ta – Nb solid solutions 
increased first and then decreased upon increasing the Nb content. The amount of solid solution of Nb in 
W – Ta – Nb solid solutions increased and the interplanar spacing increased upon increasing the Nb content. Nb has 
the effect of refining the grain of tungsten alloy. The grain size gradually decreased upon increasing the Nb 
content. The addition of a small amount of Nb can effectively improve the strength and toughness of W – Ta – Nb 
solid solutions. The addition of a small amount of Nb reduced the energy required for the initiation of ductile 
dislocations in W – Ta – Nb solid solutions. 
1. Introduction 
Tungsten based solid solution materials are widely used in aero -
space, military, advanced manufacturing and other fields because of 
their high strength, a high density, a high melting point, high hardness 
and a low coefficient of thermal expansion [ 1 – 3 ]. However, the inherent 
brittleness of pure tungsten greatly limits its further application. The 
brittleness of tungsten shows the phenomenon of anti -Schmidt ’ s law on 
the micro-scale. The main fracture plane is the crystal plane (100) and 
the close-packed plane of tungsten is the crystal plane (110). The reason 
for this is that the crystal plane (100) dislocation slips to the crystal 
plane to form a chisel point, forming the initiation of cracks, which 
serves to pin the dislocations and form immovable dislocations [ 4 , 5 ]. 
The toughness of tungsten materials can be improved by solid solu -
tions of other elements [ 6 – 8 ]. Solid solution toughened tungsten mate -
rials have become a good choice to improve the brittleness of tungsten, 
in particular, the multi-element synergistic strengthening and toughness 
of tungsten materials [ 9 – 11 ]. The addition of tantalum changes the 
deformation mechanism of pure tungsten and increases the dislocation 
density compared with pure tungsten. On the basis of screw dislocations, 
ductile dislocations appear. However, tantalum does not contribute 
enough to the deformability of tungsten based solid solutions [ 12 ]. 
According to the simulation results, the addition of Nb atoms increases 
the ratio of bulk modulus to shear modulus (B/G), Poisson ’ s ratio and 
Cauchy pressure, which means that the addition of Nb can modify the 
brittleness of pure tungsten [ 13 – 15 ]. The melting point of Nb (2468 
◦
C) 
is much lower than that of tungsten (3410 
◦
C) and tantalum (2990 
◦
C). 
Excessive addition of Nb content will significantly reduce the melting 
point of the alloy and affect the high temperature mechanical properties 
of materials [ 16 – 18 ]. In order to ensure the application effect of tung -
sten materials, the effect of a small amount of Nb on tungsten based solid 
solutions was explored. However, there are few reports on related 
experiments. 
In this study, we focus on the issue of further improving the strength 
and the toughness of W – Ta solid solutions. Nb atoms are solid-dissolved 
into the W – Ta solid solutions, replacing part of the tungsten atoms, 
improving the toughness of tungsten and further improving the 
comprehensive properties of W – Ta solid solutions. The influence of 
different compositions and melting processes on the solid solutions 
characteristics, microstructure evolution and strength and toughness of 
W – Ta – Nb alloys materials were studied in this paper.

2. Experimental procedures 
2.1. Preparation of tungsten alloys 
The raw materials used in the experiment were high-purity tungsten 
powder (particle size 3 μ m, purity 99.99%), high-purity tantalum power 
(particle size 5 μ m, purity 99.95%) and high-purity niobium power 
(particle size 1 μ m, purity 99.95%). First, the raw material composition 
was weighed according to the design and poured into the mixing tank. 
Then, argon protective gas was introduced into the tank. The ball mill 
tank was installed on the three-dimensional motion mixer for mixing for 
8 h. The ratio of ball to materials was 1:1, at rotational rate of 12 r/min.

Fig. 3 shows SEM images of tungsten based solid solution materials 
with different Nb content prepared by cold isostatic pressing and arc 
melting technology. The crystal grains were closely bonded and no 
obvious cracks were found. The structure of tungsten-based solid solu -
tion materials prepared by arc smelting was pure. The grain size of 
tungsten-based solid solutions with different niobium content was large. 
It was mainly composed of equiaxed grains and a small amount of 
elongated grains. There was still a little gap between grains in the 
W – 30Ta alloys, but there were no obvious cracks between the grains 
with Nb added. Xue et al. [ 19 ] found in laser melting W – Nb alloys that 
Nb had the effect of suppressing micro-cracks. The grain size decreased 
gradually with the increase of niobium content. It showed that Nb can 
significantly reduce the grain size of tungsten matrix solid solutions by 
comparing the grain size of W – 30Ta alloys. The size of the crystal grain 
changed significantly upon increasing the niobium content. When the 
content of Nb was 3 wt%, the minimum size of the crystal grains can 
reach 100 μ m. The reason was that the melting point of tungsten and 
tantalum were high. There was a liquid-solid semi solid range. The 
addition of niobium further increased the liquid-solid semi solid range. 
During the solidification process, there were more nucleation media, 
preferential nucleation, which played a significant role in grain refine -
ment. Studies had showed that the grain size of melted tungsten was 
very coarse, which seriously affected the properties of the material 
[ 20 – 22 ]. Kang et al. [ 23 ] realized the refinement of tungsten grains by 
arc smelting by adding SiC particles. The refinement of tungsten grains 
from millimeters to micrometers. It was reported in Refs. [ 24 , 25 ] that 
Nb can refine grains, which was similar to the results in this studied.

melting, X-ray fluorescence spectrometry was performed on positions 1, 
2, 3 and 4 in Fig. 3 . The results were shown in Table 4 . It can be seen

The relative density of W – Ta – Nb tungsten based solid solutions 
increased gradually and then tended to be constant upon increasing the 
Nb content and the relative density can reach up to 99.6%. The increase

Fig. 9 demonstrates the effect of the content of Nb on the Vickers 
hardness of tungsten based solid solutions. The Vickers hardness of 
W – Ta – Nb solid solutions increased and then decreased upon increasing 
the Nb content. This was because upon increasing the Nb content, Nb 
was completely dissolved into tungsten based solid solutions, which 
meant that the solid solution Nb content increased, which conformed to 
the principle of solid solution strengthening, so the Vickers hardness of 
the material increased. The hardness had increased with the further 
increase of the Nb content from the perspective of the solid solution 
strengthening mechanism. The grain refinement reduced the tendency 
of stress concentration, the cracks were difficult to produce and 
propagate. The refinement of the grains had the effect of grain refine -
ment strengthening. However, Nb was an additional atom. Compared 
with tungsten, the hardness of Nb was much lower than that of tungsten. 
This strength mechanism and compound effect eventually leaded to a 
decrease in the hardness of tungsten-based materials. The hardness of 
the sample was affected by solid solution strengthening, fine grain 
strengthening and composite effect. 
Fig. 10 shows the effect of the content of Nb on the hardness and 
modulus of W – Ta – Nb solid solutions. The hardness of W – Ta – Nb solid 
solutions increased and then decreased upon increasing the Nb content. 
The average hardness of W – 30Ta solid solutions without niobium 
addition was 8.3 GPa and the elastic modulus was 300 GPa. The 
maximum hardness of tungsten based solid solutions reached 10.9 GPa. 
This was due to the addition of more niobium, more niobium replaced 
tungsten in solid solution to the tungsten lattice and played a role in 
solid solution strengthening. The modulus of W – Ta – Nb solid solutions 
decreased upon increasing the Nb content, which showed a more 
obvious solid solution softening phenomenon.

4. Conclusions 
(1) The W – Ta – Nb solid solution materials with uniform structure 
were successfully prepared by cold isostatic pressing and arc 
melting technology. The main phase of the W – Ta – Nb solid solu -
tion materials was W – Ta – Nb solid solutions. The amount of Nb 
dissolved in the W – Ta – Nb solid solution materials increased upon 
increasing the Nb content. Nb had a significant effect on the 
structure of tungsten-based solid solutions. Upon increasing the 
Nb content, the lattice constant increased and the interplanar 
spacing increased. 
(2) The relative density of W – Ta – Nb tungsten based solid solutions 
increased gradually and then tended to be constant upon 
increasing the Nb content. The Vickers hardness of W – Ta – Nb 
solid solutions increased and then decreased upon increasing the 
Nb content. 
(3) The compression strength of W – Ta – Nb solid solutions first 
increased and then decreased upon increasing the Nb content. 
The maximum compressive strength was as high as 1.7 GPa and 
the strain was up to 24%. The fracture of W – Ta – Nb solid solutions 
with Nb addition was mainly intergranular fracture and trans -
granular fracture.
