Keywords: Ti3Al based alloy; powder metallurgy; expansion part; preparation. 
 
Abstract. Ti3Al based alloy has performed a comprehensive properties of low density, high tensile 
strength and excellent oxidation resistant at high temperature, which results in a good potential 
application in aerospace areas. In this paper, technologies relevant to preparation of Ti-23Al-17Nb 
expansion part for nozzle have been studied, and main properties of the part have been test and 
evaluated. The result revealed that Ti 3Al based alloy prepared by powder metallurgy can replace 
nickel based alloy to be a candidate materials for high temperature structures. 
Introduction 
Due to performing a comprehensive property of low density, good oxidation and excellent high 
tensile strength under 800˚C, Ti3Al based alloy has become one of the most important materials for 
structures applied in high temperature[1]. Compared with Inconel 718 alloy, Ti3Al based alloy can be 
performed as structural material under 800 ˚C, which results in a great lose weight by about 35% . 
The most traditional methods for preparation of Ti 3Al based alloy are foundry and forging. In 
some cases, high contents of alloying atoms may lead to segregations of chemical composition and 
microstructures during fusion and foundry, which results in a low stability and reliability of tensile 
strength. However, compacts of Ti3Al based alloy with homogeneous composition of alloying atoms 
can be gained by HIP pre -alloyed powders. Thus, problems created by segregation of chemical 
composition can be avoided [2]. Powder metallurgy can be considered as one of the most perfect 
methods for preparation Ti 3Al based alloy parts with large size and thin wall. In the example of 
preparation of Ti-23Al-17Nb expansion part for nozzle, technologies concerned with preparatio n of 
Ti3Al based alloy part with large size and thin wall have been introduced. 
Preparation of Ti-23Al-17Nb alloy expansion part for nozzle

2.1 Preparation of the capsule 
In the study of powder metallurgy of Ti 3Al based alloy, powders were condensed in a capsule 
which was made by nonferrous steel or mild steel. The shape and size of capsule have a great effect on 
those of the compact gained after HIP process. Thus, preparation of capsule for condensation is the 
emphases of technologies for preparation of powder metallurgy Ti-23Al-17Nb expansion parts. It is 
generally speaking that size and shape of the capsule depends on the tap density and theoretical 
density. The equation of design for capsule can be shown as equation (1) [3].

 ρ2: theoretical density of Ti-23Al-17Nb alloy 
However, structures and thickness of capsule have a shielding effect to pressure created by HIP 
machines which leads to the variation of pressure to powders of every part. The variation of pressure 
results in variation of deformation in every part of capsule. Machining allowance must be added to 
the size of the capsule to avoid absence of the compact. Therefore, these factors must be considered 
during the design of the capsule.

2.3 Degassing 
The ratio of tap density to theoretical density for powders is about 0.63, so 37 % of room in the 
capsule is gap. These gaps are full of air with adsorp tion of powders. Therefore, capsule must be 
degassing before HIP process, otherwise a fully condensed compact can ’t be gained. The capsule 
needs to be warmed to a certain temperature to improve efficiency of degassing. Air pressure inside 
the capsule varied with elongation of time for degassing and was shown as fig. 3. During the period of 
degassing at room temperature, air pressure inside the capsule was decreasing with elongation of time 
for degassing; when the capsule was warmed to a certain temperature, air absorbed by powders was 
leaking gradually which resulted in the increasing of air pressure inside the capsule. In the following, 
air pressure inside the capsule was decreasing slowly with elongation of time for degassing. When air 
pressure inside the capsule varie d in a small extent, the capsule c ould be sealed and condensed by 
HIP. 
 
Fig 3 Diagram of connection between air pressure inside capsule and time for degassing 
 
2.4 HIP process of the capsule loaded with Ti-23Al-17Nb pre-alloyed powders 
The capsule loaded with Ti -23Al-17Nb pre-alloyed powders is condensed in a condition of hot 
temperature and hot pressure created HIP machines. Many studies have revealed that those factors 
like temperature, pressure and time, have made a great effect on the density of the compact gained by 
HIP [7, 8]. The common method of HIP process for Ti-23Al-17Nb pre-alloyed powers is processed at 
a temperature between 900 to 1000 ˚C with a pressure large than 100 MPa for a period of 4 hours. 
Tensile properties at room temperature had been test and the result ha d been shown as table 2. It 
can be revealed from table 2 that tensile strength of the compact is reached to 815 MPa and elongation 
is 2.0%.

2.5 Microstructure modification of powder metallurgy Ti-23Al-17Nb alloy compact 
Studies of Li and Yang reveal that microstructures of Ti3Al based alloy have a great effect on their 
tensile properties at room temperature. Therefore , preparation and following hot treatments are the 
emphasis of research for Ti 3Al based alloy [ 9~10]. Tensile strengths and elongations of 
Ti-23Al-17Nb compact gained in HIP process are low which results in obstacle during engineering 
applications. Thus, microstructure modification of powder metallurgy Ti -23Al-17Nb compact must 
be performed. The most common method of microstructure modification for Ti 3Al based alloy is 
solid solution and aging. Studies of Gogia show that temperature of solid solution and cooling speed 
have a great effect on shapes, distribution of orthorhombic phase and α２, which results in variations 
of properties for alloys gained during process es[11]. Tensile properties at room temperature of 
powder metallurgy Ti-23Al-17Nb alloys gained in different processes have been shown in table 3.
