ABSTRACT 
GO reinforced high-temperature titanium alloy matrix composite with excellent high-temperature strength and 
ductility was successfully fabricated by hot isostatic pressing and heat treatment. After solution and aging 
treatment, the high-temperature strength of the composite was significantly improved because the increase of in- 
situ TiC at the interface enhanced interface bonding and the microstructure changed from equiaxed structure to 
bimodal structure. At the same time, the dissolution of silicides increased the high-temperature ductility. After 
solution at 1075 
◦
C and aging at 700 
◦
C, the tensile strength, yield strength and elongation at 600 
◦
C of the high- 
temperature titanium alloy with 0.3 wt% GO reached 749 MPa, 571 MPa and 18.5%, respectively. 
1. Introduction 
Ti – Al – Sn – Zr – Mo – Si high-temperature titanium alloys are the key 
materials of future high-performance aero-engines [ 1 , 2 ]. In order to 
improve the high-temperature mechanical properties, high-temperature 
titanium alloys are usually strengthened by ceramic materials. However, 
the ceramics strengthening is often accompanied by a sharp decline in 
ductility. The high-temperature elongations of TiC or TiB reinforced 
high-temperature titanium alloy matrix composites decreased by more 
than 50% compared with the matrix alloys [ 3 – 6 ]. 
Graphene oxide (GO) is two-dimensional carbon nanomaterial with 
large specific surface area, high tensile strength and Young ’ s modulus 
[ 7 , 8 ], which can improve the room-temperature strength of titanium or 
titanium alloys without reducing ductility [ 9 , 10 ]. After adding 0.2 vol% 
GO, the room-temperature compressive strength of pure titanium 
increased from 1323 MPa to 2062 MPa, and the plastic strain increased 
from 4.08% to 7.58% [ 9 ]. However, there are still some problems in 
achieving both high strength and high ductility at high temperature. Our 
previous study showed that the 600 
◦
C elongation of high-temperature 
titanium alloy with 0.3 wt% GO prepared by hot isostatic pressing 
(HIP) was only 5.2%, although yield strength increased by 21% [ 11 ]. 
The decrease of high-temperature ductility may be caused by the pre -
cipitation of silicides and the weak interface reaction. In this study, the 
effects of heat treatment on microstructure, silicides, interface bonding, 
high-temperature tensile property and strengthening mechanisms of 
HIPed GO reinforced high-temperature titanium alloy matrix composite 
were studied to explore the possibility of using HIP and heat treatment 
to prepare the titanium matrix composites with excellent 
high-temperature strength and ductility.

microstructure is higher than that with equiaxed microstructure. After 
heat treatment, the microstructure of GO/Ti – HIP changed from equi -
axed structure to bimodal structure, and the transformation of micro -

HT. That is, the improved yield strength contributed by the trans -
formation of microstructure is 42 MPa.

4. Conclusion 
GO reinforced high-temperature titanium alloy matrix composite 
with excellent tensile strength of 749 MPa, yield strength of 571 MPa 
and elongation of 18.5% at 600 
◦
C was successfully fabricated by HIP 
and heat treatment. The solution and aging treatment significantly 
enhanced the high-temperature strength due to the strengthening of 
interface bonding by increased interface reaction and the transformation 
of microstructure from equiaxial structure to bimodal structure. In 
addition, the dissolution of silicides improved the high-temperature 
ductility.
