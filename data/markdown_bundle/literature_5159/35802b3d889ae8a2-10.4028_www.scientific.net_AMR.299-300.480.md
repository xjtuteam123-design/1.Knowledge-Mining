Study on effect of sintering temperature on microstructure and 
compressive property of porous NiTi alloys 
Jing-yuan Yu 1, a , Qiang Li 1, 2,b

Keywords: Porous NiTi alloys; sintering temperature; microstructure; compressive property 
Abstract. Porous NiTi alloys were prepared by powder metallurgy method using NH 4HCO 3 as 
space-holder. The effect of sintering temperature on pore characteristic, phase composition and 
compressive property of porous NiTi alloys was studied by XRD, SEM, EDS and a universal testing 
machine. The results show with the increase of sintering temperature the porosity of porous NiTi 
alloys first increases and then decreases, but the content of NiTi phase, compressive strength and 
modulous of sintered products continuously increase. When sintered at 980 ℃ for 2h, the porous NiTi 
alloys have higher porosity of 53.6%, better compressive strength of 173.7MPa and elastic modulous 
of 4.2GPa. The phases of sinter products are mainly composed by TiNi, Ti 2Ni, and TiNi 3 phases. 
Introduction 
Porous NiTi shape memory alloy (SMA) has attracted great concern owing to its special shape 
memory effect and superelasticity, good mechanical properties and biocompatibility, unique porous 
structure, etc[1-3]. Recently porous NiTi SMA has been extensively used as artificial bones or teeth 
roots[4,5]. The porous NiTi SMA whose mechanical properties can be easily adjusted to match those 
of replaced hard tissues by obtaining different porosity and pore size through controlling the synthesis 
conditions has been acknowledged. Moreover, its porous structure could allow body tissues to grow 
and body fluids to be transported through the interconnected pores [6,7]. 
Comprared with self-propagating high-temperature synthesis (SHS) technology, powder 
metallurgy is a promising method for the production of near-net-shapecomponents of TiNi alloys. 
When adding appropriate space-holders, the porosity and mean pore size of porous NiTi alloys both 
increase and meet the demand of biological medicine. In the fabrication process of porous NiTi alloys 
by powder metallugy, sintering temperature is very improtant and affects the pore morphology, 
porosity, phase composition, and compressive property of sintered products. So in this paper the 
effect of sintering temperature was studied in detail and provide a reference for choosing proper 
sintering thchnics. 
Experiments 
Ni powder and Ti powder having 99.9% purity were used in the equiatomic proportion in all the 
experiments. The median particle sizes of Ti and Ni powder were 43 µm and 1-2 µm, respectively. 
The mixed powders of Ni and Ti were blended with high-purity NH 
4HCO 3 powder (average particle 
size is 250 µm, purity is 99.99%) to the compositions and then pressed into rectangle compacts 
(50mm×10mm×10mm). The content of NH 4HCO 3 is 12%. The green samples were heated to 
different sintering temperature at argon atmosphere for 2h. The porosity of sintering products was 
measured by Archimedes principle and their pore morphology was observed by 
stereoscope(OLYMPUSSZ61). The phase constituent was determined by X-ray diffraction (XRD) 
analysis. Microstructure of the specimens was analyzed using scanning electron microscopy 
(SSX-505) on their polished surfaces. Compressive strength was measured using a universal testing 
machine (CMT5105) with a loading rate of 0.5 mm/min.

Results and discussion 
Effect of sintering temperature on pore characteristic. Fig.1 shows pore morphology of porous 
NiTi alloy sintered at different sintering temperature. In Fig.1, large pores come from the 
decomposition of NH 
4HCO 3, and small pores origin form the Kirkendall phenomenon (the difference 
of diffusion velocity of Ti and Ni particles induces pores). As can be seen in Fig.1, with the increase of 
sintering temperature, the smaller pores in the pore cell become obvious. At low sintering temperature, 
there are little pores and uneven pore size in the pore cell due to slow diffusion velocity of Ti and Ni 
atoms (shown in Fig.1-a). When increasing the sintering temperature, the diffusion velocity of Ti and 
Ni atoms increases and there are a lot of voids in the alloys. The aggregation of a lot of voids can form 
more pores in the pore cell (shown in Fig.1-b and c). When sintering temperature is higher than 980 ℃, 
there are a lot of Ti-rich liquid, so the mass transport accelerates and diffusion of Ti and Ni atom is 
adequate. The pores in the pore cell shrinkage and accumulate so the porosity decreases (shown in 
Fig.1-d). In Fig.1, with the increase of sintering temperature, the mean pore size of large pores 
decreases from 204µm at 920 ℃ to 175µm at 1000 ℃. This is because diffusion reaction proceeds 
toward lower energy. For porous NiTi alloys, the shrinkage of pores can reduce the interface in the 
alloys in order to decrease the energy of the reaction system. 
Fig.2 shows the effect of sintering temperature on porosity of sinter products. In Fig.2, when 
increasing sintering temperature, the porosity of porous NiTi alloys first increases and then decreases. 
The reason is that at higher temperature the diffusion of Ti and Ni atoms is adequate and form a lot of 
Kirkendall pores, resulting in the increase of the porosity. When the temperature is higher than 980 ℃, 
the Ti-rich liquid appears and diffusion reaction finishes at a short time, so the pores contract and the 
porosity decreases.

alloys sintered at different temperature. As can be seen in Fig.3, the sinter products at different

diffusion reaction is inadequate, and the products have much Ni element besides of TiNi, Ti 2Ni and 
TiNi 3 phases. With the increase of sintering temperature, the diffusion ability of the atoms improve

Effect of sintering temperature on compressive property. Fig.6 and Fig.7 shows the effect of 
sintering temperature on compressive strength and modulous of porous NiTi alloys. In Fig.6 and 
Fig.7, with the increase of sintering temperature, the compressive strength and modulous of porous 
NiTi alloys both increase. This is because, according the foregoing XRD and pore characteristic 
analysis, the diffusion reaction of Ti and Ni atoms performs slowly and is inadequate before 980 ℃. 
The reactants have initial powder without diffusion reaction so the content of TiNi phase is less and 
the content of Ti 2Ni, and TiNi 3 phases is more. The compressive property of TiNi phase is better than 
Ti 2Ni, and TiNi 3 phases so compressive property of porous NiTi alloys increases at high temperature.

Summary 
High porosity and large pore of porous NiTi alloys can be preapred by powder metallurgy method 
using NH 4HCO 3 as the temporary space-holder. During powder metallurgy, with the increase of 
sintering temperature, the pores in the cell first increases due to the adquate diffusion reaction, and 
then decrease due to diffusion reaction toward lower energy. When sintered at high temperature, the 
phase compositon of porous NiTi alloys is TiNi, Ti 2Ni and TiNi 3 phases without Ni element. 
Simultaneously,the compressive strength and modulous of porous NiTi alloys both increase with the 
increase of sintering temperature due to the decrease of miscellaneous phases.
