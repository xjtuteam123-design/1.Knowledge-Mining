7. Conclusion
The high-strain-rate yieldandfracture stresses of two refractory two-phase alloys have been
measured under shock-wave loading at temperatures up to 680/C14C. The alloys exhibit the non-
monotonous temperature dependencies that are not observed at low strain rates. Most probably
theobservedvariationsofstrengthpropertiesathighstrainratesofshockloadingaretheresultof
structural rearrangement in the alloys under heating.
