Abstract
The microstructure and mechanical behavior of NiAl±9Mo eutectic alloys with and without 1 at.% Hf have been
investigated by using SEM, TEM and high temperature compressive tests, respectively. It was suggested that NiAl±9Mo±1Hf
shows a significant strength advantage over NiAl±9Mo due to the presence of Heusler phase in the former alloy. Their high
temperature compressive deformation behavior is proposed to be properly described by power-law equations. q 2000

2. Experimental details
The materials used in this study were arc-melted
from starting materials of 99.9% Ni, 99.9% Al and
99% Hf, 99.5% Cr and 99.9% Mo, using a non-con-
sumable tungsten electrode. Each alloy button was
turned over and remelted more than three times to
give a homogeneous specimen. Since weight losses
were generally less than 0.5%, the alloy composition
was considered to be equal to their nominal composi-
tions. The alloy buttons were homogenized at 1523
K for 24 h in evacuated silica capsules followed by
furnace cooling to room temperature. Specimens of

4. Conclusions
.1 Minor alloying addition of Hf is suggested to
interfere with eutectic solidification of NiAl±9Mo
alloy and gives rise to visible change in microstruc-
ture between NiAl±9Mo alloys with and without Hf.
.2 Hf seems to be a strong Heusler former as
observed in NiAl±Hf alloys, which, in turn, signifi-
cantly increases high temperature strength of NiAl±
9Mo.
.3 The elevated temperature compressive behav-
.iors of NiAl±9Mo Hf can be described by the
power-law equations very well. However, both stress
exponents and activation energies for the present two
alloys are different from those measured for other
NiAl-based alloys, making it difficult to comment on
their controlling deformation mechanisms.
