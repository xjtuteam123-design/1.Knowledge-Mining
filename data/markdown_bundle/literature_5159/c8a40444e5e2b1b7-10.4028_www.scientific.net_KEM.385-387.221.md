Micromechanics method to evaluate fatigue life of Ni-based superalloys 
during morphological evolution 
Wen-Ping Wua, Ya-Fang Guob, Yue-Sheng Wangc 
Institute of Engineering Mechanics, Beijing Jiaotong University, Beijing, 100044, P .R. China 
a06115206@bjtu.edu.cn, byfguo@bjtu.edu.cn, cyswang@bjtu.edu.cn 
Keywords: Ni-based superalloys, fatigue life, micromechanics, morphological evolution. 
 
Abstract. A quantitative life prediction method has been proposed to evaluate fatigue life during 
morphological evolution of precipitates in Ni-based superalloys. The method is essentially based on 
Eshelby’s equivalent inclusion theory and Mori -Tanaka’s mean field method. The shape stability 
and life prediction are discussed when the external stress and matrix plastic strain are applied. The 
calculated results show that the fatigue life is closely related with microstructures evolution of 
precipitates. T he magnitude and sign of the external stress and matrix plastic strain have an 
important effect on fatigue life of Ni -based superalloys during the morphological evolution of 
precipitates. 
Introduction 
Ni-based superalloys have been inevitable materials for the blades and vane applications in gas 
turbines, because of their excellent reliability and mechanical properties. Genera lly, the superalloys 
have naturally developed composite microstructure s consisting of 
 matrix and 
' precipitates. 
The morphological evolution of precipitates is generally associated with the creep resistance [1-3]. 
Several theoretical methods have been proposed to simulate the equilibrium microstructure 
morphologies of precipitates and explain rafting mechanism [3-5], while less attention has been 
focused on the effects on the high temperature fatigue pro perties during morphological evolution of 
precipitates. In this paper we introduce a reasonable and simplified micromechanics model based on 
Eshelby’s inclusion theory and Mori -Tanaka’s mean field method. The life prediction is discussed 
by taking account of both microscope failure mechanism and role of microstructure . A particular 
attention is paid to the evolution of the microstructure in relation with the fatigue life of the 
material. The results of these calculations are used to evaluate the fatigue life of Ni -based 
superalloys during the morphological evolution of precipitates.

Summary 
A simplified micromechanics model has been proposed to evaluate the fatigue life of Ni-based 
superalloys during morphological evolution of precipitates. Life estimation was discussed by taking 
account of microscope failure mechanism and the effect of microstructure . The calculated results 
show that the fatigue life is closely related with the microstructures evolution of precipitates. T he 
magnitude and sign of the external stresses and matrix pla stic strain play an important role on the 
fatigue life of Ni-based superalloys during the morphological evolution of precipitates.
