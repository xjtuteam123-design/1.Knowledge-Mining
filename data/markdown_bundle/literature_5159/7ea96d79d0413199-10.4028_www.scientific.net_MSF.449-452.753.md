In-Situ Composite 
Wei Li+, Haibo Yang, Aidang Shan, Jiansheng Wu 
Key Laboratory of Ministry of Education for High Temperature Materials and Tests, 
Department of Material Science and Engineering, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, 
Shanghai 200030, China 
 
Keywords: Nb/Nb 5Si3 in-situ composites; single edge-notched bending; fractur e toughness; 
metastable phase. 
Abstract. Nb/Nb5Si3 in-situ composites are very attractive structural materials for these materials 
perform a good balance in mechanical properties , including a high strength at high temperature 
(>1373K) and reasonably high fracture toughness at room temperature. Me tastable phase Nb 3Si 
plays an important role in the properties of Nb/Nb 5Si3 composites by affecting microstructure and 
volume fracture of ductile phase. In this paper, Nb-10Si-xMo a nd Nb-18Si-xMo (x=0,5,15) are 
prepared by arc melting and annealed at 1473K for 100h. Single edg e-notched bending (SENB) test 
was used to study the fracture toughness of Nb-Si-Mo alloys. The stability of metastable phase is 
analyzed by XRD. The room temperature fracture toughness of N b-10Si is 10.47MPa(m) 1/2 and 
higher than that of binary Nb-18Si alloys at near-eutectic compositions. The addition of Mo improves 
the fracture toughness of as cast Nb-Si alloys from 4.1 MPa( m)1/2 to 9.9MPa(m) 1/2 at near-eutectic 
compositions and decreases it from 10.47 MPa(m)1/2 to 8.8MPa(m)1/2 at hypoeutectic compositions. 
Introduction 
The demand of high performance structural materials at operating temperature higher than 1000℃ 
in advanced aerospace propulsion systems has led to the developme nt of many intermetallic 
compounds based on refractory metals.[1] Among these, Nb 5Si3 has a strong potential as high 
temperature structure materials.[2,3] Low fracture toughness at room temperature (~3MPa <m), 
however, becomes a serious barrier to the application of Nb 5Si3. According to previous reports,[4-8] 
Nb5Si3 compounded with Nb performs a high fracture toughness at room te mperature and a 
reasonably high strength at high temperature (>1400℃). Accor ding to the phase diagram of binary 
Nb-Si alloys, metastable phase Nb 3Si would appear in as cast Nb-Si composites because of the 
eutectic reaction of L =Nb3Si +Nb ss. The fracture toughness of Nb-Si alloys are sensitive to th e 
volume fraction of ductile phase. Nb3Si would results in the decrease of the volume fraction of ductile 
phase. Furthermore, Nb 3Si damages the strength at high temperature of Nb/Nb 5Si3 composites 
because of its low melting point. In Mendirattas report[2], Nb3Si will decompose to Nb 5Si3 + Nb ss 
after annealed at 1500℃ for 100h. According to Kims report, Mo addition will diminish Nb3Si in as 
cast Nb-Si alloys, and significantly improve the fracture toughness of Nb-18(at.%)Si. In this report, 
Effect of Mo addition on the stability of metastable phase and microstructure of alloys is investigated 
in Nb-10(at.%)Si alloys. Single edge-notched bending (SENB) test is used to measure the fracture 
toughness of Nb/Nb5Si3 in-situ composites. The properties and fractography of binary and ternary of

Experimental Procedures 
Binary and ternary Nb-10at.%Si-xat.%Mo(x=0,5,15) and Nb-18at.%Si-xat.%Mo(x=0,5,15) alloys 
were prepared by arc melting. Hereafter the alloys composition will be abbreviated as Nb-10Si instead 
of Nb-10at.%Si. The purities of raw materials were 99.7wt.% for Nb, 99.99%wt.%Si, 99.9wt.% Mo, 
respectively. The alloy buttons were re-melted at least 5 times to ensure chemical homogeneity. Heat 
treatment was carried out at 1200℃ for 100 hours in vacuum. The size of SENB specimens shown is 
3×6×30 with 24mm span
 without insertion of an initial fatigue crack. SENB specimens are measured 
according to ASTM E399-89. Peak load in the load-displa cement curve and equation are used to 
calculate the fracture toughness, that is K Q=(PQ*S/B*W3/2)[f(a/W)], Where PQ is the peak load and 
f(a/W) is the geometrical factor. X-ray diffraction was conducted on the specimens. Chemical 
compositions and morphology of constituent phase are analyzed using a scanning electron microscope 
(SEM) with energy spectrum analysis. Fracture surface was observed in scanning electron microscope.

be seen at the interface between Nb ss particles and Nb 5Si3 matrix. In the fractograph of 
Nb-10Si-15Mo, cleavage fracture and little dimple fracture are se en, as shown in Fig. 4(b). Coarse 
river pattern is seen instead of little cleavage plane and decohes ion at the interface. Mo addition 
results in the decrease of ductility of Nb ss.[6,7] As a result, the fracture mode of Nb ss particles 
changes from dimple fracture to cleavage fracture. Then the frac ture toughness of Nb-10Si-15Mo is 
lower than that of Nb-10Si due to the decrease of ductility of Nb ss. However, Mo addition improved 
the strength at high temperature of Nb-10Si.[6]

According to Kims report, effect of Mo addition on the fracture toughness of Nb/Nb 5Si3 in-situ 
composites can be summarized as three aspects: 1. The addition of Mo results in the decomposition 
of metastable phase Nb3Si. Increase of the volume fraction of ductile Nbss phase strongly affects the 
fracture toughness of Nb/Nb 5Si3 composites; 2. The addition of Mo results in the increase of the 
size of ductile Nb ss particles, which will improve the fracture toughness of Nb-10Si; 3.The 
solution of Mo in Nb ss decreases plasticity of Nb ss particles. Therefore, the fracture toughness of 
composites decreases. If the effect of former two aspects i s superior to latter, the addition of Mo 
would improve the fracture toughness of composites (for example, the fracture toughness of 
Nb-18Si is improved by addition of 5at% Mo). If the effect of latter is higher, the fracture toughness 
would decrease (for example, the fracture toughness of Nb-10Si decrea ses by addition of 15at% 
Mo). It is surprising that the fracture toughness of annealed Nb-18Si -5Mo is far higher than that of 
as cast Nb-18Si-5Mo. The reason might be that Mo addition cannot obviously affect the 
microstructure of Nb ss particles before heat treatment. After annealed at 1200℃ for 100 hours, 
bridging Nbss particles depending on the addition of Mo improve the fracture toughness. 
Summary 
In Nb-Si alloys, the addition of Mo is of benefit to eutectic rea ction L=Nb5Si3 +Nbss. In as cast 
Nb-Si-Mo alloys, there are only Nbss and Nb5Si3 phase without Nb3Si phase. The fracture toughness 
of Nb-10Si, Nb-10Si-15Mo and Nb-18Si-15Mo will not be significantly improved by Heat 
treatment. Contrarily, the fracture toughness of Nb-18Si-5Mo annea led at 1200℃ for 100 hours is 
significantly higher than that of as cast Nb-18Si-5Mo. Effect of Mo addition on the fracture 
toughness of Nb/Nb 5Si3 in-situ composites can be summarized as three aspects: a. El iminating 
Nb3Si phase; b. Affecting on the size of Nbss particles; c. Decreasing the ductility of Nbss.
