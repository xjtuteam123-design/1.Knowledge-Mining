Abstract
A series of Zr-Ti-Ni-Nb system filler alloys with different content of Zr were developed and fabricated for TiAl joining. The 
phase characteristics of typical joint interface microstructure were analyzed, and the effect of element Zr on the microstructure 
and strength of the joints was investigated. The results showed that when the content of Zr was 5
 ~ 11 wt. %, the joint w
as 
composed of Ti2Al, Ti–Al-Ni, Ti3Al, Ti2AlNi dissolved with Zr. With the content of Zr was greater than 25 wt. %, elements 
Ni, Zr and Nb were enriched in the central part of the joint, and the predominant phase in the reaction zone of the joint was 
(Ti,Zr)
2AlNi dissolved with Nb. The joint brazed with Zr(8 ~ 11)-T i(Bal.)-Ni(18 ~ 21)-Nb(15 ~ 17) (wt. %) filler at 1010 °C 
for 10 min exhibited the maximum average the shear strength of 280.2 MPa. For the filler alloys with the Zr content of 40 
wt. % and 60 wt. %, cracks were detected in the as-brazed joints.

1 Introduction
TiAl-based alloy possesses low density, high temperature 
mechanical properties, high temperature oxidation resistance 
and excellent corrosion resistance, it has been considered 
as one of the most promising alternative alloy to replace 
traditional superalloys in aviation and aerospace field for 
the significant weight reduction effect [1 , 2]. For example, 
it is particularly appropriate for the manufacturing of low 
pressure turbine blades, diffuser casing, etc.
The development of the joining plays an important role 
in the application of TiAl-based alloy. Due to the forma-
tion of brittle intermetallic phase in the joint, the reliable 
joining of TiAl-based alloy is more difficult, compared to 
other conventional alloys. In recent years, several joining 
processes, such as arc welding [3 ], electron beam welding 
[4, 5], friction welding [6 , 7], self-propagating high-tem-
perature synthesis [8 ], diffusion welding [9 ], and brazing 
[10, 11], have been investigated to improve the performance 
of TiAl joints. Even if the properly controlling the welding 
cooling was conducted, both the solidification cracks and 
solid-state cracks also cannot be avoided [12].
Brazing is considered as the most feasible technique for 
TiAl joining, especially for complex components. Al-based 
and Ag-based filler metals, such as BAlSi-4 [ 13], Ag–Cu-
In-Ti [14], BAg-8 [15], were reported to achieve the joint 
of TiAl-based alloy, and the corresponding relationship 
between interfacial microstructure and joints properties was 
analyzed in detail. However, the joint brazed with Al-based 
and Ag-based filler metals can only be served below 500 °C, 
which resulted that the high-temperature properties of TiAl 
alloys could not be fully utilized. Besides, the high-temper-
ature brazing filler metal (e.g., Ti-Cu-Ni [16] and Ti-Zr-
Cu-Ni [17] system filler alloys) for joining TiAl have been 
reported. However, the element Cu from these filler metals 
could react with Al or Ti from the TiAl base metal to form 
brittle phases such as 
 Ti2Cu, AlCuTi, and CuAl2.
In the present study, a series of Zr-Ti-Ni-Nb system filler 
alloys with different content of the element Zr were devel-
oped and fabricated for TiAl joining. Ti-Ni-Nb ternary alloys 
with several compositions were used as the reference [18–20].
