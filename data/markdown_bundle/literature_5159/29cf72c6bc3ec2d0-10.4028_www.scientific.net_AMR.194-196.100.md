Preparation of Fe-Cu-Ni-Mo-C Alloy with High Performance 
by Powder Metallurgy Warm Compaction 
Junsheng Huang a and Sheng Min b 
Faculty of Material and Energy, Guangdong University of Technology, 
Guangzhou P .R.China 510006 
aGL@gdut.edu.cn, bminsheng601@sina.com 
Key Words: Powder Metallurgy; Warm Compaction; Fe-Base Alloy; Mechanical Property; 
Microstructure 
Abstract ．． ．．Fe-1.5Cu-1.5Ni-0.5Mo-0.3C alloy was prepared by powder metallurgy(P/M) warm 
compaction. Under the conditions of compaction pressures of 600 or 800 MPa and compaction 
temperature of 100 or 120°C, sintered in cracked ammonia atmosphere at 1120°C for half an hour, 
the researched alloy samples with higher properties could be prepared. The results show: when 
formed at a compaction pressure of 800 MPa and compaction temperature of 120°C, the alloy 
presented a sintered density 7.41g/cm 3, hardness 88HRB, ultimate tensile strength 593MPa, yield 
strength 585MPa, and elongation 3.8%. Their mechanical properties, crack morphology and surface 
composition weres analyzed. 
Introduction 
At the present time, P/M is successful in two primary performance areas[1]. The first is in density 
ranges of less than 7.1 g/cm 
3 with low- to medium-strength requirements. In the future, products 
with high-density will increase fastly. The second main application area is fully dence P/M via 
powder forging. A major advance in P/M technology has been the warm compaction process which 
produces changes in the pore morphology of sintered parts, compared to that obtained with 
conventional “cold” compaction. By P/M processes, the highest green density after cold compaction 
is 7.05 ～7.25 g/cm 3. By using warm compaction technology, the final density could reach 7.25 ～
7.45g/cm 3[2 －6]. With the increasing density (reducing porosity), the mechanical properties 
(ultimate tensile strength, hardness and toughness etc.) of samples are enhanced. The warm 
compaction process is applicable to most powder/material systems[7]. However, the data obtained 
by equipments for productions are still scare and further studies are necessary. 
Experimental Procedures 
The raw powders were provided by Höganäs(China) Co. ,Ltd.,whose base material was Diataloy 
AB(composition were 1.5wt% Cu, 1.5 wt%Ni, and 0.5 wt %Mo) with diffusion alloyed grade, 
and added 0.3% graphite and some lubricants for warm compaction. During warm compaction by 
using PH-80 mechanical warm compacting machine for production made in Yangzhou Weida 
machine Co. in China, the compaction presure was 600MPa or 800MPa, and the temperaure was 
100°C or 120°C. The samples were then sintered in the mesh belt furnace in the atomosphere of 
cracked ammonia for 30min at 1120°C.

Tab 1 shows that high density was obtained under four conditions of warm compaction. ，all of 
which were over 7.38 g/cm 3 and the highest was 7.42 g/cm 3 for 600 MPa which need be paid 
attention. These indicated that the good effects of warm compacting and lubricating were realized. 
By comparation, the densities under 600MPa were nearly the same with those under 800MPa. The 
densities for 120°C were also nearly the same with those for 100°C. The high density was obtained 
on the two reasons as following[8]. Firstly the yield strength, working-hardening of the particles of 
iron powders decreased under the temperature of warm compaction. The plastic deformation of 
powders increased and the pores decreased. Secondly polymer lubricants were in the vitrification 
state and changed the surface properties under press temperature. They improved the lubrication 
effect and decreased the friction resistance. These could be benefit powders to transfer pressure and 
fill pores. 
Mechanical Properties. Data in Tab.1 indicates that the tensile strength at compaction pressure 
of 800 MPa is larger than that at compaction pressure of 600 MPa. High effective pressure could 
improve the combination among powder particles, decrease pores and enhance the mechaical 
properties. The mechanical properties for temperature of 120°C is larger than that for temperature of 
100°C. One of the selection criteria for lubricants during warm compaction is that the vitrification 
point of the lubricant has a good collocation with press temperature[9]. In this case, lubricating

C,Cu and Ni elements existed in the boundary of powder particles, and cleavage face mainly 
consisted of pure iron.. They presented the characters of Distaloy powders. Alloying elements used 
in the Distaloy process are mainly copper, nickel and molybdenum (but not graphite) in the form of 
very finely grained powders. The powder mix is heat-treated in a continuous furnace under a 
reducing atomosphere at a temperature somewhat below the melting point of the lowest-melting 
alloying element. During this heat-treatment,the fine particles of the added alloying elements are 
safely bonded to the surfaces of the coarser iron powder particles[10]. 
Conclusion 
Fe-1.5Cu-1.5Ni-0.5Mo-0.3C powders mixing with special lubricants were warm compacted at 
600/800MPa pressure under 100/120°C, and then sintered under 1120°C /30min. From which 
samples with high density and good combination mechanical properties could be get. When formed 
at a compaction pressure of 800 MPa and compaction temperature of 120°C, the alloy presented a 
sintered density 7.41g/cm 
3, hardness 88HRB, ultimate tensile strength 593MPa, yield strength 
585MPa and elongation 3.8%.
