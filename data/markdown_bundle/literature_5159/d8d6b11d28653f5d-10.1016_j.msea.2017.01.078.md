ABSTRACT
The method of continuous melting and casting was used in the research to attempt to acquire the ternary
directionally solidiﬁed Ti-Al-Nb alloy by once casting. It was investigated about microstructure and mechanical
properties of the Ti44Al2Nb alloy by changing the temperature gradient. Results show that the lamellar spacing
decreased to 1.25 µm, when the temperature gradient increases and the composition is relatively uniform in
diﬀerent regions. The tensile strength, the compressive strength and the fracture toughness of Ti44Al2Nb alloy
is 455 MPa, 1770 MPa and 23.5 MPa m
1/2 respectively, under the parameters of 50 kW and 0.5 mm/s. The high
temperature compressive strength increases, when the strain rate increases and temperature decreases. Because
the large strain rate shortens the strain time and piles up dislocations. Another, the low temperature provides
the less driving force of dislocation movement. To observe the crack propagation process and the fracture
morphology, the fracture mode contains trans-lamellar fracture and interface de-lamination. The method is a
feasibility way to prepare the directionally solidi ﬁed TiAlNb alloy and to add the high-melting-point element
into microstructure directly.
1. Introduction
Using as the light-weight high-temperature material, TiAl-based
alloys with the advantages, such as low density, high speci ﬁc strength
and good creep resistance, are regarded as promising candidates in
aerospace and automobile industry [1–3]. Nonetheless, the melting
point of the TiAlNb alloy is relatively high, and the Nb addition use the
middle alloy of AlNb during the electro-magnetic induction melting.
The Nb distribution of the billets is nonuniform. The room temperature
ductility and workability remain low level yet, that con ﬁne the wide-
spread applications in some extreme performance [4–6]. In the past
research, the powder metallurgy, isothermal forming and heat treat-
ment of alloys were paid a lot attention. Yang et al. researched the e ﬀect
of isothermal heat treatment during solidi ﬁcation process on the
microstructure evolution of a high Nb containing TiAl alloy and the
heat treatment improved the microstructural homogeneity to reduce
the tendency for crack [7]. Guo et al. investigated the heat response of
powder metallurgy Ti-6Al-4V with the hot isostatic pressing para-
meters which was temperature from 920 to 940 °C, pressure over
120 MPa and holding for 3 h, and the microstructure of powder
compact changed signi ﬁcantly after di ﬀerent heat treatments, while
there was no obvious di ﬀerence in tensile properties [8]. Sun et al.
tested the deformation behavior of the powder metallurgy Ti-47Al-
2Nb-2Cr alloy in hot working process and the ﬂow instability domain
was found to occur at strain rates higher than 0.01 s
−1 with the
microstructural observation of the grain boundary cracks, which has
to be kept away during hot processing in pursuit of achieving the
desired mechanical properties [9]. However, as is known to all, the
casting is an economical and direct method for the preparation TiAl
alloys semiﬁnished product piece. However there are some segregation
phases in the casting microstructure.
In the previous research, the TiAl-based alloys casting usually used
the smelting furnace with other alloys and continued to study, more
than ever, in directionally solidi ﬁed process. Kartavykh et al. used the
Ti46Al8Nb alloy synthesized by GfE Metalle und Materia lien GmbH
and performed the directional solidi ﬁcation experiments in a modi ﬁed
Nabertherm-3000 laboratory gradient furnace, which result was the
perfect inoculated microstructure of Ti44Al7Nb2B ingots with 100 µm
grain diameter, low scattering of dimensional grain characteristics and
high tolerance [10]. Zhang et al. researched the Ti47Al2Cr2Nb alloy
which was prepared by 5 repeated cycles of vacuum arc oxygen in high
purity protecting argon for directional solidi ﬁcation and the Y

Al2O3 crucibles was the former promising candidate crucibles for the
high quality TiAl alloys [11]. Dong et al. prepared the

Alloying is an e ﬀective means to improve the mechanical property
of alloys and the representative β stabilized element has Nb, Mo, V, Ta,
Cr, Co, Ni, Mn, W, and so on. Among these elements, the Nb element
can improve the high-temperature strength, oxidation resistance and
creep resistance [13]. The Mo element can improve the high-tempera-
ture strength, oxidation resistance and plastic deformation [14]. The Cr
element can improve the fracture toughness property and plastic
deformation [15]. The Nb element addition can re ﬁne solidi ﬁcation
microstructure and increase the room temperature ductility. It is the β
stable element and expands β phase region [16,17]. Therefore in this
paper, the research used the Nb wire in the directional solidi ﬁcation.
So the method is directional solidi ﬁcation by electromagnetic cold
crucible to melt Ti44Al alloy and Nb wire simultaneously. The Nb
element can add with the melting experimental bar gradually. The aim
is to investigate the feasibility of the method and to try a new way of
adding the high-melting element into alloy. E ﬀects of the method on
directionally solidi ﬁed microstructure, mechanical property, lamellar
colony size, microstructure homogeneity and cracking tendency were
studied and discussed.
2. Experimental
The starting materials were made from the titanium sponge,
99.98% purity, and aluminum, 99.98% purity. To consider the wastage
of aluminum element, the burning ratio of aluminum was 2 wt%
according to the previous melting experience. The Ti44Al ingots were
prepared by the vacuum induction melting furnace of ALD Vacuum
Technologies AG, and the size of ingots was Ø120 mm×220 mm. The
research acquired three specimens that size was Ø24 mm×20 mm from
the axial direction and analyzed by the AXIOS-PW4400 X-ray
Spectrometer to con ﬁrm the composition uniformity, which results
show in Table 1 . The research used the Wire Electrode Discharge
Machining to incise the round bars, and then the bars were cut twelve
holes. The brand Nb1 of the niobium wire was on the annealed
condition conforming to the ASTM B392-09 R04210 and the size of
the Nb wire was Ø1.0 mm. The Nb wire stuck in the holes and the size
of the hole was Ø1.2 mm. Therefore, the round bars with Nb wire
formed the experimental bars.
The investigated Ti46Al2Nb ingots were prepared by cold crucible
directional solidi ﬁcation, DS [18], which melted by the experimental
bars. The size of the ingots was 36 mm×36 mm×125 mm. The ingot
was cut in half longitudinally, and then polished and etched. The
macrostructure was observed by the two-mega-pixel camera about
grain growth direction, grain morphology and grain size. The micro-
structure and the phase of the ingots were examined by Olympus GX71
Optical Microscope, OM, and Quanta 200F scanning electron micro-
scopy, SEM. The composition can be analyzed by Energy Dispersive
Spectrometer, EDS, and D/max-rB Rotating Anode X-ray
Diﬀractometer Needle, XRD. The samples with the size of Ø4×6 mm
were used for room temperature, RT, compressive testing, which was
operated on Instron 5569 Electronic Universal Material Testing
Machine, EUMTM, with 0.5 mm/min loading rate. The size of the
tensile sample was 20 mm×6 mm×2 mm. The tensile testing with
0.05 mm/min loading rate and bending test with 3 mm×4 mm×22 mm
size were all experimented by EUMTM. The fracture morphology can
also be achieved by SEM. The high temperature, HT, compress testing
was experimented by Gleeble-1500D Dynamic Thermal Simulation
Testing Machine, DTSTM, with Ø6×9 mm size. The main parameters of
DTSTM

one date must be achieved by three times and the last date was the
average numerical value. These mechanical properties were tested
following Chinese National Standard for test methods of mechanical

Using the photoshop software put the images of the DS micro-

stable element of β phase and then expands the β solidiﬁcation region.
In this part, the red line means the lamellar direction and θ is de ﬁned

direction. When the primary precipitated phase is β phase, θ is 0° or
45° approximatively and when the primary precipitated phase is α

temperature gradient. The ratio of the lamellar direction is 33.3%,

stability region, the lamellar angle and spacing have great e ﬀects on the
mechanical property. In the Fig. 7 it shows the lamellar spacing of DS
microstructure with di ﬀerent temperature gradient. The measuring
result shows in the Table 3 . The average lamellar spacing is calculated

3.4. The fracture analysis of the ingots
In order to realize the fracture mode, it observes the macrostructure
and microstructure of fracture toughness specimens, which shows in
the Fig. 14 . In the Fig. 14 (a) and (b) it has trans-lamellar fracture and
interface de-lamination. The lamellar direction of trans-lamellar frac-
ture presents a large angle with loading direction. The crack propaga-
tion paths need to traverse some lamellas. The crack traversing the
lamellas will need more driving force and have bigger value of fracture
toughness. The lamellar direction of interface de-lamination has a
small angle with loading direction. The crack along the lamella
expanding and need little driving force. So in the Fig. 14(a) and (b)
the crack propagation paths length of trans-lamellar fracture is short
for the interface de-lamination. It uses the secondary electrons to
achieve the fracture morphology of the fracture toughness specimens.
In the Fig. 14(c) and (d) it can be seen that the lamella of the interface
de-lamination and the fracture surface of trans-lamellar fracture. It
also can be seen that the inter-granular fracture and the cleavage
fracture with a little of river pattern. Hence the fracture morphology is
the mixed fracture.

several results based on the above discussion.
(1) The composition uniformity is relatively superior after testing the
component content in di ﬀerent region. The grain size decreases
and the lamellar spacing decreases of 37.5% with the increasing
temperature gradient.

the as-cast improving about 40.7%. That is to say that the DS
microstructure can absorb more energy when the stress direction is
perpendicular to the columnar crystal growth direction.

compressive strength decreases. It may be that the high tempera-
