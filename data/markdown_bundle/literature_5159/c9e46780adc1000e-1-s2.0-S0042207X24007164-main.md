ABSTRACT
The service conditions of high-entropy alloys (HEAs) joints brazed with conventional filler metals (FMs) can be 
confined because few HEAs phases generate in the braze zone. In this study, a multicomponent CoCrFeNiNb
0.5 
eutectic HEAs (EHEAs) FM was developed to braze Al
0.3
CoCrFeNi HEAs in vacuum, the braze zone mainly 
consisted of face-centered cubic (FCC) and Laves lamellar structure, exhibiting a superior metallurgical 
compatibility with the Al
0.3
CoCrFeNi base metals (BMs). The shear strength and shear displacement of brazed 
joints reached 461 MPa/1.35 mm at room temperature (RT), 741 MPa/1.42 mm at 196 
◦
C and 380 MPa/1.62 
mm at 700 
◦
C, respectively. This strategy can stimulate the fabrication and wide-temperature range applications 
of HEAs brazed components by exploiting the structural and performance merits of EHEAs FMs.
1. Introduction
Modern engineering applications are in urgent need of metallic al -
loys with superior strength, excellent ductility and toughness in a wide 
temperature range. The single-phase face-centered cubic (FCC) struc -
tured Al
0.3
CoCrFeNi HEA exhibits balanced mechanical properties at 
room temperature [ 1 ], impressive strength-ductility synergy at 
extremely low-temperature [ 2 , 3 ], good softening resistance at elevated 
temperatures [ 4 ] and sluggish diffusion kinetic [ 5 ], and thus it has great 
potential for the engineering applications at extreme service conditions, 
including divertors in fusion reactor, heat exchangers, propellers in the 
icebreaker and so on [ 6 – 8 ]. Study on the brazing [ 9 – 12 ] between 
Al
0.3
CoCrFeNi HEAs can exploit its structural potentials sufficiently and 
thus is of considerable technical interest. Up to now, traditional Ni-based 
[ 13 – 15 ], Ag-based [ 16 , 17 ], Au-based [ 18 ] and Ti-based [ 19 ] FMs are 
developed to braze HEAs, but the metallurgical compatibility between 
the braze zone and HEAs BMs is poor, which may constrain the potential 
applications of brazed joints. For example, massive intra -
granular/transgranular borides are precipitated in the CoCrFeMnNi 
joints brazed with BNi-2 FM [ 14 ], which weakens the bond strength of 
joints at room and high temperatures. For the CrMnFeCoNi HEA/304 SS 
dissimilar joint [ 16 ] brazed with Ag50Cu FM, since the braze zone 
consists of Ag(s,s) and Cu(s,s), the service temperature of the joints 
cannot exceed 500 
◦
C [ 20 ]. 70Au-8Pd-22Ni gold-based FM was applied 
to braze CoCrFeMnNi HEAs [ 18 ], the interfacial reaction products are 
Au(s,s) + Ni(s,s) and AuMn intermetallic compounds (IMCs) + Ni(s,s) +
a few FCC phase. Compared to Au(s,s), Ni(s,s) and IMCs, the multi -
component FCC phase exhibits an exceptional strength-ductility synergy 
[ 7 , 21 ] at the liquid-nitrogen temperature due to its lower stacking fault 
energy, the less FCC phase in the braze zone implies that the reliability 
for the CoCrFeMnNi HEAs brazed joint might be impaired at the 
extremely low temperature.
Compared to single-phase FCC-structured HEAs, EHEAs [ 22 , 23 ] with 
a lamellar FCC/B2 or FCC/Laves structure have balanced strength and 
ductility in a wide temperature range [ 8 , 24 , 25 ], excellent liquidity and 
castability as well as relatively lower melting-points, while they have 
not been exploited as brazing FMs utilizing in the joining of HEAs. As 
CoCrFeNiNb
x 
EHEAs exhibit impressive comprehensive mechanical 
properties in a wide temperature range [ 25 , 26 ], in the present work, a 
CoCrFeNiNb
0.5 
FM is developed to braze Al
0.3
CoCrFeNi HEAs to obtain a 
joint with excellent mechanical properties in a wide temperature range 
via forming a EHEAs structure in the brazing seam. This strategy can

The CoCrFeNiNb

CoCrFeNiNb

1280

◦

universal testing machine (Instron 5967) with a displacement speed of

sample-fixed shear device in liquid-nitrogen [ 10 ]. High-temperature 
shear experiment was conducted by a universal testing machine equip -

Vacuum brazing of Al
0.3

+ Nb(s,s)/FCC[Nb]ss diffusion layer/Al
