Abstract
：
Nb-W-Mo-Zr-C alloy was prepared by pre-mixed powder sintering, electron beam melting and thermal
processing method. The effects of adding Nb
2
C particle on the microstructure and properties of the alloy were studied. The
results show that the high temperature strength of the alloy improves by choosing the Nb
2
C particles with appropriate amount
and size. Adding 0.4wt%Nb
2
C with particle size of 5
μ
m, the high temperature strength of the alloy is the best.

：
particles; niobium alloys; mechanical property
