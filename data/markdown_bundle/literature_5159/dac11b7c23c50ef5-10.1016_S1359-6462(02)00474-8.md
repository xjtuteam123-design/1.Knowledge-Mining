5. Conclusions
Microstructure,compressionstrength,andcom-
pression creep were investigated for Ir–16at.%Al,
Ir–25at.%Al, and Ir–40at.%Al alloys with the fcc
and B2-IrAl two-phase structure. When the alloy
was solid solution treated in the fcc single phase
regionandthenagedinthefccandB2two-phase
region, ﬁne B2 precipitates formed in the fcc
phase.However,whentheAlconcentrationisover
18 at.%, the solid solution treatment cannot be
done. Thus ﬁne B2 precipitates cannot be ob-
tained, instead, a coarse microstructure, which
retained the dendritic structure, was obtained.
Compression strength at 1773 K decreased with
increasing Al concentration. For the creep tests,
theminimumcreepratewasabout10
