Introduction 
Titanium alloys are widely used for aviation and ae rospace filed due to excellent mechanical 
properties, such as high specific strength, high te mperature resistance, corrosion resistance and so 
on. As the conception of aerospace design is changed, research objectives of titanium alloys are also 
changed, from the pursuit of high strength to damag e tolerance-based alloy [1]. High strength, high 
toughness and damage-tolerance titanium alloy was i nvestigated under the support of national 
project from 2003. TC21 titanium alloy is the first high strength, high toughness and damage-
tolerance titanium alloy with self-intellectual pro perty right in China. TC21 alloy possesses high 
strength (UTS 
≥1100MPa), high fracture toughness (K 1c ≥70MPa.m 1/2 ) and low crack growth rate 
(da/dN being similar to that of Ti64 with β annealing). TC21 alloy is developed from compositi on 
design. The progress of high strength, high toughne ss and damage-tolerance TC21 alloy is 
introduced in this paper, which supplies technical support for further investigation and application 
of TC21 [2,3].

Effect of Heat Treatment on Microstructures and Mechanical Properties of TC21 Alloys 
The tensile properties of TC21 alloy bars of Φ 20mm in diameter were shown in Table 3. According 
to the data, strength and plasticity had good match by six kinds of solution and aging processes, 
which were shown in Table 3. After solution at 900 °C and aging at 600 °C for 4h, tensile strength 
increased with water quench, while elongation and r eduction in area decreased. With solution at 
900 °C for 1h followed by aging at 600 °C for 4 ∼12h, the change of mechanical properties was not 
obvious by extending aging time, which showed that mechanical properties was not sensitive to 
aging time. In general, different heat treatments r esulted in the difference of tensile property. Afte r 
solution and aging treatment, the highest value of tensile strength was obtained by water quenching. 
Solution treatment at phase transformed temperature (950 ±10 °C) and aging at low temperature 
leaded to lower plasticity.

Conclusions 
According to the design of high strength, high toug hness and damage-tolerance TC21alloy, 
experimental verification in Lab., research on 350kg ingot-scale and 1ton and 3 ton industrial ingot-
scale, the results show that mechanical properties of TC21 alloy were stable. It possesses good match 
among strength, plasticity, fracture toughness and crack growth rate (UTS 
≥ 1100MPa, YS ≥ 1000MPa, 
El≥ 8% and RA ≥ 12%, K 1c ≥70MPa.m 1/2 , da/dN being similar to that of Ti64 with β annealing). 
Mechanical properties of TC21 alloy rods of Φ 20mm and Φ 90mm in diameter have good match with 
solution and aging treatment. Mechanical properties are sensitive to solution and aging temperature 
and good mechanical properties are obtained with solution at 900 °C followed by aging at 600 °C.
