Fig. 7 illustrates the crack propagation pathways of all alloys. As 
shown in Figs.7 (a) – (d), the cracks of all alloys exhibit a certain degree of

silicide phase around the eutectic cluster. At the same time, the intense 
stress concentration destroys the primary silicide phase in a eutectic 
cluster, as shown in Fig. 7 (f).
