ABSTRACT
Refractory high-entropy alloys have received increasing attention in recent years, and laser additive
manufacturing can effectively solve the formability dilemma faced by high-entropy alloys. In this paper, a
Hf
0.25
NbTa
0.25
TiZr refractory high-entropy alloy with lower density than equal atomic ratio alloy was formed by
laser melting deposition technology, and its high temperature mechanical behavior and microstructure phase
transformation behavior were systematically analyzed. The BCC phase rich in Zr and Hf and the HCP phase rich
in Zr and Ti elements will be precipitated successively at high temperature. The two precipitated phases were re-
dissolved at 1200
◦
C, and a new HCP phase was precipitated at 1400
◦
C. The phase precipitation behavior has an
effect on the distribution of small-angle misorientation in grains. The yield strength of the alloy at 600 and
800
◦
C is 675.0 and 409.5 MPa, respectively, which is 10.0 % and 17.9 % higher than that of the equiatomic as-
cast alloy. The high temperature plastic transition temperature of the alloy is increased. The high temperature
behavior of the alloy was systematically analyzed by Portevin-Le Chatelier effect and dynamic recrystallization
theory.
1. Introduction
High entropy alloy (HEA) is a special alloy type which is different
from the traditional single principal component alloy. It generally has a
variety of principal component elements with similar atomic ratios
[ 1 – 3 ]. Among them, refractory high-entropy alloys have recently
attracted great interest due to their high strength at high temperatures
[ 4 , 5 ]. Refractory high-entropy alloys are expected to have high melting
temperatures while providing strength and damage tolerance to meet
the needs of nuclear reactor systems and various propulsion systems,
such as aircraft engine components, rockets and hypersonic aircraft [ 6 ,
7 ].
HfNbTaTiZr refractory high-entropy alloy was first proposed in 2011
and quickly gained widespread attention [ 8 ]. As BCC solid solution
alloy, HfNbTaTiZr refractory high-entropy alloy system has good
ductility and an ideal combination of strength and tensile ductility [ 9 ].
And the mechanical behavior, phase transition and softening behavior
under high temperature conditions have also become one of the focuses
of researchers [ 10 ]. Nevertheless, the research on the mechanical
behavior of HfNbTaTiZr alloy family has been limited to room temper-
ature for a long time, and the research and report on high temperature
ductility have not appeared until nearly one year [ 11 – 13 ].
The Portevin-Le Chatelier (PLC) effect is a plastic instability defor-
mation of metallic materials which appear as serrated flow on stress-
strain curves. Nowadays, the most commonly accepted explanation for
PLC mechanism is based on dynamic strain aging (DSA) model. For the
HfNbTaTiZr BCC alloy, the researchers found that the actual flow stress
is much higher than the calculated value of the forest hardening model
which is attributed to the influence of dynamic strain aging and PLC
effect [ 14 ].

Dynamic recrystallization (DRX) is a recrystallization phenomenon
occurring concurrently during deformation [ 15 ]. Traditionally, the DRX
process generally occurs in FCC metals and alloys with low stacking fault
energy. This is because the a/2 < 110 > full dislocation on the {111} slip
plane tends to decompose into two a/6 < 112 > Schockley partial dislo-
cations [ 16 ]. The screw-edge mixing characteristics of the Shockley
incomplete dislocations make it easy to obtain the high dislocation
density driving DRX in the alloy which is generally considered not easy
to occur in the BCC solid solution [ 17 ]. However, the high strain rate
sensitivity of HfNbTaTiZr alloy makes grain boundary sliding become
the main deformation mechanism which in turn enables DRX to occur
[ 18 ].

In previous studies on HfNbTaTiZr HEAs, the occurrence of DRX has
been considered to be related to its high temperature softening [ 32 ]. The
DRX at the grain boundary indicates that more slip systems are activated
near the grain boundary, which is obviously related to the decrease of
yield strength [ 31 ]. On the other hand, the occurrence of DRX behavior
is generally accompanied by a significant decrease in stress level [ 15 ].
This is also consistent with the softening phenomenon of the alloy under
high temperature conditions. In general, grain boundary swelling and
other behaviors occur in FCC alloys, while HfNbTaTiZr HEAs are BCC
single-phase solid solution alloys. This shows that the alloy has a special
behavior during high temperature deformation, which is worthy of re-
searchers ’ more detailed study on this phenomenon.

4. Conclusions
In summary, the high temperature mechanical behavior, phase
transformation behavior and microstructure evolution of lower-density
LMD-ed Hf
0.25
NbTa
0.25
TiZr HEA were systematically studied, and the
following conclusions were drawn.
1. The phase precipitation of the alloy at different temperatures was
confirmed. The BCC phase begins to precipitate at 600
◦
C, while the
HCP phase begins to precipitate at 800
◦
C. Both of them dissolve
again with the increase of temperature and disappear completely at
1200
◦
C. The HCP phase is precipitated again at 1400
◦
C. And the
precipitation of HCP phase inhibits the formation of small-angle
misorientation in the crystal, while the BCC phase is the opposite.
2. Compared with the mechanical properties of HfNbTaTiZr high-
entropy alloy with equal atomic ratio, the tensile strength of
Hf
0.25
NbTa
0.25
TiZr refractory high-entropy alloy increased signifi-
cantly at 600
◦
C and 800
◦
C reaching 675.0 MPa and 409.5 MPa from
617 MPa to 347 MPa. After the composition ratio was adjusted, the
high-temperature mechanical properties of the alloy were effectively
improved. The plastic mutation temperature increases.
3. The existence of PLC effect and its influence on the high temperature
mechanical properties of the alloy were confirmed. The types and
causes of the perturbation behavior in the tensile stress-strain curves
obtained at different temperatures are confirmed and revealed.
Based on the kinetic calculation, the Hf, Ti and Zr element atoms
have a higher diffusion rate than the dislocation slip and other
element atoms in the system, so they become the constituent ele-
ments of the precipitated phase.
4. The abrupt change of high temperature plasticity comes from the
occurrence of grain boundary dynamic recrystallization behavior.
The high temperature softening behavior of the alloy can be affected
by controlling this behavior.
