AN INVESTIGATION OF THE UNIFORMITY OF MECHANICAL PROPERTIES 
OF FORGINGS OF VT9 TITANIUM ALLOY AFTER SUPERPLASTIC DEFORMATION 
AND HIGH-TEMPERATURE THERMOMECHANICAL PROCESSING 
G. A. Salishchev, R. Ya. Lutfullin, 
and M. A. Murzinova 
UDC 669.295.5:621.7.043/.044 
The results are presented of evaluation of the uniformity of mechanical proper- 
ties and structure of VT9 alloy after deformation with different degrees under 
conditions of superplasticity and high-temperature thermomechanical working. It 
is shown that the strength and plastic properties of forgings of this alloy af- 
ter superplastic deformation with degrees from I0 to 70% are stable and quite 
high. The macro- and microstructures of such forgings and uniform. High-tem- 
perature thermomechanical working with an increase in the degree of deformation 
leads to a reduction in plasticity and an increase in strength with a signifi- 
cant spread in properties apparently related to the structural nonuniformity. 
The results obtained make it possible to evaluate the advantages of treatment 
under conditions of superplasticity and in comparison with high-temperature 
thermomechanical working. 
In a number of works devoted to comparative investigation of the mechanical properties 
of titanium alloys after superplastic and some standard method of working it is noted that 
the mechanical properties of VT9 alloy after superplastic deformation correspond to the proper- 
ties of this alloy after high-temperature thermomechanial processing. This is the result 
of the formation in the alloy after superplastic deformation of a more uniform structure 
whcih apparently causes an increase in the stability of the mechanical properties. However, 
detailed investigations have not been made in this direction. 
The purpose of this work is a statistical evaluation of the mechancal properties and struc- 
ture of VT9 alloy after high-temperature thermomechanical processing and superplastic deforma- 
tion with different degrees of deformation. Such a study made it possible to reveal the in- 
fluence of nonuniformity of deformation in different cross sections of complex profile parts 
on their mechanical properties.

with subsequent water hardening were used. The working conditions were for high-temperature

It may be seen that treatment under superplastic conditions provides a stable level of 
strength and plasticity regardless of the degree of deformation. The variation coefficients 
of the strength properties even with ~ = 70% do not exceed 3% and of the plastic 7%. In 
high-temperature thermomechanical processing with an increase in E from 10% to 70% c t in- 
creases somewhat while $ and ~ drop to half. With ~ = 70% the variation coefficients for 
the strength properties are 7.3% and for the plasticity properties 25-30%. The strength 
of the material after high-temperature thermomechanical working was somewhat higher than 
after superplastic deformation but the significant spread in properties levels this dif- 
rence. 
The stability of the mechanical properties after superplastic deformation may be ex- 
plained by the uniformity of the macro- and microstructures of the alloy [1-3]. The aver- 
age grain size is 3.8 pm and the coefficient of form K = 1.01. In the microstructure of 
the forgings after high-temperature thermomechanical processing zones of difficult deforma- 
tion in which the average grain size is 2.6 pm and K = 1.16 and zones of intense deformation 
flow in which the grain size in the longitudinal direction is 1.3 pm and in the transverse 
1.3 pm and K = 2.44 may be clearly seen. The size distribution of the grains is shown in

The observed increase in strength after high-temperature thermomechanical processing 
in comparison with working under superplastic conditions is related to refinement of the 
globular structure of the alloy [4-6], the high degree of dispersion of decomposition pro- 
ducts in ~-phase [i, 4, 5], and the high dislocation density in the s-phase grains [i, 2] 
while in deformation under superplastic conditions some grain growth is observed [2] and 
the s-grains are free of dislocations [i, 2]. However, apparently the influence of these 
structural factors, which in combination must lead to a significant increase in strength 
with some reduction in plasticity after high-temperature thermomechanical working [5], levels 
the nonuniformity of structure. For example, after high-temperature thermomechanical work- 
ing with E = 70% a t = 1350-1700 N/mm 2 while after superplastic deformation o t = 1470-1520 
N/mm 2, that is, the spread does not exceed 50 N/r0J~ 2. 
Therefore the high degree of structural uniformity after superplastic deformation 
provides stability of the mechanical properties of the material with a favorable combination 
of strength and plastic properties. Stability of the mechanical properties regardless of
