Abstract: MicrostructuresXandXmechanicalXpropertiesXofXNi 3AlXbasedXintermetallicXalloysXproducedX

(EDS),XXRrayXdiffractometerXandXtensileXtestXmachine.XTheXduplexXmicrostructuralXfeatureXconsistingX 
ofXγ’XmatrixXphaseXandXsmallXintermetallicXdispersoidsXwasXobservedXtoXbeXdistributedXoverXtheXwholeX

aXroleXtoXformXanXintermetallicXNi 5ZrXphaseXforXtheXentireXalloysXinvestigated.XTheXultimateXtensileX 
strengthXofXtheXpresentXalloyXwasXsuperiorXtoXironRbasedXandXNiRbasedXdieXmaterialsXespeciallyXinXtheX

microstructuralXobservations,XphaseXanalyses. X
Introduction 
Recently,XthereXisXaXgrowingXinterestXinXtheXfieldXofXearthXenvironmentXinXrelationXwithXglobalXwarmingX 
phenomenon.X ThisX canX beX directlyX correlatedX toX theX occurrenceX ofX harmfulX gases,X whatX weX callX 
“greenhouseX gases”,X suchX asX CO 
2,X CH 4,X N 2O,X CF 4,X C2F6X andX SF 6.X FromX theX viewpointX ofX globalX 
warmingX potential,X theX mostX harmfulX gasX wouldX beX SF 6.X However,X theX emissionX ofX CO 2X gasX fromX 
industry,XautomobileXandXsoXonXwouldXinfluenceXmainlyXtoXtheXglobalXwarmingXsinceXtheXamountXofX 
CO 2XemissionXhasXbeenXknownXtoXbeXtheXlargestXcomparedXtoXtheXotherXharmfulXgases.XUnderXsuchX 
background,X theX demandX ofX advancedX highX temperatureX structuralX materialsX hasX beenX explosivelyX 
increasedXtoXuseXthemXatXtemperatureXhigherXthanX1273K,XwhichXisXtheXmaximumXavailableXoperatingX 
temperatureXofXcommercialXNiRbaseXsuperalloysXinXtheXfieldsXofXvariousXindustriesX[1R3].XSinceXcopperX 
hasXaXhighXelectricalXconductivityXthanXaluminum,XtheXuseXofXcopperXrotorXinXtheXfieldXofXhighXefficiencyX 
inductionX motorX manufacturingX industryX couldX increaseX theX electricalX energyX efficiencyX ofX theX 
inductionXmotorXdueXtoXreductionXinXohmicXlossXandXpotentiallyXprovidesXbenefitsXoperatingXcostXandX 
weightXsaving,XdependingXonXdesirableXobjectivesX[4R5].XMotorXmanufacturersXhaveXalsoXlongXrealizedX 
thatX replacingX theX aluminumX conductorX structureX ofX theX rotorX withX pureX copperX wouldX veryX 
significantlyX increaseX theX electricalX energyX efficiencyX ofX theX motorX resultingX inX energyX savingX andX 
reducingXtheXharmfulXemissionXgasesX[6R7].XAccordingXtoXtheXreportXbyXU.S.XdepartmentXofXenergy,XaX1X 
percentXincreaseXinXmotorXefficiencyXwouldXsaveX 20XbillionXkWhXperX yearXorX1.4XbillionXdollarsXinX 
electricityX[8].XUsingXtheXconventionalXdieXmaterialsXsuchXasXironRbasedXalloysXorXtoolXsteels,Xhowever,X 
heatXcheckingXwouldXbeXoneXofXmajorXissuesXtoXbeXcoveredXinXtheXcopperXrotorXdieRcastingX[9].XHeatX 
checkingXisXgenerallyXarisingXfromXinsufficientXthermalXfatigueXpropertyXorXthermalXshockXresistanceXofX 
dieXmaterialsXunderXcyclicXthermalXstressXoperatingXenvironmentXresultingXinXlimitedXlifeXcycleXofXdieX 
materials.XThusXadvancedXdieXmaterialsXtoXuseXthemXmoreXthanX1273KXwouldXbeXhighlyXrequiredXifXoneX

Conclusions 
WeXhaveXinvestigatedXNi 
3AlXbasedXalloysXwithXvariousXalloyingXelementXtoXuseXthemXasXanXadvancedX 
dieXmaterialXinXtheXfieldXofXdieRcastingXindustry.XTheXfollowingXresultsXwereXobtainedXfromXtheXstudy.X
(1) ZrXadditionXtoXtheXNi 3AlXbasedXalloyXgivesXriseXtoXformXaXNi 5ZrXintermetallicXphase,XandXMoXisX 
partitionedX intoX bothX Ni 3AlX matrixX phaseX andX Ni 5ZrX intermetallicX dispersoidX withoutX formingX 
anotherXphase.XVolumeXfractionXratioXofXtheXpresentXalloyXsystemXisXprimarilyXdependentXonXZrX
contentXbutXnotXMo.X
(2) TheXlatticeXparametersXincreasedXmonotonouslyXwithXincreasingXcontentXofXMoXinXtheXNi 3AlXmatrixX 
phase.XTheXlatticeXparameterXisXnotXchangedXwithXanXalloyingXofXZrXinXtheXNi 3AlXmatrixXphase.X
(3) UltimateXtensileXstrengthXincreasesXwithXincreasingXvolumeXfractionXofXNi 5ZrXintermetallicXphaseX 
andXwithXincreasingXMoXcontentXinXNi 3AlXmatrixXphase.XItXisXsuggestedXthatXhigherXvolumeXfractionX 
ofXNi 5ZrXphaseXandXhigherXMoXcontentXinXNi 3AlXmatrixXphaseXareXfavorXtoXincreaseXultimateXtensileX 
strengthX overX theX wholeX temperatureX rangeX investigatedX inX theXpresentX twoRphaseX Ni 3Al/Ni 5ZrX 
intermetallicXalloys.XDuctilityXincreasedXwithXincreasingXtemperatureXupXtoX1073KXshowingXtheX 
maximumXandXthenXdecreasedXrapidlyXforXMoXand/orXZrRaddedXalloys.XTheXincreaseXinXductilityX 
withX increasingX temperatureX canX beX closelyX associatedX withXaX thermallyX activatedX diffusionX 
process.X
