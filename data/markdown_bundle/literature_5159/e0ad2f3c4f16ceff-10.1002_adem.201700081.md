Effects of Solution Treatment on the Microstructure
and Mechanical Properties of Ti –22Al–25Nb Alloys**
By Yang Wu,* Hongchao Kou, Bin Tang, Degui Liu, Jizhen Li and Jinshan Li
The effects of solution treatment on the microstructure and mechanical properties of Ti-22Al-25Nb
alloy are investigated. Numerous equiaxed a2 particles distribute in a B2 matrix after solution
treatment in the a2 þ B2 region. The growth rate of the B2 grain increases with solution temperature
and decreases with increasing holding time. When the solution temperature is lower than B2-transus,
it is seen that both the strength and ductility decrease with increasing solution temperature and
holding time. After being solution-treated at a super-transus temperature, the alloy with a fully B2
microstructure shows an ultralow elongation ( /C24 5%) and a typical brittle fracture characteristic.
1. Introduction
Ti3Al ( a2)-based alloys have many advantages, e.g., low
density , high strength, and good mechanical properties at
high temperature. However, their applications are still
limited due to the poor room temperature ductility and
workability .
[1– 3] The addition of Nb can effectively improve
the ductility and fracture toughness of Ti 3Al-based alloys. [4]
In the process of adding Nb to Ti 3Al-based alloys, Banerjee
et al. ﬁrst found the ordered orthorhombic (O) phase in
Ti– 22Al– 25Nb (at%) alloys. [5] Ti2AlNb alloys have attracted
extensive attention due to their attractive properties, such as
high speci ﬁc strength, good oxidation resistance, and
excellent creep resistance. [6] These alloys show the potential
to replace the Ni-based superalloys owing to their lower
density in the temperature range of 600 – 800 /C14 Cf o ru s ei nt h e
aviation and aerospace industries. [7]
As is well known, the mechanical properties of materials
are in ﬂuenced signi ﬁcantly by their microstructural
characteristics, such as phase morphology , phase constitu-
tion, and grain size. [8] Heat treatment is commonly used
to change the microstructure and obtain the desired
mechanical properties. [9,10] Tian et al. obtained a special
bimodal microstructure with primary a (ap) laths and ﬁne
lamellar transformed b by annealing in the a þ b region, and
the results indicated that increases in the temperature and
cooling rate could increase the strength of Ti – 4Al– 1.5Mn
alloy .[11] Du et al. investigated the relationship between the
microstructure and tensile properties of a new b high-
strength titanium alloy , and the results showed that the
primary a formed below the b-transus temperature and the
size of the secondary a could affect the properties
strongly .
[12] Zhang et al. obtained different volume fractions
of the a phase by various solution treatments for laser-
deposited Ti60A alloy . [13] In Ti 2AlNb alloys, the phase
composition is different from that in other Ti and TiAl alloys.
The control of the B2 grain size in the solution treatment
process is necessary , because the mechanical properties of
Ti
2AlNb alloys are pronouncedly in ﬂuenced by the grain
size.[14,15] During the solution treatment process, the B2
grain size is dependent on the solution temperature and
holding time. Thus, studying the kinetics of B2 grain growth
is valuable. However, few reports can be found on the
relationship between the microstructural characteristics and
mechanical properties, as well as the B2 grain growth
behavior, of Ti
2AlNb alloys.
In the present work, an as-received Ti – 22Al– 25Nb alloy
sheet was solution-treated at various temperatures and
times. The volume fraction of a2, the B2 grain growth
behavior, the B2 grain growth kinetics, and the room
temperature tensile properties of this alloy were studied.
The objective of this work is not only to elucidate the effects
of solution treatment on microstructure, but also to establish
a qualitative relationship between the mechanical properties
and microstructure.

distribute at the B2 grain boundaries, and limit the growth of the
B2 grains. Thus, the migration of the grain boundaries is slowed
down, and the value of n is lower than 0.5.

4. Conclusion
In the present work, a series of solution treatment
experiments were carried out to investigate the effects of
the solution temperature and time on the microstructures and
mechanical properties of Ti– 22Al– 25Nb alloy . In addition, the
growth behavior and kinetics of the B2 grain were studied.
The main conclusions are summarized as follows:
1) After being solution-treated in the a2 þ B2 region, the
Ti– 22Al– 25Nb alloy displays a large number of equiaxed a2
particles distributed in a B2 matrix. The volume fraction of
a2 decreases with increasing solution temperature and
holding time. In addition, a continuous grain boundary a2
(a2GB) is observed during the solution treatment process.
2) The growth rate of the B2 grains increases with increasing
solution temperature and decreases with increasing
holding time. Some a2 particles pin the B2 grain
boundaries, resulting in abnormal grain growth (AGG).
The values of the B2 grain growth exponent n are 0.29, 0.31,
0.32, and 0.35 for the solution temperatures of 1 010, 1 030,
1 050, and 1 080
/C14 C, respectively . The activation energyQ of
the B2 grain growth is 326.75 kJ mol /C0 1.
3) When the solution temperature is lower than B2-transus, it is
seen that both the strength and ductility decrease with
increasing solution temperature and holding time. This can
be attributed to the decrease ofa
2 phase volume fraction and
increase of B2 average grain size. Especially , the formation of
a
2GB results in a sharp decline in ductility . After being
solution-treated at a super-transus temperature, the alloy
with a fully B2 microstructure shows an ultralow elongation
(/C25 5%) and a typical brittle fracture characteristic.
