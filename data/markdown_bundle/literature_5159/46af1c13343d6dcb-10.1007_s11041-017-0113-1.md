DEVELOPMENT OF ION-PLASMA COATINGS FOR PROTECTING
INTERMETALLIC REFRACTORY ALLOYS VKNA-1V AND VKNA-25
IN THE TEMPERATURE RANGE OF 1200 – 1250°C
S. A. Budinovskii, 1 P. V . Matveev,1 and A. A. Smirnov 1
Translated from Metallovedenie i T ermicheskaya Obrabotka Metallov, No. 2, pp. 46 – 52, February, 2017.
Multilayer heat-resistant ion-plasma coatings for protecting the parts of the hot duct of gas-turbine engines
produced from refractory nickel alloys based on VKNA intermetallics from high-temperature oxidation are
considered. Coatings of the Ni – Cr – Al (Ta, Re, Hf, Y) + Al – Ni – Y systems are tested for high-tempera -
ture strength at 1200 and 1250°C. Metallographic and microscopic x-ray spectrum analyses of the structure
and composition of the coatings in the initial condition and after the testing are performed. The effect of pro-
tective coatings of the Ni – Cr – Al – Hf + Al – Ni – Y systems on the long-term strength of alloys VKNA-1V
and VKNA-25 at 1200°C is studied.
Key words: intermetallic nickel alloys, turbine parts, protective coatings, ion-plasma technology.
INTRODUCTION
Today, the possibilities of raising the strength characte -
ristics of castable refractory nickel alloys with intermetallic
hardening in the range of operating temperatures exceeding
1100 – 1150°C for gas turbines by alloying, optimization of
the composition and improving the structure of castings have
virtually been exhausted or involve considerable apprecia -
tion of the turbine production [1, 2]. In this connection, a
possible direction of complex elevation of the operating
characteristics of the known designs of gas turbine engines
(GTE) including lowering of their mass and raising the oper -
ating temperatures is application of intermetallic nickel al -
loys for the blade sets of GTE. Russian intermetallic castable
alloys VKNA-1V , VKNA-4U, VKNA-25, VIN-3, etc. pos -
sess a satisfactory combination of mechanical properties at
up to 1200°C in 1000-h tests. The content of aluminum in the
alloys based on nickel intermetallics is higher than in com -
mercial refractory alloys with intermetallic hardening. In this
connection, the intermetallic alloys possess a higher heat re -
sistance at the operating temperatures, which makes it possi -
ble to use them without a protective coating in some cases.
However, under the conditions of intense heat loads typical
for aircraft GTE long-term operation of parts from interme-
tallic alloys including turbine jet vanes, nozzle doors, etc.,
seems impossible without protective coatings. This makes
the task of development of protective heat-resistant coatings
for parts from alloys of type VKNA for advanced GTE quite
important.
The aim of the present work was to develop and investi -
gate refractory protective ion-plasma coatings for turbine jet
vanes and other parts of the hot duct of aircraft GTE from
intermetallic castable nickel alloys for operation at 1200°C
with permissible short-term heating to 1250°C.
METHODS OF STUDY
When searching for novel experimental compositions for
protective coatings on VKNA-type alloys we used a com -
mercial combined coating SDP-2 + VSDP-16 as the base
variant. Such coatings are deposited in two stages. In the first
stage the surface of the part is coated with a layer of nickel
alloy SDP-2 and in the second stage it is coated with alumi -
num alloy VSDP-16; then the part is heat treated in vacuum.

RESULTS AND DISCUSSION
Figure 1a presents the results of the study of the heat re-
sistance of specimens of alloy VKNA-1V with a coating at
1200°C. It can be seen that all the three tested coatings are
superior to the commercial SDP-2 + VSDP-16 coating with
respect to the high-temperature strength. The lowest loss in
the mass is exhibited by the VSDP-41 + VSDP-16 and
VSDP-4 + VSDP-16 coatings. The dependences of the spe-
cific loss in the mass on the time of the test can be approxi-
mated with a high reliability by a second-power polynomial;
for the test base of over 100 h the approximating dependence
is linear (Table 2). The linear approximation allows us to
evaluate the rate of the oxidation of the compositions stud -
ied. The slope of each straight line with respect to the time
axis (Fig. 1 a ) corresponds to the rate of the loss in the mass
per unit area of the surface of the specimens (g /c47m
2 /c215h), i.e.,
the oxidation rate (vox ) of the alloy during the test. The maxi -
mum oxidation rate at 1200°C is exhibited by alloy
VKNA-1V and the minimum one is exhibited by the coatings
containing alloys VSDP-4 and VSDP-41 with reduced chro -
mium content (Table 1). Alloying with rhenium does not af -
fect substantially the high-temperature strength of the com -
bined coatings. Therefore, the oxidation rates of the coatings
SPD-2 + VSDP-16 and VSDP-3 + VSDP-16 are close.
Figure 1 b and c present the results of the study of the
high-temperature strength of specimens from alloys
VKNA-1V and VKNA-25 with coatings at 1250°C. It can be
seen that the intensity of the oxidation process and of frac -
ture of the surfaces of the specimens increases. The depen -
dences of the change in the specific mass of the specimens
on the test time are linear. In these tests too, the specimens of
alloys VKNA-1V and VKNA-25 with VSDP-41 + VSDP-16
coatings exhibit the lowest loss in the specific mass and the
lowest oxidation rate (Table 3).

CONCLUSIONS
1. Double-layer ion-plasma coatings of the Ni – Cr – Al
(Ta, Re, Hf, Y) + Al – Ni – Y system obtained with the use
of nickel alloys with reduced chromium content provide pro -
tection of alloys VKNA-1V and VKNA-25 in the tempera -
ture range of 1200 – 1250°C for 500 and 300 h respectively.
Their heat resistance exceeds that of the standard
SDP-2 + VSDP-16 coating. Lowering of the proportion of
chromium from 20 to 6 – 10% makes it possible to form
coatings based on nickel aluminides, /c98- and /c103/c162-phases not
containing the alloying elements and inclusions lowering
their refractoriness.
2. The VSDP-4 + VSDP-16 coating does not lower the
guaranteed certified parameters of long-term strength of al -
loys VKNA-1V and VKNA-25 at 1200°C after testing for
100 and 500 h.
