glasses, and limited processability of shape memory alloys have been stumbling blocks for their ad -

veloped in this work. And the Ti-Ni base bulk metallic glass composites (BMGCs) rods were prepared by
the levitation suspend melting-water cooled Cu mold process. Microstructure, thermal behavior, mechani -
