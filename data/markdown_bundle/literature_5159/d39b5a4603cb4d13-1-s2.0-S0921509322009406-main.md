ABSTRACT 
Due to a monolithic body-centered cubic solid solution structure, refractory high-entropy alloys (HEA) always 
exhibit high strength and good softening resistance at both ambient and elevated temperatures. However, high 
density and low plasticity at ambient temperature limit their widespread applications as one kind of potential 
structural materials. The present study investigated the microstructure and mechanical properties of MoNbVTa

1. Introduction 
Conventional alloys that contain one or two principal elements are 
always suffered from a limitation in their comprehensive properties 
[ 1 – 3 ]. In contrast, high-entropy alloys (HEA) consist of five or more 
principal elements in equimolar or nearly equimolar ratios, which form 
a quite disordered solid solution structure [ 4 ]. Nowadays, HEAs have 
been successfully developed in various alloys systems, among which the 
refractory HEAs are one kind of potential structural materials due to 
their excellent mechanical properties and resistance to softening at 
elevated temperatures [ 5 – 16 ]. Senkov et al. proposed MoNbTaW and 
MoNbTaWV refractory HEAs with extraordinary high-temperature me -
chanical properties [ 17 , 18 ]. Afterwards, a novel HfNbTaTiZr refractory 
HEA was developed by replacing W, Mo and V with light-weight Hf, Zr 
and Ti [ 19 ]. Although HfNbTaTiZr exhibits high strength and excellent 
plasticity at ambient temperature, the yield strength at 1200 
◦
C is less 
than 100 MPa. Hence, HfMoNbTaTiZr HEA with refractory element of 
Mo was designed, which possesses almost six times the yield strength 
compared to HfNbTaTiZr [ 20 , 21 ]. Yao et al. found that replacing W in 
MoNbTaW with V element could obtain larger Poisson ’ s ratio and better 
plastic deformability at ambient temperature [ 22 ]. The lighter MoN -
bTaV shows application potentials in components serving in extreme 
environment, which requires both high temperature mechanical prop -
erties and low density. However, the previous studies did not focus on 
the high temperature mechanical property and composition 
optimization. 
The present work systematically investigated the influences of the 
relative atomic ratio of Ta on the microstructure and mechanical prop -
erties of MoNbVTa
x 
HEAs. The elemental micro-segregation caused by 
non-equilibrium solidification, the formation criteria of monolithic solid 
solution structure, the solid solution strengthening mechanism and the 
high-temperature performance were investigated in detail.

The refractory HEAs with nominal compositions of MoNbVTa

4. Conclusions 
In the present study, the microstructures and mechanical properties 
of MoNbVTa
x 
refractory HEAs were investigated at ambient and 
elevated temperatures. The main findings could be summarized as 
follows: 
1. MoNbVTa
x 
alloys exhibited a disordered BCC high entropy solid- 
solution phase structure. Physical parameters such as density and 
lattice constant basically follow the rules of mixture, while the 
hardness values do not follow the rule of mixture due to the solid- 
solution strengthening mechanism and grain size differences. 
2. The alloys exhibit typical dendritic structure, accompanied by den -
dritic segregation induced by non-equilibrium solidification. The 
micro-segregation characteristics of the constituents linearly depend 
on the difference between their melting points and theoretical 
melting temperatures of corresponding alloys. As Ta content in -
creases, the longer solidification time induced by the wider liquidus- 
solidus range improves the degree of elemental micro-segregation, 
especially for the element Ta and V. 
3. With the addition of high melting point element of Ta, the high 
temperature performance of the alloys has been improved to a 
certain extent, attributed to the excellent thermal stability of the 
grain boundary and the retarded recrystallization process. Among all 
compositions, Ta0.5 alloy exhibits the best high-temperature me -
chanical performance, such as the yield strength near 700 MPa and 
plastic strain over 35% at 1200 
◦
C. 
4. The variation law of the yield strength of the alloys with various Ta 
contents satisfies the solid solution strengthening mechanism. 
Affected by the atomic size mismatch and modulus mismatch be -
tween components, the yield strength of MoNbVTa
x 
alloys gradually 
decreases with a higher Ta content. 
5. Compared with the commercial superalloys or typical refractory 
HEAs, Ta0.5 alloy possesses obvious advantages in high temperature 
strength and specific strength, which makes it a potential engineer -
ing material in structural applications at high temperatures.
