ABSTRACT 
In general, metastable β -Ti-alloys exhibit low ductility at room temperature, which restricts its workability and 
critical applications. Here, we report a hierarchical microstructure tailoring strategy to achieve an excellent 
strength-ductility combination in Ti-4Al-5Mo-5V-5Cr-1Nb (Ti-45551) alloy. It was revealed that high density 
deformation twinning can be successfully introduced in primary α particles of Ti-45551 alloy through warm 
dynamic plastic deformation (DPD). After warm DPD process, the hierarchical microstructure was constructed in 
Ti-45551 alloy, including equiaxed primary α particles with high density deformation twins, β -phase matrix and 
finely dispersed nanoscale secondary α lamellas. Uniaxial tensile experiments have revealed that Ti-45551 alloy 
exhibited enhanced strength-ductility synergy with hierarchical microstructure at room temperature. This 
excellent combination of strength and ductility is derived from high twin density in in primary α particles and 
unique hierarchical microstructure. Also, this study demonstrates a feasible and low-cost route to design high 
performance Ti alloy. 
1. Introduction 
Titanium (Ti) alloys have been widely applied in the structural ap -
plications of aerospace, marine, energy and biomedical implants in -
dustries due to their high strength-to-density ratio, good weldability, 
excellent hardenability and corrosion resistance [1 – 4] . Over the last 
decades, metastable β -Ti alloys become one of the most versatile classes 
of Ti alloys with respect to excellent processing and mechanical prop -
erties [5,6] . Metastable β -Ti alloys exhibit a higher strength and 
toughness compared with other Ti alloys [7,8] . However, the ductility of 
metastable β -Ti alloys is still poor. So, extensive efforts have been 
devoted to the microstructure tailoring methods to achieve an excellent 
combination of high strength and ductility in metastable β -Ti alloys 
[9 – 11] . 
For metastable β -Ti alloys, the mechanical properties were very 
sensitive to their microstructure, especially the quantity and distribution 
of α phase, which can be controlled by the thermomechanical processes 
[12,13] . As the quantity and distribution of α phase exhibited a key role 
in the mechanical performance, it is essential to profound understand 
the effects of α phase on mechanical behaviors and deformation mech -
anisms of metastable β -Ti alloys [14,15] . Previous studies showed that 
ultrafine equiaxed α particles, which were introduced by severe plastic 
deformation (SPD), can largely enhance the strength of metastable β -Ti 
alloys [16,17] . However, simultaneously improvement of the ductility 
of high-strength metastable β -Ti alloys is still very difficult [18] . 
Pre-twinning can effectively improve the ductility of many metals 
and alloys with hexagonal close packed (hcp) crystalline structure via 
activating more slip systems and reducing local stress concentration 
[19] . Pre-twinning is a promising method for improving both strength 
and ductility of metastable β -Ti alloys. In this work, the warm dynamic 
plastic deformation (DPD) was performed on one metastable β -Ti alloy 
Ti-45551. The microstructure evolutions of Ti-45551 before and after 
warm DPD were investigated. And we examined the mechanical be -
haviors of Ti-45551 with pre-twinning hierarchical microstructure. The 
microstructure characterizations were carried out to investigate the ef -
fects of twin boundaries (TBs) on mechanical behavior and plastic 
deformation of Ti-45551.

is one of the causes for the local stress reduction, which plays an 
important role in the enhancement of ductility and formability of the Ti 
alloy at room temperature. 
4. Conclusions

erarchical microstructure by warm DPD. The high density of deforma -
tion twins was introduced in primary α particle by dynamic loading and 
the twin boundaries was well retained during tensile deformation. Pre-
