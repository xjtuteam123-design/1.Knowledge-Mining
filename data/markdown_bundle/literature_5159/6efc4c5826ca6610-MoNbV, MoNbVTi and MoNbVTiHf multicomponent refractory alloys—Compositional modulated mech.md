ABSTRACT 
In this paper, equiatomic MoNbV, MoNbVTi and MoNbVTiHf refractory multi-component alloys were investi -
gated in comparative style. Single body centered cubic (BCC) structure with increased lattice parameter were 
determined by X-ray diffraction. Typical dendrite structure was demonstrated by scanning electronic microscope. 
Mechanical testing shows excellent room temperature compressive mechanical properties of MoNbVTi high 
entropy alloy (HEA), which exhibited maximum compressive strength beyond 2000 MPa and the fracture strain 
exceeds 25 %. The high temperature compressive testing shows outstanding high temperature softening resis -
tance of MoNbV medium entropy alloy (MEA), which exhibited compressive strength above 1160 MPa, and 
plasticity ductility exceeds 18 % at 1273 K. The present study implies MoNbV MEA possesses excellent high 
temperature strength and plastic ductility,which shed light on the design of high temperature structural material 
with simple composition. 
1. Introduction 
The emerge of high-entropy alloys (HEAs) can be trace back to the 
end of last century. In early years, HEA were also mentioned as multi- 
principal-element alloys(MPEAs) [1] . The core of the HEAs concept 
lies in the ideal configurational entropy maximization in solid solution 
alloys with multi-principal elements in equimolar composition [2] . The 
design strategy of HEAs was widely different from conventional alloy 
synthesis idea and opening up new avenues of alloy design to be 
explored in depth. In the last two decades, HEAs have become one of the 
most coveted topics in alloy realm for its attractive thermodynamic 
performance, such as low temperature ductility, thermal stability, 
corrosion resistance, irradiation resistance, self-sharping, etc. [2,3] . 
Especially, in many engineering fields, HEAs were considered as pro -
spective structural materials for its concurrent of strength, ductility, and 
damage tolerance [4] . Classified by functionality, HEAs were catego -
rized into refractory high temperature alloys, light weight alloys, 
high-entropy coatings, irradiation resistant material, high entropy soft 
magnetic material, etc. By fabrication route, HEAs can be divided into 
casting high entropy eutectic alloys, deformed high-entropy alloys, 
powder metallurgy HEAs, 3D printing of HEAs, spattering high-entropy 
film, laser cladding HEAs, etc. 
As an important subset of HEAs, refractory high-entropy alloys 
(RHEAs) which based upon body centered cubic (BCC) refractory ele -
ments were regarded as promising high temperature structural material, 
particularly in areas of aerospace industry and nuclear power [5] . A 
grand international scientific and engineering effort have devoted to 
expand the capabilities of nickel-based super-alloys in the last sixty 
years,but improving its performance by tuning the alloy composition 
and adjusting thermomechanical treatments seems reach the end of the 
rope, even the currently developed ferritic superalloys still exhibit low 
high-temperature strengths [6] . The design flexibility and vastness of 
composition and microstructure space makes RHEAs a fascinating 
candidate compare to conventional superalloys. In recent years, re -
searchers have aroused great researching interest in RHEAs toward 
exploring the next-generation of superalloys.As well known, the ubiq -
uitous strength – ductility trade-off were always existed in traditional 
alloys, without exception in the brittle RHEAs system for fewer effective 
slip systems than in the FCC structure and the higher Peierls-Nabarro 
stresses for moving dislocations than those in FCC and HCP structures, 
this bottleneck hindered further engineering exploration in structural 
domain [7] . Therefore, researchers have striven to optimize the 
strength – ductility relationship of RHEA in the past decade. Among the 
multiple factors which influence on mechanical properties, the chemical

strength and corrosion resistance [8] . Hf, Nb, Ti and V are intrinsically 
ductile elements which reported in literature [8] . However, few studies

that is, the integration of strength and ductility. In this work, we con -
structed equiatomic MoNbV, MoNbVTi and MoNbVTiHf as model alloys 
to delve the strength-ductility connection of different combination 
pattern. According to the configuration entropy, MoNbV was classified 
as medium entropy alloy (MEA), and the another two alloys were 
defined as high entropy alloy (HEA). To the best of our knowledge, 
comparative study between MEA and HEA was rarely reported, which

3.4. Mechanical properties 
3.4.1. Room temperature compressive mechanical properties 
The mechanical properties of MPEAs were evaluated by compressive 
loading on the cylindrical samples, which could be prepared easily by 
arc melting [11] . First, the as cast MoNbV, MoNbVTi, and MoNbVTiHf 
samples were prepared to perform room temperature testing, as Fig. 4 
shows. It was clearly seen, the compressive stress-strain curves of these 
MPEAs developed in distinctive feature, suggesting different mechanical 
response characteristics of individual composition. Obviously, the en -
gineering strain-engineering stress curves indicated that the quaternary 
MoNbVTi alloy simultaneously have maximum compressive strength 
and best fracture toughness,its compressive strength exceeds 2000 MPa 
and ultimate compressive strain reaches as high as 33 % before the 
fracture happens.This exceptional case manifest the room temperature 
brittleness which universally exist in RHEAs was effectively prevented in 
MoNbVTi system. Wang et al. reported a effectively strategy to solve 
room-temperature brittleness of RHEA NbMoTaW via grain boundary 
engineering with the addition of either metalloid B or C,and successfully 
achieved large plasticity of > 10 %, along with high strength of 
> 1750 MPa [11] , definitely we have obtained preferable mechanical 
properties in MoNbVTi system without any addition of nonmetallic el -
ements. Nonetheless, in the another two counterparts, the compressive 
ductility as well as compressive strength were far below the quaternary 
one, the remarkable differences in mechanical performance manifests 
the composition of alloys plays a critical role in mechanical character -
istics. In other words, for MoNbV alloy, the adding of titanium yields 
enhancing effect in mechanical property, inversely, for MoNbVTi alloy, 
the mechanical property became deteriorated when the hafnium adding 
to it. 
It is well known that RHEAs posses sufficient mechanical strength at 
room temperature but with poor room temperature malleability, the 
excellent performance of MoNbVTi demonstrates the general room 
temperature brittleness has greatly improved in this system. The typical 
serration behavior was obviously exhibited in MoNbVTi alloy, according 
to literature [12] , this type of serrated flow was categorized as type-B 
serration [13] , which affected by factors such as the composition, tem -
perature, irradiation damage, and strain rate. The macro-scale me -
chanical properties revealed under room temperature compressive 
loading will benefited us to design appropriate RHEA system to realize 
desirable engineering application. 
3.4.2. High temperature mechanical properties 
Fig. 5 shows the compressive engineering stress-strain curves of the 
MPEAs measured by dynamic thermal simulation machine at elevated 
temperatures, it was obviously demonstrated that all alloys exhibited 
significant serration behavior from 1073 K to 1273 K, compared to room 
temperature condition, the serration flow became more evident. Fig. 5 
(a) drawn the compressive mechanical curves at 1073 K. As demon -
strated, similar mechanical characteristic was embodied in such group 
of strain-stress curves, from which the strength-ductility synergism were 
achieved in some extent. The comparable plastic strain was not less than 
22 % and the minimum compressive strength exceeds 1530 MPa.

counterparts of MoNbVTi and MoNbVTiHf alloys, both suffered sub -
stantially reduction of compressive strength from 1173 K to 1273 K, 
approximately 300 MPa and 600 MPa, respectively. 
According to theoretical model of lattice distortion strengthening, 
the lattice distortion effect is a core factor to achieve high strength 
material, as reported in literature [14] , the lattice distortion is propor -
tional to material strength, in contrast, here we obtained some contra -
dictory results which cannot be interpreted by lattice strengthening 
mechanism. By carefully analyzing, it could be deduced there exist at 
least two factors which account for the abnormal mechanical perfor -
mance. Firstly, the typical lattice distortion strengthening model was 
established on the premise of there forming a solid solution single phase 
in the matrix, subsequently, we concluded that transformation maybe 
happened in the high temperature MoNbVTi and MoNbVTiHf samples 
and producing multiple phase constitutions. Secondly, the sluggish 
diffusion effect became more remarkable as the number of principle 
elements increased, emerging concentration fluctuation among ele -
ments, contributed to the dramatically reduce of compressive strength. 
In this regard, it is proposed that the uniformity of elements distribution 
is another important factor in tuning mechanical properties. In fact, 
aforementioned phase transformation also promoted the heterogeneous 
distribution of elements. The combined effect of these factors facilitated 
comprehensive high temperature softening effect, which has a critical 
influence on the mechanical properties of high temperature structure 
materials. It was apparently demonstrated that in our experiment, the 
MoNbV medium entropy alloy exhibit outstanding softening resistance 
and thermal stability at high temperatures. 
4. Conclusions 
In summary, three kinds of MPEAs we elaborately designed in this 
work, namely MoNbV, MoNbVTi, and MoNbVTiHf. Microstructure and 
elements distribution were carefully analyzed combined with 
compressive loading tests at different temperature. Composition domi -
nated mechanical characteristics demonstrated that MoNbVTi alloy 
have outstanding room temperature compressive properties while 
MoNbV medium entropy alloys shows excellent combination of strength 
and ductility under elevated temperature. The surprising outcome of the 
comparative study of MPEAs present a issue that expected to make a 
further theoretical examination in our future work. Our study also opens 
avenues for the design of superior high temperature structural materials.
