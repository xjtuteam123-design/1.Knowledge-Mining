Electromagnetic shaping and solidiﬁcation control of
Ni-base superalloys under vacuum
H.Z. Fu, J. Shen, L. Liu∗, Q.T. Hao, S.M. Li, J.S. Li
State Key Laboratory of Solidiﬁcation Processing, Northwestern Polytechnical University, Xi’an, Shaanxi 710072, PR China
Received 7 April 2003; received in revised form 3 July 2003; accepted 18 November 2003
Abstract
This paper aims to introduce two novel techniques of electromagnetic conﬁnement to process Ni-base alloys for castings and ingots with
shaped cross-section. Based on systematically investigation on the distribution of magnetic and temperature ﬁelds and their coupling effect,
a containerless electromagnetic shaping of aluminum alloy and superalloys samples was successfully realized by means of a specially
designed apparatus with dual induction coils and dual-frequencies and by using a tailor-made crystallizer under vacuum condition. A near-net
shaped turbine blade of superalloys with directionally solidiﬁed microstructure by electromagnetic soft-contact conﬁnement technology
was also obtained. The dynamic stability and proﬁle behavior of melt and temperature gradient during electromagnetic conﬁnement are
presented.

1. Introduction
More attention is paid to the ﬁelds of electromagnetic
shaping processing of melt, which is a high cleanness, low
contamination and near-net shape manufactured technol-
ogy. Up to now, two typical technologies of electromagnetic
processing of melt have been used in metallurgy and mate-
rials processing. There are electromagnetic casting (EMC)
[1–3] and magnetic suspension melt processing (MSMP)
[4,5]. The former is a very important technology in the met-
allurgical industry and has primarily realized containerless
continuous casting, but it is mainly used to prepare ingots of
aluminum alloys with round and rectangular cross-section
normally at atmosphere [6]. Furthermore, the solidiﬁcation
process and solidiﬁed structure are difﬁcult to control. The
latter still needs the conventional investment mould for
castings that will unavoidably lead to an interface reaction
between the melt and the mould.
With the development of hi-tech and relevant industries,
large numbers of Ni, Ti, Nb and Zr and their alloys are
extensively used in aeronautics, astronautics, energy, com-
munication and chemical engineering. NASA estimated that
Ni, Ti and their alloys would take up to 70% of all of ma-

Superalloys, titanium and these kinds of advanced materi-
als are characterized not only by high density, high melting
point, low magnetic permeability and conductivity and high
activity, but also low plasticity for deformation. According
to their physical properties, a new method called electromag-
netic conﬁnement shaping under vacuum or in inlet gas was
developed by the authors. The present paper addresses two
methods for carrying out electromagnetic conﬁnement tests
of these special alloys. One is electromagnetic conﬁnement
and solidiﬁcation (EMCS) and the other is electromagnetic
soft-contact conﬁnement and solidiﬁcation (EMSCCS).

2. Experiment design
Electromagnetic conﬁnement and solidiﬁcation process
is illustrated in Fig. 1. The upper inductor with higher fre-
quency current is mainly for heating and melting of solid
material and the lower inductor with lower frequency cur-
rent is basically for shaping. A continuous process is kept by
means of solid material feeding from the top, then melting
and solidiﬁcation within both inductors. The liquid metal
is shaped containerlessly under electromagnetic pressure
and becomes a solid part with a given cross-section. The
directional structure was obtained by pulling the sample
into a crystallizer. Due to the melting and shaping under
the heating and the conﬁnement of electron-magnetic pres-
sure instead of physical moulds, the process can be realized
without contamination.
Although EMCS can shape the liquid metal without con-
tamination, it is difﬁcult to shape ingots with more complex
cross-section and with near-net shape, for the electromag-
netic pressure exerted on the samples is difﬁcult to control.
As a result, new technology namely as electromagnetic
soft-contact conﬁnement and solidiﬁcation with two powers
and frequencies is proposed. The principle of this process
illustrated in Fig. 2 . The upper coil is used basically for
heating the master pieces to smell and the lower inductor
for continuously heating the melt and realizing electromag-
netic soft-contact conﬁnement of liquid metal to near-net
shape. The process is conducted in a magnetic mould. There

currently used high rate solidiﬁcation (HRS) technology, the
contact time of which is normally more than half an hour.

4.2. Electromagnetic soft-contact conﬁnement and
solidiﬁcation with dual-frequency ﬁelds
Extensive investigations on the effect of magnetic mould
materials, the height and position of the suseptor for dif-
ferent assembly of magnetic mold and inductors, the power
voltage on the magnetic ﬂux density and the melt menis-
cus height were conducted. Combining the electromagnetic
soft-contact shaping with directional solidiﬁcation technol-
ogy, a near-turbine blade casting of superalloy with good
surface quality were obtained, as shown in Fig. 8.
The advantage of EMSCCS process is to reduce the
contamination from the magnetic mould reduces to some
extent compared to HRS process. A novel manufacturing
technology for making castings with various complicated
cross-sections and contours with less contamination could
be developed in the near future on the basis of the electro-
magnetic soft-contact conﬁnement process.
5. Conclusions
1. Two kinds of material forming techniques were intro-
duced, that is electromagnetic conﬁnement and solidiﬁca-
tion (EMCS) and the other is electromagnetic soft-contact
conﬁnement and solidiﬁcation (EMSCCS).
2. The theoretical models of EMCS process are put for-
ward. Enough electromagnetic pressure exerted to sam-
ples and proper values of the ratio of heat to force
are required for shaping the given materials and pre-
determined cross-sections in dual-frequency magnetic
ﬁeld.
3. The samples of superalloy and aluminum alloy were ob-
tained with containerless electromagnetic shaping pro-
cess. Also, the near-turbine blade samples of superalloy
were prepared by means of soft-contact shaping process
with directionally solidiﬁed structure.
