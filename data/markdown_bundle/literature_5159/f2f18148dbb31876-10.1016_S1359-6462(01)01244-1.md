4. Conclusion
Four kinds of Cr-base alloys, each of which
contains 10 and 30 at.% of Re and W, were de-
signed and produced by arc melting. The com-
pressiveandoxidationpropertiesofthedeveloped
alloysweretestedatseveraltemperatures.Mostof
the developed alloys were ductile under compres-
sive strains. Compared with the Ni-base single
crystalandtheFe–Cralloy,someofthedeveloped
alloys had superior compressive strength and oxi-
dation resistance. Developed alloys with enough
ductility at room temperature and excellent com-
pressive strength can be produced by commercial
levelimpurityofrawmaterials.
