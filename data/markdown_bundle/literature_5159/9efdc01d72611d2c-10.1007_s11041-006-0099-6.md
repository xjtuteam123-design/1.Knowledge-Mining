EFFECT OF HIGH-GRADIENT DIRECTED CRYSTALLIZATION
ON THE STRUCTURE AND PROPERTIES OF RHENIUM-BEARING
SINGLE-CRYSTAL ALLOY
Yu. A. Bondarenko, 1 E. N. Kablov, 1 V . A. Surova,1 and A. B. Echin 1
Translated from Metallovedenie i Termicheskaya Obrabotka Metallov, No. 8, pp. 33 – 35, August, 2006.
The effect of directed crystallization with high temperature gradient at the growth front on the structure, den-
dritic segregation, porosity, and high-temperature strength of a recent rhenium-bearing refractory alloy with
single-crystal structure used for casting GTE blades is considered.
INTRODUCTION
The development of aircraft engine engineering is
closely connected with design and creation of new high-tem-
perature materials for parts of the hot conduit of gas turbine
engines (GTE) and with advancement of old and creation of
new processes of their production.
New tendencies have appeared in the world industry
(Russia, Germany, USA, Japan) in the field of high-tempera-
ture nickel alloys used for fabricating GTE blades with sin-
gle-crystal structure, which are connected with additional al-
loying of the alloys with such hardening refractory elements
as W and Ta as well as Re and Ru. W and Re increase the
melting temperature of high-temperature nickel alloys pri -
marily affecting the thermal stability of the /c103-matrix and of
the hardening /c103/c162-phase and hinder the diffusion process. Tan-
talum also promotes improvement of the resistance to gas
corrosion. Ruthenium increases the solubility of refractory
elements in the /c103/c162-phase, which improves its stability and,
consequently, the high-temperature strength of the alloys.
However, at maximum alloying with hardening elements
in amounts close to the limiting solubility and under condi -
tions of nonequilibrium crystallization that take place in the
production of GTE parts the probability of formation of ex -
cess phases not participating in the reinforcement increases;
these are /c103– /c103/c162eutectics, tcp-phases, and /c97-phases.
Russian rhenium-bearing alloys ZhS-32, ZhS-36 (3 – 4%
Re) and high-temperature ZhS-47 (9% Re) and foreign al -
loys Rene N6, CMSX-10 (6% Re), EPM-102 (6% Re and
3% Ru) (U.S.) and TMS-162, TMS-175 (6% Re and 6% Ru)
(Japan) deserve special mention among modern high-tem-
perature nickel alloys [1].
In order to ensure a high level of high-temperature pro-
perties in the production of rotor blades for recent GTE Rus-
sian and foreign producers resort to the method of directed
crystallization. This method ensures in the blades a single-
crystal structure (without grain boundaries) with specified
crystallographic orientation.
However, even this advanced process cannot solve all the
problems connected with the formation of a structure with
coarse dendritic and phase components and also with consi-
derable dendritic segregation and dendrite spacing porosity
caused by a low temperature gradient on the growth front and
a low cooling rate ensured by recent commercial equipment.
In order to lower dendritic segregation in the production
of single-crystal GTE blades, producers use high-tempera -
ture and long-term homogenizing treatment. The process is
connected with the possibility of melting of eutectic phases
and requires special vacuum devices. Foreign producers use
an additional expensive and laborious process of gasostatic
pressing in order to reduce the porosity. This process has
constraints with respect to the size of the blades.
In order to solve the problem of substantial improvement
of the quality of the structure, to ensure structural homogene-
ity, to lower the dendritic segregation and porosity, and to
eliminate casting defects in the form of flow streaks VIAM
has developed a new process of high-gradient directed crys -
tallization of high-temperature alloys (temperature gradient
G /c179200 K/c47cm) [2].
The aim of the present work consisted in studying the ef-
fect of high-gradient directed crystallization on the structure
and properties of a recent rhenium-bearing single-crystal al -
loy for GTE blades.

METHODS OF STUDY
We studied the rhenium-bearing (9% Re) high-tempera -
ture alloy ZhS47 developed by VIAM for single-crystal cast-
ing. The service characteristics of this alloy are considerably
superior to those of the known single-crystal high-tempera -
ture alloys.
The process of directed crystallization was performed in
high-gradient installations developed for the purpose by
VIAM (a module UVNÉS-4 and a pilot UVNÉS-5 ones).
The process is characterized by intense supply of heat into
the melt above the crystallization front and active removal of
heat below the crystallization front, which ensures a consi -
derable temperature drop between the heating and cooling
zones. The process and the equipment have been patented in
the Russian Federation.
We investigated the microstructure of single-crystal spe -
cimens of alloy ZhS-47 obtained at the same crystallization
rate (about 5 mm/c47min) and various temperature gradients on
the growth front. We determined the coefficient of segrega -
tion of elements in alloy ZhS-47 in the process of directed
crystallization at various gradients by the method of micro -
scopic x-ray spectrum analysis (MXRSA) and evaluated the
high-temperature strength and the fatigue resistance of the
alloy.

Growth in the temperature gradient on the front of di -

/c103– /c103/c162eutectic (Fig. 2a ) that form in the dendrite arm spacing
in the last moment of crystallization.

Studies3 of the single-crystal structure of fractured speci-
mens showed that the pores emerging in the course of
high-temperature solution annealing at the places of dissolu-
tion of the /c103– /c103/c162eutectic play an important role in the frac-
ture process. In the tests for the long-term strength under
creep the pores, which commonly have a spherical shape, de-
form and acquire a lenticular shape (Fig. 2 d ). Microcracks
nucleate on their walls. Then the microcracks merge into
through cracks that cause failure of the specimen.
Since the process of high-gradient directed crystalliza-
tion ensures a homogeneous fine-dendrite structure in the
cast state and lower dendritic segregation and porosity than
the conventional process, we determined the properties of al-
loy ZhS-47 not subjected to heat treatment after crystalliza -
tion (Table 2).
The results show that the long-term strength of the speci-
mens obtained by the method of high-gradient directed crys -
tallization without subsequent heat treatment meets the per -
formance specification. This makes it possible to develop a
simpler and more economical process for fabricating GTE
blades.
In order to commission the process of high-gradient di -
rected crystallization VIAM has created a pilot vacuum
UVNÉS-5 installation with a computerized control system.
The studies performed allowed us to develop an experi -
mental process for casting cooled rotor blades for GTE from
refractory rhenium-bearing alloy ZhS-47 with single-crystal
structure in the UVNÉS-5 installation with computerized
control system.
The development of the high-gradient process and the
creation of special vacuum commercial equipment make it
possible to use refractory rhenium-bearing alloys in the pro -
duction of most heavily loaded GTE blades for promising
engines of the fifth generation.
CONCLUSIONS
High-gradient directed crystallization of rhenium-bear-
ing high-temperature alloy ZhS-47 with single-crystal struc-
ture yields a more homogeneous fine-dendrite structure with
lower dendritic segregation and porosity than the commer-
cial process of casting of GTE blades and ensures high cha-
racteristics of high-temperature strength and fatigue resis-
tance in cast and heat-treated states.
