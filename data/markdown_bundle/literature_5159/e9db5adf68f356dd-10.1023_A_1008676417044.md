Achievements in Commercial Production of PM
Disks and Shafts for Aircraft Engines in Russia
B.I. BONDAREV , N.F. ANOSHKIN, O. KH. FATKULLIN, G.S. GARIBOV , L.S. BUSLA VSKY ,
I.A. KONONOV AND V .I. YEREMENKO
All-Russia Institute of Light Alloys Gorbunov St., 2, Moscow 121596, Russia
Abstract. The paper shows stages of development of Ni-base superalloy powder metallurgy in Russia, including
modifying of the alloys, with the reference to this new technology, production, testing and ﬂying service of turbine
disks in various aircraft engines. Histograms plotted from the results of mechanical property testing are presented.
Prospects of application of PM Ni-base superalloys in production of aircraft engines are shown.
Keywords: aerospace applications, hot isostatic pressing (HIP), mechanical properties, PM processing, super-
alloys
The main disadvantage of the technology for production modern highly-alloyed alloy of
heavy loaded gas-turbine aircraft engine parts by plastic working methods is an incomplete
elimination of microchemical and structural inhomogeneity formed during solidiﬁcation of
large metal masses into ingots. The new method of PM component production has no such
drawbacks.
The method consists in producing with high cooling rate in solidiﬁcation ( 10
3–105 deg/sec )
microingots-powders of micron tens size and the subsequent hot isostatic pressing HIP at
pressure up to 2000 atm. And segregation is shown only within one granule and segregation
is signiﬁcantly lower.
It was in early the 70s when Soviet metallurgy work dealing with designation and pro-
duction of nickel superalloy powders started. It was in that time when it became obvious:
making chemical composition of superalloys used in aerospace gas turbines complicated
is obligatory for their technical characteristics improvement. But it makes impossible to
apply traditional methods in production of large-size billets of complicated forms.
By the present time a great number of scientiﬁc and application tasks have been solved,
serial technology for production of a wide range of complex billets with a high production
yield has been introduced. The following problems solved in the powder metallurgy process
are:
1. Perfecting the chemical composition of the existing wrought and casting alloys as far
as they are produced by the powder metallurgy method. In doing so the compositions
were balanced to provide dispersed precipitation and uniform distribution of carbide
constituents in the billet structure, to provide the absence of lattice of oxicarbide precip-
itates an the initial powder boundaries after compacting and to provide optimal content
and composition of strengtheningγ
′-phase.

4. Elaboration of a number of processes for separation of powders from non-metallic and
other inclusions (magnetic and electrostatic separation) and vacuum degassing. It is
achieved due to handling of powders in a thin layer with heating and subsequent sealing
in a steel capsules. These processes being applied, the maximal permissable oxygene

20 units per 1 kg.
5. Elaboration of the process of hot isostatic pressing (HIP) in gas or liquid medium with
welded sheet capsules made of stainless steel or carbon, steel 20 are used.
For all this technology of capsules elements drawing of complicated forms has been
developed. Works on simulation of form changing power process during HIP process
enabled to make the process of capsule design automatic and to optimize isostatic com-
paction process parameters. Both factors are necessary to improve efﬁciency of this
complex and expensive technological process.
Hot isostatic pressing can be carried out according to two schemes: with pre-
heating powder in degassed capsules with subsequent processing in isostatic condi-
tions and pressing at simultaneous temperature and pressure increase in the gasostatic
vessel.
In order to produce optimum solid solution grain size from the mechanical properties
point of view, and the structure without porosity HIP is performed as a rule at tem-
peratures 10–20
◦C higher than the solvus temperature. Depending on alloy chemical
composition the mean size of solid solution grain is 30–80 m.
Performing HIP in double phase (γ+γ′) area facilitates to form the structure consist-
ing of areas with cast dendritic-cellulous structure and of areas with ﬁne recrystallized
grains. HIP is a singleγ -phase area provides formation of fully recrystallized structure.
Thus, in HIPping the powder alloys there are two parallel processes take place. In areas
of small size powder particles, undergoing signiﬁcant deformation in HIP, recrystalliza-
tion processes take place accompanied by grain growth andγ -phase particles enlarging.
In powders of large size homogenization runs with replacing cast dendritic-cellulous
structure with grain polyhedron structure.
6. Perfection the process of thermal treatment of billets made of powder alloys enables
us to obtain the material with the optimal distribution in the carbide constituents and
strengtheningγ -phase in structure when residual stresses are at the minimal level, the
factors being necessary for optimal combination of ultimate tensile strength, plasticity
and also high-temperature strength and low-cycle fatigue resistance.

7. The conducted works resulted in the development of the technology for production

EP 975P alloys has been introduced. Parts made of powder superalloys are applied in such

disks is 40,000.

PS 90A engine was 36,000 hours, including ﬂight testing time of 11,200 hours.
