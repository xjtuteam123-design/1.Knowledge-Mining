ABSTRACT
La2O3/W composite powders were prepared by a wet chemical method,using ammonium metatungstate (AMT)
and lanthanum oxides (La 2O3) as raw materials. La 2O3/W composite materials were obtained by sintering the
powder compacts at 2200 °C. The La 2O3/W composite powders were characterized by X-ray di ﬀraction (XRD),
Field emission scanning electron microscope (FESEM) coupled with energy dispersive spectrum (EDS) and
microstructure of the La 2O3/W composites was characterized by SEM and EDS. The results showed that the
La2O3/W composite powders have polyhedral shape with particle size of 100 –200 nm and some La 2O3 particles
were wrapped up by tungsten particles. The highest relative density of 96.93% was obtained for the composites
containing 0.6 wt% La 2O3. Maximum bending strength and Rockwell hardness reach 434 MPa and 70.3HRA,
respectively, for the 0.6%La 2O3/W composites. Scanning electron microscopy observations reveal that the
La2O3-W core-shell structure still remains in the sintered La 2O3/W composites.

The solubility of APT in water (smaller than 2% at 20 °C) is far less
than AMT, so APT cannot completely dissolve in water. The APT
precipitates nucleates around La(OH) 3, they cover the surface of La
(OH)3, forming a core-shell structures. Fig. 2 illustrates formation
process of the core-shell structure.
Fig. 3 shows XRD patterns of the oxide powders obtained by

contain W and O elements. And the atom ratio of W to O elements is
close to the designed value. Again, due to the low content and excellent

3.3. Physical and mechanical properties of La
2O3/W materials
Fig. 11 shows relative density of the La 2O3/W materials with La 2O3
contents of 0, 0.2, 0.4, 0.6, and 0.8, respectively. It can be seen that the
contents of La
2O3 have great e ﬀects on the sintering densi ﬁcation of the
La2O3/W green compacts. With the increase of La 2O3 content, relative
density of the specimens increases to 96.93% for 0.6 wt% La 2O3/W
composites. However, relative density decreases with further increasing
La
2O3 content from 0.6% to 0.8%. This may be the results of two
mechanisms. Firstly, during the powder preparation process, the pre-
sence of lanthanum oxide particles reduces the contact area between the
W particles and hinders the mutual di ﬀusion between the tungsten
particles. The lanthanum oxide particles attach onto the surface of
tungsten particles. This leads to the decrease of surface energy, and
inhibits the growth of tungsten particles. The re ﬁnement of the tungsten
particles is bene ﬁcial to the improvement of sinterability and the
densiﬁcation of the specimens. Secondly, the La
2O3 particles reduce
the surface energy of the powder particles, and reduce the di ﬀusion rate
between tungsten particles. Furthermore, the La 2O3 particles distribute
in the grain boundaries and hinder the migration of grain boundaries,
which will impede the sintering densi ﬁcation. The total e ﬀects of La
2O3
on the sintering densi ﬁcation may be comprehensive of the above two
mechanisms. When the content of the La 2O3 reaches to 0.8 wt%,
inhibition eﬀect on densi ﬁcation is greater than that of promotion, and
the relative density of the sintered samples decrease. The relative density
of the La2O3/W materials is up to ~5 percentage points higher than that
of pure W.

La2O3/W materials. With the increase of La 2O3 content, Rockwell
hardness and bending strength ﬁrst increase and then decrease. The
La2O3/W composites show higher strength and hardness than the pure
W, which may be ascribed the grain re ﬁne and sintering densiﬁ cation
due to La 2O3 addition. Maximum Rockwell hardness and bending

4. Conclusions
La2O3/W composite powders with core-shell structures have been
successfully prepared by a wet chemical method. The powders show
granular shape with particle size of 100 to 200 nm. A sintering
densiﬁcation of more than 96% was achieved for the La 2O3/W
materials containing 0.6% of La 2O3. Mean size of W grain in the
sintered La2O3/W samples decreases as La 2O3 adds and the particle size
distribution becomes narrow. Some core-shell structures still remain in
the sintered samples. The La
2O3 content has an obvious in ﬂuence on the
microstructure and properties of La 2O3/W composites. Maximum
bending strength and Rockwell hardness of 434 MPa and 70.3 HRA,
respectively, were achieved for La 2O3/W composites containing 0.6%
of La 2O3.
