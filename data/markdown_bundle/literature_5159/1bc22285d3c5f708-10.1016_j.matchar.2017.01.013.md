Abstract: Al-Cu-Mg matrix composites reinforced with 5-9 vol. % nano -sized TiCx 
particles were fabricated by combustion synth esis (CS) and hot pressing process 
combined with hot extrusion. Results showed that t he tensile strength, yield strength 
and fracture strain of the composites were simultaneously improved compared with 
powder metallurgy (PM) Al-Cu-Mg alloy at 493-573 K. For instance, in comparison 
with Al-Cu-Mg alloy at 573 K, the tensile strength and fracture strain of 5 vol. % 
nano-sized TiCx/Al-Cu-Mg composite increased by 2.4% and 196.0%, while those of

Keyword: Al matrix composites (AMCs); Nano -sized TiC x; EBSD; High

1. Introduction 
Nano-sized particles reinforced aluminum matrix composites (AMCs) exhibit a 
high strength and good ductility, and meanwhile offer superior wear, creep and fatigue 
performance [1-13]. These prominent properties enable them to be potential materials 
for high temperature applications in automobile and aerospace industries. However, 
the acquirement of well dispersed nano-sized particles and good particle-matrix

ACCEPTED MANUSCRIPT

interface in the matrix are essential for the practical application of such materials 
[5,7]. 
Good distribution of nano -sized particles in the Al matrix is a challenge on 
account of its extremely huge surface area and high surface energy [8]. Many methods 
have been developed to incorporate nano -sized particles into Al matrix, which could 
be divided into ex-situ [4,9-11] and in-situ fabrication methods [ 3,7]. Commonly the 
reinforced particles introduced by ex-situ method are susceptible to contamination and 
tend to agglomerate due to the poor wettability between the matrix and reinforcement, 
resulting in the degradation of the particle -matrix interface as well as the mechanical 
properties at both room and elevated temperature [ 5]. On the contrary, the particles 
generated by in situ methods, including combustion synthesis, molten salt, etc., are 
usually fine and have cleaner particle -matrix interface [12,13]. Han et al.[ 7] 
introduced 4 wt% nano-sized TiB2 into Al-Si alloy through the exothermic reaction of 
hybrid K2TiF6 and KBF4 in the molten matrix alloy. They reported that the composite 
possessed higher modulus than that of the Al-Si alloy at 20 C-350 C, but no 
apparent increase in the tensile properties. Moreover, the regions containing clustered 
TiB2 particles were damaged first in the composite. Recently, Zhou et al. [3] prepared 
nano-sized TiC-Al master alloy via combustion synthesis method and then dispersed 
it into the molten Al -Cu alloy to obtain nano-sized TiC p/Al-Cu composites . They 
found that the composites exhibited high tensile strength and excellent elongation, 
which increased by 11.08% and 187.9% , respectively compared with those of the 
Al-Cu alloy. However, further research on their high temperature tensile properties

In previous work [14], a combined method of CS and hot pressing followed by 
hot extrusion was used to prepare nano-sized TiC x/2009Al composites . Compared 
with PM 2009Al alloy, the composites reinforced with various volume fractions of 
nano-sized TiC x particles exhibit high strength but their fracture s train markedly 
decreased at room temperature. Nevertheless, high temperature tensile properties of 
the present composites are unknown, making a further study necessary. In this paper, 
the high temperature mechanical properties of 5 -9 vol.% nano -sized TiC x/2009Al 
composites were investigated at 493-573 K, and the strengthening mechanisms were 
discussed in detail as well. 
2. Experiment 
The used raw materials were CNTs (10 nm-20 nm in diameter and 20 µm-100 
µm in length) , Ti powders (~48 µm) and 2009Al alloy powders ( ~75 µm). 2009Al 
alloy contained 3.7 wt%Cu, 1.3 wt%Mg, 0.25 wt%Si, 0.05 wt%Fe and Bal. Al. Firstly, 
CNTs were milled alone for 2 h in a cylindrical stainless steel drum. Then the ground 
CNTs and Ti powders (molar ratio of 1:1 ) were mixed sufficiently with different 
volume fractions of 2009Al alloy powders by ball milling for 48 h. The same process 
was also used for 2009Al alloy powders. The blended powders and ball -milled 
2009Al alloy powders were compressed into a cylinder (Φ45 mm×35 mm). The 
cylindrical compacts were then put into a self-made vacuum furnace and heated in a 
vacuum atmosphere. The combustion synthesis reaction happened when the 
temperature measured by the W5 -Re26 thermocouple suddenly rose rapidly, and then

the reactants were quickly pressed while they were still hot and soft . Besides, the 
comparative sample for 2009Al alloy was manufactured by powder metallurgy (PM) 
in the same furnace . Hot extrusion process of 2009Al alloy and composites was 
conducted under an extrusion ratio of 19:1 at 773 K. The extruded samples were then 
T4-treated (solution treated at 783 K for 60 min, water quenching and then aged at 
room temperature for 96 h). 
The samples were detected by transmission electron microscopy (TEM, JEM 
2100F, Japan) and scanning electron microscopy (SEM , Tescan vega3 XM, Czech 
Republic) equipped with an Oxford NordlysMax EBSD detector . The samples 
prepared for EBSD characterization were first mechanical polishing up to 2000 mesh 
SiC (6.5 micron) abrasive paper, then fine polishing to mirror, and finally 
electro-polished with a hybrid solution of 20% HClO4-80% ethanol at 273 K. The step 
size (for EBSD maps) for 2009Al alloy and composites was 2 µm and 1µm , 
respectively. Maps were analyzed using the Oxford HKL Channel5 software package. 
Tensile test s were performed at a strain rate of 3×10 -4s-1 on a n INSTRON-5689 
material testing machine at 493 K, 533 K and 573 K.

nano-sized TiC x content increasing from 5 vol. % to 9 vol . %, the sizes of 
recrystallized grains distinctly decrease, meanwhile accompanying with the increase 
in deformation structure and substructured grains (red and yellow regions). 
Recrystallization fractions of 5-9 vol. % TiC x/2009Al composites are approximately 
determined to 81%, 69% and 44%, respectively. In addition, with the increa se in 
nano-sized TiCx content, a marked decrease in HAGBs and a corresponding increase 
in LAGBs are observed in Fig. 2(b-d). As shown in Fig. 3(b -d), the fLAGB of samples

56%, 79% and 95%, respectively. The average misorientation angles of these 
composites are 18.8°, 14.9° and 7.9°, respectively. It is well confirmed that nano-sized 
particles can affect the recrystallization microstructure of polycrystalline materials 
through Zener pinning [15-17]. In alloys that the ratio for volume fraction of particle 
to its diameter (Fv/d) is larger than 0.1 µm -1, recrystallization is usually retarded [17]. 
Therefore, nano-sized TiCx particles can retard the motion of LAGBs and HAGBs in 
this study, and thus leading to retard of the recrystallization nucleation and subsequent 
growth. The Zener pinning effect of the grain boundaries increases w ith increasing 
nano-sized TiC x particles content, and thus leads to the decrease in the fraction of 
recrystallized structure.

the α -Al matrix. At 493 K, θ´ precipitates in the sample for 9 vol.% nano -sized 
TiCx/2009Al composites have larger density and finer size (~150 nm) compared with 
those of the sample for 2009Al alloy (~300 nm), as shown in Fig s. 4 (a) and 4(b). 
Meanwhile, an inhomogeneous distribution of S′ precipitates can also be seen in both 
2009Al alloy and composites, and its sizes seem to be smaller in 9 vol. % nano -sized 
TiCx/2009Al composites than that in 2009 Al alloy. At 533 K, both θ´ and S′ 
precipitates distinctly coarsen (Figs. 4(c) and 4(d)). It can be observed in Figs. 4(e) 
and 4(f) that θ´ and S′ precipitates coarsening become more apparent when increasing 
the temperature to 573 K. The mean length of θ´ precipitates in 2009Al alloy and 
composites are about 450 nm and 300 nm, respectively. It was reported that increasing 
content of the precipitates and reducing their average size will increase the tensile 
strength and fracture strain [18,19]. Nano-sized Ti Cx particles could retard 
recrystallization and hinder the growth of the recrystallized grains , and meanwhile the 
diffusion distance of Cu and Mg atoms also becomes shorter in α-Al grains during the 
solution process [20]. Therefore, the nucleation sites for the precipitate s increase, and 
then the larger density as well as smaller size of θ´ and S′ precipitates formed in the 
composites during aging process . A l arge number of smaller -sized θ´ and S′ 
precipitated in composites can restrict and impede the dislocation movement, which is 
favorable for simultaneous improvement in strength and fracture strain.

The above analyses imply that the simultaneously improved high temperature 
tensile strength and fracture strain of composites have to be considered: (i) nano-sized 
TiCx strengthening, (ii) partially recrystallized microstructure induced by nano -sized 
TiCx and (iii) θ′ and S′ precipitation strengthening. Firstly, from the viewpoint of

Orowan dislocation bypassing mech anism, at high temperature, nano -sized TiC x 
particles inside the grain can pin down and accumulate dislocations within the grains. 
This dislocation accumulation inside the grains can give rise to the improvement of 
work hardening and fracture strain marked ly, which have been proved by Liu et 
al.[21]. Secondly, nano-sized TiCx particles can pin the sub -boundaries, stabilize the 
sub-structure and subsequently retard recrystallization as well as the growth of the 
recrystallized grains. Partially recrystallized materials can increase the tensile strength 
and simultaneously improve the fracture strain in some extent [2 2]. That is because 
the formation of partially recrystallized microstructure consists of small equiaxed 
grains and large recrystallized grains. Lar ge grains mainly store dislocations to 
improve fracture strain, while small equiaxed grains increase the strength. In addition, 
with increasing nano -sized TiC x content, the size of recrystallized grains decreases 
while the number of small equiaxed grain increases, as shown in Fig. 2. Therefore, the 
composite exhibits the highest fracture strain when volume fraction of nano -sized 
TiCx is 5%, while 9 vol.% nano -sized TiCx/2009Al composite possesses the highest 
strength with sacrificing a part of fracture stra in. Thirdly, precipitation strengthening 
plays a significant role in improving tensile properties for aged 2009Al alloys, and the 
precipitates' size and number are of great significance . Compared with 2009Al alloy, 
the numbers of θ´ and S′ precipitates in composites increase and their sizes become 
smaller. But with increasing temperature, the size of θ′ and S′ precipitates will grow 
up and their numbers decrease in both 2009Al alloy and its composites. During high 
temperature deformation, the precipitate strengthening can only be workable when the

precipitate phase is good in high temperature stability and does not dissolve and 
coarsen. Thus effect of θ´ and S′ precipitates on elevated temperature mechanical 
behavior of nano -sized T iCx/2009Al composites will weaken to some extent . The 
simultaneously improved tensile strength and fracture strain of nano -sized 
TiCx/2009Al composites is mainly attributed to the interactions between nano-sized 
TiCx strengthening, partially recrystallizat ion as well as the θ′ and S′ precipitation 
strengthening. 
 
4. Conclusions 
Microstructure and elevated temperature tensile properties of nano -sized 
TiCx/2009Al composites prepared by combustion synthesis me thod assisted with hot 
pressing followed by hot extrusion were studied. Compared with PM 2009Al alloy at 
493 K, 533 K and 573 K, the tensile strength of 2009Al matrix composites reinforced 
with 5 vol.%, 7 vol.% and 9 vol.% nano-sized TiCx particles increased and meanwhile 
accompanying with a markedly increase in fracture str ain. The fracture strains of 5 
vol. % nano -sized TiCx/2009Al composite increased by 28.4%, 74.5%, 196%, while 
the tensile strengths for composites contain ing 9 vol. % nano -sized TiC x particles 
were 38.6%, 18.4%, 26.6% higher than those of 2009Al alloy at temperature of 493 K, 
533 K , 573 K , respectively. Nano-sized TiC x particles c ould retard recrystallization 
and the growth of the recrystallized grains. Nano-sized TiC x particles and refined 
recrystallized grains increased the elevated temperature tensile st rength, and 
dislocations accumulation inside the large recrystallized grains improved the fracture
