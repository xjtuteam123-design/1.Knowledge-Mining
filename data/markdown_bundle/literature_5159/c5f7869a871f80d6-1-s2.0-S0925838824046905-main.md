1. Introduction
Low-density refractory alloys are increasingly sought after for high- 
temperature structural applications [1] . Inspired from the concept of 
high entropy alloys (HEAs) and primarily focusing on high-temperature 
utility, refractory high entropy alloys (RHEAs) were first introduced in 
2010 [2] . Subsequent studies have discovered a number of RHEAs sur -
passing nickel-based superalloys in mechanical properties [3,4] . They 
are classified as the first-generation RHEAs, such as VNbTaMoW and 
TiZrHfNbTa. However, these alloys possess relatively high densities. 
Therefore, the later developments have focused on producing 
low-density RHEAs that exhibit high specific yield strengths and 
ductility [5 – 8] . As a result, the first few attempts show only single-phase 
BCC RHEAs exhibiting good combination of specific strength and 
ductility at room temperature [9 – 12] .
While some stable single phase solid solutions have been produced 
by the HEA approach [10,13 – 15] . The recent research have reported the 
presence of intermetallic phases in HEAs [16 – 18] , particularly con -
taining low-density elements, such as Al and/or Cr. Among them, or -
dered B2 and laves are most commonly reported [19] . The alloys with Cr 
addition contained considerable amount of laves phases, provoked from 
Cr small atomic radius (r
Cr 
= 128 pm) [20,21] . Similarly, Al tends to form 
ordered B2 structure with Zr element. This benefits in producing low 
density alloy with enhanced room temperature hardness and yield 
strength, respectively [19] . Despite of that, the low-density RHEAs 
generally show poor ductility at room temperature, which largely limit 
the alloy processing ability for practical applications [22] .

discovery of refractory superalloys (RSAs), featuring microstructure 
characteristics identical to Ni-based superalloys [23 – 26] . The detailed

1. This study successfully introduces low-density RHEIA, characterized 
by a unique single-phase quinary B2 structure with interatomic 
spacing resembling a BCC lattice, as evidenced by SAED-TEM, FFT-
