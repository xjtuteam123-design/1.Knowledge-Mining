ABSTRACT
The simultaneous high strength and high plasticity of metastable- β titanium alloy sheets remains a dif ﬁcult
challenge. In this work, a 1.2 mm thick sheet of Ti –15Mo–3Al-2.7Nb-0.25Si titanium alloy was obtained after
rolling and annealing, and then subjected to a duplex-ageing treatment. The pre-ageing temperatures fell in the
range of 300 –390 /C14 C for 0.5 –2h. The re-ageing temperatures were in the range of 500 –600 /C14 C. After character-
ization, it was found that the alloy precipitates ω-phases during the pre-ageing process, and the distribution of
such ω-phases evolves unevenly and increases in number with the increase of the pre-ageing temperature.
Therefore, the strengthening mechanism of the alloy sheet is mainly α-phase nucleation assisted by ω-phase.
Moreover, grain re ﬁnement appears as a mechanism to ensure the plasticity of the alloy. Finally the ω-phase
evolution law is discussed in detail and the strengthening mechanism of the alloy sheet is also analyzed. At this
work, a metastable- β titanium alloy sheet with strength of 1445.75 MPa and elongation of 6.55 % was obtained.
This study provides a new idea for the microstructure design and process improvement of the metastable- β ti-
tanium alloy sheet.
1. Introduction
Metastable-β titanium alloys are widely used in aerospace, military
equipment and other ﬁelds because of their excellent comprehensive
properties such as high speci ﬁc strength and good molding performance
[1–4]. With the progress of the aviation industry, the use of titanium
alloy increases year by year, and gradually to the direction of structural
lightweight, parts integration development, the comprehensive me-
chanical properties of stabilized β titanium alloy put forward higher re-
quirements [5,6]. Tensile property is one of the important indexes of the
mechanical properties of materials, which can re ﬂect the alloy's load
carrying capacity in the limit state and resistance to deformation and
other parameters, and about the strengthening of titanium alloys are
mainly ﬁne-grained strengthening and second-phase strengthening
[7–10]. The yield stress of the grain boundary is higher than that of the
matrix, and in the process of plastic deformation, the grain boundary
forms an effective barrier to dislocations, and the smaller the grain size is,
the higher the rheological stress of the alloy is, and the more the dislo-
cations are impeded in the movement of the grain boundary, and the
strength of the material increases [ 11–15].When the crystal grain size of
titanium alloy sheet reaches the ultra ﬁne grain range (100 –1000 nm),
the high density of grain boundaries, which will produce the result of a
variety of strengthening mechanisms jointly involved in a higher
strengthening of the material, then a further increase in the strength of
the alloy can be achieved without a signi ﬁcant reduction in plasticity.
This ﬁne grain size is capable of achieving higher strengths during ageing
while keeping the plasticity in the desired range [ 16–18]. As for
second-phase strengthening, the most obvious way of strengthening ti-
tanium alloys is the pre-ageing ω-phase-assisted α-phase nucleation
mechanism [ 19,20]. ω-phase stability in Ti –Mo alloys decreases with

phase transformation of Ti –15Mo alloys, which affects the precipitation
of α-phase [35].
Although the ω-phase assisted α-phase nucleation mechanism can
obviously improve the strength of titanium alloy, within a certain range,
this strengthening method will make the overall plasticity of the material
decrease too obviously, which is not conducive to the improvement of the
comprehensive mechanical properties of titanium alloy [ 28,29]. There-
fore, this work focuses on the two strengthening methods of grain
reﬁnement and ω-phase-assisted α-phase nucleation mechanism to study
the key to the pre ageing process on the strength enhancement of β ti-
tanium alloy, and analyze the process of ω-phase transformation to
improve the comprehensive mechanical properties of β titanium alloy as
a whole.
2. Experiments
The experimental material selected for this study is TB8 metastable- β
titanium alloy with a nominal composition of Ti –15Mo–3Al-2.7Nb-
0.25Si, and its actual composition is shown in Table 1 . The difference
between its speci ﬁc and nominal compositions is small, and the content
of impurity elements is within the experimentally acceptable range. The
β-transus point was measured to be 810 /C6 5 /C14 C by metallographic
method.
In order to obtain an ultra ﬁne grain microstructure, TB8 alloy is ﬁrst
solidiﬁed in the αþβ biphasic region and cold rolled with a large amount
of deformation. Subsequently, the cold-rolled TB8 sheet was annealed at
710–740 /C14 C to obtain the ultra ﬁne grain microstructure by introducing a
competing mechanism of dual-phase recrystallization. [ 36], the as-cast
TB8 titanium alloy was rolled after an annealing heat treatment at 740
/C14 C/0.5h, and the deformation of rolling was 60 %, which resulted in a
1.2 mm thick cold rolled sheet of TB8 titanium alloy.
The heat treatment was carried out using a chamber resistance
furnace, and the heat treatment process was designed to study the effect
of ascending double-stage ageing on the microstructure and properties of
cold rolled titanium alloy sheet. According to the measured phase tran-
sition points, the following heat treatment process was adopted for the
rolled alloy, Fig. 1 shows The schematic diagram of processing routes, the
speciﬁc program is as follows:
The alloys were annealed in the ( αþβ) phase region at 750 /C14 C for 0.5h
and cooled by air cooling. The phase composition and β grain size of the
alloys annealed in the two-phase region were observed and the tensile
properties were tested. The annealed alloy was subjected to low-
temperature pre-ageing treatment at four different temperatures,
namely, 300 /C14 C, 330 /C14 C, 360 /C14 C and 390 /C14 C, with holding times of 0.5h,
1h and 2h, respectively. Changes in the microstructure of the alloy
brought about by the pre-ageing treatment were observed by scanning
electron microscopy, and the morphology of the isothermal ω-phase
distribution was observed by transmission electron microscopy. The low-
temperature ageing alloy was subjected to high-temperature re-ageing at
three temperatures of 500 /C14 C, 550 /C14 C and 600 /C14 C, respectively. The
auxiliary nucleation behavior and microstructure evolution of isothermal
ω-phase secondary α-phase under the ascending two-stage ageing process
are analyzed and studied, and the tensile properties of the alloy are tested
to analyze the strengthening mechanism of secondary α-phase in the
alloy.
The heat-treated specimens were polished on sandpaper and then
electrolytically polished in a 253K electrolyte solution of 5 % perchloric
acid, 35 % n-butanol, and 60 % methanol for a polishing time of 60 –80s
and a polishing current of about 1.8 –2.0A. The microstructures speci-
mens were characterized by SEM using Kroll's reagent (3 % HF, 7 %
HNO3 and 90 % H 2O). The TEM specimens were stamped into 3 μm
diameter discs and ﬁnally subjected to double-jet electrochemical pol-
ishing in an electrolyte solution of 5 % perchloric acid, 35 % n-butanol,
and 60 % methanol at 228 K. The specimens were then subjected to a
double-jet electrochemical polishing process. Specimens for EBSD were
electropolished with 10 % perchloric acid, 30 % n-butanol and 90 %
methanol solution after mechanical polishing. The grain size was
measured with Channel 5. The tensile property test for this work was
chosen to be carried out on INSTRON 5982 and the tensile specimen
dimensions are shown in Fig. 2.

5. Conclusions
In this work, the annealed TB8 titanium alloy was pre-aged in the
temperature interval of 300 –390 /C14 C and re-ageing at 500 –600 /C14 C,
respectively, to ﬁnally obtain TB8 sheet with higher strength and better
plasticity. And the ω-phase evolution process during the pre-ageing
process was also discussed, and ﬁnally the strengthening mechanism
was analyzed, and the conclusions obtained are as follows.
1. The number of ω-phase increases gradually with the increase of pre-
ageing temperature, and its size also increases gradually. This phe-
nomenon will aggravate the problem of ω-phase bias aggregation, in
addition, the higher pre-ageing temperature will also make αs pre-
cipitate part of the two together will weaken the ω-phase to assist the
secondary αs-phase nucleation, so the higher pre-ageing temperature
is not conducive to the enhancement of the alloy's strength.
2. With the prolongation of the ageing time, the ω phase gradually
evolves from the lattice distortion of the β phase, and TEM analysis
shows that the ω phase precipitates a part of the precursor called the
embryonic ω phase before the complete formation of the ω phase, and
ﬁnally the {111} plane in the β phase completely collapses to form the
complete ω phase. The ﬁnal ω phase provides heterogeneous nucle-
ation sites for the precipitation of the αs phase.
3. With the ω-phase-assisted αS precipitation mechanism as the strength
control unit of the alloy, and the ultra ﬁne crystalline β grains with an
average grain diameter of 1 μm formed by annealing as the plasticity
control unit of the alloy, the strength of TB8 titanium alloy sheet with
a thickness of 1.2 μm can reach 1445.75 MPa with an elongation of
6.55 % under the synergistic effect of the two.
