1. Introduction
Mechanicalalloying(MA)isaprocessformaterial
synthesis [1], which is performed in high energy ball
mills and produces as end product solid solutions,
microcrystalline [2] or amorphous materials[3].I n
recent years the MA process has been applied to
prepare nanocrystallinepowderintheTi–Al system.
The ﬁne, homogeneous microstructures produced by
MA oﬀer great potential in the development of TiAl
alloys with enhanced machinability and greatly
improved mechanical properties[4–6]. However, low
outputofMApowderpreparedinthesmalllaboratory
mills limited the practical applications to powder
metallurgical(PM)industry.Itisthereforenecessaryto
develop novel process for increasing the production
yield.Inauthors’lab,ithasbeenfoundthattheintro-
ductionofnitrogenintomillingatmosphere(nitrogen-
shocktechnique)improveddrasticallyproductionyield
of nano grain TiAl powder[7–10]. The maximum
recoveryrateofMApowderwasupto100%andthe
yield can be maintained on kilogram scale in every
processcycle.
Pulse discharge sintering (PDS), also called spark
plasmasintering(SPS)somewhere,isanewlydeveloped
process, a synthesis and processing technique, which
enables sintering and sinter-bonding at low tempera-
turesbychargingpowderparticleswithelectricalenergy
andeﬀectivelyapplyinglocallyhightemperatureatthe
interfaces. It is regarded asa rapid sintering process,
using the self-heating action from inside the powder,
similartoself-propagationhightemperaturesynthesis
(SHS) and microwave sintering. PDS oﬀers many
advantagesoverconventionalsystemsusinghotpress
(HP)sintering,hotisostaticpressing(HIP).Thenano
crystallineTiAlpowdersobtainedwiththeaforemen-
tioned MA process were consolidated by pulse dis-
chargesintering(PDS)processandthemicrostructures
of sub-micron grains formed in the as-sintered alloys
with high strength and excellent superplastic form-
ability.TheTiAlenginecomponentssuchaspistonpins
andexhaustvalveswerefabricatedsuccessfullybyone-
step sintering or sintering followed by superplastic
extrusionprocess [11,12].
