Abstract 
High temperature deformation behaviour of nanocrystalline (NC) Ni-Nb alloy having Nb segregated at grain boundary with 
various percentages (4, 8 and 10 at. % Nb) are studied using mo lecular dynamics (MD) simulations. All the simulated tensile 
tests for NC Ni-Nb alloy are carried out at 800 K temperature with 10 8 s-1 strain rate. NC Ni-Nb alloy specimens having 14.04 
nm x 14.04 nm x 14.04 nm simulation box dime nsion contains 16 numbers of grains and 254,348 atoms. Average grain size of 
NC Ni-Nb alloy specimen is taken as 6 nm. Centro-symmetry pa rameter, dislocation density, number of vacancy and Radial 
distribution function have been carried out to study structural changes under tensile loading condition. Strength of NC Ni-Nb 
specimens are found to be significantly enhan ced with increasing percentage of Nb at oms. Flow stress of NC Ni-Nb specimens 
are found to be increased with increasing percentage of Nb atoms.

2. Simulation details 
Three dimensional nanocrystalline Ni has been generated by the Voronoi construction [12] using Atomeye 
software [13]. NC Ni-Nb alloy having Nb segregated at grain boundary with various percentages (4, 8 and 10 at. % 
Nb) alloy are shown in Fig. 1. NC Ni-Nb alloy specimens having 14.04 nm x14.04 nm x 14.04 nm simulation box 
dimension contains 16 numbers of grains and 254,348 atoms. Average grain size of NC Ni-Nb alloy specimen is 
taken as 6 nm. Energy minimization and equilibration of specimen are performed using conjugate gradient method 
at 800 K temperature. All the simulated tensile tests for NC Ni-Nb alloy are carried out at 800 K temp erature with 
108 s-1 strain rate. 2 fs time step is taken. Periodic boundary conditions are applied in three directions during tensile 
test. LAMMPS package [14] is used for MD simulations with embedded atom method (EAM) potentials, which is 
developed by Zhang et al. [15] for Ni-Nb system. OVITO software [16] has been used for visualization and analysis 
of the high temperature deformation which is included th e dislocation analysis (DXA), Centro-symmetry parameter 
(CSP) analysis, Wigner-Seitz defect analysis, radial distribution function (RDF). 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
3. Results and Discussion 
Simulated stress-strain behavior of NC Ni-Nb alloy having Nb solute segregated at GB is represented in Fig. 2. It 
is observed that stress-strain curves are increased up to the ultimate tensile stress and then gradually decreases. It is 
also observed that stress-strain curves are shifted toward s upward with increasing Nb atoms at grain boundaries of 
specimen. It is because of segregating solute at GBs are effectively pinned the grain boundaries to suppress the grain 
boundary sliding motion [9].

4. Conclusion 
MD simulations of high temperature deformation for NC Ni-Nb alloy have been performed in this study to examine 
the strength with different at. % Nb atoms at GBs at hi gh (800 K) temperature. The following conclusions can be 
drawn: 
 Ultimate tensile strength (UTS) is increased monotonically with increasing of Nb atoms at grain boundaries 
for nanocrystalline Ni-Nb alloy specimen. 
 Flow stress at 20 % strain is increased with increasin g of Nb atoms at grain boundaries for nanocrystalline 
Ni-Nb alloy specimen. 
 Intergranular crack nucleation strain is increased w ith increasing of Nb atom s at grain boundaries for 
nanocrystalline Ni-Nb alloy specimen under tensile loading condition.
