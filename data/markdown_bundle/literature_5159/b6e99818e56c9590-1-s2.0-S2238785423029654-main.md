1. Introduction 
High strength β Ti – Fe-based ultrafine eutectic composites are 
promising in structural and functional applications because they exhibit 
high fracture strength (~2000 – 2600 MPa) combined with large plas -
ticity ( > 4 %) [ 1 – 5 ]. They consists of micrometric β -Ti dendrites 
dispersed in a nanostructured eutectic matrix formed by a hard Ti – Fe 
intermetallic phase and a softy β -Ti solid solution [ 1 , 3 , 6 , 7 ]. The high 
strength results from the solid solution hardening of the supersaturated 
β -Ti structured under body centered cubic phase (space group Im-3m) 
and from the strength and hardness of the Ti – Fe intermetallic also 
structured under body centered cubic phase (space group Pm-3m) [ 8 ]. 
Another phase which may be formed in β -Ti-Fe-based ultrafine 
eutectic composites is the hexagonal omega ( ω ) phase, also knows as 
Laves phase (space group P 63 mmc) This phase can be formed by either 
quenching from high temperatures (athermal ω ) or heat treatment at 
intermediate temperatures (isothermal ω ) [ 9 ]. It has been extensively 
studied because of its deleterious effects on mechanical properties, with 
a severe loss of ductility and an increase in hardness [ 9 ]. Additions of Sn 
improve ductility but reduces mechanical strength by the release of local 
strains, which minimizes lattice mismatch between β -Ti and Ti – Fe [ 8 , 
10 ], and decrease the formation of ω -like short range-order in the β -Ti 
phase. 
A possible application for the β -Ti-Fe-Nb-Sn ultrafine eutectic com -
posites is in medicine. Metallic alloys used as implants have much higher 
elastic modulus ( E ) than the human bone ( E = 10 – 30 GPa) such as 
stainless steel (200 GPa), Co – Cr Alloys (220 GPa), Ti – 6Al – 4V (110 GPa) 
[ 11 ]. This large difference in elastic modulus causes a local in -
compatibility between implant and bone known as stress shielding, 
which may result in bone resorption and the eventual implant failure 
[ 12 ]. The search for new alloys with low elastic modulus, high strength 
and biocompatibility is needed in order to solve this problem and full fill 
the requirements for biomedical applications [ 13 ]. Besides, the absence 
of toxic elements, high strength and the relatively low elastic modulus of
