Yoshio OHTA, Yukiya G. NAKAGAWA and Shinichi OHAMA
Synopsis : 
A precision casting of high pressure turbine blades and vanes consisting entirely of an oriented single 
grain of nickel-based superalloys has been introduced in most advanced aircraft engines due to their 
superior high temperature strength. This paper presents a description of the casting process for single 
crystal components and the evaluation of high temperature properties for a newly developed alloy TMS26 
in comparison with the existing alloys. Major outcomes are summurized as follows ; 
(1) Selecting the most adequate casting procedure and parameters, high quality single crystal blades in 
which their longitudinal orientation divergence from ƒ 100 „was less than 10 degree were consistently 
procedured from TMS26 and CMSX-2 (an existing alloy) with a yield in excess of 90%. 
(2)Mechanical properties ( tensile, creep and low cycle fatigue strength) and several physical properties 
of TMS26 were found to be generally superior to those of existing commercial alloys. 
Key words : superalloy ; directional solidification single crystal ; creep ; fatigue ; casting ; physical properties.
