This paper dealt with the effect of different induc tion melting technologies on mechanical properties 
of Nb-16Si-22Ti-2Al-2Hf-17Cr alloy. The cast ingots were fabricated first by arc-melting, and then 
remolten in the vacuum induction furnace. The resul ts showed that the ingot with refining process of 
1800℃ /15min and 0.1 at% C addition had finer microstruct ure and higher room-temperature fracture 
toughness. In addition, the compressive strength of the ingot with refining technology of 
1700℃ /10min was 315MPa at 1250 ℃ . However, the arc melting ingot had the lowest fra cture 
toughness and high-temperature compressive strength. 
Keywords: Nb-Si based alloys; vacuum induction melting; mechanical property. 
 
1. Introduction 
 
The Nb-silicide-based composites showed greater pro mise over nickel-based superalloys, 
based on a good balance of high-temperature strengt h, low-temperature damage tolerance 
and lower density( 
～ 7g/cm 3, as compared with 9.2g/cm 3 for Ni-based superalloys). 1-5 
Recent Nb-Si systems are alloyed with Ti, Al, Hf, a nd Cr in order to get an excellent 
balance of room-temperature toughness, high-tempera ture strength and oxidation 
resistance. A complete set of properties was descri bed for an early composite, MASC 
(metal and silicide composite, composition: Nb-25Ti -8Hf-2Cr-2Al-16Si).3 More 
compositions designating as Nb silicide A, B, 6,7 C, 6 and D 6 were developed. 
Nb-silicide-based composites are described with emp hasis on processing, 
microstructure, and performance. They were fabricat ed with a range of processes, 
including non-consumable tungsten electrode arc mel ting, plasma arc melting, induction 
skull melting (ISM), ingot casting plus thermomecha nical processing, 4,8,9,10 directional 
solidification (DS), 11 investment casting, 1 and power metallurgy processing. 11 However, 
the processing-microstructure-property relationships have not been sufficiently defined.

2. Experimental Procedures

prepared first by arc melting. Then two of them wer e remolten in the vacuum induction 
furnace using Y

only leaving one for comparison (Ingot 1). After me lting in the vacuum induction furnace, 
the ingots 2 and 3 were refined at 1700 ℃ for 10min, and 1800 ℃ for 15min respectively 
and finally cast into columnar graphite mold measur ing 60-mm diameter × 100-mm long. 
Meanwhile, 0.1 at% C had been added into the ingot 3 before the induction melting. The 
chemical compositions of the ingots were reported in Table 1.

dimensions of the specimens were 8 × 8 mm in cross section and 12 mm in height.

4. Conclusion 
Different melting technologies including arc-meltin g, vacuum induction melting with 
diverse refining processes had been used to fabrica te Nb-16Si-22Ti-2Al-2Hf-17Cr alloy. 
The following conclusions were obtained: 
(1) The arc-melting ingot had the coarsest microstr ucture while the microstructures of 
vacuum induction melting ingots were much finer.
