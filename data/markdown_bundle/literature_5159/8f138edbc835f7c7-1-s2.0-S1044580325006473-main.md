ABSTRACTS
Excellent temperature-bearing capacity over a wide temperature rasnge is critical for elevated-temperature 
application. In this study, via a unique nano-particle precipitation hardening strategy, a novel near-eutectic 
Ni-19Al-15Ti alloy multi-composited with L1
2 
and nano-sized η phase strengthened B2 intermetallics is devel -
oped, to improve the yield strength (YS) and ductility at room-medium temperature as well as to combine the 
superior microstructural stability and castability. The nano-sized η particles densely precipitated in the B2 phase 
can effectively strengthen the multi-fold composite structure, leading to the high hardness of 650 HV and super- 
high YS of about 1900 MPa at room-temperature. The samples also exhibit superior mechanic properties at high 
temperature, e.g., high YS of 790 MPa at 800 
◦
C and compressive strain larger than 50 %. The superior me -
chanical properties can be attributed to the solution and precipitation strengthening effects as well as the syn -
ergistic deformation of composite structure. These results provide a paradigm for developing alloys with an 
excellent synergy of mechanic properties, temperature-bearing capability and castability.
1. Introduction
Enhancing the temperature-bearing capability of metals is crucial for 
a variety of important applications, including aerospace, nuclear power, 
chemical processing, etc. [ 1 – 3 ]. The long-range ordered crystalline 
structures in intermetallic alloys (phases) possess the high stability even 
up to their melting point, thus exhibiting huge spaces for high- 
temperature (HT) applications. Nevertheless, the intrinsic room- 
temperature (RT) brittleness and inferior castability are still bottle- 
necks for the wide industrial applications [ 4 , 5 ]. Great efforts have 
therefore been devoted to improving the comprehensive performance. 
By using a dual-intermetallic composite concept schematically shown in 
inset Fig. 1 a1, a eutectic alloy (Ni-22Al-7Ti, at. %) consisting of lamellar 
B2 and L1
2 
phases was recently designed in our group, which can 
overcome these shortcomings effectively. The as-cast sample of this 
intermetallic eutectic alloy exhibits excellent fracture strength and 
plasticity at RT, ultra-high yield strength (YS) at HT, as well as good 
castability [ 6 ]. Even though such a dual-intermetallic composite struc -
ture exhibits the typical YS increase at a medium-high temperature, 
which is similar with the single-phase intermetallic alloys, it still ex -
hibits the insufficient YS at RT. New strategies are quite desired to 
enhance the temperature-bearing ability in a wide temperature range 
( Fig. 1 a).
As one of the most effective methods, precipitation strengthening has 
been widely applied in various alloys to improve the YS of alloys [ 7 – 9 ]. 
For instance, the YS of FCC alloy can be significantly enhanced by the 
precipitation of hard intermetallic phase, since the precipitates can 
suppress the dislocation motion effectively [ 10 ]. By introducing a high 
density of stable L2
1 
phase, Wang et al. [ 11 ] developed an ultra-strong 
eutectic high entropy alloy with the YS of 1527 MPa at RT, and 
ascribed the high performance to the low lattice misfit of the phase 
interface. In these alloys with a soft matrix and hard particles, excellent 
strength-plasticity synergy is obtained. But for intermetallic alloys with 
low plasticity, the precipitation strengthening effect should be quite

composition of different phases was analyzed by using the energy-

The hardness of the sample was measured by a Vickers hardness

5. Conclusions
In this study, a new near-eutectic composite Ni-19Al-15Ti alloy with 
B2-L1
2
- η phases was designed by CALPHAD, the strengthening mecha -
nism and microstructural stability were studied systematically. The 
following conclusions are drawn: 
1. The composite structure is composed of the primary B2 dendritic 
phase, coarse and fine composited B2/L1
2 
eutectic structure, as well 
as the nanosized η phase embedded within the B2 phase;
2. The designed alloy possesses attractive mechanical properties at RT, 
including the super-high hardness of 650 HV, σ
ys 
of 1900 MPa, σ
ucs 
of 
2900 MPa, and the decent ductility of 10 % in the as-cast state;
3. The superior RT mechanical properties are mainly attributed to the 
solution strengthening by Ti element, precipitation strengthening by 
η phase, and synergistic deformation of B2/L1
2 
eutectic structure;
4. Our designed near-eutectic alloy also exhibits outstanding HT σ
ys 
up 
to 800 MPa at 800 
◦
C, which is ascribed to the extreme stability of B2 
and L1
2 
eutectic structure.
