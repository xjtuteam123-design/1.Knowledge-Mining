Key words: Ni-based superalloys; coupled effects; rupture life; RBF Networks. 
Abstract. This paper studies the coupled effects of alloy el ements for precipitation strengthening to 
rupture life of single crystal Ni-based superalloys . Through setting up the Radial Basis Function 
model to predict rupture life, the generalization a bility test indicated that this model has better 
precision and practicability. According to this mod el, utilizing the theory of parameter sensitivity 
analysis to quantitative analysis the interactive e ffects of different alloy elements and/or groups to 
rupture life, providing the theory basis and refere nce for the alloy design and productivity of single 
crystal Ni-based superalloys. 
Introduction 
Single crystal Ni-based superalloys has become the preferred metal material of the international 
advanced aerospace engines and all kinds of turbine blades of industrial gas turbine engines because 
of their excellent high temperature properties. Wit h the continuous improving requirements of the 
alloy properties, many alloy elements in IV, V and VI B clans of the periodic table of elements are 
added to the matrix to strengthen the alloy performance. Apart from the reinforcement to the substrate 
and/or separation phase of superalloys, these alloy elements still exist the strong interactions between 
each other, thus emerging the coupled effects to th e mechanical properties of the superalloys. 
Because of the complexity of the elements of superalloys and the interactions among alloy elements, 
as well as the function of poly-crystal boundaries, science of material can only give the corresponding 
qualitative explanation according to the precipitat ion strengthening and solid solution strengthening 
of all kinds of elements in superalloys, but it is difficult and/or even impossible to calculate the 
specific contributions[1] that every alloy element does to solid solution strengthening and 
precipitation strengthening at present. Therefore, how to use the theory, technology and method of 
information science to explore the interactive coup led effects among the alloy elements, and 
quantitative analysis the reinforcements that they do to the mechanical properties of superalloys has 
become the key problem to alloy design and productivity. 
For all the mechanical properties of superalloys, w hen the product of study is the turbine blade, 
creep strength is the mainly parameter [2] that engineering and technical personnel concern with the 
temperature above 760 °C. Many scholars study and put forward the methods of calculating rupture 
life: Jaraga[3] put forward a similar experience formula to calculate the high temperature rupture life 
of single crystal alloy by considering alloy elements composition in , the atomic percentage of and 
the lattice mismatch degrees etc, three factors; In the basis of PHACOMP, according to the phase 
balance of high temperature alloy and neglecting th e interactions among alloy elements, Harada[4] 
used the method of multiple linear regression to predict the creep rupture life; others like Yoo[5], A. 
R. Ibanez[6], Zhonglou Gui[7], Weijun Li [8] used the Bayesian neural network and/or BP nerve that 
needs large sample data sets to predict the rupture life of superalloys. We use the Radial Basis 
Function Neural Networks(RBF NN) who has good stabi lity and the demand of the training data

RBF Neural Networks 
Broomhead and Lowe firstly applied Radial Basis Fun ction into neural network design and then 
constructed the Radial basis networks. RBF Neural N etworks is a partial approaches Neural 
Networks and it can approximate any functions with arbitrary precision. Although radial basis 
networks can require more neurons than standard feed-forward back-propagation(BP)networks, often 
they can be designed in a fraction of the time it t akes to train standard feed-forward networks. They 
work best when many training vectors are available [10]. Therefore, RBF NN is better than BP Neural 
Network in approximation ability, classification ability and learning speed, etc. 
The structure of RBF Networks is similar to that of Multilayer feed-forward Networks, which is 
made up of two layers. Input layer nodes just transfer the input signals to hidden layer, and the hidden 
layer nodes are made up of RBF functions, and the o utput layer nodes are made up of simple linear 
functions. 
RBF NN model of Creep rupture life 
Structure the Neural Network database. The same as BP Neural Network, RBF NN model needs to 
form neural network database first. We choose the s econd generation single crystal Ni-based 
superalloysT21 T31and the original data of performance testing to make up the neural network 
database. Input data is made up of alloy elements c ompositions, processing parameters of heat 
treatment, the specimen preparation controlling parameters and testing conditions, which contains the 
contents of Ni(66-71wt.%), Cr(5-6wt.%), Co(4-5wt.%) , W(5.5-6.5wt.%), Al(5.8-6.8wt.%), 
Ti(0-1wt.%), Ta(6-7wt.%), Mo(1.5-2.5wt.%), Nb(0-1wt .%), Re(0-1wt.%), Hf(0-1wt.%), creep 
temperature(800-1010 °C), creep strain(235-750MPa), withdrawal rate(2-7mm /min), solid solution 
processing temperature:1298 °C ±5°C, heat preservation time of solid solution process ing:4h, the 
cooling way of solid solution treatment: AC, middle heat treatment temperature: 1100 °C±10 °C, heat 
preservation time of middle heat treatment:5h, the cooling way of middle heat treatment: AC, ageing 
treatment temperature: 870 °C±10 °C, heat preservation time of ageing treatment:16h,and the cooling 
way of ageing treatment: AC. Output data is creep r upture life corresponding with all kinds of input 
data. 
After digital processing for the variables above, n amely getting the input and output variables 
database of neural network model. The input variabl es of database contains Ni wt%, Cr wt%, Co 
wt%, W wt%, Al wt%, Ti wt%, Ta wt%, Mo wt%, Nb wt%, Re wt%, Hf wt%, creep temperature, 
creep strain, withdrawal rate, solid solution proce ssing temperature, heat preservation time of solid 
solution processing:4h, the cooling way of solid so lution treatment(AC says 1, furnace cooling says 
0.5,water cooling says 2), middle heat treatment te mperature, heat preservation time of middle heat 
treatment, the cooling way of middle heat treatment, ageing treatment temperature, heat preservation 
time of ageing treatment, and the cooling way of ag eing treatment; The output variables of database 
are the actual measured values of creep rupture lif e. Eliminate the outliers and the linear related 
variable of input and output variables database. Considering the test conditions and the heat treatment 
processing parameters are independent variables relative to the alloy elements compositions, and they 
are consistent with the component content, we narrow 1% of the dimensional adjustment for the input 
variables: creep temperature, creep strain, solid s olution processing temperature , middle heat 
treatment temperature, ageing treatment temperature etc. After a series of data processing, we get 173 
samples database of input and output variables.

Co(4-5wt.%), W(5.5-6.5wt.%), Al(5.8-6.8wt.%), Ti(0- 1wt.%), Ta(6-7wt.%), Mo(1.5-2.5wt.%), 
Nb(0-1wt.%), Re(0-1wt.%), Hf(0-1wt.%). Cr, Co and Mo are prior to join γ matrix to strengthen the 
solid solution γ and Al, Ti, Ta, Nb, Hf are prior to join the ordered phase 'γ to strengthen 'γ phase, but 
W is prior to join both γ matrix and 'γ phase to produce solid solution strengthening and precipitation 
strengthening. The range of W, Al, Ti, Ta, Nb and Hf in Ni-based superalloys T21-T31is from -10 to 
10 wt.%, the change of the sensitivity of rupture l ife model is shown in Fig. 4. As is shown in the 
figure, when Ni-based superalloys T21-T31 is nearby the current components, rupture life sensitivity 
of alloy elements W, Al, Ta is far bigger than that of alloy elements Ti, Nb, Hf. The sensitivity from 
big to small is: Ta,Al,W Ti, Hf, Nb. 
The coupled effects of various alloy elements to ru pture life. Many alloy elements could be 
dissolved in the orderly phase 'γ of superalloys. In the development and application of Ni-based 
superalloys, in order to improve the performance of superalloys, a lot of alloy elements are usually
