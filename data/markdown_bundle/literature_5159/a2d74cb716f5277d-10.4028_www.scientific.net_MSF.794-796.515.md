The effect of artificial aging on the structure and properties 
of the heat-resistant alloy V-1213 of Al-Cu-Mg-Ag system 
Oglodkova Y.S., Vakhromov R.O., Lukina E.A. and Ryabov D.K. 
FSUE «All-Russian scientific research institute of aviation materials», 
17, Radio Street, Moscow, 105005, Russia. org80@viam.ru 
 
Keywords: alloys of Al-Cu-Mg-Ag system, V-1213 alloy, artifi cial aging, phase composition, 
mechanical properties, high-temperature strength. 
 
Abstract. The influence of artificial aging on the phase com position and the properties of sheets 
made of theV-1213 alloy of Al-Cu-Mg-Ag system has b een investigated. It is shown that the phase 
composition, morphology, dispersion and density of phase precipitate change with the temperature 
increase of artificial aging. After aging at the hi ghest temperature in the range from 150 to 190 °C, 
the maximum density of S' and 
Ω' phases is observed. This density provides the best combination of 
mechanical properties when extended to room tempera ture and high temperature strength 
characteristics. 
Introduction 
Aluminum wrought alloys are the main structural mat erials in modern industry due to their low 
specific gravity, the successful combination of req uired performance characteristics and 
manufacturability. There is a continuous developmen t of alloy chemical composition, structure and 
semi-products manufacturing technology. 
Al-Cu-Mg alloys are widely used in modern products of glider aircraft: damage tolerant for 
fuselage, wing bottom and power set element coverin gs; heat-resistant for the areas exposed to 
prolonged heating at elevated operating temperature s [1-4]. Currently, the major alloy for fuselage 
and wing bottom covering is the russian alloy 1163, the most common of heat-resistant alloys is the 
AK4-1ch (AA2618) serial alloy. One way to improve t he resource and heat resistance 
characteristics is alloy doping of the Al-Cu-Mg sys tem with rare and rare-earth metals such as 
zirconium, scandium, and other elements which make significant changes in alloy structure and 
phase composition [5-6]. 
It is known that the addition of silver to the Al-C u-Mg alloys, providing the 
Ω'-phase formation 
during artificial aging, improves mechanical properties of semi-finished pro ducts and helps to 
increase its heat resistance, fracture toughness an d other characteristics [7-9]. Thus, VIAM 
developed such new alloys as V-1167 and V-1213 (sim ilar to the use of 1163 and AK4-1ch alloys) 
[10, 11]. 
Al-Cu-Mg alloys are age hardable. Thus, one may var y the phase composition and, 
consequently, the mechanical properties of semi-fin ished products changing during the artificial 
aging [12-14]. 
This article presents the results of studies on the eff ect of artificial aging on the structure and 
properties of the pilot 8 mm thick sheets made of V -1213 alloy with normal plating (A) produced 
within the conditions of the Kamensk-Uralskii metal lurgical plant. The aging was carried out in 
three modes within the temperature range of 150 to 190 °C, with curing time used was 16-24 hours. 
Mode (1) - the aging temperature is close to the lo west boundary of the abovementioned range, 
mode (2) - the aging temperature is close to the middle of the abovementioned range, mode (3) - the 
aging temperature is close to the highest boundary of the abovementioned range. 
The tensile tests were carried out at room temperature in accordance with the GOST 1497-84, on 
a universal servo hydraulic testing machine MTS (US A). The long-term strength tests were carried 
out according to GOST 10145-81, the crawling tests were performed according to GOST 3248-81 
on ZST-3/3 test machines (Germany). The transmissio n electron microscope JEM 200CX was used

Result and discussion 
It is known that the main strengthening phases in t he Al-Cu-Mg-Ag alloys are the θ'(Al 2Cu), 
S'(Al 2CuMg) and Ω' phases [15-18]. 
The phase composition of the B-1213 alloy aged acc ording to (2) and (3) tempers is 
characterized by the release of a platelike Ω'-phase and an acicular S'-phase (Fig. 1, 2).

Ω'-phase plates as uniform particles are not observe d, and the corresponding diffraction effects

presence of low-intensity streaks along the [111] d irection. Also thin streaks passing through the 
matrix reflexes are observed in the electron-diffra ction pattern along the [100] direction. Such 
streaks come from the shape effect, and they may co rrespond to the plate-shaped particles (in this 
case to θ'-phase), so as to structural defects (for example, to microtwins). A uniform allocation of

As the result of solid solution studies after vario us regimes of aging it is established that the 
maximum density of Ω' and S'-phases is common for the aging regime (3). The investigation of the 
sheet structure made of the V-1213 alloy after agin g according to the mode (1) showed S'-phase 
precipitation. 
The analysis of the phase composition change and th e sheet mechanical properties (Table 1) 
showed that the highest level of mechanical propert ies at room temperature is typical for the aging 
mode (1). This may be explained by the high volume fraction of θ'-phase disperse precipitates. 
However, the low intensity decay of a solid solutio n with Ω'-phase formation leads to heat 
resistance characteristics the decrease, compared with the mode of aging (2) and (3). 
In order to maintain a satisfactory plasticity, Ω'-phase precipitates within the grain boundaries 
should not form continuous chains and the size of i ndividual particles should not exceed 15-20 nm 
(mode (3)).

Conclusions 
1. The application of different tempers of artifici al aging for the V-1213 allows to change the 
complex of properties of semi-finished products due to the changes of the phase composition, 
morphology and dispersion of hardening phase precipitates . 
2. The best combination of mechanical properties an d creep strength are provided by aging at 
the temperature close to the upper limit of the int erval 150-190 °C, when a maximum density of S' 
and Ω' phase precipitates is observed.
