Introduction 
Inωrecentωyear,ωsomeωnewωTiωbasedωalloysωhaveωbeenωregisteredωinωASTMωstandardizationωafterωCPωTiω 
andωTiE6AlE4VωELIω(extremelyωlowωinterstitial)ωbeingωregistered.ωThisωactivityωforωdevelopmentωofωnewω 
TiωbasedωalloyωwouldωbeωcorrelatedωtoωtheωrequirementωofωnonEtoxicωelementsωandωlowωelasticωmodulus.ω 
Asωfarωasωbiomedicalωapplicationωisωconcerned,ωtheωsurfaceωmodificationωwouldωbeωdesirableωforωtheω 
suppressionωofωdissolutionωofωtoxicωelementsωsuchωasωVωandωAlωthroughωtheωformationωofωstableωoxideω 
layer.ωHowever,ωthisωisωnotωguaranteedωtoωbeωmechanicallyωandωchemicallyωstableωinωhumanωbodyωforω 
longωtimeωexposure.ωMoreover,ωTiωbasedωalloyωhavingωlowωelasticωmodulusωwouldωbeωhighlyωrequiredωinω 
orderωtoωavoidωtheωstressωshieldingωphenomenonωwhichωcanωalterωtheωnormalωstressωstimuliωforωboneω 
growth:ωinωaccordanceωwithωWolff’sωlaw,ωtheωreductionωofωboneωstressesωinωcorrelationωwithωnaturalω 
situationωcausesωboneωtoωadaptωitselfωbyωreducingωitsωmassωinωaωprocessωofωresorptionωaroundωtheωimplant.ω 
Thisω canω causeω microEmotionω ofω theω artificialω jointω inω responseω toω externalω loadsω andω canω furtherω 
damageωtheωinterfacingωboneωlayerωsubsequentωtoωpossibleωlooseningωofωtheωartificialωjoints.ωUnderωtheseω 
backgrounds,ωmanyωTiωbasedωbinaryωandωternaryωalloyωsystemsωhaveωbeenωstudiedωandωreported.ωWhile,ω 
inωmanyωcasesωitωisωinevitableωtoωpreventωtheωinterstitialωelementsωsuchωasωoxygenωorωnitrogenωwhichω 
likelyωtoωbeωcontaminatedωfromωtheωcastingωstageωtoωmakeωaωspecificallyωdesignedωparts,ωbilletωandωrod.ω 
Inωtheωpresentωstudy,ωweωchoseωTiENbωbinaryωalloyωsystemωbecauseωitωhasωexcellentωbiocompatibility,ω 
andωmanyωofωTiωbasedωalloysωforωbiomedicalωapplicationsωcontainωfairlyωaωlargeωamountωofωNb.ωWeωaimω 
atωunderstandingωtheωalloyingωbehaviorωofωoxygenωonωmicrostructuralωformation,ωcrystalωstructureωofω 
constituentsωandωmechanicalωproperties.ω

Conclusions 
Microstructure,ω elasticω modulusω andω mechanicalω propertiesω wereωinvestigatedω inω orderω toω 
understandωtheωeffectωofωoxygenωalloyingωinωTiENbωbasedωalloysωproducedωbyωarcωmeltingωandωthenω 
subsequentω waterω quenching.ω Theω martensiticω structureω withω orthorhombicω crystalω structureω wasω 
changedωtoωbccωstructuredωβωphaseωwithωincreasingωNb.ωOxygenωadditionωtoωtheωpresentωalloyωwasωfoundω 
toω haveω anω effectiveω roleω toω suppressω theω occurrenceω ofω ωω phaseω andω toω stabilizeω theω βω phaseω inω 
waterEquenchedωpresentωalloy.ωElasticωmodulusωvaluesωwereωsensitiveωonωoxygenωcontentωinωtheωpresentω 
TiENbωbasedωalloys.ωYieldωstrengthωincreasedωwithωincreasingωoxygenωcontentωbutωelongationωdecreasedω
