ABSTRACT
A carbide strengthened wrought Ni-based superalloy is developed. The alloy depends on carbide dispersion 
strengthening. A high deformation plasticity in homogeneous alloy is shown in the 1100 – 1200 
◦
C isothermal 
compression test and the improvement of microstructure can be achieved by recrystallization. After aging at 
850 – 900 
◦
C, the carbide strengthened wrought alloy appears excellent tensile strength. Carbide exists higher 
microstructure stability than γ ′ in the same condition. The herein reported results reflect the potential of the 
economical wrought Ni-based superalloy to service above 800 
◦
C.
1. Introduction
Wrought nickel-based superalloys are widely used in aerospace in -
dustry due to their excellent mechanical properties and stability in 
elevated temperature [ 1 ]. The outstanding properties are attributed to 
dispersive γ ′ -Ni
3
(Al,Ti) precipitations in γ matrix [ 2 ]. In wrought su -
peralloys, primary strengthen strategy is increasing alloying degree, 
especially γ ′ forming elements [ 3 ]. A series of high performance super -
alloys have been developed with γ ′ phase exceeding 50 wt%, such as 
GH4151, GH4175, GH4975, designed for 800 
◦
C [ 4 ]. The high mass 
fraction of γ ′ phase contributes effective precipitation strengthen but 
also produces serious segregation and narrow hot working window, 
resulting in deformation resistance during hot working process [ 5 ]. 
Furthermore, the γ ′ phase will deteriorate during long-term high tem -
perature service [ 6 ]. The deteriorate of γ ′ phase in superalloy with 
excessive mass fraction leads to rapid degradation of high temperature 
strength [ 7 ]. Therefore, it is necessary to explore novel approaches to 
enhance wrought Ni-based superalloys.

4. Conclusion
In presented work, the microstructures and properties of the carbide 
strengthened wrought Ni-based superalloy is shown. The alloy with 
composition of Ni(bal.)-20Cr – 6Mo – 6W-0.8C (wt.%) is designed to 
explore potential usage at higher temperature as well as combine high 
deformability. Without rare elements, alloy is economical. This alloy 
shows good hot deformation ability after homogenization. Carbide ex -
ists higher microstructure stability than γ ′ in the same condition and 
provides stable strengthening effects at elevated temperature. Alloy has 
good tensile properties at 800 
◦
C. The novel wrought superalloy has a 
possibility of prolonged service above 800 
◦
C.
