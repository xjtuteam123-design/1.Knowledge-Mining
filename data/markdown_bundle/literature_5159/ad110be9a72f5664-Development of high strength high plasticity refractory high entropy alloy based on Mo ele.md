ABSTRACT 
Refractory high entropy alloy is a potential substitute material for high temperature structures because of its 
excellent mechanical properties of high temperature resistance. In this paper, three kinds of refractory high- 
entropy alloys, M0, M10 and M20, were designed by adjusting the content of Mo element and formed by 
Laser powder bed melting(LPBF) technology. The solidification thermodynamics, microstructure and mechanical 
properties of the three kinds of alloys were analyzed. The effect of Mo content on the microstructure and me -
chanical properties of the three kinds of alloys was studied. With the increase of Mo element content, the grain 
size of the alloy becomes smaller and smaller, the yield strength increases greatly, and the plasticity of the alloy 
decreases continuously. The compressive yield strength of high strength M20 alloy is up to 1285 MPa, the ul -
timate strength is 2447 MPa, and the elongation is 27%. The high plasticity M0 alloy exhibits compressive su -
perplasticity. The tensile yield strength and ultimate strength of M10 alloy are 1184 MPa and 1403 MPa, and the 
elongation is 5.1%. In this paper, the matching problem between plasticity and strength of refractory high- 
entropy alloys at room temperature is solved by combining alloy composition optimization and LPBF forming 
technology, which lays a foundation for the industrial application of refractory high-entropy alloys. 
1. Introduction 
Traditional alloys usually have one or two metallic elements as the 
main elements, with a small amount of other metallic or non-metallic 
elements added to further improve the performance of the alloy. It is 
difficult for traditional alloys to exert the synergistic effect of various 
elements to show the diversity of alloy properties with the effect of 
“ cocktail effect ” . In 2004, Professor Ye Junwei, a scholar from Taiwan, 
China, broke away from the traditional research and development ideas 
of alloys and proposed a new design concept of “ multi-principal 
element ” alloys [ 1 ]. “ Multi-principal ” alloys are usually composed of 
four or more metallic elements dissolved in 5% to 35% atomic per -
centage, so they are also called high entropy alloys. High entropy alloys 
have the following excellent properties: ultra-high hardness [ 2 , 3 ], high 
strength and ductility [ 4 – 6 ], outstanding thermal stability [ 7 ], good 
wear resistance [ 8 ], corrosion resistance [ 9 ], and good oxidation resis -
tance [ 10 ]. Refractory High Entropy alloy (RHEAs) is a high entropy 
alloy composed of high melting point elements W, Ta, Nb, Mo, Hf and 
Re, which is characterized by high strength and high density [ 11 , 12 ]. 
Compared with conventional high-entropy alloys, the alloys can main -
tain high strength at high temperature and have good thermal stability. 
Therefore, the research of refractory high entropy alloys is of great 
significance to the development of aerospace field. 
In 2010, Senkov designed WNbMoTa and WNbMoTaV two refractory 
high-entropy alloys for the first time. The hardness of WNbMoTaV alloy 
is as high as 5250 MPa[ 13 ]. Refractory high-entropy alloys containing 
W and Mo elements usually have poor plasticity at room temperature. 
Typical refractory high-entropy alloy systems NbMoTaW and NbMo -
TaWV have a compression strength of 405 MPa at 1600

4. Discussion 
Three refractory high entropy alloys, M0, M10 and M20, were pre -
pared by laser powder bed melting. This section discusses the effect of 
Mo ratio on the strength and plasticity of Nb30Ta30Mox(Ti2Ni)40-x 
high entropy alloy. At the same time, the advantages of laser powder 
bed melting technology as a new material preparation method in the 
process of M10 refractory high entropy alloy are discussed. The excel -
lent mechanical properties of M10 are the result of composition opti -
mization and advanced preparation technology. 
4.1. Fine grain strengthening effect 
Fine grain strengthening is an effective method to improve the 
strength and plasticity of high entropy alloys. LPBF forming technology 
has the characteristics of fast cooling speed and large temperature 
gradient under the action of high-energy laser beam, and large under -
cooling degree can refine grain. In order to highlight the advantages of 
LPBF forming technology, Vacuum Levitation Melting (VLM) of M10 
alloy was conducted in this paper, and the LPBF forming and VLM 
forming M10 alloy were compared. The electron microscopy images of 
M10 alloy formed by LPBF and VLM at 500 times are shown in Fig. 9 (a 
and b). At the same magnification, the microstructure of SLM formed is 
very fine, while that of VLM formed is very coarse. The microstructure of 
LPBF-ed alloy is dendritic, while that of LPBF-ed alloy is cellular. The 
dendrites intertwine with each other and have stronger intergranular 
binding ability than the cellular ones. Fig. 9 (c, d) shows EBSD profiles of 
SLM and VLM formed M10 alloys, respectively. According to the data in
