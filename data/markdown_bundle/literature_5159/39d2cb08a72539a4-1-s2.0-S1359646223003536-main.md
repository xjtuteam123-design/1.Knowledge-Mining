ABSTRACT 
A novel Fe-rich medium entropy alloy (MEA) space with a γ + γ’ -only phase field at 800 
◦
C is proposed where the 
volume fraction and dissolution temperature of γ’ -phase can be tuned by substituting Fe for Ni/Co and Cr. The 
γ + γ’ -structured Fe-35.5Ni-4Cr-6.3Al-3.2Ti alloy (Fe-content of 51 at.%; γ’ -volume fraction of 24%) after a 
recrystallisation and ageing treatment displays a yield strength of 902 MPa, ultimate strength of 1370 MPa and a 
tensile ductility of 24%. The presented alloy space progresses towards the development of Fe-rich MEAs with 
good strength retention ( > 600 
◦
C) for heat-resistant structural applications. 
New combinations of properties can be created by mixing alloying 
elements in large proportions. High entropy alloys (HEAs) or composi -
tionally complex alloys (CCAs) are a case in point [ 1 , 2 ]. Single-phase 
FCC HEAs such as CoCrFeMnNi have an excellent combination of me -
chanical, wear, fracture and other functional properties over a range of 
temperatures [ 3 , 4 ]. However, these alloys are limited by low strength 
compared to conventional engineering alloys. Recent works on FCC 
high- or medium-entropy alloys (HEAs or MEAs) have shown excep -
tional strengthening and toughening effects with the aid of inducing a 
heterogeneous microstructure, by dislocation strengthening and the 
formation of nano-precipitates [5 – 9] . Precipitation strengthening im -
parts the FCC matrix with excellent strength and ductility, and alloys 
such as high entropy superalloys (HESAs) can have improved perfor -
mance relative to conventional alloys over a wide range of operating 
temperatures from cryogenic to high temperatures (such as 1000 
◦
C) 
[10 – 14] . Despite these favourable properties, the commercial applica -
tions of HEAs are limited by the high cost of the alloying elements 
(especially Co). 
Here we pursue the strategy of replacing high-cost with low-cost 
alloy additions, while maintaining the mechanical properties as a path 
to the commercial adoption of CCAs. The aim is to employ a CALPHAD 
approach (ThermoCalc TCHEA5 database) to create an Fe-rich FCC 
matrix that is strengthened by nanoscale, coherent L1
2
-(Ni, Co)

a γ + γ’ -only phase field at 800 
◦

In summary, ThermoCalc (TCHEA5 database) prediction can be an 
excellent tool to design γ’ -strengthened Fe-rich alloy compositions, with 
an excellent correlation to the experimental data from literature on Al- 
Co-Cr-Fe-Ni-Ti system for a wide concentration range of alloying ele -
ments (Fe-content from 0 to 30 at.%) [5 – 36] . The discovered Fe-rich 
alloy space in the Al-Co-Cr-Fe-Ni-Ti system has been validated in the 
upper Fe range (51 at.%) by the synthesis of a MEA of composition 
Fe-35.5Ni-4Cr-6.3Al-3.2Ti. As predicted, this alloy showed a γ + γ’ 
structure and exhibited excellent mechanical properties at room and 
elevated temperatures, at least equivalent to more materially expensive 
Fe-lean and Co-rich γ’ -strengthened alloy compositions from literature.
