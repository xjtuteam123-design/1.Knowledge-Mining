ABSTRACT 
Nb alloy has become a promising material in aerospace due to high melting point, low density and excellent 
processing properties. Nevertheless, the mismatched mechanical properties and thermal stability of single-phase 
BCC-Nb made the aircraft unable to resist cyclic vibration loads under the action of heat/force/flow. Here, after 
solid solution, quenching and two-stage aging, an FCC/BCC dual-phase Nb alloy with a discontinuous grain 
boundary (GB) carbide network skeleton was formed. The results show that interstitial C atoms precipitate from 
BCC-Nb matrix resulting in volume shrinkage and form discontinuous carbide at GB, which stimulates the phase 
transition of BCC expansion to FCC near GB. Wherein, discontinuous GB carbides help pin the GB and disloca -
tions, impede grain growth, and change the deformation from local to the entire network skeleton. Meanwhile, 
the gap provides a channel for the dislocation to pass through the GB. Additionally, the FCC provides the multi- 
slip system to transfer the dislocation. The results show that the yield strength, tensile strength, elongation, 
fracture toughness and elastic modulus of Nb alloy are increased by 79.99 %, 34.52 %, 164.22 %, 204.76 % and 
1.27 %, respectively. This unique microstructure is expected to guide the design of the next generation of high- 
performance Nb alloys. 
1. Introduction 
Nb alloys are widely used in aerospace, defense, automotive and 
other important fields due to their high melting point, low density and 
excellent processability [ 1 , 2 ]. However, due to limited dislocation slip 
resistance and slip system, single-phase BCC-Nb cannot achieve high 
matching of strength, plasticity and thermal stability [ 3 ] when subjected 
to cyclic vibration load under thermal/force/flow conditions [ 4 , 5 ]. To 
solve this problem, a variety of strengthening mechanisms (such as 
solid-solution [ 6 ], fine-grain [ 7 ] and precipitation strengthening [ 8 ]) 
were introduced into the high ductility matrix to obtain excellent 
comprehensive properties [ 9 ]. Specifically, solid solution strengthening 
is achieved by adding modifying elements such as W, Mo, Zr, Hf and C to 
the Nb matrix [ 10 ], causing lattice distortion, improving the atomic size 
mismatch [ 11 ], and precipitating the strengthened phase, etc. [ 12 ]. The 
above method can improve the mechanical properties of the alloy, but it 
is far from meeting the demand of high strength, plasticity and excellent 
thermal stability materials for high specific impulse aircraft. 
It has been shown that the discontinuous precipitates formed along 
the grain boundaries (GB) by designing the heat treatment process can 
refine the grains, induce phase transformations, and convert local 
deformation into overall deformation [ 13 ]. As a result, the strength, 
plasticity, and stability of the alloy are enhanced [ 14 ]. For example, the 
introduction of nanoprecipitates into dual-phasic AlCoCrFeNi alloy 
produces additional GB, leading to grain refinement, which both hinders 
and provides storage space for dislocations, resulting in an increase in 
strength and ductility [ 15 ]. The fine grain and nano precipitates in the 
FeNiCoAlTi alloy both contribute to higher local strains and trans -
formation from FCC to martensite. Through the coupled effects of pre -
cipitation strengthening and transformation induced plasticity (TRIP),

Meanwhile, during loading, the discontinuous GB network skeleton 
deformates simultaneously, rather than locally, thus improving the 
stability of the alloy [ 17 ]. For example, TiC nanoparticles in

Besides, studies on other alloys have found that compared to single- 
phase alloys, phase transitions through heat treatment can consume 
residual lattice distortion and prevent grain growth [ 19 ], and the ac -
quired dual-phase microstructure can enhance the mechanical property 
of the alloy [ 20 ]. For example, in AlCoCrFeTiNi alloy, the activation of 
dislocation slip and stacking faults in the FCC phase, particle strength -
ening in the BCC phase, and synergistic deformation between the FCC 
and BCC phases improve the strength and plasticity of the alloy [ 20 ]. 
The dual-phase AlMo alloy has excellent thermal stability and me -
chanical properties due to the diffusion and intertwining of the 
dual-phase structure [ 21 ]. Therefore, the formation of dual-phase 
microstructure by designing heat treatment is also an effective strat -
egy to improve the strength, ductility and thermal stability of the alloy. 
Here, based on phase diagrams, first principles, and Gibbs free en -
ergy calculations, a novel three step heat treatment process (i.e., solid 
solution, quenching, and two-stage aging) was designed, and a Nb alloy 
with high strength, plasticity, and thermal stability was successfully 
prepared. This is attributed to the unique microstructure, i.e., the 
discontinuous carbide GB network skeleton and BCC /FCC dual phase. 
This three-step heat treatment can easily achieve large-scale production 
in industrial practice and is suitable for various precipitation strength -
ened alloys.

2.2. Mechanical performance 
The tensile properties were tested by DNS100 electronic universal 
testing machine at room temperature. The thermal strain response of the 
samples during heating and cooling was evaluated using a ZRPY-1700 
thermocycling microstrain device. The hardness of different samples 
was measured by Vickers hardness tester 200HBVS-30, and the average

2.3. Analysis and characterization 
Electrolytic extraction was used to extract precipitates from different 
samples [ 22 ]. The phase composition of the precipitates was analyzed by 
X-ray diffractometer (D8 Advance, Switzerland). The microstructures 
and images of the samples were analyzed by SEM (FEI Sirion 200, 
America). The element distribution in the sample was analyzed by EPMA 
(JeolJXA-8230, Japan). An electron back scattering diffraction system 
(EBSD, OIM 6.2) was used to analyze the weave, grain structure, 
orientation and recrystallization fraction of the samples. The samples 
were cut and thinned using a two-beam electron microscopy system 
(FIB, Helios Nanolab G3 UC). A spherical aberration electron micro -
scopy (Titan G2 60-300) was employed for bright field and 
high-resolution TEM imaging. The cell parameters of the precipitates 
were measured by XRD and TEM.

The TCTI2 database in the Thermal-Calc thermodynamic calculation

composition of MCx in Nb alloys. The relationship between the cohesion

4.1. Formation of special microstructures

4.2. Discontinuous GB carbide network skeleton 
The optimization mechanism of the discontinuous GB carbide 
network skeleton is as follows. 1) The dislocations between grains are 
hindered, which improves the strength of the alloy. 2) The gap provides 
a channel for the dislocation to pass through GB, thereby avoiding 
dislocation blockage and improving plasticity [ 18 ]. 3) Unlike the local 
deformation mechanism (during the loading, part of the matrix is 
deformed first, and then other matrixs are deformed or fractured), the 
network structures offset each other ’ s local deformations and transform 
them into whole carbide skeleton deformations, i.e., the overall defor -
mation mechanism [ 42 ], thereby improving the stability of the alloys. 4) 
The network skeleton hinders dislocation slip/climbing and atomic 
diffusion, making it difficult for the alloy to undergo large deformations, 
thus improving alloy stability [ 43 ]. 5) Discontinuous carbides pinned 
GB, which inhibits grain growth and thus refines the grains [ 44 ]. On the 
one hand, the short distance from the dislocation source to the GB in the 
fine grains and the presence of small dislocation plugs result in minimal 
stress concentration, making it difficult to stimulate adjacent grains to 
produce plastic deformation, thus improving the yield strength of the 
alloy [ 45 ]. On the other hand, the internal and GB of fine grains produce 
a small strain difference under the imposed load, i.e., the grains deform 
uniformly and are not prone to cracking, which in turn improves the 
plasticity of the alloy [ 46 ]. 
4.3. Dual-phase structure 
In contrast to the conflict between strengthening and plasticizing in 
the single-phase structure, the BCC /FCC dual-phase structure achieves 
simultaneous strength and plasticity matching. 1) FCC provides multiple 
slip systems and layer faults that contribute to dislocation slip and 
plasticity improvement. 2) The nano BCC particles and the BCC /FCC 
phase interface hinder the dislocation and contribute to the strength 
enhancement [ 47 ]. 3) The higher group entropy, lower diffusion coef -
ficient, and smaller phase-interface mismatch of the BCC phase 
contribute to the excellent microstructural stability [ 48 ]. 4) Nano BCC
