Abstract 
A theory guide method based on thermodynamic calcul ation and experimental verification is 
performed on the composition optimization and heat treatment process of heat-resistant 
Al-Cu-Mn-RE(La/Ce) alloys. Al-5.0Cu-0.6Mn-1.5RE(La/ Ce) is obtained to be the reasonable 
alloy composition for further investigation. The st rength of heat treatment state alloy decreases 
20-33 MPa as the temperature rises every 50 oC in the high temperature tensile experiments. The 
alloy has good heat resistance, with high temperatu re (300 oC) strength of 102.4 MPa. The high 
temperature fracture mode is a mixed forms of britt le and ductile fracture. Heat exposure 
temperature has a significant effect on both thermal and electrical conductivity, by influencing the 
morphology and distribution of heat-resistant phase s. With the increase of heat exposure 
temperature, the tensile strength of the alloy firs tly increases and then decreases. With heat 
exposure at 200 oC or 350 oC for 100 h, the room temperature tensile strength values are 232.77 
MPa or 190.52 MPa, respectively, which indicates th at the alloy has good high-temperature 
durable performance. The nano-sized Al 2Cu phase, T phase and Al 3Zr phase exist in the heat 
treatment state alloy, and result in good heat resistance. Heat exposure at 300 oC for 100 h leads to 
the growth of Al 2Cu phase and close distribution of heat-resistant p hase Al 3Zr around the Al 2Cu 
phase. Presence of eutectic phase hinders the dislo cation movement and thus improves the 
resistance to deformation at high temperature.

1. Introduction 
Cast heat-resistant aluminum alloys have good oxida tion resistance at high temperature and 
can maintain high thermal conductivity, resistance to plastic deformation and failure under high 
temperature, and long-term static or dynamic load, therefore, they are widely used in aerospace, 
automobile, ship parts, electrical engineering and other aspects [1-3]. The commonly used cast 
heat-resistant aluminum alloys include Al-Si, Al-Cu and Al-RE (RE refers to rare earths) alloys. 
The main types of Al-Cu alloy are high-strength, heat-resistant and corrosion-resistant alloys. This 
paper study the heat-resistant Al-Cu alloy as it ha s higher strength than Al-Si alloy at 
elevated-temperature. 
Al-Cu alloys have some characteristics like relativ ely high eutectic temperature (548 ), 
larger binding force between atoms, low diffusion v elocity of Cu in α-Al under 350  and small 
solid solubility change, which are all beneficial t o microstructure stability of the alloys at high 
temperature, and results in high heat resistance. Heat-resistant aluminum alloys can be obtained by 
adding high rare earth content in Al-Cu alloys [4]. The complex and bulky RE compounds in 
alloys may segregate at the grain boundary, which h inders lattice sliding, thus improves the high 
temperature stability and makes the highest working temperature of 400  or so. For example, the 
Al-Cu-RE alloy ZL206 which has a complex solid solu tion-type multiphase structure, exhibits 
excellent high temperature mechanical properties an d room temperature strength. After solid 
solution treatment, nearly all the θ(Al 2Cu) phase is dissolved in the matrix, while T(Al 20 Cu 2Mn 3) 
phase is precipitated out in the form of dispersion particles. The complex and stable solid solution 
and heat resistant phases at grain boundary are the main reasons for the good thermal strength of 
the alloy [5, 6]. 
 In order to improve the heat resistance of alumin um alloy, it is necessary to enhance the 
heat resistance of the matrix and increase the cont ent of phases with high thermal stability in the 
matrix. The heat resistance of the matrix is basica lly dependent on the binding force between 
atoms. Addition of various elements makes the compo nents of the solid solution complex, 
increases the binding force between atoms, and slows down diffusion of atoms and decomposition

decreases and thus the strength reduces. The purpos e of heat treatment of aluminum alloy is to 
change the distribution of strengthening phase and grain boundary shape, and then improve the 
stability of microstructure. 
 Heat-resistant Al-Cu alloy is prone to crack beca use of its high content of Cu. Therefore, it

understanding of mechanism of heat resistance can be obtained, which can provide a reference for 
the development of new Al-based materials. Thermal stability is one of the important references 
for the composition design of heat-resistant alumin um alloys. At room-temperature, dislocation 
motion is dominated by slip. While at high temperat ure, dislocation motion mainly takes the form 
of slip, climb and grain boundary sliding. The instantaneous strength at high temperature obtained 
by tensile test after a certain period of time is a n important data reflecting the heat resistance of 
metallic materials at high temperature.

2. Experimental procedure 
The major alloying elements of the studied Al-Cu al loys include Al, Cu, Mn, Ti, Zr and 
RE(La/Ce), and impurity elements are Fe and Si. For the cast heat-resistant Al-Cu alloys, 
composition ranges for the alloying elements are Cu 3.0 %-8.0 %, Mn 0.3 %-1.5 %, RE 
0.05 %-5.0 %, Zr 0.1 %-0.25 % and Ti:0.15 %-0.35 %, respectively, according to the selection 
principle of main elements. The thermodynamic calcu lation software Thermo-Calc [12] was used 
to analyze the effects of alloying elements on the equilibrium phase diagrams of Al-Cu-based 
alloys, so as to clarify the concerned heat-resista nt phases. Property diagrams were obtained to 
show the content of each phase and dynamic change o f phases with temperature, which reflected 
the precipitation and transition temperature points of different phases in the alloy system, the 
content and the existing temperature range, etc. In formation like solidification path of the system 
and precipitation law of phases in the solidification process was obtained through non-equilibrium 
solidification simulation calculation. Temperature of liquid-solid phase transition was obtained to 
provide reference for formulation of solid solution treatment. 
Orthogonal test was adopted to design alloy composi tion, to avoid the complicated 
calculation and test due to the wide composition ra nge. Differences in structure and performance 
before and after optimization were observed to verify the accuracy and reliability of the calculated 
composition range and thermodynamic data. According to the design components, a preliminary 
exploratory test was conducted to compare the error s or differences between the two, to provide 
reliable data support for the following adjustment of design.In order to check the rationality and 
reliability of alloy composition, exploratory test is carried out by designing and preparing the 
alloys.

3. Results and discussion 
3.1. Alloy design and heat treatment based on thermodynamic calculation 
（前面删除部分）Information on a series of phase diagrams are analy zed to accurately 
design the composition of Al-Cu-Mn-RE (La/Ce)-Ti-Zr system, by using the Thermo-Calc 
software. The orthogonal factor level table is desi gned to guide the selection of Cu, Mn, RE and 
other major elements of the alloys. Then the compos ition design of the alloys is determined 
according to the experimental results. 
For example, a three-factor and three-level orthogo nal factor level table is developed, in 
which RE content is proportioned according to the a mount of rare earth heat-resistant aluminum 
alloys in practical application. The composition of 9 groups of tests is obtained after combination. 
XRD patterns and microstructure of the as-cast alloys with different components are prepared and 
analyzed. Some examples are illustrated in Fig. 1. XRD results show that in the Al-Cu-Mn-RE 
(La/Ce) alloys with different compositions, binary phases like α-Al and Al 2Cu, and ternary phases 
like Ti 2Al 20 Ce, Al 8Cu 4Ce or Al 3CuCe etc., and quaternary phase Al 24 Ce 3Cu 8Mn can be found. Due 
to space constraints, this paper only shows the ana lysis results of some samples. The 
microstructure of as-cast alloy is composed of α-Al, θ (Al 2Cu), primary T (Al 20 Cu 2Mn 3) phase or 
eutectic. α-Al phase is the matrix, θ phase presents mesh or point spherical distribution in the grain 
boundary or inside the crystal. The T phase or eutectic phase is clustered at the grain boundary and 
distributed in continuous, semi-continuous or short bar shape. Grain of the alloys of two 
components is fine and the grain boundary is obviou s. Among them, the as-cast 
Al-5.0Cu-0.8Mn-3.0RE alloy has finer grain size and cleaner grain inside, which is expected to 
have a good influence on the material performance.

4. Conclusions 
(1) The composition of Al-Cu-Mn-RE (La/Ce) alloy is designed based on the results of 
thermodynamic phase diagram calculation. By using orthogonal analysis and comparative analysis 
methods, as well as combining with experimental ana lysis, a reasonable alloy composition range 
is obtained, and Al-5.0Cu-0.6Mn-1.5RE is selected a s the typical optimization composition. 
Through the calculation of phase diagrams, property diagrams and non-equilibrium solidification 
simulation, as well as a series of experimental ver ification of microstructure and basic properties 
of materials, the proper heat treatment temperature of the alloy system is determined. The 
following heat treatment process is obtained as an optimized process: firstly solid solution 
treatment at 520 oC for 12 h, and water quenching (40 ), then aging at 175 oC for 8-10 h, air 
cooling in the end. 
(2) High-temperature tensile experiments show that the temperature rises every 50 oC, the 
strength of heat treatment state alloy decreases 20 -33 MPa. At 300 oC, the strength of the alloy is 
102.4 MPa, indicating good heat resistance. Fractur e mode of the alloy is a mixed form of brittle 
and ductile fracture. With the increase of tensile temperature, the fracture mode gradually changes 
from cleavage brittle fracture to ductile fracture. 
 (3) Heat exposure temperature has a significant e ffect on thermal conductivity. The higher 
the temperature, the better the thermal conductivit y. Electrical conductivity of the alloy increases 
with the increase of aging time, due to the comprehensive influence of factors such as the increase 
of precipitation content of each heat-resistant pha se, more uniform distribution of strengthening 
phases, and the dispersion of some eutectic phases at the grain boundary. 
 (4) With the increase of heat exposure temperatur e, the tensile strength of the alloy firstly 
increases and then decreases. After heat exposure a t 200 oC for 100 h, tensile strength value is up 
to 232.77 MPa. And after 350 oC heat exposure, tensile strength is not less than 190.52 MPa, 
which indicates that the Al-5.0Cu-0.6Mn-1.5RE alloy has good high-temperature durable

 (5) TEM analysis results show that the nano-sized Al 2Cu phase, T phase and Al 3Zr phase

to the Al 2Cu phase. The presence of eutectic phase can hinder the dislocation movement and 
improve the resistance to deformation at high tempe rature. The second phase in the alloy distorts 
partly and is surrounded by dislocations, which still has a good pinning effect on the matrix.
