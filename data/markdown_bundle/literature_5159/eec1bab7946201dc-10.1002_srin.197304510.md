Summary
Creep rupture tests at 550 DC have been made on welding
material of three different chromium-molybdenum-vana­
dium steels containing approx. 0.08 %C, approx. 0.6 to
1.6 % Cr, approx. 0.5 to 1 % Mo and approx. 0.25 to
0.4 % V. On account of a statistical evaluation of the
results of tests comprising a stress time of more than
1000 h, data of the 100.000 h creep rupture strength as well
as the creep breaking elongation after 1000 and 10,000 h
have been disclosed. Within the range of approx. 5 000 h,
the breaking elongation reached minimum values. At
longer times, the breaking elongation increased again
slightly.
