Introduction

temperature2and2consequently2the2operating2temperature2of2the2turbine2blades2equipped.2Nickel2base2

13502 K2 because2 they2 exhibit2 superior2 performance2 at2 high2 temperatures.2 Unfortunately,2 the2

The2 high2 temperature2 creep2 lifetime2 of2 superalloys2 is2 closely2 related2 to2 its2 microstructure2 as2

creep2dislocations2can2not2penetrate2into2the2γ'2phase2and2stacked2at2γ/γ'2interfaces;2the2elastic2field2
