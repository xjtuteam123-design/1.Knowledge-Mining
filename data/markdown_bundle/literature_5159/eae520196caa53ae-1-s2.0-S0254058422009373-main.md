1. Introduction 
In recent years, the high entropy alloys (HEAs) [ 1 ] or multicompo -
nent alloys (MCAs) [ 2 ] have attracted enormous attention due to their 
interesting microstructure and properties, such as high strength [ 3 ], 
high plasticity [ 4 ], oxidation resistance [ 5 ] and high corrosion resis -
tance [ 6 ]. However, it is found that single-phase HEAs are difficult to 
achieve a reasonable balance between strength and ductility [ 7 ]. So, Lu 
et al. [ 8 ]. Designed a novel FCC (L12) + B2 dual-phase AlCoCrFeNi
2.1 
EHEA with higher strength and ductility. Subsequently, many re -
searchers have developed EHEAs with excellent properties [ 9 – 13 ]. 
Thereinto, AlCoCrFeNi
2.1 
EHEA become a research focus of EHEAs due 
to its good casting properties (fluidity), controlled microstructure, 
thermal stability, and excellent mechanical properties. However, the 
strength of the AlCoCrFeNi
2.1 
EHEA is not enough for industrial appli -
cations. Therefore, it is necessary to further improve the strength and 
broaden the practical application potential of AlCoCrFeNi
2.1 
EHEA.

5. Conclusions 
In this study, the effect of Nb content on the microstructure evolution 
and mechanical properties of AlCoCrFeNi
2.1
Nb
x 
(x = 0, 0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.4, 
and 0.5) alloys were investigated. The conclusions can be drawn as 
follows: 
(1) In the AlCoCrFeNi
2.1
Nb
x 
alloys, bimodal eutectic microstructure 
(FCC + B2/Laves) was observed at x = 0.1. For x = 0.2 – 0.4, the 
microstructure of the alloys changed into dendrite structure (FCC 
+ B2) and eutectic structure (FCC + Laves), while the double 
scale hypoeutectic structure with B2 primary phase was acquired 
when x = 0.5. 
(2) The AlCoCrFeNi
2.1
Nb
0.2 
alloy shows an excellent combination of 
high compressive yield strength of 1158 MPa and appropriate 
ductility of 29.7%, respectively. The excellent properties result 
from the volume fractions of soft FCC and hard B2 presenting 
equal proportions and the appearance of a small number of Laves 
phases. 
(3) The calculated results indicated that the main strengthening 
mechanisms for the AlCoCrFeNi
2.1
Nb
0.2 
alloy are the precipita -
tion strengthening of the Laves phase and the solid-solution 
strengthening of the FCC and B2 phases. 
(4) The Vickers hardness and compressive yield strength of the 
AlCoCrFeNi
2.1
Nb
x

erties of the AlCoCrFeNi

obtaining heterogeneous microstructure.
