Keywords : Zr-Nb alloy, heat treatment, β-Zr, twins 
Abstract. The mechanical performance and corrosion resistanc e of Zr-Nb alloy (Zr705) treated in 
different ways is tested, and it is found that those properties of Zr-Nb alloy are significantly affected 
by heat treatment. The most important parameter of the heat treatment is quenching temperature. The 
Zr-Nb alloy, quenched below the critical quenching temperature Tcq (a temperature or a range of 
temperature between 850 ℃-870 ℃) presents good corrosion resistance performance, h owever, 
quenched above the Tcq gets a higher strength and a lower corrosion resistance. Through the study on 
the basis of the evidences gotten by means of metallographic, SEM, EPMA and TEM, the conclusion 
can be drawn as that the effect of heat treatment o n the performances of Zr-Nb alloy is primarily 
because of the mechanism of the distribution change of Nb, the quantity of β-Zr, the morphology of 
grains. 
1. Introduction 
In recent years, the application of nuclear Zr alloys in nuclear power industry draws hot attention of 
scholars. Several kinds of new Nb-containing zirconium alloys were developed and are being tested in 
reactor. However, it is reported that the corrosion behavior of Nb-containing Zr alloy is very sensitive 
to the microstructures that could be changed by hea t treatment. Baek et al.[1] studied the corrosion 
resistance of Nb-containing zirconium alloys in rel ation to accumulated annealing parameter, and 
found that weight gain increased with accumulated a nnealing parameter. B.X. Zhou et al.[2-10] 
studied the corrosion behavior of nuclear power Zr- 4 alloys applied in nuclear power industries by 
autoclave tests. Their works on Nb-containing zirco nium alloys had revealed that a uniform 
distribution of fine β-Nb (containing iron) particl es were important factors for improving the 
corrosion resistance. It was found that Zr-4 alloys shows an excellent corrosion resistance in different 
service environment of nuclear power, 360 ℃/18.6MPa in deionized water or lithiated water with 
0.01mol/L LiOH, super-heated steam of 400 ℃/10.3MPa, in 550 ℃/25MPa supercritical water (SCW) 
at al. They proposed that the solid solution conten t of Fe and Cr in α-Zr resulted from different heat 
treatments was an essential factor affecting the corrosion behavior of Zircaloy-4. Gonzalez, H. J[11]. 
researched the transformation superplasticity in Zr705. In their work, the Zr705 was thermally cycled 
from 900 ℃ to 710 ℃, resulting in strain increments after each cycle that are linearly proportional to 
stress up to 2 MPa. Tensile elongations in excess of 240% were achieved without fracture. 
Zr-Nb series alloy with high strength and corrosion resistance is widely used in chemical and 
nuclear power industries. Zr705 alloy (chemical compositions of Zr705 alloy, Zr-95.1 wt%, Hf-2.20 
wt%, Fe-0.09 wt%, Cr-0.45 wt%, Nb-2.42 wt%) is a ty pical kind of Zr-Nb alloy which has wide 
application in pump and cooling equipments in petro chemical industry. With the rapid development 
of the chemical industry, the performance of Zr all oy is not only excellent corrosion resistance, also 
high strength as far as possible in order to adapt to the harsh service environment. The research on low 
cost heat treatment of Zr alloy which has simple op eration in chemical industries, especially some 
conventional process, such as water quenching and tempering and the mechanical properties of alloys

4 Discussion 
In comparison with Zr702, the introduction of Nb element makes corrosion resistance and strength 
of Zr705 improved greatly. The heat treatment can further improve strength, however, the corrosion 
resistance declines in some extent. The quenching t emperature is one of the key parameters which 
affect the performance of alloy in heat treatment. There is a Tcq, critical quenching temperature 
between 850 ℃-870 ℃, at which the performances of alloy shift dramatic ally. Quenching above Tcq, 
alloy can get a high strength. Quenching under Tcq, good corrosion resistance and low strength are 
obtained. Under Tcq, as the quenching temperature increases, the strength of Zr705 increases slowly. 
When the quenching temperature increases over the T cq, the strength has a dramatic improvement. 
Above Tcq, the increasing of quenching temperature has little effect on strength among this 
temperature range. Tempering after quenching above Tcq can reduce the strength of alloy, while 
improve the corrosion resistance. 
Heat treatment changes the performances of alloy by the mechanism of element distribution, phase 
quantity, grain morphology, etc. The Zr-Nb alloy in the process of quenching under Tcq including 
slow cooling in casting will pass the duplex area α-Zr （HCP ）+β-Zr （BCC ） and stay for a long time. 
The atom of β-Zr packs not so dense as α-Zr, Nb(BCC) has a near atom radius with Zr and is close to 
Zr in the Periodic Table of Elements, so Nb solutes in β-Zr mostly after an adequate diffusion in heat 
preservation. The β-Zr containing Nb is substitution solid solution. Then in this process the alloy gets 
a two-phase microstructure α-Zr+β-Zr at room temperature and β-Zr contains a great quantity of Nb 
proved by the result of EPMA. During the deformation, dislocations slip easily at first in β-Zr of this 
microstructure. The deformation of alloy relies mainly on the mechanism of dislocation slip. 
Quenching above Tcq, all the Zr atoms exist as β-Zr and Nb solutes homogeneously after an 
adequate diffusion in heat preservation. All the β- Zr conversed to α-Zr at room temperature during 
quenching when Nb atoms have no time to diffuse and all solutes in α-Zr which arranges closely, 
which make a severer lattice distortion than Nb sol utes in β-Zr. Dislocations are hindered when slip 
and expand difficultly. When twins exist during the deformation, a mixed deformation mechanism of 
dislocation slip and deformation twins causes the s trength increases and ductility decrease. 
Tempering after quenching above Tcq, the dynamic re covery occur. Grains annex and grow up. Nb 
which solutes in α-Zr precipitates along the bounda ries of grains. Lattice distortion releases to a 
certain degree. All these results in strength decreases and ductility increases slightly. 
Specimens 705-U and 850-T have much more β-Zr than 870-Q and 870-T. Specimen 870-T 
tempering after the same quenching condition with 870-Q, the β-Zr content of former is a little more 
than the latter which have few β-Zr. It is told by the two groups that the form of β-Zr not only need the 
alloy pass β-Zr phase region in the heat treatment, a sufficient time to make Nb elements diffuse 
adequately and enriched to a higher concentration is also necessary. The explanation can be given as 
follow. Nb is BCC structure always, while there is allotropic change at 862 ℃ with Zr 
α-Zr(HCP)←→β-Zr(BCC). As to the Zr-Nb alloy, the ph ase change can refer to the Zr-Nb phase 
diagram illustrated in Fig. 11[12]. Above T1 all th e Zr atoms exist to BCC form in Zr-Nb alloy and 
Nb atoms solutes homogeneously in β-Zr after an adequate diffusion, so is homogeneous and the Nb 
content is not high in the micro level. Quench at this moment P1 in Fig. 11[12], the Nb atoms have no 
time to diffuse and the low-energy α-Zr have enough space to accommodate all the Nb atoms. Then 
β-Zr→α-Zr and the Zr-Nb alloy takes on single-phase α-Zr where all the Nb atoms solute at the 
temperature though P1. Above mentioned in P1 can be told in Fig. 12-(a). When it comes to T1-T2, Zr 
atoms exist to BCC form partly and the other part H CP. The Nb atoms choose to solute in

There is a tight connection between the deformation behavior of Zr-Nb alloy and phase 
composition. Atoms in β-Zr arrange no more closely than that of α-Zr and have more slip systems, 
which makes β-Zr deform more easily. So under the same material conditions alloys which have more 
β-Zr deform more easily and have a lower strength. It is verified from the results of strength and 
elongation in Table 1. XRD and TEM show that 705-U and 850-T have more β-Zr, as a result, they 
have a high elongation and low strength in Table 1. Specimens 870-T, 870-Q and 1030-Q which have 
few β-Zr have a relative high strength and low elongation. The SEM results in Fig. 3 can also prove 
the relation between the quantity of β-Zr and the d uctility of Zr-Nb alloy. Macro black holes in the 
fracture are related with β-Zr which deforms easily . More the black holes on the fracture, more the 
β-Zr the alloy has. 
There is a close relation between the corrosion beh avior of Zr-Nb alloy and phase composition. 
The corrosion potential of alloys declines with the decrease of the quantity of β-Zr in general. The less 
β-Zr, the more easily the alloy is corroded. Specim ens 705-U and 850-T have more β-Zr than 
specimens 870-T, 870-Q and 1030-Q, so the corrosion potential of formers are higher. Among 
specimens 870-T, 870-Q and 1030-Q, 870-T has the hi ghest corrosion potential because it has little 
β-Zr seen in XRD and TEM while 870-Q has the lowest corrosion potential for the additional 
interface energy caused by grain boundaries besides there is little β-Zr. With different grain boundary 
，the twins belong to ordered arrangement not considered as the defects during corrosion process, so 
1030-Q has a moderate corrosion potential. Specimen s 702-C and 1030-Q have the same phase 
composition and a near level of defects, as a result, the very near corrosion parameters they get. 
Compared to the corrosion potential, passive current density of Zr-Nb alloy gets the similar effect 
by the number of defects contained in the alloy and the β-Zr content. The More defects, the larger 
passive current density. β-Zr is useful to the decr ease of the passive current densities. The 
metallographic and TEM results present that 702-C and 1030-Q have the least defects and almost no 
β-Zr, 705-U and 850-T have interface defects but co ntain a large number of β-Zr. The two factors 
combined with each other make the passive current densities of two sets of specimens in a low state. 
870-Q has defects of grain boundaries and almost no β-Zr, hence, the passive current density is the 
largest. Tempering makes 870-T have a little more β -Zr than 870-Q and grain boundaries weaken 
which lead to a decrease of interface energy, so the passive current density has been greatly reduced, 
compared with the specimen 870-Q. Specimen 870-T ha s a little more β-Zr, however, more defects 
than specimens both 702-C and 1030-Q. Specimen 870- T has less β-Zr and more defects than 
specimens both 705-U and 850-T. So the passive current density of 870-T is still at a higher level than 
the others except 870-Q. 
5 Conclusions 
1 Compared with pure Zr, Nb element introduced make s corrosion resistance and strength of Zr705 
improved greatly. 
2 Heat treatment can further change the performance of Zr-Nb alloy. Quench above Tcq, alloy can be 
highly strengthened, the strength of tempered alloy at this time will be slightly lower with a certain 
increase of corrosion resistance. Quench under Tcq, alloy can obtain a good corrosion resistance. 
3 Heat treatment changes the performances of alloy mainly rely on the mechanism of diffusion and 
distribution of Nb, the quantity of β-Zr and morphology of grains. 
4 Quench under Tcq, β-Zr rich in Nb forms. Such mic rostructure improves the corrosion resistance 
and ductility of Zr-Nb alloy. A right amount of β-Zr is beneficial to improve the corrosion resistance 
and ductility of Zr-Nb alloy.

5 Quench above Tcq, severe lattice distortion cause d by the homogeneous solution of Nb in α-Zr 
occurs. The level of lattice distortion decided by the amount of solution Nb in α-Zr makes strength of 
alloy increase and the ductility lower. However, the corrosion potential and passive current densities 
have little relation with the level of lattice distortion. 
6 Quench at a lower temperature above Tcq, a large number of short-bar shape grains and a small 
amount of twins form in the alloy, dislocation slip is the main deformation mechanism, accompanied 
by a small amount of deformation twins. Grain bound aries increase the strength but decrease the 
corrosion resistance. Temper at this moment, the du ctility and corrosion resistance of alloy increase 
due to β-Zr which precipitates along the grain boundaries. 
7 Quench at a higher temperature above Tcq, the microstructure gets fine and a large number of twins 
form. Deformation twins is the main mechanism. Allo y has a high strength but the ductility decline 
seriously. Twin boundaries on the form of coherent interface don’t lower the corrosion resistance of 
alloy. 
8 The form of β-Zr in Zr-Nb alloy need not only the alloy pass β-Zr phase region in the heat treatment, 
but also a sufficient time to make Nb elements diff use adequately and enriched to a higher 
concentration is also necessary.
