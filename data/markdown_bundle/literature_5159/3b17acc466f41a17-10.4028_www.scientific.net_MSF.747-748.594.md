Abstract. A 700 ℃ advanced ultra-supercritical (A-USC) power plant t echnology project was 
initiated in China in the year of 2011. The highest temperature components in A-USC boiler are 
superheater and reheater tubes. The fire-side metal temperature can reach 750 ℃ (even higher). Based 
on the very long time service(30-40 years) these important high temperature tubes require 10 
5h long 
time stress rupture strength higher than 100MPa and the corrosion/oxidation layer loss less than 2mm 
for 2×10 5h. The highest temperature components in 700 ℃ steam turbine are the buckets of different 
stages and require very long service time. At this severe condition only superalloys can fulfill these 
unusual requirements. This paper reviewed Fe-Ni and Ni-base superalloys from the view point of 
structure stability and long term mechanical properties and corrosion/oxidation resistance for 700 ℃ 
A-USC power plant materials selection. 
Introduction 
Electricity is an important pillar for sustainable development of national economy. Up to now 
about 70-80% electricity is generated from coal fir ed power plants in China. However, the average 
thermal efficiency of Chinese power plants is ～30% only. It is lower level in the world. High 
consumption of coal for fossil power plants with lo w thermal efficiency not only induces 
un-reasonable application of energy resources but also makes severe pollution. Today the installation 
of modern power plants with high thermal efficiency is concentrated to develop advanced 
ultra-supercritical(A-USC) technology with high steam temperature up to 700-760 ℃. China already 
initiated a project on A-USC technology in 2011 for developing 700 ℃ coal fired power plant with the 
thermal efficiency ～50% to reach the world advanced level. 
In 700 ℃ A-USC power plant the steam is generated from superheater/reheater tubes of the boiler. 
These tubes are working with 700 ℃ high temperature steam inside of the tubes and als o high 
temperature coal fired condition outside of the tubes. The highest temperature of these tubes can reach 
750-780 ℃. Based on the very long time service(30-40 years) these important high temperature tubes 
requires 10 5h long time stress rupture strength higher than 100MPa and the corrosion/oxidation layer 
loss less than 2mm for 2×10 5h. The highest temperature components in 700 ℃ steam turbine are the 
buckets of different stages and also require very l ong service time. At this severe condition only 
superalloys can fulfill these un-usual requirements.

This paper will review Fe-Ni and Ni-base superalloy s and the development of A-USC power

A-USC Power Plant Development 
Fig. 1 shows the steam temperature evolution of Chi nese fossil power plants[1]. Almost in a half 
century (from 1956 to 2005) the steam temperatures of fossil power plants in China were lower than 
566 ℃. However the situation had been quickly changed si nce 2006 because of the establishment of 
the first USC power plant with steam parameters of 600 ℃ and 25MPa. To the end of 2010, 37 power 
plants with 72 sets of 600-1000MW power units with steam temperature of 600 ℃ have been put in 
service[2]. Austenitic heat-resistant steels TP347H, Super304H and HR3C are now mainly used for 
boiler superheater and reheater materials in China. Nickel-base alloy Nimonic 80A has been used for 
600 ℃ USC steam turbine bucket material in routine production. 
 
Fig. 1 Steam parameter evolution of Chinese fossil power plants 
 
China initiated a 700 ℃ advanced ultra-super-critical (A-USC) project in 2 010 and has formed a 
National Innovation Alliance of 700 ℃ A-USC Coal-fired Power Plant Technology in 2011. I n 
consideration of 700 ℃ A-USC requirements several Fe-Ni and Ni-base super alloys are studied and 
evaluated in this paper.

700 ℃℃ ℃℃ A-USC Boiler Materials. The basic consideration of 700 ℃ A-USC boiler tube and pipe

5h long time stress rupture strength higher than 100MPa, the corrosion/oxidation

Fe-Ni-base Superalloy GH2984. GH2984 is a cobalt free Fe-Ni-base superalloy with about 
1%Nb,1%Ti and 0.4%Al for γ ＇ -Ni 3(Nb,Ti,Al) precipitation strengthening high tempera ture 
material, which was invented by the Institute of Me tal Research, Chinese Academy of Sciences[3]. 
The purpose of GH2984 development was to fulfill su perheater tube(φ25×2.5mm) application at 
700 ℃ for marine power. 
After standard heat treatment, the spherical γ ＇ strengthening phase particles with an average 
diameter of 23nm and an amount of 5.74% uniformly distributed in γ austenitic matrix. Besides, there 
is also minor MC phase, including NbC and Ti(N,C) l ocated inside the grains as well as at grain 
boundaries and having a size of 1-10µm and an amount of 0.52%. 
After 700 ℃ prolonged exposure, the radius of γ ＇ particles increases linearly with the root of 
aging time and obeys diffusion-controlled coarsenin g kinetics of γ ＇. The amount of γ ＇ gradually 
increases to 7.23% after 18,000h aging, but a small amount of σ phase also forms. 
GH2984 possesses good oxidation resistance as well as corrosion resistance and anti- 
intergranualar corrosion property, which is free of cracks. Besides these GH2984 also exhibits good 
cold and hot workability and can be easily deformed into tube products for boiler application.

Haynes230. Haynes 230 is a solid solution strengthening Ni-base alloy containing with 14%W and 
2%Mo and also by adding rare earth element 0.02%La, which was developed by Haynes International, 
USA[5]. This alloy characterizes with very stable austenitic structure and a certain amount of carbides 
WC and M 23 C6. There are no brittle phase formations such as µ a nd σ phase after long time aging. 
Haynes 230 characterizes with excellent oxidation resistance and high stress rupture strength and has 
been wildly used for air-craft engine and gas-turbi ne industries for many years. However its 10 5h 
stress rupture strength can not reach the target 10 0MPa because of its solid solution strengthening 
only. 
617B Alloy. Its original type is Inconel617 which was developed by former INCO Alloy 
International in 1975. Alloy617 is a Ni-Cr-Co-Mo ty pe solid solution strengthening Ni-base alloy 
originally developed for high temperature gas-turbi ne components with not very high strength 
requirement. For high creep rupture strength requir ement of 700 ℃ A-USC boilers Thyssen-Krupp 
VDM is making its modification such as 617B, CCA 61 7 and 617OCC etc to add certain amount of 
Al and Ti for γ ＇precipitation strengthening and also add B for grain boundary strengthening[6]. Now 
the routine production alloy of tubes and pipes by SMST and V&M in Europe is 617B. Alloy 617B is 
strengthened not only by Co, Cr, Mo solid solution strengthening but also a certain amount of γ ＇ 
(4-5%) precipitation strengthening and grain-boundary strengthening by B addition. Alloy 617B now 
is wildly adopted for tube and pipe application in Europe. However 617 and its modification 617B is 
also difficult to reach 10 5h stress rupture 100MPa target for 700 ℃ A-USC power plant application.

Ni-20Cr-20Co base by adding 6%Mo for strong solid solution strengthening and also with 2% Ti and 
certain amount of Al for precipitation Ni 3(Ti, Al) type γ ＇ strengthening. Nimonic 263 contains with 
about 10% γ ＇ and certain amount of M 23 C6 at grain boundaries. Because of the high ratio of Ti/Al,

Summary 
1. From view points of long time (10 
5h) stress rupture strength, high temperature fire-side coal ash 
corrosion resistance and structure stability at 750 ℃(even higher) the newly developed Ni-base 
superalloy INCONEL740H characterizes with the best properties in comparison with GH2984, 
HR6W, Haynes230, 617B, Nimonic263 and Inconel740. I nconel740H can be considered as a good 
candidate superheater/reheater material. 
2. The service temperature of large size pipe application for 700 ℃ A-USC boiler header and main 
steam line for connecting boiler and turbine is 700 ℃ only. Alloy 617B, Nimonic263 and 
INCONEL740H can be the candidate materials for this application. However the pipe thickness of 
617B should be much thicker than 740H, because the allowable stress of 617B is lower than 
INCONEL740H. 
3. WASPALOY was recommended for 700 ℃ steam turbine front stage buckets material and 
Nimonic80A can also be the rear stage buckets mater ial at the temperatures lower than 700 ℃. 
However the long time stress rupture tests at high temperature must be conducted to get the exact data 
for designers.
