The microstructure of the alloy was observed by means of OM and SEM, and the effect of mold temperature and casting dimension on tensile properties was studied. Results show that equiaxed grains are obtained regardless of the casting dimension. ? grain size tends to increase with an increase in mold temperature. Hot isostatic pressing of the alloy was carried out for tensile properties' comparison. Room temperature tensile test results show that Tl-6A-4V alloy produced via counter-gravity casting has good balance of strength and ductility after hot isostatic pressing (HIP). The alloy shows higher ductility due to the elimination of porosity. In both cast and HIP status, the tensile strength is inclined to decrease with an increase in mold temperature, while the ductility is prone to slightly increase. Both the strength and ductility tend to decrease with an increase in the casting dimension. Investment casting is applied to produce components with excellent surface finish, dimensional accuracy, and complex shapes 2]. Meanwhile, compared with most alloys, the fluidity of molten titanium alloys are relatively lower. Therefore, high filling capacity of titanium alloys should be achieved to yield good castings, particularly for thin wall cast parts. Counter-gravity casting is a technique by which the pressure difference created between the crucible and mold cavity brings about an upward or counter- gravity movement of the melt as the melt is poured into the mold cavity [31. It can be used to produce high performance cast parts by improving the filling capacity and solidification behavior. This process was patented in 1972 by Hitchiner Manufacturing [4] and different variants of the process have been evolved over the years. The unique mold filling approach of the counter- gravity casting technique possesses unique advantages related to casting economics, defects elimination and attainment of net-shape in the cast products. Such desirous attributes have ensured the development of the technology, especially in power and automotive [5, 61 applications . However, due to the high reactivity and 9

Mold temperature has influences on the filling capacity and the final mechanical properties of cast parts, but yet there is not much information available related to the effect of mold temperature on the mechanical properties of cast Ti-6Al-4V alloy. In the current study, cast bars with different diameters were designed to study the room temperature (RT) tensile properties. The self-developed counter-gravity casting equipment with cold crucible melting was used to fabricate the Ti-6AI-4V castings with different mold temperatures, and the effects of mold temperature and casting dimension on tensile properties were studied.
