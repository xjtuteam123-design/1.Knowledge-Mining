ABSTRACT
The fracture strength was estimated for copper-alloy
CuCrZr, and their HIPed joints with SS316L and CuCrZr,
which were the candidate materials consisting of the First
Wall of ITER. Fracture toughness and fatigue crack
propagation ratio of those materials were superior to those
of copper-alloy DS-Cu and its HIPed joint with SS316. It
was confirmed that the cracks near the CuCrZr/SS316L
HIPed boundary was propagated in copper-alloy along the
interface at a distance of about 10urn from the interface.
The lost of ductility of all materials caused the decrement of
fracture toughness and increment of crack propagation
ratio in high temperature (573K).
I. INTRODUCTION
The first wall of ITER consists of copper-alloy
'CuCrZr' heat sink built in type 316L stainless steel
(SS316L) cooling pipes and beryllium armor which are
bonded by HIP (Hot Isostatic Pressing) that is one of the
candidate fabrication methods. It is anticipated that the first
wall is exposed to the severe circumstances in reactor
operation: heat flux, repetition thermal stress, etc. So there
is a possibility that growth of defects in the first wall causes
a great damage in operation. Also it can be easily supposed
that HIPed interfaces were weak against crack propagation.
It is, therefore, indispensable that strength of candidate
materials is estimated in order to insure structural integrity
and reliability of the interface.
The objective of this paper is to evaluate fracture
strength of CuCrZr and its HIPed joints (CuCrZr/SS316L,
CuCrZr/SS316LN-IG and CuCrZr/CuCrZr) against crack
propagation. Fracture toughness JIG and fatigue crack
propagation ratio obtained by experiments on the basis of
ASTM standard were used for fracture strength evaluation.
Fracture surfaces of those materials were observed by SEM.
Furthermore, fracture strength of materials concerning
CuCrZr were compared with that of materials concerning
DS-Cu which was reported1 previously. In this report, the
phrases 'materials concerning CuCrZr' and 'materials
concerning DS-Cu' indicate as 'all the materials as follows:
two kinds of CuCrZr base metals, CuCrZr/SS316LN-IG,
CuCrZr/SS316L and CuCrZr/CuCrZr' and 'DS-Cu base
metal and DS-Cu/SS316' respectively.
Materials mocking up the first wall and fabrication
processes of those materials (HIPing conditions and

investigate the influence of thermomechanical treatment

IV. DISCUSSIONS
On fracture toughness test, CuCrZr and its HIPed
joints with SS316L, SS316LN-IG and CuCrZr had higher
TIC values than DS-Cu and DS-Cu/SS316 HIPed joint.
It was confirmed that TIC values of all HIPed joints
decreased as environmental temperature increased, because
HIPed interface lost its ductility in high temperature. SEM
images of CuCrZr/CuCrZr, CuCrZr/SS316LN-IG, and
CuCrZr/SS316L shown in Fig5 are fracture surfaces on
HIPed interface of each HIPed interface. On
CuCrZr/CuCrZr, at ambient temperature there were found
the dimples that indicated the ductile fracture, but at 573K
dimples could not be found. On CuCrZr/SS316LN-IG and
CuCrZr/SS316LN HIPed joints, dimples were formed
closely at ambient temperature, whereas at 573K, formed
sparsely and larger sizes than those at ambient temperature.

At 573K TIC value of CuCrZr/SS316L was the highest

Stretched zones of all materials except
CuCrZr/SS316LN-IG and CuCrZr/SS316L were formed
rather flatly. On CuCrZr/SS316LN-IG at ambient
temperature and CuCrZr/SS316L at ambient temperature

differences of fracture surfaces, it is conjectured that large
energy was required for beginning crack propagation on
CuCrZr/SS316L and CuCrZr/SS316LN-IG and that
CuCrZr/SS316L specially had strong resistance against
crack propagation in high temperature environment as well.

V. CONCLUSIONS
Fracture toughness test and fatigue crack propagation
test were performed on materials concerning CuCrZr. In
this study the following conclusions can be drawn.
(1) Fracture toughness J1C and fatigue crack propagation
ratio were obtained (cf. TableS). Materials concerning
CuCrZr have greater strength against crack
propagation comparing with those concerning DS-Cu.
(2) By observing the fracture surfaces, it was clear that the
loss of the ductility at high temperature environment
caused the decrement of fracture toughness and the
increment of fatigue crack propagation ratio of all
CuCrZr materials. In short, the strength against crack
propagation of all the materials decreases as
environmental temperature increase.
(3) The decrement of the strength against crack
propagation on CuCrZr/SS316L due to the increment
of environmental temperature was the smallest of the
three kinds of CuCrZr HIPed joints. That is to say,
CuCrZr/SS316L has the highest strength against crack
propagation of the three at 573 K.. Fracture strength on
CuCrZr/CuCrZr was specially small at ambient
temperature and 573K. So there is still room to
optimize HIPing condition.
(4) It was confirmed that heat treatment in HIPing affected
badly to CuCrZr base metal in fatigue crack
propagation test; crack propagation ratio of HIP heat
treated CuCrZr was higher than that of non-heat
treated CuCrZr.
