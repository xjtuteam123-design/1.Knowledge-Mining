ABSTRACT 
Nb was replaced partially by Ta in Ti
2
AlNb alloy, resulting in elevated tensile strengths at high temperatures. The 
yield and tensile strength are up to 985 MPa and 1080 MPa at 650 
◦
C, respectively. Meanwhile, the elongation 
maintained a high level and the value reached 13 %. The microstructure and deformation mechanism at high 
temperatures were investigated. The results revealed that the Ta addition improved the tensile properties by 
solution strengthening. Our findings provided new insights into designing the Ti
2
AlNb alloy with high strength 
and toughness. 
1. Introduction 
Ti
2
AlNb based alloys have great potential in aerospace applications 
due to their low density, outstanding creep resistance, oxidation resis -
tance and high specific strength [1,2] . However, the high temperature 
properties are relatively poor, especially at 650 
◦
C. Recently, in order to 
improve the tensile properties, some scholars have extensive research on 
the microstructure-properties [3,4] . The alloying is one of the ways to 
improve the properties of materials [5] . Through the addition of alloy 
elements, the high temperature properties can be improved. In recent 
years, the replacement of Nb with Mo, W, and V has been studied. The 
addition of V can improve room plasticity, while the strength and 
oxidation resistance decrease. The addition of W can refine the lath O 
phase and improve the strength and creep resistance [6] . It is also found 
that the addition of Mo corresponds to the replacement of Nb, it can 
improve the strength, creep, and oxidation resistance, but adding more 
Mo would reduce the plasticity and toughness [7,8] . Ta belongs to β 
stable element. Especially the replacement of Nb with Ta has a signifi -
cant effect on improving strength and ductility, while the strengthening 
mechanism of Ti
2
AlNb alloy with Ta element needs further to 
investigate. 
In this work, a part of Nb in Ti-22Al-27Nb alloy was substituted with 
Ta. The microstructure and tensile properties of Ti-22Al-27Nb and Ti- 
22Al-20Nb-7Ta alloys after heat treatment were investigated and pro -
posed an optimum heat treatment route. Meanwhile, the strengthening 
mechanism of Ti-22Al-20Nb-7Ta alloy at high temperatures was further 
discussed, which provides a theoretical way for designing the Ti
2
AlNb 
alloy with high strength and toughness. 
2. Material and methods 
The Ti-22Al-20Nb-7Ta alloy was prepared by vacuum induction 
melting and was remitted 3 – 4 times to endure the alloy homogenization. 
After, the alloy was forged at 1100-1200 
◦
C in β phase region after ho -
mogenizing at 1200 
◦
C for 20 h and then was rolled to 3 mm thick sheet 
in ( α
2 
+ B2 + O) phase regions. Finally, the duplex microstructure was 
obtained with solution treatment at (O + B2) phase region and aging 
treatment at (O + B2) phase region. The Ti-22Al-27Nb alloy also 
selected for comparison and the thermomechanical processing param -
eter is consistent with the Ti-22Al-20Nb-7Ta alloy. The microstructure 
of heat-treated alloys was characterized by scanning electron micro -
scopy (SEM). The SEM samples were prepared via conventional 
grinding, mechanical polishing and then etched chemically using Kroll 
reagent (10 %HF + 30 % HNO
3 
+ 70 % H
2
O). To investigate the tensile 
properties of the alloys, the tensile test at different temperatures was 
carried out in a JSM-5800 equipped with an MP-20923 tensile test 
system.

The fracture mode is transgranular fracture with ductile dimples. The 
analysis of the tensile fracture surface shows that: with the tensile 
temperature increase, the distribution of the dimples is more uniform, 
and the size gradually increases. As the temperature increases, the 
thermal activation energy increases, resulting in the increase of atom 
kinetic energy. Therefore, the dislocation is easy to be activated. 
Meanwhile, for different slip systems, the decreased rate of critical 
resolved shear stress is different with the increase of tensile temperature. 
At high temperatures, the new slip systems are activated [12,13] and it 
contributed to the deformation of the sample, which the EL of the alloy 
are better, respectively 10 %, 12 %, and 13 %. However, the distribution 
and size of the dimples are no-uniform at 750 
◦
C, and the EL is relatively 
lower (8.5 %). 
4. Conclusion 
The Ti-22Al-20Nb-7Ta alloy was prepared by thermomechanical 
processing. The Ta addition can obviously improve the tensile properties 
by solution strengthening. The optimum heat treatment route was 
970 
◦
C/1 h/WQ + 800 
◦
C/24 h/AC and it attributed to the size and 
distribution of lath O phase and α
2
/O phase particle. Furthermore, the 
tensile strength at high temperatures also were gradually decreased. The 
YS and UTS are 985 MPa and 1080 MPa at 650 
◦
C, respectively. The EL 
maintained a high level and the value reached 13 %.
