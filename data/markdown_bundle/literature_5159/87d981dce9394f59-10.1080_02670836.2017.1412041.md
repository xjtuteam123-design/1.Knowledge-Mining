ABSTRACT
The present research work investigates and analyses the effect of compaction pressure, sintering
temperature and time on mechanical characteristics, namely compressive strength and Young’s
modulus of Ni
50−xTi50Cux (x = 0, 5 and 10) alloys fabricated using powder metallurgy technol-
ogy. The effect of process parameters on mechanical characteristics was evaluated using the
Taguchi method and analysis of variance. Using the Taguchi approach, optimised values of pro-
cess parameters were found for higher compressive strength and lower Young’s modulus in each
set of Ni
50Ti50,N i45Ti50Cu5 and Ni40Ti50Cu10 alloys. With the addition of copper in NiTi alloy, a
little improvement in mechanical characteristics was observed, but bioactivity was decreased
at 10%of Cu. Therefore, above 5% of Cu in NiTi alloy was not proved successful for biomedical
applications.

Conclusions
In this work, the influence of copper addition and
process parameters of P/M process, such as com-
paction pressure, sintering temperature and sintering
time has been investigated for compressive strength
and Young’s modulus of NiTi alloys. The Taguchi
method was used to conduct the experiments and to
optimise the process parameters for two mechanical
characteristics. The following important conclusions
are drawn:
• Highervalueofcompactionpressure,lowervalueof
sintering temperature and sintering time favoured
the higher compressive strength, whereas lower
compactionpressure,highervalueofsinteringtem-
perature and sintering time favoured the lower
Young’smodulusofNiTialloys.
• Addition of Cu to NiTi alloy improves the crystal
packingandthusincreasesthecompressivestrength
a n dY o u n g ’ sm od u l u s .B u ti nt h ep r e se n tc a se ,a d d i -
tion of Cu up to 10% results in little increase in
mechanicalcharacteristics.
• Higher percentage of Cu in NiTi causes higher
porositywhensinteredathighertemperature(above
1323K) for more than 40min. This higher poros-
ity reduces the compressive strength and Young’s
modulus.
• The maximum compressive strength of 1132MPa
has been obtained for Ni
40Ti50Cu10 correspond-
ing to the optimal process parameters of com-
paction pressure 177MPa; sintering temperature,
1223Kandsinteringtime,20min.Thelowestvalue

for Ni

cess parameter settings of compaction pressure,
107MPa; sintering temperature, 1423K and sinter-
ingtime,60min.
• In the present experimentation, sintering temper-
ature remained the most contributing and criti-
cal process parameter which affects the mechanical
characteristicsofNiTialloys.
• Ni
50Ti50 showed good strain recovery which decre-
ases a little with the addition of Cu.
• Porous structure shows good biocompatibility
