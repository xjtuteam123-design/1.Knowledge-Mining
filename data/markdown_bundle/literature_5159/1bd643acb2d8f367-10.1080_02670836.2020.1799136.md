ABSTRACT
The room temperature and high temperature tensile properties of Al–7Si–0.6Mg alloy prepared
by wire arc additive manufacturing have been investigated. The results show that the strength-
ening phase gradually coarsens and the eutectic silicon phase does not grow and agglomerate,
meanwhile, the tensile strength and yield strength of the alloy gradually decrease with the
increase of temperature. The high-temperature properties of the alloy are better than that of
cast alloy. With the increase of temperature, the fracture of the alloy changes from ductile
transgranular fracture to intergranular fracture.

Introduction
Al–7Si–0.6Mg alloy is widely used in aerospace indus-
try because of its excellent specific strength, castabil-
ity and corrosion resistance [1–4]. Usually, the yield
strength and hardness of the alloy can be improved by
formingfineMg 2Siprecipitatesaftersolutiontreatment
and artificial aging [3,5]. However, with the temper-
ature exceeding 185°C, the Mg2Si phase is extremely
unstableandcoarsensrapidly ,whichresultsintherapid
decrease of the strength of the alloy [6]. Aerospace
structural parts cannot meet the needs of lightweight
development by increasing wall thickness or reinforc-
ing ribs to ensure the strength under short-term high-
temperature conditions. At present, adding alloying
elements [7–10] and increasing cooling rate [2,11–13]
are important means to improve the high-temperature
propertiesofAl–Si–Mgalloys.

Conclusion
• With the increase of temperature, the atom diffu-
sionabilityisenhanced,thestrengtheningphasesin
Al–7Si–0.6Mg alloy produced by wire arc additive
manufacturing are coarsening gradually, the inter-
face sliding ability is strengthened, and the disloca-
tion hindering ability is reduced, which results in
the strength of the alloy decreasing gradually. The
tensile strength and yield strength decreased from
363 and 314MPa to 208 and 203MPa, respectively
from room temperature to 250°C, and the yield-
strengthratioincreasedto98.7%.
• Comparedwithcastingmethod,thesecondaryden-
drite arm spacing of as-deposition Al–7Si–0.6Mg
alloy is smaller, and with the increase of tem-
perature, eutectic silicon phase does not grow up
and agglomerate during the tensile process the
alloy (T6). Therefore, Al–7Si–0.6Mg alloy pro-
duced by wire arc additive manufacturing exhibits
higher high-temperature performance mechanical
properties.
• Unlike cast alloys, the fracture characteristics of
Al–7Si–0.6Mg (T6) Alloys by wire arc additive
manufacturing changes from transgranular fracture
to intergranular fracture with increasing
temperature.
