1. Introduction 
In recently years, with development of aerospace technology, the 
service environment of high temperature components is becoming more 
and more challenging, which requires the developing of high tempera -
ture materials with low density. The TiAl alloy displays low density, 
high specific strength and stiffness, improved creep and oxidation 
resistance which is considered as the most promising high temperature 
materials in aero space [ 1 – 8 ]. Relatively poor oxidation resistance of 
TiAl alloy is the main obstacle for high temperature application. 
Therefore, promotion of oxidation resistance is very important for TiAl 
to meet the performance of high temperature components such as gas 
turbine disk [ 9 ]. The strength and oxidation resistance of TiAl alloy are 
decreasing rapidly when the temperature is above 900 
◦
C [ 10 – 12 ]. Thus, 
further developing the strength and oxidation resistance at higher 
temperature becomes an important issue in the next generation TiAl 
alloys. 
The most effective and common method to improve high- 
temperature oxidation resistance and mechanical property is alloying. 
Former studies suggested that refractory metals such as Nb, Mo, Ta and 
W could increase the strength and oxidation resistance of TiAl alloy to 
higher temperature. It suggested that both strength, ductility and 
oxidation resistance could be improved by appropriate Mo addition 
[ 13 ]. Li [ 14 ] et al. displayed that fraction of B
2 
phase is increased by Mo 
addition, which is beneficial for high temperature compressive proper -
ties. Ta improves the creep and oxidation resistance while increases the 
hot-cracking susceptibility [ 15 ]. Some scholars [ 16 ] have investigated 
the microstructure and mechanical properties of γ -TiAl plate with Ta 
addition and suggested that a combination of good creep resistance and 
ductility is obtained by refined primary γ grain with incremental frac -
tion of α
2
/ γ lamella. Nb element improves the oxidation resistance of 
TiAl alloy effectively as well as creep resistance and strength [ 17 , 18 ]. 
The solid solution effect of Nb into both γ and α
2 
phase increases the 
strength and thermal stability. However, there exists remarkable 
melting temperature between W, Mo, Ta, Nb and TiAl matrix, it ’ s hard to 
fabricate high quality component with affordable cost by traditional 
smelting and forging process. Powder metallurgy provides an attractive 
near-net forming technique which could fabricate Mo-modified TiAl 
alloy with homogeneous composition and fine grain distribution [ 19 , 
20 ].

behavior of high temperature oxidation in TiAl alloy especially Mo 
modified still need further investigation. Thus, 4 wt%Mo modified TiAl

microstructure evolution is characterized by SEM and TEM. Hot 
compressive test and oxidation test were performed to evaluate the ef -
fect of Mo addition. Finally, the strengthening and oxidation mechanism 
were analyzed which could provide theoretical support for sintering new 
TiAl alloy with moderate Nb and Mo addition. 
2. Experiment 
The pre-alloy TiAl4822 powder with particle size 106 – 180 μ m was 
produced by plasma rotating electrode process (PREP). High purity Mo 
powder (99.9%) with particle size of 2.5 – 6 μ m was prepared by RO 
process. Prealloy TiAl4822 powder and pure Mo powder were blended 
in planetary ball mill (SPEX 8000 M) for 3 h with rotation 300 r/min 
under argon gas protection. Among them, resin tank is selected for ball 
milling tank, while hard corundum is selected for grinding ball. The 
selected ball-to-powder ratio is 4 with ball diameter of 5 mm. Then cold 
isostatic pressing was performed under a pressure of 200 MPa. Hot press

4. Conclusion 
In this paper, microstructure evolution, high temperature 
compressive property and oxidation behavior of TiAl4822 and 
TiAl4822 – 4Mo alloy are investigated in detail. SEM and TEM were 
applied to characterize the microstructure difference. The effect of Mo 
addition on mechanical and oxidation properties were evaluated. The 
strengthening and oxidation mechanism were analyzed briefly. The 
main conclusions can be summarized as follows: 
1. A bi-modal structure with multi-scaled α
2 
lamella is exhibited in both 
TiAl4822 and Mo modified TiAl4822 alloy after sintering. Addition 
of 4%Mo element effectively refines the equiaxed γ grain size and α
2
/ 
γ colony size. 
2. The addition of Mo significantly improves the high-temperature 
compressive strength of TiAl4822 alloy, which is attributed to the 
lattice misfit and fine grain strengthening effect of Mo addition. 
3. Mo element is mainly distributed in the place adjacent to the alloy 
matrix. Because the valence electron of Mo is higher than O, it can 
prevent further oxidation by reducing oxygen vacancy, so as to slow 
down the oxidation rate and improve the oxidation resistance of the 
alloy. 
4. A multi layered oxide is formed in both TiAl4822 and Mo modified 
TiAl4822 alloy which mainly contains TiO
