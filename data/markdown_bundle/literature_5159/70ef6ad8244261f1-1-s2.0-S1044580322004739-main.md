ABSTRACT 
The results of a study of the influence of thermomechanical treatment modes and stabilizing annealing tem -
perature on structural-phase state parameters and mechanical properties of disperse-strengthened vanadium 
alloys of V-Me(Cr, W)-Zr system are presented. The methods of scanning and transmission electron microscopy 
were used to investigate the grain, defect, and heterophase structure of alloys. The regularities of their structural- 
phase transformation and fracture were studied depending on the conditions of treatment and tensile defor -
mation. The values of strength properties and plasticity were determined by tension at different temperatures 
after various treatment modes. It has been established that, regardless of the stabilizing annealing temperature, 
the use of a modified thermomechanical treatment (TMT-II) provides a higher level of strength properties of the 
studied alloys both at room and elevated temperatures. The influence of the main factors (elemental composition, 
volume fraction and dispersion of the second phase particles, parameters of grain and defect structure) on the 
thermal stability of the structural-phase state and mechanical properties of V-Me(Cr, W)-Zr system alloys is 
discussed. 
1. Introduction 
Low-activation vanadium alloys are currently considered as prom -
ising structural materials for new thermonuclear fission and fusion re -
actors [ 1 – 6 ]. One of the key requirements for such materials is to ensure 
the required level of high-temperature strength while maintaining 
acceptable values of low-temperature plasticity. Analysis of the pub -
lished data [ 1 , 2 , 4 , 7 – 11 ] indicates that V-Ti-Cr system alloys, that are 
characterized by good functional properties and manufacturability, 
demonstrate an acceptable level of high-temperature strength only up to 
700 – 800 
◦
C. Additional alloying of this system or thermomechanical 
(TMT) and chemical-heat (CHT) treatments implementation contribute 
to a significant increase in strength only at the indicated temperatures 
[ 8 – 18 ], but in the case of temperature increase an acceptable properties 
level is not preserved. 
The generalization of references data [1 – 5,8,19] showed that high- 
temperature strength and heat resistance of vanadium alloys of the V- 
Ti-Cr system are significantly limited by a small volume fraction and 
insufficient thermal stability of the strengthening fine particles of the 
second phase based on interstitial elements. As is known 
[ 8 , 10 , 13 , 19 – 24 ], an increase in the performance characteristics of va -
nadium alloys is ensured by an increase in the efficiency of disperse 
strengthening due to a change in the elemental composition and the use 
of new processing methods that make it possible to form a high volume 
fraction of uniformly distributed fine nonmetallic particles with a high 
thermal stability. 
In [ 19 , 25 ], based on this approach, we obtained the first results on 
the creation of alloys of V-Me(Cr, W)-Zr- С system by alloying them with 
interstitial (C, N, O) and substitution (W, Zr) elements. 
In this work, we have carried out a comparative study of mechanical 
properties characteristics, fracture features and thermal stability of 
structural-phase state of disperse-strengthened vanadium alloys of V-Me 
(Cr, W)-Zr- С system at different temperatures, depending on thermo -
mechanical treatment regimes and stabilizing annealing temperature.

4. Discussion 
A comparative study of the structural-phase state and the level of 
mechanical properties of V – Cr – W – Zr and V – Cr – Zr alloys makes it 
possible to reveal the main factors that determine the strengthening 
effects, among which: the effect of tungsten, the volume fraction and 
dispersion of second phase particles, parameters of grain and defect 
structure. 
In [ 19 ], as a result of studying vanadium alloys with a similar 
elemental composition, it was shown that alloying with tungsten 
significantly reduces the size and density of metastable vanadium car -
bide particles both in the bulk and at the grain boundaries. According to 
[ 31 ], a similar effect in carbide-strengthened niobium alloys is associ -
ated with a decrease in the rate of particle release, which is due to the 
need for a diffusion-controlled exit of tungsten from the region of their 
formation, witch has significantly higher activation energy of migration 
compared to carbon.

0.17%, and for V – Cr – Zr alloy ≈ 0.08%. As is known [ 28 ], the magnitude

5. Conclusions 
Transformation of the initial heterophase structure of V – Cr – W – Zr 
and V – Cr – Zr alloys under TMT-II conditions by the mechanism of car -
bidization from internal sources, by dissolving the initial metastable 
vanadium carbides with the subsequent segregation of a stable phase 
from a solid solution, provides a significant (up to 40%) increase in 
strength properties ( σ
0,1
) while maintaining acceptable values of plas -
ticity. In addition, modification of the structural-phase state under these 
conditions contributes to an increase in the thermal stability of these 
alloys. 
Among the main factors that provide a higher level of mechanical 
properties of V – Cr – W – Zr alloy compared to V – Cr – Zr alloy, one can 
single out: the effect of tungsten, more effective disperse strengthening 
due to the high content of Zr and C, the parameters of the grain and 
defect structure. 
Regardless of the TMT regimes, at all tension temperatures, the 
formation of a cup relief is observed, indicating a viscous transcrystal -
line type of fracture. An increase in the tension temperature is 
accompanied by an increase in the cell size. A consequence of the high 
uniformity of the volume distribution of nanosized particles based on 
ZrC after TMT-II is the strengthening of grain boundaries. 
The elemental composition of alloys of the V – Me (Cr, W) – Zr system 
predetermines the precipitation of thermodynamically more stable fine 
particles of the nonmetallic phase in the TMT process as compared to 
alloys of the V-4Ti-4Cr system.
