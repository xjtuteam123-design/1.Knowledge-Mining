INTRODUCTION 
In our earlier study [1] we showed that modification of 
high-temperature alloys with hafnium or zirconium can af- 
fect substantially the composition, sizes, shape, amount, and 
distribution of such structural components of the metal as 
carbides, the 7'-phase, and the eutectic (7 + 7')-P hase. The 
mechanical properties and the high-temperature strength of 
the alloys change accordingly. It is of interest to assess the 
influence of the addition of optimurn amounts of Hf and Zr 
on the structure and properties of high-temperature nickel 
alloys. 
In the present work we studied the effect of individual 
and joint alloying of nickel alloys ZhS6U-VI and 
ZhS6DK-VI with hafnium and zirconium on the structure 
and properties of castings. 
METHODS OF STUDY 
The alloys were fabricated by the method of remelting a 
measured preform in a UPPF-3M vacuum induction furnace 
in a 15-kg crucible with a basic lining. 10-30 sec before 
casting the metal, heated to 1600~ weighed portions of 
Hf-Ni and Zr-Ni alloying sets were added to it through 
the charging device of the furnace. Then the melt was poured 
into heated ceramic molds, fabricated by the method of pre- 
cision casting using melting-off models, to obtain pin speci- 
mens 12 mm in dialneter.
