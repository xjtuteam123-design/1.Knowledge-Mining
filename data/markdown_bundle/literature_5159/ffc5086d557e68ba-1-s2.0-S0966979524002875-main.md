1. Introduction
The emergence of high-entropy alloys （ HEA ） has provided new 
ideas for material design and opened up another important field of high- 
end alloy materials, which has attracted wide attention in recent years 
[ 1 – 8 ]. CrCoNi medium entropy alloy with face centered cubic （ FCC ） 
structure has excellent deformation ability and low temperature 
ductility [ 9 , 10 ], However, the yield strength of as-cast CrCoNi medium 
entropy alloy is less than 350 MPa, which leads to its limited application 
in production. Microalloying method can effectively improve the 
strength of CrCoNi medium entropy alloy. Currently, the main types of 
added elements in microalloying reported are Al, Ti, Mo, W, etc [ 11 – 18 ]. 
For micro-alloyed cast CrCoNi base medium alloy, rolling deformation is 
usually used to improve the strength of the alloy, Subsequently, the 
rolled MEA is then treated with an annealing process to improve the 
ductility of the alloy. Wani et [ 12 ] studied the effect of Al content on the 
strength and ductility of CrCoNi medium alloy, and found that with the 
increase of Al content, the strength of the alloy increased, but the 
plasticity decreased significantly, because the alloy underwent a phase 
transition from FCC to BCC. chen et al. [ 19 ] studied the effect of Ti 
addition on the microstructure and mechanical properties of CrCoNi 
medium entropy alloy, and found that the strengthening effect of Ti 
element on CrCoNi alloy was not obvious, Ni

times and then cooled to room temperature to get button ingot. The 
button ingot was cut by electric spark wire into 3 mm thin slices. The 
alloy was deformed by three passes of rolling at room temperature
