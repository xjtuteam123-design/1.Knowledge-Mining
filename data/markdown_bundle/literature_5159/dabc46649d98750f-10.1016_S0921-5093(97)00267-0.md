Abstract 
The relation between strength for 1 and 2% creep strain and creep rupture strength has been developed and verified. 
Analogously, the relation between steady-state creep rate and time to specific creep strain has been found for formed Ni-base 
alloys and austenitic steels of types NiCrlSFe, NiCr28FeSiCe, Cr25Ni20Si, CdONilOSi and Crl8Nil lTi, and materials in the cast 
variants, Cr20Ni30TiAl and Cr16Ni35Si. 0 1997 Elsevier Science S.A. 
Keywords: High-temperature creep and rupture; Ni-base alloys and austenitic steels 
1. Introduction 
A specific creep strain of different machine parts 
running at high temperatures is often the more impor- 
tant life-time limiting factor than the creep rupture 
strength. To help designers, the analysis of creep curves 
was therefore proposed, aimed at finding more infor- 
mation from creep tests. 
The correlation between steady-state creep rates and 
time to rupture is known. It is possible to expect also 
an analogous relation between steady-state creep rate 
and time to specific creep strain. Besides this, some 
relation between strength for specific creep strain and 
creep rupture strength could also exist. 
We have analyzed these problems when testing some 
Ni-base alloys and austenitic steels of types NiCrlSFe 
and NiCr28FeSiCe, and formed steels Cr25Ni20Si, 
Cr20NilOSi and Crl8NillTi (after solution annealing) 
and materials in the cast variants, Cr20Ni30TiAl and 
Cr16Ni35Si [l].
