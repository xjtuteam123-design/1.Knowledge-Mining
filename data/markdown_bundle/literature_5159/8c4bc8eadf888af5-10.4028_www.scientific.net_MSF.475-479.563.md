Keywords: titanium alloys, basic theory, near beta forging 
Abstract: China pays great attentions to the development of titanium alloys and their basic theory because of 
their excellent properties. New titanium alloys and their new basic theories developed in China in recent five 
years were reviewed, for example, high temperature Ti alloys, burn resistant titanium alloys, high strength and 
middle strength titanium alloys and so on. The developing directions in the next 5 to 10 years were forecast. 
Introduction 
Titanium and its alloys have been widely used because of their low density, high specific strength, corrosion 
resistance, weldability and so on. After more than 40 years arduous efforts, China forms an integral titanium 
industry system. At present, there are more than 100 units in researching and manufacturing titanium alloys. 
The main units among them are Northwest Institute for Nonferrous Metal Research (NIN), Beijing Institute 
of Aerospace Materials (BIAM), Institute of Metals Research (IMR), Beijing General Institute for Nonferrous 
Metal Research (BGINMR), Baoji Nonferrous Metal works (BNMW), Shanghai Bao Steel Group and 
Northwestern Polytechnical University (NPU). Six main researching directions for titanium alloys have been 
formed, i.e. high temperature titanium alloys, high strength titanium alloys, corrosion resistant titanium alloys, 
titanium alloys used for shipbuilding, functional titanium alloys and medical titanium alloys. More than 50 
titanium alloys were developed, and many alloys have been put into practical uses, which make important 
economy efficiency. The objective of this paper is to review the new Ti alloys and new basic theories 
developed in China in recent five years, and the developing directions in the next 5 to 10 years were 
proposed.

New basic theories 
Basic theory is an important basis to developed new materials. Researchers and engineers engaged in 
titanium alloys in China pay great attentions to the development of titanium theory. 
Effects of rare earth elements (RE). High temperature titanium alloys developed in China almost all have 
minor RE, as shown in Table 1. RE has important role in high temperature Ti alloys as followings: 
1) To refine the microstructure, leading to strengthening and toughening; 
2) To form the REyOx second phases, leading to Orowan strengthening; 
3) To be inner-oxidation and to retard the precipitation of Ti3Al phase, resulting in improved properties; 
4) To increase β transus with addition of Y , resulting in wide working window. 
For example, Ti600 alloy with addition of 0.1Y(w.t%) exhibit refined β transformed α platelet structures, 
which result in the increase of yield stress. The higher tensile ductility of Ti600 is the result of smaller β grain 
size. Y2O3 particles purify α matrix by absorbing O and Sn atom, restrain the precipitation and growth of α2 
phase during thermal exposure. These lead to the improvement of thermal stability of the alloys with Y . The 
improved creep performance of Ti600 alloy has relation to the shorter dislocation slip length. Small amount 
of Y oxides and slow growth of α2 phase have extra contributions to keeping the alloy lower creep 
deformation.

Burn resistant mechanism. The most important characteristic of burn resistant titanium alloy is its burn 
resistance. The burning behavior and its affecting factors of titanium alloys were deeply and systematically 
researched by DCSB (direct current simulation burning) method that was invented by the present authors in 
NIN. Samples of 50mm×5mm×2mm were machined. These samples were ignited by the DCSB method, in 
which the titanium was ignited for a certain time under a certain direct current (5A direct current was used to 
ignite titanium in 50s). After burning, their weight gains were calculated. The burning products were 
examined by scanning electron microscopy (SEM). Ti40 alloy has good burn resistance compared with 
conventional Ti alloys. Burn resistant model was proposed. Tenacious oxidation layer was formed at the 
interface between burning products and the matrices during burning compared with Ti-6-4, as shown in Fig.4. 
Burn resistant mechanisms of fast dispersive heat and suspend oxygen diffusion were put forward, which 
gave a good explanation on the burn resistant behavior of Ti40 alloy. 
Oxidation mechanism. Ti40 alloy has lots of alloying element V that leads to serious oxidation at high 
temperature. However, Ti40 alloy will be used up to 540 °C, therefore its oxidation behavior at high 
temperature were systematically studied. The main reason that Ti40 alloy does not possess good oxidation 
resistance over 650°C high temperature is as follows: i) protective Al
2O3 and Cr2O3 oxides cannot be formed; 
ii) porous oxides selectively form from Ti and V; iii) the vaporization of V2O5. Therefore, its oxidation 
mechanism model was proposed. 
In the process of the oxidation of Ti40 a mixture of V
2O5 and TiO2 will be formed selectively on the surface of 
the oxidized sample. Evaporation of V2O5 leads to the formation of pores within the oxidized layer. With 
increasing of the temperature, SiO compound will be formed within the oxidized layer and occupies the pores 
left by the evaporation of V2O5. Non-continuous Cr2O3 film appears also within the oxidized layer, which 
cannot impede diffusion of oxygen into the matrix, and so cannot the vanadium and silicon elements. At the 
early stage of oxidation, scales have good adherence because of the plasticity of itself. However, the plastic 
deformation of scale is very limited. With increase of thickness, cracks appear within the oxidized layer, 
especially at the edges and corners of the oxidized samples, and outer scales will flake off, and pluck up 
eventually. 
Creep mechanism of high temperature titanium alloy. Factors influencing alloy’s creep mechanism are 
complicated. It has inevitable connection with temperature, load method, alloying composition, precipitates of 
second phase and so on. The creep mechanism of 600°C high temperature Ti-alloy (Ti600) with different 
temperature was deeply studied by means of analyzing the law of effects of pre-strain at high temperature on 
alloy’s creep properties. We discovered that its creep active energy changes greatly near 600°C. At the mean 
time, its pre-strain creep active energy also alters obviously at this temperature. This reveals that its creep 
processes are controlled by different mechanisms above 600°C and below 600°C, respectively. When creep 
temperatures are between 550°C and 600°C. The alloy’s creep activity energy is low (<100KJ/mol), which is 
near to that of Ti self-diffusion activity energy and/or that of self-diffusion activity energy of solution elements 
in Ti, which indicates that the creep deformation is mainly controlled by the interaction between dislocations 
and solution elements. While temperature increasing near to 600°C, there is dislocation climbing and 
cross-sliding, and on the other hand, the creep at this temperature is still controlled by α/β grain boundary 
sliding. At the mean time, the second particles have important key roles: i) α
2 (Ti3Al) retards the dislocation 
sliding; ii) Ti5Si3 retards α/β grain boundary sliding. This reveals that the strengthening mechanism changes 
from solution strengthening to precipitation strengthening with an increase of temperature. 
Tri-modal microstructure. It is obvious that the mechanical properties of Titanium alloys vary with the 
variation of the relative volume fraction of equiaxed α and transformed β. It was found that only when the 
heating temperature was close enough to beta transus, the relative volume fraction of equiaxed alpha and 
transformed beta varied significantly, and could be effectively controlled by controlling the heat temperature. 
This is the theoretical and experimental fundamental of near beta forging. That is, the new high-temperature 
deformation strengthening and toughening process consists of heating at 10~15
0C below beta transus, rapid

Titanium alloys forged by this new process do not produce equiaxed microstructure, lamellar or bi-modal one,

equiaxed alpha ensures coherent deformation, which retards the formation and propagation of voids, 
presenting a higher ductility. The interwoven lamellar alpha, which formed by water quenching after forging 
and high-temperature toughening treatment, not only retards slip deformation, but also changes the direction 
of the crack propagation and slows it down. For this reason, tri-modal microstructures have a good

Forecast for the next 5 to 10 years development of titanium alloys 
According to users and self-development of Ti alloys, the future directions for titanium development in the 
next 5 to 10 years are as follows: 
1)high strength and high toughness titanium alloys; 2)new high temperature titanium alloys (over 600°C) and 
high performance TiAl alloys; 3)special functional titanium alloys (such as burn resistant titanium alloys, 
cryogenic titanium alloys, shape memory alloys and so on); 4)high performance titanium based composites; 
5)manufacturing technology for low cost titanium alloys; 6)titanium alloy shapes and large forgings; 7)new 
manufacturing technology and equipment; 8)computer modeling for 
design-melting-process-properties-microstructure and monitoring on line. 
Summary 
After more than 40 years arduous efforts, titanium alloys developed in China achieved great development. 
Many achievements have been gotten from the research on new titanium alloys and basic theories. However, 
the cost of titanium alloys is still high, we should pay great attentions to reduce their ratio of property to price 
and enlarge their applications by developing new technology and new equipment.
