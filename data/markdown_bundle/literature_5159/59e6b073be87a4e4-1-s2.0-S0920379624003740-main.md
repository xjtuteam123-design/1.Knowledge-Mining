1. Introduction 
Due to the harsh working environment inside nuclear fusion, espe -
cially the influence of high temperature radiation, the selection of the 
first wall material is very high. So far, only tungsten metal has been able 
to meet this basic requirement. Tungsten has superior properties and is 
suitable for applications in high temperature environments. Tungsten 
metal has high melting point, high thermal conductivity, low thermal 
expansion, high temperature strength, low sputtering rate, low tritium 
retention and so on [ 1 – 3 ]. These superior properties also enable tung -
sten metal to act as a diverter for applications in plasma armor and 
structural materials [ 4 – 6 ]. However, in the harsh working environment, 
the problems faced by metal tungsten are low temperature brittleness to 
ductility transition temperature and neutron radiation resistance. The 
experiment shows that tungsten transmutates into rhenium under 
neutron radiation [ 7 ], tungsten and rhenium alloyed to form solid 
solution, which can decrease the tough-brittle transition temperature, 
improve the ductility of W metal and enhance its radiation resistance [ 8 , 
9 ]. In fact, the study of the metal tungsten has aroused the interest of 
scientific researchers. In the course of the research, the researchers 
proposed that the purpose of modifying the properties of tungsten doped 
with rare earth elements may be a better research method. For example, 
Jiang and his team used rare earth elements V, Ti, Hf, Co, Cu, Ni, Fe, Mn, 
Cr, Zr, Tc, Ta, Re , Mo to alloyed tungsten, and found that the ductility of 
W could be improved by these elements [ 10 – 22 ]. The effect of Mn, Fe 
and Cr at high temperatures on the mechanical strength, thermal con -
ductivity, and coefficient of thermal expansion were also discussed 
[ 16 – 18 ]. It is found that as the Mn content increased, the mechanical 
strength of W-Mn alloy rapidly decays at high temperatures; the coef -
ficient of thermal expansion increases rapidly, and shows abnormal 
thermal conductivity [ 16 ]. When Fe content is 6.25at.%, W-Fe alloy 
shows ultrahigh temperature alloy material characteristics [ 17 ].

temperature mechanical and thermodynamic properties of W-Ru alloys, 
and a physical structure model is established. The elastic property, 
Cauchy pressure, Poisson ’ s ratio, hardness and melting point, anisot -
ropy, Debye temperature, heat capacity, entropy and free energy are 
investigated. The focus is on investigating the changes at high temper -
atures for the bulk modulus, equilibrium volume, thermal conductivity, 
and coefficient of thermal expansion. Through the optimization design 
of W-Ru alloys with different components, it offers a useful reference for 
its wide application in nuclear fusion and the experimental research.

3.1. Crystal structure properties 
The four models in Fig. 1 still keep the cubic structure after opti -
mization. Ru elements tend to sit in uniformly ordered W lattice posi -
tions, and it shows that the atomic solid solutions can be formed by W 
and Ru elements.

increasing Ru content. The explanation is that the atomic radius for W is

obtained results are reliable. The bond length can reflect the strength of
