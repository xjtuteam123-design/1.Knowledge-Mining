2. Experimental
Nb-22Si-20Ti- x Mo alloys with different Mo contents ( x = 0, 2, 4, 6, 
8 at%, denoted as NST-0Mo, NST-2Mo, NST-4Mo, NST-6Mo and NST- 
8Mo, respectively) were prepared using non-self-consuming vacuum 
arc furnace in an argon environment. Each ingot was flipped and melted 
for five times to achieve ingots with homogeneous chemical composi -
tion. Identical melting parameters, such as arc current, arc voltage, 
argon pressure, circulating water pressure and vacuum degree were 
applied for melting process of each ingot.
Electro-discharge machining (EDM) was used to prepare samples for 
microstructure observation and mechanical property measurement. 
Samples for observing microstructures were ground with sandpaper 
from 180#-2000# and then mechanically polished to mirror surface 
condition. Phases of different alloys were determined by X-ray diffrac -
tion (XRD, Panalytical X ′ Pert, Cu K α , 20KV, 20 – 90
◦
, 8
◦
/min). Micro -
structure observation was conducted by scanning electron microscopy 
(SEM, FEI, Quanta 200FEG) in backscattering (BSE) mode. Chemical 
compositions of different phases were analyzed by Energy dispersive 
spectroscopy (EDS, INCA x-sight 7426). Volume fraction of phases and 
average diameter of the primary phase were calculated by Image-Pro 
Plus 6.0 software.
Compression experiments were conducted at room temperature 
using Instron 5569 Mechanical Tester at a loading rate of 0.2 mm/min. 
Three samples with dimensional size of φ 4 mm × 6 mm from each ingot 
were tested and the average was taken as the compression strength of 
the alloy. Microhardness of phases in Nb-22Si-20Ti- x Mo alloys was 
measured by FM-ARS900 hardness tester under force of 100 gf for 10 s. 
Microhardness of each phase was tested for five times and the average 
was taken as microhardness of the phase for each alloy.

3.3.2. Compression properties
Compression experiments for Nb-22Si-20Ti- x Mo alloys with various 
addition of Mo were conducted at room temperature, which is shown in 
Fig. 5 . Results show that compressive strength of Nb-22Si-20Ti alloys 
decreases by increasing addition of Mo, whereas fracture strain first 
increase then decrease by increasing addition of Mo. Compressive 
strength of NST-0Mo alloy without addition of Mo is 1972.5 MPa, and 
fracture strain is 7.5 %. Compressive strength of NST-2Mo alloy with 
addition of 2 at% Mo is increased to 2160.2 MPa, which is 9.5 % higher 
than that of NST-0Mo alloy. Compressive strength of NST-6Mo alloy 
with addition of 6 at% Mo is 2261.5 MPa, and the fracture strain reaches 
the maximum of 9.9 %. However, fracture strain of NST-8Mo alloy with 
addition of 8 at% Mo decreases slightly to 9.7 %, while compressive 
strength increases to 2384.2 MPa which is increased by 20.9 % 
compared to that of NST-0Mo alloy. Compressive strength of NST-8Mo 
alloy is slightly higher than that reported by Ma et al. [21] for 
Nb-16Si-5Mo alloy (1625 MPa) and Sala et al. [22] for 
Nb-12Si-20Ti-5Mo alloy (2302 MPa).

(1) Nb-22Si-20Ti alloy is mainly consists of (Nb, X)ss phase, β-(Nb,

(Nb, X)ss/β-(Nb, X)
5

creases with increase of Mo content.
(2) Compressive strength of Nb-Si-Ti alloys at room temperature is 
improved by addition of Mo, which is increased by 20.9 % from 
1972.5 MPa for NST-0Mo alloy to 2384.2 MPa for NST-8Mo alloy. And 
fracture strain is also increased by addition of Mo and it reaches the 
maximum of 9.9 % for NST-6Mo alloy.
(3) Microhardness of phases in the Nb-Si-Ti alloys is increased by 
addition of Mo, which is improved by 14.7 % from 1298.5 HV for NST- 
0Mo alloy to 1489.9 HV for NST-8Mo alloy. And microhardness of 
eutectic structure in the alloys is increased by 11.4 % from 865.1 HV for 
NST-2Mo alloy to 964.1 HV for NST-8Mo alloy.
(4) Strength and microhardness improvement of Nb-Si-Ti alloys is 
contributed to both solid solution strengthening and grain refinement 
strengthening by addition of Mo. While fracture strain improvement is 
related to plasticity improvement caused by increase of (Nb, X)ss phase 
and formation of eutectic structure is higher than plasticity decrease 
cause by solid solution hardening.
