Molecular dynamics (MD) simulation was carried out to study the dislocation and phase transition of
/SLgamma//SLgamma′phase interface for Ni-based superalloys with twist grain boundary (GB) under uniaxial tension.
The simulation results showed that the density of the dislocation networks at the interface increased
with increasing twist angle. Linear and square types of dislocations were observed at different twist
GB. It has been found that the dislocation networks of the (001) phase interface showed different
degrees and patterns of damage under uniaxial tension. Slip systems of Ni-based s uperalloys were

1. INTRODUCTION
Ni-based superalloys is an alloy that exhibits excel-
lent mechanical strength and resistance to creep at high
temperatures.
1–3 In modern Ni-based superalloys the /SLgamma′
phase (Ni 3Al) acts as a barrier to dislocation motion. This
/SLgamma′intermetallic phase, when presents in high volume frac-
tions, drastically increases the strength of these alloys due
to its ordered nature and high coherency with the /SLgamma(Ni)
matrix.
4–7 Because of the lattice mismatch of the /SLgammaand
/SLgamma′ phases, an eigen-stress ﬁeld is presented. Moreover,
there will be a twist GB when Ni-based superalloys are
either at high temperatures, under complex stress, or in
the manufacturing process.
8–10 The inﬂuence of twist GB
plays an important role in the structure evolution of misﬁt
dislocation networks at /SLgamma//SLgamma
′phase interfaces of Ni-based
superalloys.
Atomistic simulations have been widely carried out to
study the interaction between dislocations and GB. Y ashiro
et al.
11−13 discovered the fundamental aspect of disloca-
tions at the /SLgamma//SLgamma′interface using MD simulations. Wu et al. 8
investigated the structural evolution of misﬁt dislocation
networks at /SLgamma//SLgamma′phase interfaces in Ni-based superalloys

4. CONCLUSIONS
In summary, MD simulation has been performed to study
the dislocation and phase transition for Ni-based super-
alloys with twist GB under uniaxial tension. The results
provide some new insights into the mechanical properties
and phase transition on [001] twist GB.
(1) Dislocation network is not the same as twist GB of
Ni-based superalloys with different twist angle. A linear
dislocation network can be observed when the twist angle
is 1.06
/compB. Square types of dislocations are found when the
twist angle is 3.10 /compB,6 . 3 2/compBand 7.85/compB, and the density of
dislocation networks at twist GB increases with increasing
twist angle. Compared with the perfect Ni-based superal-
loys, twist GB have substantially reduced the mechanical
properties. The reason is that the twist GB is determined
by a number of low-energy screw dislocations, the twist
GB can not effectively prevent dislocation glide.
(2) The fcc-to-hcp phase transition have been observed
during the uniaxial tensile processes. The lattices constant
are expanded in the tensile direction of [001], and the lat-
tices are compressed to a certain extent in the directions
of [100] and [010]. Consequently, the lattice constant of
fcc phase is changed into the lattice constant of hcp phase.
