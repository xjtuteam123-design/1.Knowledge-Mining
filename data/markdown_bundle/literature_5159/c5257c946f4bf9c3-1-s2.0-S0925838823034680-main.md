ABSTRACT 
Refractory high-entropy alloys (RHEAs) containing multiple principal alloying elements have created a growing 
interest in exploring superalloys applications due to their high melting points and excellent softening resistance. 
The room-temperature brittleness and oxygen sensitivity greatly restrict their further processing and application. 
Here, utilizing the unique high strength of Vanadium (V) among alloy elements and cocktail effect of HEAs, we 
designed a MoTaVW RHEAs with equal atomic ratio, that exhibit multiphase phases (Ta-rich, Mo-rich and V-rich 
phase) with different composition at different sintering temperatures and the alloy elements in BCC solid solution 
tend to be uniformly distributed as the increased sintering temperature. Exploring the room-temperature 
compressive properties and underlying deformation mechanisms, the MoTaVW RHEAs exhibit the yield 
strength ( σ
y
) of 1420 MPa and the plastic strain ( ε
p
) of 18 %, that achieved good combination of strength and 
plasticity compared with other most known BCC RHEAs, which are attributed to high solid solution strength -
ening caused by lattice distortion and the cross slip of screw dislocations on the close-packed plane that can 
enhance the synergistic effect of strength and plasticity. These results reveal the MoTaVW RHEAs is a very 
promising materials for compositional space in RHEAs. 
1. Introduction 
As the rapid development of high temperature structural materials 
field, the traditional superalloy can no longer meet the practical engi -
neering application [1,2] . It is urgent to develop new high temperature 
metal materials with high performance. In recent decades, refractory 
high entropy alloys (RHEAs) have stimulated increasing interest ever 
since the report of the first example, mostly ascribed to the excellent 
high-temperature mechanical properties and thermal stability [3 – 5] . 
RHEAs are mainly composed of four or more refractory elements (Ta, 
Mo, W, V, Nb, Hf, Ti, Zr, Cr) with equal or near-equal atomic ratios, 
which exhibits unique high-entropy effect, lattice distortion effect, slow 
diffusion effect and cocktail effect. [6] . Unlike traditional alloys systems, 
RHEAs are prone to form single-phase solid solutions rather than 
intermetallic compounds due to the above four effect [7,8] . The typical 
MoNbTaW and MoNbTaVW RHEAs were first developed in 2010 by 
Senkov et al. [9,10] , and their mechanical properties have been studied 
extensively. Studies have shown that the yield strength of MoNbTaW 
and MoNbTaVW RHEAs reached 405 MPa and 477 MPa under 1600 ℃ 
high temperature compression tests, which were much higher than 
traditional Ni alloys [11] . However, the fracture strain of these two al -
loys at room temperature (RT) are only 2.6 % and 1.7 %, which is far 
from satisfying the application requirements. 
In order to improve the comprehensive properties of RHEAs, some 
researchers mainly adjust the elemental composition or optimize the 
preparation method [12] . For example, Han et al. [13] reported that the 
strength and plasticity in MoNbTaTiW RHEAs are improved compared 
with MoNbTaW alloy by introducing Ti element and adjusting the 
content of Ti element. The strength increased from 996 MPa to 1455 
MPa, and the plasticity increased from 1.9 % to 11.5 %. In recent years, a 
variety of RHEAs with excellent mechanical properties have been re -
ported one after another, such as MoNbTaTiW [13] , NbTaVW [14] , 
MoNbTaV [15] , MoNbTaTiV [16,17] , WTaNbCrV [18] and NbTaTiV 
[19,20] . The most representative NbTaTiV alloy has more than 50 % RT 
fracture strain, but the yield strength is only 965 MPa, and the high 
temperature strength is far from enough [19,20] . Through a compre -
hensive comparative study of the strength prediction of solute 
strengthening theory and the experimental results of the reported 
RHEAs, Yin et al. [21] proposed that RHEAs containing element V 
provided highest strengthening and improved compression plasticity

Based on the classical MoNbTaW RHEAs, the paper will further 
adjust the elemental composition to explore novel RHEAs with high 
strength and good RT ductility. Various elements of HEAs have different 
characteristics, the interaction between different elements makes HEAs 
exhibit a composite effect. The “ cocktail effect ” proposed by Indian 
scholar Ranganatha [22] indicates that the role of alloy components at 
the atomic scale will eventually be reflected in the macroscopic 
comprehensive properties of the alloy. It is suggested that Mo and W 
elements with large atomic radius in MoNbTaW RHEAs can improve the 
strength and hardness of alloys, Ta element can improve the plasticity of 
the alloy, while Nb has no obvious improvement on the strength or 
plasticity of the alloy [23 – 25] . Therefore, based on the above benefits of 
V, the novel MoTaVW RHEAs with V instead of Nb in MoNbTaW alloy 
are fabricated and explored in this study. The bulk MoTaVW RHEAs at 
different sintering temperatures were prepared by frozen ball milling 
and spark plasma sintering (SPS), and the microstructure and 
room-temperature (RT) mechanical properties were characterized and 
tested. The corresponding strengthening and deformation mechanism 
were also discussed in depth. 
2. Materials and method 
2.1. Bulk sample preparation 
The high purity ( > 99.95 wt%) Mo (2.0 – 3.0 µ m ), Ta, V ( < 45 µ m ) and 
W(1.0 – 2.0 µ m) powders were mixed with equal atomic ratios and sub -
jected to a refrigerated segmented ball mill in argon atmosphere to avoid 
oxidation. Before ball milling, the mixed powders were subjected to 
ultra-low temperature treatment by liquid nitrogen. The first half of the 
ball milling process was carried out with the 8 mm large radius tungsten 
carbide balls at 250 rpm for 8 h and the ball-powder mass ratio was 10:1. 
Then replace the 5 mm small radius tungsten carbide balls and continue 
to ball mill for 8 h under the same milling process conditions as above. 
After each ball milling, the ball mill tanks were placed in 7 
◦
C low 
temperature cooling water for cooling treatment to avoid overheating. 
The content of the impurity elements (C, H and O) in the raw powders 
(W, Mo, Ta and V) and the as-milled powders were determined by LECO 
TCH 600 analyzer, the results are listed in Table 1 . It is noted that the 
content of C, H and O increased slightly after ball milling, indicating that 
the impurity elements in the air were introduced in the mechanical 
alloying process, but they were all within the acceptable experimental 
error range. 
Then the milled MoTaVW powders were placed in a 20 mm diameter 
graphite mold and subsequently sintered by SPS (HPD25, Germany FCT 
System Co., Ltd.). The specific sample preparation process is shown in 
Fig. 1 . Under the vacuum condition with axial pressure of 40 MPa, the 
mixed MoTaVW powders were sintered for 10 min at 1600 ℃ , 1650 ℃ , 
1700 ℃ , 1750 ℃ and 1800 ℃ with the heating rate of 100 ℃ /min. 
Completing sintering and cooling to below 100 ℃ , the cylindrical 
samples with diameter of 20 mm and height of 7 mm are obtained by 
pressure relief.

3.2. Microstructural evolution 
Fig. 3 presents the backscattered electrons (BSE) images and corre -
sponding element composition carried out by SEM-EDS and EPMA of 
MoTaVW RHEAs under different sintering temperatures. Different from 
other RHEAs with few phases, the study found that the alloy contains 
three different phases. It must be noted that phase identification is 
performed based on the compositional differences. As shown in Fig. 3 
(a), MoTaVW RHEAs are basically composed with three different phases 
that are visible as clouds, i.e dark gray (marked by V-rich), light gray 
(marked by Mo-rich) and “ brighter ” white (marked by Ta-rich) area, 
similar phase compositions also been reported in other RHEAs research 
[31] . The qualitatively implies that the white area may be enriched in 
elements with more electrons. The black spots were detected as micro -
pores generated during the sintering process. In the Fig. 3 (b – e), as the 
sintering temperature gradually increases, the dark gray area dissipates 
into white and light gray phase. 
When the sintering temperature exceeds 1700 ℃ , the different 
phases with different colors tend to be homogeneous, but the very small 
color difference can still be seen, indicting the obvious composition 
segregation is gradually weakened. As shown in Fig. 3 (e), the element 
distribution is relatively uniform and no obvious segregation can be 
seen. The formation of this polyphase is mainly due to the distribution of 
component elements in the process of solid solution and the slow 
diffusion effect of HEA, which will inhibit the diffusion/migration of 
atoms and hinder the formation of new phases in the alloy. The higher 
the temperature, the faster the diffusion rate of atoms, the more 
conducive to the formation of solid solution. At the same time, these 
micropores also disappeared. The relative density values ( ρ experiment/ 
ρ theory) of MoTaVW RHEAs sintered from 1600 ℃ to 1800 ℃ are is 
95.4 %, 97.7 %, 99.1 %, 99.5 % and 99,7 %, respectively, indicating that 
the densification is basically completed at 1700 ℃ . 
Fig. 4 displays the inverse polar figure diagrams (IPF) and corre -
sponding grain size distribution histograms in the electron backscatter 
diffraction (EBSD) maps of the MoTaVW RHEA sintered at different 
temperatures. The IPF figure ( Fig. 4 (a – d)) shows that the grain shape is 
irregular and the grain orientation is random, different colors corre -
spond to different crystal faces. As shown in Fig. 4 (e – h), the average 
grain sizes of the MoTaVW RHEA sintered at 1650 ℃ , 1700 ℃ , 1750 ℃ 
and 1800 ℃ are 2.12 µ m, 2.82 µ m , 3.16 µ m and 4.59 µ m respectively, 
which is positively correlated with the sintering temperature. And the 
small grains were observed to be enveloping large grains. With the in -
crease of sintering temperature and solid solubility of the solutes, the 
super-large grains and super-fine grains gradually disappear, finally the 
equiaxed grains with uniform grain size distribution are formed.

attributed to facilitated cross-slip along with the related promotion of 
dislocation nucleation and propagation. Under the action of external 
force, the soft-oriented grains in the crystal preferentially slip, resulting 
in the plastic deformation, and the dislocations form a plug group at the 
grain boundary. The cross slip of dislocation and its related dislocation 
nucleation and expansion make the slip transfer from bulk grains, a large 
number of grains slip, and the plastic deformation of metal is obvious. 
The ductility of materials is mainly determined by the proliferation 
ability of dislocations during deformation. 
4. Conclusion 
In this paper, a new MoTaVW RHEA with excellent strength and 
plasticity was designed and successfully prepared. The microstructure 
and mechanical properties of the alloy were systematically studied. 
According to the research analysis of the above content, the following 
conclusions can be drawn: 
(1) Different from other RHEAs with few phases, the MoTaVW 
RHEAs with equal atomic ratio exhibits multiphase phases (Ta- 
rich, Mo-rich and V-rich phase) with different composition at 
different sintering temperatures and the alloy elements in BCC 
solid solution tend to be uniformly distributed as the increased 
sintering temperature. 
(2) The yield strength ( σ
y
) and plastic strain ( ε
p
) of MoTaVW RHEA 
sintered at 1700
◦
C are 1420 MPa and 18 %, which achieved 
good combination of high strength and high plasticity compared 
with other most known BCC RHEAs. 
(3) The high yield strength of the MoTaVW RHEA was mainly due to 
the high solid solution strengthening caused by lattice distortion 
and grain boundary strengthening, accounting for 90 % and 10 
%. The solid solution strengthening plays a dominant role in 
MoTaVW RHEA. 
(4) The plastic deformation process of MoTaVW RHEA is dominated 
by wavy slip, and the cross slip of screw dislocations on the close- 
packed plane enhances the synergistic effect of strength and 
plasticity.
