2. Experimental 
The designed alloy ingots were prepared by arc melting and suction- 
cast into a 6 mm-diameter cylindrical copper mold under an argon at -
mosphere. The purities of raw metals are 99.999 wt % for Al, 99.99 wt % 
for Ni, Co, Cr, and Ti, and 99.95 wt % for Mo, Ta, Nb, and W, respec -
tively. These ingots were re-melted at least five times to ensure 
composition uniformity. Both alloy ingots and rods were sealed into a 
vacuum quartz glass tube and subjected to a sequence of heat treat -
ments. Alloy specimens were first solid-solutionized at 1573 K for 15 h, 
and then aged at 1173 K for different time up to 500 h. Each heat 
treatment was followed by a water quenching.
