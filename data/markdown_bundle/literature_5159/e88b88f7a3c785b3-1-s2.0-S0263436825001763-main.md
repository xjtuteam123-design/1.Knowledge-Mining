1. Introduction
In the past few years, because of the development of technology in 
high-end and specialization direction, engine materials have developed 
rapidly. Many high temperature resistant materials have been developed 
and studied [ 1 ]. However, Ni based superalloy has been difficult to meet 
the temperature requirements of new engine turbine blades because of 
their high density and limited operating temperature [ 2 – 5 ]. In order to 
improve the inlet temperature of the turbine engine and the space en -
gine propulsion, researchers hope to develop a new type of high tem -
perature resistant material system.

the most important problem in the field of Nb-Si alloy research.
Alloying is a low-cost method. Researchers have added various 
element to Nb-Si superalloys, such as Al [ 14 ], Ti [ 15 ], Hf [ 16 ], Ta [ 17 ],

2. Experimental procedures
In this experiment, pure Nb particles, pure Si particles, pure Zr 
particles, pure Cr particles, pure Hf particles, pure C powder and pure Sc 
wire which was sealed by vacuum tube were used as raw materials to 
prepare alloy ingots by non-consumable vacuum arc furnace. When C 
powder was added, a small amount of Al foil was used to wrap it. We put 
the raw materials into the water-cooled copper crucible of the electric 
arc furnace and vacuumed the electric arc furnace. The raw materials 
are melted by the electric arc gun equipped with the equipment, and the 
melting is repeated five times. After the last melting, the melted alloy 
ingots are obtained after the equipment is cooled. The nominal 
composition of the alloys are Nb-16Si-20Zr- x m( x m = 0, 4Cr, 2C, 4Hf, 
4Ta, 0.5Sc). For the convenience of description, they are referred to as 
NSZ, NSZ-Cr, NSZ-C, NSZ-Hf, NSZ-Ta and NSZ-Sc. The heat treatment 
process is carried out on Nb-16Si-20Zr- x m alloy. The heat treatment 
furnace was heated from room temperature (25 
◦
C) and the heat rate 
was set to 5 
◦
C/min. When the temperature rises to 1300 
◦
C, the Nb- 
16Si-20Zr- x m ingots isolated by the buried sand method were put into 
the heat treatment furnace. After the heat treatment was carried out for 
30 h, the heat treatment furnace was closed and the alloy ingots were 
cooled with the furnace. “ AC ” represents the as-cast Nb-Si superalloys. 
“ HT ” represents the Nb-Si superalloys after heat treatment.

Panalytical Analytical Instruments Company of Netherlands to carry out
