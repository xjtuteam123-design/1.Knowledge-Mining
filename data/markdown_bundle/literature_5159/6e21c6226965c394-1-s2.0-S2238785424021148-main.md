ABSTRACT
Controlling the evolution process of columnar grains is benefits to achieve microstructure regulation during 
subsequent hot processing in superalloys. In present research, it takes a Ni – Co – W superalloy as an example, aims 
to clarify the underlying connections between the compression direction and dynamic recrystallization (DRX) 
behaviors in microstructure evolution. The compression direction (CD) was parallel or perpendicular to [001] 
columnar has been defined as CD ‖ [001] and CD ⊥ [001], respectively. The columnar evolution and DRX char -
acteristics under two sets of experimental during hot deformation were identified deeply. The results show that 
complete DRX is more easily to occur when CD ⊥ [001], but fine DRX grains are tendency to form when CD ‖
[001]. DRX nucleation within CD ‖ [001] and CD ⊥ [001] deformed microstructure under dislocation energy was 
discussed deeply. The critical size of nucleation is decreased while the nucleation density is increased in CD ‖
[001] with high dislocation density, which benefits to form numerous fine DRX grains along the original 
columnar boundaries. In addition, according to Taylor factors (TFs) criterion, TFs difference will always existed 
in CD ‖ [001], which promotes the necklace structure gradually replaced columnar structure and some of them 
developed into fine DRX bands. With increasing of trues strain, TFs difference gradually decreased in CD ⊥ [001], 
DRX nucleation was inhibited, thus the existed DRX grains further grow and finally coarse DRX grains were 
obtained. The findings clarified the flow behaviors and DRX characteristics of [001] columnar in two directions, 
and then proposed a microstructure control mechanism of superalloys with [001] columnar based on defor -
mation vector and evolution decomposition.
1. Introduction
Ni-based superalloy are widely used in the manufacture of aero- 
engine turbine discs due to their excellent mechanical properties and 
good oxidation resistant under extreme service environment of high- 
temperature and high-load [ 1 – 4 ]. With the development of 
aero-engines to high thrust-to-weight ratio, the predesign temperature 
of the disc superalloys gradually increases until it exceeds 850 
◦
C, which 
is significantly affected by the content of alloying element [ 5 , 6 ]. How -
ever, excessive degree of alloying leads to that the hot deformation 
temperature window is narrow and DRX is difficult. In addition, the 
addition of high content of alloy elements will result in serious segre -
gation and residual as-cast microstructure, which shows a negative 
impact on subsequent hot processing [ 7 – 9 ]. Therefore, the microstruc -
ture control mechanism during hot deformation in Ni – Co – W superalloys 
with columnar grains needs to be investigated systematically.
Some research on Ni-based superalloys, especially for as-forged bil -
lets with equiaxed grain, covering the deformation behavior as well as 
microstructure evolution have been conducted [ 10 – 13 ]. Due to the 
lower stacking fault energy (SFE) of Ni-based superalloys, DRX is the 
dominant mechanism that control the microstructure evolution during 
hot-mechanical processing (TMP) [ 14 , 15 ]. The relationship between 
DRX and improving grain structure, reducing defects of superalloys, 
then enhancing their mechanical properties has attracted extensive 
attention [ 16 – 18 ]. Then, DRX behaviors and nucleation mechanisms of 
Ni-based superalloys during hot deformation were focused on. For 
polycrystalline superalloys, consist of grains and different grain junc -
tions, DRX nuclei usually prefer to form first at grain junctions, and then 
dislocation-free nuclei grow up by consuming dislocation energy [ 19 ,
20 ]. This two-step process, i.e. nucleation and growth of the nuclei is 
discontinuous dynamic recrystallization (DDRX), its important feature is 
that necklace-type small DRX grains occurred along the original grain

during CD ‖ [001] and ⊥ [001] were identified by the Electron 
back-scattered diffraction (EBSD). The DRX nucleation and micro -
structure control mechanisms in CD ‖ [001] and CD ⊥ [001] according to 
dislocation energy and Taylor factors (TFs) criterion was discussed.

3. Results
3.1. Plastic flow behaviors of columnar grains during CD ‖ [001] and CD ⊥
[001]
The effect of compression direction on hot deformation behaviors are 
shown in Fig. 2 . Both curves present three-stage characteristics, with an 
initial stage of work hardening (Stage I), followed by dynamic softening 
(Stage II: dynamic recovery (DRV) and dynamic recrystallization (DRX)) 
and steady state stage (Stage III: dynamic balance of work hardening and 
dynamic softening). The true stress increasing rapidly at stage I is 
attributed to the generation and multiplication of dislocations [ 33 , 34 ]. 
In addition, it can be seen that the critical strain with CD ‖ [001] is 0.06, 
was significantly higher than that in CD ⊥ [001] (0.03). It means that the 
onset of DRX may easier when compression direction was perpendicular 
to the growth direction of columnar grains. The peak flow stress of CD ‖
[001] are 139.1 MPa, whereas that of CD ⊥ [001] only reached 125.4 
MPa. The flow stress increased obviously is possibly related to the 
evolution of columnar grains was impeded in CD ‖ [001].
With increasing of the true strain, the flow stress gradually 
decreased, which indicates the work hardening is partially counteracted 
by dynamic softening. In addition, the onset of DRV by dislocation cross- 
slip and climb is difficult due to the low SFE in Ni-based superalloys [ 22 ,
24 ]. Therefore, DRX is the dominant dynamic softening mechanism in 
stage II. With further increase of true strain, the flow stress decreased to 
a stable value in Stage III due to the dynamic balance of work hardening 
and dynamic softening.

4. Discussion
4.1. DRX nucleation in CD ‖ [001] and CD ⊥ [001] under dislocations 
energy
As mentioned in section 3 , when the true strain is 0.11, the original 
columnar grain still existed within CD ‖ [001] and CD ⊥ [001] samples. In 
this process, the original columnar grain boundaries can provide lots of 
nucleation sites for DRX, and then new DRX grains formed, as shown in 
Figs. 6 (a) and Fig. 8 (a). This recrystallization nucleation mechanism is 
called as the sub-grain-induced nucleation. In addition, during hot 
deformation, the columnar grains gradually replaced by DRX grains, and

induced sub-boundary. Moreover, the Strain Induced Boundary Migra -
tion (SIBM) recrystallization nucleation mechanism is closely related to 
the dislocation density difference [ 35 – 37 ]. Zurob et al. [ 38 ] proposed 
the following sub-grain growth model to describe the process of 
sub-grain-induced nucleation and SIBM.

5. Conclusions
In this study, the effect of compression direction on DRX behaviors in 
microstructure evolution of a Ni – Co – W superalloy during hot defor -
mation at 1140 
◦
C with various true strain was analyzed. The columnar 
grain evolution and DRX grains characteristics of CD ‖ [001] and CD ⊥
[001] samples under various deformation conditions was identified. 
Then, a microstructure control mechanism of superalloys with [001] 
columnar based on deformation vector and evolutionary decomposition 
was proposed. The conclusions are as follows:
(1) Compression direction (CD) has great influence on microstruc -
ture evolution of [001] columnar grain in Ni – Co – W superalloys, 
and the compression deformation of columnar grain can usually 
be decomposed into CD ‖ [001] and CD ⊥ [001]. When compres -
sion direction was parallel to [001] orientation, columnar grain 
gradually deformed along CD, and part of columnar grain 
boundaries will fold toward the center of samples, which pro -
motes large number of new fine DRX grains formed. While for 
CD ⊥ [001], new DRX grains preferential formed at original grain 
boundaries, completed DRX is more easily to occur with 
increasing of the true strain.
(2) The dislocation density within CD ‖ [001] columnar is higher than 
that of CD ⊥ [001]. Higher dislocation density promote DRX 
nucleation and inhibited grain boundary movement. The critical 
size of nucleation is decreased while the nucleation density is 
increased in CD ‖ [001] with high dislocation density, which 
benefits to form numerous fine DRX grains along the original 
columnar boundaries. The dislocation density has obviously 
decreased in CD ⊥ [001], which strongly weakens the resistance of 
the boundary migration, thus the existed DRX grains can grow up 
further.
(3) According to Taylor factors (TFs) criterion, TFs difference will 
always existed in CD ‖ [001], which promotes the necklace 
structure gradually replaced columnar structure and some of 
them developed into fine DRX bands. However, TFs difference 
gradually decreased in CD ⊥ [001], DRX nucleation was inhibited, 
then coarse DRX grains were obtained.
