Abstract. Mo-Cr alloy cast iron is the most important materia l often used to make auto panel dies. 
To study high-speed machining process of auto panel dies, the material’s elastic modulus and 
fracture critical values of Mo-Cr alloy iron at 20 ℃-800 ℃ were studied based on the high 
temperature elongation test. The material’s stress- strain relation curves at various 
temperatures(20 ℃-500 ℃) and various strain-rates (500/s-5000/s) were stud ied and the dynamic 
tensile yield strength values were obtained by dyna mic SHPB(Split Hopkinson Pressure Bar) 
high-speed compression test. The test observation showed that Mo-Cr alloy iron has heat resistance 
and its behavior is between toughness and brittlene ss materials. Its toughness is enhanced with 
temperature increasing. At 20 ℃-500 ℃ and strain-rates (500/s-5000/s) the dynamic tensil e yield 
strength is decreased about 17 ％-24 ％ by temperature softening effect and is increased a bout 56 ％
-70 ％ by strain-rate strengthening effect. The strain-ra te strengthening effect prevails over 
temperature softening effect. 
1 Introduction 
Mo-Cr alloy cast iron is developed from 1980 with advantages such as high intensity, better wear 
resistance and heat resistance [1]. The main parts of the auto panel dies are large or super large 
casting, and it needs the materials of dies has the characters-anti-deformation, anti-wear, 
anti-fracture, fatigue tolerance, and anti-adhesion [2]. Mo-Cr alloy cast iron is one of the most 
important materials of auto panel dies. 
Auto panel dies has great cutting quantity especial ly for the milling process of the Mo-Cr alloy 
cast iron materials. The bad machinability of Mo-Cr alloy cast iron has an bad impact of application 
because of its high intensity and heat resisting pr operties. The cutting of the metal material is a 
complex nonlinear problem because it contains heat, force, machine and the coupling among them. 
The research of the mechanical properties of Mo-Cr alloy cast iron, especially the dynamic 
mechanical properties under the conditions of high temperatures, high strain and high strain rate, is 
one of the most problems for manufacturing auto pan el dies. Zhou [3] has a research of a kind of 
grinding ball from bainite white iron as cast by al loying and metal molding. Chen [4] prepared 
the reinforced nodular cast iron by foundry method using surface modified SiC nanometer powders 
as reinforced phase and investigated the microstruc ture and mechanical properties of reinforced 
materials with different SiC powders contents. Gong [5] studied the influence of chemical 
composition and heat treatment to produce high toug hness austemper ductile iron. All of these 
papers mainly focuses on the impact of the cast iro n components, casting process, and thermal

The mechanical property closely links with stress, strain rate and temperature. And the property 
has a great change under the conditions of large st rain rate, temperature such as the increase of the 
limitation of yield and yield strength, the decreas e of the extensibility, material fragility, and 
hardening effect of the strain rate [8]. It can be seen from Tab.2 that the dynamic yield strength has 
an obvious effect on the dynamic yield strength of the material. The dynamic yield strength of the 
Mo-Cr alloy cast iron decreases obviously. Under th e different strain rates with temperatures from 
20 ℃ to 500 ℃, the dynamic yield strength decreases between 17 ％- 24 ％. In the changes of the 
dynamic mechanical property of Mo-Cr alloy cast iron, the temperature has a softening effect to the 
dynamic yield strength , that is because of the decrease of the flow stress with the large glide plane 
and glide direction in the interior of material a s result the high temperature [9]. 
It can be get from Tab.2 that the Mo-Cr alloy cast iron’s hardening effect of the strain rate 
becomes more obvious. With the increase of the stra in rate, the Mo-Cr alloy cast iron’s dynamic 
yield strength increases obviously with different t emperatures. The value of the dynamic yield 
strength increases 56 ％-70 ％ with the increase of strain rate from 500/s to 500 0/s in different 
temperatures, that is, the strain rate has a hardening effect to the flow stress in the Mo-Cr alloy cast 
iron’s change of the dynamic mechanical. It is beca use of critical shear stress on account of the 
increase of the strain rate, material internal disl ocation, the strain of unit time, the motive number , 
the dislocation motion speed [10]. 
It can also be seen from Tab.2 that the dynamic yie ld strength with temperature(500 ℃) and 
strain rate (5000/s) increases 28 ％ comparing with that with temperature(20 ℃) and strain rate 
(500/s). It means that the strain rate hardening ef fect has much greater influence on the dynamic 
yield strength than the temperature softening effect. 
4 Conclusions 
It can be obtained from the SHPB high speed compress experiment of Mo-Cr alloy cast iron that the 
decrease of the flow stress and the temperature softening effect as a result of the great change of the 
Mo-Cr alloy cast iron’s dynamic yield strength unde r the conditions of large stress, strain rate, 
temperature and glide plane and the glide direction on account of the material internal dislocation; 
and the energy of the material internal dislocation increases because of the high strain rate, the 
critical shear stress of the material deformation, then the hardening effect of the strain rate appear s 
and is in the center stage. All of these discoveries provide a basis for the further research of the high 
speed cutting processing technique of Mo-Cr alloy cast iron.
