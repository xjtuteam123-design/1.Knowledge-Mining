Abstract 
The influence of solution and aging parameters on the microstructures and 
mechanical properties of Ti-8V-1.5Mo-2Fe-3Al alloy was studied through 
microstructural observation and mechanical property test. It is a newly designed near 
beta titanium alloy for high strength and toughness on the basis of Ti-10V-2Fe-3Al 
alloy. According to microstructural observation, the shape and volume fraction of 
primary /g302 phase is found to be determined by the solution treatment, while aging 
parameters control the precipitation behavior of secondary /g302 platelets. Moreover, the 
effect of aging time on tensile properties is slighter compared with that of aging 
temperature. Tensile test reveals the dependence of mechanical properties on the heat 
treatment parameters, according to which the tensile strength and elongation can be 
controlled in a range of 1230 ~ 1435 MPa and 9 ~ 13%. It is a little superior to the 
Ti-1023 alloy, the alloy solution treated at 800 ~ 820 °C for 1 hour and aged at 550 ~ 
600 °C for 2 ~ 8 hours has well balanced combination of strength and ductility. At the 
same strength level, the Ti-8V-1.5Mo-2Fe-3Al alloy has higher toughness and higher 
ductility than the Ti-1023 alloy, the reason is unknown and should be studied further. 
Morphological features of the fracture surfaces are consistent with the tensile test 
result and provide further information on the int ragranular fracture behavior when 
solution treated above the /g533-transus. 
Key words: beta titanium alloy; microstructure; tensile/toughness property; fracture 
behavior

1 Introduction 
Beta titanium alloys are appropriate materials for aeronautic and astronautic 
application due to their high strength-to-density ratio, excellent strength/toughness 
combination, enhanc ed processing characteristics and superior corrosion resistance. 
Based on these intrinsic advantages, they are used as structure components to lower 
the weight of aircraft, to reduce fuel consu mption and to improve thrust-weight ratio 
[1-4]. 
Ti-10V-2Fe-3Al alloy (Ti-1023), for example, is a high-strength/toughness and 
deep hardenable near beta titanium alloy. It was developed by TIMET Company in 
the 1970s to provide weight savings over high-strength steels or Ti-6Al-4V in forged 
aircraft undercarriage components, such as the landing gear system of airplanes in 
A380 and Boeing 777 [5]. The alloy possesses good hot die forgeability due to a low 
/g533 transus of ~ 800 °C, almost 200 °C below the /g533 transus of the high-strength /g302+/g533 
alloy Ti-6Al-4V [6]. 
As a beta titanium alloy, it is well known that its high strength comes from the 
microstructure of precipitated fine /g302 phase particles (platelets) with a high volume 
fraction in /g533 phase matrix, which is produced by solution treatment and subsequent 
aging. During heat treatment process, the /g533 phase may decompose into primary /g302 
phase (/g302
p), secondary /g302 phase (/g302s) and/or /g550 particles, depending on the solution 
temperature, aging temperature and time [7]. The shape and the morphology of the /g302p 
phase can be altered by giving appropriate strain during forging/rolling in the /g302+/g533 
phase field at the given working temperature. The morphologies and the distribution 
of the /g302
s and the /g550 phase can be altered by varying the aging temperature and time. A 
number of studies have been carried out to study its mechanical properties in /g533 
solution treated and aged as well as (/g302+/g533) solution treated (up to 30 pct /g302p) and aged 
conditions. It has been shown that by incorporating appropriate combinations of 
solution treatment and aging, it is possible to obtain a wide range of yield strengths 
ranging from 900 to 1450 MPa in this alloy [8-11]. As a result, the combination of the 
strength, ductility and toughness can be varied significantly by modifying the treating

parameters. Given all of that, Ti-1023 alloy was compositionally modified into a new 
alloy with nominal composition of Ti-8V-1.5Mo-2Fe-3Al, which was expected to 
maintain or even to improve its performance. Meanwhile, great care is also taken 
during alloy design to minimize its cost by replacing 2wt.% V with 1.5wt.% Mo. 
Hence, low-cost Fe-80V and Al-62Mo master alloys can be directly added together 
with a small amount of pure Al in the melting procedure. The ratio of V to Fe is 
reduced to 4 while keeping its molybdenum equivalent (Mo
eq) constant at 11. The

1.25[Ni] + 1.7[Mn] + 1.7[Co] + 2.5[Fe] – 1.0[Al]. It has proved that a minimum value 
of about 10% is necessary to stabilize th e beta phase during quenching to room 
temperature [13]. 
Due to the significant influence of heat treatment parameters on the 
microstructure and mechanical property, it is of extreme importance to understand 
factors, such as microstructural parameters, tha t affect the mech anical properties of 
the new alloy. The objective of this work is to understand how these factors affect 
alloy’s tensile/toughness properties and to ascertain th e microstructure/property 
relationship in this alloy by altering its solution and/or aging treatment. 
2 Materials and experimental 
2.1 Melting and processing 
The experimental material of Ti-8V-1.5Mo-2Fe-3Al was firstly prepared using 
sponge titanium, pure Al, and Fe-80V and Al-62Mo master alloys through double 
V AR (vacuum arc remelting) process. The chemical composition of the alloy is listed 
in Table 1. The ingot was firstly /g533-cogged down at 1000 °C and subsequently forged 
in the /g302+/g533 phase field to a 35-mm-thick and 75-mm-width plate. The /g533-transus 
temperature of the alloy was determined metallographically and was found to be 
845±5 °C. The forged plate was examined metallographically at the center of the

section plane normal to its axis. The optical picture, shown in Figure 1, reveals its 
microstructure consisting of /g533 phase, elongated /g302 phase and unbroken grain boundary 
/g302 phase. 
Cylindrical specimens for tensile tests were machined from the plate keeping the 
axis parallel to the longitudinal orientation. The solution treatments were carried out 
at temperatures between 780 °C and 890 °C, for a time period of 1 hour, followed by 
water quench (WQ). The solution treated specimens were then aged at 500 °C, 550 °C, 
and 600 °C repectively, for 2, 4 or 8 hours followed by air cooling (AC) to room 
temperature. All the specimens were heated in a pre-heated air furnace. 
 
2.2 Specimen preparation and mechanical test 
After removal of oxide layer, specimens for microstructural observation were 
mechanically polished with a standard metallographic procedure, and then etched in 
the Kroll’s reagent (5% HF + 10% HNO
3 + 85% H 2O). Metallographic observation 
was performed on a Zeiss Axiovert 200 Mat optical microscopy (OM), and a 
JSM-7001F field emission scanning electron microscopy (FESEM) operated at a 
voltage of 15 kV in order to examine detailed morphology of secondary /g302 phases as 
well as fracture features of the broken specimens after tensile test. 
Standard cylindrical tensile specimens, a gauge length of 30 mm and a gauge 
diameter of 6 mm, were machined from both the solution treated and aged materials. 
The tensile tests were performed at room temperature using a screw driven INSTRON 
machine of 50 kN capacity with a nominal strain rate of 5×10
-4 s-1 determined by a 
contacted extensometer. Three samples of each condition were tested for an average 
value of property indexes, including ultimate tensile strength (UTS), yield strength 
(YS) (that is, 0.2% proof stress), elongation (El) and reduction of area (RA). 
Mode I fracture toughness specimens with the geometry of a 25-mm thickness 
compact tension specimen, as described in ASTM E-399 [14] were machined with 
notches in L-T orientations. Fracture toughness test was done at room temperature on 
a MTS 810-500KN testing machine. Three tests were carried out for each condition.

3 Results and discussion 
3.1 Microstructural characteristics 
As mentioned above, the Ti-8V-1.5Mo-2Fe-3Al alloy is a near beta titanium 
alloy with the /g533 transus temperature of 845±5 °C. For beta titanium alloys, solution 
treatment above the /g533 transus temperature (known as /g533 solution treatment) results in 
coarse /g533 grains. And solution treatment slightly below the /g533 transus (known as /g302//g533 
solution treatment) leads to precipitates of primary /g302 phase [15]. 
Reprensentative optical micrographs of the Ti-8V-1.5Mo-2Fe-3Al alloy after 
different solution treatments followed by water quench are shown in Figure 2. Figure 
2a ~ 2d refer to the alloy solution treated below the /g533-transus temperature, while 
Figure 2e and 2f are indicative of that above the /g533-transus. It can seen that, profuse 
primary /g302 phases (/g302
p) are homogeneously distributed in the /g533 matrix when solution 
treated below the /g533-transus. The volume fraction of /g302p decreases with increasing 
solution temperature from 780 °C to 820 °C. Moreover, the shape of /g302 p seems to 
transform from globular to strip while ex tending solution time from 1 hour to 4 hours 
at 780 °C, by comparing Figure 2a and Figure 2b. This is consistent with Leyens’ 
result that the shape and fraction of /g302
p are determined by solution temperature and 
time, and the shape transformation is facilitated by the stored deformation energy 
induced in the hot working process [15]. Moreover, it has also been proved that the 
important characteristic of the /g302p phase concerns its ability to pin the prior /g533 grain 
boundaries and reduce their mobility, which limits the recrystallization and grain 
growth of /g533 phase [16]. However, when solution treated above the /g533-transus in case of 
Figure 2e and 2f, the microstructures consist of almost equiaxed /g533 grains, with the 
absence of any /g302p. On the other hand, higher solution temperature facilitates the 
growth of equiaxed /g533 grains, compared with Figure 2e and 2f. 
As can be seen that, Figure 2c, 2d and 2e are representative of typical 
microstructure with different /g302p volume fractions, thus they are resultantly selected for 
aging treatment and tensile tests to further investigate the effect of primary /g302 phase on 
the mechanical properties.

The microstructures of the solution treated alloy after aging treatments are shown 
in Figure 3 (OM) and Figure 4 (SEM). Although the morphology of secondary /g302 
phase (/g302
s) is indiscernible due to the limited amplification by optical observation, OM 
pictures in Figure 3 properly illustrate their shape and distribution. Meanwhile, the 
corresponding SEM pictures in Figure 4 provide detailed information on the volume 
fraction and platelet size of /g302
s. Secondary /g302 phase is precipitated from the /g533 phase 
matrix in form of fine /g302 platelets with thickness less than 0.5 /g541m. 
It has been reported that both the aging temperature and time have a significant 
influence on the volume fraction of /g302s as well as the shape and distribution of /g302p [17]. 
The appropriate temperature region for aging is reported to be between 500 °C and 
600 °C, just above the solvus temperature of the metastable coherent particles /g550 
which act as precursors for the nucleation of /g302s platelets [18]. By elevating the aging 
temperature from 500 °C in Figure 4c to 600 °C in Figure 4d, the precipitated /g302s 
platelets in SEM pictures grows thicker, whereas the shape and volume fraction of /g302p 
remain approximately the same according to OM pictures. 
As shown by Figure 4a and 4b, the effect of prolonging the aging time from 2 
hours to 8 hours at the same aging temperature of 550 °C is twofold. For one thing, 
larger /g302
s platelets participate in the /g533 matrix as shown by the SEM pictures. For 
another, there is an obvious increase in the volume fraction of /g302p while the general 
shape transforms from globular to strip. This indicates that /g302s nucleates preferentially 
at grain boundaries of /g302p and grows along the close-packed crystal plane of /g533 matrix. 
Duerig and Terlinde have named the nonuniform and preferential nucleation schemes 
of equilibrium /g302s as sympathetic nucleation [19], which is reported to be able to 
improve the macroscopic plasticity at the cost of slightly reducing the tensile strength. 
In Figure 3e and 4e the /g302s phase close to the grain boundary clearly demonstrates 
the main characteristic of all /g533 alloys, that the /g302s platelets nucleates preferentially at 
the grain boundaries of /g533 matrix [20]. This trend is continued with aging time 
prolonged to 8 hours as shown in Figure 3f and 4f. Microscopy study has already 
revealed that continuous /g302 layer forms along the /g533 phase grain boundaries in /g533

3.2 Mechanical properties 
Tensile properties of the alloy solution treated at below and above the transus 
temperature are shown in Table 2. It is obvious that the strength decrease with an 
improved ductility, with increasing the solution temperature. On the other hand, the 
alloy in solution condition has quite a good combination of strength and ductility. The 
ultimate strength is about 1000 MPa while elo ngation is about 9 ~ 14%. The alloy 
solution treated at below the transus exhibits a little higher strength (>1000 MPa) than 
that at above the transus (991 MPa), however, the high solution temperature results in 
an improved ductility (elongation change from 7% to 14%). The strength difference 
seems to be associated to two reasons. The first is that the primary /g302 phase dotted in 
the /g533 matrix has an ability to hinder the growth of /g533 grains, and the second is that the 
need-like /g302
p phases have an effect on increaseing strength. Moreover, it is reported 
that the spherical /g302p phase has a direct influence on the ductility rather than the 
strength of beta titanium alloys [22]. While the /g533 solution treatment leads to a slight 
decrease in strength with improved ductility. This is usually as to elimination of 
residual internal defects and stress relief due to recrystallization [23]. 
The tensile properties of the alloy after aging treatments, varied in temperature or 
holding time, are shown in Figure 5. It should be pointed out that for beta titanium 
alloys the size and volume fraction of fine /g302s platelets have been proved to be the 
major determinant for strength levels [24]. When solution treated at 800 °C and 
820 °C, the material manifests an obvious decrease in strength and increase in 
elongation while elevating the aging temperature from 500 °C to 600 °C in Figure 5a. 
This mainly results from the growth of /g302
s platelets as shown in Figure 4c and 4d,

which will weaken the dispersion strengthening effect as the interaction mode 
between dislocation and /g302s particle transforms from cutting to by passing. As can be 
seen from Figure 5b, the effect of aging time on tensile properties is much slighter 
compared with that of aging temperature. As pointed out already in Figure 4a and 4b, 
by prolonging aging time /g302
s nucleates refere ntially at grain boundaries of /g302p and 
facilitate the growth of /g302p. Therefore, as more /g302-stabilized solute Al is transferred 
from the metastable /g533 matrix to the strip shaped /g302p by thermal activated diffusion, the 
driving force left for /g302s platelet growth is weakened. As a result, the sluggish growth 
of /g302s platelets with time is responsible for the comparatively slight effecter of aging 
time. Moreover, the influence of sympathetic nucleation on tensile properties is 
limited, at least for the aging process of Ti-8V-1.5Mo-2Fe-3Al alloy. 
When solution treated at 870 °C, the material exhibit poor plasticity with its 
elongation less than 3%, as seen in Figure 5a. Especially in the case of 870 °C/1h + 
500 °C/2h the tensile specimen fractured even before yielding. It has been proved 
experimentally that for high strength /g533 alloys plastic deformation takes place 
preferentially along the continuous /g302 layers at /g533 grain boundaries [25]. As can be seen 
in Figure 4e and 4f, without any /g302
p it is unavoidable for /g302s to nucleates on the /g533 grain 
boundaries during the aging treatment. Therefore, the present material should not be 
solution treated above the /g533-transus although the corresponding UTS can be as high as 
1572 MPa, in condition of 870 °C/1h + 550 °C/2h. 
From Figure 5b tensile strength is found to increase with increasing solution 
temperature. According to the pseudo-binary phase diagram of titanium alloy, the 
volume fraction of /g302
p decreases with increasing solution temperature, which is 
consistent with the observation in Figure 2. Because the total volume fraction of /g302 
phase (coarse /g302p plus fine /g302s) is fixed by the alloy chemistry, less /g302p implies more /g302s 
which determines higher yield stress level through the same aging treatment. Based 
on mechanical test results, the tensile strength and elongation of the present 
Ti-8V-1.5Mo-2Fe-3Al alloy can be controlled in a range of 1230 ~ 1435 MPa and 9 ~ 
13% respectively, which is comparable to the reported tensile results of Ti-1023 as

3.3 Fracture analysis 
Figure 6 presents the typical tensile fracture fractographs at low and high 
magnification for Ti-8V-1.5Mo-2Fe-3Al alloy observed by SEM. The morphological 
feature of shear region is obvious in Figure 6a and 6c, and detailed observation 
revealed homogeneously distributed dimples with diameter less than 10 /g541m in Figure 
6b and 6d. These four fractographs illustrate that the fracture mechanism of 
specimens heat treated under 800 °C/1h+550 °C/2h and 820 °C/1h+550 °C/4h is 
ductile in nature, which is consistent with the relatively high value of elongation of 8% 
and 9% in Figure 5b. 
/g533 grain grows bigger when solution temperature is elevated from 800 °C to 
820 °C, as can be seen from the larger grain size facets in Figure 6c than in Figure 6a. 
Grain growth is always considered harmful to the macroscopic ductility, however the 
tested elongation of Figure 6c is slightly higher than that of Figure 6a. The seemingly 
paradoxical phenomenon can be interpreted by the effect of sympathetic nucleation as 
the aging time is prolonged from 2 h to 4 h. As a result, the drop in ductility caused by 
the coarsened /g533 matrix can be compensated by the participation of /g302
s platelets. 
Intragranular fracture takes place when heat treated under 870 °C/1h+550 °C/4h 
in Figure 6e and 6f, and the corresponding elongation is as low as 2% in Figure 5b. As 
previously discussed, the region close to the /g533 grain boundaries is softer compared 
with the age-hardened interior region due to the formation of continuous /g302 layer. This 
microstructure feature is consistent with the poor ductility and large fracture facet on 
which exist a small amount of dimples in Figure 6f. 
3.4 Comparison with Ti-1023 alloy 
As mentioned above, Ti-1023 alloy is a high strength near beta forging alloy 
useful for engines and airframes. Its /g533-transus temperature is reported to be 790 ~ 
805 °C. The key to successful application of this alloy lies in its excellent forgability

11 
 
and high combination of tensile and toughness properties. In general, Ti-1023 alloy is 
applied in the solution treated and aged condition, which can be customized as 
necessary to suit the application [26]. 
The combination of tensile strength and toughness of Ti-1023 alloy is shown in 
Table 3. The alloy in the strength level of 1240 ~ 1385 MPa has toughness ranging 
from 46 to 62 MPa·m
1/2. However, the combination of strength and toughness of 
Ti-8V-1.5Mo-2Fe-3Al alloy designed in the present research are superior to the 
Ti-1023 alloy, as shown in Table 3. Although heat treatments varies in different 
strength levels and toughness, the alloy exhibits a relative excellent balance of 
strength-ductility-toughness. The reason for the sizable difference in toughness may 
be related to the replacing 2% V to 1.5% Mo. Alought Mo has a stronger stability than 
V , 1.5% Mo and 2% V share a similar effect on the alloy’s stability and the two alloys 
have the similar Mo equivalent as above. However, it seems to be a little farfetched. 
Further investigation should be certainly studied. Although the reason is remained 
unknown, the well balanced of strength-du ctility-toughness should make this new 
alloy underlying and adaptive for applications in requirments of high strength and 
high toughness, compared to the Ti-1023 alloy. 
4 Conclusions 
The microstructural characteristics and tensile properties and the relationship 
between the both of the Ti-8V-1.5Mo-2Fe-3Al alloy were investigated systematically. 
The alloy was solution treated below and above the /g533 transus temperature, and aged at 
temperatures ranging from 500 °C to 600 °C afterwards. The main results are 
summarized as follows: 
(1) The Ti-8V-1.5Mo-2Fe-3Al alloy newly designed has a relatively lower 
formulation cost than Ti-1023 alloy, due to directly adding low-cost Fe-80V and 
Al-62Mo master alloys in the melting procedure; 
(2) The effect of aging time on tensile properties is slighter compared with that 
of aging temperature, which is found to be determined by the sympathetic

participation behavior of secondary /g302 platelets; 
(3) The Ti-8V-1.5Mo-2Fe-3Al alloy solution treated at 800 ~ 820 °C for 1 hour 
and aged at 550 ~ 600 °C for 2 ~ 8 hours has well balanced combination of strength 
and ductility. The corresponding tensile strength are in the range of 1230 ~ 1435 MPa 
with an elongation of 9 ~ 13%, it is a little superior to the Ti-1023 alloy; 
(4) At the same strength level, the Ti-8V-1.5Mo-2Fe-3Al alloy has higher 
toughness and higher ductility than the Ti-1023 alloy, the reason is unknown and 
should be studied further.
