Yuji NAKASONE, Keijiro HIRAGA and Tatsuhiko TANABE

Received November 18, 1991 
The present paper investigates creep crack growth behavior in Ni-base superalloys, i.e., 
Ni-26%Cr-17%W-0.5%Mo alloys, at 1,273K in a He gas environment. The alloys have been 
developed as candidate structural materials for heat exchangers in Japanese high-temperature 
gas-cooled reactors (HTGRs) and shown to have good creep resistance. 
Creep crack growth tests were made on the alloys having different carbon contents in a 
simulated HTGR He gas environment. Tests were also made in a low vacuum for a comparison 
purpose. Creep crack growth rate dajdt was evaluated by the fracture mechanics parameter 
C*. The addition of carbon suppresses the load-line deflection in the primary and secondary 
stages of creep in the present alloys, whereas it accelerates the deflection rate in the final 
stage. The dajdt vs. C* diagrams revealed that, in the He gas, dajdt was much faster in the 
carbon-added alloy than in the non-carbon-added one. This may be attributed to drastic in­
crease in creep ductility in the He gas. The results also showed that the He gas environment 
accelerated dajdt, especially in higher C* range; nevertheless, this environmental effect was 
smaller than the effect of carbon-addition. 
KEYWORDS: carbon-addition, creep, ductility, crack growth, fracture mechanics, 
helium gas, HTGR type reactors, nickel-base superalloys, precipitation hardening 
I. INTRODUCTION 
Nickel-base superalloys are the most pro­
mising candidate materials for very high 
temperature use; e.g., structural components 
of piping and heat exchangers in high-tem­
perature gas-cooled reactors (HTGRs), turbine 
discs in advanced aircraft engines, etc. They 
can maintain good creep resistance in a very 
high temperature range of, say, 1,273 K or 
higher. 
The Ni-26%Cr-17%W-0.5%Mo alloys have 
been developed as candidate structural mate­
rials for heat exchangers in Japanese HTGRs 
(1)(2). The alloys have good creep resistance; 
e.g., the 2,000 h creep rupture strength is 
higher than 40 MPa at 1,273 K<•J-<•J; however, 
little has yet been known about creep crack 
growth behavior in this alloy family at 1,273 K 
in a He gas environment. 
The present paper investigates creep crack 
growth behavior in the precipitation-hardened 
Ni-26 %Cr-17 %W -0.5 %Mo alloys at 1,273 K 
in a simulated HTGR He gas environment. 
Creep crack growth tests were carried out on 
the non-C-added and C-added alloys at 1,273 K 
in the He gas, and on the C-added alloy at 
1,273 K in a low vacuum for a comparison 
purpose. The effect of C-addition and that 
of the He gas environment will be discussed.

IV. CONCLUSION 
The present paper investigates creep crack 
growth behavior in Ni-26%Cr-17%W-0.5%Mo 
alloys at 1,273 K in a simulated HTGR He 
gas and a low vacuum environments. The 
results show that the addition of C suppressed 
the load-line deflection in the primary and 
secondary stages of the creep process, where­
as it accelerated the deflection rate in the 
final stage. Creep crack growth rate da/ dt, 
however, was markedly faster in the C-added 
alloy than in the non-C-added alloy. The He 
gas environment also accelerated da/dt in the 
C-added alloy, especially in higher C* range; 
nevertheless, this environmental effect was 
smaller than the effect of C-addition.
