1. Introduction
In recent years, in order to meet the strict requirements of the en -
gineering industry, people have been designing and developing new 
alloys to achieve excellent mechanical properties, such as the best 
combination of high specific strength, strength and ductility, excellent 
fracture toughness at low temperature, excellent fatigue resistance and 
creep resistance [ 1 – 6 ]. Medium and high entropy alloys (M/HEA) are 
considered as emerging advanced alloys due to their excellent proper -
ties. Compared with traditional alloys, M/HEA has a variety of main 
elements to form a simple microstructure. Due to its unique design 
concept, the mechanical, corrosion resistance, high temperature oxida -
tion resistance and other properties of FCC structure M/HEA such as 
CrCoNi, CoCrFeMnNi and CoCrFeNi have been designed and widely 
studied [ 7 – 10 ]. However, due to the high price of Co element, FCC 
M/HEA structures such as FeNiCr [ 11 – 13,55 ], FeMnNiCr [ 14 ] and 
CuCrFeNi [ 15 ] have also attracted the attention of many researchers. Lu 
et al. [ 16 ] achieved significant strength-plastic synergistic properties by

The structure of CR-1000 sample is mainly composed of recrystal -
lized grains with many twins. The twins in the FCC alloy are formed by 
the slip of the Shockley part on the adjacent {111} plane. For annealing 
twins, the twinning part mainly comes from the migration of recrystal -
lized GB. Therefore, annealing twins are always accompanied by 
recrystallization, and at least one side of the annealing twin is connected 
to the grain boundary. Twins can significantly increase the strength/ 
ductility of the material. On the one hand, twins can provide a smooth 
path for dislocation slip to increase plasticity. On the other hand, the 
grain refinement effect is induced by introducing additional twin 
boundaries, resulting in a significant dynamic Hall-Petch effect. In order 
to further reveal the synergistic enhancement effect of annealing twins

5. Conclusion
Partially recrystallized and fully recrystallized Al
5

25

25

42.5

2.5 
HEA with excellent tensile mechanical properties were prepared by a 
simple cold rolling and short annealing process. The microstructure 
evolution, mechanical behavior and deformation mechanism were sys -
tematically studied. The main conclusions are as follows. 
(1) Partially recrystallized CR-900 HEA grains have high dislocation 
density and ultra-high tensile strength of 1258 MPa, but some 
plasticity is lost.
(2) The dislocation density in the CR-1000 HEA grains with almost 
complete recrystallization is low, and a high density of annealing 
twins (42.6 %) is generated. The annealing twin boundary can be 
used as an obstacle to dislocation slip, and a reasonable combi -
nation of high strength (792 MPa) and good ductility (62 %) is 
achieved.
(3) The effect of high density of annealing twins and independent 
dislocation on strength-plasticity is revealed by using PFC simu -
lation. The annealing twin boundary improves the strength and 
plasticity by increasing the grain refinement and the mean free 
path of dislocation movement. In the process of continuous 
loading, the high density of independent dislocation will produce 
subcrystals, resulting in grain refinement to improve the yield 
strength of the alloy, but will reduce the mean free path of the 
dislocation, resulting in the decrease of material plasticity.
