1. Introduction
Tungsten copper (W-Cu) alloy has attracted much attention from
scientists. The tungsten copper alloy compounds the advantageous
properties of both W and Cu. Tungsten has a remarkably high melting
point (3410 ℃ ) and density (19.25 g/cm
3
), while copper exhibits a lower
melting point (1080 ℃ ) and density (8.92 g/cm
3
). Additionally, Tung-
sten showcases exceptional hardness, robust corrosion resistance, and
minimal coefficient of thermal expansion [1 – 3] . Copper has superior
electric conductivity, thermal conductivity, and toughness [4 – 6] . The
combination of W and Cu makes W-Cu alloy has the characteristics of
high strength, high density, high temperature resistance, Moderate
thermal and electrical conductivity, and resistant to arc ablation, which
is extensively used in high-voltage switch alloys, military high-
temperature resistant and microelectronic materials [7 – 9] , and is
widely used as components and parts in various industries, such as in
aviation, aerospace, electronics, machinery, sports equipment,
metallurgy, electricity, nuclear fusion, etc [7,10 – 20] . As a high tem-
perature resistant material for aerospace, W-Cu alloy can be used as
rocket engine nozzle, missile, nose cone, air rudder, gas rudder, etc.
[7,10 – 17] . As a plasma orientation component in nuclear fusion, W-Cu
alloy also requires high temperature resistance and strong resistance to
high-temperature airflow erosion [18 – 20] . It mainly uses copper vola-
tilization at high temperatures formed by sweating refrigeration, and
reduces the surface temperature of W-Cu alloy, and ensures its normal
use under high temperature extreme conditions [21 – 27] .
For now, the research of researchers is mainly manifested in the use
of different experimental methods to obtain high density tungsten-
copper alloys. Because of the huge difference in performance between
tungsten and copper, tungsten-copper alloy is mostly infiltrated by the
skeleton infiltration method, which includes the step of solid-state sin-
tering [22,28,29] . However, the skeleton penetration method has de-
fects, and it is difficult to form high density alloy materials [22,28,29] .
At the same time, high temperature sintering will lead to the W grains

copper alloy is very little. In addition, the effect of oxygen impurities on
nuclear fusion is dangerous and even causes explosions. Therefore, it is
necessary to investigate the surface O adsorption properties of tungsten

Therefore, the mechanical, thermodynamic, and surface O adsorp-
tion properties of tungsten-copper alloy are calculated by first principles
method. For example, some basic mechanical and thermal parameters of
alloys are calculated and analyzed. Especially the influence of high
temperatures on thermal conductivity, thermal expansion behavior and
bulk modulus is being investigated and analyzed. In addition, consid-
ering the significant difference between tungsten and copper in melting
point and their immiscibility, they represent typical pseudoalloys.
Therefore, the solid solution model is used to study the properties of
tungsten-copper alloy. By heating the metal material to a high temper-
ature, the alloying elements are fully dissolved in the matrix to achieve a
supersaturated state. This can significantly improve the strength, hard-
ness, corrosion resistance, heat resistance and other properties of the
alloy material. Through the design and analysis of tungsten-copper alloy
systems, it provides a relatively beneficial value reference for its appli-
cation in various fields under high temperature.
