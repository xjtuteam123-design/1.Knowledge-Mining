1. Introduction
Since the advent of high-entropy alloys (HEAs) in 2004 [1,2] , they 
have been extensively studied due to their unique microstructure and 
excellent properties. Numerous HEA systems have been explored, such 
as Al-Co-Cr-Fe-Ni [3 – 6] , Co-Cr-Fe-Mn-Ni [1,7,8] , Co-Fe-Ni-Cr-Ti [9,10] , 
Cu-Cr-Fe-Ni-Ti-Al [11] , Al-Co-Cr-Fe-Ni-Cu [12,13] , among which 
Al-Co-Cr-Fe-Ni is one of the earliest and most extensively investigated 
HEAs systems [14] . Many HEAs with excellent properties in this alloy 
system have been developed and deeply investigated within this system,

Traditional Ni-based alloys are extensively used in critical fields such 
as aerospace, energy, and chemical engineering due to their outstanding 
comprehensive performance [27 – 32] . However, the chemical compo -
sition of traditional Ni-based alloys is relatively complex, often con -
taining over ten elements, including rare and expensive ones such as W, 
Re, Ta, and Ru [33 – 37] . There is a requirement for designing new 
Ni-based multi-component alloys with common and cheaper elements to 
reduce cost. The Al-Co-Cr-Fe-Ni system, which contains Ni along with 
more common and inexpensive alloying elements, especially Al, Cr, and 
Fe, offers a promising alternative. If novel Ni-based multi-component 
alloys can be designed within this system, which are relatively simple in 
composition compared to traditional Ni-based alloys and exclude rare 
and expensive elements such as W, Re, Ta, and Ru, the overall cost of 
Ni-based alloys will be significantly reduced, making them more suitable 
for large-scale applications and resource conservation. We believe this 
assumption holds, as Al, Co, Cr, and Fe are all very important mechan -
ical strengthening elements for traditional Ni-based alloys [31,38,39] , 
and they are also widely used in Ni-based and other alloys. Al, a critical 
element for forming γ’ precipitates (Ni

impeding dislocation propagation in the γ matrix phase of Ni-based 
superalloys [31] . Co and Cr elements belong to those that exert solid

high strength by increasing the resistance to dislocation motion [31] . In 
Ni-Fe based alloys represented by GH2984, the relatively high Fe 
content not only reduces the cost but also enhances both the process -

alloys from further oxidation [31,41,42] . Therefore, it is feasible for us

Al-Co-Cr-Fe-Ni quinary alloy system.
