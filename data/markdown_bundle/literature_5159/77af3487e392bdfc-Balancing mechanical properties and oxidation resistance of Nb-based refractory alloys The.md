3.2. Mechanical properties
Hardness of the Nb-based refractory alloys at room temperature is 
shown in Table 2 , where C103 exhibits the lowest hardness, 148 HV5, 
while the 15Ti alloy exhibits the highest hardness, 314 HV5, and the 
other benchmark alloy WC3009 has the second highest hardness of 278 
HV5. As expected, hardness in general shows a positive correlation with 
solute concentration in that the higher solute concentration the higher 
hardness, evidencing the solid solution strengthening in these single- 
phase Nb-based refractory alloys. In addition, hardness indeed has a 
better correlation to the solute concentration of Hf + W ( Fig. 4 (b)), than 
the total solute concentration of Hf + W + Ti ( Fig. 4 (a)), further indicating 
Hf and W contribute to hardness more significantly than Ti, in agree -
ment with the known alloying effects.
