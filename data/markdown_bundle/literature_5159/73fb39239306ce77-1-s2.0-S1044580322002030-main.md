ABSTRACT 
This study developed a series of Al
x
(TiZrHfNb)
100-x 
(x = 0, 3, 5, 7, 12 at. %) refractory high-entropy alloys 
(RHEAs) with an aim to improve the poor oxidation and wear resistance at elevated temperature of the base alloy 
TiZrHfNb. These alloys were prepared by arc melting, and the mechanical properties, oxidation and wear 
resistance at elevated temperatures were studied systematically. The results indicate that all the alloys are single 
solid solution phase with body-centered cubic (BCC) structure. The addition of Al significantly improves the 
strength of the RHEAs due to the large solid solution strengthening effect, and the yield strength and Al content 
obey the liner relation. The oxidation and wear test results indicate that the Al element will preferentially react 
with O atoms and accumulate on the surface to form a dense oxide layer, which prevents other elements reacting 
with O atoms and makes the alloy have better oxidation resistance at high temperatures, especially in alloys with 
12% Al. The friction coefficient and wear rate of the RHEAs dramatically decrease with the increase of Al, which 
can be attributed to the combined effect of mechanical properties and oxidation resistance. This study provides 
an effective method for improving the oxidation resistance and wear resistance of RHEAs in a relatively high 
temperature range. 
1. Introduction 
With the development of aerospace, petrochemical engineering and 
other engineering fields, more stringent performance requirements are 
put forward for metal materials used at high temperatures [ 1 ]. The wear 
resistance of metal materials at high temperatures is an important 
manifestation of these performance requirements, which is determined 
by the original mechanical properties and oxidation resistance at 
elevated temperatures [ 2 ]. As mechanical and tribological properties 
generally degrade at temperatures above about 60% melting tempera -
ture, an exploration for metallic alloys based on refractory elements 
with high melting points may increase the maximum operating tem -
perature [ 3 ]. 
High-entropy alloys (HEAs) owing to their promising mechanical 
properties have attracted extensive research attention [ 4 – 6 ]. These new 
alloys break the design principle with one or two primary elements and 
aim at producing single solid-solution phase by maximizing the entropy 
of mixing [ 7 – 11 ]. Refractory high-entropy alloys (RHEAs) are a special 
kind of HEAs using the refractory metals as constituents, which were 
proposed by Senkov, aiming at overcoming the temperature limitation 
of superalloy [ 12 ]. RHEA was originally composed of five refractory 
elements W, Nb, Mo, Ta, and V, and then expanded to nine elements (Ti, 
V, Cr, Zr, Nb, Mo, Hf, Ta, W) [ 13 , 14 ]. In addition, in order to improve 
the physical or mechanical properties of the alloy, Al, C, Co, Ni, Re, Si, 
etc. are also used as additional elements [ 15 – 20 ]. The most stringent 
problems for RHEAs are high density, poor oxidation and wear resis -
tance at relatively high temperatures, which limit their engineering 
applications [ 21 ]. 
It is worth noting that Al possesses the strong ability to stabilize BCC 
phase due to its high solubility in Ti, Zr and Hf. In HfNbTaTiZr alloy, the 
addition of Al partly replacing Hf can increase the hardness and yield 
strength by 29% and 98%, respectively [ 17 ]. In Al
x
MoNbTaTiV alloy, 
the addition of Al improves the mechanical properties in a wider tem -
perature range [ 22 ]. A small amount of Al added in MoNbTaTiV alloy 
can exhibit promising oxidation resistance, especially at the temperature

4. Conclusions 
A series of Al
x

100-x
(x = 0, 3, 5, 7, 12 at. %) alloys with 
excellent phase stability were prepared. After studying the mechanical 
properties and oxidation resistance of the alloys, a preliminary evalua -
tion of their wear resistance has been made. The friction and wear 
behavior of the alloy at room temperature and elevated temperature and 
the corresponding wear mechanism have been emphatically investi -
gated. The main findings of the study are as follows: 
(1) The alloys with different Al content maintain a stable BCC single- 
phase structure. The equiaxed grain structure with approximate grain 
size is obtained by the heat treatment process. And the compressive yield 
strength of the alloy has been increased from 331 MPa to 1095 MPa. The 
oxidation resistance of the alloy with higher Al content has also been 
greatly improved. 
(2) By improving the mechanical properties and oxidation resistance, 
the wear resistance of Al-12% alloy has also been greatly improved. The
