SYNOPSIS
Mo-Si-Al alloy and Mo-Si-AUSiC composite were fabricated by the powder metallurgical 
process from a novel Mo-Si-Al prealloyed powder, and their microstructure and mechanical 
properties were evaluated. The Mo-Si-Al and Mo-Si-Al/15 vol%SiC mixed powders were 
hot-pressed in an argon atmosphere. XRD analyses revealed that Mo-Si-AUSiC composites were 
composed of MoSiZ (tetragonal, CllƒÐ structure), Mo(Si,Al)2 (hexagonal , C40 structure), 
a-Al2O3, Mo¬5Si3C¬1 and R-SiC. Almost no glassy SiO2 phase was found in Mo-Si-Al and 
Mo-Si-AUSiC systems contrary to MoSi2 and MoSi2/SiC . The formation of a-Al2O3 was 
attributed to the predominant oxidation of aluminum at thesurface of the Mo-Si-Al prealloyed 
powder. Mo-Si-Al based systems showed the better high-temperature strength mainly due to the 
elimination of glassy SiO2 and the crystallographical change from t-MoSiZ to h-Mo(Si ,Al)2. 
Incorporation of fine SiC particles enabled to control microstructure and then to enhance 
mechanical properties.

1 Introduction
Molybdenum disilicide (MoSi2) is a promising 
material for various applications such as aerospace 
exposed to temperatures more than 1500Ž, mainly 
because of its high melting point (2030Ž) and fairly 
good oxidation resistance. However, its fracture 
toughness and strength are not high enoughfor 
high-temperature structural applications. Further-
more, glassy SiO2 which is present in the 
metallurgically produced MoSi2 powder strongly 
degrades the high-temperature mechanical proper-
ties'. Thus, main efforts have focused on im-
proving mechanical properties of this material. 
Especially, "second phase dispersion and "SiO
2 
elimination have been widely investigated . 
 Recently "alloying" has been also adapted to im-
prove various properties of MoSi2.Mo-Si-Al 
is one of the most fascinating system because of its 
good cyclic oxidation resistance16"'71 and of crys-
tallographical modification from tetragonal Cll

structure of MoSi2 to hexagonal C40 of 
Mo(Si,AI)2. Silva et al . reported that ele-
mental Al addition enabled to convert SiO 2 to Al2O3
in powder processed MoSi2. This "in-situ displace-
ment reaction" technique controls Al content easely . 
However, using fine Al metal powder is sometimes 
disadvantageous; fine Al metal is flammable and it 
has relatively high vapor pressure during heating. 
 In this investigation, a novel Mo-Si-Al prealloyed 
powder was used to fabricate Mo-Si-Al alloys. In 
order to control microstructure and to improve 
mechanical properties, fine SiC particles reinforced 
alloys were also fabricated. High temperature 
strength as well as room temperature mechanical 
properties was evaluated for Mo-Si-Al and Mo-Si-Al 
/SiC composites.

Alloying effect 
 In order to clarify the alloying effect, the results 
from finer MoSi2 and Mo-Si-Al were compared. 
Although Mo-Si-Al has somewhat larger grain size, 
its hardness and strength were higher than thoseof 
MoSi2. Harada et. al have briefly described the 
hardness of Al-alloyed MoSi2 fabricated by using arc 
melting; the hardness was decreasing with increasing 
the Al content in the range of 0-10at.% (i.e. MoSi2 
to MoSi,,Al0.3) and then increasing with increasing it 
in the range of 10-15 at.%(i.e. MoSi„Al03 to 
MoSi1.55Al0 .45). Cllb dissolved Al up to 3 at.% and 
single phase C40 appeared more than 10 at.% Al. 
They explained that the decrement of hardness was 
due to the solid solution "softening" (i.e. Al into 
Si-site of Cll b) and the increment was due to the 
crystallographical modification (i.e. Cllb to C40). 
For powder metallurgically processed systems, 
however, other phases which are dispersing in 
alloys affect the hardness in addition to above 
mentioned mechanisms. Although Cllb phase 
should be softened by dissolving Al (up to 3at.%), 
AI2O3 and Mo¬5Si3C¬1 dispersion should give hard-
ening effect. Combination of these opposite effects, 
the hardness value of Mo-Si-Al was a little higher 
than that of MoSi2. As for strength, the strength-
ening was mainly due to the Al2O3 formation instead 
of glassy SiO2.. On the contrary to hardness and 
strength, the toughness value of Mo-Si-Al was
slightly smaller than that of MoSi2. This was 
because the intragranular fracture was dominant for 
Mo-Si-Al in contrast with MoSi2; both inter- and 
intragranular fracture was observed for MoSi2. 
Furthermore, the change of intrinsic bonding 
strength may affect the toughness. 
Combination of alloying and dispersion
It has been reported that SiC dispersion is fairly 
effective to enhance mechanical properties of 
MoSi2. Alloying and composite techniques 
were simultaneously adapted to obtain better 
mechanical properties. The Mo-Si-Al/15vol%SiC 
system possessed relatively fine grain size and fairly 
high mechanical properties, especially for hardness. 
The hardening was due to the hard phases (i.e. 
Al2O3, Mo¬5Si3C¬1, and SiC) dispersion, the grain 
growth inhibition and the decrease of solid solution 
softening effect with decreasing total Al content. The 
strengthening was mainly attributed to the grain 
growth inhibition, and the toughening was caused 
by residual stress induced by thermal expansion 
mismatch.

4 Conclusions
The present work reveals microstructure and 
mechanical properties of the Mo-Si-Al alloy and the 
Mo-Si-Al/SiC composite. The results are summa-
rized as follows; 
1. Mo-Si-Al alloy and Mo-Si-Al/SiCcomposite 
were fabricated by the powder metallurgical process 
from a novel prealloyed powder. The alloy was 
composed of MoSi2, Mo(Si,Al)2, (ƒ¿-Al2O3 and Moss 
Si3C¬1. The composite was composed ofthese four 
phases and (3-SiC. The intergranular glassy SiO2 
phase could be changed into thermodynamically 
stable (ƒ¿-Al2O3 by the alloying.
2. Mechanical properties such as hardness and 
strength were enhanced by alloying. Combination 
of alloying and composite techniques, hardness, 
strength, toughness and high-temperature strength 
were simultaneously improved.
3. These results indicate that the combination of 
alloying and composite techniques has great advan-
tages to fabricate MoSi2-based materials with well-
balanced mechanical properties. Further optimi-
zation of fabrication conditions will enable to 
improve the mechanical properties of MoSi2-based 
system.
