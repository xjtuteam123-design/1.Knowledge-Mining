ABSTRACT
It is quite sensitive for TiAl alloy to composition, and the solidification microstructure and mechanical properties 
may be changed by compositional minor-adjustment. In this paper, two TNM-based alloys with different solid -
ification modes (TNM-45Al alloy and TNM-0.3Si-0.7C alloy) were obtained basing on TNM alloy via two 
different compositional minor-adjustment routes. Compared with TNM alloy, the effects of compositional minor- 
adjustment routes on localized segregation, solidification microstructure and compression properties at room 
temperature were investigated. It is found that the solidification mode of TNM-45Al alloy is still β solidification, 
compared with TNM alloy, localized segregation is evidently weakened, lamellar colonies are coarsened and 
solidification texture is strengthened, and the yield strength, compressive strength and fracture strain at room 
temperature are increased by 14.04 %, 30.21 % and 28.97 %, respectively. And the solidification mode of TNM- 
0.3Si-0.7C alloy is transformed into peritectic solidification, with regional polarization of localized segregation, 
rapid coarsening of lamellar colonies and severe strengthening of solidification texture, the yield strength 
increased by 16.51 % at room temperature, and the compressive strength and fracture strain decreased by 32.01 
% and 71.31 %, respectively. It is indicated that the coarsening of lamellar colonies and the intensification of 
solidification texture of TNM-45Al alloy can be inhibited by the precipitation of TiB, but those of TNM-0.3Si-0.7C 
alloy can not be restrained by the TiB/Ti
5
Si
3 
symbiotic structure found for the first time and the precipitation of 
TiB. In addition, the change of localized segregation behavior is the main factor for the strengthening and 
toughening of TNM-45Al alloy, and the solid solution of Si/C atoms is the main factor leading to the embrit -
tlement of TNM-0.3Si-0.7C alloy.
1. Introduction
TiAl alloy has excellent properties such as low density, high specific 
strength, high elastic modulus, good high-temperature creep resistance, 
and high-temperature oxidation resistance. It is currently recognized as 
a new type of lightweight and high-temperature structural material that 
can replace nickel based high-temperature alloys [ 1 – 3 ]. It has shown 
remarkable development prospects in aerospace materials. And it is the 
preferred material for advanced military aircraft engines, high-pressure 
compressors, and low-pressure turbine blades with high thrust to weight 
ratio [ 4 – 6 ].
However, the hot deformability of traditional TiAl alloy is poor, and 
it is easy to crack during hot working. In order to improve the difficulty 
of hot working of TiAl alloy, researchers designed the composition of 
TiAl alloy by reducing Al content and increasing β stabilizer elements [ 1 ,
7 ]. Among them, TNM alloy (Ti-43.5Al-4Nb-1Mo-0.1B) developed by 
Clemens team is a typical representative [ 8 ]. The high temperature β 
phase region of the alloy can be extended to the hot working tempera -
ture range. And the high temperature disordered β phase with more 
independent slip systems can be introduced into the hot deformation 
temperature, which has excellent high temperature plasticity. Then the 
hot working temperature and deformation resistance can be signifi -
cantly reduced and the hot working window can be expanded. Thus the 
hot workability of TiAl alloy can be effectively improved. Compared 
with the traditional wrought TiAl alloy, it has obvious advantages [ 8 ].
Although TNM alloy has obvious advantages in hot working, during 
the cooling process after hot working, part of the high temperature 
disordered β phase can be retained and transformed into β

3.1. Solidification microstructure
3.1.1. Segregation behavior in solidified structure
The SEM-BSE morphology of the solidification microstructure of the 
three TNM-based alloys is shown in Fig. 1 . And the low magnification 
microstructure of TNM, TNM-45Al, and TNM-0.3Si-0.7C alloy is shown 
in Fig. 1(a) – (d) and (g) , respectively. According to the BSE contrast 
caused by element distribution, it is not difficult to find that there is 
solidification segregation caused by the redistribution of Al elements 
[ 20 ] at the grain boundary of the primary β phase in the solidification 
microstructure of three TNM-based alloys, but there are differences in 
type and degree. Among them, the solidification segregation of TNM 
alloy is the weakest, and the slight enrichment of Al element at the grain 
boundary of primary β phase leads to the S-segregation [ 21 ]. The type of 
the solidification segregation of TNM-45Al alloy is the same as that of 
TNM alloy, but the degree is stronger and the discontinuous network 
distribution is more obvious. But the solidification segregation of 
TNM-0.3Si-0.7C alloy shows distinct continuous network distribution. 
From our previous research result, the type of segregation is different 
from the other two, which is peritectic segregation [ 19 ]. Based on the 
above results, the classification of solidification segregation types for the 
three TNM-based alloys is shown in Table 1 .
According to numerous previous studies, in addition to solidification 
segregation caused by the redistribution of Al element during the solid- 
liquid phase transition stage, there are also two types of segregation that 
occur during the solid-solid phase transition stage in the solidification 
microstructure of TiAl alloys as the composition changes, namely 
β -segregation and α -segregation [ 14 , 22 , 23 ]. In order to study the effect 
of compositional minor-adjustment routes on the β / α -segregation 
behavior of TNM-based alloys, the solidification microstructure of three 
alloys were studied by dividing into two regions, that is, the primary β 
phase region and the intergranular segregation region. The SEM-BSE 
morphology of typical primary β phase region and intergranular segre -
gation region of TNM alloy is shown in Fig. 1 (b) and (c), respectively. It 
is not difficult to find that there is obvious β -segregation distributed in 
colony boundaries in the primary β phase region of TNM alloy, and only 
a few α -segregation bands are located inside colony, while the

3.1.3. Solidification texture
Fig. 3 shows the IPF orientation distribution and pole plot distribu -
tion of γ lamellae in the solidification microstructure of three alloys. It 
can be seen that the γ lamellae orientation of TNM alloy has the lowest 
polar density, only 19.67, while that of TNM-45Al alloy increases 
significantly to 25.87, which is 31.52 % higher than that of TNM alloy. 
The value of TNM-0.3Si-0.7C alloy is the highest of the three, reaching 
28.31, which is 43.92 % and 9.43 % higher than that of TNM alloy and 
TNM-45Al alloy, respectively. This means that the preferred orientation 
of lamellar structure of TNM alloy is the weakest and the orientation 
distribution is the most uniform. The preferred orientation of lamellar 
structure of TNM-45Al alloy and TNM-0.3Si-0.7C alloy is significantly 
higher than that of TNM alloy, and the concentration of orientation 
distribution is significantly increased, especially in TNM-0.3Si-0.7C 
alloy. From the perspective of the uniformity of orientation distribu -
tion of lamellar structure, two TNM-based alloys after compositional 
minor-adjustment are worse than that of TNM alloy to a certain extent, 
but TNM-45Al alloy is slightly better than TNM-0.3Si-0.7C alloy.

4. Discussion
4.1. Solidification behavior and localized segregation
4.1.1. Solidification behavior
According to our previous research results [ 19 ], different composi -
tional micro-adjustment routes have different effects on the solidifica -
tion behavior of TNM-based alloys. The solidification path of TNM alloy 
is typical β solidification [ 9 ], and the solidification microstructure can 
be divided into primary β phase region and S-segregation region. After 
increasing the Al content of TNM alloy to 45 %, the solidification path of 
the TNM-45Al alloy can not be changed, but the S-segregation is 
intensified. However, the solidification behavior is changed obviously 
after trace doping of Si and C in TNM-0.3Si-0.7C alloy. Although the 
primary phase is still β phase, the liquid-solid phase transformation is 
completed in the form of peritectic solidification in the solidification 
segregation region due to the enrichment of α stabilizer elements, and 
the solidification microstructure can be divided into primary β phase 
region and peritectic segregation region.
4.1.2. Localized segregation
It can be seen from 3.1.1 that different compositional minor- 
adjustment routes have different effects on the localized segregation 
behavior of TNM-based alloys. There is opposite segregation behavior 
between the primary β phase region and intergranular segregation re -
gion of TNM alloy, which is mainly related to the composition difference 
between two regions. Due to the solute redistribution during the solid- 
liquid phase transformation [ 46 ], the primary β phase region is rich in 
β stabilizer elements (Nb/Mo) and poor in Al, while the intergranular 
segregation region is rich in Al and poor in β stabilizer elements 
(Nb/Mo). Theoretically, the higher the content of localized β stabilizer 
elements and the lower the content of Al, the more inclined to appear 
β-segregation. On the contrary, it is easy to appear α -segregation. So 
β/ α -segregation can be changed with the change of localized chemical 
composition. Based on this analysis, assuming that the content of other 
alloy elements is constant and only the Al element is increased, the alloy 
microstructure as a whole must show a weaker β/ α -segregation distri -
bution, which well explains the weaker β/ α -segregation distribution of 
the TNM-45Al alloy as a whole compared with the basic TNM alloy.
Compared with TNM alloy, the difference in chemical composition of 
TNM-0.3Si-0.7C alloy is only the addition of Si/C element, while the 
contents of Al element and β stabilizer elements are the same. So the 
localized segregation behavior of TNM-0.3Si-0.7C alloy is completely 
different from that of TNM alloy. Both Si and C are α stabilizer elements 
[ 47 ], which tend to be enriched in the intergranular segregation region 
of TNM-0.3Si-0.7C alloy. The Si content in the intergranular segregation 
region is 64.29 % higher than that in the primary β phase region. And 
silicide is precipitated in the solid-solid phase transition stage because 
the Si content exceeds the limit solid solubility of this region, while the 
complete solid solution of Si element is realized in the primary β phase 
region.
4.2. Lamellar colonies
From 3.1.2 to 3.1.3, it can be known that different compositional 
minor-adjustment routes have different effects on the lamellar colonies 
of TNM-based alloys, which are mainly reflected in two aspects: colony 
size and lamellae orientation. The main reasons for the influence in two 
aspects: one is the difference in solidification path and localized segre -
gation behavior, and the other is the difference in the precipitation 
behavior of trace compound phase.
4.2.1. Effect of solidification path and localized segregation on the lamellar 
colonies
For TNM alloy, the liquid-solid phase transformation is completed by 
β solidification, and then both the primary β phase region and the S- 
segregation region undergo solid-solid phase transition in the form of 
β→ α . There is a very high nucleation rate for α phase (the predecessor of 
lamellar colonies) because of the multiple nucleation effect of β→ α [ 14 ,
46 , 48 , 49 ], coupled with the overall low Al content, the growth of α 
grains can be effectively restricted by β-segregation. Hereby, favourable 
microstructure of lamellar colonies can be led to, and the average size 
and texture are the best of the three.
After increasing the Al content of TNM alloy to 45 %, although the 
solidification path remains unchanged in the overall range, it is known 
from the phase diagram of TNM alloy [ 1 ] that the synchronous increase 
of the temperature range of β→ α phase transformation and the decrease 
of the overall degree of β-segregation can be resulted in by the increase 
of Al content. And the growth of α grains can be jointly promoted 
dynamically. Thus, the coarsening of lamellar colonies in TNM-45Al 
alloy is caused. It is worth noting that the non-uniformity factor of 
lamellar colonies in TNM-45Al alloy increases synchronously with the 
average size. It can be known from 3.1.1 that the S-segregation can be 
aggravated by the increase of Al content, resulting in the difference of Al 
concentration between the primary β phase region and the S-segregation 
region [ 50 ], which increases the difference in the temperature range of 
β→ α phase transition and the degree of β-segregation between the two 
regions. Ultimately, an increase in the gap in the size of lamellar colonies 
between the two regions can be led to. In addition, the solidification 
texture can also be strengthened by the overall coarsening of lamellar 
colonies.
However, after trace doping of Si and C into the TNM alloy, the so -
lidification path of the solidification segregation region is transformed 
into peritectic solidification. And the area between several primary β 
phase dendrites is connected by an peritectic α grain, resulting in the 
rapid coarsening of lamellar colonies. But there is still the refinement 
effect caused by the multiple nucleation of β→ α in the primary β phase 
region. Thus, the bimodal size distribution of lamellar colonies in TNM- 
0.3Si-0.7C alloy can be caused. In addition, due to the peritectic α phase, 
there is not only the largest average size of lamellar colonies for TNM- 
0.3Si-0.7C alloy, but also the most significant solidification texture.

4.4. Summary of compositional minor-adjustment strategies
This paper is based on a typical β-solidified TiAl alloy, namely TNM 
alloy, which aims to reduce the volume fraction of β(β
0
) phase in the as- 
cast microstructure of TNM-based alloy through two compositional 
minor-adjustment paths, namely increasing the Al content and adding 
strong α stabilizer elements. The goal is to achieve the strengthening and 
toughness of the as-cast TNM-based alloy at room temperature. Based on 
the preliminary analysis in this paper, it can be seen that under the 
compositional minor-adjustment path of increasing the Al content and 
the premise of still controlling the solidification mode within the β-so -
lidification range, the increase in Al content can reduce the volume 
fraction of β(β
0
) phase in the as-cast microstructure through the change 
in localized segregation behavior, and ultimately achieve a significant 
synergistic improvement in compressive strength and plasticity at room 
temperature. However, under the compositional minor-adjustment path 
of adding strong α -stabilizer elements, although the volume fraction of 
β(β
0
) phase in the as-cast microstructure can be reduced through the 
decrease of localized segregation with the transformation of solidifica -
tion mode from β-solidification to peritectic solidification, the embrit -
tlement effect introduced along with solid solution strengthening is 
obviously stronger. Therefore, the strengthening and toughness of the 
as-cast TNM-based alloys at room temperature cannot be achieved by 
this path. Obviously, the compositional minor-adjustment idea for TNM- 
based alloys summarized in this paper can be extended to other β-so -
lidified TiAl alloys to guide the next compositional design work.
5. Conclusions
In this paper, two TNM-based alloys with different solidification 
modes (TNM-45Al alloy and TNM-0.3Si-0.7C alloy) were obtained 
basing on TNM alloy via two different compositional minor-adjustment 
routes. Compared with TNM alloy, the effects of compositional minor- 
adjustment routes on localized segregation, solidification microstruc -
ture and compression properties at room temperature were studied. The 
main conclusions are as follows:
(1) With the increase of Al content, the overall β/ α segregation of 
TNM-45Al alloy is weaker than that of TNM alloy. There is net- 
like segregation formed by alternating mixture of β/ α segrega -
tion in the primary β phase region duo to the doping of Si/C, for 
TNM-0.3Si-0.7C alloy, while there is no β/ α segregation in the 
intergranular segregation region.
(2) Duo to the increase of Al content, the lamellar colonies of TNM- 
45Al alloy are coarsened and the solidification texture is 
strengthened, while there is the opposite effect generated by the 
precipitation of TiB.
(3) The lamellar colonies are sharply coarsened and the solidification 
texture is strongly intensified owing to peritectic solidification, 
for TNM-0.3Si-0.7C alloy, while this tendency can not be 
inhibited by the TiB/Ti
5
Si
3 
symbiotic structure found for the first 
time and the precipitation of TiB.
(4) Compared with TNM alloy, the yield strength, compressive 
strength and fracture strain of TNM-45Al alloy at room temper -
ature are increased by 14.04 %, 30.21 % and 28.97 %, respec -
tively. And the change of localized segregation behavior is the 
main factor for the strengthening and toughening of TNM-45Al 
alloy.
(5) Compared with TNM alloy, the yield strength is increased by 
16.51 % at room temperature, and the compressive strength and 
fracture strain are decreased by 32.01 % and 71.31 %, respec -
tively. And the solid solution of Si/C atoms is the main factor 
leading to the embrittlement of TNM-0.3Si-0.7C alloy.
