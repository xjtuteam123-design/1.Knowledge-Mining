abstract
T o determine the influence of R e and Ru on the phase morphologies and microstructur e in Ni-based su-
perallo ys, a series of model alloy s deriv ed from the 4th gener ation TMS-1 38 superallo y ha ve been in-
vestigat ed with syst emically changing R e and Ru additions. All alloy s are subjected to solid solution and
aging treatments to achieve the typical γ / γ′ two phases microstructur e. Phase transition temper atures are
measured by using differential scanning calorimetry . The R e is found to increase the γ′ -solvus temper ature
with a slope of r oughly 5.4 ℃ per wt% R e in Ru-fr ee alloy s and 2.3 ℃ per wt% R e in 2Ru alloy s, while the Ru
e xhibits much weaker effect. Microstructur e and composition of the two phases are e xamined to under -
stand the R e and Ru effects on γ′ size, shape, volume fraction, the elemental partition beha vior , and the γ / γ′
lattice misfit. The results show that the addition of R e affects size and shape of γ′ precipitat es, and also
reduces γ / γ′ lattice misfit significantly . Mean while, R e increases partition coefficients of Cr and Ru. In
comparison, the addition of Ru seems to ha ve little effect on morphology of γ′ pr ecipitates and the γ / γ′
lattice misfit, but Ru appears to alter partitioning beha viors of other elements. Because R e and Ru are two
key elements for high gener ation Ni-based single crystal (SX) superallo ys, this wor k might pro vide
guidelines for designing ne xt gener ation of SX superallo ys.

4. 1. The dependence of elements partitioning beha vior on Re and Ru
elements
The partitioning beha vior of refr actory elements often de-

Fig. 6 show s the partitioning coefficient of elements in two phases
after heat treatm ent. It is found that Co, Cr , Mo, W , R e and Ru seg-
regat e to the γ phase, and the seq uence is R e > Cr > Co > Ru > Mo >
W in accor dance with the tenden cy to partition into the matrix; Al
and T a fa vor γ′ phase. The addition of 4% Ru to TMS-1 38 alloy results
in more R e and Mo partitioning to the γ′ phase. This phenomenon is
so-called “r everse partitioning effect” induced by Ru [32,38] . The
presence of Ru leads to more γ formers partition to the γ′ phase,
re ducing concentra tion of refr actory elements in the matrix, which
inhibits the formation of TCP phases. This is fa vor able for Ni-based
super alloy s because TCP phases consume a large number of re-
fract ory elements from the γ -matrix (such as Mo, R e, Cr , W , etc.) and
thus weaken strength of the solid solution matrix. Moreo ver , TCP
phases are all brittle phases, which w ould become the source for
crac k initiation and propagation during service condition.
4.2. The dependence of γ ’ size on Re and Ru elements
The γ′ size is observ ed to decrease as the misfit strain became
progr essiv ely more negativ e. The inter diffusion coefficients of tran-
sition metals in Ni indicate that R e is the slow est diffuser [39–4 1] ,
and elements with low diffusion coefficient can inhibit the gro wth of
γ′ phase. R e mainly distributes in the γ matrix, and its concentra tion
in the γ′ phase is e xtre mely low . The higher the concentration of
slo w-diffusion R e in the matrix, the strong er the hindrance to the
gro wth of the γ′ phase. Among eight alloy s in this study , as the R e
concentra tion in the matrix gr adually increases, the hindrance effi-
ciency to the gro wth of γ′ phase is enhanced, re sulting in decreased
γ′ size ( Fig. 4 ). Ru is enriched in γ phase, and the low diffusion
coefficient might hinder γ′ gro wth, therefor e, γ′ size decreases with
the addition of R u in R e-fr ee alloy s. In alloy s with 5% R e and v arious
R u additions, due to “r everse partitioning effect” of Ru , less γ -

5. Summary and conclusion
The effects of R e and Ru on the phase morphologies and micro-
structure of Ni-based SX superallo ys ha ve been syst emically in-
vestigat ed by a series of model alloy s deriv ed from the paren t TMS-
1 38 alloy . The element R e play s an important role on the γ′ volume
fraction, γ′ size, γ/γ′ partition coefficient and γ/γ′ lattice misfit. In
contrast, the effect of Ru is much less ob vious. Our findings can be
summarized as follow s:
1 . The addition of R e effectiv ely increases the γ′ sol vus temper ature
of the alloy . Quantitativ ely , the sol vus temper atur e incr eases at a
rat e of about 5.4 ℃ per wt% of R e in Ru-fr ee alloy s and 2.3 ℃ per
wt% in containing Ru alloy s. Ho wev er , R u has less effect on the
solvus temper ature than R e.
2. The incr ease of R e concentra tion decreases volum e fraction and
size of γ′ phase, which possibly re sults from the low diffusion
coefficient of R e. In comparison, the increase of Ru only reduces
the γ′ volume fraction , but has little effect on the size of γ′ phase.
3. R e aggra v ates the segregation of Cr and R u in matrix, while the
addition of Ru alleviat es the tendency of R e into matrix, ther efore
increasing R u concentra tion would result in increase of γ′ size in
alloy s with 5 wt% R e.
4. The addition of R e reduces the two-phase misfit significantly ,
while Ru has less effect. The shape of precipitat es appears to
depend on the sign of misfits. For e xample, γ′ precipitat es e xhibit
spherical morphologies in alloy s with low R e-concentration

shapes in alloy s with R e addition which leads to negativ e lattice
misfit.
