The possibility of replacing commercial high-temperature alloys of the ZhS6U type by alloys based on nickel 
aluminides is considered. A complex study of alloys based on nickel aluminide has been conducted with deter- 
mination of ways to increase their ductility, heat-resistance, and strength in a wide range of operating tempera- 
tures. 
It is known that the working and nozzle blades of gas- 
turbine engines (GTE) operate under considerable stresses 
and temperatures in an aggressive medium under a large 
number of heat cycles. 
Analyzing published data, we will see that the most 
promising high-temperature materials are nickel aluminides 
(Ni3AI and NiAI) [1, 2]. 
Alloys based on them can potentially be used in 
high-temperature parts of aircraft GTE, high-temperature 
fastening hardware, parts of casting injector installations, 
pumps, dies, equipment for casting aluminum, bearings, 
heating elements, etc. [2, 3]. 
In Russia materials based on nickel aluminides find limi- 
ted use. This is mainly connected with the scarceness of data 
on the properties of aluminides, the principles of their alloy- 
ing [4], the microstructure, and results of their testing and 
use in industry. 
Nickel aluminides have high melting temperatures and 
oxidation resistance. They are more ductile than ceramic ma- 
terials, possess good technological properties in synthesis 
and melting in vacuum, and have a wide range of homogene- 
ity, which makes it possible to alloy them with the aim of ob- 
taining requisite properties (Table 1) [1]. The main short- 
coming of nickel aluminides is their elevated brittleness at 
room temperature [3]. 
A complex study of nickel aluminides allowed us to find 
ways to increase their ductility, strength, and heat resistance 
in a wide temperature range. As a result we developed two 
alloys VKNA-2M (based on Ni3A1 and NiAI) and VKNA-IV 
(based on Ni3AI) used for facing in large-scale, repair, and 
pilot production of GTE to improve their working and nozzle 
blades and parts of combustion chambers [5, 6].

Alloy VKNA-2M is recommended for facing contact 
surfaces of shroud flanges of working blades made of alloys 
of the ZhS6U type operating for a long time at 900 - 975~ 
under compressive stresses and frequent impacts with the 
aim of increasing the resistance to deformation, oxidation, 
and wear. This replacement increases the service life of the 
blades by a factor of 2 - 3 [5]. 
However, the alloy is inapplicable for production of parts 
that experience mechanical loads under high temperatures. 
The low ductility and the insufficient high-temperature 
strength (at 1100~ are caused by the presence of a 13-phase 
that embrittles the alloy. Experience acquired in designing 
the alloy allowed us to determine the main rules for alloying 
alloys based on the intermetallic compound Ni3AI and for 
creating in the alloy a heterophase structure that contains 
85 - 90% y' and 10 - 15% ), without formation of 13-phase 
[4]. As a result of a comprehensive study we developed a 
high-temperature casting alloy VKNA-IV that has a high 
heat resistance and strength and withstands long-term use at 
900- 1250~ The alloy can be used for fabricating parts 
that experience loads typical of GTE.
