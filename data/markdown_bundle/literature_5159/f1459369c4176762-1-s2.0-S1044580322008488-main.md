ABSTRACT

1. Introduction 
Currently, the service temperature (above 1200 
◦

based superalloys is close to their melting point, which has been diffi -
cult to meet the development of hot section materials of aero engines 
[ 1 – 3 ]. Among various high temperature structure materials, Nb silicide 
in-situ composites can withstand an operating temperature above 
1200 
◦

based superalloys [ 4 , 5 ]. The main composition of Nb-Si based alloys is

3

In order to solve the above problems and obtain an optimized Nb-Si 
based alloy with excellent comprehensive properties at room tempera -
ture and high temperature, extensive attempts have been made via 
suitable alloying using Mo [ 9 , 10 ], C [ 11 ], Zr [ 12 , 13 ] and Fe [ 14 ], etc. 
The doping of alloying elements will induce the generation of various

composition optimization.

The nominal compositions Nb-24Ti-16Si-2Al- x Zr- y Fe ( x = 0, 4, 6, 8;

non-consumable arc melting in a water-cooled copper crucible. All

composition. Hereafter, the investigated alloys are sequentially abbre -
viated as 0Zr-0Fe, 4Zr-4Fe, 6Zr-4Fe, 8Zr-4Fe, 8Zr-2Fe and 8Zr-6Fe, 
respectively.
