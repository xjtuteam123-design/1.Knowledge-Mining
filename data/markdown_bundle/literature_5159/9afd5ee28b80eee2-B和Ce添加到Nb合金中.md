ABSTRACT
The e ﬀects of trace Ce and B additions on the microstructure Nb-22Ti-3Si alloys were studied. The
microstructure of the alloys was observed by scanning electron microscope (SEM), and their phase
compositions were analyzed with X-ray di ﬀraction (XRD) and Electro-Probe micro-analyzer (EPMA). The
distributions of the elements were detected by Spectrum analyzer. The interface of the phases in the alloys was
investigated by transmission electron microscopy (TEM). The results indicated that two phases of Nbss and
Nb3Si presented in Nb-22Ti-3Si, Nb-22Ti-3Si-0.2Ce and Nb-22Ti-3Si-0.2B alloys. The segregation of Ti at the
interface between Nbss and Nb 3Si was promoted and the volume fraction of silicides in the alloy increased with
the trace B and Ce addition to the Nb-22Ti-3Si alloy respectively. And there was no single and de ﬁnite
orientation relationship between Nb 3Si and Nbss in Nb-22Ti-3Si, Nb-22Ti-3Si-0.2Ce and Nb-22Ti-3Si-0.2B
alloys. Compared with the Nb-22Ti-3Si alloy, the Nbss superlattice structure was found in Nb-22Ti-3Si-0.2Ce
and Nb-22Ti-3Si-0.2B alloys.
1. Introduction
Higher engine e ﬃciency, reduced speci ﬁc fuel consumption, im-
proved component durability and reliability, reduced noise and re-
duced life cycle costs are continually desired in turbines used for
propulsion and power generation [1]. The hot-end components of the
engines are subjected to elevated temperature, high stress, and serious
oxidizing, corrosive and erosive environments. Signi ﬁcant advances in
the performance and e ﬃciency of engine components are strongly
dependent on the development of lighter, more durable high-tempera-
ture materials [2–4]. To meet these demands, the materials beyond the
capabilities of Ni-based superalloys are being sought. Niobium-silicide
based alloys have the potential to replace Ni-based superalloys in
future high temperature aero-engines applications owing to their lower
densities, higher melting points and high strength at elevated tem-
peratures. However, the poor ductility and toughness at room tem-
perature is one of the main problems to limit the application of Nb-Si
system alloys.
The two most important phases in the microstructure of Nb-Si
based alloys are the Nb solid solution (Nbss) and silicides.
Microstructure and phase structure of alloys are the important factors
to a ﬀect the mechanical property of Nb-Si system alloys. So far,
alloying has been widely used to alloys for improving the microstruc-
ture and mechanical properties. According to the previous research [5–
11], alloying with transition metals, including refractory metals, and
with simple metals and metalloids is essential for improving the
mechanical behavior and oxidation of Nb-Si based alloys. B and Ce,
as alloying element, have been studied by adding them to Multi-
component Nb-Si based ultrahigh temperature alloys which the content
of Si in is about 14 –18 at% [12–15].
Based on the previous work, it was found that the Nb-Si based alloy
with 3 at% Si content has both excellent plasticity and a certain extent
of room/high temperature strength. The aim of the present investiga-
tion is to demonstrate the e ﬀect of trace B and Ce addition on the
microstructure and phases interface of the Nb-3Si-22Ti alloy.
2. Experimental procedures
All of the starting materials used in this work are high purity
elements: 99.99% Nb ingots, 99.9% Ti sponges, 99.99% Si crystals,
99.9% Ce and 97% B particles. The purities of starting materials are
given in weight percent. The Nb-3Si-22Ti alloy was used as the base
composition and the Nb was replaced by 0.2 at% B and Ce respectively.
Each alloy ingot was prepared by non-consumable tungsten electrode

3. Results and discussions
3.1. Phases structure and microstructure
3.1.1. Phases composition
XRD patterns of cast Nb-22Ti-3Si alloys added with 0.2 at% B and
Ce were shown in Fig. 1. As shown in Fig. 1, the XRD results ( Fig. 1(a)–
(c)) indicated that two phases of Nbss and Nb
3Si existed in the three
alloys of Nb-22Ti-3Si, Nb-22Ti-3Si-0.2B and Nb-22Ti-3Si-0.2Ce,
which was also con ﬁrmed by EPMA results, as shown in Fig. 2 (a)–(c)
and Table 1. It suggested that the trace addition of B and Ce could not
change the phase constitute of Nb-3Si-22Ti alloys.
3.1.2. Microstructure
Fig. 2 showed the backscatter electron (BSE) photographs for the
Nb-22Ti-3Si alloys with the addition of B and Ce. And the data in
Table 1 summarized the analyses values for each element in the phases
of the alloys by EPMA. It is evident from Table 1 and Fig. 2 that the
microstructure strongly depends on the trace addition of B and Ce.
As shown in Fig. 2 (a) and (b) , there are two phases, which have
gray-white and dark-gray contrasts in backscattered electron image in
Nb-3Si-22Ti alloy. The EPMA analysis revealed that the gray-white
phase is Nbss, and dark-gray phase is Nb 3Si. It can be observed that
Nb3Si phase has two morphologies, i.e., network shape and particle
shape (with the average size of about 8 µm) distributed in the Nbss
matrix. The thickness of the network Nb 3Si phase was less than 5 µm.
And the volume fraction of Nb 3Si phases was 8% calculated by the
metallographic image analysis software.
For the Nb-3Si-22Ti-0.2B alloy, it can be seen from Fig. 2(c) and (d)
that the microstructure of the alloy consists of gray-white phase Nbss,
dark-gray phase Nb 3Si and black phase Nb 3Si-rich Ti. Compared with
the Nb-3Si-22Ti alloy, the trace B addition decreased the content of Ti
in Nb 3Si phase and promoted the segregation of Ti at the interface
between Nbss and Nb 3Si. So that some Nb 3Si-rich Ti phase was
present. Meanwhile, the part of net-work Nb 3Si phase was broken as
B added. The volume fraction of Nb 3Si phase and Nb3Si-rich Ti phase
were 7.2% and 1.5%, respectively.
The microstructure of the Nb-22Ti-3Si-0.2Ce alloy was given in
Fig. 2 (e) and (f). It can be found that the trace Ce addition changed
remarkably microstructural morphology of the alloys. The microstruc-
ture of the Nb-22Ti-3Si-0.2Ce alloy was also composed of gray-white
phase Nbss, dark-gray phase Nb
3Si, a small amount of black phase
Nb3Si-rich Ti and a new white phase CeO 2. Generally speaking, the
microstructure of the Nb-22Ti-3Si alloy was re ﬁned with trace Ce
addition. And parts of the dark-gray Nb 3Si network distributed on
boundaries of the Nbss phases were broken and the volume fractions of
Nb3Si increased to 8.8% from 8% in Nb-22Ti-3Si alloy ( Fig. 2 (b)).
Simultaneously, Nbss phase presented as more obvious dendrite
morphology as the trace amount of Ce was added. Compared with
the Nb-22Ti-3Si alloy, the formation of Nb
3Si-rich Ti phase was due to
the segregation of Ti element by the trace addition of Ce.
In addition, in the process of alloy melting, the Ce addition caused
the ﬂow ability of the alloy liquid to become worse, so that the
distribution of Ce element was not homogenous in the alloy. And
element Ce mainly existed in the form of CeO 2 which can be veri ﬁed by
the spectrum analysis ( Fig. 3 ).
3.2. Phases interface and superlattice in Nb solid solution
3.2.1. Phases interface
The TEM images of Nb-22Ti-3Si alloy are given in Fig. 4 . Fig. 4 (a)
shows the bright ﬁeld micrographs of the phase interface between Nbss
and Nb3Si. It was found that the interface was ﬂat and clean. And there
was no a new precipitate, but the cracks appeared at the interface. It
suggested that the interface bonding strength is very weak and hence
the cracking source was easy to form at the interface under the action of
a stress. Fig. 4 (b) and (c) described the electron di ﬀraction pattern
from Nb3Si and Nbss, respectively. It was di ﬃcult to get the composite
district electron diﬀraction pattern consisting of the electron di ﬀraction
pattern both Nb 3Si and Nbss simultaneously. This indicates that there
was no a single and de ﬁnite orientation relationship between Nb 3Si and
Nbss. In addition, the electron di ﬀraction pattern from Nb 3Si (beam
direction is near Nb 3Si [110]) and Nbss (beam direction is near
Nbss[111]) are also single spots. It was proofed again that the
microstructure of the Nb-22Ti-3Si alloy is mainly composed of Nbss
and Nb
3Si phase as mentioned above.

be seen that the interface between Nbss and Nb 3Si was also ﬂat and
clean. Meanwhile, there was no a precipitate present yet with B
addition. Just like the Nb-22Ti-3Si alloy, there was no a single and

4. Conclusion
1. The trace addition of B and Ce can't change the phase constitute of
Nb-3Si-22Ti alloy, but the volume fraction of silicide in the alloy
increases. Meanwhile, B and Ce additions promote the segregation
of Ti element. In addition, the solubility of Si in Nbss can be
improved as B added.
