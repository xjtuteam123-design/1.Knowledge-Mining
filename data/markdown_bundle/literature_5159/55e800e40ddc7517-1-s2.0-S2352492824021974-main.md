1. Introduction
In the 21st century, the rapid development of high-end 
manufacturing in aerospace, ocean engineering, large aircraft, high- 
speed rail equipment, biomedicine and other fields requires the devel -
opment of a large number of new multifunctional materials. The 
development of new materials with excellent properties starts from the 
component design of new materials. The composition of traditional al -
loys is mainly composed of a single principal component element. 
Adding trace elements is to adjust the performance of traditional alloys 
[1 – 4] . The limitation of component design method for traditional alloy 
is that only a small part of the design space for a given material 
component is allowed to be improved, thus limiting the development of 
new material components and performance improvement [5] . In 2004, 
composition design of high entropy alloys (HEAs) changed traditional 
composition design [6 – 8] . HEAs defined by Ye need to contain more 
than five main elements and each element content is between 5 % and 
35 % (at%); another component of HEA is defined as equiatomic ratio or 
near equiatomic ratio, which is beneficial to formation of single-phase 
solid solution [6,7] . For example, the equiatomic ratio multicompo -
nent CoCrFeMnNi HEA known as “ Cantor alloy ” presents a single-phase 
solid solution phase [8] . Based on the definition of high entropy alloy, 
the early research ideas mainly focused on configuration entropy and 
searching for single-phase solid solution alloy [9] . This idea excludes 
new results in new alloy systems only according to the number of ele -
ments used or the number and type of phases formed, thus limiting the 
number of new alloy systems to be explored. Due to the fact that the 
mixing entropies of ternary near equiatomic ratio ZrNbHf alloys [10] , 
quaternary equiatomic ratio NbMoTaW [11 – 13] and NbTiVZr [14,15]
are larger than those of traditional alloys, the three alloys also have large 
mixing entropies, that is, typical HEAs, but smaller than those of five or 
more HEAs [5] . Therefore, some researchers expand the definition of 
HEA, and call ternary or quaternary alloy with equal or near equal 
atomic ratio as medium entropy alloy. In addition, the light

some scholars have proposed names such as multi-principal component 
alloys (MCAs), multiphase alloys, multiphase composite alloys, and 
complex concentrated alloys. Among them, multi-principal component 
alloys include all alloys in the field of HEAs [9] .
HEA or MCAs is superior to conventional metal materials in hard -
ness, strength, toughness, thermal stability, electromagnetism, corro -
sion resistance and radiation resistance, which has high academic 
research value and industrial development potential [18 – 23] . For 
example, the face-centered cubic high entropy alloy has good radiation 
resistance [18,24] and plastic deformation [25,26] , while the 
body-centered cubic high entropy alloy has high strength [27] at high 
temperature and good biocompatibility [28] . In recent years, to improve 
the ductility and strength balance of single-phase HEAs, researchers 
have developed eutectic HEAs with good strength and plasticity com -
bination, which has attracted extensive attention [29 – 32] . In addition, 
the basic idea of component design of CoCrFeNiNb eutectic HEA is to 
find elements with eutectic points in binary phase diagram, which has 
been proved in the design of metallic glass components, namely “ deep 
eutectic rule ” or “ mixing method of binary eutectic unit ” [33 – 35] . At 
present, the design of MCAs components comes from the design of high 
entropy alloy and metallic glass components. In metallic glasses, the 
atomic percentages of the main elements are generally in the eutectic 
range [33 – 35] . Eutectic unit is used as a condition for the selection of 
component in MCAs to obtain relatively stable solution. For multiphase 
MCAs, the stable solution need the stable intermetallics to break the 
equilibrium during cooling process, and the precipitation of stable phase 
is restrained by other precipitates, which makes that the alloys have the 
small particle size. The alloy with the samller particle has the higher 
strength. The intermetallics were found in HEAs [36 – 38] . When the 
alloy melt is cooled, stable intermetallics will nucleate first, and break 
the balance of melt, leading to the separation of competing crystalline 
phases [39] . The eutectic melt can slow down the nucleation and growth 
of intermetallics or competing crystalline phase. Therefore, the high 
strength Ti – Cu – Ni – Nb multiphase MCAs were reported by mixing the
