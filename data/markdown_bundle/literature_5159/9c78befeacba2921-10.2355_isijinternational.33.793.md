l. Introduction
The use of ordered oe2 phase (Ti3Al) to strengthen c(
phase titanium alloys is one possible approach in the
development of newhigh temperature titanium alloys.
The authors' group examined the effects of volume
fraction of the o(2 phase on creep and tensile properties
of oe+ o(2 tyPe titanium alloys using ten Ti-A1SnZr al-
loys designed by thermodynamiccalculations.1'2) Based
on their results, at 823K the Vc(2 range from 0.1 to 0.2
wasproposed as the best design condition for this type
of alloys.
In addition, Nband Si are potential additives because
Nbis knownto improve ductility in the ce2 phase3) and
Si is effective in improving creep resistance.4)
In the present investigation, the program was set up
to construct a design methodfor oe +0e2 type Ti-Al-Sn-
ZrNb-Si alloys. Athermodynamicanalysis of o( and c(2
phases in the Ti-A1Sn-Zr-Nb system was performed
by meansof two sublattice model.5) As the next step,
five Ti-Al-Sn-Zr-Nb alloys (GT-79, 88-91), which
have Voc2 of 0.1 or 0.2 (proposed design condition2)),
were designed by thermodynamic calculations. The
target of this study is to examine the effects of micro-
structure on creep and room temperature tensile prop-
erties in these designed alloys.
