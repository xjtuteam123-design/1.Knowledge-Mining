ABSTRACT
The inter-diffusion of segregating elements (Nb, Mo, and Ti) in the cast ingot of Inconel 718
superalloy during homogenisation at elevated temperature was studied by a new model based
on the characteristic diffusion length. The proposed simple method was used for obtaining
the homogenisation time required for back-diffusion of each element. Therefore, the devel-
oped model can be used for both studying the diffusion of alloying elements and estimation
of homogenisation time, where the latter is important from the industrial standpoint.

Summary
In summary, the inter-diffusion of Nb, Mo, and Ti
as the main segregating elements in the cast ingot of
Inconel 718 superalloy during homogenisation at ele-
vated temperature was studied by a new model, which
is based on the characteristic diffusion length. This
simplemethodwasusedforobtainingthehomogenisa-
tion time required for back-diffusion of each element.
Therefore, the developed model can be used for both
studying the diffusion of alloying elements and esti-
mation of homogenisation time, where the latter is
important from the industrial standpoint, where the
inhibition of grain growth and saving the processing
timeareofutmostimportance.
