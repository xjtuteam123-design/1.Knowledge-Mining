Introduction Recently, development of heat-resistant alloys is strongly required from the efficiency of heat-energy exchanger at elevated-temperatures. Many trials have been performed to develop heat-resistant alloys. Consequently, nickel base super-alloys such as Inconel 718 and Rene41 are used for the high-temperature service at temperatures up to 1273 K. Ni base super-alloys are composed of various elements; main el- ements are Ni, Cr, and Fe, additional elements Nb, Mo, Ti and so on. In laboratory scale, several studies of heat resistant alloys were studied. Ishikawa et al. investigated on workability of a Cr��50Ni alloy by the addition of Ti, Nb, Zr and Re. l) They re- ported that Ti, Nb and Zr were effective for the improvement of workability. Nakagawa et al. reported that hardness of a 2) Cr��50Ni alloy was increased by the addition of aluminum. In addition, Abiko and co-workers found that the ductility of 3��6) a Cr��Fe alloy is remarkable improvement by purification. In this paper, a high-purity Cr��50Ni alloy was melted and ex- amined for the mechanical properties at high temperatures. 2. Experimental Procedure 2.1 Preparation of Cr��50Ni ingot A high-purity Cr��50Ni alloy of 2 kg was melted with high frequency induction heating by using a cold copper crucible7) in argon atmosphere of 4.0 x 104 Pa. The base pressure be- fore putting the argon gas was 1.1 x 10-7 Pa. Before the melt- ing, the chamber together with the crucible and raw materials were baked in order to reduce the water vapor pressure. Fur- ther more, the raw materials were pre-heated in an ultra-high vacuum of 5.1 x 10��6 Pa in order to remove the gaseous im- purities on their surface. 2.2 Preparation of specimens The ingot was die-forged to bars of 7 mm in diameter, af- ter holding at 1373 K for 300 s in the atmosphere of high- purity argon. The reduction was more than 80%. Specimens for tensile-testing at high-temperatures were machined from these bars. The dimension of the specimens is shown in Fig. 1. With alloy phase diagram, Cr�� 50Ni alloy consists of a phase which is high Ni concentration and y phase which is high Cr concentration. In the Ni-rich grains, the analyzed contents corresponded with the expected contents from phase diagram. In the Cr-rich grains, the an- alyzed contents of Cr concentration is lower than expected contents. For the cause, it seemed to receive the effect of the Ni-rich grain around the Cr-rich or revise of the apparatus is necessary. Generally, that the phase controls influences mechanical propeny in the two phases alloy. Then the control of these phases seems to be very important in this Cr��50Ni alloy. 4. Conclusion A high-purity Cr��50Ni alloy possessed the excellent strength and ductility at high-temperatures. Further improve- ment of properties on the ductility and the strength will be realized by the control of microstructure with Cr-rich phase and Ni-rich phase and funher purification.
