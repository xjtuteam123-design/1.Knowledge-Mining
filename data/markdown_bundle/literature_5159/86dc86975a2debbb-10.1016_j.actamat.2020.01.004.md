Abstract: Since half a century ago, researche rs have continuously focused on 
developing γʹ -strengthened Co-base superalloys to achieve an increased power and 
efficiency; these alloys can supposedly operate at higher temperatures than Ni -base 
superalloys. However, the yielded results have failed to meet the expectations. Herein, 
we successfully design novel W-free Co-V-Ta-base alloys by employing machine 
learning algorithm and CALPHAD methods, which exhibit low mass density (8.67-8.86

g/cm3), an extremely wide γ/γʹ region, a high γʹ solvus temperature (up to 1044 ℃), and 
a high strength. The atom probe tomography results show that titanium is a n extremely 
strong γʹ-former; therefore, it is expected to improve the thermodynamic stability of the 
γʹ phase. Furthermore, b esides the very high tensile strength (18.7 GPa) of γʹ phase, 
indicated by first-principles calculations, the strength of Ti -incorporated alloy is higher

750 ℃, which is comparable to that of a few commercial Ni-base superalloys. Therefore, 
the possibility of the Co-V-Ta-base system being a candidate for developing novel 
Co-base superalloys is strongly suggested in this study.

1. Introduction 
Superalloys, including Ni -, Co-, Fe-, and Ni-Fe-base alloys, are widely employed 
in numerous fields, such as the petrochemical industry, power generation equipment, 
gas turbine, and aerospace industry [1-3]. Nowadays, further improvements in 
temperature capability of the alloys are being actively sought to satisfy the increasing 
demands in efficiency and power [4] . The widely used Ni-base superalloys are believed 
to have reached their limit due to their low solidus temperature [5]. Therefore, materials 
with higher temperature capabilit ies are desired. Co -base superalloys exhibit a higher

## Page 4

3 
 
solidus temperature than Ni-base superalloys, but their high-temperature strength is not 
the best [1]; further, the lack of L12-ordered γʹ phase, which is the primary strengthening 
phase in Ni -base superalloys , is believe d to be responsible for their inferior strength. 
Thus, for more than half a century , researchers have been trying to introduce γʹ phase 
into Co -base superalloys to improve their high -temperature strength [1 , 6-10]. The 
developed Co-base superalloys, however, have failed to meet the expectations; this is 
due to their poor performance, which is partially derived from the large lattice mismatch 
between the matrix and the strengthening γʹ phase. 
In 2006, a substantial discovery of γʹ -Co3(Al, W) was made by our collaborator s 
Ishida et al. [11]; they discovered the lattice mismatch of the Co-Al-W alloy to be close 
to that of Ni -base superalloys , which was adjustable through the incorporation of 
additional alloying elements such as Ta and Mo. Moreover, the Vickers hardness of 
Co-Al-W-base alloys is higher than that of Waspaloy (Ni-base superalloy ). The 
subsequent studies showed that the Co -Al-W alloy displays an anomalous temperature 
dependence on strength, and the temperature related to the peak strength in the 
anomalous temperature range is higher than that of L1 2-ordered Ni 3Al [12 -13]. The 
elements, Ta, Mo, Rh, Ir, Pt, Ni, Nb , and Ti, were found to play a positive role in 
stabilizing the γʹ -Co3(Al, W) phase [13 -15]. Additionally, γʹ-reinforced Co -base 
superalloys generally demonstrate a positive lattice misfit , which decreases with rising 
temperatures; this results in a better microstructural stability at high temperatures 
[16-18]. Meanwhile, good fatigue resistance of the γʹ-reinforced Co -base superalloys

## Page 5

4 
 
can be expected due to the N -type rafts that are formed during the compression test, 
while P-type rafts are observed in the tensile test [16] . Therefore, the development of 
γʹ-reinforced Co-base superalloys has become a significant subject for research. 
However, the very narrow γ/γʹ two -phase region [11] and considerably high mass 
density (presence of element W ) [19] in the Co-Al-W system impede their applicability 
in aerospace industries. Besides, the re is still a need for improvement in the 
high-temperature strength of Co -Al-W-base alloys when compared to that of Ni -base 
superalloys [20] . Therefore, currently, the most necessitated research for the novel 
γʹ-reinforced Co-base superalloys is the discovery of a novel system that exhibits a low 
mass density, a good high -temperature strength, an improved solvus temperature 
(Tγʹ-solvus) and solidus temperature (Tsolidus), and a wide γ/γʹ two -phase region. Thus far, 
the L1 2-ordered phase is observed in Co -Ga-W [21] , Co -Ge-W [22] , Co -Nb-V [23] , 
Co-Ta-V [23-25], Co-Mo-Al [26], Co-Ti-Cr [27], Co -Ti-V [28-29], and Co-V-Al [30] 
ternary systems. For finding a new γʹ phase in the novel ternary systems, we previously 
investigated the phase relationship in Co -V-X (X: Mo, Ti, Ta, Mn and Al) ternary 
systems [28 -33]. Besides the γʹ -Co3(V, Al) ternary compound in the Co-V-Al system 
[30], a V-rich ternary compound γʹ-Co3(V, Ta) in the Co -V-Ta ternary system at 800 ℃ 
was also discovered. Recently, the machine learning method, which builds a 
mathematical model for describing the relationships among the compositions, 
microstructures, process parameters , and properties of materials, has shown significant 
potential in material design [34-36] and in the efficient prediction o f the properties of

thousands of systems; moreover, various materials have already been optimized by this 
method [37-39]. To the best of our knowledge, only a few studies have been aimed at 
designing Co-base superalloys using the machine learning method. In the present study, 
machine learning was used to predict the alloy -composition-dependent solvus 
temperature (Tγʹ-solvus) and area fraction (Aγʹ-area fraction) of γʹ phase in the Co-V-Ta ternary 
system; subsequently, the design of γʹ -reinforced Co -V-Ta alloys is accelerated . 
Meanwhile, the alloying elements, Ti and Al, were introduced to stabilize the γʹ phase 
and γ/γʹ microstructure. The high -temperature mechanical properties of Co -V-Ta-base 
alloys were studied to exploit their potential for high-temperature applications. 
2. Materials and Methods 
2.1 Machine learning, grain boundary segregations and first -principles 
calculations 
The alloy-composition-dependent Tγʹ-solvus and Aγʹ-area fraction were predicted using the 
machine learning algorithm. The details of the Tγʹ-solvus predicting model h ave been 
published in our previous paper [40]. The Aγʹ-area fraction predicting model was established 
using the data collected from related references and the experimental d ata provided by 
our group (unpublished paper). The model features considered in the analysis included 
alloy composition, aging time , and aging temperature. Different machine learning 
algorithms were applied to build the predicting model , and the 5 -fold cross-validation 
method was used to evaluate the ir performances. According to the testing results, the

## Page 7

6 
 
random forest algorithm showed the highest accuracy and was chosen for building the 
Aγʹ-area fraction predicting model. 
The grain boundar y composition was calculated using the method provided by 
Hillert who proposed a parallel tangent law for calculating grain boundary segregation 
by using the Gibbs energy curve of the liquid phase [41] . The same method was 
successfully utilized for calculating the grain boundary segregation of the Inconel 718 
superalloy in our recently reported study [42]. Thermodynamic calculations were 
performed by Thermo-Calc software using TTNI8 database [43]. 
The density functional theory (DFT)-based first -principles calculations for 
investigating the strengthening phase γʹ were performed using the projector augmented 
wave (PAW) method [44], as implemented in the Vienna Ab initio Simulation Package 
(VASP) [45-46]. The exchange-correlation functional was treated within the generalized 
gradient approximation (GGA) parameterized by Perdew -Burke-Ernzerhof (PBE) 
[47-48]. Wave functions were expanded in a plane wave up to a cutoff energy of 420 eV. 
Brillouin zone sampling was performed using the Monkhorst -Pack scheme [49]. The 
smallest allowed spacing between the k points was 0.25 Å-1. To obtain reliable results for 
elastic properties, a higher dense k -mesh with the smallest spacing of 0.2 Å -1 was set. 
Throughout the calculations, the convergence thresholds of total energy and the 
maximum force acting on ions were set to 10 −5 eV/atom and 10 −3 eV/Å, respectively. 
Spin polarization was considered because of the ferromagnetism of Co.

3.4.1 Natural properties of the γʹ phase 
In the present study, t he DFT-based first-principles calculations were performed to 
study the properties of the γʹ -Co3(V, Ta) phase. To match the experimental composition 
of the γʹ phase (Co-18.09V-6.07Ta) as much as possible, a cubic 108 -atom supercell 
(81Co-21V-6Ta (Co-19.44V-5.56Ta)) is generated using the special quasi -random 
structure approach, as displayed in the inset of Fig. 4. The calculated bulk (B), shear (G), 
and Young’s (E) moduli of the γʹ -Co3(V, Ta) phase are 250, 143, and 360 GPa , which 
are 23.2, 25.4, and 25.0 % , respectively, higher than that of γʹ-Co3(Al, W) [53] . This 
suggests better mechanical propert ies of the alloys reinforced by γʹ -Co3(V, Ta ), 
compared with the γʹ-Co3(Al, W)-strengthened alloy. Note that γʹ -Co3(V, Ta) shows a 
theoretical B/G value of 1.75 equaling the critical constant of intrinsic 
ductility-brittleness [54] . Although the value is close to that of γʹ -Co3(Al, W) 
(B/G=1.78) [53], which is prove n to be intrinsic ductility experimentally [55] , it is 
difficult to estimate the ductility/brittleness of γʹ-Co3(V, Ta) theoretically. Therefore, 
further experimental determination of ductility/brittleness of si ngle-crystal γʹ-Co3(V, Ta) 
is supposed.

4. Conclusions 
We successfully designed novel W -free high-strength Co-V-Ta-base alloys by 
employing machine learning algorithm and CALPHAD methods. The designed alloys 
show the low mass densities (8.67~8.86 g/cm 3) that are considerably lower than that of 
Co-9Al-9.8W (9.82 g/cm 3), high γʹ solvus temperature (up to 1044 ℃) that is higher 
than that of Co -9Al-9.8W (990 ℃), and high strength that is more intriguing compared 
with that of Co-base superalloys including traditional Co-base superalloys and novel 
γʹ-reinforced Co-base superalloys. Meanwhile, the extremely wide γ/γʹ two-phase region 
and broad processing window exhibited in the Co-V-Ta system are believed to support 
composition adjustment and microstructure control in the industries. Furthermore, 
owing to the thermodynamically stable of γʹ-Co3Ti phase and same site occupations of

## Page 28

27 
 
Ti and V atoms in the γʹ-Co3(V, Ti) phase as reported in our previous study, the 
Co-V-Ta-Ti-base alloys are believed to exhibit better microstructure stability and 
mechanical properties.
