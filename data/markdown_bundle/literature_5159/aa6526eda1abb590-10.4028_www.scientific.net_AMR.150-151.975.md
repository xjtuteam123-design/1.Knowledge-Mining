Keywords: hot tearing; Ni-based superalloys; Directional solidification; Microstructure 
Abstract. The effect of elements such as Ti, Al, Hf, Zr and B on hot tearing susceptibility during 
directional solidification was explored by tube-lik e samples with thickness of 1.0 mm, 1.5mm and 
2.0mm in wall. Results of SEM of elements on solidi fication of casting Ni-based superalloys were 
presented. The influential extent among the investi gated elements is found to be: the largest is Ti, 
then Zr, B and Al, and Hf is the smallest. Titanium promotes the formation of (γ+γ´) eutectic, 
increases sizes of the eutectic. Increasing Al content results in the size of γ+γ´ e utectic and the 
number of γ´. Increasing Zr content promotes γ+γ´ e utectic.Varying B content shows little 
microstructural evolution. 
Introduction 
Superalloys are promising structure materials for a ero/space-crafts due to their excellent properties, 
such as high specific strength, high specific rigid ity, high oxidization resistance and high creep 
resistance at high temperature [1,2].Currently dire ctionally solidified turbine blades have been 
widely used in high performance aviation engine[3-5 ].But it is found that alloys with high strength 
display bad castability, especially thin walled bla des with complex lumen(A directionally solidified 
blade with hot tearing is shown in Fig.1).

Propensity of hot tearing is the most important sig n to measure the castability of directional 
solidified alloys[6]. Numerous beneficial effects a re demonstrated that grain boundary crack 
destroyed turbine blades[7]. The insufficient resis tance against grain boundary in some superalloys 
prevented the application of directional solidifica tion processing in certain alloys. The most 
common solution for the problem of hot tearing is c ompositional modification. Theoretically, hot 
tearing as a source for bad castability has been st udied extensively during the past 
decades[8,9].There is still certain confusion about the exact mechanism of hot tearing although this 
topic has been studied extensively.

The IC10 with Al or B addition slightly influenced the microstructure of the directionally 
solidified alloy in Fig.4(d) and (e).However, a fur ther increase of Zr promotes (γ+γ´) formation, 
showed a significant detrimental effect on castabil ity of the alloys, even when Zr content was kept 
at a relatively low level, i.e. <0.1% as seen in Fi g.4(f). Zr acts as a getter element in superalloys. Zr 
is traditionally used to reduce the detrimental eff ect of trace elements such as sulfur. This particul ar 
role of Zr may not be as important in modern supera lloys as it used to be in the past decades. since 
the detrimental trace elements have been depressed to a very low level today. In fact, there has been 
reported that in some Ni-based superalloys, either in DS form or in equiaxed form, Zr does not 
show any beneficial effect on the mechanical properties. 
Conclusions 
The influential extent among the investigated eleme nts is found to be: the largest is Ti, then Zr, B 
and Al, and Hf is the smallest. 
The castability has been improved when Ti is remove d from IC10. Tube castings display severe 
cracks with the increasing of Ti content because of either eutectic with larger size and number or 
total freezing range. 
IC10 with Hf addition shows no hot tearing, Increas ing Al, B and Zr increases the hot tearing 
sensitivity of IC10 impressively.
