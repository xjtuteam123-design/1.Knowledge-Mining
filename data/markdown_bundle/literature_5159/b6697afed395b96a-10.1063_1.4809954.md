Nanostructured Nb reinforced NiTi shape memory alloy composite
with high strength and narrow hysteresis

An in-situ nanostructured Nb reinforced NiTi shape-memory alloy composite was fabricated by
mechanical reduction of an as-cast Nb-NiTi eutectic alloy. The composite exhibits large elastic
strain, high strength, narrow hysteresis, and high mechanical energy storage density and efﬁciency
during tensile cycling. In situ synchrotron high-energy X-ray diffraction revealed that these
superior properties were attributed to the strong coupling between nanostructured Nb and NiTi
matrix during deformation. Furthermore, this study offers a good understanding of the deformation
behavior of the nanoscale reinforcement embedded in the metal matrix deformed by stress-induced
phase transformation.

The quest for efﬁcient energy-related technologies
necessitates the high performance materials with superior
mechanical and functional properties, which are very impor-
tant for realizing the miniaturization and simpliﬁcation of
devices. It is known that NiTi shape-memory alloys (SMAs),
due to their excellent shape-memory effect, superelasticity
and biocompatibility,
1,2 have been extensively used in a vari-
ety of areas such as aerospace, automotive, biomedical and
oil exploration. 3,4 However, conventional NiTi SMAs usu-
ally exhibit a low critical stress of stress-induced martensitic
transformation ( /C24 600 MPa)1,2 and a large transformation
hysteresis5,6 during tensile cycling. The low critical stress
severely limits their applications in micro-devices that
require ultrahigh strength, and the large transformation hys-
teresis seriously complicates the mechanical designs and
applications of devices. Moreover, because of the low criti-
cal stress and large transformation hysteresis, NiTi SMAs
used as mechanical energy storage reservoirs usually exhibit
very low mechanical energy storage density (the area under
the loading curve) and storage efﬁciency (the ratio of areas
under the loading and unloading curves).
7,8 To improve the
strength and reduce the transformation hysteresis of NiTi
SMAs, the most effective way so far is to use the severe cold
deformation method. But the severely cold-deformed NiTi
SMAs usually exhibit very poor plastic deformability.
9,10
Freestanding nanowires possess ultrahigh elastic strain
limit (4–7%) and yield strength, 11–14 which are one of the
best reinforcements because of their extraordinary mechani-
cal properties and high aspect ratio. Here, an in-situ nano-
structured Nb reinforced NiTi SMA composite was
fabricated by mechanical reduction of an as-cast Nb-NiTi
eutectic alloy. The composite exhibits a large elastic strain,
high strength, narrow hysteresis, high mechanical energy
storage density and efﬁciency, and considerable plastic
deformability during tensile cycling. In situ synchrotron
high-energy X-ray diffraction (HE-XRD) revealed that these
superior properties originated from the strong coupling
between nanostructured Nb and NiTi matrix during
deformation.

To uncover the underlying mechanism of the high
strength and narrow hysteresis, in situ synchrotron
HE-XRD20,21 was carried out on the composite during defor-
mation (inset of Fig. 3(a)). Evolution of a section of
HE-XRD patterns obtained from the composite sample dur-
ing tensile loading is shown in Fig. 3(b). The d-spacing
strains for B2-NiTi (110) and Nb (110) planes perpendicular
to the loading direction versus the applied stress are shown
in Fig. 3(a). It can be seen that the B2-NiTi (110) curve devi-
ates upwards as the stress rising above 1.5 GPa, illustrating
that the lattice elastic strain of NiTi matrix exhibited a very
slow increase with increasing applied stress in the high stress
stage of above 1.5 GPa. The nanostructured Nb showed an
opposite trend in comparison with the NiTi matrix. Because
elastic strain is the load-bearing component of total strain, it
is indicated that the load taken by nanostructured Nb rapidly
increased with increase applied stress in the high stress stage
above 1.5 GPa. Moreover, it is noted that the nanostructured
Nb exhibit an ultra-large elastic strain limit of 4% in the
NiTi matrix that underwent an elastic deformation followed
by a stress-induced transformation deformation during ten-
sile loading. This value is signiﬁcantly larger than that of the
nanowires in the conventional dislocation slip plastic defor-
mation matrix ( /C24 1.5%),
22–24 and even comparable to that of
freestanding nanowires (4–7%). 11–14 Using Hooke’s law, the
load taken by nanostructured Nb upon tensile loading can be
estimated to be up to 3.7 GPa (Young’s modulus of the Nb
nanowires with h110i orientation is about 93 GPa).

The transformation hysteresis of NiTi SMAs is a result
of elastic strain energy dissipation accompanied by phase
interfacial movement.
5,6 It is expected that the energy dissi-
pation would decrease with decreasing interfacial movement
distance. Previous reports suggested that single-pair martens-
ite variants were formed in nanocrystalline NiTi instead of
the triangular arrangement of variants in coarse-grained
NiTi.
28,29 It is considered that the phase interfacial move-
ment distance in nanocrystalline NiTi matrix is smaller than
that in coarse-grained NiTi matrix. 28 The small interfacial
movement distance would lead to the reduction of transfor-
mation hysteresis, and the similar effect of grain size on
transformation hysteresis was reported in previous studies of
pure NiTi.

Evolution of a section of HE-XRD patterns obtained
from the composite sample in the tensile strain cycle of 5%
is shown in Fig. 3(c). The d-spacing strains for B2-NiTi
(110) and Nb (110) planes perpendicular to the loading
direction versus the applied strain are shown in Fig. 3(d).I t
can be seen that the nanocrystalline NiTi matrix underwent a
recoverable lattice elastic strain of 2.0% in the tensile strain
cycle of 5%. Thus, the NiTi matrix just underwent the stress-
induced transformation deformation of 3.0% (5% /C0 2.0%
¼ 3.0%) in the whole tensile cycle. The lattice elastic strain
of nanostructured Nb exhibits linear increases and decreases
with increasing and decreasing macrostrain of sample, indi-
cating that the nanostructured Nb underwent the reversible
elastic deformation without hysteresis in the tensile cycle.
Moreover, it can be speculated that the internal stress
between nanostructured Nb and NiTi matrix may be devel-
oped during their co-deformation due to their elastic strain
mismatch. The internal stress is expected to favorably select
martensite variants or result in variant coalescence that
reduces the energy dissipation.
