METHODS OF STUDY
We have designed an alloy based on/c103/c162-phase using the ear-
lier studied [see Part 1, Metalloved. Term. Obrab. Met., No. 2,
32 – 38 (2012)] (/c103/c162+ /c103) alloys of the Ni – Al – Cr – Mo – W –
Ta system. The main design parameters were as follows:
– volume fraction of /c103/c162-phase in the alloy about 95%;
– lattice parameter of /c103/c162-phase 0.357 – 0.358 nm;
– solidus temperature of the alloy 1350 – 1370°C;
– liquidus temperature below 1390 – 1400°C;
– admissible phase stability.

was as follows. The specified combinations of each of the
five alloying components of the chosen alloying system were
divided into three levels (minimum, medium, and maximum
ones). Then we constructed a design matrix of CF of type
2n + 1 (where n = 5) for five variable factors. Computer ex -
periment was used to evaluate the balance of chemical com -
position for all of the 33 variants of the alloy, which was de -

CONCLUSIONS
1. Computations of the phase composition, solidus and
liquidus temperatures, crystal lattice parameters of the /c103/c162-
and /c103-phases, and tensile mechanical properties of polycrys -
talline (/c103/c162+ /c103) alloys based on Ni3Al intermetallic of the Ni –
Al – Cr – Mo – W system have been used to design a new re-
fractory intermetallic alloy with melting temperature 1365°C,
which contains about 95% /c103/c162-phase and a nickel /c103-solid so-
lution.
2. To improve the phase stability, the total content of the
refractory (Ta, W, Mo) and /c103/c162-forming (Al, Ta) alloying ele-
ments in the designed alloy was balanced. The microstruc-
ture of single crystals with axial orientation/c225001/c241of the alloy
is represented by columnar crystallites of /c103/c162-phase oriented
over crystallographic direction /c225001/c241and separated by hete-
rogeneous ( /c103/c162+ /c103/c162) layers consisting of the matrix /c103-phase
and dispersed segregations of /c103/c162-phase.
3. We have determined the temperature dependences of
the characteristics of strength and ductility due to tension of
single crystals of the designed alloy based on /c103/c162-phase with
orientation /c225001/c241in the temperature range of 20 – 1200°C.
At 800°C the strength properties of the single crystals attain
maximum values. The single crystals are characterized by
decrease in the ductility upon growth in the temperature from
20 to 600°C; above this temperature the ductility remains
virtually unchanged and amounts to5–6 % .
