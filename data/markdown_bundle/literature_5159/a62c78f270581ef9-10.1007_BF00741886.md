INFLUENCE OF RETARDED COOLING FROM THE TEMPERATURES OF THE y-AREA 
ON THE STRUCTURE AND MECHANICAL PROPERTIES OF KhN62BMKTYu 
HIGH-TEMPERATURE STRENGTH ALLOY 
A. S. Kleshchev, N. N. Korneeva, 
A. B. Notkin, and L. V. Smurova 
UDC 621.78:669.245 
The use of a treatment as the result of which there is formed a recrystallized struc- 
ture makes it possible to increase the stress-rupture strength of high-temperature strength 
alloys [i] at temperatures not exceeding the equicohesive. With an increase in service tem- 
perature, particularly of type KhN62BMKTYu (EP742) alloys, above 650°C, the processesof re- 
covery and recrystallization occur intensely, which leads to softening of the material and 
impossibility of use of the strain-hardening effect. Consequently, it is necessary to search 
for other methods of increasing the high-temperature strength at high service temperatures 
based on use of certain heat-treatment cycles for material with the original recrystallized 
structure. 
To determine such heat-treatment cycles in this work an investigation was made of the 
influence of retarded cooling from the y-area to different temperatures of existence of the 
(y +y')-phases on the structure and mechanical properties of KhN62BMKTYu high-temperature 
strength nickel alloy (0.05% C, 13.7% Cr, 5.0% Mo, 2.6% Ti, 2.5% AI, 10% Co, 2.6% Nb). 
The samples were given the following stepped heat treatment: heat to I120°C, which is 
20°C higher than the temperature of complete dissolution of y'-phase (solvus); hold for 4 h; 
furnace cool at a rate of 1 deg/min to I000, 1050, and I080°C (cycles I, 2, and 3, respec- 
tively), which are I00, 50, and 20°C below the solvus temperature of the alloy; harden at a 
rate of more than 70 deg/min; age at 780°C for 16 h; and air cool. For comparison samples 
were also heat treated according to cycle 4 by heating to !120°C, holding for 2 h, cooling 
at a rate of more than 70 deg/min, aging at 780°C for 16 h, and air cooling. All of the sam- 
ples were tested in tension, impact bending at 20°C, and 100-h stress-rupture strength at 
650, 750, and 800°C (specimens with and without stress raisers). The structure of the alloy 
was investigated on light and electron microscopes with the use of replicas and foil. The 
grain size was determined by the random intercept method, and the size and volume share of 
the coarse yV-phase particles, by the method of chords and the point method, respectively.

An analysis of the change in mechanical properties after stepped heat treatment to dif- 
ferent cycles showed that the optimum combination of high short-term and 100-h stress-rupture 
strengths of EP742 alloy with preservation of the effect of hardening up to 800°C is reached 
after heat treatment to cycle 2. The obtaining of such a combination of properties may be 
explained by features of the structure formed during treatment to cycle 2. Probably the de- 
termining influence on the properties is made by the formation of the specific winding grain 
boundaries. Let us consider the possible mechanism of the creation of such a structure° The 
results of electron-microscopic investigations made on foils showed that in heating to I120~C 
and holding for 4 h a fine-grained recrystallized structure is formed. In subsequent slow 
cooling at a rate of 1 deg/min to I050°C three processes occur simultaneously: selective re- 
crystallization as the result of migration of the boundaries starting at II00°C (solvus tem- 
perature of the alloy), precipitation of y'-phase, and coagulation of it. All of these pro- 
cesses are diffusion ones and occur with time, and the slow rate of cooling from the tempera- 
ture of treatment for solid solution provides sufficiently full occurrence of them~ Subse- 
quent accelerated cooling from I050°C limits the occurrence of these processes, as the re- 
sult of which interaction of the grain boundaries with the y'-phase precipitates is possible. 
On the basis of the data presented above it may be assumed that in cooling from 1120 to 
I050°C as the result of coagulation of the y'-phase particles close to the grain boundaries 
areas free of precipitates and capable of migration between two adjoining precipitates are 
formed. Such selective migration of the boundaries is observed primarily in zones occupied 
by y'-phase and absent in areas where it is not. The most prone to migration are large-angle 
boundaries [3]. Probably there is a limiting temperature below and above which twisting of 
the grain boundaries by migration does not occur or it occurs to a lesser degree. Actually, 
in cooling from 1120 to 1000°C the volume share of uniformly precipitated y'-phase is greater 
than in cooling to I050°C, 20-23 and about 10%, respectively. The particles block the grain 
boundaries and reduce their capacity for migration. In cooling to I080=C the volume share 
of y'-phase is small (about 5%) or precipitation of it is practically absent, and therefore 
free migration of the grain boundaries is possible. At the same time, twisting (with a large 
amplitude) of the boundaries is almost completely absent. 
Therefore, the recrystallized structure of the alloy with a grain size of 100-140 ~m 
and twisting of the grain boundaries with a large amplitude in the presence, togetherwith the 
dispersed coherent hardening phase precipitated in aging, of coarse (0.4-0.7 ~m) coherent par- 
ticles of y'-phase in a quantity of 10% provides the most optimum combination of service prop- 
erties of type EP742 alloy.
