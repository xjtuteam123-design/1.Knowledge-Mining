ABSTRACT
The influence of long-term thermal exposure on the microstructure and microhardness of a new
Ni–Fe-based superalloy, for advanced ultra-supercritical coal-fired power plants, was studied.
Spherical γ
′particles were coarsened with the increase of aging time, while the volume frac-
tion of γ′was decreased. The amount of M23C6 carbides was increased with aging time at 750°C,
and the coarsening rate of the width of M 23C6 was consistent with that of the LSW model. The
microhardness of the alloy was maintained following 935 h of aging time and then was slightly
decreased. It was found that the transition from the strongly coupled dislocations model to the
Orowan model was dependent on the size and volume fraction of γ
′.
