ABSTRACT 
The understanding of softening mechanisms for high-temperature titanium alloys at service temperature is 
essential to guarantee their service stability and safety. High-temperature softening is mainly manifested as grain 
boundary softening for metallic materials. To clarify the softening mechanism and failure mode of high- 
temperature titanium alloy TA15 (Ti-6.5Al – 2Zr – 1Mo – 1V, wt.%) at temperature around service temperatures, 
coarse prior β grains were obtained by spark plasma sintering at 1300 
◦
C, other than 1000 
◦
C which is the 
traditional power metallurgy (P/M) consolidation temperature for TA15. Meanwhile, the effect of grain 
boundary softening was amplified. The high-temperature tensile test was carried out at 500 – 650 
◦
C at intervals 
of 50 
◦
C. The experimental results showed the maximum prior β grain size of P/M TA15 alloy is 2.9 μ m, and its 
tensile strength decreases from 579 to 389 MPa with the increase of tensile temperature from 500 
◦
C to 650 
◦
C. 
Combined with calculation according to the Read-Shockley formula and analysis by macro-fracture, which show 
that the grain boundary strength is higher than the grain strength at 500 and 550 
◦
C, and there are obvious grain 
and phase boundary torsion. The fracture mode demonstrated as a trans -granular fracture. The specimens soften 
rapidly, grain boundary strength decreases to less than grain strength, which led to fracture mode changed to 
inter-granular fracture at tensile temperatures up to 600 
◦
C. If the crack extends to the critical length, the stress at 
the crack tip reaches easily the fracture strength of P/M TA15 alloy, and crack propagates rapidly at the tem -
perature above 600 
◦
C. Moreover, which corresponds to the elongation of true stress-strain decreasing with the 
increase of test temperature. OM and EBSD result demonstrated that the equi-cohesive temperature of TA15 alloy 
between 550

◦
C. Our findings provide a promising route to improve service temperature of high 
temperature TA15 alloy by raising the equi-cohesive temperature area in zone II to a higher temperature. 
1. Introduction 
Near α -titanium( α -Ti) alloys have been widely used in high- 
temperature components such as in aviation jet engines, turbine en -
gines, air rudders, and automobile engines due to their high specific 
strength, corrosion resistance, high-temperature creep properties, high- 
temperature mechanical properties, and good processing properties 
[ 1 – 5 ]. The long service temperature of mature high temperature tita -
nium alloys is between 550 
◦
C and 600 
◦
C [ 6 – 8 ], such as TA15 [ 9 ], Ti60 
[ 10 ], IMI829 [ 6 ], Ti-1100 [ 11 ] and IMI834 [ 12 ]. Alloys will soften 
quickly and lose their overall mechanical properties at temperatures 
higher than service temperature. Accordingly, to further enhance their 
high-temperature stability and service safety; these researches on soft -
ening mechanism and fracture mechanism of these alloys have been 
necessary at an elevated temperature above service temperature [ 13 ]. 
Previous studies on softening mechanism and behavior of near α -Ti 
alloys, such as TA15(Ti-6.5Al – 2Zr – 1Mo – 1V, wt.%), show that disloca -
tion density continues to increase when it deforms at 750 
◦
C while its 
flow stress reaches its maximum value [ 14 ]. The grain boundaries 
atomic density is low and readily diffused at high temperatures. To be 
more specific, the alloy mechanical property is further weakened due to 
dislocation slip, dislocation climbing, grain or phase boundary migra -
tion, and grain rotation [ 15 , 16 ]. According to the propagation of cracks 
through cavitation: A general theory [ 17 ], grain boundary migration 
requires cooperation of intracrystalline migration. The trigeminal point 
of the two-dimensional grain boundary will play a role in grain 
boundary migration. Significantly, the incongruity between 
inter-crystalline migration and grain boundary migration cause stress 
concentration, which lead to form micro-crack at the grain boundary 
[ 18 , 19 ]. Another study showed that [ 20 – 22 ] α -Ti is prone to fracture

2. Materials and methods 
2.1. Material synthesis and specimen preparation 
The spherical TA15 powder (purity: ≥ 99.9%) used in this work was 
prepared by aerosolization (Avimetal Powder Metallurgy Technology, 
China). The TA15 (diameter ~ 150 μ m) powder with a good sphericity 
can be seen from the inset of Fig. 1 (a). Table 1 shows the chemical 
composition and content of TA15 powder. Fig. 2 (a) is the XRD diffrac -
tion pattern of TA15 powder. As shown in Fig. 2 (a), the peak is mainly α 
phase. As TA15 is a near α -Ti alloy, the content of β phase is less, and the 
diffraction peak of β phase is difficult to be observed in Fig. 2 (a). 159 g of 
TA15 powder was pre-pressed in a graphite mold with diameter of 30.0 
mm and height of 100.0 mm. The green compacts were consolidated by 
spark plasma sintering (SPS, LABOX330, Sinter Land, Japan). Sintering 
temperature was set as 1300 
◦
C with a heating rate of 120 
◦
C/min under 
a vacuum of ~5Pa, and the pressure is maintained for 60min under the 
pressure of 30 MPa. After sintering program was completed, specimen 
was cooled to room temperature along with the furnace. The size of 
sintered specimens is Φ 30 × 50 mm. 
2.2. Microstructure observation 
The phase composition of original powder was analyzed by X-ray 
diffractometer (XRD, XRD-7000, SHIMADU, Japan). Optical microscope 
(BX-51P, Olympus, Japan) and field-emission scanning electron 
microscope (FESEM, JSM-6500F, JEOL, Japan) were used to observe 
powder morphologies, as-sintered specimen ’ s microstructure, and ten -
sile fracture morphologies. Identification of phase and dislocation 
behavior were determined by transmission electron microscopy (TEM, 
JEM-2100, JEOL, Japan). The crystallographic information of speci -
mens, including grain size, phase distribution, phase content, etc., ware 
observed and analyzed by electron backscatter diffraction (EBSD) device 
TSL (TSL Digi View IV; EDAX, USA) installed in field emission scanning

5. Conclusion 
P/M TA15 alloy was prepared by SPS at 1300 
◦
C in this work. By 
amplifying the softening effect of grain boundaries at high-temperature, 
the softening mechanism and fracture mode transformation behavior of 
TA15 alloy were studied. The article describes the softening mechanism 
of P/M TA15 alloy at high temperatures in terms of GNDs, activation of 
slip system, grain boundary strength, burgers orientation relationship 
between variant α and parent β , dislocation motion, and fracture 
toughness of specimens. The main conclusions include the following. 
1. P/M TA15 specimens ’ strength decrease with increasing tempera -
ture. When the temperature is below 550 
◦
C, the fracture mode is an 
intergranular fracture, and when the temperature is above 600 
◦
C, 
the fracture mode is a trans -granular fracture. 
2. Grain boundary strength decreases with increasing temperature. The 
equi-cohesive temperature range of TA15 titanium alloy is between 
550 
◦
C and 600 
◦
C, and the tolerance value of specimens to crack can 
be improved by increasing the equi-cohesive temperature.
