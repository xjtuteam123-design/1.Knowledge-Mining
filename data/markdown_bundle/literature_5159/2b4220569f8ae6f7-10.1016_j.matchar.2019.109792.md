1. Introduction
High entropy (HE) alloys firstly introduced by Yeh et al. [1,2] are
initially defined as solid solution alloys that contain more than five
principalelementsinequalornearequalatomicpercentage,whichare
alsonamed“multi-componentalloys”byCantoretal.[3–5].Asaresult
oftheircompositionalcomplexity,HEalloysarecharacterizedbyfour
primaryfeatures:high-entropy,latticedistortion,sluggishdiffusionand
cocktail effects [2], and due to their extraordinary properties as com-
pared to conventional metals and alloys, they are considered as pro-
mising candidates for advanced structural applications [6–9]. Grain
refinement [10], interstitial strengthening [11] and precipitation
hardening [12] are practical measures to improve the mechanical
performance of HE alloys for balanced mechanical performance for
practicalapplications[13–15].
While the new HE alloys are not constrained to the alloys with
principal elements in near equal atomic percentage [16], design
strategy is extended to complex alloy systems that undergo elemental
segregation, precipitation,chemicalordering andspinodaldecomposi-
tion,tokeepasignificantamountofdisorder[17,18].Forexample,the
coherentL12 precipitates,whicharewell-knowntobeemployedinNi-
based superalloys, have pronounced strengthening effects in the fcc-
basedHEalloys.ItispossibletoprecipitatethenanosizedcoherentL1 2-
Ni3(Ti,Al) reinforcing phase in an fcc-FeCoNiCr HE alloy matrix using
minor additions of Ti and Al to derive outstanding tensile properties
[19]. The new high-density L1
2-phase strengthened complex alloy
system, i.e. (FeCoNi)86-Al7Ti7, exhibits superior strengths of 1.5GPa
and ductility as high as 50% in tension at ambient temperature [20].
However, the L12 precipitates cannot always promise the best combi-
nation of strength and ductility. The Al0.5CrCuFeNi2 HE alloys, where
theexpensiveCoelementisreplacedwithNiintheextensivelystudied
AlxCoCrCuFeNisystem,showneedle-likefineL1 2structuredphasesand
yield only an average elongation of 5.6% [21], whose product of
strength-elongationisabout6.1GPa%.

5. Summary
To achieve strength-toughness and strength-ductility synergy re-
mains the pursuit for the development of HE alloys as in the case of
most conventional metals and alloys. The aged carbon-containing
Al0.3Cu0.5FeCrNi2 HE alloys were investigated to examine the pre-
cipitation strengtheningeffects to balancethe strength-ductilitytrade-
off.Themainconclusionsfromthisworkarelistedbelow:
1. The three aged alloys exhibited excellent balance of strength and
ductility, i.e. high product of strength and elongation
(35.3–51.4GPa%).
2. It was shown that the carbon content of 0.073at.% was enough to
promote the formation of M
23C6 phase, which had a preference to
precipitateatthegrainboundary.
3. WiththecontributionofM 23C6 carbides,theyieldstrengthandul-
timate tensile strength of the sample aged at 900°C increased by
193MPa and 415MPa, respectively, compared tothe solid solutio-
nizedsample,whileitselongationremainedashighas88%.
4. NanoscaleL1
2-orderedprecipitatesoccurredinthealloyafteraging
at700°Cand550°C.Cu-richphasewasonlyfoundinthealloyaged
at700°C,anditsprecipitationkineticsisslowerthanthatoftheL1 2
phase.ThesynergisticstrengtheningeffectsofL1 2 phaseandM 23C6
carbidesintheagedsampleA550andA700renderedtheimproved
