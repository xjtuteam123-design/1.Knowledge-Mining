ABSTRACT 
To obtain the balanced mechanical properties of Nb – Si based alloys, the effects of multiple additions of Zr and Y 
on microstructure, fracture toughness and tensile strength were investigated. The compositions of the alloys were 
designed as Nb – 16Si – 23Ti – 4Cr – 2Al-2Hf-(3Zr-0.6Y), (5Zr-0.3Y) and (5Zr-0.6Y). The addition of Zr promotes 
silicide transition from α -Nb
5
Si
3 
to γ -Nb
5
Si
3
; in 5Zr alloys, the γ -Nb
5
Si
3 
is the sole silicide. Added Y accelerates 
the spheroidization rate of silicide during the heat treatment process. The toughness of 3Zr-0.6Y, 5Zr-0.3Y and 
5Zr-0.6Y alloys are 23.1, 17.9 and 20.0 MPa m
1/2
, respectively. Alloys containing more Y and less Zr have better 
toughness, accompanied by the greater degree of crack deflections, more microcracks and rougher fracture 
surfaces. The tensile strength of the three alloys are 919, 1209 and 1159 MPa, respectively. The higher strength 
of 5Zr alloys is attributed to greater orientation difference and immobile dislocations. The two mechanical 
properties are balanced successfully in the 5Zr-0.6Y alloy. 
1. Introduction 
Due to the limitation of the higher-temperature application of nickel- 
based alloys, Nb – Si based alloys with a higher melting point, lower 
density and exceptional high-temperature mechanical properties have 
caught wide attention [ 1 – 3 ]. In Nb – Si based alloys, the silicides (e.g. 
Nb
5
Si
3 
or Nb
3
Si) could provide high-temperature strength, creep resis -
tance and oxidation resistance; the ambient fracture toughness is offered 
by ductile Nb solid solution (Nbss) [ 4 – 6 ]. By adjusting the modest ratio 
of the two phases, the balancing properties could be achieved. 
To enhance the comprehensive properties, such as oxidation resis -
tance, tensile strength and fracture toughness, etc., alloying is consid -
ered as one of the most important and effective ways. In the past 
decades, the alloy system has been developed from Nb – Si binary alloy to 
Nb – Si – Ti – Cr – Al-Hf multicomponent system in which the effects of 
alloying elements on phase selection, microstructure, oxidation resis -
tance and mechanical properties including strength and fracture 
toughness have been studied [ 7 – 12 ]. Adding Ti could decrease P – N 
Barrier Energy and thus improves toughness [ 13 , 14 ]. The oxidation 
resistance could be enhanced with the addition of Cr and Al, owing to 
the formation of Laves phase Cr
2
Nb and suppressing the oxygen diffu -
sion [ 15 ]. Hf can strengthen the Nbss relying on solid solution 
strengthening effects and improve high-temperature oxidation resis -
tance of alloys. Unfortunately, the properties required for industrial 
applications are still difficult to meet [ 16 , 17 ]. 
On the basis of the multicomponent system, the alloying elements, 
including B [ 18 ], Ta [ 17 ], W [ 17 ], Fe [ 19 ], Mo [ 20 ] and Ga [ 21 ] are 
further added to improve strength and/or fracture toughness. But the 
two properties which are generally contradictory haven ’ t been balanced 
with the single addition of alloying elements. Actually, the multi-alloyed 
Nb – Si based alloys are always the development tendency. Among the 
elements added into multicomponent system, Zr has been reported to 
improve the strength [ 22 ] and owing to its same outermost electron 
number with Ti, the fracture toughness might not decrease. Also, Y, as 
the rare earth element, was found to heighten the fracture toughness of 
many other materials [ 23 – 26 ]. Thus, in the present work, multiple ad -
ditions of Zr and Y are introduced to overcome the strength-toughness 
dilemma. We believe the research results would be significant to the 
future industrial application.

determined by not only the ductile Nbss but also the brittle silicide [ 36 ].

and extrinsic toughening mechanisms, respectively [ 37 , 38 ]. Toughness

been observed ahead of the crack tips ( Fig. 8 ). When forming ahead of

cracks propagation, which actually increases the required energy of 
cracks propagation, reduces the stress concentration at the crack tip and 
restrains the propagation of cracks. Therefore, with the microcracks 
generating, the toughness of Nb – Si based alloys is efficaciously 
improved. The contribution of microcracks to toughness is as Equation

4. Conclusions 
On the basis of the master alloy (Nb – 16Si – 23Ti – 4Cr – 2Al-2Hf) [ 27 ], 
the present compositions of Nb – Si based alloy are designed as 
Nb – 16Si – 23Ti – 4Cr – 2Al-2Hf-(3Zr-0.6Y), (5Zr-0.3Y) and (5Zr-0.6Y), 
respectively. The effects of multiple additions of Zr and Y on micro -
structures, fracture toughness and tensile strength are investigated. The 
main conclusions are as follows:

toughness is achieved in the 5Zr-0.6Y alloy, which has moderate tensile 
strength and fracture toughness.
