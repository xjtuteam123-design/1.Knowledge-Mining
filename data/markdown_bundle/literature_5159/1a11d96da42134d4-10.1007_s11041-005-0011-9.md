PROPERTIES OF SHEETS FROM HIGH-PROCESSIBILITY ALLOY V1341
I. N. Fridlyander, 1 O. E. Grushko, 1 L. M. Sheveleva, 1 and S. V . Danilov1
Translated from Metallovedenie i Termicheskaya Obrabotka Metallov, No. 12, pp.3–6 , December, 2004.
Corrosion, mechanical (under static and dynamic loads), and fatigue characteristics, crack resistance, and high
temperature strength of sheets from alloy V1341 with controlled recrystallized structure in state T1 are pre -
sented. The properties of the sheets are also studied after operational heating to 100 – 150°C with holds for
10 – 500 h. Sheets from alloy V1341T1 are compared with sheets from alloy AMg6M used for articles serving
at medium loads.
INTRODUCTION
Alloy V1341 (of type A V) belongs to the class of heat-
hardenable low-alloy alloys based on the Al – Mg – Si sys-
tem [1, 2]. Its main hardening phase is the Mg
2Si compound.
Due to the combination of high adaptability to manufacture,
good corrosion resistance, satisfactory weldability, and me-
dium strength, cold-rolled sheets of this alloy with controlled
recrystallized structure formed as a result of the thermo-
mechanical treatment suggested by the authors [2] find appli-
cation in aircraft, automotive, and other industries.
In order to solve the question of specific applications of
the alloy in various fields, the set of its properties should be
studied in detail, which is especially important for articles of
aircraft engineering. In this connection, we have undertaken
an all-sided study of the mechanical, corrosion, and fatigue
properties of sheet alloy V1341T1 as well as the crack resis -
tance and the high-temperature strength.
METHODS OF STUDY
The properties were determined for cold-rolled sheets
with a thickness of 1.5 – 2.5 mm, which were fabricated
from alloy V1341T1 bearing 0.83 – 1.2% Si, 0.6 – 0.77%
Mg, and 0.30 – 0.44% Cu, in state T1 (heating for hardening
to 530°C in a saltpeter tank, 5-min hold, water cooling + arti-
ficial aging at 170°C for 14 h).
The obtained sheets had a recrystallized structure with a
grain size of 30 – 100 /c109m, which met the requirements en -
suring reliable operation of aluminum alloys in aircraft struc-
tures [3]. The properties of the sheets were determined in ac-
cordance with the active standards of the Russian Federation.
We also studied the properties of the alloy after addi-
tional heating to 100 – 150°C with a hold of 10 – 500 h.
RESULTS AND DISCUSSION
The results of the determination of tensile and compres-
sive mechanical properties of the alloy at various test tem -
peratures are presented in Tables 1 and 2. It follows from the
tables that the sheets were isotropic because their properties
in the longitudinal and transverse directions were close.
The impact toughness (KCU ) of the sheets was virtually
twice higher than the specific work of fracture of a specimen
with a crack ( a
cr ), which is typical for aluminum alloys in -
cluding those with a long service life like D16.
As the test temperature was decreased to – 70°C, the
ductility of the alloy remained unchanged, which follows
from the constancy of the values of /c100, KCU, and acr ;a tt h e
same time the ultimate yield strength increased by 20 MPa.
At test temperatures exceeding 100°C the short-term
strength characteristics of the sheets decreased somewhat,
and the elongation increased. On the whole, the combination
of the strength and ductility of the sheets tested in a tempera-
ture range from – 70 to + 200°C was favorable, which allows
us to recommend them for the production of articles serving
at these temperatures. The obtained level of properties of al -
loy V1341T1 exceeds substantially the level of properties of
alloys of the magnalium group recommended for operation
at up to 200°C. For example, the ultimate rupture strength of

CONCLUSIONS
1. The results of complex tests of corrosion and mechani-
cal properties of sheets of alloy V1341 with controlled struc-
ture in state T1 at test temperatures of – 70 to 200°C under
static and dynamic loads and of a study of their fatigue cha -
racteristics and crack resistance give us grounds to recom -
mend such sheets for wide use in aircraft, automotive, con -
structional, and other industries.
2. The mechanical and corrosion properties of sheets
from alloy V1341T1 remain virtually unchanged after addi-
tional heating to 100 – 150°C with 10 – 500 h holds, which
indicates the high stability of the properties of the alloy.
3. Sheets from alloy V1341T1 have considerable advan-
tages over sheets of the magnalium group (like AMg6) with
respect to the yield strength at room and enhanced tempera-
tures, fatigue characteristics, and sensitivity to stress concen-
trators. Sheets from alloy V1341T1 should replace the now
widely used sheets from alloys of the magnalium group.
