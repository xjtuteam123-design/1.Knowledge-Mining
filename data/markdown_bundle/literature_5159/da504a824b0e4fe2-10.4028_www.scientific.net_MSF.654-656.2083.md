The High Temperature Connecting Strength and Thermal Stability of 
Ti 44 Ni 47 Nb 9 SMA Pipe Coupling 
Wei Jin 
Institute of Metal Research, Chinese Academy of Sciences, Shenyang 110016, China 
wjin@imr.ac.cn 
Keywords: Ti 44 Ni 47 Nb 9, pipe coupling, 1Cr18Ni9Ti tube, high temperature, connecting strength, 
thermal stability 
Abstract. The creeping properties of Ti 44 Ni 47 Nb 9 shape memory alloy (SMA) were studied at 300 ℃
. The connecting strength, deformation and thermal stability of the connected units were investigated 
under constant load and high temperature, and the units were composed of SMA pipe couplings and 
φ 16×1.5mm 1Cr18Ni9Ti tubes. The results indicated th at (1) Ti 44 Ni 47 Nb 9 alloys had excellent 
creeping properties at 400MPa stress and 300°C; (2) the connected units had good connected 
properties and thermal stabilities at 8445N axial t ensile load and 400°C; (3) the essence of high 
temperature relaxation of the connected units was t hat the strength of SMA pipe couplings and 
connected tubes reduced with increasing temperature , and the pipe couplings had relative 
displacement to the connected tubes under axial tensile load. 
Introduction 
It is generally recognized that the application of TiNiFe SMA pipe couplings has been the most 
successful to date [1-3]. However, the TiNiFe pipe couplings must be expanded, stored, transported 
and assembled in liquid nitrogen to prevent premature recovery. This situation limits the widespread 
commercial use of SMA pipe couplings. Raychem Corporation developed a wide hysteresis TiNiNb 
SMA to solve the low temperature storage problem in 1986 [4-5]. When it comes to SMA pipe 
couplings, two aspects must be considered: overheat ing and cooling. Overheating results in stress 
relaxation due to conventional creep processed, just as would occur in any other metal. Dynamically 
controlled stress relaxation test had been conducte d on TiNiFe alloy in order to quantify the upper 
service temperature limit by Profit [6]. The former investigation was limited within stress relaxation 
of the SMA itself, for application the connecting s trength and high temperature stability of the 
connected units of shape memory alloy pipe couplings and connected tubes were more important. 
The purpose of the present investigation was thus to provide systematic and straight forward data on 
the connecting strength and high temperature stabil ity of SMA couplings and stainless steel tube 
subassemblIES under constant load and overheating situation. 
Experimental Procedures 
This test program was to evaluate the connecting st rength and high temperature stability of SMA 
couplings and stainless steel tube subassemblies un der constant load and overheating situation. 
Stainless steel tubes and a SMA coupling were installed as a connected subassembly, as figure 1. The 
tube material was 1Cr18Ni9Ti, and the dimension was φ 16.0×1.5mm. The SMA couplings were 
made of Ti 
44 Ni 47 Nb 9. They were machined by Ti 44 Ni 47 Nb 9 alloy rod at first, then expanded under -60 
°C. The inner diameter of SMA coupling finished products was 16.2mm. The recovery strain of the 
coupling was 7%. The SMA coupling was placed on the stainless steel tubes, then heated by hot air 
blower. Before testing, all the subassemblies were heated to 300 ℃ holding for 10 minute in furnace.

Results and Discussion 
The creeping properties of Ti 
44 /glyph1197i 47 /glyph1197b 9 shape memory alloy. The 100 hours creep tests of 
Ti 44 Ni 47 Nb 9 alloy specimens were carried out at 100MPa, 150MPa, 200MPa, 300MPa and 400MPa 
under 300°C, as shown in figure 2. When the stress is less than 200 MPa, the deformation of the 
specimens is less then 0.01mm and the residual stra in is not more than 0.01%. It is shown that the 
creep curves of 100MPa, 150MPa and 200MPa overlap. When the stress reaches 300 MPa, the strain 
is 0.38% while the residual strain is 0.88%. When the stress is 400 MPa, the strain is 1.98% and the 
residual strain is 1.58%. For all specimens, the st rain is not increase as time increasing. The result s 
indicate that Ti 44 Ni 47 Nb 9 alloy has excellent creep properties at 300°C. 
 
Figure 2 The 300 °C /100 hours creep curves of Ti 44 Ni 47 Nb 9 alloy under different stresses 
 
Ti 44 Ni 47 Nb 9 alloy itself has excellent creep properties. But when the stress exceed 300 MPa, the 
strain is big enough and would affect the shape mem ory strain and results in looseness of the 
connected units. On the other hand, even though the stress is small and creeping would not occur, the 
temperature is high enough to affect recovery stres s, which leads to the looseness of the connected 
units. Figure 3 depicts the deformation-time curves under 6333N constant load/450°C of ф16×1.5mm 
1Cr18Ni9Ti stainless steel tube and connected unit. It can be seen that the deformation of 1Cr18Ni9Ti 
tube is less than 0.002mm, while deformation of the connected unit is 0.059 mm. At 450°C, the yield 
stress of the stainless steel tube is 180MPa, under 6333N load, the stress of stainless steel tube is 
93MPa, there is not creep occur. It is indicated that high temperature or high stress would result in the 
stainless steel tube ‘soft’ and the SMA coupling an d stainless tube of the connected unit move 
relativity.

The high temperature connecting strength and stability of SMA coupling subassembly. In order 
to quantify the upper service temperature limit under different loads, four specimens of connected unit 
were loaded to 4222N, 6333N, 8445N and 10500N respe ctively. The specimens were heated, 
increasing 50°C holding 5 minutes every time. Figure 4 shows the deformation-temperature curves of

Figure 4 The deformation-temperature curves of connected units under four different loads 
It can be seen that the deformation difference between four specimens of different load is small 
and no more than 0.1mm when the temperature is lower than 400°C. With the temperature increasing, 
the deformation difference increases. When the temp erature reaches 500°C, the deformation 
difference between 4222N and 10500N load is up to 0.2mm. When the temperature exceeds 500°C, 
the deformations increase rapidly with temperature increasing. The higher the load is, the bigger the 
deformation is. The higher the load is, the lower t he separate temperature is. The 
deformation-temperature curves of 4222N and 6333N o verlap each other. When the temperature is 
lower than 500°C and the constant load is not bigger than 8445N, the deformation difference between 
different constant load is very small. 
In order to evaluate the high temperature connecting strength and stability of the SMA coupling 
subassemblies, three SMA coupling subassemblies were loaded to 8445N and then heated to 300°C, 
400°C, 500°C respectively. The specimens were exposed to high temperature under the load. Figure 5 
illustrates the deformation-time curves of connecte d units under 8445N constant load at different 
temperatures.

It is shown that the deformation difference of SMA couplings subassembly is very small between

The essence of high temperature relaxation of the c onnected units is that the strength of SMA 
pipe couplings and connected tubes reduce with the increasing temperature, and pipe couplings have 
relative displacement to connected tubes under axial tensile load. 
Summary

44 Ni 47 Nb 9 alloys had excellent creeping properties at 400MPa stress and 300°C. 
2. The connected units had good connected properties and thermal stabilities at 8445N axial tensile 
load and 400°C. 
3. The high temperature looseness of connected units due to the strength of connected tube and SMA
