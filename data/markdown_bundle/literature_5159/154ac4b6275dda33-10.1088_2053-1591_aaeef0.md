Effect of Ti Addition on the Microstructure and
Properties of AlCrCuFeNi Alloy
Wang xin, Zhang YunPeng , Xie Jing
xi’an university of technology. Xi’an 710048, China
Abstract: The microstructure, hardness, compression and wear resistance of AlCrCuFeNi，AlCrCuFeNiTi0.5 and
different heat treatment (600 ℃/12h,800℃/12h,1000℃/12h)annealed AlCrCuFeNiTi 0.5 High Entropy
Alloys(HEAs) fabricated by arc melting and casting were investigated by XRD,SEM and EDS in detail. The
results indicate that all the alloy tested in this paper are typical dendritic structures and possesses simple
BCC+FCC phases. AlCrCuFeNi alloy projects excellent compressive mechnical properties with fracture strength
of 1624MPa and plastic strain of 21%. After the addition of Ti,the hardness and wear resistance of
AlCrCuFeNiTi0.5 alloy improves.With the incerase of heating temperature, the strength, hardness, plasticity and
wear resistance of AlCrCuFeNiTi0.5 alloy are improved. Especially after holding at 800℃ for 12h, the strength
and plasticity of the alloy reached the highest with 1525MPa and 21%, respectively.
Key words: High entropy alloy; Microstructure; Mechnical properties; Heat treatment temperature
The discovery of high entropy alloys(HEAs) whose
properties are eminent is based on the research of bulk
amorphous alloys.The properties of traditional alloys can be
modified by means of adding different alloying elements
whose base element is only one kind. Different from
conventional alloy with a content greater than 50%, HEAs are
composed of multiprincipal elements in equimolar or
near-equimolar ratios which are defined as alloys that have at
least five principle elements with concentrations between 5%
and 35%[1,2,3]. Owing to the high mixing entropy and the
difficult diffusion of atoms, it tends to form higher thermal
stability of solid solution, nano and amorphous structures in
the HEAs[1,4,5,6]. Thus HEAs have an unique microstructure
features and outstanding properties such as high hardness,
high strength, high wear resistance and corrosion resistance,
resistance to cremation, excellent high temperature oxidation
properties and high thermal stability[4,5,7-16].At present, the
research hotspots of high entropy alloys mainly focus on light
high entropy alloys, ultra-fine grained high entropy alloys,
soft magnetic HEAs, nano precipitation strengthening
HEAs[17,18] and so on. It is applicable to various fields, such
as biomaterials, superconducting materials, radiation resistant
materials[19,20,21].It is worth mentioning that the study of
HEAs mostly concentrate on the microstructure and properties
of as-cast alloys, but little research was done on their heat
treatment. Therefore, AlCrCuFeNiTi0.5 alloy with lower cost is
chosen as the systerm to learn the change of microstructure
and properties after different heat treatment, which provides
convenience for futher discuss of HEAs.
1 Experiment
The equimolar alloys were prepared from commercial-purity
Al(99.99%)，Cr(99.99%)，Cu(99.99%)，Fe(99.99%)，Ni(99.99%)
and Ti(99.99%) by arc melting and casting method under argon
atmosphere. The oxide scale on the surface of the raw material
should be removed to make sure the purity and homogeneity
before remelting the alloys several times.The heat treatment
process was carried out in the MF-1400C- II box resistance
furnace. The heating and cooling rates were 5℃ /min and 10℃
/min respectively.
Afterwords, the samples were sintered at 600℃,800℃ and
1000℃ for 12 hours firstly, then they were allowed to get cold
to room temperature in the furnace itself. Samples were
sanded with 400, 600, 800, 1000 and 1200 SiC abrasive

2 Results and Discussion
2.1 Microstructure and properties of the as-cast alloys
Fig.1 shows the SEM backscattered electron images of the
as-cast AlCrCuFeNi and AlCrCuFeNiTi 0.5 alloys.The
microstructure exhibits a typical cast dendrite structure and
possess similar form. Two parts of the dendrite defined as
DR-A and DR-B are observed in these alloys.
Fig.1 SEM backscattered electron images of as-cast (a,b)
Ti0,(c,d)Ti0.5 alloys
Fig.2 XRD analysis of the as-cast AlCrCuFeNi and
AlCrCuFeNiTi0.5 alloys
Among them, the DR-B region is uniformly distributed
with black spherical particles, and the interdendritic region is
an amplitude modulation decomposition product. With the
addition of Ti, the black spherical particles in the DR-B region
become larger, and the spinodal decomposition products in the
interdendritic region tend to become a network structure.
According to the EDS chemical composition of the cast alloys
in Table 1, For Ti0, the DR-A region is rich in Al, Ni and Cu;
the DR-B region is rich in Cr and Fe accompanied with the
black spots gathered with Al, Ni; while in the interdendritic
region, the content of Cu is much higher than other elements,
and the content of Cr and Fe are very few.

The segregation of Cu and the content of Al,Ni between the
dendrites are much larger than that of Cr,Fe can be explained
by the positive mixing enthalpies of + 12 and + 13KJ/mol
between Cu-Cr and Cu-Fe respectively which is much higher
than that of -1 and 4KJ/mol between Cu-Al and Cu-Ni[24].
This is consistent with the literature[22] that the more negative
the mixture, the more stable the combination of elements is.
Therefore, the EDS results are consistent with the XRD, that
is to say for Ti0.5,the main component of DR-B is Cr, Fe and
the structure exhibits BCC, so it can be considered that the
corresponding phase of BCC in XRD is DR-B phase and a
small amount of Ni,Al andTi is dissolved in solid solution and
the interdendritic is a Cu-rich FCC phase.
The XRD patterns of the as-cast AlCrCuFeNi and
AlCrCuFeNiTi0.5 HEAs can be seen in Fig.2.The alloys
composed of simple BCC and FCC phase. Only one BCC
diffraction peak can be observed in AlCrCuFeNi alloy,which
can be ascribed to the same crystal lattice type and close
lattice constants of NiAl and FeCr phases, 2.887 and 2.876
respectively. The diffraction peak shift to the left can be
attributed to the lattice constant of BCC phase has a slight
increment as more Ti with large atomic radius solves into
BCC solution, so diffraction peaks corresponding to two BCC
solid solutions can be seen obviously in AlCrCuFeNiTi0.5
alloy.
Fig.3 reveals the compressive true stress-true strain
curves of the as-cast samples. It is clearly that Ti0 alloy
possess excellent compressive mechanical properties with
high strength of 1624MPa and large ductility of 21%.

Table 2 compares the hardness and wear resistance of the two
alloys tested in this research. It can be seen that the hardness of
the alloy did not increase significantly with the addition of Ti. It
does not change the phase composition of the alloy, most of
them are concentrated in the DR-A phase, and the low solid
solubility in DR-B phase wil not contribute to the serious lattice
distortion and thus will not bring about significant improvement
in hardness.
Fig.5 The wear surface morphology of (a)Ti0 (b) Ti0.5 alloys
According to Holm-Archard's classical theory, the wear
resistance of materials is proportional to the hardness[23].
HEAs with higher hardness (Ti0.5) have better wear resistance
than the soft alloy(Ti0). The wear pattern of AlCrCuFeNi alloy
in Fig.5a is abrasive wear because of the absence of cracks on
the surface, but the plough lines that is parallel to the friction
direction. Due to the raised irregular shape of the worn surface,
it can be judged that the wear type of AlCrCuFeNiTi0.5 alloy in
Fig.5b is adhesive wear.

2.3 Effect of heat treatment on properties of
AlCrCuFeNiTi0.5 HEAs
Fig.8 shows the stress-strain curves of the four heat treatment
alloys(as-cast,600℃/12h，800℃/12h，1000℃/12h) tested in this
research. It is clearly to see that the strength and plasticity of the
alloy can be improved by the heat treatment process. Especially
after holding at 800℃ for 12h, the strength and plasticity of the
alloy reached the highest with 1525MPa and 21% respectively.
The reason for the decrease of plasticity at 1000℃ is the reduce
of FCC phase.
On the one hand, combined with XRD results and
SEM photographs, it can be seen that the lattice distortion
in the tissue becomes severe after heat treatment, which
leads to an increase in the strength of the alloy; On the
other hand, the phase of DR-B in the alloy tends to grow in
the middle of the DR-A after heat treatment, rather than the
two parts of each phase separate growth. Thus, the interface
between the BCC phase rich in Cr and the FCC phase rich
in Cu becomes less, and the number of interfaces between
the NiAl phase and the ordered BCC becomes larger.

3 Conclusions
1) The as-cast AlCrCuFeNi and AlCrCuFeNiTi0.5 alloys
exhibit typical dendritic structure. The DR-A region on the
dendrite riched Al, Ni and Cu; the DR-B region riched Cr
accompanied with the black spots gathered with Al, Ni; while
in the interdendritic region, the content of Cu is much higher
than other elements, and the content of Cr and Fe very few.
2) AlCrCuFeNi alloy projects excellent mechnical
properties with fracture strength of 1624MPa and plastic strain
of 21%. The fracture mode of the alloys exhibit to be typical
cleavage fracture and the wear mechanisms of them are
abrasive wear and adhesive wear.
3) The dendrite volume fraction of AlCrCuFeNiTi0.5 high
entropy alloy decreases with the heating temperature
rising.and the organization of DR-A,DR-B regiontends to be
integrated. XRD and EDS analysis indicate that DR-A phase is
NiAl-based compounds, DR-B is Cr based BCC phase and ID is

Ti 对 AlCrCuFeNiTi0.5 高熵合金组织与性能的影响
王欣，张云鹏，颉晶
（西安理工大学，陕西 西安 710048)
摘 要： 研究了非自耗电弧熔炼 AlCrCuFeNi，AlCrCuFeNiTi0.5 高熵合金以及 AlCrCuFeNiTi0.5 高熵合金在不同条件 （600℃/12h, 800 ℃/12h,
1000℃/12h）热处理后的微观组织、硬度、压缩及耐磨性。结果表明，合金的微观组织均为典型的树枝晶结构，高熵效应使得体系的相
组成为简单的 FCC＋BCC。AlCrCuFeNi 高熵合金的断裂强度和应变分别为 1624MPa,21%。Ti 的加入提高了合金的硬度和耐磨性，且合
金的摩擦磨损主要以黏着磨损为主。热处理工艺使得 AlCrCuFeNiTi0.5 合金的强度，硬度，塑性和耐磨性均得到提高，尤其在800℃热处
理 12h 后合金断裂强度和最大应变率达到最高，分别为 1525MPa 和 21%。
关键词：高熵合金; 热处理温度; 微观组织; 力学性能
