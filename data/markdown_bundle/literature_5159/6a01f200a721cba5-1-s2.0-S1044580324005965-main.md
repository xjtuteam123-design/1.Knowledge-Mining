ABSTRACT
The anomalous increase of mechanical property of TiAl-based alloys at high temperature caters to the property
requirements as the high-temperature structural materials, which is thus important and worth studying. How-
ever, people currently are not clear whether the fracture toughness of TiAl-based alloys has an anomalous in-
crease at high temperature, and if so, how much it increases, and why it increases. In order to answer these
questions, the forged Ti-44Al-8Nb model alloy with fine and uniform microstructure was used. The fracture
toughness of the alloy at different temperatures was tested. The results indicated that the alloy did show an
anomalous increase of fracture toughness at 700
◦
C – 800
◦
C. The main crack deflected a large angle at 700
◦
C,
and terminated at the upper part at 750
◦
C and 800
◦
C. The fracture morphology was fine, and the cleavage plane
became smaller and less at high temperature. These characteristics corresponded to the anomalous increase of
fracture toughness at high temperature. Through reverse thinking, we found that the high dislocation density was
the root causing the anomalous increase of fracture toughness at high temperature. The forging-induced texture,
such as B2 〈 100 〉 //RD and γ -TiAl 〈 011]//RD could also promote the dislocations slip on the main slip surfaces
during the high temperature fracture toughness test, contributing to the final high dislocation density. The
critical crack size at 700
◦
C – 800
◦
C was about 4 times that of room temperature, corresponding to the higher
fracture toughness of the alloy at high temperature.
1. Introduction
For our most commonly used iron-based materials, the mechanical
properties at above 600
◦
C will decrease [ 1 – 3 ]. However, for TiAl-based
alloys, as the intermetallic, many mechanical properties anomalously
increase at high temperature [ 4 – 6 ]. This behavior of TiAl-based alloys
just meets the property requirements of high-temperature structural
materials. In addition, the alloys have the low-density advantage [ 7 , 8 ].
Therefore, TiAl-based alloys have attracted special attention in aero-
space and military fields [ 9 – 12 ]. People are accustomed to call the in-
crease of mechanical properties of TiAl-based alloys at high temperature
as an “ anomalous ” behavior.
Since the anomalous behavior of TiAl-based alloys is so important
that caters to the property requirements of high-temperature structural
materials, it is necessary to study this anomalous behavior and the
mechanism behind it. Lin et al. [ 13 ] prepared the classic Ti-48Al-2Cr-
2Nb (48 – 2-2) alloy by electron beam melting technology. The tensile
strength at 600

C and 700
◦

temperature (RT ~ 530 MPa, 600

believed to cause the anomalous temperature dependence of the tensile
strength. Zupan et al. [ 14 ] prepared the Al-rich single crystal Ti

From the above typical reports, we knew that people had paid
attention to the high-temperature anomalous behavior of TiAl-based
alloys for a long time. The dislocation theory was most commonly
used to explain this behavior in the existing research, achieving the
satisfactory results.
Fracture toughness is a very important mechanical property, which is
the synthesis of material strength and plasticity. Fracture toughness
reflects the resistance of materials to crack initiation and propagation
[ 17 , 18 ]. Especially for the brittle TiAl-based alloys, the fracture toughness
is particularly significant. Nevertheless, no research has focused on
whether the fracture toughness of TiAl-based alloys will increase
anomalously at high temperature, and if so, how much it will increase,
and why it will increase. In view of this, this study will answer these
questions. In this paper, the classic Ti-44Al-8Nb (at.%, Ti for the
remainder, the same below) alloy was selected as the model alloy. The
alloy was forged to obtain the fine and uniform microstructure, avoiding
the fluctuation of fracture toughness caused by coarse and uneven
microstructure. The room-temperature and high-temperature fracture
toughness of the forged alloy was tested, and it was found that the alloy
did show an anomalous increase of fracture toughness at high temper-
ature. The property data was analyzed, the crack initiation and propa-
gation behavior were investigated, the fracture morphology was
observed, the microstructure evolution was studied, and the mechanism
of anomalously increased high-temperature fracture toughness was
explored.
