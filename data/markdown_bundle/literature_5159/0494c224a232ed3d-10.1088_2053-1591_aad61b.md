Microstructure and high temperature compressive properties of 
Nb-based alloys fabricated by plasma arc-induction hybrid melting 
Xianyu Meng1, Chen Liu1, Yanxing Jiang2, Fuxin Wang1, Yanjin Xu3, Yanqing Su1*, Jingjie 
Guo1, Liangshun Luo1* 
1. National Key Laboratory for Precision Hot Processing of Metals, School of Materials Science 
and Engineering, Harbin Institute of Technology, Harbin 150001, China 
2. Shanghai Sinotec Co., Ltd, Shanghai 201703, China 
3. A VIC Manufacturing Technology Institute, Beijing 100024, China 
Abstract: A new melting system called plasma arc -induction hybrid melting technology was 
designed in this work to fabricate Nb -based alloys, which combines the advantages of plasma 
arc melting and levitation melting. The ternary alloy Nb-16Si-22Ti alloy ingot prepared by the 
new method was found to have a high level of chemical homogeneity with the difference of 
<0.5 at% in the concentration distribution . Three alloys C103, C109 and M532, which are the 
widely used alloy grades in industry, are chosen as base. Then, 16%Si and 22%Ti was added in 
the three base alloys and new alloys BC103, BC109 and BM532 were obtained to study the 
effects of Si and Ti addition on the microstructure and mechanical properties of Nb -based alloys. 
Among them, alloy Nb-16Si-22Ti-5Mo-2Cr-2Al-2Hf (BM532) was found to have the best high 
temperature compressive properties, which was used as base and W element was added as a 
replacer of Hf by 0, 1, 2 at% to further improve the high temperature mechanical strength of the 
alloy. The results indicated that alloy Nb -16Si-14Ti-5Mo-2Cr-2Al-1W has the best high 
temperature compressive properties, with a compressive strength of 1034MPa at 1200°C and 
725MPa at 1500°C. 
Key words: Plasma arc -induction hybrid melting ; Nb-based alloy; High temperature 
compressive properties; 
 
1 Introduction 
Over the past few decades, Ni-based superalloys have been used extensively as structural materials in 
aerospace environment because of their excellent high temperature properties and corrosion resistance. 
Monocrystalline Ni -based superalloys can tolerate temperatures as high as 1200 °C, 90% of the ir 
melting point , which is the limit for their further use in extreme high -temperature environments[1-4]. 
Several structure materials, such as intermetallics, refractory alloys, ceramics and C/C composites, have 
been found as repla cement of Ni -based superalloys. Among them, Nb-Si based a lloys are particularly 
promising due to their high melting points, low density, good high temperature strength, excellent creep

based alloy s, t he intermetallic phases (Nb 5Si3, Nb3Si) provide high -temperature creep and oxidation 
resistance, while the Nb solid solution (Nb ss) improves the ambient -temperature fracture resistance of 
alloy matrix. Nb-Si based alloy s have been candidates for high -temperature structural applications in 
the aerospace and military as for instance turbine hot section components [5-7]. The development of Nb 
based alloys is important since the metal Nb in its pure stat e presents low hardness and low oxidation 
resistance, which strongly reduce its applications [8, 9] . Applied at an elevated temperature, the 
mechanical properties of Nb-Si based alloys, such as high temperature strength and creep resistance, 
would be the mo st important issues to be focused on. Successful improvement in the high temperature 
strength of Nb based alloy has been achieved by increasing the volume fraction of niobium silicide or 
alloying elements Mo, Hf, Ta and W for solid -solution strengthening . The compressive strength of 
Nb-18Si-10Ti-10Mo-15W prepared by directional solidification is as high as 650MPa at 1700K.[10, 11] 
A variety of processing schemes have been used to fabricate Nb-Si based alloy s, including 
arc-melting, induction melting , investment casting, directional solidification and pow der metallurgy 
processing. Arc melting is the most widely used method for preparing Nb-Si based alloys, because of 
the high enough arc temperature. However, it also lead to the component segregation and thus the 
non-uniformity in structure and properties of alloy s. Levitation melting is a very efficient way to obtain 
a uniform component through strong turbulent stirring motions in the melt induced by rotational part of 
electromagnetic forces [12-14]. However, it is difficult to melt the high melting point alloy elements such 
as Nb, Hf, Ta, etc., since the temperature provided by induction coil is low. In this study, we designed a 
new system, called plasma arc-induction hybrid melting, which combines the advantages of plasma arc 
melting and levitation melting, such as superheating and cleanness. Molten metal never comes into 
contact with the crucible wall which is a source of contamination in conventional melting technique . It 
was on the basis of this study that the effects of alloying elements on the microstructure and properties 
of Nb-Si based alloys, especially on the high temperature compressive properties were investigated in 
details. The high temperature strength of Nb-Si alloys has been one of the main concerns as preferential 
issue and has been increased as high as possible by the solid solution strengthening or increasing the 
volume fraction of the Nb5Si3[15-17].

3 Experimental 
Table 1 shows the nominal compositions of the ten alloys studied in this paper. Nb-16Si-22Ti (alloy 
X) was selected to study the structur e and component distribution of the ingot prepared by plasma 
arc-induction hybrid melting . Three a lloys (Grade C103, C109 and M532) are the widely used alloy 
grade in industry. Alloy BC103, BC109 and BM532, which belongs to Nb-Si-Ti system, were fabricated 
to study the effects of S i and Ti addition on the microstructure and mechanical properties of Nb-based 
alloys. Then, alloy BM532 was used as base and W element was added in alloy BM532 as a replace of 
Hf by 0, 1, 2 mol% to further improve the high temperature mechanical strength of the alloy. Button 
ingots of each about 700g were prepared by plasma arc -induction hybrid melting using commercial 
purity elements (Nb 99.99%, Si 99.99%, Ti 99.7%, Hf 99.9%, W 99.8%, Mo 99.99%, Al 99.99%, Cr 
99.99%, Zr 99.8%, Y 99.7%) under argon atmosphere for twice. 
The microstructures were observed using scanning electron microscopy (SEM), and the compositions 
of each phase and bulk w ere analyzed by X -ray spectroscopy (ED S) and X-ray diffraction (XRD). 
Inductively coupled plasma (ICP) analysis were used to determine the chemical composition of 
Nb-16Si-22Ti alloy. High temperature compression testing was performed on Gleeble 1500D machi nes 
using the sample with dimension s of φ4×6 mm2. The samples were heated to 1200°C or 1500°C with a 
heating rate of 10°C/s and hold for 20s at 1200°C or 1500°C. The testing was carried out at a strain rate 
of 2×10-3/s until that the deformation arrived at 10%.

4 Results and analysis 
4.1 Chemical homogeneity of Nb-16Si-22Ti alloy ingot 
Fig. 2 shows the Nb-16Si-22Ti alloy ingot with the cross section of about 50*60mm2, wh ich was 
partitioned into twelve areas. The atom concentrations of each area detected through ICP analysis was 
listed in Table 2. The concentration distribution of Ti and Si element was mapped as shown in Fig. 3. 
There was macrosegregation of Si and Ti in the edge and central parts of the ingot with a variation of Si 
and Ti concentration of 21.75~22.17 at% and 15.83~16.11 at%, respectively. The central section of the 
alloy ingot, which is the last to freeze (LTF) region, is enriched with Si element, while Ti atoms tend to 
concentrate in the edge of the ingot during electromagnetic stirring and freezing process, due to the low 
density and low melting point of Nb ss compared with Nb5Si3. The standard deviation of the 
concentration distribution of Nb, Ti and Si w as 0.12, 0.14 and 0.08, re spectively, Obviously, the 
difference of <0.5 at% in the concentration distribution is much smaller than that of samples prepared 
by arc -melting[19], indicating the high level of chemical homogeneity of ingot prepared by plasma 
arc-induction hybrid melting.

niobium silicides are generated in this case, and they inhibit the grain growth . A homogeneous and 
refined microstructures is potentially desired to improve the high temperature mechanical properties for 
Nb-Si-Ti system[20].

Fig. 6 illustrates the compressive stress-strain curves of alloys at 1200°C. It can be seen that the 0.2% 
yield strength σ 0.2 of Nb-Si-Ti system (alloy BC103, BC109, BM532) is markedly higher than that of 
alloy C103, C109, M532, which is attributed to the dispersion strengthening of the eutectic structure of 
(Nb, x)ss/(Nb,x)5Si3 and the solid solution strengthening of Ti element in alloy matrix. It is noteworthy 
that the stress of alloy M532, BC103, BC109 and BM532 decrease s slowly after reaching the peak 
compressive stress, σ max, while alloy C103 and C109 keep an increasing stress in the yield stage, 
especially for C109, showing a strain -hardening behavior. As shown in Fig. 6, alloy BM532 has the 
highest compressive strength among the six alloys, with σmax of 788MPa and σ0.2 of 725MPa, indicating 
the best high temperature compressive properties of alloy BM532. In order to further improve the high 
temperature strength of alloy BM532, W element was introduce d into alloy BM532 as a replace of Hf, 
considering the excellent solid solution strengthening capacity of W element in Nb-Si based alloys. The 
element W has been found one of the most efficient elements for improving high -temperature strength, 
which is mainly attribute to the solid-solution strengthening of the Nbss by W.[21]

Fig. 9 (a) and (b) show the compressive stress-strain curves for the alloy W0, W1 and W2 at 1200°C 
and 1500 °C, respectively. It can be seen that alloy W1 has the best high temperature compressive 
strength, with σmax of up to 10 34MPa at 1200 °C and 7 25MPa at 1500 °C, σ0.2 of up to 952 MPa at 
1200°C and 680 MPa at 1500°C. It is suggested that the Nbss dendrites provide the 
ambient-temperature fracture resistance of alloy matrix and the intermetallic phases Nb 5Si3 with high 
melting temperature and good strength retention at elevated temperature provide the high -temperature 
strength. Composites consisting of a strong ductile phase Nbss and a high strength, stable inter -matallic 
phase Nb 5Si3 can achieve improvements in high t emperature strength. Jiangbo Sha et al. has 
investigated the effects of W on the microstructure and the mechanical response of Nb -Si based alloy at 
room temperature and high temperatures. The Nb -Si-W ternary was strengthened at high temperatures 
by the add ition of W or Si, respectively, by the solution -strengthening mechanism of W and the 
hard-phase strengthening mechanism of Nb5Si3[21]. 
In our work, the increase of W content results in a decrease of Nb 5Si3, and thus a degradation of high 
temperature compre ssive strength of alloy. While the increasing W content also leads to the 
enhancement of solid solution strengthening effect on the Nbss, which improves the high temperature 
compressive strength of alloy. The two opposite effects result in that alloy W1 with middle content of W 
has the best compressive properties at 1200°C and 1500°C.
