SYNOPSIS
Fine-grained sintered molybdenum alloys dispersed with 0.8mol% ZrC or TaC particles were prepared by mechanical 
alloying and spark plasma sintering, and tensile properties of the molybdenum alloys were investigated at 300K and 
at 1170-1770K. The particle-dispersed alloys exhibited much higher strength than a pure molybdenum which was 
fully recrystallized. Furthermore, the ZrC particle-dispersed alloy showed a large elongation of 120% at 1770K.
These excellent tensile properties of the particle-dispersed alloys are attributed to fine-grained microstructure and 
uniform dispersion of the fine particles.
KEY WORDS
molybdenum, fine grain, particle dispersion, tensile property, strengthening mechanism
