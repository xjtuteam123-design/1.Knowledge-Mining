Abstract
The synergetic effect of Re and Ru on g/g? interface strengthening of Ni-base SC superalloys has been in vestigated by performing
DMol3 calculations. Results show that the synergetic effect of Re and Ru on the interface strengthening is better than that achie ved
by the individual Re or Ru due to Re- d/Ru-d, Re-d/Ni-d and Ru-d/Ni-d hybridizations. The electronic mechanism underlying the
synergetic effect of Re and Ru on g/g? interface strengthening is related to the charge transfer of electrons and the enhancement of d-
bonding hybridization among Re /C3/Ru, Re /C3/Ni and Ru /C3/Ni atoms.

1. Introduction
The development of new generation of Ni-base single
crystal (SC) superalloys is more increasingly controlled
at a micro-scale. For instance, mechanical properties
serve as examples where macroscopic beha vior is
strongly influenced by the g/g? interface interactions at
atomic-scale [1]. As experimental techniques for study-
ing these interactions are limited, researchers are making
increasing use of first principles quantum-mechanism
calculations as tools in the in vestigation of such inter-
facial interactions [2 /C1
/4]. In particular, the study of the g/
g? interface strengthening of Ni-base superalloys by
refractory elements has been the subject of computa-
tional investigation [5].
As a refractory element, Re is pro ven to be a potent
strengthener and key element for the impro vement of
mechanical properties of SC superalloys [6 /C1
/8]. With
particular substitution of Ru for Re, the fourth genera-
tion SC superalloys can yield a better balance of
properties [9], and thus are considered as a promising
alternative to the third generation superalloys to in-
crease the temperature capability without high density
and microstructural instability features. The new alloys
show more oxidation resistant [9], better corrosion
resistance, significantly higher creep strength, and less
propensity to the precipitation of TCP phases. In a
recent study, we ha ve performed first principles quan-
tum mechanics DMol calculations on a cluster designed
to model the strengthening effect of individual Re or Ru
substitution at the g/g? interface [5]. We found that the
Re(Ru)-d/Ni-d hybridization between first nearest
neighbor (FNN) Re(Ru) and Ni atoms can lead to the
strengthening of interface cohesion and shear strength.
This might be beneficial for the creep rupture strength of
the alloys from an electronic point of view. Atom probe
experiments re vealed [10 /C1
/12] that Re mostly partitions
to g matrix, and pileups ahead of the g/g? interface,
whereas Ru distributes uniformly among g matrix, g?
precipitate and across the g/g? interface [13]. Studies of
interactions between Re and Ru at the interface are
crucial in the complete understanding of the strengthen-
ing. In the current research, our attention has been
directed to the synergetic effect of Re and Ru on the
interface strengthening. To our knowledge, there are no
direct experimental techniques that can be used to
identify this synergetic effect between Re and Ru
elements on the interface strengthening. As an alter-

2. Calculations and results
We report the results of DMol cluster calculations on
a local en vironment of g/g? interface with both Re and
Ru simultaneously substituting for Al and Ni atoms.
The details of the DMol cluster calculation ha ve been
described elsewhere [5,14]. Fig. 1 depicts the computa-
tional cluster model with 63 atoms in size used in the
calculations. As in the pre vious study [5], we placed a
single Re atom into Al(1) site as suggested by experi-
ments [15,16] at 0 /C1
/0 layer. There are no direct experi-
mental reports, such as by the atom probe, on the Ru
distribution on the g/g? interface for Re-containing Ni-
base SC superalloys. In order to study the synergetic
effect of Re and Ru on the interfacial strengthening, we
substituted two Ru atoms for Ni(3) sites on the 1 /C1
/1
layer located in the upper g region, in which the Re and
Ru atoms are within the FNN separation. This model
allows us to in vestigate the Re /C3/Ru interactions without
changing the symmetry of the model cluster. The
synergetic effect between Re and Ru can be effecti vely
examined by DMol calculations. The interaction be-
tween Re and Ru beyond the FNN range can be
neglected without altering the conclusion, and it has
been confirmed by the bond order calculations. The
relaxation of the cluster was performed for the system.

3. Summary
The synergetic effect and mechanism between Re and
Ru on g/g? interface strengthening of Ni-base SC
superalloys has been in vestigated by performing first
principles quantum mechanics DMol calculations. The
electron charge density distributions re veal that strong
bonds are formed among the FNN Re /C3
/Ru, Re /C3/Ni and
Ru /C3/Ni atoms, which can be explained in terms of Re- d/
Ru-d, Re-d/Ni-d and Ru-d/Ni-d hybridizations. The net
vertical and horizontal bond order a BOs indicate that
the combination of Re and Ru can significantly increase
the interface cohesion and the shear strength, and the
synergetic effect of Re and Ru on the interface
strengthening is better than that achie ved by the
individual Re or Ru. Charge transfers described by
MOPA show that 4 s(Ni) and 6 s(Re) electrons transfer
to their 3d and 5d orbitals, respectively, while 5s and 4d
electrons of Ru transfer to the 5 p orbital. Our results
suggest that the electronic mechanism underlying the
synergetic effect of Re and Ru on g/g? interface
strengthening is related to the charge transfer of
electrons and the enhancement of d-bonding hybridiza-
tion among FNN Re /C3
/Ru, Re /C3/Ni and Ru /C3/Ni atoms.
