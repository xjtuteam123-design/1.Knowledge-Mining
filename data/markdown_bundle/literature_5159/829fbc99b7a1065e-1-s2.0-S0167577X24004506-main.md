1. Introduction 
High entropy alloys (HEAs) have received widespread attention due 
to their unique properties [1,2] . However, it seems difficult to achieve a 
balance between strength and plasticity in single-phase HEAs [3] . Lu 
et al. [4] proposed and prepared a dual-phase lamellar AlCoCrFeNi
2.1 
EHEA with high strength and excellent ductility. The difficulty in 
balancing the strength and plasticity of single-phase high entropy alloys 
has been solved. 
Currently, casting technology is mostly used to prepare eutectic high 
entropy alloys. The high cost and potential for casting defects caused by 
a series of casting steps seriously hinder the production and application 
of these alloys [5] . The double-wire arc additive manufacturing (D- 
WAAM) technology developed based on arc additive manufacturing 
technology has the advantages of high deposition efficiency, high ma -
terial utilization, and low equipment cost, and can effectively solve the 
above problems. In particular, the double-wire feeding system uses 
combined cable wire (CCW) as the main welding wire and locates the 
eutectic point by adjusting the bypass Al wire feeding speed, which 
greatly improves the flexibility of alloy design. Moreover, our team has 
used arc additive manufacturing technology to prepare HEAs with 
excellent performance, which has a good working foundation [6 – 8] . 
Therefore, for the first time, this paper adopts D-WAAM technology, 
using self-designed CCW as the main welding wire and Al wire as the 
side axis feeding wire to locate the eutectic point and prepare an 
Al
20.33
Co
21.60
Fe
30.45
Ni
27.62 
eutectic high entropy alloy with a strength 
plasticity balance. 
2. Experimental 
2.1. Materials and methods 
In this paper, cold metal transfer smart arc additive manufacturing 
equipment was used to prepare EHEAs. As the main welding wire, CCW 
is transported vertically by CCW equipment, and the Al welding wire is 
transported by a side-axis wire feeder, as shown in Fig. 1 (a) . Among 
them, the CCW is designed independently. It consists of two Fe wires, 
two Co wires and three Ni wires. The wire diameters are all 0.6 mm(as 
shown in Fig. 1 (a
1
) ). The wire feeding speed is 7 m/min. The Al wire is a 
pure Al wire with a diameter of 0.8 mm, and the wire feeding speed is 
3.4 m/min.

tensile strength of 1022 MPa, and an elongation of 13.2 %. Under true 
stress – strain conditions, these values are 667 MPa, 1152 MPa, and 12.3 
% respectively. Moreover, the true stress – strain hardening rate and true 
strain curve in the attached figure also show that the alloy exhibits 
excellent work hardening ability. The figure shows typical three-stage 
hardening behavior. It decreases sharply in stage I, decreases slowly in 
stage II and decreases sharply in stage III until it breaks. The decrease in 
the strain hardening rate in stages I and III can usually be attributed to 
the dynamic recovery of dislocations and the exhaustion of the mate -
rial ’ s deformation capacity [9,10] . The nearly constant stage II usually 
originates from the slip system of soft and hard phases and the dislo -
cation accumulation at the phase boundary [9,11] . The excellent work 
hardening ability indicates effective necking (and failure) delay [12] , 
which is crucial for reliability in engineering applications. Therefore, 
excellent work hardening ability of the EHEA is the main reason for its 
high plasticity. As shown in Fig. 3 (b) , compared with EHEAs prepared 
by casting and arc melting, EHEA prepared by D-WAAM has better 
strength and plasticity. The combination of the soft L1
2 
phase and the 
hard B2 phase and the nano-lamellar microstructural morphology are 
the main reasons for its excellent comprehensive mechanical properties 
[13] . The L1
2 
phase is beneficial to the plasticity of the alloy, while the 
B2 phase is beneficial to the strength of the alloy. In addition, D-WAAM 
uses arc as the heat source to have a faster cooling rate and form a finer 
lamellar structure, resulting in an increase in the density of the interface 
between the soft L1
2 
phase and the hard B2 phase, suppressing 
dislocation movement and improving the strengthening effect [14,15] .

4. Conclusions 
This study successfully prepared the Al
20.33
Co
21.60
Fe
30.45
Ni
27.62 
EHEA using D-WAAM technology and characterized and studied its 
microstructure and mechanical properties. Microstructure analysis 
revealed that the prepared EHEA had an L1
2
/B2 biphasic lamellar 
eutectic structure. Tensile tests showed that the prepared EHEA had 
excellent strength-plastic balance. Its yield strength is 632 MPa, its ul -
timate tensile strength is 1022 MPa, and its elongation is 13.2 %. These 
mechanical properties are superior to those of other EHEAs prepared by 
traditional casting and arc melting techniques. Therefore, D-WAAM 
EHEAs may be more competitive than other methods in structural 
applications.
