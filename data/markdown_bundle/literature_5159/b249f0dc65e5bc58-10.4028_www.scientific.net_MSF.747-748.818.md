Abstract. Some research results are introduced briefly in this paper. A new kind of titanium alloy was 
designed based on nine elements system, which belongs to ( α+β) type. Bars with diameter of 14 mm 
were produced via Vacuum Arc Remelting (VAR) meltin g and conventional forging and rolling 
facilities. Influence of alloy elements’ contents and heat treatments on microstructure and mechanical 
properties of the new alloy were studied. The resul ts show that Ultimate Tensile Strength (UTS) is 
about 1200~1700MPa and Elongation (EL) is normally less than 5% at R.T., though Electron Beam 
(EB) welding can be proceeded, but the toughness of the alloy is poor at R.T.High temperature (HT) 
short-term strength of the alloy is also acceptable up to 600 ℃. Based on the above performances, the 
new material can be applied in the corresponding circumstances. 
Introduction 
Titanium alloys are broadly used in various economic domains due to their special characteristics, 
such as non-magnetic, corrosion-resistant, biocompa tibility, higher strength to ratio. Usually the 
ultimate strengths of the service high strength titanium alloys are no more than 1200 MPa, and if UTS 
is larger than 1200 MPa, then it should belong to ultra-high strength titanium alloys. Many researchers 
study the ultra-high strength titanium alloys with relatively higher ductility simultaneously [1~3], and 
even some traditional titanium alloys (Ti-153, BT-22, etc.) exhibit higher strength at certain shapes, 
i.e. sheets [4,5]. But in this paper, a new kind of titanium alloy with super high strength and low 
plasticity is developed, and it should be adopted to apply to some certain circumstances in the future, 
such as parts of the expendable aerospace flying objects. 
Experimental Procedure 
The new alloy was designed by a semi-empirical meth od. First, a nine elements alloy system is 
selected by empirical, that is Ti-Al-Sn-Zr-Mo-Cr-Nb-Ni-Si system. Second, the detail data of content 
were determined by crystal structure metallurgical method, which defined as Ti- （6.5~7.9 ）Al-
（1.6~2.6 ）Sn-（1.6~2.6 ）Zr-（1.8~2.8 ）Mo-（0.9~2.3 ）Cr-（1.6~2.6 ）Nb-（0.8~1.8 ）Ni-（0~0.5 ）
Si in mass or weight percentage, which is transformed from atom percentage originally [6]. As shown 
in Table 1, three chemical compositions were design ed to study the actual difference in extreme 
situations, alloy S1 added with all maximum data of the alloying elements, and alloy S2 with 
maximum β stabilized elements and minimum α stabilized elements & neutral stabilized elements, 
while alloy S3 is the vice-versa of S2, i.e. with m aximum α stabilized elements Al & neutral 
stabilized elements Sn, Zr and minimum β stabilized elements Mo, Cr, Nb, Ni, Si. 
Preparation and study procedure of the new alloys a re as follows: raw materials selection → 
preparation of consumable electrodes → multi-VAR melting → 10 kg / per ingots → turning and 
hot-top removing → hot cogging in β phase → forging across β phase → billet samples for 
microstructure and mechanical properties study and the balance → quasi-β or ( α+β) hot rolling → 
φ14 mm bars → annealing → microstructure analysis, mechanical properties tes ting, and also the 
welding properties evaluation.

Results and Discussion 
Bars were cut and processed to heat treatment (mill annealing) or observed the metallographic 
microstructure, or done the conventional tension te sting to measure the tensile properties. Tension 
properties at room temperature or high temperature were shown in Table 2, 3 and UTS & EL vs. 
Temperature were shown in Fig.1, Electron Beam Welding samples were shown in Fig. 2. Toughness 
test results were listed in Table 4. Microstructures of the rolling bars at different states were shown in 
Fig. 3. 
The results show that S1 alloy exhibits the highest RT strength with the lowest plasticity, while S3 
alloy possesses the lowest RT strength and highest plasticity at R states. After mill annealing, 
plasticity changes small but strengths decrease remarkable especially for S1 alloy, the decline values 
is about 25 MPa (S3)~ 400 MPa (S1), and S2 alloy becomes the strongest one. After EB welding plus 
annealing treatment, S1 alloy also shows the highest strength and the three alloys plasticity decrease 
generally, the welding strength coefficients are more than 0.8, which demonstrates that the new alloy 
possesses certain weld ability. 
Toughness reviewing at R.T. shows that the impact t oughness αKU is generally no more than 10 
J/m 2, and the fracture toughness K IC is no more than 30 MPa √m, tough the values can be improved 
somewhat after mill annealing. It can be judged that the new alloy is a kind of near-brittle material, but 
when compared among the three, S3 is the ductile one relatively.

When the temperatures changed, the tensile strength including ultimate tensile strength & yield 
strength and the plasticity including elongation & reduction in area will change accordingly. 
Temperature range between 25 ℃ to 600 ℃ was studied. When temperature increases from RT to 
550 ℃, UTS & YS declined almost 50%, especially for YS and for alloy S1, while plasticity increases 
2~10 times of that at RT; tough S1 alloy also exhibits the highest UTS strength, but S3 alloy occupied 
the second in UTS and the number one in YS, while S2 alloy becomes the number one in EL & RA. 
When temperature increases continuously from 550 ℃ to 600 ℃, both UTS and YS of alloy S3 
occupied the number one position, while S2 also wit h the lowest strength values. Whenever 
temperature changes up and down, alloy S1 always shows the lowest plasticity. 
Microstructure observation shows different morphologies among the three sub-alloy compositions, 
especially between S3 and S1/S2. Dispersed particles with size of 1~3 µm homogeneously distributed 
in ( α+β) matrix is the characteristics of S1 alloy (Fig.3 (a)), after annealing, the size of the spherical

Summary 
1. A nine elements system, ( α+β) two phase, and ultra-high titanium alloy was desi gned and 
developed. Three extreme composition sub-alloys stu dies show that the UTS of the new titanium 
alloy can reach 1300~1700 MPa, but with lower plast icity and ductility , normally EL less than 5%, 
α
KU less than 10 J/m 2, and KIC less than 30 MPa √m. It ’s an ultra-high strength but brittle Ti alloy with 
certain EB weld feasibility, and can be serviced for special circumstances. 
2. High temperature tension test is also a kind of insufficient annealing procedure actually, 
annealing makes the alloy softens. Temperature incr eases from RT to 550 ℃, and up to 600 ℃, the 
strength sorting changes from S1 > S2 > S3 to S1 > S3 > S2, and to S3 > S1 > S2 at last, while the 
plasticity sorting changes from S3 > S2 > S1 to S2> S3 > S1, which showing that αphase-rich S3 
alloy exhibits better heat-resistance while β phase-rich S2 alloy exhibits better plasticity in elevated 
temperature. 
3. Characteristics of metallographic microstructure s: spherical metastable second phase particles 
disperse in ( α+β) matrix homogeneously for S1 and S2 alloy at as-ro lling state, which contribute to 
the strength increment at RT. The strength declined somewhat after annealing because of dissolving 
of the particles. Basket wave type of microstructure of alloy S3 changes little before or after annealing, 
which indicates better high temperature strengths.
