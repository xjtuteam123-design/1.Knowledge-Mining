ABSTRACT 
It is necessary to improve the high temperature strength and maintain reasonable room temperature toughness of 
refractory high entropy alloy. A novel type of HfMoScTaZr refractory high entropy alloy was prepared by vacuum

1. Introduction 
In recent years, the aerospace industry urgently needs to improve the 
efficiency of gas turbine engine. This is mainly due to the need to reduce 
fuel consumption. Increasing engine operating temperature is one of the 
most effective ways to improve engine efficiency. However, the initial 
melting point of Ni based alloy obviously limits the working tempera -
ture. Therefore, in the development of the next generation of gas tur -
bine, it is very important that the material has a high enough melting 
point. Refractory metals, such as refractory high entropy alloys, have 
high melting point and high mutual solubility of alloying elements, so 
they are considered as candidate materials for such engineering 
applications.

2. Experiments 
The pure metals Ta, Zr, Hf, Nb and Sc with purity more than 99.9% 
were used as raw materials. The refractory high entropy alloy ingot of 
HfMoScTaZr was prepared by vacuum arc melting equipment according 
to equal mole ratio. The melting process was protected by argon. In 
order to ensure that the material will not be oxidized in the melting 
process, it is necessary to melt pure metal Ti first to absorb the residual 
gas of EAF (electrical arc furnace). Because of containing refractory 
metal elements, the alloy ingot shall be turned over for smelting at least 
5 times, and the time for each time shall not be less than 5 min, so as to 
ensure uniform composition.

mainly single BCC solid solution structure. It can be seen from the DSC 
analysis results of HfMoScTaZr refractory high entropy alloy in Fig. 2

entropy alloy phase structure are Δ H

distortion, thus alleviating the local stress concentration in the lattice. 
This is helpful to improve ductility of refractory high entropy alloy.
